Looking for a direct comparison of altRx vs SnagRx? Quick verdict: for buyers willing to commit to a 12 month plan SnagRx advertises the lowest annual rate and price lock; for a no-commitment, low starting monthly price altRx advertises semaglutide from $89/month and emphasizes same price every month and free 2-day delivery.

How pricing and billing differ

Price is the clearest difference between altRx and SnagRx. altRx advertises semaglutide starting at $89/month and tirzepatide from $149/month as a limited offer. SnagRx advertises semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on the 12 month plan, with month-to-month rates of $99 and $149 respectively. SnagRx explicitly requires the 12 month plan to get the $69 and $119 figures. altRx promotes no membership or hidden fees and multiple multi month discounts for longer plans.

Price predictability and repricing during titration

altRx emphasizes “same price every month” for its advertised program. SnagRx highlights a price locked flat at every dose for the life of treatment and at renewal, which it presents as protection against repricing during titration. If predictable per dose pricing over time is important, SnagRx documents an explicit price lock on its 12 month plan.

Clinician review, pharmacies, and state coverage

Both providers deliver prescriptions through compounding pharmacies, and both include telehealth in the process. SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and the OpenLoop Health clinician network and states clinician review is typically within five hours with next day shipping. altRx states prescriptions are filled by independently owned state licensed 503A compounding pharmacies in its network, offers 100% online medical visits, and says the membership is available to residents of all 50 U.S. states, with prescribing authority subject to state rules. SnagRx states OpenLoop Health clinicians cover 48 states.

Shipping, delivery speed, and included supplies

altRx advertises free and discreet 2 day delivery and includes a complete treatment kit with vials, syringes, prep pads, and guidance. SnagRx advertises next day UPS or FedEx shipping in temperature controlled packaging and states free shipping arriving in 1 to 2 days depending on order timing. Both say shipping is discreet and both include injection supplies with the price.

Program extras and guarantees

altRx highlights buy now pay later options and no commitment with cancel anytime. SnagRx highlights a full refund if a prescription is declined and mentions a 6 month weight loss guarantee with terms. SnagRx also states LegitScript certification which customers can verify. altRx stresses low advertised starting prices and ongoing clinician support as part of its all inclusive pricing.

Regulatory note and medication status

Both platforms provide compounded medications. The sources state that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. altRx and SnagRx both present telehealth clinician review and pharmacy fulfillment as part of their programs and advise that a provider determines whether treatment is appropriate.

How to choose between altRx and SnagRx

Choose SnagRx if you want the lowest published annual plan rates and an explicit price lock across titration and renewal, and if you value named partner pharmacies and a clinician network that advertises fast review and next day shipping. Choose altRx if you prefer a low advertised nonannual starting price with no membership fees, 100 percent online visits, a 50 state membership availability statement, free 2 day delivery, and the option to pay with Affirm, Klarna, or Afterpay.

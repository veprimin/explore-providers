Quick verdict: IVIM Health emphasizes clinician-guided optimization and unusually strong third-party ratings, while FeelGood Meds emphasizes lower advertised starting price tiers, no membership fee, and free shipping. This comparison of IVIM Health vs FeelGood Meds focuses on pricing and fees, protocol claims and evidence, verification and ratings, medication options, and shipping or membership terms.

Key difference 1: Pricing, starting costs, and membership fees

IVIM Health lists pricing that includes a promotional free month with code GIFT75 and published rates that start at $75 in some listings and commonly present a $99 first month and $174/month ongoing rate in FAQ text. IVIM also notes a $74.99/month program fee in some materials. FeelGood Meds advertises compounded injections starting at $149/month, compounded tablets starting at $249/month, and a branded option shown from $1,999/month, and explicitly states no membership fee.

See IVIM pricing and GIFT75 offer

Key difference 2: Protocol claims and supporting evidence

IVIM Health promotes a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol that the platform says can accelerate weight loss outcomes by up to 40% compared to standard GLP-1 protocols. IVIM cites internal publications and retrospective observational studies in Obesity Pillars and reports that patients following the Ivim Protocol lost an average of 46% more weight over 68 weeks compared to another published GLP-1 trial, while noting this was not a head-to-head randomized trial. FeelGood Meds does not present a comparable proprietary protocol claim in the captured material.

Key difference 3: Third-party ratings and satisfaction signals

IVIM Health is documented as having 4.9-star ratings on both Google and Trustpilot, which the source describes as a rare dual third-party verification signal. FeelGood Meds materials do not include a noted dual third-party rating in the captured content.

Key difference 4: Medication formats and branded options

Both providers offer compounded medications. IVIM lists compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide pricing examples and explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved. FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections and tablets and also presents a branded medication option at a higher starting price. Both sources include regulatory notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Key difference 5: Shipping, fees, and cancellation features

FeelGood Meds highlights no membership fee and free shipping in the captured materials. IVIM highlights a no-obligation free consultation valued at $75 for new patients and a promotional free month with code GIFT75, and notes direct-pay operation with no insurance billing. Specific cancellation or renewal terms for FeelGood Meds were not captured in detail; IVIM materials reference program fees and ongoing monthly rates.

Bottom line and how to choose

IVIM Health vs FeelGood Meds breaks down mainly to whether you prioritize clinician-guided protocol optimization and strong third-party ratings or you prioritize lower advertised starting tiers, no membership fee, and free shipping. Both offer compounded medication options and both include regulatory cautions that compounded drugs are not FDA-approved.

Choose IVIM Health if you want a clinician-guided GLP-1 ID protocol backed by IVIM’s published retrospective studies and value the reported 4.9-star ratings on Google and Trustpilot and the included no-obligation $75 consultation and promotional GIFT75 free-month membership.

Choose FeelGood Meds if you want advertised lower starting tiers for compounded injections, no membership fee, and free shipping, and you will confirm eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms, and state availability before enrolling.

Head-to-head summary: Sprout Health vs CoreAgeRx

Both providers deliver clinician-reviewed, direct-pay GLP-1 or related peptide treatment options and note that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. They also preserve regulatory caveats that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Below we compare the most distinctive differences supported by the source.

Key difference 1: Pricing model and first-month cost

Sprout Health promotes a time-limited offer: get $50 off your first month, bringing the initial cost to $199, with ongoing pricing at $249 per month after the discounted month. CoreAgeRx emphasizes flat monthly pricing and no membership fees, listing semaglutide at $79 per month and tirzepatide at $129 per month with the claim of the same price every month.

Key difference 3: Medication offerings and dosing notes

Sprout Health positions itself in the GLP-1 market offering compounded semaglutide rather than branded Ozempic or Wegovy, and its messaging frames potential body weight reductions of 10 to 26 percent over 12 months based on GLP-1 literature, while noting no formal outcome guarantee. CoreAgeRx lists both tirzepatide and semaglutide options with any dose up to 7.5 mg for tirzepatide and up to 1.0 mg for semaglutide as shown on the captured page, and presents those dose ranges alongside its flat pricing model.

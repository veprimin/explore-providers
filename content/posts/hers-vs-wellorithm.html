How Hers and Wellorithm differ on medication type and regulatory status

Hers offers FDA-approved, brand-name Wegovy (semaglutide) and explicitly states it does not provide compounded medications. Wellorithm offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. Both sources include a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Pricing and membership structure compared

Hers lists Wegovy starting at $149/mo and injectable options from $199/mo while noting an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required ($39 for the first month, auto-renews at $149/month thereafter) and that total cost includes the membership and medication which are billed separately. Wellorithm displays starting amounts such as $147 and advertises “Only $147 + Fast, Free Shipping” and other starting prices, but its site does not publish full pricing for every plan and states you receive a personalized quote after completing the intake quiz. Wellorithm also markets “No membership or hidden fees.”

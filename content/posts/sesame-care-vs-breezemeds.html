Sesame Care vs BreezeMeds: if you need quick clarity, Sesame Care emphasizes clinician choice and flexible subscriptions starting at $59/mo while BreezeMeds highlights injectable and oral GLP-1 options with prices starting at $199/mo. Early verdict: pick Sesame Care if you want a marketplace to choose your clinician and subscription options; pick BreezeMeds if you prioritize discreet home delivery and simple injectable or oral starting prices.

How they differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: clinician choice and marketplace model, subscription vs listed product starting prices, medication formats and pricing tiers, and shipping or delivery emphasis and billing transparency. All claims below come from the supplied source material and preserve qualifiers such as “starting at,” “as low as,” and “may.”

Clinician choice and care model

Sesame Care positions itself as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select providers by specialty, availability, and pricing, so experience can vary by clinician. BreezeMeds describes clinician-reviewed online treatment and requires a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness, but the source emphasizes intake and personalized treatment plans rather than a marketplace where you pick providers.

Pricing structure and subscription options

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Medication costs are separate and vary by insurance and pharmacy; cash-pay GLP-1 medications are shown as low as $149/mo for semaglutide and $299/mo for tirzepatide, with prices with insurance noted as low as $25 for some doses. BreezeMeds displays starting prices: semaglutide injection from $199/mo; tirzepatide injection from $299; and oral semaglutide and oral tirzepatide displayed from $399. A separate $169 figure appears on BreezeMeds without a clear product association. Fees, billing commitments, and state availability were not fully visible for BreezeMeds in the captured evidence.

Medication formats, FDA and compounding notes

Both sources include regulatory qualifiers: Sesame Care notes branded products like a Wegovy pill and Zepbound KwikPen are FDA-approved for specified indications, and that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed. BreezeMeds explicitly states that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. The supplied evidence cautions that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Delivery, shipping and ongoing support

BreezeMeds highlights discreet home delivery and ongoing support as a feature. Sesame Care emphasizes nationwide access in many states and flexible scheduling but frames its offering around clinician choice and ongoing video visits rather than discrete shipping claims. Both providers present features related to ongoing care, with BreezeMeds focusing on discreet shipping and Sesame Care on clinician network and scheduling flexibility.

Bottom line and how to choose

Choose Sesame Care if you want a marketplace model with the ability to browse and select clinicians, flexible scheduling, and subscription-level care starting at as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month commitment and separate cash-pay medication pricing as low as $149/mo.

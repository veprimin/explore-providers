Price and promotional structure

MedVi advertises a winter promotional first month of $179, including fast, free shipping, but notes the ongoing standalone month is $299 with a three-month plan at $649. Wellorithm lists a starting price of $147 in some marketing lines but does not publish full pricing; it provides personalized quotes after the intake quiz and mentions other starting points and occasional promotional offsets like “Enjoy $100 off your first month.”

Medication options: semaglutide only versus semaglutide plus tirzepatide

MedVi focuses on clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only. Wellorithm offers both compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, listing multiple product formats and starting points. This is a core difference for patients who want access to tirzepatide in addition to semaglutide.

State coverage and clinician access

MedVi operates via telehealth clinician review and does not require clinic visits, but its materials do not claim universal state clinician coverage. Wellorithm explicitly states licensed clinicians in all 50 states, which the source identifies as an advantage for people in states with limited telehealth availability.

Process, support, and virtual care

Both platforms use virtual assessments and ship compounded medication to the patient. MedVi highlights a simple online intake, no clinic visits, and 24/7 support from certified medical professionals. Wellorithm emphasizes a 2-minute quiz, virtual consultations, and ongoing virtual check-ins after titration.

Regulatory and medication clarity

Both providers use compounded medications. Both sources state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Each provider notes prescriptions are subject to clinician review and medical appropriateness.

Other notable differences

  • MedVi markets a specific promotional price and highlights 24/7 certified medical support and a fully online process with no clinic visits.
  • Wellorithm highlights broader clinician coverage across all 50 states and the availability of tirzepatide in addition to semaglutide.
  • MedVi discloses ongoing pricing increases after the first month; Wellorithm does not publish full pricing on site and issues personalized quotes after intake.

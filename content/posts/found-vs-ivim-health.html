Early verdict: Found emphasizes personalized, medically supervised plans and broad insurance acceptance starting as low as $39; IVIM Health emphasizes a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol, dual 4.9-star ratings, and a free month offer via code GIFT75.

How they differ at a glance

This comparison highlights five supported differences: pricing and insurance; personalization and program approach; third-party ratings and evidence claims; offers and trial access; and compounded medication regulatory notes. Each point compares Found and IVIM Health directly using only the supplied facts.

Pricing and insurance

Found: Offers “get started for as low as $39” with affordable monthly plans and most insurance now accepted. Found advertises handling insurance paperwork and programs like the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program that may lower brand-name costs for eligible Part D members. IVIM Health: Publishes membership pricing such as $75/mo for compounded semaglutide, $133/mo for tirzepatide, and a noted program fee; IVIM operates on a direct-pay model and does not bill insurance. Pricing difference summary: Found emphasizes lower entry pricing and insurance acceptance; IVIM uses direct-pay pricing with promotional codes and a program fee.

Program approach and personalization

Found: Describes a biology-first approach that conducts a thorough medical evaluation and builds a personalized medically supervised program tailored to individual metabolic biology, hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavioral factors. IVIM Health: Markets GLP-1 ID, a proprietary clinician-guided titration and optimization protocol that aims to be a structured, personalized approach rather than a fixed-dose regimen and claims up to 40% faster weight loss versus a cited comparator; IVIM notes that the 40% figure is its own protocol claim and not an independent clinical trial result.

Third-party ratings and evidence claims

Found: Evidence captured emphasizes its biology-first message, insurance handling, and varied pricing and programs; no third-party rating was supplied. IVIM Health: Cites a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot, described as a rare dual third-party verification and a strong satisfaction signal. IVIM also references internal and published retrospective observational studies supporting clinical outcomes but clarifies these are not randomized head-to-head trials and include both branded and compounded formulations.

Offers, trials, and onboarding

Found: Advertises affordable flexible pricing, cancel anytime language, and specific promotional offer details captured on the landing page, including discounts under certain payment and insurance conditions. IVIM Health: Promotes a no-obligation free consultation (a $75 value) for new patients and a Limited Offer free month membership via code GIFT75; IVIM notes an initial free consult and the GIFT75 free month lower the barrier to starting but states it does not bill insurance.

Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both providers prescribe or reference compounded GLP-1 medications. Found explicitly states “Compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy, and not available in all states,” and that Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility. IVIM Health also states compounded drugs are not FDA-approved, have not undergone FDA review for safety, effectiveness, or quality, and are prepared by independent licensed compounding pharmacies; IVIM notes these products carry risks and are not appropriate for everyone. Both providers preserve regulatory qualifiers and limitations in their statements.

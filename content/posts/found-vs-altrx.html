How Found and altRx differ on pricing and offers

Pricing is one of the clearest differences between Found and altRx. Found advertises getting started for as low as $39 and references multiple membership and pricing points with advertised branded GLP-1 pricing tied to insurance and 12 month commitments. altRx promotes a limited offer: compounded semaglutide starting at $89/month and compounded tirzepatide at $149/month, with multi-month prices available and the claim of being the most affordable GLP-1 program on the market.

Care model: biology-first personalization versus GLP-1 focused access

Found frames itself as a biology-first program that conducts a medical evaluation to build a personalized program around metabolic biology, hormones, blood sugar, sleep, and behavioral factors. altRx is built around delivering GLP-1 therapy affordably, focusing on 100% online visits and quick enrollment for patients ready to start GLP-1 treatment.

Insurance, membership, and billing differences

Found states that most insurance is now accepted and that it handles insurance paperwork for some offerings; advertised pricing for branded GLP-1s is associated with insurance and 12 month commitments in some cases. altRx explicitly operates with no membership, no insurance required, no hidden fees, and a direct-pay model where insurance is not needed. If insurance is a deciding factor, Found may be the option referenced for insurance handling while altRx is a transparent direct-pay alternative.

Telehealth, delivery, and availability

altRx emphasizes 100% online medical visits, a 2-minute quiz to get started, and free discreet 2-day delivery, with membership available to residents of all 50 U.S. states though availability and prescribing authority vary. Found offers telehealth supervision as part of a medically supervised program but captured evidence highlights in-person distinctions less, and notes that compounded GLP-1s are not available in all states. Both providers say prescriptions are subject to provider review and clinical eligibility.

Regulatory and medication notes

Both Found and altRx offer compounded GLP-1 medications in their programs and include regulatory notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Found also notes certain medications are FDA-approved for conditions other than weight and includes specific trademark attributions. altRx states compounded medication status and that prescriptions are filled by state-licensed compounding pharmacies in its network.

Conclusion: Found vs altRx

Found vs altRx comes down to whether you prioritize an individualized, biology-first medically supervised program that works with insurance or the lowest advertised monthly price and quick online access to compounded GLP-1s. Both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that prescriptions are subject to clinician review.

Choose Found if you want a program that emphasizes personalization, a holistic biology-first approach, and the possibility of working with insurance as part of pricing and access.

Choose altRx if your primary goal is the limited low monthly price on compounded GLP-1s, 100% online visits, and fast discrete shipping without using insurance.

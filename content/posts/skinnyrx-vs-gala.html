3. Shipping speed and delivery

4. Care model and included services

SkinnyRx emphasizes personalized care, ongoing support and licensed provider adjustments, with unlimited access to a patient care team and monthly refill forms for subscription services. Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. SkinnyRx specifically states partner pharmacies perform third-party testing every 3 to 6 months to confirm concentration within plus or minus 10% of the active ingredient for compounded lots when prescribed.

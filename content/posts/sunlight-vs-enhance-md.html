Head-to-head overview of Sunlight vs Enhance MD

Both providers offer clinician-reviewed telehealth pathways to GLP-1 medications. Key early differences: Sunlight advertises Personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo with injections and oral options and “Same price forever”; Enhance MD advertises semaglutide from $49 first month and tirzepatide from $99 first month with flat-rate pricing that remains the same at every dose. These are directly comparable claims from the providers’ landing information and FAQ material.

1. Pricing: entry cost versus ongoing certainty

Sunlight lists Personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo and emphasizes “Same price forever” and no membership. Enhance MD highlights semaglutide from $49 first month and tirzepatide from $99 first month, with flat-rate ongoing pricing that “stays the same at every dose” after the introductory period. The immediate difference is lower first-month sticker price at Enhance MD versus Sunlight’s stated starting monthly price.

2. Compounds and dosing options

Sunlight describes injections and oral options and says it offers personalized GLP-1 plans, without listing branded versus compounded details on the captured evidence. Enhance MD specifies both semaglutide and tirzepatide are available on one platform and notes compounded medications are used when offered. Enhance MD’s evidence explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality; Sunlight’s regulatory captured note advises medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

3. Pricing model and transparency

Sunlight markets “Same price forever” and “no hidden fees” on captured landing content and emphasizes no monthly membership and cancel anytime. Enhance MD promotes flat-rate pricing with the same price at every dose and points out the $49/$99 rates are first-month introductory prices while the ongoing flat-rate is higher. Enhance MD’s FAQ highlights that flat-rate pricing avoids escalation during titration; the captured evidence also notes ongoing rates and sample monthly figures for some compound and dose scenarios.

4. Shipping, membership, and service features

Sunlight’s captured copy repeatedly mentions fast, free shipping, free & discreet 2-day shipping in landing evidence, and no monthly membership. Enhance MD’s captured content emphasizes clinician-directed care, metabolic lab testing every six months in some plans, and promotional first-order savings up to $150 in its marketing language. Neither source in the captured evidence supplies exhaustive membership or cancellation mechanics beyond these statements; coverage and terms may vary by state and are subject to clinician review.

5. Track record and scale

Enhance MD documents a 10,000+ patient treatment volume in its captured material as a sign of real-world clinical experience. Sunlight’s captured content includes multiple customer testimonial excerpts and claims about affordability versus other providers but does not provide a specific patient-count figure in the captured evidence.

Comparing Sesame Care vs RxActivate: Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets you choose a clinician and shows pricing, while RxActivate requires an eligibility questionnaire and contact info before revealing plan prices. Early verdict: pick Sesame Care if you want visible pricing and clinician choice; pick RxActivate if you prefer a clinician-matched flow that reveals costs after eligibility screening.

See Sesame Care pricing and clinicians

How they differ: pricing transparency and enrollment flow

Sesame Care lists subscription and medication price information up front. It advertises ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs, and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment, and cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo. Sesame also notes that insurance is accepted for weight loss medications and that medication costs vary by insurance status and pharmacy. By contrast, RxActivate requires completion of a two-minute eligibility questionnaire and requests name, email, and phone number before revealing any plan or price; no price, medication fee, membership fee, shipping charge, cancellation term, or billing commitment was displayed before the contact-information gate.

Distinctive differences to weigh

1) Clinician choice versus clinician matching

Sesame Care emphasizes a marketplace model where patients can browse and choose providers by specialty, availability, and pricing, giving more control over the care relationship. RxActivate presents a clinician-matched GLP-1 plan delivered after screening and clinician review; the flow matches patients to clinicians after eligibility screening rather than offering open browsing.

2) Upfront pricing detail versus gated pricing

Sesame Care provides detailed examples: semaglutide cash-pay from $149 with price with insurance as low as $25 for some strengths, and tirzepatide cash-pay examples such as $299 with price with insurance as low as $25 for certain doses. Sesame states medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary. RxActivate displays no pricing before the contact-information step and explicitly requires contact details to reveal plan or price.

3) Enrollment screening and state availability

RxActivate documents a state and medical-safety screening within its two-minute questionnaire and confirmed serviceability in Alabama during a synthetic test. Sesame Care offers nationwide access in many states and a large clinician network, though experience may vary by clinician.

4) Marketing claims and outcome context

Sesame Care cites milestones based on a study of injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg in non-diabetic adults with obesity or overweight when paired with lifestyle changes and notes results may vary. RxActivate publishes membership counts but the source cautions that provider-published outcome claims are not independent evidence.

What the evidence supports about care and costs

Sesame Care supports a predictable browsing experience and publishes example cash-pay and insurance prices for semaglutide and tirzepatide strengths, while cautioning medication costs depend on insurance and pharmacy. RxActivate requires medical and state screening and delays cost disclosure until after contact information is provided, which means total cost, renewal terms, and cancellation terms are not visible in the initial flow.

Safety and regulatory notes

Both sources include regulatory reminders: medication-specific approval status must be confirmed; branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. RxActivate specifically notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Conclusion: Choose based on what matters most

Sesame Care vs RxActivate comes down to transparency and control versus a gated clinician-matched process. Choose Sesame Care if you want visible pricing examples, the ability to browse and select clinicians, flexible scheduling, and published cash-pay medication examples starting at $149 and subscription options starting at $59/mo under the stated commitment. Choose RxActivate if you prefer a clinician-matched plan and do not mind completing a two-minute eligibility questionnaire and providing contact information before learning pricing, with state and safety screening applied.

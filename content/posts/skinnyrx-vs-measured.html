How SkinnyRx vs Measured differ at a glance

Both companies offer clinician-prescribed, compounded GLP-1 options and note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Beyond that shared regulatory note, the most distinctive differences are medication format and choice, price, shipping speed, and financial tools like FSA/HSA eligibility.

Medication format and options

SkinnyRx focuses on daily oral semaglutide tablets described as an oral form of the same medication used in GLP-1 injections and taken daily under the tongue. Measured offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, giving patients an option beyond semaglutide alone. Both require an online consultation and prescriptions issued at a clinician’s discretion.

Price and promotional offers

Starting prices differ notably in the supplied evidence. SkinnyRx lists semaglutide tablets as low as $249/mo. Measured advertises promotional compounded semaglutide from $89/mo and compounded tirzepatide from $189/mo, and notes promotional pricing may change.

Shipping and decision speed

SkinnyRx advertises free overnight shipping on all orders and says patients receive shipping and tracking info within 2 business days after prescription processing. Measured offers free two-day shipping and highlights same-day prescription decisions.

Financial flexibility and support

SkinnyRx explicitly states FSA/HSA eligibility and the ability to use pre-tax dollars toward treatment, plus an option to split payments with Affirm. Measured promotes cancel anytime terms, no insurance required, unlimited messaging, and dietitian support but does not list FSA/HSA eligibility in the supplied evidence.

Safety, testing, and clinical claims

Both providers state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. SkinnyRx adds that partner pharmacies perform third-party testing through FDA- and DEA-compliant labs every 3 to 6 months to confirm concentration within plus or minus 10 percent. Measured includes an internal analysis claim about average weight loss at six months but notes internal outcome claims are not independent evidence.

Practical differences that matter day to day

If you want oral tablets and the ability to use FSA or HSA funds, SkinnyRx documents both features and also promises free overnight shipping and clinician-guided subscription care. If you want lower promotional pricing options, same-day Rx decisions, and access to tirzepatide in addition to semaglutide, Measured documents those features and emphasizes dietitian support and unlimited messaging.

The Luvo Health vs Telos Rx comparison hinges on pricing transparency and treatment formats. Early verdict: Telos Rx provides explicit starting prices (semaglutide as low as $99/month; tirzepatide as low as $139/month) while Luvo Health emphasizes ongoing support, dose adjustments, and multiple formulation options but has a starting price that is not verified.

Key difference 1: Pricing transparency and starting cost

Telos Rx publishes explicit starting prices: semaglutide as low as $99 per month and tirzepatide as low as $139 per month, with the note that a US-licensed provider must approve you. Telos also notes prices such as $299 and $399 and that final price depends on your plan. Luvo Health’s materials focus on ongoing support, dose changes, and month-by-month structure but show the starting price as not verified on the captured landing page.

See Telos Rx starting prices and plans

Key difference 2: Medication formats and dosing options

Both providers offer semaglutide and tirzepatide treatment options under clinician review. Luvo Health lists Tirzepatide Injection, Semaglutide Injection, Semaglutide Tablets, and microdosing options for both injections and tablets, and highlights dose adjustments based on clinical response. Telos Rx emphasizes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide as prescription-only options and explains the distinction that tirzepatide acts on GLP-1 and GIP while semaglutide acts on GLP-1.

Key difference 3: Clinician review and eligibility

Both services require clinician review. Telos Rx states semaglutide and tirzepatide are prescribed only after review by a US-licensed provider and that a US-licensed provider decides which medication is right. Luvo Health notes treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that dose adjustments are based on clinical response.

Key difference 4: Regulatory notes and compounded medication status

Both sources include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Luvo Health explicitly repeats that compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and are not reviewed or approved by the FDA. Telos Rx similarly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are subject to medical approval by a US-licensed provider.

Key difference 5: Cancellation and ongoing support

Luvo Health highlights ongoing support, dose changes, and a cancel anytime statement in captured materials. Telos Rx states cancel anytime with no penalties and includes a pay $0 if not approved note. Both providers advise confirming eligibility, cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

Conclusion: Luvo Health vs Telos Rx

In summary, Luvo Health emphasizes ongoing support, dose adjustments, multiple formulation options including injections and tablets and microdosing, while Telos Rx provides clearer published starting prices for compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and explicit statements about US-licensed provider approval and cancel anytime policies. Both warn that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and require clinician review.

Choose Telos Rx if you need published starting prices and clear monthly baselines such as semaglutide as low as $99/month or tirzepatide as low as $139/month, subject to clinician approval and plan-dependent pricing.

Choose Luvo Health if you prioritize ongoing support, dose adjustments based on clinical response, and a wider set of listed dosing formats including injections, tablets, and microdosing, while confirming starting price and availability first.

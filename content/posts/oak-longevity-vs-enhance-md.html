Ongoing cost structure and dose pricing

Oak markets one flat price that covers all dosages, meaning Oak presents a single price point per compound rather than tiered pricing. Clinical intake, shipping, and support

Oak describes an online process with no video call required and an asynchronous clinician review by board certified physicians in your state. Enhance MD is described as a licensed telehealth platform with clinician directed compounds and notes metabolic lab testing every six months as part of its program.

Regulatory and eligibility notes

Both providers offer compounded medications. Oak explicitly states compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Enhance MD likewise notes compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Oak also notes patients with BMI below 20 do not qualify for GLP 1 treatment and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

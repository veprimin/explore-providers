Medication types and regulatory notes

Shed is explicit that it offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded alternatives on one platform, giving patients the option to choose based on budget and preference. altRx is presented as a compounded program with semaglutide and tirzepatide offered in compounded form as part of a limited offer. Coaching, care model, and clinical review

Shed integrates continuous coaching into treatment, describing coaching as part of the program rather than a separate add-on, and uses a 2-minute approval assessment before clinician review. Shed positions coaching as a differentiator for patients who want behavioral support alongside medication. altRx provides clinician-guided support and an assigned care team; its model highlights 100% online medical visits, ongoing care team support throughout membership, and a 2-minute quiz to start.

Availability, delivery, and commitment

altRx states free and discreet 2-day delivery, availability to residents of all 50 U.S. states with provider licensing and prescribing authority that vary by state, no membership or hidden fees, and no commitment with cancel anytime. Shed notes that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate; shipping and membership details are provided after intake and a personalized plan. Shed offers a membership option and refers readers to Terms and Conditions for full details on the weight-loss guarantee.

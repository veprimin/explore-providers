Approach to care: biology-first versus clinician-guided

Found positions itself as biology-first, emphasizing a comprehensive medical evaluation that builds a program around hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavioral factors. Omzo describes clinician-guided plans where licensed U.S. providers review care and make prescribing decisions. Both use clinical review, but Found highlights a broader metabolic focus while Omzo emphasizes the licensed clinician decision process.

Pricing and payment differences

Pricing is a clear differentiator in the evidence. Found advertises getting started for as low as $39 and calls out affordable monthly plans and flexible pricing. Omzo lists semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279. Found also details multiple pricing notes including membership tiers and offers tied to insurance and Medicare programs. Omzo includes payment within its fees for compounded medication orders and notes product costs are included in fees collected by Omzo.

Medication sourcing, compounded status, and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications and include regulatory cautions. Found explicitly states compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy and are not available in all states. Omzo states its compounded medications are prepared by state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacies and are not FDA-approved, and notes branded prescriptions, if issued, must be filled by patients at their chosen pharmacy. Neither source guarantees results and both preserve that individual results may vary.

Insurance, shipping, and logistics

Found states most insurance is now accepted, that it handles insurance paperwork, and describes specific Medicare-related programs and pricing options, while noting coverage, pricing and terms vary and Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility. Omzo notes FSA and HSA eligibility and that medication costs are included in the fees Omzo collects and that shipping and pharmacy capacity can affect rates. Omzo also notes it does not sell or ship branded medications.

Making the choice: Found vs Omzo

In short, Found positions itself for people seeking a biology-first, medically supervised program with low entry pricing and insurance handling, while Omzo is a clinician-guided platform focused on compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with higher starting prices but clinician-led prescribing and pharmacy coordination. Both note compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that final prescriptions are subject to provider review and clinical eligibility.

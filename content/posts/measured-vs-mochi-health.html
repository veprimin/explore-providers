How they differ at a glance

Measured and Mochi Health both offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, but they differ on pricing structure, visit style, fulfillment speed, support features, and transparency about membership. Below are the most distinctive supported differences between Measured and Mochi Health.

1. Pricing structure and promotions

Measured advertises promotional compounded semaglutide from $89/mo and compounded tirzepatide $189/mo. Mochi Health uses a membership-plus-medication model with pricing from $99/month and a 50% Off Your 1st Month promotion via code BESTWLHALFOFF. Mochi also lists compounded semaglutide starting $99 $60 and compounded tirzepatide starting at $199 $90 in promotional examples. Measured presents flat promotional monthly price points; Mochi presents membership plus medication where total monthly cost varies by plan and medication.

2. Clinical visit model and speed of prescribing

Measured emphasizes same-day Rx decisions and unlimited provider messaging with dietitian support. Mochi Health distinguishes itself with live video visits bookable in about 5 minutes, which the source frames as a synchronous physician visit rather than an asynchronous intake review. Both platforms state prescriptions are issued at a provider’s discretion; Measured says Rx decisions can be same day, Mochi says licensed physicians evaluate cases during live video consultations.

3. Shipping, fulfillment, and logistics

Measured advertises free two-day shipping and “fast & free shipping on every order.” Mochi notes doctor-prescribed treatments shipped to your door and highlights promotional pricing, but the source does not claim a guaranteed two-day shipping window. Both offer shipped medications, but only Measured explicitly advertises free two-day shipping.

4. Support and ongoing care

Measured highlights unlimited messaging, 24/7 Care Team, and dietitian support. Mochi Health highlights 24/7 support and ongoing appointment availability throughout the program and positions live video visits as enabling real-time clinician interaction. Both emphasize continuing care, but Mochi frames ongoing appointments and 24/7 support within a membership model while Measured pairs dietitian support with unlimited messaging.

5. Regulatory and product notes

Both sources state compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Measured repeats: Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Mochi Health includes a regulatory note: Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Each also notes prescriptions are issued at clinician discretion.

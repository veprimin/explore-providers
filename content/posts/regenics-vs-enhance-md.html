Regenics vs Enhance MD: headline differences at a glance

This comparison focuses on five distinctive differences supported by source evidence: first-month pricing, ongoing pricing model, compounded medication status, clinician model and clinical volume, and additional offerings such as Sermorelin or testing. All statements below preserve source qualifiers like “starting at,” “first month,” and “compounded.”

1) First-month pricing and entry cost

Enhance MD advertises semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month, described as “the cheapest first month for both compounds.” Regenics lists semaglutide $139 first month and tirzepatide $189 first month. If lowest possible first-month price is the priority, Enhance MD is materially cheaper based on the supplied rates.

2) Ongoing pricing and escalation

Enhance MD emphasizes flat-rate pricing: “the same price at every dose, with no fee escalation during titration.” The source notes ongoing flat-rate amounts that are higher than introductory rates. Regenics shows promotional first-month pricing that “renews” at higher monthly rates (semaglutide renews at $199/month and tirzepatide at $279/month according to the provider FAQ), so Regenics starts mid-pack and increases on renewal.

3) Compounded medications and regulatory note

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. The sources explicitly state that “compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.” Regenics highlights that medications are prepared by U.S. licensed compounding pharmacies and shipped with free shipping. Enhance MD also uses compounded formulations and notes the same regulatory qualifier.

4) Clinician model and platform scale

Regenics is described as nurse-practitioner-led care where “licensed nurse practitioners oversee every prescription” and prescriptions are available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Enhance MD is a clinician-directed telehealth platform and cites a larger real-world volume, stating “10,000+ patients treated.” Both require clinician review before treatment.

5) Additional offerings and features

Regenics explicitly offers a Sermorelin peptide therapy as an add-on and notes a 15mg injectable peptide priced at $399 in the FAQ. Enhance MD emphasizes additional program features like metabolic lab testing every six months in some published pricing lines and a unified dual-compound offering (both semaglutide and tirzepatide) with flat-rate pricing. Availability of branded products was not supplied; both sources specify compounded products only.

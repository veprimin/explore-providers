Quick verdict: Start Willow vs Eden Health

Start Willow focuses on direct-pay compounded semaglutide with a fast delivery promise and no insurance or membership required; Eden Health advertises lower monthly pricing for semaglutide and a lifetime price guarantee with free expedited shipping. If fast compounded access and a direct-pay model matter, Start Willow stands out. If lower published monthly pricing and a lifetime price guarantee matter, Eden Health stands out.

How these services differ at a glance

This comparison uses only captured claims from each provider. Start Willow emphasizes compounded semaglutide prescribed after an online clinician review, with pricing starting at $249 for the first month and $299/month ongoing and a delivery guarantee of 3 days or less. Eden Health presents monthly pricing for semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129, with a lifetime price guarantee and free expedited shipping. Both say no insurance is required and both state there are no membership fees.

Core difference 1: Pricing model and listed rates

Start Willow: From $249 per Month with a first-month price noted at $249 and $299/month ongoing. The source notes the ongoing $299/month rate is on the higher end compared with other platforms. Eden Health: Monthly pricing at every dose, with semaglutide listed at $99 and tirzepatide at $129 and a stated lifetime price guarantee. These are the captured advertised monthly prices; users should confirm eligibility and terms directly with each provider, as Eden notes pricing and eligibility may change.

Core difference 2: Compounded medication and regulatory notes

Start Willow offers compounded semaglutide described as a customized treatment prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies and explicitly notes that compounded semaglutide is not FDA-approved and is not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Eden Health’s capture notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Both sources require a licensed clinician to determine appropriateness where applicable.

Core difference 3: Delivery and shipping commitments

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less and captures statements about same-day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping for in-stock items. Eden Health advertises free expedited shipping and frames its offering as medication, care, and support together under one monthly price. The explicit speed guarantee of 3 days or less is a specific Start Willow claim in the captured evidence; Eden emphasizes expedited shipping without a specific days-guarantee in the captured content.

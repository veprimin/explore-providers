Quick verdict: TrimRx is the lower starting-price option with Lifetime Price Protection and very fast 1-2 day shipping for its compounded programs, while HermanRx offers all-inclusive monthly pricing, explicit 24-72 hour timelines and the convenience of compounded GLP-1 with B12 across all 50 states. This article compares TrimRx vs HermanRx on the most distinctive differences so you can choose based on what matters to you.

Explore TrimRx plans

Price structure and what you pay each month

TrimRx advertises weight-loss plans starting at $149/month for compounded GLP-1 and $249/month for GLP-1+GIP, with Lifetime Price Protection that locks in today’s price for life and claims no hidden fees. HermanRx lists compounded semaglutide + B12 at $249/month and tirzepatide + B12 at $349/month as genuinely all-inclusive monthly prices with no membership or consultation fee.

Medications offered and program design

Both providers sell compounded GLP-1 medications, but TrimRx highlights both compounded GLP-1 (semaglutide) and compounded GLP-1+GIP (tirzepatide dual agonist) options. HermanRx lists compounded semaglutide with B12 and compounded tirzepatide with B12. TrimRx also notes an alternative pathway where a provider may write a prescription for FDA-approved branded medications that the patient fills separately; HermanRx states it may offer both FDA-approved and compounded medications depending on the provider’s recommendation.

Direct difference

TrimRx emphasizes two product lines (GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP) with starting prices and Lifetime Price Protection. HermanRx emphasizes bundled semaglutide or tirzepatide with B12 at set all-inclusive monthly rates.

Shipping speed, service timelines, and coverage

TrimRx advertises free 1–2 day shipping for its compounded program. HermanRx publishes a roughly 24 hour provider review and about 72 hour delivery if prescribed, and also gives a wider 3–7 business day window in FAQ material; HermanRx delivers in all 50 states with free discreet shipping.

Clinical process, support, and scope

HermanRx matches patients with U.S. licensed, board-certified physicians across multiple treatment categories and advertises a ~5-minute assessment, ~24 hour provider review and ongoing support via a patient portal. TrimRx operates a telehealth model that provides prescriptions and telemed visits included in price and highlights 24/7 support and a large customer base of 100,000+ happy customers and a 4.8 Excellent Rating.

Regulatory notes and trust signals

Both providers explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. TrimRx emphasizes scale with 100k+ customers and a 4.8 rating. HermanRx highlights U.S. licensed, board-certified physicians, availability in all 50 states and clearly stated timelines.

How to choose between TrimRx vs HermanRx

TrimRx may be the clearer choice if your priority is a lower starting price and the promise of Lifetime Price Protection plus very fast 1–2 day shipping and a large user base.

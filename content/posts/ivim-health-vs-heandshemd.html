Head-to-head summary: ratings, support, price structure

Comparing IVIM Health vs He & She MD directly, three key differences stand out from the supplied evidence: third-party ratings and trust signals, the scope of bundled coaching and clinician access, and how pricing and promotional terms are structured and presented.

1. Third-party ratings and trust signals

IVIM Health is described as having an unusually strong third-party signal: a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot. The source frames that dual 4.9-star rating as a rare verification in the market. He & She MD does not have a comparable dual third-party rating cited in the provided evidence; its selling points focus on app-based coaching and clinician messaging instead.

2. Coaching, clinician access, and program support

He & She MD bundles a coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific guidance and progress tracking at no extra cost. The source says every plan includes those app features and free expedited shipping and that the app and messaging are built into the plan rather than sold separately. IVIM Health emphasizes a clinician-guided proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol and structured titration, but the supplied evidence does not describe an equivalent built-in app with 1:1 messaging.

3. Pricing format and promotional terms

IVIM Health lists specific monthly medication prices and a program fee: examples include Semaglutide (compounded) at $75/mo, Tirzepatide (compounded) at $133/mo, plus a $74.99/mo program fee; IVIM also advertises a no-obligation free consultation worth $75 and a free month membership code GIFT75. The FAQ notes IVIM starts at $99 first month and $174/month ongoing in one place, and elsewhere the landing content lists the lower formulation prices noted above; both pricing references are included in the supplied material. He & She MD presents six-month introductory rates: semaglutide injections at $139/month introductory (regularly $199/month after six months) and tirzepatide at $199/month intro (regularly $289/month after six months). The He & She MD source stresses that headline prices are six-month promos that renew higher.

Outcomes and evidence claims

IVIM Health cites internal and published-observational data: the source reports IVIM patients following the Ivim Protocol lost an average of 46% more weight over 68 weeks when compared to another published clinical GLP-1 trial, and IVIM claims its GLP-1 ID protocol can accelerate weight loss outcomes by up to 40%. The source explicitly qualifies these as not head-to-head trials and notes results may vary. He & She MD references self-reported retrospective figures and program messaging about weight loss but the supplied evidence calls those self-reported and not trial data. Neither provider claim is described as a randomized head-to-head clinical trial in the supplied material.

Regulatory and safety notes

Both sources include regulatory cautions: the IVIM material repeats that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. He & She MD also states that medications included are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy or quality. Each source says prescriptions are subject to clinician review and not all customers will qualify.

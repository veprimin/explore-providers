How they differ at a glance

This comparison uses only supplied facts to contrast JoinFridays and JoinSkinny across pricing, medication sourcing, shipping and fulfillment, membership and cancellation, and clinical support.

1. Pricing structure and starting rates

JoinFridays offers compounded GLP-1 options starting at $117/month and notes multi-month plans lower the effective rate. The platform lists a standalone one-month plan at $249 and other term examples such as three months for $596, six months for $1,050, and twelve months for $1,800. JoinSkinny advertises weight loss plans starting at $149 with a stated $140 promotional discount available and flat-rate pricing so your monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases. JoinSkinny also offers monthly and multi-month bundles and installment options with Afterpay, Klarna, and Affirm.

2. Medication type and pharmacy fulfillment

JoinFridays provides compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide options and also lists name brand semaglutide and tirzepatide prices separately on its landing evidence, while noting compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny works with licensed U.S. pharmacies that fill branded and compounded GLP-1 and GLP-1 + GIP preparations, states prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners, and notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

3. Shipping speed and temperature control

JoinSkinny advertises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time and next-day UPS shipping in a temperature controlled package. JoinFridays landing evidence does not supply a specific shipping speed or temperature-controlled shipping claim in the source provided. Both providers note pharmacy partners or compounded products have regulatory qualifiers.

4. Membership, cancellation, and commitments

JoinFridays emphasizes no membership fees, cancel anytime with no long-term contract, and a Fridays membership included free in some listings. JoinSkinny also states no monthly membership and that you can cancel before the next billing cycle, with support available 24/7 to pause or adjust plans. JoinFridays lists multi-month plans that reduce effective monthly cost, and JoinSkinny lists monthly and bundle commitments that require confirmation of total charge and eligibility.

5. Clinical model and ongoing support

JoinFridays describes personalized, clinician-guided care and says treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. JoinSkinny describes a licensed provider review after a health questionnaire, monthly check-ins, dose adjustments, ongoing support through telemedicine and 24/7 chat, and that all care is delivered by licensed U.S. providers with HIPAA protections. Both providers present clinician review and ongoing guidance as part of their programs in the supplied evidence.

Bottom line and final recommendation

JoinFridays vs JoinSkinny narrows to priorities. JoinFridays is centered on the lowest starting price in its tier at $117/month, aggressive promo codes, and flexible cancellation with compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide options. JoinSkinny is oriented toward flat-rate pricing stability, branded pharmacy fulfillment and free temperature-controlled 1 to 2 day shipping, with licensed U.S. pharmacies and 24/7 support. Both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Choose JoinFridays if you value the lowest advertised starting price, access to compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide on one platform, and a cancel-anytime structure.

Choose JoinSkinny if you prioritize flat-rate pricing, fast temperature-controlled shipping and pharmacy fulfillment through U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified partners.

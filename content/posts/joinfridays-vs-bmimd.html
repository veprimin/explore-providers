JoinFridays vs bmiMD: what they offer and how they differ

The primary difference between JoinFridays vs bmiMD is in medication variety, pricing structures, and commitment terms. Both prescribe compounded GLP-1 medications subject to clinician review and both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. JoinFridays highlights compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide starting at $117/month with no long-term contract; bmiMD focuses on compounded tirzepatide with multi-month discounts starting at $139/month and free shipping.

Distinctive difference 1: Medication selection

JoinFridays provides both compounded tirzepatide and compounded semaglutide options on one platform, while bmiMD captured evidence shows a focus on compounded tirzepatide. JoinFridays markets tirzepatide as “Most Effective” and semaglutide as “Most Affordable”. bmiMD emphasizes compounded tirzepatide only in its captured material.

Distinctive difference 2: Pricing and billing options

JoinFridays lists a starting price of $117/month and shows multi-month plans that reduce effective monthly cost, with examples like a 12-month average of $150/month and a standalone one-month plan at $249. bmiMD shows tiered monthly equivalents for tirzepatide: $179 month-to-month, $159 for three months, $149 for six months, and $139 for a 12-month plan. bmiMD therefore places its lowest rate behind a 12-month commitment.

Distinctive difference 3: Commitment, cancellation and shipping

JoinFridays explicitly advertises no long-term contracts and the ability to cancel anytime with no membership fees. bmiMD highlights free subscription shipping and multiple billing options but notes the lowest price requires a 12-month commitment and that total prepaid charge should be confirmed. Both providers state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Distinctive difference 4: Clinician access and program features

JoinFridays promotes personalized, clinician-guided care and membership included free as part of medication plus coaching and care. bmiMD documents 24/7 clinician access and online refills and delivery. Each platform frames clinician review as required; bmiMD also presents a 100 percent refundable policy if not eligible for the program in captured material.

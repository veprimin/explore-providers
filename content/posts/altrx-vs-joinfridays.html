How these platforms differ: pricing, care model, and extras

When comparing altRx vs JoinFridays, the most distinctive differences are price and offers, medication options and labeling, care and membership terms, and payment and delivery features. Both provide compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide pathways and both note that compounded medications are not FDA approved. The specific pricing, promotional approach, and service framing are where they diverge.

Pricing and promotional approach

altRx positions itself as the most affordable GLP-1 program with a limited offer: compounded semaglutide starting at $89/month and tirzepatide at $149/month. It advertises same price every month and multi-month plan discounts. JoinFridays lists a starting price of $117/month, notes active promo codes (for example NEWYOU12 and NYNY12) and presents both one-month and multi-month pricing structures that reduce the effective monthly rate over time.

Medication options and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide options. altRx explicitly markets compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide with prescriptions filled by state licensed 503A compounding pharmacies in its network and notes that treatment is subject to clinician review. JoinFridays also offers compounded options and additionally advertises availability of brand name medications on higher priced plans while stating compounded medications are not FDA approved. Both include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA reviewed for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Care model, membership and cancellation

altRx emphasizes 100% online medical visits, a 2-minute quiz to get started, no membership, no insurance required, and cancel anytime. It highlights ongoing care team support and clinician oversight as part of the monthly fee. JoinFridays emphasizes personalized clinician-guided care, a Fridays membership included on some plans, no long-term contracts, and the ability to cancel anytime. JoinFridays frames itself as offering more personalized dosing guidance rather than a templated protocol.

Payments, shipping, and extras

altRx advertises buy now pay later options through Affirm, Klarna, and Afterpay, free and discreet 2-day delivery, and an all inclusive monthly fee that covers medical oversight and medication. JoinFridays highlights active promo codes and membership benefits bundled into programs, and presents multiple plan lengths where longer commitments lower the effective monthly rate. JoinFridays also notes that actual product appearance may differ from website images and that promo code availability can change.

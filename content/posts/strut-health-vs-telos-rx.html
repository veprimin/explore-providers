Prescribing, eligibility and regulatory qualifiers

Both sources emphasize clinician review and regulatory realities. Strut Health states compounded medication is prescribed based on medical necessity and that medical necessity is required by a licensed physician. Telos Rx states semaglutide and tirzepatide are compounded medications, prescription-only, and prescribed only after review by a US-licensed provider, adding that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Both note compounded medications are not FDA-approved; Telos Rx repeats that compounded products are not reviewed by FDA for safety or effectiveness and that individual results vary.

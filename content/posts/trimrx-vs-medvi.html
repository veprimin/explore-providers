2. Medication options and dosing

TrimRx supports compounded GLP-1 injections starting at $149/month and compounded GLP-1+GIP injections starting at $249/month, which indicates both semaglutide and a tirzepatide dual agonist option. 3. Shipping and logistics

TrimRx advertises free 1–2 day shipping and states medication arrives in 1–2 days. MedVi advertises fast, free shipping for the promotional month; landing copy repeatedly references free shipping for the program. Both providers operate a direct-pay telehealth model and do not require insurance for the compounded medication programs.

4. Clinical process and support

TrimRx provides telehealth visits included with the program, 24/7 support, and claims of 100k+ customers with a 4.8 rating. MedVi provides a simple online intake with no clinic visits needed, an online clinician review, and 24/7 support from certified medical professionals. MedVi also notes that OpenLoop Health clinicians review eligibility and retain prescription discretion after checkout.

5. Regulatory and quality notes

Both providers include regulatory language: TrimRx states its compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy. MedVi states compounded GLP-1s are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety, efficacy, or quality. Each provider recommends clinician review and notes results vary and are not guaranteed.

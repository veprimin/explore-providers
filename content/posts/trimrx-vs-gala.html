TrimRx vs Gala: what differs most

The evidence shows three primary differences: starting price and pricing model, how each describes medications and regulatory status, and shipping and included care. Both are direct-pay telehealth platforms offering compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options, but the supplied materials emphasize different selling points and disclosures.

1. Pricing approach and starting price

TrimRx advertises weight-loss plans starting at $149/month and a promotional $140 discount. TrimRx also promotes Lifetime Price Protection that locks in today’s price for life and claims no hidden fees and no membership. Gala advertises one flat all-inclusive price with a $220 promotion visible on the landing page, but the base dollar price was not exposed in readable page text. Gala states “one flat price at every dose” and “no hidden fees.”

2. Medication type and regulatory note

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options. TrimRx explicitly describes a primary compounded medication program prepared by state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacies and states that compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. Gala likewise includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. TrimRx further notes it may alternatively write prescriptions for branded FDA-approved medications, but TrimRx does not sell, dispense, or ship branded drugs; those prescriptions must be filled separately by the patient. Gala materials do not supply comparable branded-prescription details in the captured evidence.

3. Clinical visit and care features

Both platforms include telehealth/provider visits as part of the service. TrimRx says prescription and telemed visits are included. Gala states provider visit and prescription are included and highlights provider check-ins and dose monitoring. TrimRx highlights 24/7 support in its descriptions; Gala emphasizes provider check-ins and ongoing care in its marketing claims. Treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate, per Gala’s captured copy.

4. Shipping speed and fulfillment claims

TrimRx advertises free 1–2 day shipping. Gala advertises free 2-day shipping. TrimRx also emphasizes fast, discreet packaging in testimonials captured on the landing page copy. Gala’s captured copy repeats that shipping was fast and discreet in testimonial-style lines.

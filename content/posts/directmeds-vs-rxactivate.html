Key differences at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences between Directmeds and RxActivate: pricing transparency and gating, compounded medication formats and regulatory notes, shipping and included services, membership and cancellation terms, and state availability or lead-capture requirements. Each point below compares both providers using only supplied facts.

1. Pricing transparency and gating

Directmeds publishes a promotional starting price of $147 and FAQ standard prices of $297/mo for semaglutide and $399/mo for tirzepatide, with a noted summer promotion that “saves $150.” RxActivate does not display prices before a user completes an eligibility questionnaire and provides name, email and phone number; no price, medication fee, membership fee, shipping charge, cancellation term or billing commitment was shown before the contact-information gate.

2. Medication format, compounding and regulatory notes

Directmeds offers clinician-reviewed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats shipped from U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies and explicitly states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. RxActivate describes clinician-matched GLP-1 plans after eligibility screening and notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed; the source also states that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

3. Shipping, included services, and refunds

Directmeds advertises medication, clinician review, telehealth visits, supplies and free next-day UPS/FedEx shipping included, plus a full refund if a prescription is not approved. RxActivate’s public flow did not disclose shipping, medication, membership or cancellation terms before lead capture, so those details are not supplied.

4. Membership, cancellation and enrollment terms

Directmeds states no separate monthly membership fee, no hidden fees, month-to-month cancellation for month-to-month customers and that subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term. RxActivate discloses no membership fee or cancellation terms before the contact-information step in the captured evidence.

5. State availability and eligibility flow

Directmeds notes unavailability in Mississippi and Louisiana. RxActivate’s captured flow confirmed Alabama serviceability in a synthetic test and requires state and medical-safety screening as part of a two-minute eligibility questionnaire before showing plans.

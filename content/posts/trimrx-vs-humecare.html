Head-to-head: price, protocol, and support for TrimRx vs HumeCare+

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences between TrimRx and HumeCare+: starting price and pricing model; whether the program bundles supportive ingredients and tracking; shipping and logistics; clinical claims and guarantees; and how each treats compounded versus branded medications. All statements below reflect only the supplied provider material and its qualifiers.

1. Pricing and pricing model

TrimRx lists weight-loss plans starting at $149/month for compounded GLP-1 and $249/month for GLP-1+GIP, with a Lifetime Price Protection feature that locks in today’s price for life, and a promotional $140 discount noted. HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/month and a tirzepatide protocol from $299/month, with pricing presented as promotional and cash-pay only.

2. What is included in the protocol

TrimRx includes prescription and telehealth visits, says medication and telemed visits are included, and highlights free 1-2 day shipping and 24/7 support. TrimRx’s primary program provides compounded medications, and it notes that branded medications prescribed would need to be filled separately by the patient.

HumeCare+ sells a bundled protocol: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide plus glycine and vitamins B6 and B12 built into the formulation, a body-composition scan, an app, and ongoing clinician optimization. HumeCare+ frames these additions as muscle-preserving or side-effect mitigation supports.

View HumeCare+ protocol and tracking features

3. Shipping, access, and cancellations

Both providers promote free fast shipping. TrimRx advertises free 1-2 day shipping, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime. HumeCare+ advertises free next-day delivery, 24/7 clinician access, and cancel subscription anytime. Both operate on a cash-pay model and note that compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated products.

4. Clinical claims, outcomes, and guarantees

TrimRx presents scale and satisfaction signals: 100k+ customers and a 4.8 Excellent Rating, and describes its program as designed to help patients lose up to 20% of body weight with an average outcome claim included in its materials. TrimRx explicitly states its compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and that branded prescriptions would be filled separately.

HumeCare+ reports internal program results and offers a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months, with the provider urging readers to verify terms for eligibility and documentation. HumeCare+ also presents internal headline figures and emphasizes tracking lean mass via body-composition scans rather than weight alone.

Conclusion: TrimRx vs HumeCare+

TrimRx vs HumeCare+ comes down to tradeoffs in price and program design. TrimRx is the lower-cost, scale-focused option with Lifetime Price Protection, free 1-2 day shipping, and telehealth visits included. HumeCare+ is pricier but bundles glycine and B vitamins into its compounded protocol, offers monthly body-composition scans, an app, and a 10%-or-money-back promise over six months. Compounded medications from both providers are not FDA-evaluated; check eligibility, terms, and state availability directly.

Choose TrimRx if you want a lower starting price, Lifetime Price Protection, and fast shipping. Choose HumeCare+ if you want built-in supportive ingredients, body-composition tracking, and the money-back promise.

Formats and treatment options

Both platforms offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, but Try Ageless sells three formats: semaglutide injection, tirzepatide injection, and an uncommon needle-free tirzepatide oral drops format. Bodybuilding Health+ highlights GLP-1 options alongside NAD+, sermorelin, and sexual performance programs on one platform.

Commercial model and discounts

Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes no hidden fees, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime flexibility while offering single-month to 12-month bundles for GLP-1s. Try Ageless advertises the discount built into every plan and the additional SLIM50 code for an extra $50 off, plus free discreet shipping and B12 included in every plan.

Clinical oversight and regulatory notes

Bodybuilding Health+ states licensed U.S. providers review medical intake and a named physician, and notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Try Ageless likewise dispenses compounded products prepared by state-licensed 503A pharmacies and notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved; it adds that the oral tirzepatide drops are an off-label compounded format because tirzepatide is not FDA-approved in oral form.

Program scope and customer features

Bodybuilding Health+ markets a multi-program telehealth platform spanning weight management, longevity, and sexual performance with medical oversight and shipping to your door. Try Ageless focuses on GLP-1 formats, unlimited messaging, format switching under one plan with no new consult fee, and B12 bundled into every plan.

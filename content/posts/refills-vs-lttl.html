Refills vs LTTL compares two telehealth providers that offer compounded GLP-1 options. Quick verdict: Refills emphasizes flat pricing from $99/month and 24/7 concierge support, while LTTL highlights pharmacy verification and an offer of up to $140 off your first month.

Start Refills from $99/mo

How price and pricing model differ

Price is the clearest difference. Refills lists GLP-1 from $99/month (~$5/day) and promotes flat pricing, the same price at every dosage, with specific examples including semaglutide at $99/mo and tirzepatide at $159/mo. LTTL shows higher listed figures and an enrollment offer: up to $140 off your first month with a code auto-applied and example prices such as $249 and $129 noted on its materials. LTTL also uses phrasing like same price, every dose. Both mention compounded medication status; neither claims FDA evaluation.

Pharmacy quality, testing, and verification

Refills states its products are from U.S. compounding pharmacies that are Legitscript-certified with third-party USP testing, and notes that compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated by the FDA. LTTL states its medications are compounded by U.S.A. licensed pharmacies, prepared in state-licensed pharmacies, and independently verified in FDA registered labs, with confirmation every 3 to 6 months to stay within ± 10% of target strength. Both highlight compounded status and include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Support, shipping, and membership terms

Refills advertises free discreet shipping, express shipping, 24/7 health concierge support, no membership or app fees, month-to-month plans, and cancellation anytime with no cancellation fees. It describes a process: complete a health intake quiz, a licensed clinician will review information, you may chat with a doctor or receive a prescription within 24 hours, medication processed and shipped with tracking in 2 business days, and a 4-week supply each month with unlimited access to patient care team and clinicians at no extra cost. LTTL notes delivery to your door when needed but current details on membership, renewal terms, or exact shipping cadence were not published or could not be verified on the captured landing page. LTTL emphasizes confirming eligibility, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

Offers and enrollment differences

Refills highlights a promotional new patient special noted as 40% off in some landing-page evidence. LTTL repeatedly lists up to $140 off your first month with a code auto-applied. LTTL’s materials caution that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change and recommend confirming details. Refills says there are no monthly membership or app fees and that your plan is month-to-month, allowing cancellation anytime.

Bottom line and how to choose

In a straight comparison of Refills vs LTTL, Refills is positioned around lower listed starting cost, flat pricing at every dose, free discreet shipping, and 24/7 concierge support with month-to-month terms. LTTL is positioned around pharmacy verification in FDA registered labs and a promotional offer of up to $140 off the first month while advising customers to confirm eligibility, pricing, renewal terms and state availability.

Choose Refills if you prioritize lower advertised starting price, flat pricing across dosages, free discreet shipping, and 24/7 patient support. Choose LTTL if pharmacy verification in FDA registered labs and an introductory credit of up to $140 off your first month are your primary priorities, and you will confirm eligibility and renewal terms before enrolling.

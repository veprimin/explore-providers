Found vs Shed boils down to two different approaches: Found emphasizes a biology-first, personalized medically supervised program with affordable monthly plans starting at $39, while Shed blends name-brand and compounded GLP-1 options with continuous coaching and compounded plans starting at $199. Early verdict: choose Found if you want a biology-tailored, insurance-friendly entry point; choose Shed if you want built-in coaching and the option of FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1s.

How pricing and entry costs compare

Found promotes affordable monthly plans with the ability to get started for as low as $39 and notes additional pricing details such as Found membership starting at $49/mo when billed annually and other plan tiers. Shed lists compounded semaglutide injections starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month, plus a limited time offer: 10% off your first month.

Medication options: compounded, brand, and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1s, and both include regulatory disclaimers that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Found lists compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and mentions branded GLP-1s such as Wegovy and Zepbound in pricing contexts, with specific statements that certain medications are FDA-approved for conditions other than weight and that Found is not affiliated with Novo Nordisk or Eli Lilly. Shed offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 options and compounded alternatives and explicitly notes that compounded medications are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a provider’s prescription.

Care model: biology-first personalization vs integrated coaching

Found emphasizes a biology-first approach: a thorough medical evaluation and a personalized program that targets hormones, metabolism, sleep, blood sugar regulation, and behavior. Shed differentiates by pairing medication with continuous coaching integrated into treatment rather than issuing a script alone. These core distinctions affect who each provider may suit: Found centers on individualized medical assessment, while Shed pairs medication choice with behavioral support.

Conclusion: Found vs Shed — which to pick

Found vs Shed centers on priorities: Found focuses on a biology-first, medically supervised, affordability-oriented entry with insurance handling and plans starting as low as $39. Shed focuses on offering both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1s and compounded options plus continuous coaching, with compounded plans starting at $199 and a 10% first-month offer.

Choose Found if you want a biology-tailored program and lower-cost entry that accepts most insurance and emphasizes a medically supervised evaluation. Choose Shed if you want the option of FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1s plus integrated coaching and a quick intake assessment.

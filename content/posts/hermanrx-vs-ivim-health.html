HermanRx vs IVIM Health: both offer clinician-prescribed compounded GLP-1 options, but they differ sharply on pricing structure, timeline transparency, and third-party reviews. Early verdict: choose HermanRx for straightforward all-inclusive monthly billing and fast published timelines, and choose IVIM Health if third-party ratings and a clinician-led protocol are your priority.

How pricing and fees compare

HermanRx publishes clear all-inclusive monthly pricing: compounded semaglutide with B12 at $249 per month and compounded tirzepatide with B12 at $349 per month. That price is presented as covering provider review, prescription, medication, ongoing support and free discreet shipping with no separate membership or consultation fee. IVIM Health presents lower entry pricing but a different fee structure: published entry examples include $75 per month for semaglutide and $133 per month for tirzepatide, and the site references an additional $74.99 monthly program fee and a promotional free month with code GIFT75. IVIM also advertises a $99 first month and $174 ongoing in some FAQ text. Use the pricing details that match your preferred billing model.

Three distinctive differences to weigh

1. Billing model and transparency

HermanRx emphasizes an all-inclusive cash-pay model with no membership fee and no separate consultation charge. IVIM Health highlights promotional entry points such as a free month with code GIFT75 and a no-obligation free consultation valued at $75, but its ongoing pricing is presented with additional program fees and a higher ongoing rate in some materials.

2. Published timelines and delivery

HermanRx lists a roughly 5-minute assessment, about 24 hour provider review and roughly 72 hour delivery when prescribed, with a wider 3 to 7 business day window for some orders. IVIM Health does not publish the same specific timeline in the supplied evidence but emphasizes clinician-guided weekly adjustments under its GLP-1 ID protocol. If rapid, published fulfillment timelines matter to you, HermanRx provides more specific timing claims.

3. Third party validation and protocol claims

IVIM Health highlights unusually strong third-party validation, claiming 4.9-star ratings on both Google and Trustpilot and citing internal and published observational studies that support its GLP-1 ID protocol. IVIM states its protocol can accelerate weight loss by up to 40 percent in its materials, noting that this is a proprietary claim and not a head-to-head trial. HermanRx does not present dual third-party star ratings in the supplied evidence but stresses platform breadth across five treatment categories and consolidation benefits.

Clinical pathway and program style

Both platforms operate on a cash-pay telehealth model and prescribe compounded GLP-1 medications only when a licensed clinician determines treatment is appropriate. IVIM emphasizes a structured GLP-1 ID protocol with weekly adjustments and clinical materials referencing observational studies. HermanRx emphasizes matching patients to licensed, board-certified physicians across multiple treatment categories and ongoing support without separate consultation fees.

Safety and regulatory notes

Both providers state that compounded GLP-1 medications are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HermanRx explicitly states that its compounded medications are not FDA-approved. IVIM includes similar regulatory language and notes that compounded products are prepared by independent compounding pharmacies and are not appropriate for everyone.

Final comparison and recommendation

HermanRx versus IVIM Health boils down to priorities. HermanRx stands out for clear all-inclusive monthly pricing, explicit fast review and delivery timelines, and no membership or consultation fee. IVIM Health stands out for external validation via 4.9-star reviews on both Google and Trustpilot and for marketing a clinician-led GLP-1 ID protocol supported by observational studies and internal data.

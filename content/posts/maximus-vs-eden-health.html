Eden Health advertises monthly pricing at every dose with semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129, and a lifetime price guarantee. The evidence does not supply insurance billing details beyond “no insurance required” for Eden, nor details on billing mechanics for renewals beyond the lifetime price guarantee claim.

Medication sourcing and regulatory notes

Maximus explicitly uses compounded semaglutide filled through trusted U.S. pharmacies and the source includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Eden Health lists semaglutide and tirzepatide pricing and states that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Use of compounded medication and approval status are distinct facts present in each provider record.

If FDA approval or branded products matter to you, the sources require confirming product type and approval status with each provider before enrolling.

Care model and program support

Maximus emphasizes convenient telemedicine access, prescriptions approved by licensed doctors, and filling through U.S. pharmacies. The offering is described as a straightforward, direct-pay model without in-person visits. Eden Health positions itself as a guided GLP-1 program that combines medication, care, and support under one monthly price and highlights clinician-reviewed programs. The captured Eden content emphasizes a guided program and clinician review but does not supply granular details on the nature or frequency of coaching or support interactions.

Final comparison and how to choose

Maximus vs Eden Health is a choice between a simple, low flat-rate compounded semaglutide direct-pay plan and a lower advertised monthly price with a lifetime price guarantee combined with a guided program. Maximus: compounded semaglutide, telemedicine prescribing, U.S. pharmacy fulfillment, $132.99 per month and availability in most states. Eden Health: semaglutide at $99 per month, tirzepatide at $129 per month, lifetime price guarantee, and a guided clinician-reviewed program.

Choose Maximus if you want a straightforward direct-pay compounded semaglutide option prescribed via telemedicine and filled through U.S. pharmacies at a low flat rate and if you are comfortable with compounded medication and Maximus’s stated state availability.

Choose Eden Health if you prioritize a lower listed monthly price for semaglutide, a lifetime price guarantee, and an advertised guided GLP-1 program with clinician review, provided you confirm eligibility, product type, pharmacy sourcing, renewal terms and state availability before enrolling.

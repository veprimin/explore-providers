Hers vs LTTL splits on two clear axes: Hers emphasizes FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy® (starting at $149/mo) and a required membership, while LTTL offers compounded GLP-1 medications with up to $140 off your first month. Early verdict: choose Hers for FDA-approved, brand-name semaglutide and a personalized care team; choose LTTL if you prioritize compounded formulations and an applied first-month code.

How Hers and LTTL differ on medication type and regulatory status

Hers offers FDA-approved Wegovy® (brand-name semaglutide) and states it does not offer compounded medications. LTTL provides compounded GLP-1 medications prepared by U.S.A. licensed pharmacies and notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. This is a primary distinction: Hers sells name-brand, FDA-approved products; LTTL emphasizes compounded formulations.

Pricing structure and up-front costs compared

Hers lists Wegovy® from $149/mo and injectable options from $199/mo, and it states an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required ($39 first month, auto-renews at $149/month thereafter) with medication billed separately. LTTL lists a starting price of $140 and marketing content saying up to $140 off your first month with a code auto-applied and claims such as same price every dose and example price points like $249 and $129 (4 doses/month). The providers present different billing models: Hers requires a membership for medication access; LTTL highlights a promotional first-month credit.

CTA and enrollment notes

Both providers say treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Hers notes medications are not available without an active membership and that prescription is not guaranteed. LTTL materials caution pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Care model: personalized team vs compounded fulfillment

Hers advertises a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins as part of its offering. LTTL emphasizes pharmacy preparation and lab verification: compounded medications are prepared in state-licensed pharmacies, independently verified in FDA-registered labs, and confirmed every 3–6 months to stay within ± 10% of the target strength. Hers focuses on provider-directed dose check-ins; LTTL emphasizes pharmacy quality-control processes for compounded products.

Availability notes, eligibility and state coverage

Hers states not available in all 50 states, prescription required, and that Ozempic® and Mounjaro® are FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes and available only to patients who meet clinical eligibility criteria determined by a licensed provider. LTTL cautions pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change. Both require clinician review as part of access.

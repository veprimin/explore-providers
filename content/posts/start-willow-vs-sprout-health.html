Comparing Start Willow vs Sprout Health? Quick verdict: Start Willow prioritizes a 3-day delivery guarantee and personalized compounded semaglutide from $249/month, while Sprout Health emphasizes lightning-quick approval and a $50 first-month discount to $199, then $249 ongoing.

How they differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: pricing and first-month discounts, approval speed and onboarding friction, delivery and shipping commitments, and the use and regulatory status of compounded medications. The two providers both operate as licensed telehealth platforms prescribing compounded GLP-1 products after clinician review and do not require insurance.

Price and first-month offers

Start Willow: From $249 per month the first month, rising to $299/month ongoing. No membership fees and no insurance required. Sprout Health: $50 off the first month, bringing the initial cost to $199, with ongoing pricing at $249/month. Both sources state the ongoing rates are higher than several lower-cost competitors.

Approval speed and onboarding friction

Start Willow uses a direct-pay model that eliminates insurance pre-authorization, positioned for patients who found prior-authorization processes frustrating. Sprout Health highlights a lightning-quick approval process and is described as fast-entry and low-friction. If avoiding insurance paperwork matters, Start Willow explicitly markets bypassing prior authorization; if the priority is the fastest possible clinician review, Sprout frames its onboarding as lightning-quick.

Delivery and fulfillment commitments

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less from its licensed pharmacy network and advertises same-day prescriptions with free 2-Day shipping in captured landing-page evidence. Sprout Health’s captured page did not include a verified shipping promise; the source states medication ships to your door following approval but no specific delivery guarantee was captured.

Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both Start Willow and Sprout Health prescribe compounded semaglutide formulations after an online clinician review. Start Willow’s source repeatedly notes that compounded semaglutide is not FDA-approved and that the FDA does not review compounded medications for safety, effectiveness, or quality; they state these products are prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies required to meet USP compounding standards. Sprout Health’s captured content also notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Treatment availability is subject to a licensed clinician determining it is medically appropriate.

Outcome framing and patient expectations

Sprout Health frames outcomes around losing 10 to 26 percent of body weight over 12 months, while also noting that individual results vary and that Sprout does not offer a formal outcome guarantee. Start Willow describes semaglutide as a GLP-1 receptor agonist that may slow digestion, curb hunger, and signal fullness, and includes patient-reported experiences on its landing page. Neither source guarantees medical outcomes; both emphasize clinician review and individualized plans.

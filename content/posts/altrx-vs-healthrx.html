How altRx and HealthRx differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the source material: advertised starting prices and plan structure, medication compounding and regulatory notes, shipping approach and speed, clinician review and prescription process, and availability or enrollment conditions.

Pricing and plan structure

altRx advertises compounded semaglutide starting at $89/month and compounded tirzepatide at $149/month. altRx presents these as a limited offer and highlights no membership, no hidden fees, and buy now pay later options. HealthRx advertises semaglutide from $99/month but specifies that the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid 12-month semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal). HealthRx lists tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month and notes plan pricing does not change when dose changes.

Medication type and regulatory note

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by licensed pharmacies. Each source includes the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. altRx specifically says prescriptions for altRx members are filled by state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies in its network. HealthRx likewise states compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and is not FDA approved.

Clinician review and prescribing process

altRx: a licensed provider reviews your health evaluation and conducts a telehealth consultation, which may be live video or asynchronous depending on state telehealth laws; if the provider determines treatment is appropriate, a prescription will be sent. HealthRx: the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is appropriate or whether neither is; you choose the plan, and the clinician determines the medication. Both emphasize clinician review and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician finds it medically appropriate.

Shipping and delivery

altRx advertises free and discreet 2-day delivery for approved prescriptions and typical processing and delivery time of approximately 4 to 5 business days. HealthRx advertises overnight cold shipping with cold chain tracked free on every plan and no shipping line at checkout. If cold-chain overnight shipping is a priority, HealthRx documents an explicit overnight cold shipping commitment; altRx documents free 2-day delivery.

Final thoughts on altRx vs HealthRx

In altRx vs HealthRx, altRx is the lower advertised starting price with fully online visits, no membership, and a limited promotional offer for semaglutide at $89/month and tirzepatide at $149/month. HealthRx advertises a slightly higher starting price but highlights clinician discretion on medication choice and overnight cold shipping; its lowest monthly price is tied to a 12-month prepaid plan.

Choose altRx if you prioritize lower advertised starting pricing, a fully online intake and membership available across all 50 states, and free 2-day delivery. Choose HealthRx if clinician-directed medication selection and documented overnight cold chain shipping are your primary priorities and you are willing to confirm prepaid plan terms and state availability before enrolling.

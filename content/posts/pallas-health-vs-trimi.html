Pallas Health vs Trimi: both offer clinician-prescribed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, but they differ on pricing structure, brand access, shipping speed, clinician network and refund/renewal terms. Early verdict: choose Trimi for the lowest flat monthly compounding price and overnight shipping; choose Pallas Health if you want a clinician who can prescribe brand-name GLP-1s or a per-medication compounded plan priced by medication.

Pricing model and starting cost

Pallas Health offers compounded semaglutide with a first month price of $139, then $597 every 12 weeks (a $199/month average). Compounded tirzepatide starts $179 first month, then $897 every 12 weeks ($299/month average). Pallas prices compounded plans by medication rather than by milligram, so titrating up does not raise the plan price. Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/month on an annual plan and tirzepatide at $125/month, with flat pricing that never rises as dose increases.

Brand-name options versus compounded-only focus

Pallas Health explicitly offers both FDA-approved brand-name GLP-1s (Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound and Mounjaro) and clinician-personalized compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide when a brand medication is not the right fit. Trimi focuses on compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prescribed and shipped to your door; brand-name availability is not supplied in Trimi evidence.

Shipping and fulfillment

Trimi promotes free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping. Pallas Health offers free expedited and discreet shipping from US-licensed compounding pharmacies but does not claim overnight temperature-controlled shipping in the supplied facts.

Clinician network, oversight and refunds

Both providers use US-licensed clinicians to determine eligibility and prescribe. Trimi states a board-certified physician founded the medical oversight network and that providers review plans through Arora Health, with a 6-month money-back promise if you follow the plan and do not lose weight, terms apply. Pallas Health states eligibility is determined by a US-licensed clinician and that care and check-ins are included in the plan price; Pallas refunds in full if a clinician decides treatment is not right for you. Trimi also notes an automatic refund if a patient is not approved by the medical team.

Regulatory and practical notes

Both providers state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Pallas clarifies it does not manufacture or compound medication; US-licensed compounding pharmacies prepare compounded products and the medication you receive may differ in appearance from images. Trimi lists named 503A pharmacies and offers a certificate of analysis on request in the captured landing-page evidence.

How to decide: Pallas Health vs Trimi

If your priority is the lowest flat monthly cost on an annual plan and overnight temperature-controlled shipping, Trimi is the supported choice. If you want a clinician who can prescribe FDA-approved brand-name GLP-1 options in addition to compounded preparations and a per-medication compounded pricing model, Pallas Health is the supported choice.

2. Price and payment structure differences

Entry pricing differs: SkinnyRx lists as low as $249/mo. Try Ageless advertises lower entry pricing on a yearly plan with tirzepatide drops from $159/mo and semaglutide from $199/mo. 3. Bundled features and delivery

Both providers include shipping. SkinnyRx offers free overnight shipping and emphasizes ongoing provider access, refill forms, and cancellation before prescription review for refunds. Try Ageless offers free discreet shipping and bundles B12 in every plan for energy support. Try Ageless also allows switching molecules or formats under one plan with no new consult fee.

4. Safety, testing and regulatory notes

Both sell compounded medications. SkinnyRx specifies partner pharmacies perform third-party testing every 3 to 6 months confirming concentration within plus or minus 10 percent. Try Ageless notes its products are compounded by state-licensed 503A pharmacies and that oral tirzepatide drops are an off-label compounded format. Both state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved or reviewed by the FDA for safety or effectiveness.

5. Flexibility, switching and plan terms

Try Ageless highlights format switching under a single plan with no new consult fee and cancel-anytime flexibility, though the lowest prices require prepaid yearly plans. SkinnyRx operates via online consultation and subscription service that can be canceled at any time; SkinnyRx allows full refunds any time before a licensed provider completes the prescription review, after which orders become non-refundable.

Conclusion: Which to choose in the SkinnyRx vs Try Ageless comparison

SkinnyRx if: you specifically want daily oral semaglutide tablets, need FSA or HSA eligibility to use pre-tax dollars, and value free overnight shipping plus documented third-party testing cadence.

Try Ageless if: you want choice across three GLP-1 formats including needle-free tirzepatide drops, prefer the lowest advertised yearly entry price from $159/mo, value B12 bundled into plans, or want the ability to switch molecules or formats under one plan with no new consult fee.

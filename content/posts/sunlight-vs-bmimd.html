Key differences at a glance

This comparison focuses on five supported differences: starting price and billing, medication formulation and regulatory notes, plan length and commitments, shipping and membership terms, and clinician access and refill management. Each point is drawn from the supplied evidence for Sunlight and bmiMD.

1. Starting price and pricing model

Sunlight advertises personalized GLP-1 “from $88/mo” with messaging like “Same price forever” and promotions stating “As low as $88.” bmiMD lists a month-to-month price of $179 and multi-month options that reduce the monthly equivalent to $159 for three months, $149 for six months, and $139 for a 12-month plan. Sunlight frames low entry pricing while bmiMD emphasizes lower long-term monthly equivalents with prepaid options.

2. Medication formulation and regulatory notes

Sunlight offers “injections and oral options” and promotes prescription and telehealth included. bmiMD specifically offers compounded tirzepatide and includes the regulatory statement that “compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.” Sunlight materials note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

3. Plan length, commitments, and refund policies

Sunlight highlights “No Monthly Membership” and “Cancel Anytime” alongside a summer promo and statements like “Same price every month.” bmiMD provides four billing options and shows that the lowest price requires a 12-month commitment, noting “Total prepaid charge should be confirmed” and offering a claim of “100% Refundable if not eligible for program.”

4. Shipping, delivery, and membership features

Sunlight advertises “Free shipping in 1-2 days,” “Fast, Free Shipping,” and “Free & Discreet 2-day shipping” in its captured landing evidence. bmiMD advertises “Free subscription shipping” and “free shipping on every subscription order.” Sunlight repeatedly notes “No Monthly Membership” while bmiMD centers shipping on subscription orders tied to its billing options.

5. Clinician access, refills, and oversight

Both providers operate online with clinician review. Sunlight highlights a “100% online medical visit” and clinician adjustments in testimonials. bmiMD emphasizes “24/7 clinician access,” online refills and delivery, and that treatment is “available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.” bmiMD also notes batch confirmation “every 3–6 months to stay within ± 10 % of the target strength” for preparations.

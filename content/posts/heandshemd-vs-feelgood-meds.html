Top differences at a glance for He & She MD vs FeelGood Meds

This comparison uses only supplied facts. Both providers offer online clinician review and compounded GLP-1 options, and both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

1. Bundled coaching and clinician access

He & She MD explicitly bundles a coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific guidance and progress tracking at no extra cost. The source says every plan includes that app and messaging. FeelGood Meds lists clinician-reviewed treatments but does not present a bundled coaching app or 1:1 messaging as included in the captured evidence.

2. Pricing structure and promotional terms

He & She MD advertises semaglutide injections starting at $139/month and oral semaglutide or tirzepatide injections at $199/month as six-month introductory rates that renew higher; the source cautions the intro rates cover the first six months and then the plan renews at the standard six-month price. FeelGood Meds shows compounded injections starting at $149/month and compounded tablets from $249/month, plus a branded option shown from $1,999/month. FeelGood Meds also highlights no membership fees and free shipping in the captured evidence.

3. Medication formats and branded option

He & She MD lists three formats: semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection. FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections, compounded tablets, and a branded medication option shown from $1,999/month. He & She MD frames its offerings as a GLP-1 Platinum Program with injectable and oral options; FeelGood Meds emphasizes a branded alternative in addition to compounded forms.

4. Shipping, fees, and guarantees

Both sources mention free shipping and no insurance required. He & She MD states no membership fee. FeelGood Meds also states no membership or hidden fees and includes a Weight Loss Money Back Guarantee in the captured claims. The He & She MD source notes free expedited shipping and no membership fee as well.

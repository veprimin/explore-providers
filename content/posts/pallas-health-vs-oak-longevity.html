Pricing and billing: which is cheaper for ongoing use

Pallas advertises a lower intro month for compounded semaglutide at $139 first month, with renewal priced as $597 every 12 weeks, a $199/month average, and compounded tirzepatide starting $179 first month then $897 every 12 weeks ($299/mo avg). Oak lists semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month as a flat price for all dosages. Oak bills monthly on a non-recurring basis and states no subscription is required, while Pallas compounds renew every 12 weeks for its compounded plans. The article preserves that both providers use compounded products when applicable and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Access to brand-name GLP-1s and clinician choice

Pallas explicitly offers clinician-selected options including FDA-approved Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound and Mounjaro or clinician-personalized compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared in the USA when a brand medication is not the right fit. Oak emphasizes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies and does not list brand-name pens. If access to a clinician decision that may prescribe FDA-approved brand medications matters, the source supports that Pallas offers that route.

Care model, approvals and logistics

Pallas includes provider care, ongoing check-ins and care-team messaging inside plan prices and operates 100% online with asynchronous intake in most states; a video visit is required in seven states plus Washington, D.C. Oak describes asynchronous review by a licensed physician, board-certified physicians in your state, free health coaching, and same-day approval for qualifying patients. Both note that eligibility is determined by a US-licensed clinician and that compounded products are not FDA-reviewed finished products.

When shipping, refunds, and cancellations differ

Pallas offers free expedited and discreet shipping and notes compounded plans renew every 12 weeks, so cancellation timing matters. Oak offers free shipping with tracking, states three-month prescriptions are billed upfront in some cases, and says no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped. Both sources caution that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Final thoughts on Pallas Health vs Oak Longevity

Pallas Health centers clinician choice between brand-name pens and compounded preparations and bundles provider care and messaging in plan prices, with compounded semaglutide $139 first month then $597 every 12 weeks. Oak Longevity focuses on lower flat monthly compound pricing, semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month, no subscriptions, and free health coaching.

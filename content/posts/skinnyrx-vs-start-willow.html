How SkinnyRx vs Start Willow differ at a glance

Both SkinnyRx and Start Willow offer compounded semaglutide options, but they differ across several clear areas: SkinnyRx focuses on daily oral semaglutide tablets with FSA/HSA eligibility and free overnight shipping, while Start Willow offers once weekly compounded semaglutide injections with a direct-pay model, no membership fees, and a 3 day delivery guarantee.

Formulation and dosing: oral semaglutide tablets vs weekly injections

SkinnyRx offers daily oral semaglutide tablets taken under the tongue. Start Willow provides a once weekly subcutaneous injectable semaglutide. The difference in route and dosing is central to the comparison and may guide patient preference. SkinnyRx markets daily tablets as an alternative for people who prefer not to use injections. Start Willow notes needle size is small and provides written and video injection instructions.

Price and payment details

Both list a starting price around $249. SkinnyRx lists “As low as $249/mo” and mentions the option to split payments with Affirm Buy Now Pay Later. Start Willow lists pricing “From $249 per Month” for the first month and notes ongoing pricing rises to $299/month. Start Willow also emphasizes no insurance required and no membership fees.

Shipping and delivery speed

SkinnyRx advertises free overnight shipping on all orders and states after prescription processing you receive shipping and tracking information within 2 business days. Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less from its licensed pharmacy network and also notes free 2-Day shipping when in stock and same day prescriptions in some cases.

Insurance, membership, and clinician review

Start Willow operates on a direct-pay model that requires no insurance and charges no membership fees, bypassing insurance prior authorization. SkinnyRx highlights FSA/HSA eligibility so patients may use pre-tax dollars toward treatment, a flexibility framed as meaningful for those managing out of pocket GLP-1 costs. Both require an online clinician review before a prescription is written.

Safety, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both providers state their semaglutide products are compounded medications and include the regulatory disclosure that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. SkinnyRx adds that partner pharmacies perform third party testing through FDA and DEA compliant labs every 3 to 6 months confirming concentration within plus or minus 10 percent of the active ingredient. Start Willow notes compounding pharmacies operate under state board of pharmacy oversight and are required to meet USP compounding standards.

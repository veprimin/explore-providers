Found vs TrimRx: 3 to 5 key differences that matter

This comparison draws directly from each provider’s published claims. The most distinctive supported differences are: program approach and personalization, pricing and pricing protections, insurance and billing, medication options and regulatory notes, and shipping and membership models.

1. Program approach and personalization

Found describes a “biology-first” approach that builds a personalized medically supervised program around a patient’s metabolic biology, hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavioral factors. TrimRx presents a clinically designed program built around compounded GLP-1 injections and GLP-1+GIP options and emphasizes scale with 100,000+ customers and a 4.8 rating. Both use telehealth, but Found emphasizes broader biological evaluation while TrimRx emphasizes a standardized compounded medication program.

2. Pricing, protections, and starting costs

Found advertises getting started “for as low as $39” and mentions multiple membership and pricing tiers and that advertised pricing for branded GLP-1s is via insurance with commitments noted. TrimRx lists compounded GLP-1 from $149/month and GLP-1+GIP from $249/month and highlights “Lifetime Price Protection” that locks in today’s price for life with the same price on every dose and no hidden fees. TrimRx also advertises a $140 promotional discount on its landing page evidence.

3. Insurance, payments, and billing

Found states “Most insurance now accepted” and that it handles insurance paperwork and offers some Medicare Bridge options for eligible Part D members, while also noting coverage, pricing, and terms vary. TrimRx operates on a direct-pay model with no insurance required and says it is HSA/FSA eligible. TrimRx explicitly notes it does not accept insurance for its primary compounded medication program.

4. Medication options and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 options in their respective programs and include regulatory notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Found lists both compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and also references branded GLP-1s via insurance pathways. TrimRx offers compounded GLP-1 and compounded GLP-1+GIP, and if a branded prescription is written the patient fills it separately; TrimRx does not sell or ship branded medications.

5. Shipping, membership, and customer scale

TrimRx highlights free 1–2 day shipping and no monthly membership or cancel-anytime language. TrimRx also cites 100k+ customers and a 4.8 Excellent Rating. Found emphasizes affordable monthly plans, cancel-anytime membership tiers, and insurance handling; shipping details for Found were not supplied in the captured evidence.

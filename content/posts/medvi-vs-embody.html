Quick direct comparison

This head-to-head focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: starting and ongoing price structure, medication options, shipping speed and delivery, level of clinical support and program features, and regulatory notes about compounded medicines. Both platforms are licensed telehealth providers that prescribe compounded GLP-1s online with no clinic visits required.

Price structure and promos

MedVi advertises a winter promotional first-month price of $179 including fast, free shipping, but notes ongoing standalone months can cost $299 and a three-month plan is $649. Embody lists semaglutide at $69/month (reduced from $299) and tirzepatide at $119/month with flat locked-in monthly pricing and no long-term commitment. If low recurring cost is the priority, Embody’s $69/month semaglutide offer is presented as the leaner option. MedVi emphasizes a low promotional entry price but warns that ongoing price is substantially higher.

Medication options and clinical dosing

MedVi focuses on clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only, and cites clinician review and prescription discretion by affiliated clinicians. Embody offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide at separate price points, so Embody supports an additional medication option beyond semaglutide. Both emphasize that medications are compounded and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products, with regulatory notes preserved.

Shipping speed and delivery

MedVi includes fast, free shipping with its promotional month. Embody promises free shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days after clinician approval, highlighting faster delivery timing. If delivery speed after approval matters, Embody is presented as having one of the fastest commitments.

Support, program features, and commitments

MedVi promotes 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and a simple online intake with no clinic visits. It also describes the first-month cost covering physician review, a personalized plan, 1:1 guidance, and the medication. Embody highlights doctor-prescribed treatment with prescription and telemed visits included, no monthly membership, cancel anytime, and 24/7 support access for dosing and side-effect questions. Embody positions itself as a no-frills, flat priced program without integrated coaching or advanced monitoring that some pricier platforms offer.

Regulatory and outcome notes

Both sources clearly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. MedVi includes additional statements about results varying based on adherence and clinician decisions, and that compounded medications are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but not FDA-approved as finished products. Embody similarly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and that final pricing may vary based on dose and clinician recommendation.

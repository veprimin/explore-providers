How TrimRx and Hers differ in medication type and regulatory status

TrimRx: primary program provides access to compounded GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP injections prepared by state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacies. The source explicitly states these compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and the FDA has not reviewed them for safety, effectiveness, or quality. TrimRx also notes providers may write prescriptions for branded FDA-approved medications that patients fill separately; TrimRx does not sell or ship branded medications.

Hers: offers FDA-approved Wegovy as a brand-name semaglutide option and states it does not offer compounded medications for weight loss. The source emphasizes Wegovy and Zepbound as FDA-approved options and notes Ozempic is FDA approved for diabetes but may be prescribed off-label for weight loss at a clinician’s discretion.

Pricing model, membership, and guarantees

TrimRx lists weight-loss plans starting at $149/month with GLP-1+GIP from $249/month. TrimRx advertises Lifetime Price Protection so you can lock in today’s price for life and pay the same price on every dose, with no monthly membership and the ability to cancel anytime. The source mentions a promotional $140 discount and that HSA/FSA eligibility is available.

Hers lists Wegovy from $149/mo and injectable options from $199/mo and higher. The source makes clear an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required to access medication: $39 for the first month then auto-renews at $149/month thereafter. The captured evidence also states membership and medication are billed separately and medication is not available without an active membership.

Care model and support differences

TrimRx: telehealth visits and prescription & telemed visits are included in the plan according to the source. TrimRx markets 24/7 support and describes its platform as a licensed telehealth service that prescribes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide through online medical review with U.S. licensed providers.

Hers: emphasizes a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins. The source frames Hers as offering doctor-trusted brand GLP-1s with a personalized care team and ongoing follow up, and notes treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Availability, shipping, and timing

TrimRx: advertises free 1–2 day shipping for its shipped products and states medications are currently in stock per the source. TrimRx also stresses no monthly membership and the ability to cancel anytime.

Hers: the source notes medication and membership are subject to state availability and clinical eligibility; it explicitly says not available in all 50 states and that prescription is required. Hers does not offer compounded alternatives according to the captured evidence.

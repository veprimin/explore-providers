Quick comparison: bmiMD vs JoinSkinny

The primary difference between bmiMD vs JoinSkinny is how each structures price, medication sourcing, and ongoing clinical support. Early verdict: bmiMD leans toward lower long-term monthly pricing for compounded tirzepatide if you commit to 12 months, while JoinSkinny emphasizes flat-rate pricing, rapid temperature-controlled shipping and flexible cancellation.

Top differences at a glance

The most distinctive supported differences are: pricing and billing commitments; medication type and regulatory notes; shipping speed and temperature control; and ongoing clinician access and monitoring. The sections below compare these directly using only supplied facts.

1. Pricing and billing commitments

bmiMD lists four billing options for compounded tirzepatide: month-to-month at $179, three-month at $159/mo, six-month at $149/mo, and a 12-month option as low as $139/month. bmiMD also notes the lowest price requires a 12-month commitment and that total prepaid charges should be confirmed.

JoinSkinny offers plans starting at $149 with monthly and 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles. JoinSkinny advertises flat-rate pricing where the monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases and allows canceling before the next billing cycle.

View JoinSkinny plans and bundles

2. Medication type, pharmacy sourcing, and regulatory notes

bmiMD promotes compounded tirzepatide prepared in a state-licensed pharmacy and states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. bmiMD’s landing-page evidence repeats that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

JoinSkinny offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and says prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners. JoinSkinny also includes the captured regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved in the comparison data. JoinSkinny states branded prescriptions are filled and paid for separately when applicable.

Check bmiMD medication details

3. Shipping speed and temperature control

bmiMD offers free subscription shipping on every subscription order. JoinSkinny advertises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1–2 days and says prescriptions received before 2 pm central time are shipped the same day, with next-day UPS in a temperature controlled package and tracking provided.

See JoinSkinny shipping details

4. Clinician access and ongoing support

bmiMD lists 24/7 clinician access and clinician-reviewed online weight-management treatment with treatment available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. JoinSkinny describes telemedicine visits, monthly check-ins, 24/7 chat, dose adjustments guided by a licensed provider, and ongoing support with HIPAA protection.

Final thoughts on bmiMD vs JoinSkinny

Both services deliver clinician-reviewed online care, but they focus on different tradeoffs. bmiMD markets compounded tirzepatide with multi-month savings and free subscription shipping, with the lowest monthly price tied to a 12-month commitment and a reminder that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. JoinSkinny emphasizes flat-rate pricing, fast temperature-controlled shipping, telemedicine with monthly check-ins and 24/7 chat, and uses FDA-regulated, LegitScript-certified pharmacies.

Regulatory and safety notes

Both sites clearly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Measured states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that prescriptions are issued at the provider’s discretion. TrimRx states compounded drugs are prepared at state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacies and that they are not FDA-approved finished products. These qualifiers are important when weighing safety and oversight.

Medication form and regulatory status

Ro.co is the only mainstream platform in this comparison with access to the Wegovy pill, described as the first and only FDA-approved oral GLP-1 weight loss medication on the captured page. The source emphasizes that Ro offers FDA-approved finished medication rather than compounded products. Telos Rx prescribes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and explicitly states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. These regulatory distinctions are central when comparing Ro.co vs Telos Rx.

Price entry point and ongoing cost framing

Ro.co highlights “Get Started Online for Just $39” as an entry point, while noting branded medications like the Wegovy pill carry higher ongoing costs. Telos Rx lists compounded semaglutide “as low as $99 per month” and tirzepatide “as low as $139 per month,” and presents per-month pricing subject to provider approval. Both sources say treatment is available only after clinician review; Ro.co emphasizes no hidden fees and disclosed pricing before completing consultation, while Telos Rx cautions that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Medication choice and clinical matching

Ro.co focuses on providing the branded oral option, framed as meaningful for injection-averse patients. Telos Rx offers two compounded molecules: semaglutide (GLP-1) and tirzepatide (GLP-1 + GIP), noting a US-licensed provider decides which molecule is right. Telos Rx frames tirzepatide as dual-action versus semaglutide as the original GLP-1 pathway.

Transparency, limitations, and best-fit patients

Ro.co positions itself as an established full-service telehealth platform with disclosed pricing and regulatory assurance because it prescribes FDA-approved finished products. The captured evidence lists Ro.co’s cons: branded medications can cost significantly more than compounded alternatives. Telos Rx directs readers to confirm eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment and warns pricing and terms may change. Both platforms require clinician review and medical appropriateness.

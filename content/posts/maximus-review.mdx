## About Maximus

Maximus is a telemedicine platform whose weight-loss line prescribes **compounded GLP-1 medication** — the same active ingredients as the branded drugs, prepared by licensed pharmacies but not FDA-approved as finished products. Prescriptions are approved by licensed doctors and filled through trusted U.S. pharmacies. The model is straightforward direct-pay: no insurance is billed, and the monthly cost is what you pay.

## How It Works

The process is fully online. You complete a telemedicine intake covering your health history and weight-loss goals, a licensed doctor reviews your case for eligibility, and — if approved — your compounded GLP-1 prescription is filled through a U.S. pharmacy and shipped. There are no clinic visits at any stage. Domestic-pharmacy sourcing is a point of reassurance for patients concerned about supply-chain quality.

GLP-1 therapy for chronic weight management is well documented; the [NIH](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8859692/) summarises the clinical evidence base behind supervised semaglutide treatment.

No. The entire process is online — a licensed doctor reviews your case and approves your prescription, which is then filled through a U.S. pharmacy and shipped to you.

### Where does Maximus get its medication?

Maximus fills prescriptions through trusted U.S. pharmacies, with all prescriptions approved by licensed doctors. The medication is compounded using the same active ingredient as the branded GLP-1 products.

Yes. It is a telemedicine platform whose prescriptions are approved by licensed doctors and filled through U.S. pharmacies. It is a smaller, newer platform with fewer public reviews than larger competitors, and its medication is compounded rather than FDA-approved.

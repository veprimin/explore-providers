Medication form, regulatory notes, and clinician review

SnagRx supplies compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. The source repeatedly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. SnagRx names four US 503A partner pharmacies and uses OpenLoop Health clinicians; OpenLoop clinicians retain prescribing decisions and cover 48 states. Willow offers an oral GLP-1 program delivered via a licensed telehealth team; the captured material states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. The captured Willow page notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Program support and logistics: shipping, follow-up, and tracking

SnagRx advertises next-day UPS or FedEx shipping in temperature-controlled packaging and a clinician review typically within five hours; a declined prescription is refunded in full. SnagRx states no monthly membership, cancel anytime, and includes injection supplies and shipping in the price. Willow emphasizes progress tracking tools, follow-up consultations, and a structured digital workflow to convert medication into sustainable outcomes. The captured Willow evidence did not verify shipping or membership terms.

  • Program structure: SnagRx does not advertise coaching or nutrition programming; Willow focuses on progress tracking and follow-up support.
  • Clinician and fulfillment details: SnagRx names its clinician network and partner pharmacies and states next-day shipping; Willow emphasizes licensed telehealth but captured shipping details are not available.

Pricing model and what is included

Omzo presents starting prices: semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279. Omzo connects patients with licensed U.S. providers who determine medication and may include a compounded medication program shipped by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy. HermanRx lists explicit all-inclusive monthly prices for compounded GLP-1 plus B12: semaglutide + B12 at $249/month and tirzepatide + B12 at $349/month, with no membership fee and no separate consultation charge. HermanRx advertises that the provider review, prescription, medication, ongoing support and free shipping are included in the monthly price.

Compounded status and prescription options

Both providers state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Omzo specifically notes its primary program provides access to compounded medications produced by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy and also says a provider may write a prescription for an FDA-approved branded medication, which the patient must fill independently. HermanRx notes it offers both FDA-approved and compounded medications depending on the clinician and makes clear its compounded offerings are not FDA-approved.

Service speed, shipping, and geographic availability

HermanRx publishes specific timelines: roughly a 5-minute assessment, about 24-hour provider review and about 72-hour delivery when prescribed, plus free discreet shipping to all 50 states. Omzo states fast, discreet delivery and that user-level rates may vary by pharmacy capacity and state regulations; Omzo also emphasizes state availability, and that providers determine eligibility subject to their review. Where precise shipping timelines matter, HermanRx supplies a clearer published timeline.

Platform scope and patient fit

Omzo positions itself as a clinician-guided platform with personalized GLP-1 care and the potential to prescribe branded medications when appropriate. HermanRx emphasizes a cash-pay telehealth model with five treatment categories beyond weight loss and a straightforward monthly billing model useful for consolidating subscriptions. Omzo recommends people confirm eligibility, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment. HermanRx highlights availability in all 50 states and no insurance requirement.

Final comparison and how to choose

In summary, Omzo vs HermanRx hinges on whether you prioritize clinician-guided flexibility and potential access to branded prescriptions or prefer transparent all-inclusive monthly pricing and published service timelines. Both make clear compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

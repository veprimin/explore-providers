If you want the cheapest entry to online GLP-1 therapy, Embody lists compounded semaglutide starting at $69/month and free 1–2 day shipping after approval. HumeCare+ starts at $199/month for semaglutide and focuses on a bundled protocol with glycine, B12 and B6, body composition scans, and an app. Early verdict: Embody is for price-driven patients who want a no-frills prescription and fast delivery; HumeCare+ is for people who want protocolized support and tracking rather than the lowest price.

How they differ at a glance

Both providers offer clinician-prescribed, compounded GLP-1 medications and free shipping, but they position their services differently: Embody advertises steeply reduced monthly prices and a lean, prescription-first model. HumeCare+ bundles supportive ingredients, body-composition tracking, an app, and a money-back promise tied to weight-loss results over six months, subject to terms.

Top differences to compare

1. Price and product type

Embody: Compounded semaglutide at $69/month, reduced from $299, and compounded tirzepatide at $119/month. Final pricing may vary based on dose and clinician recommendation and the $69 pricing is a limited-time offer that may increase. Embody emphasizes flat, locked-in monthly pricing and no monthly membership.

HumeCare+: Semaglutide from $199/month (marked down from $299) and a tirzepatide protocol from $299/month (marked down from $399). HumeCare+ notes pricing reflects a promotional discount that may change.

2. Protocol and supportive features

Embody provides a no-frills telehealth prescription model with prescription and telemed visits included, 24/7 support access for dosing and side-effect questions, and no integrated coaching or advanced monitoring. HumeCare+ packages medication with glycine plus vitamins B6 and B12, monthly body-composition scans to track fat versus lean mass, a dedicated app for tracking, and continuous physician optimization.

3. Delivery speed and shipping

Embody advertises free shipping with delivery in 1–2 days after clinician approval. HumeCare+ offers free next-day delivery and discreet shipping. Both platforms say medication ships from licensed pharmacies after a clinician review.

4. Guarantees and claims

Embody does not advertise a money-back weight-loss promise; it highlights value and fast delivery. HumeCare+ advertises a 10 percent or your money-back promise over six months, with eligibility and terms to be verified directly with HumeCare+.

5. Regulatory and product notes

Both providers use compounded medications. Each site states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Embody specifically notes its compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products but use the same active ingredients as certain branded drugs. HumeCare+ also clarifies that compounded medications are distinct from branded products and dispensed from U.S. pharmacies licensed and inspected by state Boards of Pharmacy.

Quick verdict: Maximus vs He & She MD

The main difference in Maximus vs He & She MD is pricing and bundled support. Maximus is a lower flat-rate direct-pay program for compounded semaglutide starting at $132.99/month with telemedicine and U.S. pharmacy fills. He & She MD starts semaglutide at $139/month as a six-month introductory rate and adds a coaching app, 1:1 clinician messaging, and tracking included at no extra cost. Early verdict: choose Maximus for the simplest lower monthly price and domestic pharmacy sourcing; choose He & She MD if you want built-in coaching and clinician messaging with an intro price that renews higher.

Sprout Health vs HealthRx: which telehealth GLP-1 option fits you? Early verdict: Sprout Health prioritizes lightning-quick approval and a $50 first-month discount, while HealthRx emphasizes lower starting pricing from $99/month, prepaid plan terms, and overnight cold shipping with clinician-directed medication choice.

How they differ at a glance

This comparison highlights the most distinctive supported differences between Sprout Health and HealthRx: price and discount structure, approval speed and onboarding, medication options and clinician review, shipping and fulfillment, and regulatory/compounded status.

1. Price and discount structure

Sprout Health markets a $50 off the first month, bringing the initial cost to $199, then lists an ongoing rate of $249/month. The source frames that ongoing price as higher than several competitors. HealthRx advertises plans starting from $99/month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month. HealthRx notes the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan and that plan price does not change when your dose changes.

2. Approval speed and onboarding

Sprout Health emphasizes a lightning-quick approval process and a low-friction, fully online experience designed for patients who want to start quickly. HealthRx requires clinician review to determine medication appropriateness and highlights clinician-decided medication selection rather than a user-picked menu; eligibility, state coverage, and renewal terms may change and should be confirmed before enrollment.

3. Medication options and clinician review

Sprout Health prescribes compounded semaglutide after an online clinician review and notes its compounded medications are not FDA-approved. HealthRx offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription and states the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is the right fit, or whether neither is. Both providers present clinician review as a gate to treatment.

4. Shipping and fulfillment

Sprout Health describes home delivery following approval but the captured page did not verify shipping details beyond that. HealthRx explicitly promotes overnight cold shipping, free and tracked on every plan, with a cold chain; it also notes compound preparation in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom. Where shipping speed and cold-chain tracking matter, HealthRx supplies explicit claims about overnight cold shipping.

5. Regulatory and product notes

Both sources include regulatory qualifiers: Sprout Health warns that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HealthRx likewise states compounded medication is not FDA-approved and is prepared by a licensed pharmacy for an individual patient with a valid prescription. Sprout also notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Direct comparison takeaways

Sprout Health vs HealthRx breaks down to speed and a one-time discount versus lower advertised entry pricing and explicit cold-chain shipping. Sprout positions itself for people who want a low-friction, lightning-quick start and a clear outcome framing of 10 to 26 percent body weight reduction over 12 months as part of its marketing. HealthRx positions itself around lower starting monthly pricing on prepaid plans, clinician-directed choice between semaglutide and tirzepatide, and overnight cold shipping with no additional shipping charge on plans.

Practical selection guidance

Choose Sprout Health if you prioritize the fastest possible online approval and a simple first-month discount while accepting a higher ongoing monthly rate. Choose HealthRx if you want lower advertised starting pricing on a prepaid semaglutide plan, explicit overnight cold shipping, and clinician-determined medication choice between semaglutide and tirzepatide.

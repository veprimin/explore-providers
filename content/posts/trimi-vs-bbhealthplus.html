Price and pricing model compared

Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/mo on an annual plan and compounded tirzepatide from $125/month, with the stated benefit that every dose is the same price. Bodybuilding Health+ lists semaglutide from $179/mo and tirzepatide from $209/mo with multi-month bundles that lower effective monthly cost. Membership, cancellations, and billing transparency

Trimi describes a flat membership that covers medication, clinician visits, ongoing check-ins, and free shipping as part of its offering, including a 6-month money-back promise with terms. Bodybuilding Health+ explicitly markets no monthly membership, no hidden fees, and cancel anytime with no penalties. If you prefer no membership commitment, Bodybuilding Health+ is positioned to provide that flexibility; if you want a single flat membership that includes ongoing care, Trimi states that one flat membership covers care and shipping.

Shipping, pharmacy partners, and regulatory notes

Both providers rely on compounded medications and include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Trimi advertises free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and names state-licensed 503A pharmacies, including VialsRx and GreenwichRx, with certificates of analysis available on request. Bodybuilding Health+ indicates medications are shipped to your door from licensed U.S. pharmacies but does not list specific pharmacy partners in the captured evidence.

Medication options and clinical oversight

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prescribed by U.S.-licensed clinicians and note that a provider must determine treatment is medically appropriate. Trimi highlights clinical oversight by a board-certified physician and a named clinical network led by Dr. Sean Arora, MD, and emphasizes prescriptions through Arora Health. Bodybuilding Health+ states medical oversight is led by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D., and positions itself as a multi-program telehealth platform including longevity and sexual performance services. Both preserve the qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Refunds, guarantees, and enrollment limits

Trimi offers a 6-month money-back promise and states that if not approved by their medical team, an automatic refund is provided. Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes transparent pricing and cancel-anytime policies but does not advertise a comparable money-back guarantee in the captured evidence. Both warn that availability may be limited by state and clinician approval.

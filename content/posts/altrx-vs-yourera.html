Medication type and regulatory note

Both companies dispense compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide rather than FDA-approved branded products. Each source explicitly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. altRx states its medications are filled by state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies, while YourEra notes compounding by state-licensed US pharmacies and emphasizes that compounded products are not the same as brand-name medications.

Access, availability, and shipping speed

Both platforms use telehealth with licensed clinicians and ship medication to your door without requiring insurance or clinic visits. altRx describes a representative processing and delivery time of approximately 4 to 5 business days and offers free discreet 2-day delivery as part of its plan descriptions. YourEra highlights free, discreet shipping that typically arrives in about 1 to 2 days from licensed US pharmacies. Provider availability and prescribing authority vary by state for both services; altRx specifies its membership is available to residents of all 50 U.S. states but notes provider availability varies by state.

Support, included services, and pricing transparency

Both services bundle clinician oversight and ongoing support. altRx lists 100% online medical visits, ongoing care team support, step-by-step guidance, and an all-inclusive monthly fee covering medical oversight and medication. YourEra bundles the provider consult, prescription, cold shipping, dose adjustments, and 24/7 support into its flat plan price and notes the plan holds the same price at every dose. YourEra also highlights LegitScript certification and licensed US clinicians; altRx emphasizes no membership, no hidden fees, and buy now pay later options.

Who each is best for

altRx is built for patients who have been held back by price and want the least expensive advertised entry point without a required multi-month commitment. YourEra is best for people willing to commit to a 6-month plan to access the lowest advertised per-month rate and who want bundled cold shipping, dose adjustments, and flat pricing at every dose.

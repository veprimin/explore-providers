Comparing Hers vs Luvo Health: quick verdict — Hers emphasizes FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy® starting at $149/mo with an active membership requirement and no compounded medications, while Luvo Health highlights ongoing support, dose adjustments and multiple formulation options but the starting price was not verified.

How they differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on five distinctive differences supported by the source: price transparency and starting price, FDA-approved name-brand vs compounded options, membership and billing rules, product and dosing options, and state/eligibility limitations and clinician review.

Price transparency and starting cost

Hers provides a clear starting price: Wegovy® pill from $149/mo and injectable options from $199/mo, with some tirzepatide options from $299/mo and a note that prices include medication only if prescribed. Luvo Health did not publish or verify a starting price on the captured landing-page evidence; the source explicitly states current details were not published or could not be verified.

FDA-approved brand medication versus compounded possibilities

Hers highlights FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy® (brand-name semaglutide) and states it does not offer compounded medications, with a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Luvo Health materials note that compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and are not reviewed or approved by the FDA, and the captured evidence covers compounded options and microdosing formats. In short, Hers positions brand-name, non-compounded GLP-1s while Luvo Health’s captured content references compounded formulations and microdosing options.

Membership, billing and accessibility rules

Hers requires an active Hers Weight Loss Membership to access medication: $39 for the first month, auto-renews at $149/month thereafter, and medication is not available without an active membership; total cost includes the membership fee and medication billed separately. Luvo Health captured pages mention cancel anytime and monthly stages (MONTH 1, MONTH 2–3, 3+ MONTHS) with dose adjustments based on clinical response, but the captured evidence did not verify specific pricing or renewal terms.

Product range, dosing support and clinical review

Hers lists name-brand options including Wegovy®, notes availability of higher doses and mentions tirzepatide offerings; it also emphasizes a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Luvo Health lists tirzepatide injection, semaglutide tablets and injections, microdosing options, and ongoing support with dose changes; it similarly states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

State availability, eligibility and evidence clarity

Hers notes not available in all 50 states and says prescriptions and clinical eligibility are determined by a licensed provider; Luvo Health’s captured evidence warns pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change and that current details were not published or could not be verified on the captured affiliate landing page.

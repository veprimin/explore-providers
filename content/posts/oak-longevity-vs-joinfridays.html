Price and billing structure compared

Oak Longevity advertises semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month with one price covering all dosages and billing monthly on a non recurring basis. Three month prescriptions are billed upfront and Oak states no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped. JoinFridays advertises a starting price of $117/month, notes that a standalone one month plan is $249, a three month plan is $596, and longer commitments lower the effective rate, with a 12 month plan averaging $150/month. JoinFridays also promotes active promo codes that may reduce initial cost.

Memberships, cancellations, and commitments

Oak emphasizes no subscriptions, no membership fees, and that patients choose to renew each month without being auto charged. JoinFridays highlights no long term contracts, no membership fees, and the ability to cancel anytime. For patients prioritizing zero ongoing commitment and no auto renewal, Oak presents a non recurring monthly billing approach. For those who value the option to try a program and cancel without a long term contract, JoinFridays stresses cancel anytime flexibility.

Clinical review and care model

Oak Longevity states board certified physicians in your state review every case and that the process does not require a video call, with clinical review described as asynchronous. JoinFridays positions itself with personalized, clinician guided care and dosing guidance throughout treatment. If you prefer an asynchronous review with no video call requirement, Oak documents that model. If you want explicit clinician guided dosing and care framing, JoinFridays presents that pathway.

Promotions, effective price, and product types

Oak lists a promotional code OAKSAVE200 for $200 off a three month supply or $50 off the first month. JoinFridays lists aggressive promo codes including NEWYOU12 for up to $500 off tirzepatide and NYNY12 for up to $400 off semaglutide and notes that promo availability changes over time and should be confirmed at checkout. Both platforms offer compounded GLP 1 medications; JoinFridays also highlights options for name brand medications while noting many offerings are compounded.

Both providers state that compounded medications are not FDA approved finished products and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. JoinFridays notes promo code availability can change and the lowest tier reflects introductory or shortest term pricing.

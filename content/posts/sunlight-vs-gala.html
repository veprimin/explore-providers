How Sunlight and Gala differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences between Sunlight and Gala: starting price and pricing transparency, medication type and regulatory notes, format and included services, shipping and membership terms, and evidence cited on outcomes.

1. Price and pricing transparency

Sunlight advertises personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo and repeatedly states “Same price forever” and “No price increases ever” in its materials. Gala advertises a flat all-inclusive price covering the provider visit, medication, shipping and ongoing care and presents a $220 promotion. Gala’s captured evidence notes that the base dollar price was not visible in readable page text.

Compare Sunlight pricing details

2. Medication options and regulatory notes

Sunlight promotes “injections and oral options” and “Personalized GLP-1.” Gala offers “compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide.” Important regulatory qualifiers from the sources: Gala’s materials state that “compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.” Sunlight’s regulatory note captures that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

3. Format of care and included services

Both providers use 100 percent or fully online medical visits with telehealth. Sunlight lists “100% online medical visit — injections and oral options available” and notes prescription and telehealth are included with no insurance required. Gala lists “Provider visit & prescription included” and states provider check-ins and dose monitoring are included. Both advertise no monthly membership and cancel anytime terms in captured evidence.

4. Shipping and membership terms

Sunlight’s captured landing-page evidence repeatedly states “Fast, Free Shipping,” “Free shipping in 1-2 days,” and references free and discreet 2-day shipping in different lines. Gala’s captured evidence states “Free 2-day shipping straight to your door.” Both indicate no monthly membership and cancel anytime in the captured materials.

5. Presented outcomes and testimonials

Both providers include customer testimonial claims on their pages. Sunlight’s captured content includes multiple testimonials and weight-loss anecdotes such as “Lost 22 lbs in my first two months” and other user-reported outcomes. Gala’s materials include promotional weight-loss claims and multiple examples like “28 pounds in three months.” The sources label testimonials as marketing evidence and Gala’s notes explicitly flag that testimonials are provider marketing.

Overview comparison: Regenics vs He & She MD

This direct comparison uses only supplied facts. Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with free shipping noted, but they differ on pricing structure, clinical support model, medication formats and added therapies.

1. Pricing structure and promotional terms

Regenics lists semaglutide at $139 first month and tirzepatide at $189 first month, with renewals reported at $199 and $279 respectively. These are promotional first-month prices. He & She MD advertises semaglutide from $139/month and tirzepatide from $199/month as six-month introductory rates that renew higher after the initial six-month term. The key difference is Regenics promotes a first-month entry price while He & She MD frames its lower price as a six-month introductory rate.

2. Clinical support and program structure

Regenics: care is delivered by licensed nurse practitioners who review intake before anything is prescribed. He & She MD: every plan bundles the He & She MD app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific coaching and progress tracking at no extra cost. Regenics emphasizes nurse-practitioner-led telehealth review; He & She MD emphasizes ongoing clinician messaging and coaching built into the program.

3. Medication formats and additional therapies

Both sell compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. Regenics explicitly offers Sermorelin peptide therapy as an add-on. He & She MD lists semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection options. If oral semaglutide or a bundled coaching app matters, He & She MD documents those formats. If a secondary peptide line is important, Regenics documents Sermorelin availability.

4. Pharmacy, regulatory notes and shipping

Regenics states medications are compounded by U.S. licensed compounding pharmacies and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved as finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Regenics also notes its pharmacy network is LegitScript certified and free shipping with medication compounded in the USA. He & She MD states medications are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies but not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy or quality, and offers free expedited shipping with no membership fee. Both include regulatory qualifiers about compounded medications not being FDA-reviewed.

Price and payment options

Direct comparison: SkinnyRx lists oral semaglutide “As low as $249/mo” and mentions payment splitting with Affirm Buy Now Pay Later. Omzo lists semaglutide “Starting at $179” and tirzepatide “Starting at $279.” Both note FSA and HSA eligibility on their pages.

Medication format and dosing

SkinnyRx provides daily oral semaglutide tablets taken under the tongue, positioned as an alternative to injections. Omzo offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide primarily as one weekly injection and also connects patients to prescriptions for branded medications if the provider chooses. The format is a clear point of difference in the SkinnyRx vs Omzo comparison.

Clinical oversight, prescribing, and pharmacy

Both platforms use licensed providers and clinician review. SkinnyRx states prescriptions are available after an online consultation with a licensed provider and mentions ongoing provider access to tailor and adjust dosing. Omzo connects users to licensed U.S. providers who determine eligibility and may prescribe compounded medications or write prescriptions for branded drugs; Omzo notes that it is not a medical practice and that clinical services are provided by third party networks.

Shipping, fulfillment, and testing

SkinnyRx advertises free overnight shipping on all orders and says shipping and tracking information is provided within 2 business days after prescription processing. SkinnyRx also states partner pharmacies perform third-party testing through FDA- and DEA-compliant labs every 3 to 6 months to confirm concentration within plus or minus 10 percent for compounded lots. Omzo describes fast, discreet delivery and that its compounded drugs are prepared and shipped by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy; Omzo also notes that compounded medications offered through its platform are produced in FDA-registered facilities but are not FDA-approved.

Regulatory notes and expected results

Both SkinnyRx and Omzo include regulatory qualifiers that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not been evaluated by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. SkinnyRx states that compounded semaglutide has not been approved by the FDA for weight loss but that studies indicate potential benefits and that results may vary. Omzo similarly warns that results may vary and that eligibility and outcomes are not guaranteed.

Omzo emphasizes clinician-guided compounded GLP-1 care with weekly injections, lower starting pricing for semaglutide, and access to compounded or branded prescriptions based on provider judgment.

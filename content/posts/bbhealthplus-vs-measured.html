Head-to-head summary: Bodybuilding Health+ vs Measured

Both sell compounded GLP-1s online with provider review, but they differ on starting price, program range, shipping and membership model. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are available only when a licensed clinician determines treatment is medically appropriate.

Most distinctive differences

  • Starting price: Measured advertises compounded semaglutide from $89/mo; Bodybuilding Health+ lists semaglutide from $179/mo and tirzepatide from $209/mo.
  • Shipping and fulfillment: Measured highlights free two-day shipping; Bodybuilding Health+ notes medications are shipped to your door but does not supply a free two-day shipping claim in the provided source.
  • Program scope: Bodybuilding Health+ offers GLP-1 plus longevity and sexual performance options (NAD+, sermorelin, ED treatments); Measured pairs GLP-1 with dietitian support and unlimited messaging.
  • Commercial model: Bodybuilding Health+ stresses no monthly membership and cancel anytime, with multi-month bundles; Measured notes promotional pricing may change and also says cancel anytime.
  • Outcome and evidence: Measured cites an internal analysis of members reporting average loss at six months but that internal outcome claim is not independent evidence; Bodybuilding Health+ does not present independent average outcome claims in the supplied material.

Bodybuilding Health+ does not provide independent outcome numbers in the supplied content; its materials emphasize science-backed treatments and clinician review.

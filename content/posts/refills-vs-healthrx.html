How pricing and payment structure differ

Price is one of the clearest contrasts in this Refills vs HealthRx comparison. Refills advertises personalized compounded GLP-1 from $99/month (~$5/day) with flat pricing and the same price at every dosage. HealthRx shows a $99/month semaglutide option only on a 12-month prepaid plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) and notes the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan. Both list tirzepatide starting prices, with Refills at $159/mo and HealthRx at $179/month for tirzepatide plans.

Early verdict on money and flexibility

Refills is positioned for month-to-month flexibility with no membership or app fees. HealthRx ties its lowest per-month price to a prepaid 12-month commitment.

Shipping, handling, and how medication arrives

The two providers differ on shipping method and presentation. Refills offers free discreet shipping and expresses that customers receive shipping and tracking information in two business days after prescription processing. HealthRx advertises overnight cold shipping with tracked, free cold chain delivery on every plan. HealthRx explicitly states cold chain shipping and overnight shipment on plans.

Regulatory status and compounded medication notes

Both providers offer compounded medications and include regulatory qualifiers. Refills states compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality by the FDA. HealthRx states compounded medication is not FDA-approved and is prepared by a licensed pharmacy for an individual patient with a valid prescription. Refills also notes its compounding pharmacies are LegiScript-certified U.S. compounding pharmacies with third-party USP testing and that compounded medications may be prescribed if deemed medically appropriate by partner clinicians.

Clinical review, dosing, and support

Both services require clinician review before treatment. Refills describes a health intake quiz followed by a licensed clinician review; depending on needs, you may have a chat with a doctor or receive a prescription directly within 24 hours, plus unlimited access to patient care team and clinicians at no extra cost. HealthRx emphasizes that the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is right or whether neither is, and highlights that the plan price does not change when your dose does.

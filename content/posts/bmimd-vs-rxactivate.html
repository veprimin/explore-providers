How bmiMD and RxActivate differ on pricing transparency

bmiMD publishes tirzepatide rates by billing commitment, with prices listed as $179 month to month; $159 per month for a three month plan; $149 per month for a six month plan; and $139 per month for a 12 month plan. RxActivate does not display any price, medication fee, membership fee, shipping charge, cancellation term, or billing commitment before the intake gate where contact information is requested.

Billing options and payment commitments

bmiMD offers four billing options and specifies the lowest monthly equivalent requires a 12 month commitment. The provider notes a total prepaid charge should be confirmed. RxActivate requires completion of the medical eligibility questionnaire and contact information before revealing plan or price, so billing options and commitments are not shown upfront.

Shipping, refills, and clinician access

bmiMD advertises free subscription shipping on every order, online refills and delivery, and 24/7 clinician access. RxActivate emphasizes a clinician-matched plan and state and medical safety screening through its eligibility flow, but shipping and refill terms are not disclosed before the contact-information step.

Regulatory and compounded medication notes

Both sources state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. bmiMD explicitly warns that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. RxActivate includes a similar regulatory caution that medication specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Eligibility flow and state availability

RxActivate uses a two minute eligibility questionnaire requesting goal, height, weight, target weight, timeline, medication history, sex assigned at birth, date of birth, state and safety screening and displayed likely eligibility before requiring first name, last name, email and phone number. The synthetic test confirmed Alabama service availability. bmiMD requires clinician review to determine appropriateness and emphasizes confirmation of eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

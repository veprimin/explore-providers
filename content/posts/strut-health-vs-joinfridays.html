How they differ at a glance: price and medication options

Both platforms offer clinician-guided telehealth, but their positioning and starting prices differ. Strut Health advertises oral GLP-1 options and lists pricing from $199 per month. JoinFridays advertises compounded GLP-1 options starting at $117 per month and explicitly offers both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide on one platform.

Five direct differences that matter

1) Medication focus and availability

Strut Health specializes in oral-based program options and highlights oral semaglutide lozenges and oral specialization as a core feature. JoinFridays provides both compounded tirzepatide and compounded semaglutide, calling tirzepatide the most effective and semaglutide the most affordable on its platform.

2) Starting price and pricing structure

Strut Health lists “From $199 per Month.” JoinFridays starts at $117/month and also documents multiple plan structures and effective monthly averages for longer commitments. The source states JoinFridays has the lowest starting price in its tier at $117/month and multi-month plans can lower the effective rate.

3) Discounts, promos, and fees

JoinFridays advertises active promo codes with examples including NEWYOU12 and NYNY12 and describes aggressive promo codes up to specific dollar amounts. Strut Health’s landing evidence indicates “No Hidden Fees” and free shipping, but no promo-code examples are provided in the supplied facts.

4) Care model and specialty

Both platforms emphasize clinician guidance. Strut Health stresses guided consultations and ongoing progress communication with an oral specialization. JoinFridays emphasizes personalized, clinician-guided care across compounds and highlights no long-term contracts and a cancel-anytime structure.

5) Compounded medication regulatory note

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 options in some form and the supplied facts state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Strut Health explicitly notes compounded semaglutide is prepared pursuant to an individualized prescription and is not FDA-approved. JoinFridays similarly states compounded medication offerings are not FDA-approved.

Conclusion: Choose based on what matters to you

Strut Health vs JoinFridays: choose based on delivery form and price. Strut Health emphasizes oral semaglutide specialization, guided consults, and free shipping starting from $199 per month. JoinFridays emphasizes lower starting pricing at $117/month, access to both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide, active promo codes, and cancel-anytime flexibility.

Choose Strut Health if you want an oral-first program with guided consults and free shipping and are comfortable with the listed starting price.

Choose JoinFridays if you want the lowest documented starting price, access to compounded tirzepatide as well as semaglutide, and aggressive promo-code options with flexible cancellation.

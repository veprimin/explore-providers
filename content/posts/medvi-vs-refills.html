How they differ: price model, medication choices, support, and process

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences between MedVi and Refills: pricing approach, available medications, clinical process and visit requirements, provider support, and regulatory notes about compounded drugs. Each point below contrasts both providers directly using only the supplied facts.

1. Pricing model and introductory offers

MedVi: Promotes a winter promotional first-month price of $179 including fast, free shipping, but notes the ongoing standalone month is $299 and a three-month plan is $649 (about $216/month). The source emphasizes the first-month promo and that ongoing pricing is substantially higher.

Refills: Offers flat pricing from $99/month (~$5/day) with the same price at every dosage and no membership or app fees. Refills states month-to-month plans with no contracts and the ability to cancel anytime.

2. Medication options and dosing

MedVi: Offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only. The source notes MedVi provides compounded semaglutide and does not list tirzepatide or branded options available through their basic offer.

Refills: Offers personalized compounded GLP-1s from $99/mo and specifically lists semaglutide at $99/mo and tirzepatide at $159/mo, plus a GIP+GLP-1 dual agonist available. Refills highlights the same price at every dosage.

3. Clinical process and visit requirements

MedVi: Emphasizes a simple online intake with no clinic visits required. A licensed clinician reviews the assessment after checkout and determines prescription eligibility. The assessment does not create a doctor patient relationship by itself until clinician review.

Refills: Uses an intake quiz where a licensed clinician will review information. Depending on needs, you may chat with a doctor or receive a prescription directly within 24 hours. Refills supplies a 4-week supply each month and requires a refill form at the end of each month.

4. Support, shipping, and convenience

MedVi: Promotes 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and fast, free shipping included in the promotional price. MedVi positions itself as allowing patients to start with no in-person visits and to receive medication shipped to their door.

Refills: Promotes free discreet shipping and free 24/7 health concierge support. Refills highlights express shipping, monthly tracking updates, and unlimited clinician access at no extra cost for questions about progress, side effects, or dosage.

5. Regulatory and pharmacy notes

Both providers: The supplied sources state compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. MedVi notes its compounded GLP-1s are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but are not FDA evaluated. Refills notes compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality by the FDA. Refills also states its compounding pharmacies are licensed and inspected and uses partner clinicians to determine appropriateness.

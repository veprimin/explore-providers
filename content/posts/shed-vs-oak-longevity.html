Quick verdict: Shed vs Oak Longevity

The key difference is focus: Shed combines name-brand and compounded GLP-1 options with integrated coaching, while Oak Longevity emphasizes lower flat pricing on compounded GLP-1 and no subscription. Early verdict: choose Shed if you want name-brand choices plus continuous coaching; choose Oak Longevity if you want the lowest compounded prices and no membership commitment.

How pricing and plans compare

Shed lists compounded semaglutide injections starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month, and advertises 10% off your first month. Shed does not publish standard pricing for name-brand options online; you receive a personalized quote after intake. Oak Longevity advertises semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month with one flat price for all dosages, billed monthly on a non recurring basis and no subscription required.

Medication types and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications. Shed explicitly offers both FDA approved name-brand GLP-1 and compounded options on one platform. Oak Longevity focuses on compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide with one price covering all dosages. Both sites note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies.

Care model: coaching, approval, and clinician review

Shed integrates continuous coaching into treatment and promotes a 2-minute approval assessment followed by clinician review before prescriptions are issued. Oak Longevity includes free health coaching with every plan, free shipping, and states that board certified physicians in your state review each case; Oak says no video call or insurance is required and approval can be same day for qualifying patients.

Access, shipping, and membership commitments

Oak advertises free shipping with tracking, no subscriptions, no membership fees, and billing on a non recurring monthly basis so you choose to renew each month. Oak also notes three month prescriptions are billed upfront and no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped. Shed offers medication shipped to your home after clinician review and markets a Shed Membership option for additional services; Shed bundles coaching into monthly cost rather than charging it separately.

Final comparison and how to choose

Both platforms sell compounded GLP-1, but they prioritize different trade offs. Oak Longevity emphasizes the lowest flat prices, one price for all dosages, no subscriptions, free shipping, and free coaching. Shed emphasizes offering both name-brand and compounded GLP-1 plus integrated continuous coaching and a quick 2-minute assessment, with a personalized quote for name-brand options and 10% off the first month.

Choose Shed if you want name-brand GLP-1 availability plus integrated, ongoing coaching and a quick eligibility assessment.

Choose Oak Longevity if you want the most affordable compounded GLP-1 options, flat pricing across dosages, free shipping and no subscription commitment.

1. Pricing structure and promo clarity

MedVi advertises a winter promotional first month at $179 including fast, free shipping, then notes a standalone month at $299 and a three-month plan at $649. HealthRx advertises a lower entry price of $99/month only on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan and states tirzepatide plans start at $179/month. HealthRx explicitly ties its lowest advertised monthly price to a longer prepaid commitment. MedVi highlights a promotional first-month rate and warns ongoing price is higher after the promo.

2. Medication options and clinician discretion

MedVi focuses on clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only. HealthRx offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription, and states the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is the right fit, or whether neither is. If having tirzepatide as an option matters to you, HealthRx is the supported choice; if you want semaglutide specifically and a single-product offering, MedVi is the provider in this comparison that lists semaglutide only.

3. Shipping and handling differences

MedVi includes fast, free shipping in its promotional first month. HealthRx emphasizes overnight cold shipping with cold chain tracking and free shipping on every plan. HealthRx makes the cold chain explicit; MedVi indicates fast, free shipping as part of the promo but does not capture an explicit cold chain statement in the supplied facts.

4. Support, process, and clinician review

MedVi promotes a simple online intake with no clinic visits required and 24/7 support from certified medical professionals. HealthRx also uses clinician review to determine eligibility and emphasizes dose adjustments and that plan price does not change when your dose does. Both platforms require a clinician to decide medical appropriateness. MedVi highlights continuous support availability; HealthRx highlights clinician decision making about medication choice and stable pricing across dose changes.

5. Safety, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both sources state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HealthRx adds that compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom. MedVi notes its compounded semaglutide is produced in FDA-regulated facilities but emphasizes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety, efficacy, or quality and that the decision to use compounded drugs is guided by licensed providers subject to clinician review.

How to decide: choose based on price, meds, or shipping

Choose MedVi if you want a simple, no-clinic process, 24/7 certified medical support, and a promotional first-month price of $179 with fast, free shipping. Choose HealthRx if you need the option of tirzepatide versus semaglutide decided by a clinician, want the lowest advertised monthly price via a 12-month prepaid plan from $99/month, and prefer explicit overnight cold shipping and cold chain tracking.

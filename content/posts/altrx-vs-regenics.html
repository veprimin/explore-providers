2. Who reviews and prescribes treatment

3. Pharmacy compounding and regulatory note

Both altRx and Regenics rely on compounded medications prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies. The altRx source identifies state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies in its network. Regenics notes medications are compounded by licensed U.S. pharmacies and calls out a LegitScript certified pharmacy network. Both sources explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

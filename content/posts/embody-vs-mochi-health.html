How they differ at a glance

This comparison uses only the supplied facts about pricing, visit style, shipping, medication type, and membership structure. Below are the most distinctive supported differences organized into clear sections.

1. Pricing model and entry cost

Embody advertises compounded semaglutide at $69/month and compounded tirzepatide at $119/month, with that $69 semaglutide price described as cut from $299 and labeled a limited-time offer. Mochi Health uses a membership-plus-medication structure with standard pricing from $99/month and promotes 50% off your first month with code BESTWLHALFOFF. Mochi also cites compounded semaglutide starting $99 and promotional rates lowering it to $60 for new pricing examples.

2. Clinical access: live video versus asynchronous review

Embody offers licensed provider review handled online; the documentation indicates telemed visits are included but does not claim live video visits. Mochi Health distinguishes itself by offering live video visits with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes, which the source calls a fundamentally different clinical experience compared with asynchronous intake questionnaires used by many competitors.

3. Shipping and fulfillment speed

Embody states free shipping with delivery in 1–2 days after clinician approval, described as one of the faster delivery commitments in the GLP-1 telehealth space. Mochi Health indicates doctor-prescribed treatments are shipped to your door but does not provide the same 1–2 day delivery claim in the supplied evidence.

4. Medication types and regulatory note

Both platforms focus on compounded medications in the supplied material. Embody explicitly lists compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and notes the regulatory caution that compounded medications are not FDA approved finished products. Mochi Health lists compounded semaglutide injections, compounded tirzepatide, a compounded oral semaglutide pill, and other compounded options, and includes the same regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

5. Membership, features, and additional services

Embody emphasizes no monthly membership, cancel anytime, and flat locked in monthly pricing for medications, plus 24/7 support access for dosing and side-effect questions. Mochi Health operates on a membership-plus-medication model, offers 24/7 support and ongoing appointment availability, and promotes additional service areas such as dermatology and hair care. Mochi notes that its membership pricing and terms may vary by state and that the low promotional rates apply to first month only in some cases.

## Yucca Health Review — Compounded Semaglutide and Tirzepatide by Telehealth

Yucca Health, which now markets itself as TryYucca, is a direct-to-patient telehealth platform that prescribes **compounded GLP-1 medication** for weight management. It is one of the more visible low-cost entrants in a crowded category, and it is the highest-traffic weight-loss URL we cover — so it is worth being precise about what it actually is, and what it is not.

## About Yucca Health

Yucca is not a pharmacy and it is not a manufacturer. It is a platform that connects you to a licensed provider who can prescribe **compounded semaglutide** (the active ingredient in Ozempic and Wegovy) or **compounded tirzepatide** (the active ingredient in Mounjaro and Zepbound), which is then filled by a U.S. compounding pharmacy and shipped to you. The entire flow is online: there is no clinic visit, and no insurance is billed.

The distinction that matters most here is *compounded* versus *branded*. Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide use the same active molecules as their branded counterparts, but they are not FDA-approved finished products. They are prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies under pharmacy-board oversight. That is a legitimate, widely used route to GLP-1 therapy, but it carries a different regulatory status than a branded pen, and any honest review has to say so plainly.

## How It Works

1. **Online intake.** You complete a medical questionnaire covering weight history, current medications, and relevant conditions. It takes roughly ten minutes.
2. **Clinician review.** A licensed provider reviews your submission and decides whether GLP-1 therapy is appropriate, and if so, which medication and starting dose.
3. **Pharmacy fulfilment.** If approved, the prescription is sent to a licensed U.S. compounding pharmacy.
4. **Shipping.** Medication is shipped to your door, typically via an expedited service, so most patients receive their first order within a few business days of approval.
5. **Ongoing care.** Dose adjustments and follow-up are handled through the platform as your treatment progresses.

Because tirzepatide activates both the GIP and GLP-1 receptors, it is associated with higher average weight loss in clinical trials than semaglutide alone. The [National Institutes of Health](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9486455/) has published trial data on tirzepatide showing mean weight reductions well into the double digits, which is why platforms that offer both let the clinician match the medication to the patient.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Yucca Health a legitimate GLP-1 provider?

Yes. Yucca is a licensed telehealth platform that prescribes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide through a clinician review, filled at licensed U.S. compounding pharmacies. Compounded GLP-1 medications are not individually FDA-approved finished products, but they use the same active ingredients as the branded versions and are prepared under state pharmacy-board oversight.

### What is the difference between Yucca Health and TryYucca?

TryYucca is the current brand name for the platform previously known as Yucca Health. The program — personalized compounded GLP-1 treatment with online approval and home delivery — is the same.

### Does Yucca offer tirzepatide?

Yes. Alongside compounded semaglutide, Yucca offers compounded tirzepatide, which activates both GIP and GLP-1 receptors and is associated with higher average weight loss than semaglutide alone.

### Is compounded semaglutide as effective as Ozempic or Wegovy?

Compounded semaglutide uses the same active ingredient at clinically equivalent dosing. The difference is the manufacturing source: branded semaglutide is made by Novo Nordisk in FDA-regulated facilities, while compounded semaglutide is prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies. The FDA does not individually evaluate compounded medications.

## Verdict

Yucca is a credible, accessible compounded-GLP-1 platform whose real strengths are payment flexibility and offering both semaglutide and tirzepatide in one place. Its limitations are inherent to its model: compounded-only medication, no insurance, and no outcome guarantee. For a patient who understands the compounded route and wants an affordable, flexible on-ramp to GLP-1 therapy, it is one of the stronger options — provided you verify the current pricing and renewal terms yourself before ordering.

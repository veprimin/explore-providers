Quick comparison: Maximus vs JoinSkinny

The Maximus vs JoinSkinny choice comes down to price and plan structure. Quick verdict: Maximus is the lower flat-rate compounded semaglutide option at $132.99 per month with telemedicine and U.S. pharmacy fulfillment, while JoinSkinny starts at $149 and emphasizes flat-rate dosing, multi-month bundles, and temperature-controlled next-day shipping when eligible.

How they differ: five key comparison points

1) Price and billing model

Maximus advertises a low flat rate at $132.99 per month for compounded semaglutide with a direct-pay model and no insurance billing. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149, offers monthly and 3, 6, and 12 month bundles, and notes a $140 promotional discount in captured marketing. JoinSkinny also mentions installment options with Afterpay, Klarna, and Affirm.

View JoinSkinny plans and bundles

2) Medication type and pharmacy sourcing

Maximus provides compounded semaglutide filled through trusted U.S. pharmacies and notes compounded medications are not FDA approved. JoinSkinny works with licensed U.S. pharmacies that fill GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP products, states partners meet FDA regulations, and holds LegitScript certification when applicable. Both sources include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA approved.

3) Shipping speed and temperature control

Maximus highlights free priority or expedited shipping in captured landing page text. JoinSkinny specifies free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, with next-day UPS in a temperature controlled package and tracking for every shipment. JoinSkinny also notes weekend handling rules for temperature management.

Check Maximus shipping details

4) Care model, monitoring, and support

Both providers operate via telemedicine with prescriptions approved by licensed doctors. Maximus emphasizes convenient telemedicine access, no clinic visits, and prescriptions approved by licensed doctors. JoinSkinny describes clinician-reviewed telemedicine, monthly check ins, dose adjustments, ongoing support, and 24/7 chat. JoinSkinny explicitly notes HIPAA protection for patient information.

5) Plans, flexibility, and eligibility

Maximus promotes a straightforward direct-pay model with no membership fees and availability in all 50 states except Mississippi. Maximus also allows providers to work with documented previous GLP-1 usage for custom titration, except for certain prepackaged starter packs. JoinSkinny uses transparent flat-rate pricing that stays the same regardless of dosage increases, allows cancellation before the next billing cycle, and determines state eligibility dynamically during signup where regulations allow telehealth-based prescribing.

Eden Health vs FeelGood Meds: overview and early verdict

Both companies offer clinician-reviewed online GLP-1 weight-management treatment, but they differ in advertised starting prices, whether medications are branded or compounded, and how each frames guarantees and shipping. Early verdict: Eden lists Semaglutide at $99/mo and Tirzepatide at $129/mo with a lifetime price guarantee and free expedited shipping. FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections from $149/mo, compounded tablets from $249/mo, a branded option from $1,999/mo, free shipping, and a weight loss money back guarantee.

Key differences compared directly

1. Advertised starting price and price guarantees

Eden Health: Monthly pricing at every dose with Semaglutide at $99 and Tirzepatide at $129. Eden emphasizes one monthly price and a lifetime price guarantee. FeelGood Meds: Compounded injections start at $149/mo, compounded tablets start at $249/mo, and a branded option is shown from $1,999/mo. FeelGood Meds promotes starting prices and a weight loss money back guarantee.

See Eden’s $99 and $129 plans

2. Branded versus compounded medication options

FeelGood Meds explicitly offers compounded injections and compounded tablets as options and also shows a branded medication option from $1,999/mo. The provider notes that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Eden markets Semaglutide and Tirzepatide programs but includes the regulatory reminder that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

3. Shipping, membership and extra terms

Eden states free expedited shipping, no insurance required, and no membership fees. FeelGood Meds lists free shipping, no membership fee, and no insurance required. Both recommend confirming eligibility, medication specifics, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment. FeelGood Meds also highlights a weight loss money back guarantee in its captured material.

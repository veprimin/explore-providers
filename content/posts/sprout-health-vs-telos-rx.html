Sprout Health vs Telos Rx: what the providers offer

This comparison of Sprout Health vs Telos Rx focuses on the differences the sources highlight: pricing and discounts, speed and onboarding, medication options, outcome framing and regulatory notes. Both providers prescribe compounded GLP-1 medications only after a clinician review and state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Distinctive difference 1 — Pricing and introductory offers

Sprout Health advertises a $50 off first month promotion, bringing the initial cost to $199, with an ongoing standard price of $249/month after the discounted first month. Telos Rx lists semaglutide as low as $99 per month and tirzepatide as low as $139 per month, and states “Pay $0 if not approved” in some captured messaging. Telos also notes prices like $299 and $399 in captured rows, and says your price depends on your plan.

Check Telos Rx pricing for semaglutide and tirzepatide

Distinctive difference 2 — Speed, onboarding and approval

Sprout Health emphasizes a lightning-quick approval process and a fully online, fast-entry experience for patients who want minimal onboarding friction. Telos Rx materials capture that medications are prescribed online after review by a US-licensed provider; Telos advises confirming eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

Distinctive difference 3 — Medication options and molecules

Sprout Health prescribes compounded semaglutide and markets outcome framing tied to semaglutide-type GLP-1 results. Telos Rx explicitly offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, noting tirzepatide acts on GLP-1 and GIP together and that a US-licensed provider decides which is right for you.

Distinctive difference 4 — Outcome framing and guarantees

Sprout Health frames outcomes around a 10–26% body weight reduction over 12 months but does not offer a published outcome guarantee. Telos Rx states individual results vary and are not guaranteed. Both sources preserve that outcomes vary with adherence and other factors.

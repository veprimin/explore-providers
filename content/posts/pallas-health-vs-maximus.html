Telemedicine access, state rules, and visit requirements

Both platforms use online clinician review and telemedicine. Pallas positions itself as 100 percent online and asynchronous in most states, noting that 43 of 50 states need no video visit while a video visit is required in seven states plus Washington D.C. Maximus emphasizes convenient telemedicine access with no clinic visits and says its prescription approvals are handled by licensed doctors; Maximus notes availability in all 50 states except Mississippi for its Weight Loss Protocol.

What plan price includes and care model

Pallas states its plan price includes provider care, ongoing check-ins and care-team messaging with no membership fee and free expedited shipping. Pallas also notes self-serve portal features and a free companion app. Maximus highlights prescriptions approved by licensed doctors, medication filled through trusted U.S. pharmacies, and free priority shipping; it promotes a simple direct-pay model and transparent monthly pricing.

Regulatory and refund notes

Both sources include the regulatory caution that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Pallas notes it does not manufacture or compound medication and that compounded medications are prepared by U.S. compounding pharmacies and may differ in appearance from product images. Maximus says if you are not approved for treatment your medication costs will be fully refunded.

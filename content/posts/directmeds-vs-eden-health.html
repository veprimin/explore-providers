Directmeds vs Eden Health: which telehealth GLP-1 program fits you? Early verdict: Eden Health advertises lower listed monthly prices and a lifetime price guarantee, while Direct Meds emphasizes compounded injectable and sublingual options, free next-day shipping, and clinician and nursing support.

See Direct Meds offers and details

How pricing and guarantees compare

Price is the clearest difference in the Directmeds vs Eden Health comparison. Eden Health lists monthly pricing at every dose: semaglutide $99 and tirzepatide $129 with a stated lifetime price guarantee. Direct Meds advertises treatment starting at $147 and lists standard FAQ prices of semaglutide $297/month and tirzepatide $399/month; Direct Meds also promoted a summer promotion that saves $150.

Medication formats, compounding and regulatory notes

Direct Meds offers clinician-reviewed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats dispensed from U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. The source emphasizes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Eden Health materials note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Treatment at both providers is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Supported difference: Direct Meds offers compounded sublingual option

Direct Meds explicitly lists injectable and sublingual compounded options and U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. Eden Health does not list sublingual formulations in the captured details.

Shipping, membership and cancellation

Both providers include shipping, but wording differs. Direct Meds advertises free temperature-controlled UPS or FedEx next-day shipping and includes medication, clinician review, telehealth visits, supplies and free next-day UPS/FedEx shipping. Direct Meds states no monthly membership fee, no hidden fees, month-to-month cancellation for customers, and subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term. Eden Health advertises free expedited shipping and no membership fees, and promotes a single monthly price for medication, care, and support under one clear monthly price.

Support and refund policies

Direct Meds highlights ongoing clinician and nursing support, and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Eden Health frames its offering as a guided GLP-1 program that brings medication, care, and support together under one monthly price, but details on refunds or clinician and nursing support levels were not captured in the source.

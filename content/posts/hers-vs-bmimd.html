Quick verdict: Hers vs bmiMD

The primary difference in this Hers vs bmiMD comparison is medication type and pricing model. Hers offers FDA-approved Wegovy® (brand-name semaglutide) from $149/mo with a personalized care team and ongoing dose check-ins, while bmiMD offers compounded tirzepatide as low as $139/mo with multi-month pricing and free shipping. Hers requires an active membership to access medication; bmiMD emphasizes subscription plans and free shipping. This review compares those distinctions so you can decide which fits your priorities.

Medication type and regulatory notes

Hers provides FDA-approved, brand-name Wegovy® and states it does not offer compounded medications. The provider materials note that Wegovy® and Zepbound® are FDA-approved for weight loss, and that Ozempic® is FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes but may be prescribed off-label for weight loss at a clinician’s discretion. bmiMD offers compounded tirzepatide. The bmiMD material includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Those are the core regulatory differences between the two services.

Pricing structure and commitments

Hers lists pricing examples such as Wegovy® pill from $149/mo and injectable options from $199/mo. Hers also indicates that an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required and that membership and medication are billed separately, with membership details noted in provider material. bmiMD lists tiered pricing for compounded tirzepatide with four billing options: $179 month-to-month, $159/mo for three months, $149/mo for six months, and $139/mo for a 12-month plan, and free shipping on every subscription order. bmiMD notes that the lowest price requires a 12-month commitment and that total prepaid charges should be confirmed.

Care model: clinician access and dose management

Hers highlights a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins as part of its offering. bmiMD highlights clinician-reviewed online treatment and 24/7 clinician access and online refills and delivery. Both emphasize clinician involvement and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Shipping, fees, and logistics

bmiMD advertises free subscription shipping on every order and online refills and delivery. Hers materials note pricing examples and membership requirements and do not present free shipping claims in the captured content. Both providers state prescriptions are required and that availability may vary by state according to the captured material.

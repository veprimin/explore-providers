Product types and clinical oversight

Both providers offer compounded medications. Trimi specifies compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide prescribed by US-licensed clinicians and supplied from named state-licensed 503A pharmacies with certificates of analysis available on request. Trimi also names a medical director and Arora Health as its clinical network. FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections and tablets plus a branded medication option without the same named clinical network in the supplied evidence. Both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Shipping, refunds, and guarantees

Trimi highlights free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and a 6-month money-back promise where you can request a refund if you do not lose weight after following the plan for 6 months, terms apply. Trimi also states that if a plan is not approved by their medical team there is a 100% automatic refund. FeelGood Meds highlights free shipping, a Weight Loss Money Back Guarantee, and emphasizes no membership or hidden fees. Details and terms for FeelGood Meds guarantees are subject to the live offer per the supplied evidence.

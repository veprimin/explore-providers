How Measured and Gala differ at a glance

The clearest differences are pricing presentation, whether the provider visit is explicitly included, claims about shipping and timing, and how outcome claims are framed. Below we compare these points directly using only the supplied evidence.

Pricing structure and visible starting prices

Measured advertises promotional compounded semaglutide from $89/mo and compounded tirzepatide from $189/mo. Gala advertises a $220 promotion and a flat all-inclusive price covering provider visit, medication, shipping and ongoing care, though Gala’s base dollar price was not visible in the readable page text. Both state no insurance is required.

Prescription and visit handling

Measured lists same-day Rx decisions and unlimited messaging with dietitian support. Gala states provider visit and prescription are included as part of its direct-pay model and offers provider check-ins and dose monitoring. Both note treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Shipping and cancellations

Both providers claim free two-day shipping. Measured highlights free 2-day shipping and cancel anytime language. Gala likewise advertises free 2-day shipping, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime. Both caution that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Outcome and marketing claims

Measured presents an internal analysis reporting an average loss of 31 lbs at 6 months among members who reported weight data, and includes language that individual results vary and are not guaranteed. Gala advertises various testimonial-style weight loss figures such as 28 pounds in three months but the page frames testimonials as provider marketing. The evidence supplied does not include independent clinical verification for either provider.

Measured vs Gala: final take

Measured vs Gala is primarily a choice between visible promotional starting prices and same-day Rx plus dietitian messaging, versus a flat all-in price that includes the provider visit and ongoing check-ins plus a $220 promotion. Both offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, free two-day shipping, and state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Choose Measured if you want the lower advertised starting price and same-day prescription decisions with dietitian support. Choose Gala if you prefer a flat all-inclusive price that explicitly covers the provider visit and prescription and a $220 promotion.

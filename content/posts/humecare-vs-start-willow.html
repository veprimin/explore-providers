HumeCare+ sells a protocol, not just a prescription: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide combined with glycine and vitamins B12 and B6, plus monthly body-composition scans and an app for medication tracking and food logging. 3. Delivery speed, insurance and model

Start Willow emphasizes a direct-pay model with no insurance required, no membership fees, and a delivery guarantee in 3 days or less. HumeCare+ highlights free next-day delivery and clinician support but operates as a cash-pay program with promotional pricing noted in the source. Start Willow positions its no-insurance approach as an advantage for patients who face insurance denials or prior-authorization delays.

4. Clinician access and support promises

HumeCare+ advertises unlimited clinician access and a U.S. care team available 24/7, an app for tracking, and continuous physician optimization as part of the protocol. Start Willow promotes personalized physician review during the online intake and written and video injection instructions plus care-team support, and highlights responsiveness in testimonials. Both require a licensed clinician to determine appropriate treatment.

5. Guarantees and limitations

HumeCare+ advertises a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months with terms and eligibility that must be verified, and it cautions that compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated. Start Willow does not advertise a weight-loss refund guarantee in the supplied evidence but does state fast shipping and direct-pay convenience. Both sources repeat the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are prepared under state board oversight.

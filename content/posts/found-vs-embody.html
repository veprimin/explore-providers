Found vs Embody answers a common choice: a personalized, biology-first weight loss program that accepts most insurance versus a lean, low-cost GLP-1 telehealth option with fast delivery. Early verdict: choose Found if you want a medically supervised, biology-based plan that can work with insurance; choose Embody if you prioritize the lowest cash price for compounded semaglutide and fast shipping.

How Found and Embody differ at a glance

Both platforms provide telehealth-prescribed GLP-1 options, but they take different approaches. Found emphasizes personalized programs built around metabolic biology and accepts insurance for many patients. Embody emphasizes low, flat cash pricing for compounded GLP-1s, fast free shipping, and no insurance requirement.

Distinctive differences to weigh

1. Care model and personalization

Found: Offers a personalized medically supervised weight loss program that starts with a medical evaluation to tailor treatment to hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavior. The provider describes a holistic whole-body approach rather than prescribing a single medication protocol for all patients.

Embody: Focuses on a prescription-forward, no-frills model. It provides clinician review and telemed visits for compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide but emphasizes straightforward medication delivery without integrated coaching or advanced monitoring features.

2. Pricing structure and insurance

Found: Advertises starting options as low as $39 and notes most insurance is now accepted. Found handles insurance paperwork and markets having options to use insurance benefits to save on compound GLP-1s for some patients. Pricing and coverage vary by insurance and may require commitments for advertised branded GLP-1 pricing.

Embody: Promotes a flat cash price of $69/month for compounded semaglutide (reduced from $299) and $119/month for compounded tirzepatide, with no insurance required. Embody advertises no membership and cancel anytime flexibility. Final pricing may vary by dose and clinician recommendation, and the $69 price is noted as a limited-time offer that may change.

3. Medication types and regulatory notes

Found: Offers both compounded and branded GLP-1 options including semaglutide and tirzepatide and references branded GLP-1s such as Wegovy and Zepbound with specific offer terms. The provider explicitly states that certain medications are FDA-approved for conditions other than weight and that compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Embody: Offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide only. Embody notes its compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and that prescriptions follow a licensed provider review with pharmacy fulfillment. Embody does not offer branded Ozempic, Wegovy, or Zepbound.

4. Delivery, membership, and speed

Found: Emphasizes membership plans and handling insurance paperwork. Found advertising shows membership starting points and mentions options tied to insurance commitments for certain prices. Specific delivery timing was not supplied in the captured Found evidence.

Embody: Promises free shipping with delivery in 1–2 days after clinician approval and highlights no monthly membership and cancel anytime policies. Embody markets fast delivery as one of its features.

Bottom line: Found vs Embody

Embody focuses on aggressive cash pricing for compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, plus free 1–2 day shipping and no requirement to use insurance.

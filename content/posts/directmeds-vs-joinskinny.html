1. Pricing and promotions

Directmeds promotes a starting price of $147 and the source captures that a summer promotion saves $150. The same source also records Directmeds FAQ standard pricing as $297 per month for semaglutide and $399 per month for tirzepatide. JoinSkinny advertises plans starting at $149 with a captured promotional $140 off and offers monthly and 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles. Both say no separate monthly membership fee. The comparison shows Directmeds highlights a promotional headline that differs from its FAQ standard prices while JoinSkinny emphasizes flat-rate dosing and bundle options.

2. Medication type, pharmacy and regulatory notes

Directmeds offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats prepared by U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies, and the source explicitly states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and works with licensed, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners to fill prescriptions when appropriate. The JoinSkinny source also notes that its compounds contain the same active molecules used in brand-name medications when prescribed. Both sources include regulatory notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved when applicable.

3. Shipping, availability and state limitations

Both providers advertise free temperature-controlled shipping and next-day or 1-2 day delivery. Directmeds specifically states free next-day UPS/FedEx shipping and notes service is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. JoinSkinny states prescriptions received before 2 pm central ship same day with next-day UPS in temperature-controlled packaging and mentions availability depends on state eligibility during signup. If state availability matters to you, JoinSkinny confirms eligibility during signup while Directmeds lists two states where it is unavailable.

4. Ongoing care, prescriptions and cancellation

Directmeds includes clinician review, telehealth visits, supplies and shipping in its offering and highlights ongoing clinician and nursing support. JoinSkinny describes licensed U.S. providers who review questionnaires, prescribe when appropriate, and provide ongoing support, dose adjustments, monthly check-ins and 24/7 chat. Cancellation terms differ in presentation: Directmeds offers month-to-month cancellation with subscribe-and-save plans that require the stated term; JoinSkinny says you can cancel anytime before the next billing cycle and offers multi-month plans that require bundle commitments.

Conclusion and recommendation

Directmeds vs JoinSkinny comes down to whether you prefer compounded, injectable or sublingual GLP-1 options with promotional pricing and no membership fee, or a flat-rate plan with licensed pharmacy fulfillment, branded GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options plus same-day shipping when eligible. Both advertise free temperature-controlled shipping and clinician review, and both include regulatory notes about compounded medications when applicable.

Choose Directmeds if you want compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide formats, a promotional starting price headline of $147, no separate monthly membership fee, month-to-month cancellation, and free next-day UPS/FedEx shipping. Choose JoinSkinny if you prefer flat-rate pricing that stays the same with dose increases, branded GLP-1 or GLP-1+GIP options filled by FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacies, same-day shipping for orders before 2 pm central, and multi-month bundle options.

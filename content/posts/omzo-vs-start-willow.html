Clinician involvement and prescription model

Omzo connects users with licensed U.S. providers and emphasizes clinician-guided plans reviewed by clinicians who determine eligibility and final prescriptions. Start Willow also prescribes after an online clinician review but highlights a direct-pay model that requires no insurance and no membership fees.

Medication types and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and reference tirzepatide options. Each source includes the regulatory caution that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Omzo explicitly states its compounded drugs are prepared by a state licensed sterile compounding pharmacy and that branded prescriptions may be written but must be filled by the patient at a pharmacy of their choice. Start Willow states compounded semaglutide uses the same active ingredients as branded versions but is not FDA approved and is prepared by state licensed compounding pharmacies operating under state board oversight.

Delivery and shipping commitments

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less from its licensed pharmacy network and highlights same day prescriptions and free 2 day shipping in captured landing page evidence. Omzo notes fast, discreet delivery and that medication costs are included in fees collected by Omzo and paid to pharmacies, but no firm guaranteed delivery window is supplied in the source evidence.

Insurance, membership, and access

Start Willow markets itself as requiring no insurance and charging no membership fees, positioning its direct-pay model as a benefit for patients frustrated by prior authorization denials. Omzo does not sell branded medications and states that if a branded medication is prescribed the patient must fill it and pay associated costs; Omzo also recommends confirming eligibility, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

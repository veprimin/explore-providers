How Refills and Telos Rx differ at a glance

Both providers advertise compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options and both state compounded medications are not FDA-approved. The most distinctive differences supported by the sources are pricing model and transparency, bundled clinician and support features, explicit price points for tirzepatide, state and approval caveats, and membership or fee terms. Below we compare those points directly.

Pricing model and starting price

Refills: GLP-1 from $99/mo (~$5/day) with flat pricing, the same price at every dosage. Refills lists tirzepatide at $159/mo in the captured evidence.

Telos Rx: Semaglutide as low as $99 per month; tirzepatide as low as $139 per month. Telos Rx emphasizes prices are “as low as” and that final price depends on approval by a US-licensed provider and plan details.

Clinician review and prescription process

Refills: Once you complete the health intake quiz, a licensed clinician will review your information; you may either have a chat with a doctor or receive a prescription directly within 24 hours. Refills says clinicians come from partner telehealth networks and that your prescription and consultations are bundled into a monthly price.

Telos Rx: Treatment is prescribed only after review by a US-licensed provider. Telos Rx repeatedly notes that a licensed provider decides which medication is right and that offerings are subject to clinician approval.

Support, shipping, and membership terms

Refills: Free discreet shipping and express shipping are listed; Refills states “Never Pay Monthly Membership or App Fees” and that plans are month-to-month with cancel anytime and no cancellation fees. Refills also highlights free 24/7 Health Concierge support and unlimited access to patient care team and doctors at no extra cost in the captured evidence.

Telos Rx: The captured evidence notes cancel anytime with no penalties and indicates price depends on plan. Specific shipping, membership fee, and ongoing support details were not published or could not be verified on the captured landing page.

HermanRx vs JoinSkinny: both are telehealth options for GLP-1 treatment, but they differ on pricing models, medication sourcing, shipping timelines and availability. Quick verdict: HermanRx offers all-inclusive compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide pricing with no membership or consultation fee, while JoinSkinny emphasizes lower starting plans, flat-rate dosing and faster temperature-controlled shipping.

How the two services compare at a glance

Pricing and what is included

HermanRx lists specific all-inclusive monthly prices: compounded semaglutide + B12 at $249/month and compounded tirzepatide + B12 at $349/month. The source repeatedly states those prices cover the provider review, prescription, medication, ongoing support and free discreet shipping with no separate membership or consultation fee. JoinSkinny advertises plans starting at $149 with promotional savings such as a $140 offer and multiple bundle lengths. JoinSkinny emphasizes flat-rate dosing and that the monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases. Both providers state no insurance is required.

Medication sourcing, compounded status, and pharmacy handling

HermanRx explicitly markets compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide formulations and includes a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny states prescriptions are filled by licensed U.S. pharmacies that meet FDA regulations and hold LegitScript certification and that they prepare authentic GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP when prescribed. The two providers therefore differ in how the source frames medication preparation and regulatory context.

Shipping speed, handling and timelines

HermanRx describes a roughly 5-minute assessment, provider review within about 24 hours, and delivery in about 72 hours if prescribed, and highlights free discreet shipping in plain unmarked packaging to all 50 states. JoinSkinny emphasizes free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1-2 days, same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time and insured shipments. These differences may matter if next-day temperature-controlled delivery is a priority or if discreet packaging is preferred.

Availability, commitments and support

HermanRx is presented as available in all 50 states and as a multi-specialty telehealth platform where GLP-1 care is one of several categories. JoinSkinny clarifies it provides care in all eligible U.S. states where its licensed providers operate and advises state availability is confirmed during signup. JoinSkinny also highlights cancel anytime before the next billing cycle and ongoing monthly check-ins, dose adjustments and 24/7 chat support; HermanRx lists 24/7 patient support and a secure patient portal.

Safety and outcome language from sources

JoinSkinny includes a statement that results vary and that GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP medications are clinically shown to help patients lose 15 to 22 percent of their body weight over 12 to 15 months when paired with consistent use and lifestyle guidance. HermanRx includes a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Both providers include qualifying medical review language that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Final thoughts on HermanRx vs JoinSkinny

In short, HermanRx centers on transparent all-inclusive compounded pricing and a published ~24h provider review with ~72h delivery and nationwide discreet shipping. JoinSkinny highlights lower starting plans, promotional pricing, temperature-controlled next-day shipping when processed early, licensed U.S. pharmacy partners and bundle options with cancel-anytime terms. Which fits you depends on whether you prioritize an all-inclusive compounded plan with no membership fees or a lower starting price with temperature-controlled pharmacy fulfillment.

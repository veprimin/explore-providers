Price and monthly cost comparison

Direct-cost is the clearest difference between Maximus and BreezeMeds. Maximus advertises compounded semaglutide starting at $132.99/month and describes a low flat rate and transparent pricing. BreezeMeds displays a range: semaglutide injection from $199/month; tirzepatide injection from $299; and oral options from $399, plus a separate $169 figure that is not clearly tied to a specific product.

Medication types and availability

Maximus focuses on compounded semaglutide filled through U.S. pharmacies and notes compounded medication status. BreezeMeds lists injectable and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide options and indicates both injectable and oral forms are displayed. For BreezeMeds, medication-specific approval status and whether products are branded or compounded must be confirmed.

Delivery model, telemedicine, and personalization

Both providers use online intake or telemedicine but present different emphases. Maximus emphasizes convenient telemedicine access with prescriptions approved by licensed doctors and filled through trusted U.S. pharmacies, with no clinic visits required. BreezeMeds highlights online intake, a personalized treatment plan, and discreet home delivery, and notes that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Regulatory and compounding notes

Both sources include regulatory qualifiers. Maximus states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. BreezeMeds notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. These qualifiers mean you should confirm product status and clinician review when enrolling with either provider.

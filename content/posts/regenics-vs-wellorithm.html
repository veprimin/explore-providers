1. Starting price and renewal clarity

Regenics lists concrete promotional starting prices: semaglutide starts at $139 for the first month and tirzepatide at $189 for the first month, with explicit renewal rates noted at $199 and $279 respectively. Wellorithm promotes starting offers around $147 and multiple “starting at” lines on landing material, but the source shows that Wellorithm often requires completion of an intake quiz to receive a personalized quote and does not publish consistent renewal rates. If upfront, published promotional pricing matters, Regenics provides clearer first-month and renewal numbers in the sourced material.

2. Clinician oversight and state coverage

Both platforms deliver telehealth GLP-1 care and ship medication to the home. Regenics uses licensed nurse practitioners who review intake before prescriptions and notes that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Wellorithm advertises licensed clinicians in all 50 states and positions that nationwide coverage as its main strength for patients in states with limited access. For patients who need a clinician network that explicitly covers all states, Wellorithm is the supported option; for patients prioritizing nurse-practitioner-led care with a stated intake review, Regenics is the supported choice.

3. Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and the sources explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Regenics specifically notes its pharmacy network is LegitScript certified and that medications are compounded by U.S. licensed pharmacies. Wellorithm also notes compounding at licensed pharmacies. Both require a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness.

4. Additional product differences: Sermorelin and program focus

Regenics lists a Sermorelin peptide therapy add-on and a 15mg injectable peptide priced at $399 in its FAQ material, positioning a secondary peptide option for recovery, energy, and muscle support. Wellorithm materials provided focus on semaglutide and tirzepatide compounds, oral dissolving formulations, and program outcomes but do not list Sermorelin. This is a clear difference in additional offerings supported by the sources.

How to decide: Regenics vs Wellorithm

Regenics is best for patients seeking a straightforward, mid-pack cash-pay entry with clearly stated promotional and renewal pricing, nurse-practitioner oversight, a LegitScript-certified pharmacy network, free shipping, and an optional Sermorelin peptide add-on. Wellorithm is best for patients who need licensed clinician coverage in all 50 states, prefer a fully virtual intake with a 2-minute quiz to check eligibility, and are willing to complete the intake to receive a personalized price quote.

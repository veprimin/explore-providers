Comparing Directmeds vs Mochi Health? Quick verdict: Directmeds emphasizes transparent flat pricing, no membership fee, and compounded injectable or sublingual GLP-1s; Mochi Health emphasizes live video visits, membership-plus-medication pricing, and wide product variety. This article compares pricing, visit model, medication formats, membership and availability so you can pick the better fit for your needs.

How Directmeds vs Mochi Health differ at a glance

Directmeds and Mochi Health both offer clinician-reviewed, doctor-prescribed options that include compounded medications. Key differences include pricing structure and promotions, visit format, medication formats and membership requirements. Below are the most distinctive supported differences organized for a direct comparison.

1. Pricing structure and promotions

Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 with standard FAQ pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. Directmeds states medication, clinician review, telehealth visits, supplies and free next-day UPS/FedEx shipping are included and that there is no monthly membership fee. Mochi Health uses a membership-plus-medication model with pricing starting from $99/month and a 50% off first month promotion with code BESTWLHALFOFF. Mochi notes total monthly cost varies by plan and medication and that the $39 promotional rate applies to the first month only for new customers where applicable, with standard rates thereafter.

Compare Mochi Health membership and first-month offer

2. Visit model: live video vs typical telehealth review

Mochi Health highlights live video visits with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes, offering a synchronous consultation where a physician evaluates your case and answers questions in real time. Mochi contrasts this with the asynchronous questionnaire review that many competitors use. Directmeds describes clinician-reviewed telehealth visits but does not claim live video as Mochi does in the captured evidence.

3. Medication formats and product variety

Directmeds offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats and specifies U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. Mochi Health lists compounded options including once-weekly subcutaneous injections, a compounded oral semaglutide pill, and other compounded oral products, plus references to both compounded and some branded FDA-approved medications in its broader offering. Both providers state compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

4. Membership, cancellation and refunds

Directmeds states no separate monthly membership fee, month-to-month cancellation for customers, and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Mochi Health operates on a membership-plus-medication model with varying terms by state, prepaid multi-month plans available, and continued member care beyond the first month. Mochi notes that ongoing pricing is less transparent than flat-rate competitors because total cost depends on plan and medication.

5. Availability and shipping

Directmeds advertises free temperature-controlled UPS or FedEx shipping generally in 1 to 2 days and notes treatment is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. Mochi Health claims prescription access in all 50 states and describes treatments shipped to your door; specific shipping timing details are not supplied in the captured evidence. Both providers caution that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Final thoughts: Directmeds vs Mochi Health

Directmeds vs Mochi Health come from different tradeoffs: Directmeds centers on flat pricing transparency, no membership fee, compounded injectable and sublingual options, and free next-day shipping with some state exclusions. Mochi Health centers on live video physician visits fast, a membership-plus-medication pricing model with first-month promotions, and a broader list of compounded formats including oral options. Both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Choose Directmeds if you want clinician-reviewed compounded injectable or sublingual GLP-1 care with transparent standard pricing, no separate monthly membership fee, included supplies and free next-day shipping, and month-to-month cancellation options.

Choose Mochi Health if you prefer a live video visit with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes, a membership-plus-medication model with first-month promotional pricing, and a wider set of compounded product formats including oral options.

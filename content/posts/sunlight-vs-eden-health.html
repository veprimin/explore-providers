Direct comparison: Price and monthly cost

Price is one of the most distinctive differences in this Sunlight vs Eden Health comparison. Sunlight advertises personalized GLP-1 plans starting at $88/mo and highlights “same price forever” and promotional language including “as low as $88.” Eden lists monthly pricing by medication: semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129 and states a “lifetime price guarantee.” Both emphasize no insurance required, but exact eligibility, renewal terms, and state availability are not guaranteed and should be confirmed before enrollment.

Medication options and delivery formats

Sunlight promotes “injections and oral options” and describes personalized GLP-1 plans. Eden lists semaglutide and tirzepatide specifically and positions a guided GLP-1 program. Price guarantees, fees, and membership

Sunlight uses the wording “Same price forever” and highlights no monthly membership and cancel anytime language in captured material. Eden uses the phrase “Lifetime price guarantee” and also states no membership fees. Both capture commitments to a stable monthly price in their marketing; confirm renewal and coverage terms directly with each provider because pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Shipping and fulfillment

Sunlight advertises free shipping in 1-2 days and mentions “Fast, Free Shipping” multiple times. Eden advertises free expedited shipping. Both present expedited delivery options in captured material, though exact shipping timelines and availability should be checked during sign-up.

Clinician review and regulatory notes

Both Sunlight and Eden state that prescriptions and telehealth are included and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. The captured sources also include the regulatory caution that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

3. Clinician model and support

MedVi emphasizes 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and clinician review without clinic visits, describing a simple online intake and clinician review process. Regenics states medications are compounded by licensed U.S. pharmacies with a LegitScript certified pharmacy network. MedVi says it partners with multiple USA certified pharmacies and that its compounded semaglutide is produced in FDA-regulated facilities, while explicitly noting compounded medications are not FDA-approved as finished products. Both providers include regulatory qualifiers that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that prescription decisions are subject to clinician review.

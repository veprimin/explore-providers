How pricing and pricing models differ

Price is the clearest difference in this JoinFridays vs Refills comparison. Refills advertises flat pricing from $99/month, the same price at every dosage, and lists semaglutide at $99/month and tirzepatide at $159/month. JoinFridays lists a starting price of $117/month, multi-month plans that lower the effective monthly rate, and standalone month pricing that can be higher.

What each provider emphasizes

Refills emphasizes a single flat price per month and no membership or app fees plus free shipping and concierge support. JoinFridays emphasizes active promo codes, clinician-guided personalization, and no long-term contracts.

Medication choices and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 options, but the evidence and marketing differ. JoinFridays highlights compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide on one platform and notes options for brand-name or compounded products; JoinFridays also explicitly states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Refills offers compounded GLP-1 from $99/month and also states that compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated by the FDA.

Sesame Care vs Mochi Health: both offer telehealth weight loss care but they differ in clinician choice, visit model, pricing structure, and medication sourcing. Early verdict: choose Sesame Care if you want a marketplace to pick a clinician and insurance-friendly branded medication access; choose Mochi Health if you prioritize live video visits in about 5 minutes and lower membership-plus-compounded medication entry prices.

How they differ at a glance

Both provide telehealth access to GLP-1 weight loss care, but their approaches are distinct. Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select providers, with ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo* and cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo. Mochi Health emphasizes synchronous live video visits bookable in about 5 minutes, a membership-plus-medication model starting from $99/month, and compounded medications with lower starting prices such as compounded semaglutide starting $60.

1. Clinician model and visit experience

Sesame Care: marketplace model where patients choose clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing. Experience can vary by clinician. Mochi Health: live video visits with a doctor in about 5 minutes, providing a real-time physician consultation rather than an asynchronous questionnaire review. This synchronous model is Mochi’s defining feature.

2. Pricing structure and transparency

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo* based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Medication costs are separate and vary by insurance status and pharmacy. Sesame advertises cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo and notes price-with-insurance options as low as $25 for some medications.

Mochi Health operates primarily on a membership-plus-medication direct-pay model. New patients can get 50% off their first month with code BESTWLHALFOFF. Mochi lists membership-plus-medication pricing starting from $99/month and specific compounded medication starting prices such as compounded semaglutide starting $60 and compounded tirzepatide starting $90. Mochi also notes that ongoing pricing is less transparent because total monthly cost varies by plan and medication.

3. Medication types and sourcing

Sesame Care highlights access to branded FDA-approved medications including semaglutide and tirzepatide with listed cash-pay and in-network price ranges and mentions Wegovy pill and Zepbound KwikPen details where applicable. Sesame also includes the regulatory reminder that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Mochi Health emphasizes compounded medication options with starting prices for compounded semaglutide, compounded tirzepatide, and a compounded semaglutide pill. Mochi includes a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Mochi also offers a broader set of prescribed treatments shipped to your door and notes that compounded products are prescribed at provider discretion.

4. Availability and support

Sesame Care offers a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states according to its listing. Mochi Health advertises reliable GLP-1 prescription access in all 50 states, 24/7 support, and ongoing appointment availability throughout a member’s program.

Sesame Care is best for people who want broad telehealth choice, the ability to pick their clinician, and clearer pathways to branded FDA-approved medication access with insurance options.

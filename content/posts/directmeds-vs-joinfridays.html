Directmeds vs JoinFridays answers whether you should prioritize lower upfront cost, shipping speed, or compounded formats. Early verdict: choose JoinFridays if the lowest starting monthly rate and cancel-anytime flexibility matter most; choose Directmeds if you want injectable and sublingual compounded options, free next-day UPS or FedEx shipping, and transparent standard FAQ pricing.

How these two platforms differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on five supported differences: starting price and standard pricing, formulations and compounded status, shipping and availability, membership and cancellation terms, and refund or promotional notes. All statements below preserve qualifiers from each provider.

Starting price and ongoing pricing

Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and lists standard FAQ pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. JoinFridays starts at $117/month and lists program pricing that can vary by plan length; standalone one-month plans are $249, three-month plans are $596, six months $1,050, and twelve months $1,800, with multi-month plans lowering the effective monthly rate. JoinFridays also notes tirzepatide as low as $198/month and semaglutide at $117/month in promotional contexts.

Formulations and compounded versus brand options

Directmeds offers clinician-reviewed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats. JoinFridays provides compounded options for both tirzepatide and semaglutide and also advertises availability of brand-name options alongside compounded medication; JoinFridays states that it does not manufacture compounded medications and that actual product appearance may differ from website images. Both sources note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Shipping speed and geographic availability

Directmeds advertises free temperature-controlled UPS or FedEx delivery generally in 1 to 2 days and free next-day shipping in other summaries. JoinFridays highlights pricing and program structure but does not supply a specific standard transit time in the captured evidence. Directmeds also states treatment is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana; JoinFridays does not provide a comparable state restriction in the supplied evidence.

Membership, cancellation, and refunds

Directmeds advertises no separate monthly membership fee, no hidden fees, and month-to-month cancellation with subscribe-and-save plans requiring a stated term. Directmeds also states a full refund if a prescription is not approved. JoinFridays advertises no membership fees, cancel anytime with no long-term contract, and multi-month plans that lower the effective monthly cost; promo codes and discounts are called out as aggressive but subject to change.

Quick conclusion and how to choose

Directmeds vs JoinFridays comes down to three main tradeoffs supported by the sources: lowest advertised starting monthly rate (JoinFridays), formulation options and shipping speed (Directmeds), and membership or plan flexibility (JoinFridays for cancel-anytime; Directmeds for no separate membership fee and month-to-month cancellation). Both use clinician review and both offer compounded medications that are not FDA-approved.

Choose JoinFridays if you prioritize the lowest advertised starting monthly price, flexible cancel-anytime terms, and aggressive promo codes that can reduce tirzepatide or semaglutide costs.

Choose Directmeds if you want clinician-reviewed compounded injectable and sublingual options, free temperature-controlled next-day or 1 to 2 day shipping, transparent FAQ pricing, and a stated full refund if a prescription is not approved.

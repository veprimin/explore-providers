Outcome framing and evidence

Sprout Health frames outcomes as a 10–26% body weight loss over 12 months, attributing that to GLP-1 clinical outcome literature and noting individual results vary. Sprout explicitly states it does not provide a published outcome guarantee. RxActivate’s captured evidence did not include independent outcome proof; the provider-published claims were not treated as independent evidence in the source capture.

Availability and regulatory notes

RxActivate’s captured flow confirmed Alabama serviceability in a synthetic test and states 250,000+ members. Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Treatment on both platforms is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

How to choose: final guidance for Sprout Health vs RxActivate

Choose Sprout Health if you want a fast, low-friction online start with a visible first-month price ($199 after $50 off) and accept that ongoing pricing is $249/month and that the provider prescribes compounded semaglutide. Choose RxActivate if you prefer to confirm clinician matching, state availability and plan details via a two-minute eligibility questionnaire and do not mind providing contact information to see pricing and enrollment terms. Both require clinician determination for treatment and note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

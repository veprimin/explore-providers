1. Pricing model and what you pay for

HermanRx lists semaglutide + B12 at $249 per month and tirzepatide + B12 at $349 per month and describes those rates as all-inclusive. The HermanRx listing explicitly says the all-inclusive price covers provider review, prescription, medication, ongoing support and free discreet shipping with no separate membership or consultation fee. Hers lists Wegovy® pill pricing from $149 per month and injectable pricing from $199 per month but notes that the stated medication prices do not include membership. 2. Compounded products versus FDA-approved brand-name medication

HermanRx sells compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide formulations combined with B12 and explicitly states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Hers emphasizes FDA-approved Wegovy® as a brand-name semaglutide option and states it offers no compounded medications. That means Hers promotes a product approved for weight loss, while HermanRx offers compounded alternatives and includes the provider’s regulatory note about compounding.

3. Membership, enrollment and availability

HermanRx advertises no membership fee and no separate consultation charge and labels itself cash-pay with availability in all 50 states. Hers requires an active membership to access medication; Hers also states treatments and specific brand medications may not be available in all 50 states and that a prescription and clinician-determined eligibility are required.

HermanRx publishes a timeline that includes a roughly 5-minute assessment, provider review in about 24 hours and delivery in about 72 hours if prescribed, with a wider FAQ window of 3 to 7 business days for some orders. Hers highlights a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins but captured landing-page evidence notes state availability and shipping details vary and that prescription and clinical eligibility are required.

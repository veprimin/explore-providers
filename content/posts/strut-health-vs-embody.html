How these providers differ at a glance

This comparison emphasizes five supported differences: starting price, oral versus injection options, shipping speed and delivery, program features and clinical focus, and regulatory notes about compounded medications. Both prescribe compounded GLP-1 products in some form, but their models and priorities differ.

1. Price and entry cost

Strut Health: starting price is From $199 per Month. Embody: listed at $69/month for compounded semaglutide and $119/month for tirzepatide, with the $69 semaglutide price noted as reduced from $299 and described as a limited-time offer that may increase. Embody positions itself as the lowest entry price on the list, while Strut Health positions around a higher starting monthly price.

2. Oral specialization versus broader compounded GLP-1 options

Strut Health emphasizes specialized oral-focused treatment options and highlights Oral Semaglutide lozenges as a core offering, with guided consultations and ongoing progress communication. Embody offers compounded semaglutide injections and compounded tirzepatide injections and emphasizes compound GLP-1 and GLP-1 plus GIP options at aggressive pricing. 3. Shipping speed and delivery commitments

Embody explicitly advertises free shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days after clinician approval, describing that as one of its faster delivery commitments. Strut Health lists free shipping among its landing-page claims but does not supply a specific 1 to 2 day delivery timeframe in the captured evidence.

4. Program features and clinical support

Strut Health highlights guided consults, ongoing progress communication, and a clinical focus on oral medication management rather than splitting attention across injection and oral protocols. Embody emphasizes a lean, prescription-forward model with telemed reviews, doctor-prescribed medication, no long-term commitment, and 24/7 support access for dosing and side-effect questions in the captured copy. Embody also notes prescription and telemed visits are included and that there is no monthly membership.

5. Compounded medication regulatory note and availability

Both providers use compounded semaglutide in some form. The record for Strut Health states GLP-1 compounded weight management therapy with Semaglutide and notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved. The Embody material similarly states its compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and that final pricing may vary based on dose and clinician recommendation. Strut Health specifies compounded semaglutide is prepared by a U.S. licensed compounding pharmacy pursuant to an individualized prescription and that compounded medication is prescribed based on medical necessity and subject to a licensed physician determining medical necessity.

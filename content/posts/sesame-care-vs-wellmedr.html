Sesame Care vs Wellmedr: quick verdict

Quick verdict: Sesame Care is best if you want broad clinician choice, flexible scheduling and insurance options. Wellmedr is best if you want locked pricing, same-day review and on-time shipping with a money back guarantee.

Comparing Sesame Care vs Wellmedr shows two different approaches to online GLP-1 care. Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace where patients choose their clinician and can get ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Wellmedr leans on lifetime locked-in pricing, no dose-based price increases and same-day review with free overnight shipping, with semaglutide plans from $89/mo.

How pricing and payment differ

Sesame Care lists ongoing care starting at $59/mo and cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo. It also notes that medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on insurance status and pharmacy, and that insurance is accepted for weight loss medications. Wellmedr advertises semaglutide from $89/mo and emphasizes lifetime locked-in pricing and no dose-based price increases. Wellmedr also promotes promotional sale language on the captured landing page.

Clinician access and care model

Sesame Care operates as a marketplace that lets patients browse and select providers based on specialty, availability and pricing, giving more choice in the clinician-patient relationship. Wellmedr requires medical evaluation and clinician approval as part of enrollment but does not present a marketplace model; its captured evidence focuses on plan features like shipping and refund guarantees.

Medication sourcing and regulatory notes

Sesame Care lists branded medications with example prices such as semaglutide cash-pay $149 and tirzepatide cash-pay $299 with price-with-insurance as low as $25 for some strengths, and notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed. Wellmedr states that it offers compounded GLP-1 medications exclusively from licensed U.S. pharmacies and includes a required safety notice that compounded medications are not evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy. Wellmedr also lists risks associated with GLP-1 medications and emphasizes that compounded products are not FDA-approved.

Features, shipping and guarantees

Sesame Care highlights a large clinician network, flexible scheduling and nationwide access in many states with easy booking and broad treatment coverage. Wellmedr highlights lifetime locked-in pricing, no dose-based price increases, same-day review and free overnight shipping, plus a money back guarantee and promotional offers captured on the landing page.

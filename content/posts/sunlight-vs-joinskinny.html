Quick verdict: Sunlight is notable for lower starting pricing and a “same price forever” promise, while JoinSkinny emphasizes GLP-1 plus GLP-1+GIP options, flat-rate dosing, temperature-controlled shipping and multi-month bundles. This comparison checks pricing, medication options, shipping and plan structure for Sunlight vs JoinSkinny.

How pricing and promos differ

Sunlight lists Personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo and advertises “Same price forever” plus promotional messaging like “As low as $88” and a Summer Promo. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149, offers a $140 promotional discount in its captured evidence, and promotes monthly and multi-month bundles. Both include prescription and telemed visits and state that no insurance is required. Pricing specifics, eligibility and state availability require confirmation during signup.

Medication options and regulatory notes

Sunlight: offers injections and oral options and positions treatment as personalized GLP-1. JoinSkinny: offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and flat-rate dosing. JoinSkinny material explicitly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that prescriptions are filled by licensed U.S. pharmacies; the source also states branded prescriptions are filled and paid for separately. Sunlight includes a regulatory reminder that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Shipping, fulfillment, and timing

Sunlight: captured claims include Fast, Free Shipping and Free shipping in 1-2 days. JoinSkinny: captured claims include Free temperature-controlled shipping and that prescriptions received before 2 pm central time will be shipped the same day with next-day delivery via UPS, and that weekend orders ship Monday to preserve temperature management. Both sources state medication ships directly to the door and emphasize fast delivery in 1-2 days in landing-page evidence.

Care model, support and plan flexibility

Sunlight: highlights a 100% online medical visit, prescription and telehealth included, no insurance required, and says there is no monthly membership and you can cancel anytime in the captured landing text. JoinSkinny: describes a fully online medical service with a quick health questionnaire, licensed providers reviewing information, ongoing support, dose adjustments, monthly check-ins, and 24/7 chat. JoinSkinny also notes cancel before next billing cycle and offers installment options like Afterpay, Klarna, and Affirm according to the captured evidence.

State availability, eligibility and commitments

JoinSkinny states care is provided in all eligible U.S. states where its licensed providers operate and where telehealth prescribing is allowed, with state entry confirming availability during signup. Sunlight captured evidence notes that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change and recommends confirming those details before enrollment. Both sources say people should confirm eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

Final take on Sunlight vs JoinSkinny

Sunlight vs JoinSkinny diverges most on starting price and pricing promise, medication variety, shipping specifics, and plan structure. Sunlight emphasizes lower starting cost from $88/mo, injections and oral options, and a same price forever claim. JoinSkinny emphasizes GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options, flat-rate dosing, temperature-controlled shipping with same-day processing cutoffs, and multi-month bundles.

Choose Sunlight if you prioritize a lower starting price and a stated “same price forever” approach and want injections or oral GLP-1 options. Choose JoinSkinny if you want GLP-1+GIP options, explicit temperature-controlled shipping and multi-month bundle choices. Confirm eligibility, medication-specific approvals, total cost and state availability directly with each provider before enrolling.

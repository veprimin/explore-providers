1. Bundled supportive ingredients and tracking

HumeCare+ sells a protocol that pairs physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide together with glycine, vitamin B12, and B6, and includes monthly body-composition scans and an app. The provider positions these additions as muscle-preserving and as aimed at side-effect relief. BetterMe Rx does not advertise built-in glycine or B vitamin support or body-composition scans; its focus is medication formats and supplies.

2. Medication formats and choices

BetterMe Rx lists four medication and administration options: injectable and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide, including oral tirzepatide as a needle-free tablet option. 3. Pricing structure and billing terms

HumeCare+ lists semaglutide starting at $199/month and tirzepatide plus B12 and glycine from $299/month. BetterMe Rx advertises lower monthly-equivalent pricing on longer plans: compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan, with specific 12-month equivalents for injectable and oral products (semaglutide injection as low as $145/mo on 12-month plan; tirzepatide injection as low as $215/mo; oral semaglutide as low as $185/mo; oral tirzepatide as low as $215/mo). BetterMe Rx notes that the lowest prices require a 12-month plan and that longer plans are billed according to the selected term.

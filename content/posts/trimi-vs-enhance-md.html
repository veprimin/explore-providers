Quick verdict: Trimi vs Enhance MD

The core difference in Trimi vs Enhance MD is pricing structure and entry cost: Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/mo with free overnight shipping and a 6-month refund promise, while Enhance MD advertises semaglutide from $49 for the first month and flat-rate pricing thereafter. Both prescribe compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide through licensed clinicians; compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Pricing and introductory cost

Enhance MD offers a lower first-month entry price for semaglutide, advertised as “semaglutide from $49” and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month. Trimi positions semaglutide from $99/mo on its annual plan and tirzepatide at $125/month on annual plans. Both emphasize a flat price at every dose, but the evidence supplied shows Enhance MD highlights a notably lower first-month price point.

Flat-rate pricing and dose escalation

Both providers state “same price at every dose” or “flat pricing that never rises as your dose increases.” Trimi repeatedly notes “flat pricing every dose” and “same price at every dose.” Enhance MD emphasizes flat-rate pricing with no fee escalation during titration and clarifies the first-month offer is introductory with an ongoing flat-rate that can be higher after that period.

Compounded medication status and regulatory notes

Both Trimi and Enhance MD supply clinician-directed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. The sources explicitly state that compounded medications “are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.” Trimi also notes its compounding occurs at state-licensed 503A pharmacies and offers certificates of analysis on request.

Shipping, refunds, and clinical oversight

Trimi advertises free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and describes a “6-month money-back promise” where customers who are “not approved by our medical team” receive an automatic refund and those who follow the plan for six months may request a refund if they do not lose weight, with terms applied. Trimi also references Arora Health and a named medical director and state-licensed providers. Enhance MD highlights clinician-directed care and a platform that has treated “10,000+ patients,” and notes patient-facing features like metabolic lab testing every six months in some published price examples.

Conclusion: choosing between Trimi vs Enhance MD

Trimi vs Enhance MD narrows to priorities: choose based on entry cost, shipping, refund terms, and compounding transparency. Trimi emphasizes free overnight shipping, flat pricing per dose from its stated monthly rates, named pharmacies, and a 6-month money-back promise. Enhance MD emphasizes the lowest first-month pricing for semaglutide and tirzepatide and a large treated-patient base with flat-rate ongoing pricing after introductory offers.

Choose Trimi if you prioritize free overnight shipping, named compounding pharmacies, and a 6-month money-back promise. Choose Enhance MD if your priority is the lowest first-month entry price for semaglutide or tirzepatide and access to both compounds on one platform.

## How It Works

Rugiet uses a streamlined telehealth process to assess, prescribe, and deliver ED medications online. The workflow is formal, clinically-focused, and optimized for speed.

### Online consultation flow

- Start on the Rugiet site and complete the intake form.
- A licensed provider reviews submissions and medical history.
- Approved prescriptions are sent to a partner pharmacy for fulfillment.

### Medical questionnaire

The online medical questionnaire captures medical history, current medications, allergies, cardiovascular risk factors, and sexual health concerns. Questions are designed to support safe prescribing of sildenafil or tadalafil and to identify contraindications.

### Doctor evaluation

Licensed clinicians—typically physicians or nurse practitioners—evaluate responses. The evaluation focuses on safety, drug interactions (notably nitrates), and dosing suitability for rapid-acting options.

### Prescription approval

When clinically appropriate, the provider approves a prescription. Rugiet emphasizes timely decisions to enable its “works in 15 minutes” messaging for select formulations.

### Pharmacy fulfillment

Approved prescriptions are routed to partner pharmacies for processing and quality-controlled dispensing. Rugiet reports partnerships with licensed pharmacies to ensure authenticity and safe packaging.

### Shipping & ongoing care

Shipments are handled with standard tracking and discrete packaging. Ongoing care includes follow-ups, refill coordination, and access to clinician support for adverse events or dosing adjustments.

## Medications Offered

Rugiet offers a range of ED treatment options to meet different onset, duration, and lifestyle needs. This catalog supports patients seeking sildenafil online prescription options as well as tadalafil online.

- **Sildenafil (generic Viagra)**
  - Dosage ranges: typically 25 mg, 50 mg, 100 mg.
  - Price context: generic pricing is competitive versus branded alternatives; cost varies by dose and quantity.
  - Best for: men seeking effective, short-duration erections with on-demand dosing.
- **Tadalafil (generic Cialis)**
  - Dosage ranges: commonly 5 mg, 10 mg, 20 mg.
  - Price context: slightly higher than sildenafil generics but offers long-duration benefit.
  - Best for: men wanting extended window of effectiveness (up to 36 hours).
- **Daily tadalafil**
  - Dosage ranges: low-dose daily regimens typically 2.5 mg or 5 mg.
  - Price context: ongoing monthly cost but supports spontaneity.
  - Best for: men with frequent sexual activity or preference for daily coverage.
- **Rapid-dissolve tablets**
  - Formulation: fast-melt or rapid-dissolve sildenafil or similar compounds that claim onset within 15 minutes.
  - Price context: premium vs standard generics due to formulation.
  - Best for: users prioritizing ultra-fast onset and immediate readiness.
- **Behavioral / performance support programs**
  - Includes counseling resources and performance coaching offered or recommended alongside medication.
  - Best for: those with psychological contributors to ED or seeking combined care.

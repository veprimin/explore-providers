Bodybuilding Health+ vs He & She MD: what they each prioritize

Both providers sell compounded GLP-1 medication online, but the pair differ in three main areas supported by the source: baseline and promotional pricing structure, bundled coaching and clinician access, and program scope and cancellation terms. The primary keyword Bodybuilding Health+ vs He & She MD is used here to frame a direct comparison of those differences.

1) Pricing structure and introductory offers

Bodybuilding Health+ lists semaglutide from $179/mo and tirzepatide from $209/mo as straightforward starting prices, with multi-month bundles that lower effective monthly cost. He & She MD advertises semaglutide injections starting at $139/mo as a six-month introductory rate that renews higher after the initial six months; oral semaglutide and tirzepatide injections start at $199/mo intro and also renew at higher standard six-month prices. These are not identical models: BBHealthPlus highlights ongoing starting prices, while He & She MD uses time-limited intro pricing that later renews at a higher rate.

2) Coaching, app features, and clinician access

He & She MD bundles a coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific guidance and progress tracking at no extra cost. Bodybuilding Health+ is presented as a multi-program telehealth platform spanning weight management, longevity, and sexual performance but the source does not describe an included coaching app or 1:1 messaging as part of its standard GLP-1 offering.

3) Membership, cancellation, and term differences

Bodybuilding Health+ repeatedly states No Monthly Membership and Cancel Anytime, billing only for chosen treatment programs. He & She MD states no membership fee but structures pricing as six-month introductory terms that renew at higher standard six-month prices; the source notes the intro rates are limited time and renew 30 to 45 percent higher in typical examples. That makes BBHealthPlus appear to prioritize month-to-month flexibility while He & She MD emphasizes bundled term-based programs with a discounted introductory term.

4) Program breadth and specialist oversight

Bodybuilding Health+ is described as a broad telehealth platform offering GLP-1s plus NAD+, sermorelin, and sexual performance options, and names medical oversight by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D. He & She MD markets GLP-1-focused care across injection and oral formats and highlights a named chief medical officer and USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies for medication production. Both require clinician consultation and prescription and note not all applicants will qualify.

5) Regulatory and pharmacy notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1s and the sources explicitly state compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. He & She MD additionally notes medications are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies; Bodybuilding Health+ notes compounded medications are prepared by licensed U.S. pharmacies but not FDA-evaluated.

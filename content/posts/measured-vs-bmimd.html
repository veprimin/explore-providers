Key differences at a glance

This side-by-side looks at the most distinctive differences between Measured and bmiMD: starting price and pricing structure, medication availability, shipping speed and fulfillment, clinician and dietitian support, and regulatory disclosures.

Measured advertises promotional compounded semaglutide from $89 per month and compounded tirzepatide $189 per month. bmiMD lists tirzepatide at $179 month-to-month with multi-month savings down to $139 per month on a 12-month plan. bmiMD notes that the lowest monthly price requires a 12-month commitment and that total prepaid charge should be confirmed. Measured highlights promotional pricing and includes specific promotional amounts in marketing claims.

Measured pairs compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and mentions same-day prescription decisions. bmiMD focuses on compounded tirzepatide, described as GLP-1/GIP, with the same price at every dose and confirmed target strength within ± 10 percent every 3 to 6 months. Both providers clearly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Measured includes an internal analysis claim that members reporting weight data at 6 months had an average loss of 31 pounds with a qualifier that individual results vary and are not guaranteed. bmiMD includes customer and landing-page language describing weight loss but does not present an independent outcome analysis in the captured evidence.

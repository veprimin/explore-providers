Looking for a straightforward comparison of Enhance MD vs Refills? Quick verdict: Enhance MD undercuts Refills on first-month pricing for semaglutide and tirzepatide, while Refills emphasizes month-to-month flat pricing with free discreet shipping and 24/7 health concierge. This article compares pricing, compound options, flat-rate policies, shipping and patient support, and regulatory notes so you can decide which fits your needs.

Key difference 1: First-month starting price

Enhance MD lists semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month, explicitly described as introductory prices. Refills lists GLP-1 from $99/month (~$5/day) with semaglutide at $99/mo and tirzepatide at $159/mo. The direct comparison shows Enhance MD offers a lower entry price for semaglutide and the same first-month tirzepatide price as Refills reports only for semaglutide, not for tirzepatide at $159.

Key difference 2: Compounds available on one platform

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 options. Enhance MD highlights availability of both semaglutide and tirzepatide on one platform and calls out both compounds at specific first-month price points. Refills also offers semaglutide and a GIP+GLP-1 dual agonist option and lists tirzepatide pricing separately. Both emphasize compounded products rather than branded finished products.

Key difference 3: Flat-rate pricing and ongoing cost structure

Enhance MD markets flat-rate pricing with the same price at every dose and no fee escalation during titration, noting the introductory rates apply to first month and that ongoing flat-rate pricing is higher and not always prominently published before intake. Refills also advertises flat pricing, the same price at every dosage, and a month-to-month plan you can cancel anytime with no contracts or cancellation fees. Both providers present flat-rate models; Enhance MD emphasizes flat-rate to avoid dose escalation surprises, while Refills emphasizes simple month-to-month billing and no membership fees.

Key difference 4: Shipping, support, and fulfillment

Refills explicitly includes free discreet shipping, express shipping options, and 24/7 health concierge support, plus shipping and tracking notifications and a monthly refill workflow. Enhance MD materials captured note program details and promotional language but do not supply the same explicit shipping and 24/7 support details in the captured evidence. If shipping speed and ongoing concierge access matter, Refills documents these features directly.

Key difference 5: Regulatory and pharmacy sourcing notes

Both sources state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Refills notes its products come from U.S. pharmacies with boards of pharmacy oversight and third-party USP testing mentioned in captured materials. Enhance MD states its medications are compounded and not FDA-approved and also highlights clinician-directed review before treatment is available.

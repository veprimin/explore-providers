Quick comparison: SnagRx vs BetterMe Rx

The primary difference between SnagRx and BetterMe Rx is price structure and format options: SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan with a price locked at every dose, while BetterMe Rx lists compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan with both injectable and oral formats. Early verdict: for lowest annual-plan monthly pricing and a stated price lock across titration, SnagRx is the cost-focused option; for a wider choice of administration formats, BetterMe Rx is the format-focused option.

Compare SnagRx pricing and 12-month plan

Five direct differences that matter

1. Starting price and plan billing

SnagRx: compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on the 12-month plan; month-to-month equivalents are $99 and $149. BetterMe Rx: 12-month equivalents start at $145/month for semaglutide injection and $215/month for injectable tirzepatide, with month-to-month pricing shown higher.

2. Price lock versus variable monthly-equivalents

SnagRx states the rate is locked flat at every dose and at renewal for the life of treatment on the selected plan. BetterMe Rx offers lower monthly-equivalent pricing for longer 3-, 6-, or 12-month supplies but does not advertise a dose-locked flat rate in the supplied evidence.

3. Medication formats

SnagRx offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide injections only in the supplied evidence and emphasizes compounded products. BetterMe Rx explicitly offers both injectable and oral compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options, including oral tablet formats at separate price points.

4. Named partners, clinician network and review timing

SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and OpenLoop Health as its clinician network and states clinician review typically within five hours and always within 24, with next-day UPS/FedEx shipping. BetterMe Rx indicates a licensed provider review and partnered U.S. pharmacy compounding with free 1 to 2 day shipping but does not list named pharmacies in the supplied evidence.

5. Included services and guarantees

SnagRx states medication, prescription and telemed visits are included, and lists a full refund if the prescription is declined plus a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms. BetterMe Rx lists medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies and free shipping included and notes cancel-anytime language alongside prepaid plans in the supplied evidence.

How each provider positions safety and regulation

Both providers note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. SnagRx adds that its compounded products are produced in FDA-regulated facilities and encourages discussion with a provider about risks; SnagRx also highlights that OpenLoop clinicians apply exclusionary criteria and retain prescribing decisions. BetterMe Rx states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Practical differences that affect users

If your primary concern is locking a single monthly rate across titration and saving on a 12-month plan, SnagRx explicitly markets that locked flat pricing and lists lower 12-month-equivalent monthly figures. If you need a needle-free option or want both oral and injectable formats, BetterMe Rx documents oral tablet options and separate pricing for oral products.

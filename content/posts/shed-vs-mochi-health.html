How Shed vs Mochi Health differ at a glance

Medication options and regulatory status

Shed vs Mochi Health diverge most clearly on available medication types. Shed explicitly offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 and compounded alternatives, letting patients choose based on preference and budget. The platform notes that name-brand options carry higher costs while compounded alternatives are more affordable. Mochi focuses on compounded options across injectables and oral forms, with listed starting prices for compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and additional compounded products. Both providers include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Clinical experience: coaching versus live video

Shed integrates continuous coaching into every treatment plan rather than issuing a prescription alone. The platform positions coaching as ongoing support for titration, lifestyle changes, and plateaus. Mochi Health makes live video visits with a licensed physician its defining feature, enabling a real-time conversation where a doctor can answer questions and evaluate the case directly. Choose based on whether you prefer ongoing behavioral coaching alongside medication or synchronous doctor interaction during initial and follow up visits.

Pricing and membership differences

Both platforms disclose promotional pricing but structure costs differently. Shed lists compounded semaglutide starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month with a limited time 10% off your first month. Mochi uses a membership-plus-medication model: pricing starts from $99/month, with promotional lower first-month rates and 50% Off Your 1st Month via code BESTWLHALFOFF; the platform notes ongoing costs vary by plan and medication and that the $39 promotional rate applies to the first month only for new customers in some offers.

Availability, speed, and ongoing care

Mochi highlights reliable GLP-1 prescription access in all 50 states and 24/7 support with ongoing appointments. Shed emphasizes a fast 2-minute assessment to check eligibility, followed by clinician review before prescriptions are issued and coaching beginning from the start of the plan. Both say a licensed clinician evaluates your case before any prescription is issued. The choice here depends on whether you prioritize national live video availability and 24/7 access or rapid intake plus integrated coaching.

Conclusion: Choose based on care style and cost

In short, Shed vs Mochi Health come down to coaching and medication choice versus live video clinical access and membership pricing. Choose Shed if you want integrated, continuous coaching and the option to select FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 alongside compounded options. Choose Mochi Health if you prioritize a live video visit with a doctor in about 5 minutes, membership-plus-medication pricing starting from $99/month, and 24/7 support. Both platforms require a licensed clinician to evaluate your case and both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

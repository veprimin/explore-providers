Omzo vs Luvo Health: Pricing and transparency

Omzo lists semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279, with medication costs included in fees collected by Omzo. Luvo’s captured information did not verify a specific starting price; the landing evidence shows month-by-month structures and ongoing support but does not publish a verified starting price in the captured material.

Compounded medication status and regulatory notes

Both providers’ captured information states that compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and are not reviewed or approved by the FDA. Omzo’s materials explicitly note its Compounded Medication Program uses state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacies and that these compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. Luvo’s captured material likewise notes that compounded medications are not reviewed or approved by the FDA.

Clinician involvement and program model

Omzo: described as clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed U.S. providers and a platform that connects you with licensed medical providers who determine eligibility and prescribe based on professional judgment. Omzo also states clinical services are provided by third party networks of U.S.-licensed clinicians who make final determinations.

Luvo Health: described as clinician-reviewed online weight-management treatment with ongoing support, dose changes, and clinician-determined medical appropriateness. The captured Luvo material highlights dose adjustments based on clinical response, month 1 and month 2 to 3 structures, and the ability to cancel anytime as captured.

Delivery, support and program features

Omzo notes fast, discreet delivery and that medication costs are included in fees Omzo collects; if a branded medication is prescribed the patient is responsible for filling it at a pharmacy. Luvo emphasizes ongoing support, dose changes, and educational guidance such as dosing guides and content on managing side effects, as captured.

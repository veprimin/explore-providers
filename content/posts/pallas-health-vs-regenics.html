Pricing structure and renewal details

Pallas lists compounded semaglutide at $139 for the first month, then $597 every 12 weeks, which the source states is a $199 per month average; compounded tirzepatide is $179 first month then $897 every 12 weeks ($299/mo average). Pallas also publishes cash-pay brand-name pricing for Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound and Mounjaro on its site. Regenics shows promotional first-month pricing: semaglutide $139 first month (renewing at $199/month) and tirzepatide $189 first month (renewing at $279/month), with promotional pricing applying to the first month only.

Provider care, membership, and billing

Pallas includes provider care, ongoing check-ins and care-team messaging in a single plan price and states there is no membership fee. Pallas is cash-pay only and does not bill insurance; FSA/HSA use requires a Letter of Medical Necessity per the source. Regenics does not require insurance to start treatment and the source states plans are FSA/HSA eligible.

Additional therapies and pharmacy notes

Regenics uniquely lists Sermorelin peptide therapy as an add-on. Both providers rely on US-licensed compounding pharmacies when offering compounded medications. Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

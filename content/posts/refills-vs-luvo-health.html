Key differences at a glance for Refills vs Luvo Health

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: pricing transparency, compounded medication disclosures and regulation, shipping and membership terms, clinician review and ongoing support, and state or eligibility variability. Each point uses only the supplied evidence.

1. Pricing transparency and structure

Refills: the source explicitly lists GLP-1 from $99/month (~$5/day) and states flat pricing, the same price at every dosage, plus examples like semaglutide at $99/mo and tirzepatide at $159/mo. Luvo Health: the captured landing-page evidence shows program stages and mentions month 1, month 2 to 3, and 3+ months pricing columns, but current price details were not published or could not be verified on the captured affiliate landing page.

2. Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both providers include regulatory qualifiers about compounded medications. Refills repeatedly states that compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality by the FDA and that their compounded products may be prescribed if deemed medically appropriate by partner clinicians. Luvo Health captured evidence likewise notes compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and are not reviewed or approved by the FDA and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

3. Shipping, membership, and cancellation policies

Refills: the source lists free discreet shipping, express shipping, no monthly membership or app fees, availability in all 50 US states, and a month-to-month plan with cancel anytime language and no cancellation fees. Luvo Health: captured evidence mentions cancel anytime and suggests ongoing support and dose changes across months, but specific shipping, membership fee, and refund details were not published or could not be verified on the captured affiliate landing page.

4. Clinician review, consultations, and ongoing support

Refills: after the intake quiz, a licensed clinician will review your information. Depending on needs, you may have a chat with a doctor or receive a prescription directly within 24 hours. Refills states you get unlimited access to the patient care team and doctors for questions at no extra cost, text and email shipment updates, and a monthly refill form. Luvo Health: the capture highlights dose adjustments based on clinical response and ongoing support across months, but detailed clinician interaction timing and support scope were not verified on the captured affiliate landing page.

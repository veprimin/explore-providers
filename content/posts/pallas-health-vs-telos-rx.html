How they compare at a glance

This comparison uses only the supplied facts: both providers offer clinician-reviewed online prescribing of GLP-1 compounds; both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. The most distinctive differences are pricing structure and brand versus compound pathways, scope of online care and visit requirements, renewal and billing cadence, and explicit pricing disclosures.

Direct differences we use below

  • Pricing and starting cost: Telos Rx lists semaglutide as low as $99 per month; Pallas shows compounded semaglutide $139 first month, then $199/mo average.
  • Brand-name access: Pallas offers FDA-approved Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound and Mounjaro as options chosen by a clinician; Telos Rx captures only compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in the supplied evidence.
  • Plan scope and fees: Pallas states one all-in plan price with provider care, check-ins and messaging included and no membership fee; Telos Rx materials emphasize compounded medications prescribed online with pricing subject to approval.
  • Renewal cadence and pricing presentation: Pallas compounds renew every 12 weeks at specified amounts that translate to monthly averages; Telos Rx frames per-month pricing as low as figures and notes price depends on plan and approval.

Pricing structure and transparency

Pallas Health provides explicit figures for compounded plans: semaglutide $139 first month then $597 per 12 weeks (a $199 per month average) and tirzepatide $179 first month then $897 per 12 weeks (a $299 per month average). It also lists cash-pay brand pen prices for Wegovy, Zepbound, Ozempic and Mounjaro. Telos Rx presents “as low as” monthly figures and notes that your price depends on your plan and medical approval, with semaglutide as low as $99 per month and tirzepatide as low as $139 per month in the captured evidence.

Care model and program inclusions

Pallas frames a single intake that can lead to either FDA-approved brand pens or clinician-personalized compounded medications. It states clinician review and ongoing check-ins, provider messaging and free expedited shipping are included in plan pricing, and there is no membership fee. Telos Rx materials emphasize that a US-licensed provider decides which medication is right and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Telos Rx notes cancel anytime language and that pricing and eligibility are subject to approval.

Renewals, cancellation and practical notes

Pallas specifies compounded plans renew every 12 weeks, which affects cancellation timing, and notes that care-team hours are weekdays 9 AM to 5 PM ET with no emergency services. Telos Rx notes cancel anytime with no penalties in the captured evidence and that your price depends on plan and approval. Both vendors include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

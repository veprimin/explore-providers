Pricing and product mix

On price alone Telos Rx lists semaglutide from $99 per month and tirzepatide from $139 per month, if a licensed provider approves you. SkinnyRx reports a starting price as low as $249 per month for daily semaglutide tablets. The sources state those starting figures and note that actual price depends on plan or approval.

Medication formats and clinical options

SkinnyRx focuses on daily oral semaglutide tablets designed with clinically supported ingredients as an oral alternative to GLP-1 injections. Telos Rx offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, noting tirzepatide acts on GLP-1 and GIP while semaglutide acts on GLP-1. The Telos Rx source emphasizes that a US-licensed provider decides which molecule is right for you.

FSA/HSA eligibility, shipping, and fulfillment

SkinnyRx explicitly states FSA/HSA eligibility and free overnight shipping on all orders, and that shipping and tracking information is provided within 2 business days after a prescription is written. Telos Rx materials do not publish FSA/HSA claims on the captured landing-page evidence and note that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy and state coverage may change.

Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both sources clearly say compounded medications are prescription-only and are not FDA-approved or reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. SkinnyRx adds that partner pharmacies perform third-party testing through FDA- and DEA-compliant labs every 3 to 6 months to confirm concentration within plus or minus 10 percent of the active ingredient. Telos Rx emphasizes that compounded products and availability are subject to clinician review and state coverage.

Clinician review, eligibility, and cancellation

Each provider requires a licensed clinician to review and approve prescriptions. SkinnyRx specifies that its treatment is only available if prescribed after an online consultation with a healthcare provider, that plans are subscription services cancelable at any time, and that refunds are available if canceled before a licensed provider completes the prescription review. Telos Rx states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that you can cancel anytime with no penalties, while also warning that pricing, eligibility and renewal terms may change.

How to choose between SkinnyRx and Telos Rx

Choose SkinnyRx if you prioritize an oral compounded semaglutide tablet with explicit FSA/HSA eligibility and free overnight shipping, plus partner pharmacy third-party testing every 3 to 6 months. Choose Telos Rx if lower advertised starting prices and access to both compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, subject to approval by a US-licensed provider, are your main priorities.

In short, SkinnyRx vs Telos Rx comes down to format and financial features versus lower starting price and broader molecule options, all with clinician review and the shared caveat that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

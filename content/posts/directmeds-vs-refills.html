Directmeds vs Refills answers the key question: which telehealth GLP-1 provider better fits your needs? Quick verdict: Refills is the lower flat-price option starting at $99/month and available in all 50 states; Directmeds emphasizes compounded injectable and sublingual options with a promotional starting price of $147, clinician review, and free next-day UPS/FedEx shipping.

How they differ at a glance

Both providers offer clinician-reviewed compounded GLP-1 medications and state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. The most distinctive differences supported by the sources are price structure, formulation and delivery options, shipping speed and availability, ongoing support and concierge services, and membership or fee structure.

Price structure and promotions

Refills advertises flat pricing from $99/month (~$5/day) with the same price at every dosage and lists tirzepatide at $159/month. Refills also advertises a 40% new patient special offer. Directmeds promotes a starting price of $147 and a summer promotion that saves $150, but its FAQ lists standard pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. Directmeds notes no separate monthly membership fee and no hidden fees; Refills explicitly states no monthly membership or app fees.

Formulations and pharmacy details

Directmeds offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats and uses U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. Refills offers personalized compounded GLP-1 and a GIP+GLP-1 dual agonist option and states it works with U.S. pharmacies and partner clinicians; Refills notes clinicians come from partner telehealth networks as part of the review process. Both sources state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Shipping, availability, and turnaround

Directmeds advertises free temperature-controlled UPS or FedEx next-day shipping generally in 1 to 2 days, but notes treatment is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. Refills offers free discreet express shipping, available in all 50 US states, and describes a process where prescriptions are processed and shipping/tracking information is provided in about 2 business days after the prescription is written.

Support, consultations, and cancellation

Refills highlights 24/7 health concierge support, unlimited access to patient care team and clinicians at no extra cost, a 3-minute approval quiz, and month-to-month plans with no contracts or cancellation fees. Directmeds offers ongoing clinician and nursing support, telehealth visits included, month-to-month cancellation for standard customers, and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Directmeds also states subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term.

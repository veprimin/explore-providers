Quick answer: MedVi vs Oak Longevity

MedVi and Oak Longevity both sell compounded GLP-1s online, but they differ most on starting price, available medications, support model, and subscription terms. Early verdict: Oak Longevity is the lower-cost option and offers tirzepatide; MedVi emphasizes a simple no-clinic process plus 24/7 certified medical support and a first-month promo at $179.

Top differences at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: starting price and ongoing billing; medication choices; level and availability of clinical support; subscription or membership requirements; and regulatory notes about compounded medications.

Price and billing: promotional vs flat low price

MedVi: promotional first-month price captured as $179 including fast, free shipping with ongoing standalone months at $299 and a three-month plan at $649. Oak Longevity: semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month with one flat price that covers all dosages and no subscription required. Oak also states three-month prescriptions are billed upfront and that there are no subscriptions or membership fees.

Medications offered: semaglutide only vs semaglutide and tirzepatide

MedVi: offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only; no tirzepatide option is supplied. Oak Longevity: offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, with explicit starting prices for both.

Clinical process and support

MedVi: advertises a simple online intake with no clinic visits needed, 24/7 support from certified medical professionals, and a clinician review after intake; an OpenLoop Health clinician meets with an individual after checkout to determine prescription eligibility. Oak Longevity: requires a short online health questionnaire with a licensed physician reviewing cases asynchronously, no video call required, and claims board-certified physicians review every case. Oak includes free health coaching with every plan.

Subscriptions, memberships, and shipping

MedVi: promotional landing evidence states no membership or hidden fees and fast, free shipping for the promo; some pages reference a $99 membership + medication cost as an option but the primary captured evidence emphasizes no membership or hidden fees for the promo. Oak Longevity: explicitly states no subscriptions, no membership fees, free shipping with tracking, and non-recurring monthly billing so you choose to renew payment each month.

Regulatory and outcome notes

Both providers supply compounded medications. The evidence for each includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. MedVi materials also state that results vary and that medications are dispensed at the discretion of medical providers; Oak materials note results vary by individual and list eligibility limits such as BMI restrictions in some cases.

Pricing and plan structure compared

SnagRx vs Telos Rx shows a distinct pricing approach. SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and compounded tirzepatide from $119/month as the 12-month plan rates; month-to-month equivalents are $99 and $149. SnagRx emphasizes a price locked flat at every dose and at renewal. Telos Rx lists semaglutide as low as $99/month and tirzepatide as low as $139/month, with messaging that prices depend on the chosen plan and clinician approval. The SnagRx rate requires a 12-month commitment to get the advertised $69 and $119 figures; Telos Rx states prices are “as low as” amounts and that your price depends on your plan.

Pharmacy partners, shipping speed, and transparency

SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies, states next-day UPS/FedEx temperature-controlled shipping and claims orders ship next-day after approval. Telos Rx materials captured do not publish named pharmacies or verified shipping details in the captured landing-page content; the provider states compounded medications are prescription-only and subject to approval. If named pharmacies and next-day shipping are important to you, SnagRx supplies more specific claims in its materials.

Clinician review, state coverage, and approval process

Both providers require clinician review and a prescription. SnagRx states medical care is delivered by OpenLoop Health, a network of US-licensed clinicians that covers 48 states and that clinician review typically occurs within five hours and always within 24, subject to clinician review. Telos Rx says a US-licensed provider decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is appropriate and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Telos Rx captured content warns eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change and recommends confirming those details before enrollment.

Compounded medications and regulatory note

SnagRx and Telos Rx both offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. Both sources include the regulatory caution that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. SnagRx materials further state compounded GLP-1s are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but are not FDA-approved finished products, and that individuals may receive medication that looks different than portrayed. Telos Rx captured content likewise states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prescription-only after provider review.

Guarantees, refunds, membership, and cancellation

SnagRx states no membership fee, free shipping, cancel anytime, a full refund if the prescription is declined, and a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms that apply. SnagRx also emphasizes “price locked flat at every dose, for life” on enrolled rate. Telos Rx materials captured note cancel anytime with no penalties and that you pay $0 if not approved; other specific guarantees or pharmacy names were not published in the captured landing-page content. Both providers advise that individual results vary and are not guaranteed.

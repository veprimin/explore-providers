How Eden Health vs JoinSkinny differ on price and billing

Both services say no insurance is required, but their pricing structure is different. Eden Health advertises monthly pricing at every dose with semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129 and a lifetime price guarantee. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149, offers a $140 promotional discount in some offers, and supports monthly and 3, 6, or 12 month bundles.

Shipping speed, handling, and fulfillment

Eden Health highlights free expedited shipping. JoinSkinny specifies free temperature controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days for prescriptions shipped same day when received before 2 pm Central, with weekend handling described (Friday to Sunday shipped Monday) and insured shipments.

Care model, telemedicine, and ongoing support

Both describe clinician-reviewed online care and telemedicine. Eden positions its offering as a guided GLP-1 program combining medication, care, and support under one monthly price. JoinSkinny emphasizes a quick health questionnaire, licensed provider review, telemedicine visits, ongoing support, monthly check ins, dose adjustments, and 24/7 chat. JoinSkinny also states all care is delivered by licensed U.S. providers and that prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners.

Medication types, dosing, and regulatory notes

Eden lists semaglutide and tirzepatide pricing and explains semaglutide helps regulate appetite signals; it adds that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Eden also notes compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options, flat-rate dosing, and says compounded medications are not FDA approved and not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny states providers determine starting dose based on medical history and that dose adjustments are guided by the provider at no extra cost.

Conclusion: Choose based on price guarantee versus shipping and bundles

Quick verdict: Sunlight emphasizes low starting price and a “same price forever” promise while He & She MD bundles GLP-1 meds with a coaching app and 1:1 clinician messaging. If price stability matters pick Sunlight; if built in coaching matters pick He & She MD. This article compares Sunlight vs He & She MD across price, program structure, medication formats, coaching and regulatory notes.

See Sunlight plans starting at $88/mo

Price and billing: starting rates and renewal structure

Sunlight advertises personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo and states “Same price forever” with no price increases ever and no hidden fees. He & She MD lists semaglutide from $139/mo and tirzepatide from $199/mo as promotional six month intro rates that renew higher. He & She MD captures that the headline prices are six month introductory rates that renew 30 to 45 percent higher and that pricing is structured as six month terms rather than true month to month.

Program structure and support: coaching and clinician access

Sunlight presents a 100 percent online medical visit with prescription and telehealth included. He & She MD bundles a coaching app that includes 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific coaching and progress tracking at no extra cost. That makes He & She MD notable for integrated coaching and clinician messaging while Sunlight emphasizes the telehealth visit and prescription as included features.

Check Sunlight telehealth and prescription details

Medication formats and availability

Both providers offer multiple formats. Sunlight lists injections and oral options. He & She MD offers semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, or tirzepatide injection. He & She MD specifies pricing per medication type with promo and renewal rates; Sunlight lists personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo but notes people should confirm eligibility, medication and state availability before enrollment.

Shipping, fees, membership and cancellation

Sunlight advertises free shipping in 1 to 2 days, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime with the claim of “Same price every month”. He & She MD notes free expedited shipping, no membership fee, and highlights that promo pricing applies to the first six month term and then renews at the standard six month price. Both state no insurance required.

Regulatory and quality notes

Sunlight includes a regulatory note that medication specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. He & She MD states that medications included in the GLP-1 Program are produced in USP compliant, state regulated pharmacies but are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality and labels compounded medications as not FDA approved. These are distinct regulatory statements to consider when comparing Sunlight vs He & She MD.

Conclusion: Choose based on price stability or integrated coaching

Sunlight vs He & She MD comes down to priorities. Sunlight is best for people who prioritize a low starting price and a stated same price forever commitment while confirming eligibility, medication and state availability before enrollment. He & She MD is best for people who want an integrated coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging and progress tracking bundled with GLP-1 medication but who understand headline prices are six month intro rates that renew higher. Consider the regulatory notes for compounded products and confirm approval status where relevant.

Choose Sunlight if you want a lower advertised starting price and a same price forever claim and will confirm eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment. Choose He & She MD if you want coaching and clinician access built into the plan rather than sold separately.

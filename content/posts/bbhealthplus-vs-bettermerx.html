How the two compare at a glance

This direct comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences between Bodybuilding Health+ and BetterMe Rx: pricing and billing structure, medication formats, program breadth and extra services, shipping and delivery, and regulatory status of compounded medications. Both use U.S.-licensed providers and offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, with clinician review required before treatment.

Pricing and billing structure

Bodybuilding Health+ lists semaglutide starting at $179/mo and tirzepatide from $209/mo, and it highlights single-month to 12-month bundles that reduce the monthly cost for longer commitments. Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes no monthly membership and cancel anytime access. BetterMe Rx advertises lower monthly-equivalent prices on longer prepaid plans, with compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan; semaglutide injection is $205 monthly or as low as $145/month on a 12-month plan, and tirzepatide injection is $275 monthly or as low as $215/month. BetterMe Rx notes that the lowest prices require a 12-month plan and that longer plans are billed according to the selected term.

Medication formats and options

BetterMe Rx offers four medication and administration options: injectable semaglutide and tirzepatide plus oral semaglutide and oral tirzepatide, with oral tirzepatide presented at parity with injection pricing in some plans. Bodybuilding Health+ offers GLP-1 options including compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and packages across single-month to 12-month bundles, and also lists NAD+, sermorelin and ED options on the same platform.

Program breadth, clinical oversight and extras

Bodybuilding Health+ positions itself as a multi-program telehealth platform for weight management, longevity and sexual performance, listing NAD+ and sermorelin from $129/mo and ED options from $96/mo, and names medical oversight by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D. as part of its model. BetterMe Rx focuses specifically on compounded GLP-1 options with provider review, supplies included, and a free evaluation; it emphasizes injectable and needle-free oral options for customers comfortable with prepaid multi-month billing.

Shipping and cancellation

BetterMe Rx includes pharmacy compounding, supplies and free 1–2 day shipping as part of its offering. Bodybuilding Health+ states medications are provider-reviewed and shipped to your door; both note no membership fee and that users can cancel, with Bodybuilding Health+ repeatedly stating no monthly membership and cancel anytime flexibility while BetterMe Rx requires longer prepaid plans to reach its lowest monthly equivalents.

Regulatory status

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications prepared by licensed U.S. pharmacies, and both explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Treatment availability is subject to clinician review and medical appropriateness for each patient.

Which to choose

Choose Bodybuilding Health+ if you want a wider multi-program telehealth platform with GLP-1 options starting at $179/mo, explicit no monthly membership language, cancel-anytime flexibility, and additional longevity and sexual performance services on the same site. Choose BetterMe Rx if you prioritize injectable and oral compounded GLP-1 formats, free 1–2 day shipping, and the potential for lower monthly-equivalent pricing as low as $145/mo when you select a 12-month plan.

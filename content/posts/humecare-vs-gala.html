HumeCare+ vs Gala: quick verdict

HumeCare+ focuses on a bundled protocol that pairs physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with glycine and B vitamins plus monthly body-composition scans, starting at $199/month for semaglutide. Gala emphasizes a flat all-inclusive telehealth model with provider visits, compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, and free two-day shipping, with a page advertising a $220 promotion and a listed starting price of $179. Early verdict: choose HumeCare+ if you want protocolized muscle-preserving support and body-composition tracking; choose Gala if you prefer a flat-price, no-membership telehealth experience with fast shipping.

How the two programs differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences: bundled supportive ingredients and scans, pricing transparency and offers, shipping and delivery, membership and cancellation terms, and clinician access and monitoring. Both sell compounded GLP-1 options and state that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Both note that compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated for safety, quality, or efficacy.

1. Protocol and supportive ingredients

HumeCare+ sells a bundled protocol rather than just a prescription. Its plans wrap physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide together with glycine, vitamin B12, and B6, which HumeCare+ positions as muscle-preserving and side-effect support. Gala offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with provider check-ins but does not advertise built-in glycine or B vitamins as part of its base offering in the captured content.

2. Body-composition tracking vs standard monitoring

HumeCare+ includes monthly body-composition scans that track fat versus lean mass over time and an app for medication tracking and food logging. Gala describes provider check-ins and dose monitoring included but does not present body-composition scans in the provided evidence.

3. Pricing structure and promotions

HumeCare+ lists compounded semaglutide from $199/month and a tirzepatide protocol with B12 and glycine from $299/month. HumeCare+ also advertises a 10% or your money back promise over six months with terms to verify. Gala markets a flat all-inclusive price covering provider visit, medication, shipping and ongoing care, and the page advertises a $220 promotion; a starting price of $179 appears in the captured metadata but the readable page text emphasized a flat price and a separate line saying compounded semaglutide from $249/month.

4. Shipping, membership, and cancellation

HumeCare+ offers free next-day delivery and says you can cancel your subscription anytime. Gala advertises free two-day shipping, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime. Both providers state cancel anytime in the captured evidence.

5. Clinician access and care model

HumeCare+ highlights unlimited clinician access and a U.S. care team available 24/7, plus continuous physician optimization. Gala emphasizes provider visit and prescription included and provider check-ins and dose monitoring as part of a fully online telehealth model.

What the evidence supports and limits

Both providers supply compounded GLP-1 medications and emphasize telehealth delivery. The evidence shows HumeCare+ claims a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months and cites internal analysis of member results; those claims carry eligibility and documentation caveats and should be verified in full with the provider. Gala’s page markets a $220 discount and a single flat price that covers visits and shipping; the readable page text did not expose a consistent base dollar price. Neither provider’s captured content replaces medical advice. Both state treatment availability is subject to clinician review.

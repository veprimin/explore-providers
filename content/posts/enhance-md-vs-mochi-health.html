Enhance MD vs Mochi Health: quick contrast

This direct comparison highlights the most distinctive differences: starting price and introductory offers, clinical visit model, medication lineup and compounding status, and ongoing pricing transparency. The primary keyword Enhance MD vs Mochi Health appears here to orient search intent.

1) Starting price and introductory offers

Enhance MD advertises semaglutide starting at $49 and tirzepatide starting at $99 for the first month, calling these among the lowest first-month prices in the market for either compound. Mochi Health promotes 50% off your first month with code BESTWLHALFOFF and lists compounded semaglutide starting $99 (promotional examples show reduced rates to $60). Both providers reference first-month promotions; exact ongoing costs vary by platform.

2) Clinical access model and consultation style

Enhance MD operates as a licensed telehealth platform with clinician-directed review; the source notes clinician review is required and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Mochi Health distinguishes itself by offering live video visits with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes, a synchronous model that the source says differs from most competitors that use asynchronous text review. Mochi also touts 24/7 support and ongoing appointment availability.

3) Medication options and compounding notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications rather than branded finished products. Enhance MD explicitly offers both semaglutide and tirzepatide on one platform. Mochi Health lists compounded semaglutide, compounded tirzepatide, a compounded semaglutide pill, and other compounded options. The regulatory note for each source states: Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

4) Ongoing pricing and transparency

Enhance MD emphasizes flat-rate pricing: the same price at every dose with no fee escalation during titration, and positions that as a budgeting advantage. The source also notes the introductory $49/$99 rates are first-month prices and that ongoing flat-rate pricing is higher and not always prominently published before intake. Mochi Health uses a membership-plus-medication model where total monthly cost varies by plan and medication; the source says ongoing pricing is less transparent than flat-rate competitors and that members continue care beyond the first month.

Quick verdict: Start Willow vs He & She MD

Start Willow and He & She MD both sell compounded GLP-1 medications but differ most on pricing structure, bundled coaching, medication formats, shipping promises, and how they present regulatory notes. Early verdict: pick Start Willow if you want a direct-pay, no-membership option with a 3-day delivery guarantee; pick He & She MD if you value included 1:1 clinician messaging and a coaching app with lower introductory pricing.

How pricing and term structure compare

Start Willow advertises compound semaglutide from $249 per month for the first month and $299/month ongoing. He & She MD advertises semaglutide injections starting at $139/month as an introductory six-month rate that renews higher; oral semaglutide and tirzepatide injections start at $199/month introductory and also renew at higher six-month prices. The difference is simple: Start Willow shows a stable direct-pay monthly price after the first month while He & She MD uses six-month promotional terms that renew at a higher rate.

Support and program features: coaching vs streamlined prescribing

He & She MD bundles a coaching app, 1:1 clinician messaging, and GLP-1 specific progress tracking at no extra cost with each plan. This is core to their pitch: meds plus structured support. Start Willow emphasizes a fully online, direct-pay model with clinician review but the primary highlights are access without insurance and no membership fees rather than bundled coaching.

Medication formats and regulatory note

He & She MD lists three formats: semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection. Start Willow focuses on a once-weekly compounded semaglutide injection as a customized option. Both providers dispense compounded GLP-1 medications; the sources explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. He & She MD notes its medications are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies and that not all customers will qualify for a prescription.

Shipping and delivery promises

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less from its licensed pharmacy network and advertises same day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping in some messaging. He & She MD offers free expedited shipping and ships medication straight to your door. The providers both promise expedited delivery but Start Willow specifies a 3-day delivery guarantee.

Side effects, tapering, and clinical oversight

Start Willow materials note common semaglutide side effects such as nausea and recommend working with a Willow physician to taper dose to manage weight changes after stopping. He & She MD requires a consultation and prescription by a clinician through its platform and states not all customers will qualify. Both providers therefore rely on clinician review before dispensing compounded medication.

Bottom line on Start Willow vs He & She MD

Start Willow and He & She MD serve different priorities. Start Willow is set up as a direct-pay option that highlights no insurance required, no membership fees, and a 3-day delivery guarantee for compounded semaglutide priced from $249 initially and $299/month ongoing. He & She MD pairs lower introductory pricing with a coaching app and 1:1 clinician messaging, but its headline rates apply for six-month terms and renew at higher prices. The primary differences to weigh are pricing structure and term length, the presence or absence of bundled coaching, medication format options, and the speed of shipping.

Key differences at a glance for JoinFridays vs Hers

This comparison uses only captured provider statements. It focuses on pricing and promos, medication type and regulatory notes, membership and billing, and available medication options and care model.

1) Pricing and promotions

JoinFridays lists options “Starting at $117/month” and highlights “as low as $117/mo” on semaglutide, with tirzepatide “as low as $198/mo” in some plans and multi-month plans that lower effective monthly rates. JoinFridays advertises active promo codes including NEWYOU12 and NYNY12 for specified savings. Hers lists “Wegovy® pill from $149/mo” and injections “from $199/mo” in captured material, and states prices are “From $149/mo” while also noting that a membership is required and medication and membership are billed separately.

2) Medication type and regulatory note

JoinFridays provides compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide options and explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Hers offers FDA-approved Wegovy® and other brand-name GLP-1s, noting Wegovy® is FDA-approved and that Hers does not offer compounded medications.

3) Membership, billing, and cancellation

JoinFridays emphasizes “No membership fees, cancel anytime” and says there are no long-term contracts. Hers specifies an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required, with an initial $39 first month that auto-renews at $149/month thereafter, and that medication is not available without an active membership. Hers also notes membership and medication are billed separately and a prescription is not guaranteed.

4) Medication selection and clinician oversight

JoinFridays lists both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide on one platform, positions tirzepatide as “Most Effective” and semaglutide as “Most Affordable,” and emphasizes personalized clinician-guided care with dosing guidance. Hers highlights FDA-approved Wegovy® with a “personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins” and states Ozempic® may be prescribed off-label at clinician discretion while Wegovy® and Zepbound® are FDA-approved for weight loss.

Omzo vs Measured: which fits your priorities? Early verdict: choose Omzo if you want clinician-guided plans with access to provider-written branded prescriptions; choose Measured if low promotional pricing, same-day Rx decisions, and free two-day shipping matter most.

Explore Omzo clinician-guided plans

How they differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on five key differences supported by source material: starting price and promotional pricing, prescribing model and clinical oversight, compounded versus branded medication options, shipping and delivery promises, and support features like dietitian access and messaging.

Pricing and promotional offers

Measured advertises promotional compounded semaglutide from $89 per month and compounded tirzepatide from $189 per month, plus promotional messaging such as “From $89/mo.” Omzo lists semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279, describing its programs as personalized and clinician-guided.

Prescribing model and clinical oversight

Omzo connects customers with licensed U.S. providers in third party networks; providers determine eligibility and may prescribe based on their professional judgment. Omzo states clinical services are provided by third party networks of U.S.-licensed clinicians and that providers make the final determination to prescribe.

Measured emphasizes same-day prescription decisions and notes that all prescriptions are issued at a provider’s discretion. Measured pairs compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with same-day Rx decisions and repeatedly states Rx required and clinician determination of medical appropriateness.

Compounded versus branded medication handling

Both providers offer compounded medications and include regulatory notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Omzo specifically calls out a Compounded Medication Program prepared by state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacies and notes these compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. Omzo also says providers may write prescriptions for branded medications and that Omzo does not sell, dispense, or ship branded medications; customers are responsible for filling those prescriptions at a pharmacy.

Shipping, delivery and cancellation features

Measured advertises free two-day shipping on every order and promotes fast and free shipping and cancel anytime terms. Omzo highlights fast, discreet delivery and notes medication is prepared and shipped by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy when using its compounded medication program; specific shipping timelines are not supplied in the Omzo evidence.

Support, outcomes messaging, and evidence notes

Measured highlights unlimited messaging, a 24/7 care team, dietitian support, and a 4.8 star Trustpilot rating from 51,000+ users in its captured content. Measured also provides an internal average result claim: based on internal analysis of members reporting weight data at 6 months, average loss was 31 lbs, with the qualifier that individual results vary and are not guaranteed and that internal outcome claims are not independent evidence.

Omzo focuses on clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed U.S. providers and shares a customer example about steady weekly progress; Omzo explicitly states that individual results may vary, weight loss is not guaranteed, and that results reflect patient experiences related to Omzo by medical providers rather than public clinical studies.

Key distinctions summarized

  • Cost focus: Measured promotes lower promotional monthly pricing from $89 while Omzo lists higher starting prices and emphasizes personalized care.
  • Clinical approach: Omzo highlights clinician-guided plans and third party licensed providers; Measured highlights speed of same-day Rx decisions and additional dietitian support.
  • Branded Rx option: Omzo notes providers may write prescriptions for branded medications but Omzo does not fill them; Measured does not highlight branded Rx handling in the captured evidence.
  • Shipping promise: Measured promotes free two-day shipping; Omzo notes fast, discreet delivery without specific two-day claims in the captured content.

Conclusion: Choose based on what matters to you

Choose Omzo if you prioritize clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed U.S. providers and the option that a provider may write a prescription for branded medications while recognizing its compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that starting prices are higher.

Clinical model, prescriptions, and regulatory notes

Both platforms operate online with telehealth reviews. Sunlight emphasizes prescription and telehealth included and a 100% online medical visit. MedVi uses an online intake and clinician review and states OpenLoop Health clinicians meet with individuals after checkout and retain prescribing discretion. MedVi highlights that compounded medications are produced in FDA regulated facilities but are not FDA approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Sunlight’s captured content includes a regulatory reminder that medication specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Support and program features

Sunlight promotes free and fast shipping in 1 to 2 days, no monthly membership, cancel anytime, and same price every month as landing-page claims. MedVi highlights 24/7 support from certified medical professionals, a physician review, personalized plan, 1:1 guidance, and a metabolic report included in program cost.

Ongoing costs and what to expect after the promo

Sunlight’s messaging states same price forever and no price increases ever. MedVi explicitly warns the promotional first month is lower and that ongoing monthly cost rises to $299 for a standalone month while offering a three-month plan at $649. MedVi FAQ language also notes the ongoing price is substantially higher than the first-month promo.

Comparing IVIM Health vs Mochi Health: quick verdict up front — IVIM stands out for its dual 4.9-star third-party ratings and a no-obligation $75 consult plus a free month code GIFT75, while Mochi stands out for live, bookable video visits in about 5 minutes and a 50% off first month offer with code BESTWLHALFOFF.

Ratings, trust signals, and published outcomes

IVIM Health emphasizes third-party verification: it reports a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot, an unusually strong satisfaction signal cited repeatedly in its materials. IVIM also publishes internal and retrospective observational study results, including a claim that patients following IVIM’s protocol lost an average of 46% more weight over 68 weeks compared to a published clinical GLP-1 trial; IVIM notes this is not a head-to-head study and is a proprietary claim.

Mochi Health does not present a matching dual third-party rating claim in the supplied evidence. Mochi highlights operational legitimacy via licensed telehealth services and promotes outcomes as self-reported averages for members, noting individual results vary and are not guaranteed.

Clinical model and patient interaction

IVIM Health delivers a clinician-guided program called GLP-1 ID with structured weekly dose adjustments and clinician oversight. The model is described as a personalized, clinician-guided titration protocol rather than a fixed-dose plan, with prescriptions issued after clinical evaluation.

Mochi Health’s defining clinical difference is synchronous live video visits with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes, which the provider contrasts with the asynchronous text-review model common among competitors. Mochi also promotes 24/7 support and ongoing appointment availability.

Pricing structure, promos, and transparency

Both companies use a membership or direct-pay model and feature promotional offers, but the structures differ. IVIM advertises a free month membership via code GIFT75, a no-obligation free consult valued at $75, and published starting prices for compounded medications (semaglutide $75/mo, tirzepatide $133/mo) plus a $74.99/mo program fee; IVIM’s FAQ also states a first month $99 and ongoing $174/month in one place of its materials.

Mochi advertises 50% off the first month with code BESTWLHALFOFF and lists membership-plus-medication pricing starting from $99/month, with specific promotional starts for some compounded meds (semaglutide starting $99, tirzepatide starting $199) and statements that first-month promotional rates vary and ongoing cost depends on plan and medication. Mochi emphasizes membership-plus-medication pricing rather than a single flat rate.

Medications offered and regulatory notes

Both IVIM and Mochi prescribe compounded GLP-1 medications. IVIM explicitly states its offerings include compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and repeatedly notes these are compounded medications that are not FDA-approved and have not undergone FDA review for safety, efficacy, or quality. IVIM also states medications are prepared by independent licensed compounding pharmacies and may not be available in all states.

Mochi similarly advertises compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options, a compounded oral semaglutide pill, and other compounded agents with starting prices; Mochi’s materials also include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

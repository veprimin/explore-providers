Primary differences at a glance: HumeCare+ vs Enhance MD

Compare the two providers by price structure, protocol composition and monitoring, compound availability, clinician access and regulatory notes. Each of these differences is supported by the source material below.

1. Pricing model and starting cost

HumeCare+ lists semaglutide starting at $199/month and tirzepatide plus B12 and glycine from $299/month. Enhance MD advertises semaglutide from $49 for the first month and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month, with flat-rate pricing thereafter. Enhance MD highlights that its flat-rate means the same price at every dose, avoiding escalation during titration.

2. Protocol composition and added supports

HumeCare+ sells a bundled protocol: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide combined with glycine, vitamins B6 and B12, a body-composition scan, and an app. The company positions these supports as muscle-preserving and as help for side effects. Enhance MD emphasizes clinician-directed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide availability but does not claim bundled glycine or B vitamins as part of the protocol in the supplied evidence.

3. Monitoring, scans and clinician access

HumeCare+ includes monthly body-composition scans that track fat versus lean mass and offers unlimited clinician access with a U.S. care team available 24/7, according to the source. Enhance MD documents metabolic lab testing every six months in some published pricing examples and promotes clinician-directed review, with more than 10,000 patients treated; its source emphasizes flat-rate pricing and clinical volume rather than monthly scans.

4. Guarantees, claims and regulatory notes

HumeCare+ advertises a 10%-or-money-back promise over six months, and notes that eligibility and documentation requirements apply; the source says verify full terms directly with HumeCare+. Both providers’ supplied materials state that their compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Treatment from either provider is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Conclusion: HumeCare+ vs Enhance MD

HumeCare+ vs Enhance MD boils down to bundled support and monitoring versus entry cost and flat-rate transparency. HumeCare+ bundles glycine and B vitamins, monthly body-composition scans, clinician access, and a 10%-or-money-back promise; Enhance MD emphasizes a very low first-month price for semaglutide and tirzepatide and ongoing flat-rate pricing with no dose-based escalation. Both use compounded medications and require clinician determination that treatment is medically appropriate.

Choose HumeCare+ if you want a ready-made protocol that includes muscle-preserving additives, monthly body-composition scans, continuous clinician access, and a stated 10%-or-money-back promise.

Choose Enhance MD if your primary concern is minimizing initial out-of-pocket cost and having flat-rate pricing that stays the same at every dose, with access to both semaglutide and tirzepatide on one platform.

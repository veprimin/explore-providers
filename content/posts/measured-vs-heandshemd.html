Measured vs He & She MD is primarily a choice between lower promotional pricing and fast shipping from Measured versus bundled coaching and multiformat medication options from He & She MD. Early verdict: Measured is the lower promotional price with same-day Rx decisions and free two-day shipping, while He & She MD bundles coaching, 1:1 clinician messaging, and progress tracking with six-month intro rates that renew at higher prices.

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive, supported differences: starting price and promotional structure, shipping and prescription timing, support and coaching features, medication formats offered, and regulatory notes about compounded medications. Throughout the article we compare Measured and He & She MD directly using only supplied facts.

Starting price and promotional structure

Measured advertises promotional compounded semaglutide from $89 per month and compounded tirzepatide from $189 per month. He & She MD lists semaglutide injections starting at $139 per month and oral semaglutide or tirzepatide injections at $199 per month as six-month introductory rates. Important qualifier: He & She MD headline prices are six-month intro rates that renew higher; the provider states those promo rates apply to the first six months and then plans renew at the standard six-month price.

Shipping speed and prescription timing

Measured highlights free two-day shipping on every order and same-day prescription decisions. He & She MD offers free expedited shipping as well, and notes medication is shipped straight to your door. The difference is Measured emphasizes same-day Rx decisions while He & She MD emphasizes expedited shipping paired with its app and six-month program structure.

Support, coaching, and messaging

Measured lists unlimited provider messaging, a 24/7 Care Team, and dietitian support as features. He & She MD bundles its app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific coaching and progress tracking at no extra cost. Both providers include clinician messaging, but He & She MD positions coaching and tracking as integrated in the app while Measured highlights dietitian support and unlimited messaging plus a 24/7 care team.

Medication formats and clinical notes

Measured pairs compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and promotes both compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide options. He & She MD provides three formats: semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection. He & She MD explicitly lists oral semaglutide as an offered format in addition to injections.

Regulatory and outcome qualifiers

Both providers include regulatory language: compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Measured also reports an internal analysis claim about average member weight loss at six months but qualifies that internal outcome claims are not independent evidence and individual results vary. He & She MD notes some retrospective self-reported figures and emphasizes that promo pricing is introductory and subject to renewal terms.

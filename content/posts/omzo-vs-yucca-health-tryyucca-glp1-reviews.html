Quick answer: Omzo vs Yucca Health

Omzo is clinician guided with compounded semaglutide starting at $179 and options to get prescriptions for branded drugs, while Yucca Health offers lower starting monthly pricing for compounded semaglutide from $146/month and compounded tirzepatide from $258/month with fast online approval. Early verdict: choose Omzo for clinician-guided plans with clinician review and branded prescription pathways; choose Yucca Health for lower monthly pricing, fast 24 hour approval, and BNPL options.

Top differences at a glance

The most distinctive supported differences are price structure, medication options and regulatory notes, approval and shipping speed, and payment or financing options. Below we compare these points directly using only the supplied facts.

Price and plan structure

Omzo reports semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279 as program entry points. Yucca Health reports compounded semaglutide from $146/month and tirzepatide from $258/month with multi month plans such as a 6 month semaglutide plan that totals $875. Yucca presents explicit monthly and multi month pricing while Omzo lists starting prices for each medication program.

Medication sourcing and regulatory status

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide programs. Omzo emphasizes a compounded medication program shipped by a state licensed sterile compounding pharmacy and explicitly states these compounded drugs are not FDA approved. Omzo also notes providers may instead write prescriptions for branded FDA approved medications which the patient fills at their chosen pharmacy. Yucca Health also confirms its compounded medications are not FDA approved and that medications are prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies.

Clinical review, approvals, and logistics

Omzo connects patients with licensed medical providers who determine eligibility and providers make the final determination to prescribe. Omzo states clinical services are provided by third party networks of U S licensed clinicians and that the assessment on Omzo does not create a doctor patient relationship. Yucca Health states licensed providers operate in all 50 states, online intake takes about 10 minutes, and a licensed provider typically issues a decision within 24 hours. Yucca Health also describes UPS 2 Day Air shipping on most orders and most patients receive their first shipment within 3 to 5 business days of completing intake.

Payment and convenience features

Omzo notes medication costs are included in fees collected and that FSA and HSA eligibility may apply but details should be confirmed. Yucca Health lists multiple Buy Now Pay Later options including Klarna, Afterpay, and Affirm, a Lifetime Price Lock code to freeze pricing, and states it does not accept health insurance. Yucca highlights fast online approval and free 2 day shipping as convenience features; Omzo highlights fast discreet delivery and potential FSA and HSA eligibility.

Evidence and outcome claims

Omzo presents patient experience text such as after three months a user reported fewer cravings and steady weekly progress and warns that user results reflect internal paid order statistics and may vary. Omzo states compounded medications offered are not FDA approved and have not been evaluated by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality. Yucca Health cites clinical research ranges when describing expected average weight loss for semaglutide and tirzepatide and also notes that individual results vary and no warranties are provided. Both providers emphasize that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Personalization and care model

Found describes a “biology-first” approach: a thorough medical evaluation that builds a personalized program around metabolic biology, hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavioral factors. Found positions itself as a personalized medically supervised weight loss program rather than a platform purely focused on GLP-1 medication delivery.

Start Willow offers a personalized treatment plan as well, describing compounded semaglutide prescribed by a doctor “as part of a personalized treatment plan.” Start Willow stresses ease for patients who want clinically guided personalization without insurance hurdles and highlights physician collaboration for compounded formulations.

Medication types and regulatory notes

Both providers reference compounded medications and include regulatory qualifiers. Found lists tirzepatide and semaglutide and explicitly states “Compounded GLP-1s are not FDA- approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy, and not available in all states.” Found also notes certain medications are FDA-approved for conditions other than weight and that Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility.

Start Willow provides compounded semaglutide described as “not approved by the FDA, and the FDA does not review compounded medications for safety, effectiveness, or quality.” Start Willow emphasizes compounded alternatives to branded products and that a licensed clinician determines appropriate medication during online review.

Shipping, delivery and convenience

Found materials captured do not specify a guaranteed delivery window in the provided evidence; they emphasize insurance handling and program design. Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less and notes same day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping from its pharmacy network, presenting faster, predictable fulfillment as a core strength.

Ongoing costs, commitments and limitations

Found highlights flexible pricing, cancel anytime language in bullets, and varying membership and plan price points. Found advertises offers and specific program pricing details tied to commitments in captured landing page text and warns that pricing, terms, copays, coinsurance and deductibles vary.

Start Willow warns that the price rises from $249 first month to $299/month ongoing and states that $299/month ongoing is on the higher end of the list provided in its FAQ content. Start Willow has no membership fees but notes higher ongoing costs compared with some other platforms.

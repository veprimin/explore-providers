Sesame Care vs Hers answers a core question quickly: Sesame Care sells clinician choice, flexible telehealth and subscription care starting at $59/mo*, while Hers emphasizes FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy from $149/mo with a required membership. Early verdict: pick Sesame Care for clinician choice and lower starting subscription fees; pick Hers if you want brand-name Wegovy and ongoing dose check-ins.

How they differ at a glance: model, price, and product

Both platforms offer telehealth weight-loss care and GLP-1 medications, but their emphases differ. Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace focused on letting patients choose clinicians, with ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo* and cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo. Hers focuses on FDA-approved, brand-name Wegovy® from $149/mo and offers a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins. Hers notes an active membership is required and that medication is not available without membership.

Distinctive difference 1: clinician choice versus a curated care team

Sesame Care highlights a marketplace model that lets patients browse and select clinicians by specialty, availability and pricing so the experience can vary by clinician. Hers positions a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins. If you want to shop clinicians and choose a provider, Sesame Care supports that; if you prefer a defined care team and routine dose check-ins, Hers emphasizes that structure.

Distinctive difference 2: pricing structure and membership

Sesame Care promotes subscription services “as low as $59/mo*” for ongoing visits, with medication costs listed separately and varying by insurance. It also lists cash pay medication prices and price-with-insurance examples. Hers lists Wegovy® from $149/mo and notes an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required with initial $39 then auto-renew at $149/mo; it states total cost includes both membership fee and medication when prescribed and that medication is not available without membership.

Distinctive difference 3: branded versus compounded policy

Hers emphasizes FDA-approved, brand-name Wegovy® and explicitly states no compounded medications. Sesame Care lists branded semaglutide and tirzepatide options and provides price tiers and a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

What their evidence says about results and limitations

Sesame Care cites milestones based on a study of injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg in non-diabetic adults with obesity or overweight when paired with lifestyle changes and notes results may vary. Hers highlights FDA-approved options and example weight outcomes in its captured evidence but also notes that treatments are available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Bottom line: Sesame Care vs Hers

Sesame Care vs Hers comes down to choice versus guaranteed brand-name pathways. Sesame Care is best for people who want broad telehealth choice, flexible scheduling and lower starting subscription pricing. Hers is best for people who prioritize FDA-approved Wegovy® brand-name treatment with a personalized care team and ongoing dose check-ins, and who accept a membership requirement.

Sprout Health vs JoinSkinny: both are telehealth GLP-1 options but they diverge on speed, pricing structure, medication sourcing, shipping, and ongoing support. Early verdict: Sprout Health prioritizes lightning-quick approval and a $50 first-month discount; JoinSkinny emphasizes bundled pricing, shipping speed, and clinician-monitored ongoing care.

How they differ at a glance

Comparing Sprout Health vs JoinSkinny shows five distinctive differences supported by the sources: initial discount and ongoing price, approval speed and onboarding friction, whether medication is compounded or filled by U.S. pharmacies, shipping logistics and timelines, and plan flexibility including bundles and cancellations.

Pricing and discounts

Sprout Health offers a $50 off first month, bringing the initial cost to $199, with ongoing pricing of $249/month after the discount. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149, offers multi-month bundles (3, 6, 12 months), and an example promotional discount of $140. Sprout Health positions the discount as an introductory offer; JoinSkinny uses bundle options and flat-rate dosing to keep monthly price consistent across dosage changes.

Approval speed and onboarding

Sprout Health markets a “lightning-quick approval” process and a no-frills, fast-entry experience after an online intake and clinician review. JoinSkinny also uses a quick health questionnaire and licensed provider review, but emphasizes ongoing monitoring, dose adjustments, monthly check-ins, and 24/7 chat. If speed of initial approval is the priority, Sprout Health highlights that advantage; if continuing clinical support and monitoring matter more, JoinSkinny emphasizes that ongoing care.

Medication sourcing and regulatory notes

Sprout Health prescribes compounded semaglutide after clinician review and notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny states prescriptions are filled by licensed U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners and also notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Both providers include regulatory qualifiers about compounded products; JoinSkinny additionally highlights use of branded and FDA-regulated pharmacy partners when applicable.

Shipping, delivery, and logistics

JoinSkinny advertises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, with tracking and insured shipments. Sprout Health notes home delivery following approval but the captured page did not provide verified shipping timelines or temperature-control details. If next-day temperature-controlled shipping is important, JoinSkinny supplies specific timelines and insured shipment language in the source evidence.

Claims about outcomes and limits

Sprout Health frames outcomes as 10–26% body weight loss over 12 months based on GLP-1 clinical outcome literature and notes it does not offer a formal outcome guarantee. JoinSkinny cites clinical ranges of 15–22% body weight loss over 12–15 months when paired with consistent use and lifestyle guidance. Both sources state individual results vary and include regulatory qualifiers about compounded products.

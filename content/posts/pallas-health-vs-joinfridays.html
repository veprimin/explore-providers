Pallas Health vs JoinFridays compares two telehealth GLP-1 options. Quick verdict: Pallas centers on clinician choice between brand-name pens and US-compounded GLP-1s with an all-in plan price, while JoinFridays emphasizes the lowest starting price, flexible cancellation, and aggressive promo codes.

Pallas Health vs JoinFridays: 3 to 5 key differences

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: medication route and options, starting price and pricing structure, membership and cancellation terms, promo codes and discounts, and regulatory notes about compounded medication.

Medication options and clinician role

Pallas Health offers both FDA-approved brand-name GLP-1s (Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound, Mounjaro) and clinician-personalized compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared in the USA when a brand medication is not the right fit. JoinFridays offers compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide and also advertises brand-name options; both platforms require a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness.

Pricing and how costs are structured

Pallas Health lists compounded semaglutide at $139 for the first month, then $597 every 12 weeks (a $199/month average); compounded tirzepatide starts at $179 first month, then $897 every 12 weeks (a $299/month average). Pallas also posts cash-pay brand-name monthly figures for Wegovy, Zepbound, Ozempic and Mounjaro. JoinFridays advertises starting pricing at $117/month, with standalone month and multi-month options; the site notes a one-month standalone plan is $249 and multi-month plans reduce the effective monthly rate.

Membership, cancellation, and plan cadence

Pallas Health charges no membership fee and includes provider care, check-ins and messaging inside the plan price. Compounded plans renew every 12 weeks, which means cancellation timing matters. JoinFridays emphasizes no long-term contracts, no membership fees, and cancel anytime flexibility; multi-month commitments lower effective monthly cost on JoinFridays.

Promo codes and perceived value

JoinFridays highlights active promo codes such as NEWYOU12 and NYNY12 with claimed up to $500 off tirzepatide and up to $400 off semaglutide, and promotes savings versus retail. Pallas does not feature promo-code marketing in the supplied evidence; its price differentiation comes from offering either branded pens or compounded preparations and an introductory compounded-month price.

Regulatory and product notes

Both sources include the regulatory caution that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Pallas notes compounded medications are prepared by US-licensed compounding pharmacies and that Pallas does not manufacture or compound medication. JoinFridays similarly notes that compounded products will differ in appearance from website images.

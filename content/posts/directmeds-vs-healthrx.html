Directmeds vs HealthRx: both offer clinician-reviewed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, but they differ on pricing structure, shipping speed, membership and availability. Early verdict: choose Directmeds for no membership fee and month-to-month options; choose HealthRx for a lower advertised prepaid rate if you can commit to a longer term.

Head-to-head overview: pricing, shipping, formulation, and terms

The primary differences between Directmeds and HealthRx are pricing model and commitments, shipping timing and cold-chain handling, compounded formulation approach, and state availability and refund policies. Below we compare these items directly using only supplied evidence.

1. Pricing structure and promotional vs prepaid offers

Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and states standard FAQ pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. Directmeds also notes a Summer promotion saves $150 and mentions subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term. HealthRx advertises semaglutide from $99/month on a 12-month prepaid plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month. HealthRx states the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan and that plan price does not change when dose changes.

See HealthRx prepaid plan details

2. Shipping and supply handling

Directmeds includes free temperature-controlled next-day UPS or FedEx shipping, described as 1-2 day delivery and free next-day shipping in multiple places. HealthRx advertises overnight cold shipping with a cold chain, tracked, free on every plan and no separate shipping line at checkout. Both describe cold handling, but HealthRx emphasizes an overnight cold-chain service on every plan while Directmeds highlights temperature-controlled next-day shipping.

3. Medication format and compounding notes

Directmeds offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats and states U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies prepare the medications. HealthRx offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription and says compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom. Both include the regulatory qualification that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Check Directmeds formulation options

4. Membership, cancellation and refund policies

Directmeds explicitly states there is no monthly membership fee, no hidden fees, month-to-month cancellation for month-to-month customers, and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. HealthRx emphasizes that the lowest price requires a longer prepaid plan and notes pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change; captured evidence does not supply HealthRx cancellation or refund details beyond renewal terms for the prepaid plan.

Final verdict and how to choose

Directmeds vs HealthRx comes down to whether you prefer flexible month-to-month terms and no membership fee or a lower advertised prepaid price with fixed plan terms. Use Directmeds if you want no separate monthly membership fee, month-to-month cancellation options, injectable or sublingual compounded options, and free next-day temperature-controlled shipping. Use HealthRx if you are prepared to commit to a 12-month prepaid plan to access the $99/mo semaglutide rate and value overnight cold-chain shipping with fixed plan pricing through dose changes. Both providers note compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that a licensed clinician must determine medical appropriateness.

Choose Directmeds if you want no membership fee, month-to-month options, and next-day temperature-controlled shipping. Choose HealthRx if you can commit to a longer prepaid plan to access the lowest advertised monthly price.

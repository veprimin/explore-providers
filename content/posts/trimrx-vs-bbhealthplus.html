Quick verdict: TrimRx is the lower-cost entry point with compounded GLP-1 from $149/month and Lifetime Price Protection plus free 1–2 day shipping, while Bodybuilding Health+ starts at $179/month and stands out for multi-program offerings, clinician-led oversight, and bundle pricing. This comparison looks at pricing, medication options, pricing model and shipping to help you decide between TrimRx vs Bodybuilding Health+.

Head-to-head overview: pricing and pricing model

Price is a core difference. TrimRx advertises weight-loss plans starting at $149/month and highlights a Lifetime Price Protection that locks in “today’s price for life” with “same price on every dose.” Bodybuilding Health+ lists compounded semaglutide from $179/mo and tirzepatide from $209/mo and offers multi-month bundles that reduce effective monthly cost (examples: 3-, 6-, and 12-month options). Both state “no monthly membership” and “cancel anytime.”

Medication options and how they differ

Both platforms provide compounded GLP-1 options, but TrimRx explicitly lists compounded GLP-1 injections starting at $149/month and compounded GLP-1+GIP (dual agonist) starting at $249/month. Bodybuilding Health+ offers compounded semaglutide from $179/mo and tirzepatide from $209/mo, plus other programs like NAD+ and sermorelin. TrimRx also notes it can issue prescriptions for branded medications which must be filled separately by the patient; TrimRx does not sell or ship branded drugs.

Regulatory note both share

Both providers state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Bodybuilding Health+ and TrimRx both use licensed U.S. pharmacies or compounding partners for their compounded offerings.

Shipping, speed and service

TrimRx highlights free 1–2 day shipping as a feature and claims 24/7 support, while Bodybuilding Health+ states medications are “shipped to your door” and emphasizes transparent pricing with “no hidden fees.” TrimRx promotes fast shipping specifically as free 1–2 day delivery; Bodybuilding Health+ does not provide the same delivery time claim in the supplied facts.

Clinical oversight and platform scope

Bodybuilding Health+ positions itself as a multi-program telehealth platform spanning weight management, longevity, and sexual performance with medical oversight by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D., and a clinician review determining treatment eligibility. TrimRx positions itself as an online GLP-1 weight loss care platform with telehealth visits included; it highlights serving 100,000+ customers and a 4.8 rating. Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes broader program choices (NAD+, sermorelin, ED treatments) and bundle flexibility, while TrimRx emphasizes scale, lifetime pricing protections, and included telehealth visits.

How they compare on transparency and eligibility

TrimRx emphasizes “Lifetime Price Protection,” “no hidden fees,” HSA/FSA eligibility, and 0% interest financing options. Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes “no hidden fees,” no membership, cancel anytime, and clinician approval is required for treatment; BBHealthPlus notes treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that not all applicants will qualify.

Conclusion: Choose based on what matters most

Both platforms use telehealth and compounded GLP-1 programs and explicitly note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. If lower advertised starting cost, lifetime price stability, and explicit fast shipping are your priorities, TrimRx may be preferable. If you value bundled pricing options across multiple health programs, clinician-led oversight highlighted by a named physician, and a single platform for weight, longevity, and sexual performance, Bodybuilding Health+ may be the better fit.

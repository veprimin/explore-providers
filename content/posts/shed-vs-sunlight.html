Medication choices and regulatory notes

Shed explicitly offers both name-brand FDA-approved GLP-1 and compounded alternatives, with the supplied regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a provider’s prescription. Sunlight advertises injections and oral options and advises that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed; the source warns that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Coaching, clinical review, and how treatment is delivered

Shed emphasizes continuous coaching integrated into treatment rather than a prescription-only approach. It also uses a 2-minute online assessment followed by a clinician review before any prescription is issued. Sunlight provides a 100% online medical visit with prescription and telehealth included and highlights clinician involvement and customer support, but it positions itself as a prescription-and-telehealth service without the same coaching claim.

Pricing, promos, and predictable billing

Shed lists compounded semaglutide starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month and advertises 10% off your first month. The source also notes that standard pricing is not published and that you receive a personalized quote after intake. Sunlight markets plans starting at $88/month with a Summer Promo as low as $88, claims “same price forever” and no monthly membership, and highlights fast, free shipping and a cancel anytime policy. The Sunlight source includes a note that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Shipping, membership, and customer experience

Sunlight repeatedly highlights fast, free shipping in 1-2 days, free discreet 2-day shipping, and customer testimonials about quick delivery and clinician adjustments. Shed confirms medication is shipped after clinician review and offers a membership option; coaching is bundled into the monthly cost rather than charged separately. Neither source supplies exhaustive state availability details in the provided material; Sunlight specifically cautions readers to confirm eligibility and state availability.

Quick summary to decide

In short, Shed vs Sunlight breaks down to choice and support versus lower starting price and fixed billing. Shed is differentiated by offering both name-brand and compounded GLP-1 plus integrated, continuous coaching and a 2-minute assessment, while Sunlight is focused on low starting pricing (as low as $88/mo), injections and oral options, fast free shipping, and a same-price-forever promise.

Choose Shed if…

You want integrated coaching paired with the option to select FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 or compounded alternatives, and you value a 2-minute assessment plus clinician review before treatment.

Choose Sunlight if…

Your priority is the lowest advertised starting price and predictable monthly billing with fast, free shipping and no membership commitment.

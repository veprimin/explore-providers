Quick comparison and verdict: Sesame Care vs Telos Rx

Sesame Care vs Telos Rx differs mainly in clinician choice, medication type, pricing structure, and regulatory notes. Early verdict: choose Sesame Care if you want a marketplace of licensed clinicians and insurance-covered branded options; choose Telos Rx if you prioritize lower advertised starting cash prices for compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide subject to clinician approval and the regulatory caveats noted.

See Sesame Care clinician options

How they differ: clinician model and choice

Sesame Care operates as a telehealth marketplace where patients browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing, offering flexible appointments and a large clinician network. Telos Rx presents care as clinician-reviewed online treatment with a single care team approach; a US-licensed provider decides which medication is right for you and prescribes only after review.

Medication type and regulatory status

Sesame Care lists branded oral and injectable medications, with references to FDA-approved products such as Wegovy pill and Zepbound KwikPen as part of its detailed pricing notes; Sesame also adds the regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Telos Rx emphasizes that semaglutide and tirzepatide are compounded medications, prescription-only, and are not FDA-approved; treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Pricing structure and insurance

Both providers publish pricing ranges but with different framing. Sesame Care promotes ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment, and lists cash-pay medication prices as low as $149/mo for semaglutide and $299/mo for tirzepatide with price-with-insurance figures “as low as $25” for certain products. Telos Rx lists compounded semaglutide from $99 per month and tirzepatide from $139 per month, with statements that pricing is “as low as $99 per month” and that medicines are prescribed only if a licensed provider approves you. Telos Rx also notes “Pay $0 if not approved” in captured landing evidence and that you can cancel anytime, no penalties.

Distinctive differences summarized

  • Clinician selection: Sesame Care is a marketplace model with choice; Telos Rx uses a clinician-reviewed model where a US-licensed provider decides treatment.
  • Medication type: Sesame references FDA-approved branded options alongside cash-pay pricing; Telos Rx offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide that are not FDA-approved.
  • Pricing framing: Sesame lists subscriptions “as low as $59/mo” and cash-pay branded medication prices with insurance as low as $25; Telos Rx lists compounded medication prices “as low as $99 per month” and notes variability based on approval.
  • Regulatory notes: Sesame reminds that medication approval status must be confirmed; Telos Rx explicitly states compounded meds are not FDA-approved and not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

What the pricing and approval notes mean for you

Sesame Care presents both subscription care and options that accept insurance for weight loss medications, while also showing cash-pay prices. Their “as low as $59/mo” subscription is noted as “Based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment” and medication costs are not included in the subscription price. Telos Rx focuses on compounded medication pricing with examples of semaglutide from $99/month and tirzepatide from $139/month, and emphasizes that these compounded medications are prescription-only and subject to medical approval.

Patient experience and commitments

Sesame Care highlights flexible scheduling, nationwide access in many states, and a large clinician network; it also notes “Experience can vary by clinician.” Telos Rx highlights that a US-licensed provider reviews eligibility and that cancellation terms allow cancel anytime with no penalties according to captured evidence, while also warning that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Conclusion: Sesame Care vs Telos Rx

Sesame Care vs Telos Rx comes down to choice versus compounding and price framing. Sesame Care offers a marketplace with clinician choice, flexible scheduling, and both insurance-accepted branded medication options and cash-pay pricing; Telos Rx highlights lower advertised starting prices for compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide but states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prescribed only after clinician review.

Choose Sesame Care if you want a clinician marketplace, potential insurance coverage for branded medications, and subscriptions starting as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month commitment. Choose Telos Rx if you are considering compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with advertised prices as low as $99 per month and accept that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and treatment is subject to a US-licensed provider’s approval.

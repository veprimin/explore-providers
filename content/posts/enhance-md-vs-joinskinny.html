Overview comparison: Enhance MD vs JoinSkinny

This comparison focuses on the five most distinctive differences supported by the sources: starting price, compound options, pricing model details, shipping and pharmacy fulfillment, and clinical and regulatory notes. Both providers use licensed clinicians to review patients and offer clinician-directed treatment, but they present different price and fulfillment tradeoffs.

Starting price and introductory offers

Enhance MD advertises semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99 for your first month, described as the cheapest first-month prices for both compounds on its platform. JoinSkinny shows weight loss plans starting at $149 and lists a promotional $140 off offer and bundle pricing. These figures are presented as starting points and include qualifiers such as “first month” for Enhance MD and “starting at” for JoinSkinny.

Compound options and treatment scope

Enhance MD explicitly offers both compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide on one platform and highlights access to both active compound options for patients choosing between them. JoinSkinny offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and describes prescriptions filled by licensed U.S. pharmacies as authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1 + GIP when prescribed. Both providers note that compounds contain the same active molecules used in brand-name medications, with different fulfillment approaches.

Pricing model and dose escalation

Both platforms use flat-rate pricing so the monthly price stays the same regardless of dose increases. Enhance MD emphasizes “flat-rate pricing: the same price at every dose, with no fee escalation during titration” and frames that as a budgeting advantage. JoinSkinny also states “same price, every dose. No hidden fees” and adds monthly and multi-month bundle options and installment choices. Enhance MD highlights very low first-month pricing followed by an ongoing flat-rate that may be higher than the introductory rate.

Shipping, fulfillment, and logistics

JoinSkinny advertises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1-2 days for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, with next-day UPS in a temperature-controlled package and tracking. JoinSkinny fills prescriptions through U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners. Enhance MD’s captured evidence focuses on pricing and does not supply the same fulfillment details; it does include a clinician-directed review process and notes metabolic lab testing every 6 months in some pricing disclosures.

Clinical oversight, volume, and regulatory notes

Enhance MD notes it has treated 10,000+ patients and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Enhance MD also states that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny states all care is delivered by licensed U.S. providers, patient information is protected under HIPAA, prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners, and that compounded products are not FDA-approved.

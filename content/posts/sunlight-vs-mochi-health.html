Sunlight vs Mochi Health is a choice between a flat-rate GLP-1 plan and a membership-plus-medication telehealth model. Early verdict: Sunlight centers on a low advertised starting price and a flat monthly rate, while Mochi emphasizes live video visits, membership features, and compounded medication options.

How pricing and billing compare

Sunlight advertises personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo with a stated promise of the same price forever and no monthly membership. Mochi Health uses a membership-plus-medication model with pricing from $99/month and a promotional 50% off the first month via code BESTWLHALFOFF. Mochi notes that the first month promotional rate may differ from ongoing membership pricing and that total monthly cost depends on plan and medication.

Sunlight vs Mochi Health: clinical visit model and access

One of the clearest differences is visit format. Sunlight markets a 100% online medical visit process, typically via telehealth and prescription included, while Mochi Health highlights live video visits with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes. Mochi positions synchronous video as fundamentally different from the asynchronous questionnaire review used by many competitors. Mochi also advertises reliable GLP-1 prescription access in all 50 states and ongoing appointment availability with 24/7 support.

Medication types, regulatory notes, and shipping

Sunlight promotes injections and oral options and highlights prescription and telehealth included with free shipping in 1-2 days and no insurance required. Mochi lists multiple options including compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide injections and pills, a compounded orforglipron oral option, and both compounded and FDA-approved branded products at various starting prices. Mochi’s materials include a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Sunlight’s materials include a regulatory reminder that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

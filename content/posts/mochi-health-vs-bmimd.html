Mochi Health vs bmiMD: Pricing structure and commitments

Mochi uses a membership-plus-medication model with standard pricing that starts from $99/month and promotes 50% Off Your 1st Month with code BESTWLHALFOFF. Promotional offers include a $39 first-month membership rate for new customers in some states, with the standard rate noted as $79 per month after that in captured material. bmiMD sells compounded tirzepatide with four billing options: $179 month-to-month, $159 per month for three months, $149 per month for six months, and $139 per month for a 12-month plan. bmiMD highlights that the lowest price requires a 12-month commitment and that total prepaid charges should be confirmed. Medication options and important regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded medications. Mochi lists compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options with specific starting prices and also mentions compounded oral options like semaglutide pills and orforglipron. Mochi’s landing copy reiterates that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

bmiMD focuses on compounded tirzepatide and states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. bmiMD also states medications are prepared in state-licensed pharmacies and independently verified in FDA- and DEA-registered labs in captured claims.

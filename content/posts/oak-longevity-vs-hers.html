Pricing and billing: who pays less up front

Oak Longevity advertises semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month with one flat price covering all dosages and no subscription. Medication type and regulatory status

Oak Longevity offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. The source specifies that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Hers offers FDA-approved Wegovy and other brand-name GLP-1s, not compounded products. That distinction is central: Oak emphasizes lower-priced compounded options, while Hers emphasizes FDA-approved brand-name medications.

Membership, renewals, and cancellation differences

Oak states billing is monthly on a non-recurring basis with no subscription, and three-month prescriptions are billed upfront. Oak also notes no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped. Hers requires an active Hers Weight Loss Membership for medication access; membership and medication are billed separately. The Hers source warns medication is not available without an active membership and membership auto-renews at the stated rates.

Care model and included services

Oak offers free health coaching included with every plan, free shipping with tracking, and an asynchronous clinician review process so no video call is required. Oak also notes board-certified physicians in your state review every case. Hers highlights a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins and promotes doctor-trusted, FDA-approved options. Choose based on whether you prefer included coaching and asynchronous review or ongoing team-based dose management with an active membership.

Safety, eligibility, and important limits

Both sources include regulatory notes. Oak explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Oak also notes patients with BMI below 20 do not qualify for GLP-1 treatment and not for patients under 18, pregnant, or nursing. Hers notes Wegovy and Zepbound are FDA-approved for weight loss and that Ozempic and Mounjaro are FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes and may be prescribed off-label. Hers also states treatments are available only when a licensed clinician determines they are medically appropriate and that not all 50 states may be covered.

Conclusion: Oak Longevity vs Hers

Oak Longevity vs Hers comes down to priorities.

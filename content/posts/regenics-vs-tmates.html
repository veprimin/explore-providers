Regenics vs TMates: quick verdict — Regenics is the lower promotional entry price with nurse-practitioner-led telehealth and an added Sermorelin option, while TMates offers a flat $199/month rate with 24/7 U.S. doctor access and same price at every dose. Treatment availability for both is subject to clinician review and compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Direct comparison: pricing model and what you pay

Price is one of the clearest differences in the Regenics vs TMates comparison. Regenics advertises compounded semaglutide starting at $139 for the first month and tirzepatide at $189 for the first month, with renewal prices listed as higher. TMates advertises a flat $199/month price that remains the same at every dose, with no step-up fees as dosage escalates.

How promotions and renewals differ

Regenics notes promotional pricing applies to the first month only and renewal pricing increases to the stated monthly rates. TMates emphasizes a single flat monthly price for semaglutide programs so cost does not increase during titration.

Clinical oversight and access

Clinician access is another major distinction. Regenics’ care is delivered by licensed nurse practitioners who review intake before anything is prescribed. TMates highlights 24/7 access to U.S.-based doctors across all 50 states and includes virtual check-ins and physician availability beyond messaging.

See TMates with 24/7 U.S. doctor access

Medication selection and formulation

Both providers supply compounded semaglutide and state their medications are formulated in U.S. facilities, but the pair differs in public detail. Regenics explicitly offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide plus a Sermorelin peptide option. TMates markets clinically dosed compounded semaglutide with limited public detail on tirzepatide availability.

Quality, regulatory notes, and shipping

Both platforms use compounded medications and include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Regenics states its pharmacy network is LegitScript certified and offers free shipping with medications compounded in the USA. TMates also offers free shipping and emphasizes U.S.-formulated medications.

Availability and best use cases

TMates advertises licensed coverage in all 50 states and direct physician availability. Regenics is telehealth-only; treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Neither provider’s materials on the captured pages guarantee in-person clinics.

How these services differ at a glance

This comparison uses only source details for MedVi and JoinSkinny. The most distinctive differences are starting price and promotional structure, medication options, pricing model and bundles, shipping and fulfillment timing, and ongoing support and eligibility rules.

1. Starting price and promotional structure

MedVi advertises a winter promotional first-month price of $179 including fast, free shipping, with standalone months rising to $299 and a three-month plan at $649. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149 and references a $140 promotional discount along with monthly and multi-month bundles. The MedVi price is explicitly described as a promotional first-month rate that increases afterward; JoinSkinny emphasizes same-price-every-dose flat-rate options and bundles.

2. Medication options: semaglutide only versus GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP

MedVi focuses on clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only, with no tirzepatide option and no branded Ozempic or Wegovy offered through its compounded program. JoinSkinny offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options when prescribed, and states prescriptions are filled as authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1 + GIP when appropriate.

3. Pricing model and commitments

MedVi is a cash-pay telehealth model with a promotional entry price and higher ongoing monthly rates; three-month plan pricing is available. JoinSkinny highlights transparent flat-rate pricing where the monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases, plus monthly and 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles. JoinSkinny also notes installment options like Afterpay, Klarna, and Affirm.

4. Shipping, fulfillment, and timing

Both providers advertise free shipping. MedVi states fast, free shipping included in the promo. JoinSkinny specifies temperature-controlled packaging, same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, and next-day UPS delivery in most cases with tracking and insured shipments.

5. Support, eligibility, and regulatory notes

MedVi offers 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and a fully online intake with no clinic visits; OpenLoop Health clinicians review eligibility and retain prescribing discretion. MedVi includes a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny provides ongoing support including monthly check-ins, 24/7 chat, licensed U.S. providers, HIPAA protections, and pharmacy partners described as FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified; it includes the same regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

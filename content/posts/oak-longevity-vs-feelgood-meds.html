What the two providers say about price and product formats

Price is the clearest difference in this comparison. Oak Longevity lists semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month, describing one flat price for all dosages. FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections starting at $149/month, compounded tablets starting at $249/month, and a branded option shown from $1,999/month. Both providers present compounded medication options; FeelGood Meds additionally advertises a branded medication option at a much higher listed price.

Clinical review, eligibility, and regulatory notes

Both providers state that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Oak Longevity emphasizes that the process requires a short online health questionnaire and that a licensed clinician reviews the case without a required video call. Oak also notes patients with BMI below 20 do not qualify for GLP 1 treatment and later materials indicate not appropriate for BMI under 22 without comorbidities. FeelGood Meds notes eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability should be confirmed before enrollment. Both providers include the regulatory disclaimer that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Billing, refunds, and renewal differences

Oak Longevity markets no subscriptions or membership fees and states billing is monthly on a non recurring basis so you are not auto charged; three month prescriptions are billed upfront. Oak explicitly states no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped. FeelGood Meds also advertises no membership or hidden fees and free shipping, and it advertises a Weight Loss Money Back Guarantee. FeelGood Meds says medication specific renewal terms require confirmation and that pricing and eligibility are subject to the live offer. If precise refund and renewal mechanics are important to you, the documented claims require reviewing each provider’s live terms before enrolling.

Support and extras

Oak Longevity includes free health coaching with every plan and emphasizes same day approval availability for qualifying patients. FeelGood Meds highlights free shipping and lists a money back guarantee as part of its landing claims. Oak notes 10,000 plus patients served in its materials. Neither source provides independent evidence of outcomes beyond the providers’ stated examples and testimonials, so those claims are presented as the providers’ own marketing statements.

Final comparison and how to choose: Oak Longevity vs FeelGood Meds

Oak Longevity is the lower listed starting price option and focuses on one flat price for all dosages, no subscription billing, free shipping, and free health coaching, with asynchronous clinician review and specific BMI eligibility notes. FeelGood Meds offers compounded injections at a slightly higher starting price, compounded tablets, and a branded medication option at substantially higher listed cost, plus an advertised money back guarantee and a recommendation to confirm eligibility and renewal terms with the live offer.

Choose Oak Longevity if you are cost conscious, want semaglutide from $133/month or tirzepatide from $199/month, prefer a one price for all dosages model, want free health coaching, and accept asynchronous clinician review and the stated refund limitations.

Choose FeelGood Meds if you want the option of compounded tablets or a branded medication option shown at a higher price, if the provider advertised money back guarantee is important to you, or if you want to confirm medication specific renewal terms and availability before enrolling.

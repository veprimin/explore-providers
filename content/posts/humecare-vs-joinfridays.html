HumeCare+ vs JoinFridays: headline differences

This comparison centers on five distinctive differences supported by the sources: price and promos, support and bundled protocol, body-composition tracking, clinician access and cancellation, and compounded medication regulatory notes. Both sell clinician-prescribed GLP-1 options, but they package and price those options differently.

1. Price and promos

JoinFridays starts at $117/month and advertises active promo codes such as NEWYOU12 and NYNY12. It lists tirzepatide as low as $198/mo in some presentations and multi-month plans that lower effective monthly rates. HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/month and a tirzepatide plus B12 and glycine protocol from $299/month. Both note prices reflect specific plan structures and promotional pricing; JoinFridays emphasizes the lowest starting price in its tier while HumeCare+ emphasizes a bundled formulation price.

2. Support and bundled protocol

HumeCare+ sells a bundled protocol: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide combined with glycine and vitamins B6 and B12 for muscle support and side-effect relief, not sold separately. HumeCare+ includes an app for tracking and claims unlimited clinician access and a U.S. care team available 24/7. JoinFridays emphasizes personalized clinician-guided care and membership included free but frames its offering around competitive pricing and flexibility rather than a single bundled formulation.

3. Body-composition scans and tracking

HumeCare+ includes monthly body-composition scans that track lean mass versus fat mass and positions that as addressing a GLP-1 limitation. The provider states it tracks lean mass, not just weight, so members see what is actually leaving the body each month. JoinFridays does not supply landing-page evidence of body-composition scans in the material provided; its emphasis is on medication options, coaching, and membership flexibility.

4. Clinician access, cancellation, and plan terms

Both platforms require a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness. HumeCare+ highlights unlimited clinician access and 24/7 U.S. care team availability and notes cancel subscription anytime. JoinFridays stresses no membership fees, cancel anytime with no long-term contract, and offers month-to-month and multi-month plan options where longer commitments lower the effective rate. JoinFridays explicitly frames itself as low risk for patients unsure about committing to multi-month spend.

5. Compounded medication and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications in some form. HumeCare+ offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide and explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated for safety, quality, or efficacy. JoinFridays also offers compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide and notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. These regulatory qualifiers are present in both sources and are important to consider.

Conclusion and final guidance on HumeCare+ vs JoinFridays

HumeCare+ is built around a bundled GLP-1 protocol with glycine and B vitamins, monthly body-composition scans, an app, 24/7 clinician access, and a 10%-or-money-back promise over six months. JoinFridays focuses on the lowest starting price, flexible cancellation, and aggressive promo codes while offering both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide with clinician-guided care. Both provide compounded options and include regulatory disclaimers about compounded medications.

Choose HumeCare+ if you want a bundled protocol that includes glycine and B vitamins, monthly body-composition scans, unlimited clinician access, and a stated 10% money-back promise over six months, understanding that medications may be compounded and are not FDA-evaluated.

Choose JoinFridays if your priority is the lowest starting price, flexible cancel-anytime terms, clinician-guided care, and aggressive promo codes that can reduce up-front cost.

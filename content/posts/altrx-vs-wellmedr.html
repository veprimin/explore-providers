How altRx and Wellmedr differ at a glance

Both companies offer GLP-1 plans starting from about $89 per month, but they position those prices differently and pair them with distinct commitments. Below are the most distinctive supported differences: advertised pricing and promotional terms, compound medication regulatory notes, delivery and refill guarantees, and pricing guarantees or locking offers.

1. Price presentation and promotional terms

altRx advertises a limited offer: compounded semaglutide starting at $89/month and compounded tirzepatide at $149/month, described as the most affordable GLP-1 program. The source repeatedly notes the offer is limited and references same price every month and multi-month tier pricing reductions. Wellmedr also lists plans starting at $89/month and highlights sale messaging such as locking in a fixed monthly discount or lower 12-month plan pricing. Wellmedr emphasizes lifetime locked-in pricing and sale offers such as Labor Day or new customer lifetime offers in captured messaging.

2. Compounded medications and regulatory notices

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications. altRx explicitly states its medications are compounded and includes a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Wellmedr likewise states its compounded GLP-1 medications are not evaluated by the FDA and includes a required safety notice listing possible risks and urging immediate care for certain symptoms. These regulatory qualifiers are present in both providers’ captured evidence.

3. Delivery timing and refill guarantees

altRx promotes free and discreet 2-day delivery and typical processing and delivery time of approximately 4 to 5 business days. Wellmedr promotes same-day review plus free overnight shipping and on-time refills guaranteed, with tirzepatide and semaglutide shipped every 4 weeks for monthly plans. The two services therefore differ in the shipping promise language and refill cadence guarantees documented in the sources.

4. Pricing commitment and payment options

altRx highlights buy now, pay later options through Affirm, Klarna, and Afterpay, no membership or hidden fees, and cancel anytime with same price every month messaging. Wellmedr emphasizes lifetime locked-in pricing and statements about no dose-based price increases, plus specific promotional refund and money-back claims captured on its landing page. The source for Wellmedr also cautions that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

5. Availability and clinician review

altRx states its membership is available to residents of all 50 U.S. states, while noting provider availability and prescribing authority vary by state and that a licensed provider will review your evaluation and conduct telehealth. Wellmedr tells readers to confirm eligibility, pharmacy and state coverage before enrollment and requires clinician approval as well.

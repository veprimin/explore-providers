Quick verdict: HumeCare+ focuses on a bundled GLP-1 protocol that pairs compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with glycine, B vitamins, body-composition scans, and a 10%-or-money-back promise; Eden Health emphasizes lower listed monthly prices and a lifetime price guarantee for semaglutide and tirzepatide. This article compares HumeCare+ vs Eden Health across pricing, clinical protocol and formulation, monitoring and support, guarantees and policy, and regulatory notes.

Explore HumeCare+ plans and protocol

Price and what is included

HumeCare+ lists compounded semaglutide starting at $199/month and a dual-action tirzepatide protocol from $299/month. Those prices are presented as bundled plans that include medication plus supportive elements. Eden Health lists semaglutide at $99/month and tirzepatide at $129/month with a stated lifetime price guarantee and no membership fees. Both providers note free expedited or next-day shipping in their captured evidence.

Protocol and formulation: compounded protocol vs guided prescription

HumeCare+ sells a protocol rather than only a prescription. Its offering wraps physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide together with glycine, vitamin B12, and B6 built into the formulation, plus an app and clinician access. Eden presents a guided GLP-1 program at a single monthly price. The sources indicate Eden brings medication, care, and support together under one price, while HumeCare+ explicitly advertises personalized compounded formulations with added supportive ingredients.

HumeCare+ supportive ingredients vs Eden standard guidance

HumeCare+ positions glycine and vitamins B6 and B12 as built-in supports for sleep, muscle protection, recovery, and mitigation of nausea or fatigue. The evidence for Eden does not list added compounds or nutrients in the medication; Eden’s captured text describes guided GLP-1 programs and monthly pricing at every dose.

Monitoring, clinician access, and tracking

HumeCare+ includes monthly body-composition scans to track lean mass versus fat and offers an app for medication tracking and food logging plus unlimited clinician access and a U.S. care team available 24/7 according to the captured evidence. Eden’s materials emphasize a guided program with care and support under one monthly price but do not provide the same captured detail about body-composition scans or 24/7 clinician access in the source evidence.

Guarantees, promises, and limitations

HumeCare+ advertises a 10%-or-money-back promise over six months with terms and eligibility that must be verified in the provider terms. The source repeatedly notes eligibility and documentation requirements apply and that consumers should see full terms. Eden advertises a lifetime price guarantee for the monthly price. Both providers include wording that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Regulatory and safety notes

The captured HumeCare+ evidence includes a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. The HumeCare+ listing explicitly states its medications are compounded and dispensed from U.S. pharmacies licensed by state Boards of Pharmacy. Eden’s captured content notes medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

2) Medication options and dispensing approach

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in their captured descriptions. Omzo describes a Compounded Medication Program where medications are prepared and shipped by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy, and notes these compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. Omzo also states providers may write prescriptions for branded, FDA-approved medications that the patient must fill at a pharmacy because Omzo does not sell or ship branded products. Telos Rx likewise notes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are prescription-only, prescribed after review by a US-licensed provider, and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Neither source supplies details outside these statements.

3) Clinician review, eligibility, and provider role

Both Omzo and Telos Rx emphasize clinician review. Omzo says clinical services are provided by third party networks of U.S.-licensed clinicians who determine eligibility and make the final determination to prescribe. Telos Rx states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that a US-licensed provider decides which molecule is right for you. Both sources preserve that prescriptions are subject to clinician review and that individual eligibility is determined by providers.

4) Regulatory and outcome disclaimers

Each source includes a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Omzo additionally records statements that results vary, weight loss is not guaranteed, and the information is for educational purposes and does not constitute medical advice. Telos Rx captures that individual results vary and are not guaranteed. Those qualifiers are part of the provided evidence and are preserved here.

5) Cancellation, renewal, and customer commitments

Telos Rx captured messaging that users can cancel anytime with no penalties and that prices depend on plan. Omzo captured messaging about pricing and shipping policy but did not provide the same explicit cancel-anytime language in the supplied content. Both sources caution that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage, and renewal terms may change.

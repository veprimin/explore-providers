Quick contrast: price, prescribing, and plan style

This direct comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: starting price points, pricing model and billing, clinical review and visit requirements, included services, and regulatory notes about compounded medications. It uses only the supplied evidence for Oak Longevity and Telos Rx.

1) Starting price and advertised low cost

Telos Rx lists semaglutide as low as $99 per month and tirzepatide as low as $139 per month, if a licensed provider approves you. Oak Longevity lists semaglutide from $133 per month and tirzepatide from $199 per month. Both sources state prices for compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, with Telos Rx advertising the lower starting numbers.

2) Pricing model and billing differences

Oak Longevity: one flat price that covers all dosages and billed monthly on a non recurring basis, with three month prescriptions billed upfront; Oak states no subscriptions, no membership fees, and no hidden costs. Oak also notes a promotional code is available for certain discounts on three month supply or first month, per captured FAQ text. Telos Rx: advertises “cancel anytime, no penalties” and notes your price depends on your plan; the captured content cautions that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change and that details were not published or could not be verified on the captured landing page.

3) Clinical review, visit format, and approvals

Oak Longevity: the process is online with no video call required and clinical review is asynchronous by board certified physicians in your state; Oak says treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Telos Rx: medications are prescribed online after review by a US licensed provider and the captured content repeatedly emphasizes approval by a licensed provider before pricing applies and before medication is prescribed.

4) Included services and shipping

Oak Longevity includes free health coaching and free shipping with tracking according to the captured evidence and notes same day approval is available for qualifying patients. Telos Rx captured details do not list included coaching or shipping in the captured evidence; the landing page copy focuses on clinician review and that you may pay $0 if not approved, as captured.

5) Regulatory and product notes

Both providers’ captured material states that semaglutide and tirzepatide offered are compounded medications and that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Oak Longevity explicitly repeats that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety effectiveness or quality.

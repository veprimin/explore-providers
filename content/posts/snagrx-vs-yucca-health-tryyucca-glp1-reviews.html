Quick verdict: SnagRx is the lower-cost compounded option when you commit to a 12-month plan, while Yucca Health (TryYucca) offers broader state coverage, BNPL options, and a faster 24-hour approval window. This article compares SnagRx vs Yucca Health across pricing, coverage and approvals, medication types, shipping and pharmacy claims, and guarantees.

Head-to-head summary: price, approvals, and scope

SnagRx promotes compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan, with the rate locked flat at every dose. Yucca Health advertises compounded semaglutide from $146/month and tirzepatide from $258/month with 6-month plans available and a lifetime price lock code. Both use compounding pharmacies and do not offer branded FDA-approved products.

Key difference 1: Starting price and pricing model

SnagRx: From $69/month semaglutide and $119/month tirzepatide as 12-month plan rates; month-to-month equivalents are $99 and $149. SnagRx emphasizes a price lock flat at every dose for the life of treatment.

Yucca Health: Compounded semaglutide from $146/month and tirzepatide from $258/month. Yucca offers a 6-month semaglutide plan at $875 as a path to a lower per-month rate, and a lifetime price lock option via a code.

Pricing takeaway

For patients focused purely on the lowest advertised per-month number with a year commitment, SnagRx is the lower-cost option. Yucca Health provides a mid-range price with multi-month plans and BNPL flexibility.

Key difference 2: Approval time, clinician coverage, and states

SnagRx states clinicians (OpenLoop Health) typically review intake within five hours and within 24 hours; its clinician network covers 48 states. Yucca Health states licensed providers in all 50 states and typically issues decisions within 24 hours.

Key difference 3: Shipping, pharmacies, and logistics

SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and advertises next-day UPS or FedEx shipping in temperature-controlled packaging. Yucca Health uses licensed U.S. compounding pharmacies and most orders ship via UPS 2-Day Air with first shipments typically within 3 to 5 business days.

Key difference 4: Payment options and guarantees

SnagRx includes the medication, injection supplies, clinician review and shipping in its per-month price and states a full refund if a prescription is declined plus a 6-month weight-loss guarantee, terms apply. Yucca Health accepts Klarna, Afterpay, and Affirm for split payments and does not offer a weight loss warranty or outcome guarantee in the supplied evidence.

Key difference 5: Medication types and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. SnagRx emphasizes compounded medications and states they are not FDA-approved finished products. Yucca Health similarly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and states its compounded semaglutide uses the same active ingredient as branded products but the manufacturing source differs.

How CoreAgeRx and HealthRx differ: 4 key comparisons

The evidence supports four distinctive differences: pricing model, compounded medication status and preparation, shipping and cold chain, and whether plans require prepaid commitment. Below we compare these points directly using only supplied facts.

1) Pricing model and starting prices

CoreAgeRx lists semaglutide starting at $79 per month and tirzepatide at $129 per month with messaging that the price is the same every month, no membership fee, and no surprise price hikes. HealthRx advertises semaglutide from $99 per month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan and tirzepatide plans starting at $179 per month; HealthRx states the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan and provides sample totals ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) for the 12-month semaglutide plan.

2) Compounded medication and regulatory notes

HealthRx specifies that semaglutide or tirzepatide are compounded to prescription, prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom, and that compounded medication is not FDA-approved. CoreAgeRx notes that when compounded medications are offered, they are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Both sources include the regulatory qualifier that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

3) Shipping and cold chain handling

HealthRx explicitly offers overnight shipping with cold chain, tracked, and free on every plan. CoreAgeRx content captured asks “How fast is shipping?” but does not supply explicit shipping speed details in the captured evidence. If overnight cold shipping is a priority, HealthRx documents that feature; CoreAgeRx does not provide that information in the supplied material.

4) Dose options, clinician review, and dose-based pricing

CoreAgeRx lists dose allowances: tirzepatide any dose up to 7.5 mg and semaglutide any dose up to 1.0 mg and emphasizes “flat monthly pricing—same price every month” noting other programs may raise bills as dose increases. HealthRx indicates the clinician decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is right and that your plan price does not change when your dose does; HealthRx also notes the plan you choose is the plan the medication arrives on rather than picking a medication off a menu.

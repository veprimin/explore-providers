Early verdict: pick Strut Health if you want oral Semaglutide options and guided consults focused on oral medication management; pick HumeCare+ if you want a protocol that pairs GLP-1 with glycine, B vitamins, body-composition scans, and a 10% money-back promise for qualifying members.

Direct comparison: approach, extras, and pricing

Both companies offer GLP-1 options starting at $199 per month, but they package care differently. Strut Health emphasizes oral Semaglutide and guided consults with ongoing progress communication. HumeCare+ packages physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with glycine, vitamins B6 and B12, an app, monthly body-composition scans, and clinician access.

Key difference 1: Medication format and oral specialization

Strut Health is specialized in oral-based program options and highlights oral Semaglutide lozenges plus guided consultations. It positions its clinical team around oral medication management rather than splitting focus across injection and oral protocols. HumeCare+ offers compounded semaglutide as an option and also offers a dual-action tirzepatide protocol at a higher starting price.

Key difference 2: Bundled supportive ingredients and measurements

HumeCare+ pairs GLP-1 medication with glycine and vitamins B6 and B12, positioning those additions as muscle-protecting and side-effect reducing. HumeCare+ also includes monthly body-composition scans to track fat versus lean mass. Strut Health focuses on oral protocols and ongoing clinician communication but does not list body-composition scanning or built-in glycine and B vitamins as part of its offering in the captured evidence.

Key difference 3: Pricing options and program promises

Both list semaglutide starting at $199 per month. HumeCare+ lists tirzepatide plus B12 and glycine from $299 per month and advertises a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months, subject to full terms and conditions. Strut Health advertises oral GLP-1 from $199 per month and highlights no hidden fees, free shipping, and cancel anytime in captured material. Both providers note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved in their regulatory language.

Key difference 4: Clinical access and support model

HumeCare+ describes unlimited clinician access with a U.S. care team available 24/7, a connected app for medication tracking and food logging, and continuous physician optimization. Strut Health describes guided consultations and ongoing progress communication with an oral specialization, but the evidence does not claim 24/7 clinician access or a dedicated app in the captured material.

Safety and regulatory language to note

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications in some form. How treatment pathways compare over time

Strut Health materials emphasize dosing schedules for oral lozenges once daily and injections once weekly when injections are used, and recommend combining treatment with activity and a reduced calorie diet. HumeCare+ explains dose escalation over weeks, that it can take up to 8 to 9 weeks to feel full effects, and presents internal outcome claims and timelines for members while noting individual results may vary and terms apply to the refund promise.

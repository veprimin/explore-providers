Shed vs Start Willow: what differs most

1. Medication type: name-brand plus compounded vs compounded only

Shed offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 options and compounded alternatives, letting patients choose between branded and compounded formulations. Start Willow provides compounded semaglutide and medications comparable to Ozempic and tirzepatide; its offerings are compounded alternatives using the same active ingredients as the branded versions. Both providers note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

2. Support model: integrated coaching vs direct-pay physician care

Shed integrates continuous coaching into treatment rather than only prescribing medication, positioning coaching as part of the program. Start Willow provides clinician-prescribed compounded medication with personalized treatment plans and physician oversight, emphasizing direct-pay access and no membership fees rather than bundled coaching.

3. Pricing and promotional details

Shed advertises compounded semaglutide starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month and a limited time 10% off your first month; standard pricing is provided after intake and depends on name-brand versus compounded selection. Start Willow lists pricing from $249 per month and notes a first month price of $249 with an ongoing rate of $299/month; Start Willow states there are no membership fees and no insurance is required.

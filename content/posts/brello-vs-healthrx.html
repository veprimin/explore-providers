Compounded medication and regulatory notes

Both sources note compounded medications when offered are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HealthRx explicitly states its compound is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and that compounds are prepared per prescription. Brello’s captured content includes the regulatory note that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Shipping, cold chain and fulfillment

HealthRx documents overnight cold shipping and tracked cold-chain delivery free on every plan. For Brello, shipping, fees and membership cancellation details were not verified on the captured landing page. If guaranteed overnight cold shipping is a deciding factor, HealthRx is the only provider here with captured shipping claims.

Clinician review and treatment eligibility

Both providers require a licensed clinician to determine whether treatment is medically appropriate. HealthRx emphasizes that the clinician decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide or neither is the right fit and that you choose the plan the medication arrives on. Brello states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Neither source supplies comparative clinical outcomes or eligibility rules beyond clinician review.

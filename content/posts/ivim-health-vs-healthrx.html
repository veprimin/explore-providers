Pricing structure and upfront costs

IVIM Health lists semaglutide at $75/mo and tirzepatide at $133/mo with an additional $74.99/mo program fee in captured materials. IVIM also advertises a no-obligation free consultation (a $75 value) and a free month membership with code GIFT75. HealthRx advertises lower per-month pricing on longer prepaid plans: semaglutide from $99/mo on a 12-month prepaid plan and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/mo. HealthRx notes the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan and that the plan price does not change when dose changes.

Medication type, clinician decisions, and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared per prescription. IVIM is explicit that these are compounded medications and states they are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HealthRx likewise states its compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and is not FDA-approved. Both platforms require a clinician to evaluate whether treatment is medically appropriate.

Clinician protocols and reported outcomes

IVIM promotes a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol and cites published internal studies claiming patients following the IVIM Protocol lost an average of 46% more weight over 68 weeks when compared to another published clinical GLP-1 trial; IVIM notes this was not a head-to-head study and that the 40% faster claim is a proprietary protocol claim, not an independent clinical finding. HealthRx emphasizes that clinicians decide whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is appropriate and focuses on plan selection rather than a named proprietary titration protocol. No independent head-to-head efficacy comparisons between the two providers were supplied.

Shipping, support, and logistics

HealthRx explicitly markets overnight cold shipping with tracked cold chain included on every plan and no shipping charge. IVIM’s captured materials include program fees and pricing details but did not supply the same explicit overnight cold shipping claim in the captured evidence. Both rely on clinician oversight and prescription fulfillment by pharmacies; both sources state compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Ratings, trust signals, and who each is best for

IVIM Health reports a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot, presented as an unusually strong dual third-party verification signal. IVIM is described as best for patients who want a clinically structured program with a strong satisfaction track record. HealthRx is described as best for people who confirm eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

Conclusion: IVIM Health vs HealthRx

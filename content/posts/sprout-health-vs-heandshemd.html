Quick verdict: Sprout Health is best for people who want lightning-quick approval and a low-friction start, while He & She MD is better for patients who want coaching and 1:1 clinician access bundled with medication. How they differ at a glance

Both platforms offer compounded GLP-1 medications shipped to your door and operate on a direct-pay model, but they emphasize different priorities. Sprout Health leads with fast approval, a first-month discount and explicit outcome framing. He & She MD pairs lower introductory pricing with a coaching app that includes 1:1 clinician messaging and progress tracking.

1. Pricing and promotional structure

Sprout Health advertises a $50 off first month offer, bringing the initial cost to $199, with ongoing pricing noted as $249/month after the discount. He & She MD lists semaglutide injections starting at $139/month as a six-month introductory rate, with oral semaglutide and tirzepatide at $199/month intro; those promo rates renew higher after six months. Both providers note that promo pricing is limited and renews at a greater rate. The comparison here is straightforward: Sprout has a one-month discount then a stated ongoing price, while He & She MD uses multi-month intro rates that renew higher.

2. Onboarding speed and approval

Sprout Health emphasizes a lightning-quick approval process and a fast, no-friction online intake designed for quick entry to GLP-1 therapy. He & She MD offers a fast 2-3 minute intake as well, but emphasizes that its pricing is structured in six-month terms rather than month-to-month. Both require clinician review and prescription; neither guarantees that every applicant will qualify.

3. Bundled support and program design

He & She MD bundles a coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific guidance and progress tracking at no extra cost, positioning itself for patients who want ongoing support built into the plan. Sprout Health is positioned as a no-frills, fast-entry experience without published bundled coaching features on the captured page. If coaching and tracking are important, He & She MD documents those features explicitly.

4. Medication formats and regulatory notes

He & She MD offers multiple formats: semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection according to the captured evidence. Sprout Health prescribes compounded semaglutide and frames expected outcomes as 10–26% body weight loss over 12 months based on GLP-1 literature. Both sources note that compounded medications are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality, and both require clinician review to determine whether treatment is medically appropriate.

5. Outcome framing and targets

Sprout Health explicitly frames outcomes as losing 10–26% of body weight in 12 months, citing GLP-1 clinical outcome literature and noting individual results vary. He & She MD promotes a meds-plus-support approach aimed at long-term success and references self-reported short-term weight figures in promotional copy, but the captured evidence warns that some figures are retrospective and not trial data. Neither provider offers a published outcome guarantee on the captured pages.

Conclusion: Choose based on priorities

For a quick verdict on Sprout Health vs He & She MD: choose Sprout Health if you prioritize lightning-quick approval and a simple, fast-entry model with a $50 first-month discount; choose He & She MD if you value bundled coaching, 1:1 clinician messaging, progress tracking and multiple medication formats with lower introductory pricing for six months. Both prescribe compounded GLP-1s subject to clinician review and both note compounded products are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Choose Sprout Health if you want a fast, low-friction start and an explicit first-month discount. Choose He & She MD if you want coaching and clinician access built into the plan and are willing to enroll in a six-month introductory pricing term.

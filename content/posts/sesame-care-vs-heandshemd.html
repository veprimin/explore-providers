Clinician choice and care model

Sesame Care uses a marketplace model that lets patients browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing. That means the experience can vary by clinician because you pick who provides ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging. He & She MD bundles care around its GLP-1 Platinum Program with an app that includes 1:1 clinician messaging and coaching built in at no extra cost, designed for people who want built-in accountability rather than choosing from a broad clinician network.

Pricing structure, insurance, and term differences

Pricing is a major contrast. Sesame Care advertises ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging starting at $59/mo* and cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo, with medication costs varying by insurance status and pharmacy. Sesame notes specific price-with-insurance figures “as low as $25” for certain branded meds and cash-pay tiers for different strengths. He & She MD highlights promotional six-month introductory rates: semaglutide injections start at $139/month introductory, oral semaglutide and tirzepatide injections at $199/month introductory, with plans structured as six-month terms that renew at higher six-month prices. He & She MD also states no insurance is needed and free expedited shipping is included.

Medication format and regulatory notes

Sesame Care lists branded options including semaglutide and tirzepatide with multiple strength and price tiers and notes that Wegovy pill is FDA-approved for certain adults. Sesame Care also includes a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. He & She MD offers compounded GLP-1 medications produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies and explicitly states those compounded medications are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality. He & She MD emphasizes that medications require clinician consultation and prescription and that not all customers will qualify.

Bundled support, shipping and logistics

He & She MD bundles coaching, 1:1 clinician messaging and progress tracking in its app at no extra cost and includes free expedited shipping with no membership fee. Sesame Care focuses on flexible scheduling, nationwide access in many states, and letting patients choose clinicians for ongoing video visits, labs and messaging; medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary by insurance and pharmacy.

How to choose between Sesame Care and He & She MD

Choose Sesame Care if you want broad telehealth choice and the ability to use insurance for weight loss medications, or if you prefer to browse clinicians, compare specialties and pricing, and hold a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment that supports ongoing provider relationships and labs.

Choose He & She MD if you prioritize bundled coaching and 1:1 clinician messaging built into an app, want free expedited shipping with no membership fee, and are willing to accept six-month introductory pricing that renews at a higher six-month rate; note that He & She MD uses compounded GLP-1 medications that are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality.

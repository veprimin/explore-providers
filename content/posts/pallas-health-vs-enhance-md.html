Pricing detail and payment structure

Pallas Health publishes a clear first-month figure for compounded semaglutide of $139, then a renewal cadence of $597 every 12 weeks (roughly $199 per month average). Compounded tirzepatide starts at $179 then renews $897 every 12 weeks (about $299 per month average). Pallas also lists cash-pay prices for branded GLP-1s by month. Enhance MD emphasizes the lowest first-month prices in the market with semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99, and highlights flat-rate pricing where the ongoing price remains the same regardless of dose after the introductory period.

Drug options and clinical routing

Pallas Health explicitly offers both FDA-approved brand-name GLP-1 options and clinician-personalized compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared in the USA when a brand medication is not the right fit. Enhance MD provides compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide only. Both providers note that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and both include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Telehealth access, patient volume, and operational notes

Pallas markets itself as 100% online with asynchronous care in most states, stating that 43 of 50 states need no video visit while seven states plus Washington D.C. require a video visit. Enhance MD highlights a large real-world volume, saying it has treated more than 10,000 patients, and notes clinician-directed compounds and metabolic lab testing cadence in its materials. Pallas includes provider care, check-ins and messaging in its plan price with no membership fee; Enhance MD emphasizes predictable budgeting through flat-rate pricing.

Shipping, promotions, and consumer considerations

Pallas states free expedited and discreet shipping on every plan and that compounded medications are prepared by US-licensed compounding pharmacies. Enhance MD mentions promotional savings such as up to $150 off a first order and positions its introductory prices as market-leading for the first month. Neither provider bills insurance according to the published material; both operate on cash-pay models and note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

How to choose between Pallas Health vs Enhance MD

Choose Pallas Health if you want a clinician to consider FDA-approved brand-name pens such as Wegovy or Ozempic as well as US-compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide, if you value free expedited shipping, or if you prefer a plan that includes provider care, check-ins and messaging with no membership fee. Choose Enhance MD if your top priority is the lowest first-month compounded price for semaglutide or tirzepatide and a flat-rate ongoing pricing approach that does not increase as dose rises. Both providers require clinician review and both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Trimi vs Wellorithm: headline differences

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: pricing transparency and starting price, clinician coverage and state availability, shipping and fulfillment, compounded medication regulatory notes and guarantees, and intake or onboarding process. Both offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prescribed by licensed clinicians, but the specifics diverge.

1. Pricing transparency and starting cost

Trimi lists compounded semaglutide from $99/month on the annual plan and tirzepatide at $125/month with flat pricing that does not rise as the dose increases. Wellorithm advertises starting prices such as $147 and higher depending on form and medication, but does not publish full pricing for every plan and provides a personalized quote after the intake.

2. Clinician network and state coverage

Wellorithm explicitly highlights licensed clinicians in all 50 states and frames that coverage as a primary strength for patients in states with limited telehealth GLP-1 options. Trimi also uses licensed U.S. providers and notes a clinical network (Arora Health) with medical oversight; Trimi states its Medical Director is licensed in all 50 states, but the source emphasizes Trimi’s network and named compounding pharmacies rather than an explicit 50-state claim as Wellorithm makes.

3. Shipping and fulfillment

Trimi advertises free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and flat pricing that includes shipping. Wellorithm notes fast, free shipping in its messaging and ships medication to your home after a virtual assessment. Both platforms present shipping as included, but Trimi specifically promotes overnight, temperature-controlled shipping while Wellorithm emphasizes fast free shipping without the overnight claim in the captured evidence.

4. Compounded medication notes and guarantees

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Trimi additionally advertises a 6-month money-back promise where you can request a refund if you do not lose weight after following the plan for 6 months, with terms applied. Wellorithm advertises a “Weight Loss Guarantee” in its captured messaging but does not provide the specific refund terms in the supplied evidence.

5. Onboarding and assessment process

Wellorithm highlights a 2-minute online quiz to check eligibility and a fully virtual consultation that leads to a personalized quote and medication shipment. Trimi emphasizes care prescribed by licensed clinicians through Arora Health with named compounding pharmacies and certificate of analysis available on request; the Trimi evidence emphasizes medical oversight and clinician review rather than a short quiz intake.

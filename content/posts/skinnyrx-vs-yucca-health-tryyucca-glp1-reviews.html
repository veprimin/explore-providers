SkinnyRx vs Yucca Health: quick verdict

SkinnyRx emphasizes oral semaglutide tablets with FSA/HSA eligibility and free overnight shipping, starting at as low as $249/mo. Yucca Health (TryYucca) undercuts price with compounded semaglutide from $146/mo and also offers tirzepatide plans starting at $258/mo; approvals are typically fast, with UPS 2-Day Air shipping on most orders. Early verdict: choose SkinnyRx if FSA/HSA eligibility and overnight delivery matter most; choose Yucca Health if lower starting price or tirzepatide access is your priority.

How the two providers differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: price and payment options, medication choices (semaglutide vs tirzepatide), shipping and approval speed, FSA/HSA eligibility and financial features, and notes on compounded medication status and clinical review.

Price and payment options

SkinnyRx lists daily compounded semaglutide tablets from as low as $249/mo and mentions split payments with Affirm Buy Now Pay Later. Yucca Health advertises compounded semaglutide from $146/month and tirzepatide from $258/month, plus 6-month plan options (for example a 6-month semaglutide plan at $875) and multiple Buy Now Pay Later options including Klarna, Afterpay, and Affirm.

Medication selection and clinical framing

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications. SkinnyRx emphasizes daily oral semaglutide tablets as an alternative to injections. Yucca Health offers both compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, noting tirzepatide activates both GIP and GLP-1 receptors and is available from approximately $258/month.

Shipping, approval speed, and delivery

SkinnyRx highlights free overnight shipping on all orders and says patients receive shipping and tracking information within 2 business days after prescription processing. Yucca Health advertises fast 24-hour approvals, UPS 2-Day Air shipping on most orders, and first shipments arriving within 3 to 5 business days after completing the intake.

FSA/HSA and financing

SkinnyRx states its plans are FSA/HSA eligible so patients can use pre-tax dollars subject to their plan administrator rules. Yucca Health says some patients may be able to use HSA/FSA funds but advises confirming eligibility with your plan administrator; Yucca also promotes a Lifetime Price Lock code and multiple BNPL options.

Clinical oversight and quality assurances

Both platforms require a licensed provider review before a prescription is written. SkinnyRx states partner pharmacies perform third-party testing through FDA- and DEA-compliant labs every 3 to 6 months confirming concentration within plus or minus 10 percent for compounded lots. Yucca Health states medications are filled at licensed U.S. compounding pharmacies with physician-led clinical review; both providers note compounded medications are not FDA-reviewed finished products.

Realistic expectations and supported claims

Neither provider guarantees weight-loss outcomes. SkinnyRx cites potential early changes like losing up to 8.6 lbs your first month in landing content and references clinical research about semaglutide effects; Yucca Health references clinical ranges and notes research suggesting tirzepatide can drive higher average weight loss than semaglutide in trials. Both note results vary and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

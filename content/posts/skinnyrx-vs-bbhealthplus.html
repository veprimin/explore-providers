Top differences at a glance

We compare the most distinctive supported differences: starting price and pricing model, medication choices and formats, membership and fees, shipping and fulfillment, and regulatory notes about compounded medications.

Pricing and subscription model

SkinnyRx lists daily semaglutide tablets starting at “As low as $249/mo” and mentions split payments with Affirm Buy Now Pay Later. Bodybuilding Health+ offers compounded semaglutide “from $179/mo” and tirzepatide “from $209/mo.” Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes no hidden fees, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime, while SkinnyRx presents subscription services that can be canceled at any time but notes specific refund timing tied to provider prescription review.

Medication choices and delivery format

SkinnyRx focuses on daily oral semaglutide tablets as an alternative to injections, highlighting a tablet taken daily under the tongue and positioning tablets as clinically supported ingredients to support weight loss and metabolic health. Bodybuilding Health+ offers a broader GLP-1 lineup including compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, plus non-GLP-1 programs such as NAD+ and sermorelin and sexual performance options.

Membership, billing, and cancellation

Bodybuilding Health+ repeatedly states “No Monthly Membership” and “Cancel Anytime,” and lists multi-month bundles that lower effective monthly cost. SkinnyRx describes subscription plans that can be canceled at any time, and notes you may cancel and receive a full refund any time before a licensed provider completes your prescription review; once prescription fulfillment begins, orders become non-refundable.

Clinician oversight, testing, and fulfillment

Both platforms operate with licensed providers reviewing online consultations before prescriptions are issued. SkinnyRx emphasizes ongoing access to patient care teams and that partner pharmacies perform third-party testing through FDA- and DEA-compliant labs on every compounded lot every 3 to 6 months, confirming concentration within plus or minus 10 percent. Bodybuilding Health+ notes provider-reviewed medications and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Regulatory note and efficacy claims

Both sources include a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. SkinnyRx states compounded semaglutide has not been approved by the FDA for weight loss and that benefits outlined are based on third-party studies, with results that may vary. Bodybuilding Health+ similarly notes compounded GLP-1s have not been evaluated or approved by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality.

How Regenics vs Telos Rx compare at a glance

This comparison uses only published details from each provider. Both offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prescribed online and note that compounded medications are not FDA approved. Key differences are starting price, clinical oversight model, renewal or price framing, additional peptide options, and pharmacy or certification details.

Top distinctions to watch

  • Price framing: Regenics lists semaglutide at $139 first month and tirzepatide at $189 first month with renewals noted at $199 and $279. Telos Rx presents semaglutide as low as $99 and tirzepatide as low as $139.
  • Prescribing clinicians: Regenics describes licensed nurse practitioners reviewing intake and overseeing prescriptions. Telos Rx states a US-licensed provider reviews and prescribes.
  • Additional therapies: Regenics offers Sermorelin peptide therapy as an add-on. Telos Rx information does not list a peptide add-on in the captured content.
  • Pharmacy and certification: Regenics says medications are compounded by U.S. licensed pharmacies with a LegitScript certified pharmacy network. Telos Rx notes compounded medications are prescription-only and prescribed after review by a US-licensed provider.
  • Cancellation and renewals: Regenics notes promotional pricing applies to the first month and renewal rates. Telos Rx materials state cancel anytime with no penalties and that your price depends on your plan.

Clinical oversight and safety notes

Both sites make clear that a licensed clinician must determine if treatment is appropriate. Regenics specifically describes nurse-practitioner-led care and a LegitScript certified pharmacy network. Telos Rx emphasizes that a US-licensed provider reviews eligibility and prescribes. Both repeat the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved as finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Who offers extra therapy options and shipping

Regenics lists a Sermorelin peptide therapy option and states free shipping with medication compounded in the USA. Telos Rx information captured does not list a peptide add-on or a shipping promise in the captured content. If a peptide add-on or free shipping matters to you, Regenics documents those features in its materials.

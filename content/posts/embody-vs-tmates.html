Head-to-head: Price, clinical support, shipping, medication type, and availability

This Embody vs TMates comparison focuses on five distinctive differences backed by the supplied evidence: starting price, physician access, shipping speed and location, compounded medication details, and program features such as dose pricing and membership. Each section compares both providers directly.

1) Starting price and pricing model

Embody: Starting price for compounded semaglutide is $69/month, reduced from $299, and compounded tirzepatide is $119/month. Pricing is presented as flat locked monthly pricing with no monthly membership and cancel anytime policies. Final pricing may vary based on dose and clinician recommendation and the $69 pricing is a limited-time offer that may increase.

TMates: Flat $199/month at every dose with no step-up fees as dosage escalates. TMates markets a consistent $199/month rate and emphasizes the same price at all doses for predictable budgeting.

2) Clinical access and oversight

Embody: Prescribes compounded GLP-1s after a licensed provider review and includes prescription and telemed visits. The evidence notes licensed provider review handled entirely online and 24/7 support access for dosing and side-effect questions.

TMates: Offers 24/7 access to U.S.-based doctors across all 50 states and includes virtual check-in appointments for ongoing medical oversight. TMates positions actual physician availability as a distinguishing clinical supervision feature compared with messaging-only models.

3) Shipping speed and fulfillment

Embody: Free shipping with delivery in 1–2 days after clinician approval, described as one of the faster delivery commitments in the GLP-1 telehealth space.

TMates: Free shipping is included, with shipments to most states and availability based on medical eligibility. The supplied evidence does not claim a 1–2 day delivery window for TMates.

4) Medication formulation and regulatory notes

Both providers supply compounded semaglutide rather than branded Ozempic, Wegovy, or Zepbound. Embody offers compounded tirzepatide at $119/month; TMates’ public detail on tirzepatide availability is limited. Both pages include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

5) Program features and commitments

Embody emphasizes a no-frills, cost-driven model: flat locked monthly pricing, no monthly membership, cancel anytime, and prescription and telemed visits included. TMates emphasizes a clinically supervised experience with consistent physician availability, nationwide licensed coverage, and virtual check-ins. TMates accepts most major insurance plans in general but the supplied evidence also explains TMates can be used as a direct-pay flat-rate option for those without insurance.

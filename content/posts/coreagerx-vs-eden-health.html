Quick verdict: CoreAgeRx vs Eden Health

CoreAgeRx and Eden Health both offer clinician-reviewed online GLP-1 programs, but they differ on listed monthly prices, price guarantees, and some visit and shipping promises. Early verdict: CoreAgeRx emphasizes flat same-price every month pricing and short online visits; Eden Health emphasizes a lower semaglutide entry price for some patients and a lifetime price guarantee plus free expedited shipping.

Pricing and guarantees compared

Both vendors list a monthly price for GLP-1 medications without requiring insurance. CoreAgeRx advertises semaglutide at $79 per month and tirzepatide at $129 per month with “Same Price. Every Month.” Eden Health lists semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129 and advertises a “Lifetime price guarantee.” Both explicitly state no membership fees in the captured evidence.

Program experience: visits, support, and shipping

CoreAgeRx highlights a “3-MINUTE ONLINE VISIT” and says treatment is clinician-reviewed. Eden Health emphasizes a guided GLP-1 program that “brings medication, care, and support together” and includes “Free expedited shipping” in the captured details. Eden states the program is one monthly price, guaranteed for life.

Dose limits and medication notes

CoreAgeRx lists dose limits in captured details: tirzepatide any dose up to 7.5 mg and semaglutide any dose up to 1.0 mg. Eden Health lists monthly pricing at every dose but the captured evidence provides the two price points only. Both note that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

1) Price and promotional positioning

Measured lists promotional compounded semaglutide from $89/month and tirzepatide from $189/month, positioning itself as the lower-cost option on starting price and advertising free two-day shipping. HumeCare+ starts semaglutide at $199/month and tirzepatide plus B12 and glycine at $299/month, which is higher but reflects a bundled protocol rather than bare medication.

2) Treatment protocol: bundled support versus medication-only focus

HumeCare+ sells a protocol: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide combined with glycine, vitamins B6 and B12 built into the formulation, plus an app and ongoing clinician optimization. Measured pairs compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with dietitian support and personalized plans but does not bundle glycine or vitamins into the medication as a listed feature.

Direct contrast: HumeCare+ positions glycine and B vitamins as muscle-preserving and side-effect support built into its protocol. Measured emphasizes dietitian support and meal plans alongside the compounded GLP-1 medication.

3) Monitoring and outcome claims

HumeCare+ offers monthly body-composition scans that track lean mass versus fat over time and advertises a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months subject to terms. Measured cites internal member-reported averages such as 31 lb loss at six months in its marketing, and offers meal plans and dietitian support; its outcome claims are internal and not independent evidence.

4) Access, shipping, and convenience

Measured highlights same-day prescription decisions, unlimited messaging, a 24/7 care team and free two-day shipping. HumeCare+ offers unlimited clinician access and free next-day delivery, plus a dedicated app for tracking medication and food logging. Both advertise cancel-anytime policies.

5) Regulatory notes and safety qualifiers

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications and the sources state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HumeCare+ explicitly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate; Measured includes similar regulatory cautions in its captured evidence.

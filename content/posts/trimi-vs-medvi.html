Comparing Trimi vs MedVi quickly: Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/mo with flat pricing that never rises as dose increases and free overnight shipping, while MedVi promotes a winter promotional first month of $179 with fast, free shipping and 24/7 certified medical support. Early verdict: choose Trimi if you prioritize flat per-dose pricing and an explicit money-back promise; choose MedVi if you value a simple no-clinic process and 24/7 support and a low promotional first month.

Key differences at a glance

The most distinctive differences between Trimi and MedVi are price structure and promotional messaging, availability of tirzepatide at Trimi, shipping and fulfillment promises, and the level and type of clinician support and guarantees. Below we compare each directly using only the supplied evidence.

1. Pricing structure and promotions

Trimi: Semaglutide from $99/month on the annual plan with flat pricing every dose and the claim that “same price at every dose.” The detailed description repeats “Semaglutide from $99/mo · Flat pricing every dose.” MedVi: a winter promotional price of $179 for the first month, including fast, free shipping, with ongoing standalone months reported higher in the source. The MedVi source also notes the promotional first month then higher refill rates; the FAQ captured says the $179 rate is a promotional first-month price and that ongoing price is substantially higher.

2. Medication options: semaglutide and tirzepatide

Trimi explicitly lists both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide, with tirzepatide noted at $125/month and semaglutide at $99/month on annual plans. The source emphasizes “Compounded Tirzepatide | $125/month” and compounds for both active ingredients. MedVi focuses on clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only; the source notes MedVi offers semaglutide and that it offers compounded semaglutide only, not tirzepatide.

3. Shipping and fulfillment

Trimi states “Free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping” and repeats “Free overnight shipping” multiple times. MedVi advertises “fast, free shipping” as part of the winter promotional first month. Both reference compounded medications filled at regulated pharmacies, but only Trimi specifically repeats overnight temperature-controlled shipping in the captured content.

4. Clinical oversight, guarantees, and member experience

Trimi highlights that prescriptions are reviewed and prescribed by licensed U.S. providers through Arora Health and mentions a 6-month money-back promise: “Follow your plan for 6 months, and if you don’t lose weight, you can request a refund. Terms apply.” Trimi also notes compounding by state-licensed 503A pharmacies and availability of a certificate of analysis on request. MedVi emphasizes a simple online intake with no clinic visits, 24/7 support from certified medical professionals, and clinician review via an affiliated clinician network; MedVi does not publish a weight-loss guarantee but notes results vary and that medication prescriptions are at the clinician’s discretion.

What the sources say about safety and regulation

Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Trimi explicitly states that compounded medications are prepared per individual prescription and are not themselves FDA-approved as drugs. MedVi similarly notes compounded GLP-1s are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but are not FDA-approved and encourages discussion with a provider about risks and benefits.

Direct practical tradeoffs

If your priority is steady, predictable per-dose cost and an explicit refund policy, Trimi’s repeated claims of flat pricing “that never rises as your dose increases” and the 6-month money-back promise are central selling points. If you prefer a lower up-front promotional entry price and round-the-clock certified support with a no-clinic process, MedVi’s winter promotional first month at $179 and 24/7 support may appeal, but the source indicates ongoing months can cost more.

Conclusion: Trimi vs MedVi

Trimi vs MedVi comes down to price predictability and medication choice versus promotional entry pricing and continuous access to support. Trimi offers compounded semaglutide from $99/mo with flat pricing every dose, free overnight shipping, a 6-month money-back promise, and a tirzepatide option. MedVi offers a winter promotional first month at $179 with fast, free shipping, a simple no-clinic online process, and 24/7 certified medical support but documents higher ongoing costs after the promo.

Choose Trimi if you want flat per-dose pricing, a tirzepatide option, overnight temperature-controlled shipping, and an explicit 6-month money-back promise. Choose MedVi if you want a lower promotional first-month price, a fully online no-clinic intake, and 24/7 certified medical support.

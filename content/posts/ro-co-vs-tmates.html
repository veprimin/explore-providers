How Ro.co and TMates differ at a glance

Ro.co and TMates both offer online GLP-1 weight loss care but differ on medication type, pricing model, clinical access, and regulatory status. The most distinctive supported differences are: medication formulation and FDA approval; entry and ongoing pricing; clinician availability and support; and U.S. manufacturing versus branded finished product access.

Medication type and FDA status

Ro.co: Offers access to the Wegovy pill, described as the first and only FDA-approved oral GLP-1 weight loss medication. The platform emphasizes that the Wegovy pill is an FDA-approved finished medication, not compounded. Treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Ro.co notes that compounded medications, when offered by others, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

TMates: Offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide formulated in U.S. facilities. TMates states that compounded semaglutide uses the same active ingredient mechanism as Wegovy and Ozempic but is compounded rather than an FDA-approved finished product. The source explicitly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Top differences at a glance for Hers vs Ro.co

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by source facts: oral versus injectable availability, starting price and entry cost, FDA-approved finished products versus compounded alternatives, membership and billing requirements, and how pricing is disclosed.

Oral Wegovy availability and medication type

Ro.co is the only mainstream platform here explicitly offering access to the Wegovy pill, the first FDA-approved oral GLP-1 for weight loss. Hers offers FDA-approved Wegovy as a brand-name semaglutide option as well, but Ro.co uniquely emphasizes an injection-free oral route for patients who are injection-averse. Both providers note that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Starting prices and entry costs

Ro.co advertises a Get Started Online fee of just $39 as the direct-pay entry point. Hers lists Wegovy pill pricing From $149/mo and injectable options From $199/mo, and also states From $149/mo in other semaglutide listings. Hers further notes a membership requirement and separate medication billing that affects total cost.

Membership, billing, and pricing disclosure

Hers requires an active Hers Weight Loss Membership to access medication. The source states an initial membership fee ($39 for the first month) that auto-renews at $149/month thereafter, and that membership and medication are billed separately. Hers also cautions that a prescription is not guaranteed and medication is not available without an active membership. Ro.co emphasizes transparent pricing disclosed before completing a consultation and states no hidden fees on captured pages.

Conclusion: Hers vs Ro.co

Hers vs Ro.co hinges on oral availability, entry cost, and membership. Ro.co is the practical pick for patients who want the Wegovy pill and a low $39 start with pricing disclosed upfront. Hers is the better match for patients who want a membership-based program that offers brand-name Wegovy with ongoing dose check-ins and who accept membership billing requirements. Both require clinician review and note that compounded medications are not FDA-reviewed.

Choose Ro.co if you need injection-free access to the FDA-approved Wegovy pill and want a low $39 online start with pricing disclosed before consultation.

Choose Hers if you want a membership program that provides FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy, personalized care team support and ongoing dose check-ins, and accept the membership billing structure.

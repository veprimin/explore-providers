## About Hone Health

Hone Health is a telehealth provider that treats erectile dysfunction as one
symptom in a broader picture rather than a standalone complaint. Instead of
opening with a fixed medication, it opens with diagnostics: an at-home blood
draw that measures testosterone and other hormonal markers, read by a
physician who then designs a plan around the results.

For men whose ED has a hormonal contributor — low testosterone being the
common one — that ordering matters. A vascular-only pill does nothing for a
hormonal cause, and Hone's premise is that you should know which you are
dealing with before you medicate. The trade-off is that this is a longer,
more involved path than an ED-only service, and it is priced per protocol
rather than as a flat monthly rate.

## How It Works

Hone Health runs a structured, fully online workflow, but the diagnostics come
first rather than last.

### At-home hormone panel

The process begins with an at-home lab kit. You collect the sample, send it
back, and the panel measures testosterone and related hormonal markers. This
step is what separates Hone from an ED-only provider — the labs inform the
plan rather than confirming a decision already made.

### Physician review of results

A licensed clinician reviews the panel alongside your medical history,
current medications, and symptoms. Because there is real diagnostic data on
the table, the review looks at hormonal contributors to ED, not just the
symptom in isolation.

### Personalised protocol

If treatment is appropriate, the clinician builds a protocol around the
results. Depending on what the labs show, that can include ED medication,
hormonal support, or a combination — rather than a single fixed formula
dispensed to everyone.

### Ongoing monitoring

Hone is built for continuity: follow-up messaging, refills, and repeat testing
where a protocol calls for it, so treatment can be adjusted against fresh
numbers rather than set once and left.

## What the Programme Covers

Because the plan follows the panel, what a given patient receives varies. In
broad terms the programme spans:

- **Diagnostics** — the at-home hormone panel that anchors the whole approach,
  covering testosterone and related markers.
- **ED treatment where indicated** — standard PDE5-inhibitor options
  (sildenafil, tadalafil) when the clinical picture points to a vascular cause.
- **Hormonal support where indicated** — treatment aimed at a hormonal
  contributor the labs surface, which an ED-only service would never test for.
- **Physician-led planning** — the specific mix, dosing, and monitoring set by
  the reviewing clinician against your results, not a template.

## Pros and Cons

**Pros**

- At-home lab testing is included, so hormonal contributors to ED actually get
  looked at rather than assumed away
- Physician-led treatment planning built around your results, rather than a
  single fixed formula
- Continuity of care — monitoring and repeat testing where the protocol calls
  for it
- Addresses causes an ED-only pill provider is not set up to find

**Cons**

- More steps than an ED-only service — labs and review before any treatment
- Total cost depends on the personalised protocol, and no flat rate is
  published up front
- Slower to first dose than a same-day questionnaire-and-ship service
- Overkill if your ED is straightforward and you just want an on-demand pill

## Who Hone Health Is For

It makes most sense for men who:

- Suspect a hormonal contributor — low energy, low libido, or low testosterone
  alongside the ED
- Want the underlying cause investigated before committing to a medication
- Value ongoing monitoring over a fire-and-forget subscription
- Are comfortable with a longer intake and protocol-based pricing

It is a poor fit if you want the cheapest possible generic sildenafil, a fixed
monthly price you can see before signing up, or a pill in hand the same week.

## How to Get Started

1. **Order the panel** — create an account and request the at-home hormone kit.
2. **Collect and return the sample** — a simple at-home blood draw sent back to
   the lab.
3. **Physician review** — a licensed clinician reads the results with your
   history and symptoms.
4. **Personalised protocol** — if appropriate, treatment is built around the
   labs and shipped, with monitoring set up for ongoing care.

## Verdict

Hone Health is the right call when you think your ED is a symptom of something
else — a hormonal issue in particular — and you want that investigated before
you medicate. Leading with an at-home hormone panel and building a
physician-led protocol around the results is a genuinely different model from
the questionnaire-to-pill services that dominate the category, and for the
right patient it is the more thorough one.

The honest counterweight is that it costs you time and predictability. There
are more steps before your first dose, and there is no flat published price —
the total depends on the protocol the clinician sets. If your ED is
straightforward and you simply want an on-demand pill at a known price, an
ED-only provider is the cleaner choice. If you want the cause looked at
properly, Hone is built for exactly that.

Shed vs JoinSkinny pits a coaching-focused platform that offers both name-brand and compounded GLP-1 options against a flat-rate plan provider with bundled shipping and quick delivery. Early verdict: choose Shed if you want ongoing coaching paired with medication; choose JoinSkinny if you prioritize transparent flat-rate pricing and fast, temperature-controlled shipping.

How they differ at a glance: coaching, pricing structure, meds, and shipping

Both platforms require clinician review and offer telehealth prescribing, but they emphasize different elements. Shed centers on integrated, continuous coaching and gives access to both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 and compounded options. JoinSkinny emphasizes flat-rate pricing, monthly and multi-month plans starting at $149, and free temperature-controlled shipping that can arrive in 1 to 2 days.

Coaching and ongoing support

Shed integrates continuous coaching into treatment rather than only issuing a prescription. The source describes coaching as part of every treatment plan, supporting titration, lifestyle changes, and plateaus. JoinSkinny provides ongoing support, dose adjustments, monthly check-ins, and 24/7 chat via its telemedicine dashboard, but the emphasis is on clinician-reviewed prescriptions and monitoring rather than a coaching-first model.

Medication options and regulatory notes

Shed offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded GLP-1 options. The source explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a provider’s prescription. JoinSkinny offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and states prescriptions are filled by licensed, U.S.-based pharmacies that meet FDA regulations and hold LegitScript certification. Both providers include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-reviewed for safety, effectiveness, or quality when compounded products are relevant.

Pricing, promotions, and billing

Shed lists compounded semaglutide injections starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide injections starting at $299/month, and advertises 10% off your first month. Shed also notes name-brand options carry significantly higher costs than compounded alternatives and that standard pricing is not published until after intake. JoinSkinny advertises weight loss plans starting at $149, offers a $140 promotional discount in the captured landing-page evidence, and uses transparent flat-rate pricing with monthly or multi-month bundles and installment options. JoinSkinny states you can cancel anytime before your next billing cycle.

Shipping, delivery, and logistics

JoinSkinny advertises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days with same-day processing for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, and notes weekend orders ship Monday to ensure temperature management. Shed states medication is shipped to your home after approval but the captured evidence does not specify guaranteed next-day shipping windows or temperature-controlled claims in the provided source text.

Cancellation, membership, and commitments

JoinSkinny repeatedly specifies no monthly membership and that you can cancel before the next billing cycle, with customer support available 24/7. Shed describes a coaching-bundled model where coaching is included in the monthly cost and notes that coaching-bundled models can cost more than bare-prescription platforms; standard pricing is provided after intake and coaching is integrated rather than optional per the source.

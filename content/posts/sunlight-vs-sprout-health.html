How Sunlight vs Sprout Health differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: pricing structure, medication format and regulatory notes, approval speed and onboarding, outcome framing, and shipping or membership terms. Each section compares both providers directly using only captured facts.

1. Pricing structure and promotions

Sunlight: captured landing-page evidence lists Personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo and repeated claims of “Same price forever” and promotional copy saying “as low as $88.” Sunlight advertises no monthly membership and cancel anytime language on the captured page.

Sprout Health: offers “Get $50 Off Your First Month,” which the FAQ frames as bringing the initial month to $199, with standard ongoing pricing of $249/month after the discounted first month. The source notes the ongoing $249 rate is higher than several competitors.

2. Medication options and regulatory notes

Sunlight: the captured content repeatedly states “injections & oral options” and presents Sunlight as offering prescription and telehealth included, no insurance required. The regulatory note in Sunlight’s source requires confirming medication-specific approval status; branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Sprout Health: the captured evidence specifically states its medication is compounded and notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sprout’s FAQ reiterates that its prescribing is for compounded semaglutide, and the regulatory note also reminds that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

3. Approval speed and onboarding experience

Sunlight: the captured text emphasizes a “100% online medical visit” and includes testimonials about quick clinician review and receiving medication within about a week. Specific timing beyond those testimonials is not independently verified in the capture.

Sprout Health: the captured page and FAQ emphasize “lightning-quick approval” and position the offering as a fast, low-friction entry to GLP-1 therapy. The source frames Sprout Health as well-suited to patients who want minimal onboarding friction.

4. Outcome framing and expectations

Sunlight: marketing testimonials on the captured page describe individual weight-loss experiences but the source includes no formal outcome guarantee. Sunlight’s messaging highlights individualized clinician adjustments in captured testimonials.

Sprout Health: frames outcomes around “Lose 10–26% of Your Body Weight in 12 Months,” citing clinical outcome literature as a basis for that framing, while also noting individual results vary and that Sprout does not offer a formal outcome guarantee in the captured content.

5. Shipping, membership, and cancellation

Sunlight: the landing-page evidence includes claims of “Free & Discreet 2-day shipping” and repeated “Fast, Free Shipping” language and states “No Monthly Membership” and “Cancel Anytime.”

Sprout Health: captured landing-page evidence did not include verified shipping, membership, or cancellation details. The source explicitly states that shipping/fees/membership/cancellation were not verified on the captured page.

2. Membership, fees, and cancellation

Gala: Marketed as direct-pay with no monthly membership, cancel anytime, and one flat price at every dose claimed. Provider visit and prescription are included in the flat price and no insurance is needed.

IVIM Health: Offers a membership model with a program fee of $74.99/mo. IVIM advertises a free month with code GIFT75 and a no-obligation free consultation. IVIM notes ongoing pricing rising from first month promotional rates to higher ongoing rates in its FAQ evidence.

3. Clinical protocol and oversight

Gala: Provides provider check-ins and dose monitoring included; treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. The site repeatedly notes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are offered and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

IVIM Health: Promotes a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol with weekly dose adjustments and clinician-guided optimization. IVIM cites internal studies and published retrospective observational studies supporting its protocol claims, while also noting that its 40% faster claim is proprietary and not an independent clinical trial result. IVIM requires an RX after clinical evaluation and prescribes compounded medications only after consultation.

4. Third-party ratings and evidence

Gala: The supplied page includes testimonials and marketing claims such as weight-loss examples, but no verified dual third-party rating is listed in the captured evidence.

IVIM Health: Emphasizes unusually strong external validation with a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot as a standout trust signal. IVIM also references two peer-reviewed retrospective observational studies published in Obesity Pillars for clinical outcomes, noting those are not randomized controlled trials and are independent studies.

5. Shipping, visits, and convenience

Gala: Advertises free 2-day shipping straight to your door and fully online care with no in-office visits required. Provider visit and prescription are included in the flat-price model and the landing-page evidence repeats “no clinic visits, no insurance hoops.”

IVIM Health: Operates as a clinician-guided telehealth platform and prepares compounded medications at licensed compounding pharmacies. Evidence notes medications are prepared by independent compounding pharmacies, may not be available in all states, and are used under ongoing supervision of an IVIM provider.

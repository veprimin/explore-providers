Pricing structures and promotions

Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and states FAQ standard pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. There is no separate monthly membership fee and month-to-month cancellation is available, though subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term. He & She MD offers lower introductory rates such as semaglutide at $139/month and tirzepatide at $199/month for the first six months, but those rates renew at higher six-month prices after the introductory term. Both providers present promotional pricing; Directmeds emphasizes no membership fee while He & She MD emphasizes bundled coaching during the promo term.

Support, coaching, and clinician access

Directmeds includes clinician review, telehealth visits, nursing support and ongoing clinician access as part of its offering. He & She MD bundles the He & She MD app that provides 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific coaching and progress tracking at no extra cost. The core difference is packaging: Directmeds highlights clinician and nursing support with medication and supplies included, while He & She MD markets an integrated coaching app with built-in messaging and tracking.

Medication formats, compounding, and regulatory notes

Directmeds offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats and notes that its medications are compounded in U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. He & She MD offers semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide and tirzepatide injection and states medications are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies. Both providers explicitly note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Shipping, availability and cancellation

Directmeds advertises free temperature-controlled next-day UPS or FedEx shipping generally in 1 to 2 days and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. It also notes service is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. He & She MD advertises free expedited shipping to your door, no membership fee and states medications require consultation and prescription with not all customers qualifying. He & She MD pricing is structured as six-month terms and the introductory rates renew at higher prices; Directmeds emphasizes month-to-month cancellation for standard customers.

Who each provider is best for

Directmeds is best for patients seeking compounded injectable or sublingual GLP-1 care with transparent standard pricing and no separate membership fee. He & She MD suits patients who want coaching and clinician access built into the plan rather than sold separately and who are comfortable with six-month introductory rates that renew higher.

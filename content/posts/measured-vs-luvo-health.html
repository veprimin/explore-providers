Measured vs Luvo Health is a direct comparison of two online weight-management providers that reference compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options. Quick verdict: Measured is explicit about promotional pricing and free two-day shipping, while Luvo Health lists tirzepatide and semaglutide options but current pricing and some details were not verified on the captured page.

How they differ on pricing and transparency

Measured provides explicit promotional pricing: compounded semaglutide from $89 per month and compounded tirzepatide from $189 per month, with repeated landing-page claims of promotional savings. Luvo Health shows a multi month structure and mentions MONTH 1, MONTH 2–3 and 3+ months with dose adjustments, but captured landing-page evidence did not verify current pricing or starting amounts.

Key point

Measured: starting price listed as 89 and 189 for promotional compounds. Luvo Health: pricing details were not published or could not be verified on the captured affiliate landing page.

Medications offered and formulation notes

Both providers reference semaglutide and tirzepatide options. Measured explicitly pairs compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and cautions that compounded medications are not FDA approved. Support, shipping and service differences

Measured emphasizes same-day prescription decisions, unlimited provider messaging, dietitian support, and free two-day shipping on every order. The captured Measured content also notes a 4.8 star Trustpilot rating from 51,000+ users and repeat claims of cancel anytime with no insurance required. Luvo Health describes ongoing support, dose changes, and cancellation and refund information but the capture did not verify specifics such as shipping speed, ratings, or exact support levels.

Regulatory and outcome notes to compare

Both providers include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Measured also includes an internal outcome claim based on internal analysis of members reporting weight data at 6 months with an average loss figure and the qualifier that individual results vary and are not guaranteed. The Luvo Health capture includes educational guides and dosing guides but did not publish independent outcome data on the captured page.

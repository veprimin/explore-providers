1. Pricing and plan structure

Telos Rx lists semaglutide as low as $99 per month and tirzepatide as low as $139 per month, with listed monthly price points and notes that your price depends on your plan. JoinSkinny reports weight-loss plans starting at $149, offers a $140 promotional discount at capture time, and sells monthly and 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles plus installment options through Afterpay, Klarna, and Affirm. 2. Medication type, compounding and regulatory notes

Both providers offer GLP-1 options and GLP-1 + GIP choices. Telos Rx explicitly states semaglutide and tirzepatide are compounded medications, prescription-only, subject to medical approval by a US-licensed provider, and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny notes GLP-1 and GLP-1 + GIP options and also includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. JoinSkinny additionally states prescriptions are filled by licensed, U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners when applicable and that they prepare authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1 + GIP when prescribed.

Direct comparison: Telos Rx highlights compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and repeated regulatory caution that compounded products are not FDA-approved. JoinSkinny highlights GLP-1 and GLP-1 + GIP options and states prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated pharmacies and LegitScript-certified partners when prescribed.

3. Prescribing, monitoring and pharmacy flow

Telos Rx requires review and approval by a US-licensed provider and states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. JoinSkinny describes a quick health questionnaire reviewed by a licensed provider, telemedicine-based dose adjustments, monthly check-ins, 24/7 chat, and that care is delivered by licensed U.S. providers with HIPAA protections. JoinSkinny explicitly says prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners; Telos Rx emphasizes compounded meds and that formulations are prescribed only after clinician review.

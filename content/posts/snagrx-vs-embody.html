Quick direct comparison: what differs most

This comparison focuses on four distinctive differences supported by the source material: pricing structure and commitment, price lock and re-pricing risk, transparency about clinician network and pharmacies, and guarantees plus support features. Both providers ship in 1 to 2 days and offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide at similar entry prices, but the qualifiers below explain how they are not identical offers.

1. Pricing structure and commitment

SnagRx lists semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan. The source states the advertised $69 and $119 figures require the 12-month plan; month-to-month pricing is $99 and $149. Embody lists semaglutide at $69/month and tirzepatide at $119/month with no long-term commitment and an explicit cancel anytime policy. In short, both show the same headline price, but SnagRx ties that price to a 12-month plan while Embody advertises $69 as a no-commitment price.

2. Price lock versus limited-time pricing

SnagRx advertises that its rate is locked flat at every dose and at renewal, explicitly noting that the rate does not rise as dose escalates. The source highlights that many compounded programs re-price during titration and positions SnagRx as charging one rate per medication for the duration. Embody states flat locked in monthly pricing and also notes the $69 offer was reduced from $299, with a source statement that the $69 price is a limited-time offer that may increase. The practical difference is that SnagRx emphasizes an ongoing price lock across titration for customers on the annual plan, while Embody frames $69 as a current low entry price that may change.

3. Transparency about clinicians and pharmacies

SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and its clinician network, OpenLoop Health, and states OpenLoop clinicians cover 48 states. The source says SnagRx states LegitScript certification and that clinicians typically review intake within five hours. Embody is described as a licensed telehealth platform with medication filled at a licensed pharmacy, but the captured source does not name individual partner pharmacies or an equivalent clinician network by name. Use this difference if pharmacy naming and named clinician networks matter to you.

4. Guarantees and support

SnagRx lists a full refund if a prescription is declined and a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms that apply. The source also notes clinician review timelines and next-day temperature-controlled shipping. Embody highlights free 1 to 2 day shipping and states 24/7 support access for dosing and side-effect questions. Embody emphasizes a lean, no-frills model and fast delivery. The distinction: SnagRx advertises outcome guarantees and refund terms explicitly, while Embody emphasizes continuous support access and no long-term commitment.

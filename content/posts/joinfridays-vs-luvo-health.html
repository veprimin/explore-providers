Overview: JoinFridays vs Luvo Health at a glance

This comparison focuses on the differences supported in the source material: starting price and discounts, medication formats offered, membership and cancellation terms, clinical oversight and dose adjustment language, and the availability of public pricing details. Treatment for both is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Key difference 1: Published starting price and discounts

JoinFridays: The captured JoinFridays pricing page shows a clear starting price, “starting at $117/month,” and lists promo codes such as NEWYOU12 and NYNY12 that the page describes as offering up to $500 off tirzepatide and up to $400 off semaglutide. The page also notes longer commitments can lower effective monthly rates with examples across one, three, six, and twelve month plans.

Luvo Health: The Luvo Health landing evidence did not publish verifiable starting price values on the captured affiliate landing page. It shows a pricing structure layout for month ranges and notes cancel anytime and dose adjustments based on clinical response, but specific dollar amounts were not verified on the captured page.

Key difference 2: Medication formats and labeling

JoinFridays: The JoinFridays content specifies both compounded tirzepatide and compounded semaglutide options on one platform and also mentions brand name pricing examples for semaglutide and tirzepatide as month to month options. The page includes a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and that Fridays does not manufacture compounded medications so actual product appearance and labeling will differ from website images.

Luvo Health: The Luvo Health captured content lists tirzepatide injection, semaglutide injection, and semaglutide tablets as available options and reiterates that compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and are not reviewed or approved by the FDA. It also shows illustrative images and informational links about dosing and side effect topics.

Key difference 3: Membership, cancellation, and commitment

JoinFridays: The source states JoinFridays has no membership fees, allows cancel anytime, and advertises no long term contract. Multi month plans are shown with examples and the page frames the cancel anytime model as patient friendly.

Luvo Health: The Luvo Health capture states “cancel anytime” and describes month 1, month 2 to 3, and 3 plus months structure with dose adjustments based on clinical response, but did not publish the specific renewal or fee details on the captured affiliate landing page.

Key difference 4: Clinical support and dose changes

JoinFridays: JoinFridays presents “personalized, clinician guided care” with clinician review determining appropriateness of treatment and dosing guidance during the program. The source emphasizes clinician guided personalization rather than templated protocols.

Luvo Health: Luvo Health highlights ongoing support and dose changes and specifically notes dose adjustments based on clinical response. The captured material also links to guides on dosing, microdosing, stopping semaglutide, and other clinical topics, but expanded FAQ answers were not captured.

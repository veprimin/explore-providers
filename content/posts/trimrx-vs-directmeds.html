Pricing and price protections compared

TrimRx emphasizes a starting price of $149/month for compounded GLP-1 and a separate $249/month start for GLP-1+GIP, plus Lifetime Price Protection so you purportedly pay the same price on every dose. Direct Meds advertises a promotional starting price of $147, with standard FAQ prices of $297 for semaglutide and $399 for tirzepatide, and notes subscribe-and-save plans require a stated term. Both state no separate monthly membership fee. The sources preserve promotional and FAQ distinctions, so reported prices differ depending on which figure you rely on.

Medication format, pharmacy and regulatory notes

Direct Meds explicitly lists injectable and sublingual compounded options. TrimRx highlights compounded injectable GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP and notes that if a branded FDA-approved medication is prescribed, the patient must fill and pay for that separately. Both sources state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Direct Meds also specifies U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies.

Shipping, support and program scale

TrimRx advertises free 1–2 day shipping and cites 100,000+ customers with a 4.8 rating, and promotes 24/7 support and HSA/FSA eligibility. Direct Meds advertises free temperature-controlled next-day UPS or FedEx shipping, clinician and nursing support, and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Direct Meds also notes it is unavailable in Mississippi and Louisiana.

How cancellation and refunds differ

TrimRx states no monthly membership and cancel anytime. Direct Meds states month-to-month cancellation is available for standard customers, while discounted subscription plans require the stated term; Direct Meds also advertises a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Both providers say there are no hidden fees in their landing-page materials.

Final comparison and recommendation: TrimRx vs Directmeds

Both platforms provide telehealth access to compounded GLP-1 and tirzepatide with free shipping and no separate membership fees. The clearest supported differences are TrimRx\’s Lifetime Price Protection and large reported customer base versus Direct Meds\’s injectable plus sublingual format options, explicit refund if a prescription is not approved, and stated unavailability in two states.

Choose TrimRx if you want a stated lifetime price lock and the provider\’s reported scale and 1–2 day shipping. Choose Direct Meds if you need injectable or sublingual compounded options, want the explicit full refund-if-not-approved policy, or prioritize Direct Meds\’s described next-day temperature-controlled shipping.

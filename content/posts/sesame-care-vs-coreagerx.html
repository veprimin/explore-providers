Sesame Care vs CoreAgeRx answer different needs: Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace emphasizing clinician choice and flexible care with subscriptions starting at $59/mo*, while CoreAgeRx emphasizes flat monthly pricing of $79/mo for semaglutide or $129/mo for tirzepatide with no membership fee. Early verdict: pick Sesame Care if you want broad clinician choice and insurance options; pick CoreAgeRx if you want predictable same price every month.

1. Pricing structure and predictability

Sesame Care presents a marketplace price structure with subscriptions “as low as $59/mo*” for ongoing visits, labs and messaging and notes cash-pay medication prices such as semaglutide from $149 and tirzepatide from $299, while also saying medication costs vary by insurance status and pharmacy. CoreAgeRx positions itself around predictable recurring charges: semaglutide at $79/mo and tirzepatide at $129/mo with “Same Price. Every Month.” CoreAgeRx emphasizes “No price hike, no membership fee, no billing surprises.”

2. Clinician access and care model

Sesame Care is described as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select from a “large clinician network” by specialty, availability and pricing so patients have choice in their clinician. CoreAgeRx emphasizes clinician-reviewed online weight-management visits and a brief “3-MINUTE ONLINE VISIT,” focusing on streamlined clinician review rather than marketplace selection. This is a direct tradeoff between broader choice at Sesame Care and streamlined, rapid visits at CoreAgeRx.

3. Medication offerings and dosing notes

Both platforms offer semaglutide and tirzepatide but present different dose and price details. Sesame Care lists semaglutide cash-pay prices (for example $149) and multiple tirzepatide strengths with corresponding cash-pay prices and notes branded products like Wegovy pill and Zepbound KwikPen with regulatory qualifiers. CoreAgeRx lists semaglutide up to 1.0 mg and tirzepatide up to 7.5 mg with flat monthly pricing. Both sources include the regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

4. Insurance and eligibility considerations

Sesame Care explicitly says “Insurance accepted for weight loss medications” and that medication costs will vary by insurance status and pharmacy. CoreAgeRx states “No insurance required” but also cautions that users should confirm eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment. Both sources warn that pricing and availability can change and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

5. User expectations and commitments

Sesame Care references a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment and milestones based on a study of injectable semaglutide when paired with lifestyle changes, with qualifiers that results may vary. CoreAgeRx warns that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change, and highlights consistent monthly bills as its differentiator. Neither source provides guarantees of outcomes; both require clinician review for medical appropriateness.

Quick recap

Sesame Care vs CoreAgeRx comes down to marketplace choice and insurance flexibility versus flat, predictable monthly pricing and streamlined visits. Sesame Care: choice, insurance acceptance, variable medication costs. CoreAgeRx: fixed monthly price tiers, no membership fee, emphasize price stability.

Choose based on these facts

Choose Sesame Care if you want broad telehealth choice, the ability to browse a large clinician network, and the option to use insurance for weight loss medications where accepted. Choose CoreAgeRx if you prioritize the advertised same price every month with no membership fee and prefer predictable monthly charges for semaglutide or tirzepatide.

Quick verdict: SnagRx is the lower-cost compounded option when you commit to a 12-month plan and names its US 503A partner pharmacies; JoinSkinny starts higher but emphasizes licensed, FDA-regulated pharmacy partners and ongoing medical support. This article compares SnagRx vs JoinSkinny across price structure, pharmacy/regulatory approach, clinical support, product type, and shipping and guarantees so you can decide which fits your priorities.

Price structure and commitments

SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan; month-to-month equivalents are $99 and $149. SnagRx states the $69 and $119 figures are the 12-month plan rates and that the annual plan locks that rate for the life of treatment. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149, with monthly and 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles and promotional discounts such as a $140 offer noted on its landing page. Both providers state flat-rate dosing where the monthly price stays the same regardless of dose increases, but SnagRx requires the 12-month commitment to access the lowest advertised rates.

Pharmacy and regulatory differences

SnagRx highlights named US 503A compounding pharmacies and states its compounded medications are produced in FDA-regulated facilities while also noting compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products. JoinSkinny states prescriptions are filled by licensed, U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners and that it supplies authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1 + GIP when prescribed. The core difference here is SnagRx emphasizes compounded products via named 503A partners, while JoinSkinny emphasizes licensed FDA-regulated pharmacy partners and LegitScript certification.

Clinical review, support, and ongoing care

SnagRx operates with a clinician network (OpenLoop Health) and states an OpenLoop clinician typically reviews intake within five hours and always within 24 hours; prescriptions are subject to clinician review and clinicians retain the decision to prescribe. SnagRx includes prescription and telemed visits with each price but does not offer coaching or nutrition programming. JoinSkinny describes care by licensed U.S. providers, monthly check-ins, dose adjustments, and 24/7 chat for ongoing support. JoinSkinny therefore positions itself as offering continuous medical support and dose management, while SnagRx focuses on clinician review speed and prescription inclusion but not ongoing coaching services.

Product type and what is supplied

SnagRx supplies compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide only; the provider explicitly states it offers compounded GLP-1 and GLP-1 + GIP and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. JoinSkinny states it provides GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and that its pharmacies prepare authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1 + GIP when prescribed, while also noting that branded prescriptions are filled and paid for separately. If whether a product is compounded versus filled through licensed pharmacies is a key factor for you, SnagRx and JoinSkinny take different approaches.

Shipping, refunds, and guarantees

Both providers advertise next-day or 1-2 day delivery in temperature-controlled packaging. SnagRx states approved orders ship next-day via UPS or FedEx in discreet, temperature-controlled packaging, offers full refunds if a prescription is declined, and mentions a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms that apply. JoinSkinny states prescriptions received before 2 pm central time ship the same day and that shipments are insured for lost prescriptions with certain exceptions; JoinSkinny also advertises free temperature-controlled shipping. Each provider includes cancellation or pause options: SnagRx says “Cancel Anytime” and no monthly membership; JoinSkinny says you can cancel before your next billing cycle and pause or adjust plans with support available 24/7.

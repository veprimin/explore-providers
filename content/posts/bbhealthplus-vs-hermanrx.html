Direct comparison: Bodybuilding Health+ vs HermanRx

This comparison focuses on five key differences supported by the source: published starting prices, what is included in the monthly price, program breadth and add-ons, availability and shipping, and the regulatory note about compounded medications. Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide subject to clinician review; the distinctions below are based only on the supplied evidence.

1. Published starting price and pricing structure

Bodybuilding Health+ lists compounded semaglutide starting at $179/month and tirzepatide starting at $209/month with multi-month bundles that reduce effective monthly cost. HermanRx lists semaglutide + B12 at $249/month and tirzepatide + B12 at $349/month as all-inclusive monthly prices. The sources present Bodybuilding Health+ as lower starting prices and HermanRx as higher, single monthly rates that include B12.

2. What the monthly price includes

HermanRx explicitly markets its GLP-1 prices as all-inclusive: provider review, prescription, medication, ongoing support and free discreet shipping are covered with no separate membership or consultation fee. Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes no hidden fees and no monthly membership, and it highlights provider-reviewed medications and transparent pricing; the source notes bundles and multi-month savings but does not specify an identical all-inclusive line-item list as HermanRx.

3. Program breadth and add-ons

Bodybuilding Health+ presents a multi-program platform spanning weight management, longevity (NAD+, sermorelin) and sexual performance (ED options) on one site, with specific starting prices for NAD+ and sermorelin from $129/month and ED options from $96/month. HermanRx covers multiple categories too, including weight loss, hormones, longevity, hair loss and sexual health, and bundles B12 with its GLP-1 offerings. Both platforms allow consolidation of care across categories, but the sources highlight different add-on framing: Bodybuilding Health+ lists additional therapies and bundle discounts, HermanRx bundles B12 and advertises 24/7 support and a secure portal.

4. Availability, timelines and shipping

HermanRx states availability in all 50 states, fast timelines with about a 5-minute assessment, ~24 hour provider review and ~72 hour delivery in some descriptions, plus free discreet shipping in plain unmarked packaging. Bodybuilding Health+ notes not available in all U.S. states and emphasizes shipping to your door and fully online care, but the source does not supply HermanRx-like published turnaround estimates.

5. Regulatory note and clinician oversight

Both sources state that compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved as finished products and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Each platform also makes clear that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Bodybuilding Health+ names medical oversight by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D., while HermanRx says independent licensed, board-certified physicians review intakes.

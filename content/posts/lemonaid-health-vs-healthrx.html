Direct differences that matter

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: published pricing and plans, compounded medication and regulatory notes, shipping and cold chain, clinician review role, and the visibility of enrollment terms. Each point below compares Lemonaid Health and HealthRx using only the supplied evidence.

1. Pricing and plan transparency

HealthRx: The captured evidence lists prices: $99 per month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month. It states the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan and that plan price does not change when your dose does.

Lemonaid Health: The captured affiliate landing page did not publish or verify current price, price range, or starting price. The evidence explicitly notes that current details were not published or could not be verified.

2. Compounded medications and regulatory status

HealthRx: Offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription and states compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom. It also notes that compounded medication is not FDA-approved and is prepared by a licensed pharmacy for an individual patient with a valid prescription.

Lemonaid Health: The captured evidence notes that when compounded medications are offered they are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Details about whether Lemonaid used compounded products were not verified on the captured page.

3. Clinician review and treatment eligibility

Both: Treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. HealthRx emphasizes that the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is the right fit, or whether neither is, and what you are choosing is the plan it arrives on. Lemonaid Health’s evidence also states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

4. Shipping, cold chain and delivery

HealthRx: Captured evidence lists overnight shipping, cold chain tracked shipping free on every plan, and no shipping charge at checkout. Lemonaid Health: Shipping, fees, membership and cancellation details were not verified on the captured landing page.

5. Plan features and account handling

HealthRx: Notes that nothing else is added to plan pricing — no consultation fee, no shipping charge, and the plan price does not change when dose changes. It also highlights dose adjustments and that your account, dose changes, and support are part of the offering. Lemonaid Health: The captured landing page did not publish comparable feature detail.

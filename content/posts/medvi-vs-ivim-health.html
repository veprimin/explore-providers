Price and promotions compared

MedVi advertises a winter promotional first-month price of $179 including fast, free shipping, but notes the ongoing standalone month rises to $299 and a three-month plan is available at $649. IVIM Health promotes a free month membership with code GIFT75, a no-obligation free consultation, and publishes ongoing pricing around $174/month. Both operate on a direct-pay model and state that compounded medications are cash-pay.

Medication choices and clinical approach

MedVi focuses on compounded semaglutide with clinician review and promises clinically dosed compounded formulations. IVIM Health offers both compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, and highlights a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol intended to personalize titration and optimization. IVIM presents protocol outcomes as a proprietary claim and notes these are not independent head-to-head trial results.

Trust signals, outcomes claims, and regulatory cautions

IVIM Health emphasizes unusually strong third-party validation with 4.9-star ratings on both Google and Trustpilot. IVIM also references internal and published observational studies supporting its protocol, while noting these are not randomized controlled head-to-head trials. MedVi cites self-reported patient data and notes results vary; it also states compounded medications are produced in regulated facilities but are not FDA-approved. Both providers include regulatory notes that compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and may carry different risks than FDA-approved branded products.

Prescribing, access, and support differences

MedVi’s selling points are a fully online intake, a claim of 24/7 certified medical support, and no required clinic visits. IVIM provides a free consultation for new patients and a clinician-guided GLP-1 ID protocol with ongoing dose adjustments. Both require clinician review and prescriptions are at the clinician’s discretion.

Final comparison and how to choose

MedVi vs IVIM Health comes down to priorities: MedVi is centered on a low promotional entry price, no clinic visits, and round-the-clock certified support. IVIM Health is centered on clinician-guided optimization, broader medication options including tirzepatide, a published protocol claim about accelerated outcomes, and strong third-party ratings.

Choose MedVi if you want the lowest advertised first-month promotional price, a simple online process with no clinic visits, and 24/7 certified medical support. Choose IVIM Health if you value verified third-party ratings, a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol, and the option of compounded tirzepatide as well as semaglutide.

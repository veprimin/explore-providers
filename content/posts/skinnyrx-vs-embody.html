1. Price and who pays what

SkinnyRx lists oral semaglutide starting as low as $249/mo and highlights FSA and HSA eligibility as a distinguishing financial feature. Embody advertises a much lower entry price of $69/month for compounded semaglutide, cut from $299, and a $119/month option for tirzepatide. Embody positions its model as flat monthly pricing with no membership and cancel anytime flexibility.

2. Medication format and options

SkinnyRx emphasizes daily oral semaglutide tablets taken under the tongue as an alternative for people who prefer not to use injections. The provider describes clinically supported ingredients and positions tablets as the oral form of the same medication used in GLP-1 injections. Embody offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, noting injections availability in its listings. Both use compounded medications where applicable; the sources note compounded drugs are not FDA approved.

3. Delivery speed and logistics

Both providers advertise fast delivery. SkinnyRx promises free overnight shipping on all orders and sends shipping and tracking information within 2 business days after the prescription is processed. Embody advertises free shipping with delivery in 1 to 2 days after clinician approval. Shipping timelines are presented as part of each provider’s fulfillment process.

4. Clinical review, support, and features

SkinnyRx emphasizes personalized care with licensed providers who tailor treatment, ongoing support, and monthly refill forms for subscription plans. It states patients have unlimited access to its patient care team after prescription. Embody describes telemed visits and a licensed provider review handled online, with 24/7 support access for dosing and side effect questions and no long term commitment.

5. Regulatory and safety notes

Both sources explicitly state that the medications are compounded and that compounded medications are not FDA approved, not reviewed by the FDA for safety effectiveness or quality. SkinnyRx notes partner pharmacies perform third party testing every 3 to 6 months with a plus or minus 10 percent concentration confirmation for each lot. Embody notes its compounded medications use the same active ingredients as branded GLP 1 medications but remain compounded products.

How to choose between SkinnyRx and Embody

Choose Embody if you prioritize the lowest entry price for compounded semaglutide and want a lean, no membership, quick delivery program with online clinician review. Choose SkinnyRx if you prefer daily oral semaglutide tablets, want the option to use FSA or HSA funds, and value overnight shipping plus expanded patient care access.

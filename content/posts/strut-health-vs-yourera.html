How these two providers differ at a glance

Both providers offer telehealth-prescribed GLP-1 options and use compounded medications where noted. The most distinctive differences supported by the source evidence are: 1) oral specialization versus multi-molecule compounded choices, 2) advertised starting prices and required commitments, 3) what each bundle includes, and 4) regulatory status of compounded medications and clinical eligibility rules.

1. Oral specialization and program focus

Strut Health markets specialized oral-focused treatment options with Oral Semaglutide and guided consultations. The clinical team emphasizes oral medication management rather than splitting attention across injection and oral protocols. YourEra provides compounded GLP-1 and GLP-1 plus GIP options, including compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, but does not advertise a focused oral-only specialization.

2. Pricing and commitment differences

Strut Health lists pricing From $199 per Month and highlights Oral GLP-1 from $199/month. YourEra advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/month and tirzepatide from $199/month on the 6-month plan, with per month figures that can be shown as low as $79.20/month when reflecting the 6-month plan. YourEra notes that the lowest advertised rates require a 6-month commitment and that shorter plans cost more per month. Strut Health detail shows a starting price From $199 per Month; no multi-month commitment requirement is supplied in the Strut Health evidence.

3. What is bundled and included

Strut Health emphasizes guided consultations and ongoing progress communication as features, while also listing free shipping and cancel anytime language in landing-page evidence. YourEra explicitly bundles the provider consult, prescription, cold shipping, dose adjustments, and 24/7 support into a flat price and highlights same price at every dose and no membership fee. YourEra also advertises free, discreet delivery in 1 to 2 days from licensed US pharmacies.

4. Medication types and regulatory notes

Strut Health discusses Oral Semaglutide and mentions GLP-1 compounded weight management therapy with Semaglutide, noting that compounded medication is prescribed based on medical necessity and that compounded Semaglutide is prepared by a U.S.-licensed compounding pharmacy pursuant to an individualized prescription. YourEra dispenses compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide prepared by state-licensed US compounding pharmacies pursuant to an individual prescription. Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

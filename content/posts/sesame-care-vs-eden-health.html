1. Pricing model and transparency

Sesame Care presents a marketplace pricing model: subscription care components are advertised “as low as $59/mo” with a star note that it is based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Medication cash-pay examples are labeled with specific amounts and with insurance price ranges, for example semaglutide cash-pay $149 and price with insurance as low as $25. Eden Health publishes a single monthly price by medication: semaglutide $99 and tirzepatide $129, and emphasizes no insurance required and a lifetime price guarantee.

2. Clinician choice versus guided program

Sesame Care is described as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select providers based on specialty, availability, and pricing, offering more control over the care relationship. Eden Health describes a guided GLP-1 program built around you with clinician review and a single monthly price; it notes treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

3. Insurance, membership, and billing differences

Sesame Care states “Insurance accepted for weight loss medications” and that medication costs will vary based on insurance status and pharmacy. Eden Health states “No insurance required” and “No membership fees” while promising a lifetime price guarantee. Both capture regulatory notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that compounded products are not interchangeable.

4. Shipping and added service features

Eden Health specifically lists free expedited shipping as part of its offering. Sesame Care emphasizes nationwide access, flexible scheduling, and a large clinician network but captured evidence does not list free expedited shipping or a lifetime price guarantee.

How these differences affect your choice

Sesame Care vs Eden Health comes down to choice and pricing structure. If you value browsing many clinicians, variable insurance pricing, and flexible scheduling, Sesame Care is positioned as a marketplace for that model. If you prefer a single monthly price by medication with no insurance needed, free expedited shipping, and a lifetime price guarantee, Eden Health presents that option.

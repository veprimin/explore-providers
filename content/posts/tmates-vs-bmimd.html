What differs most between TMates and bmiMD

This comparison focuses on five distinctive differences supported by the sources: medication offered, pricing and billing options, clinician access, shipping and commitments, and regulatory status. The primary keyword TMates vs bmiMD appears here and is used throughout.

Medication offered: semaglutide versus tirzepatide

TMates offers compounded semaglutide as its featured GLP 1 option. bmiMD offers compounded tirzepatide, described as a compounded GLP 1 GIP product. These are different active medications according to each provider description. Each provider states their medication is compounded and not FDA approved as a finished product.

Pricing and plan structure

TMates advertises a flat $199/month rate at every dose, with examples listed for multi month bundles and a direct pay model. bmiMD lists a month to month price of $179 and lower monthly equivalents for longer commitments down to $139/month for a 12 month plan. bmiMD highlights lower long term monthly equivalent rates but notes the lowest price requires a 12 month commitment.

Clinician access and medical oversight

Both providers emphasize clinician oversight, but the emphasis differs. TMates highlights 24/7 access to U.S. based doctors across all 50 states and virtual check in appointments. bmiMD states 24/7 clinician access as well and notes clinician review and prescription availability is subject to medical appropriateness.

Shipping, refunds, and commitments

TMates lists free shipping, same price at all doses, and cancel anytime with a direct pay model. bmiMD includes free subscription shipping on every order and a claim of 100 percent refundable if not eligible for the program, while also noting the lowest monthly price requires longer prepaid commitments. Both indicate online refills and delivery.

Regulatory note and formulation

Both sources include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. TMates notes medications are formulated in U.S. based facilities. bmiMD states medications are prepared in state licensed pharmacies and independently verified in FDA and DEA registered labs per its captured landing page language.

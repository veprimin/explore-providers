Pricing and promotional structure

BetterMe Rx lists compounded GLP 1 treatment from $145/month on a 12 month plan with monthly equivalents for semaglutide injection $205 or as low as $145 on a 12 month plan; tirzepatide injection $275 or as low as $215 on a 12 month plan; oral semaglutide $250 or as low as $185; oral tirzepatide $265 or as low as $215. Measured advertises promotional compounded semaglutide from $89/month and compounded tirzepatide $189/month, noting promotional pricing may change.

Medication formats and availability

BetterMe Rx explicitly offers both injectable and oral compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options, including an uncommon needle free daily tirzepatide tablet at parity pricing with injections according to the source. Measured pairs compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide but the source emphasizes same day Rx decisions and does not list oral versus injectable distinctions beyond the two medications.

Clinical review, support, and extras

Both providers require a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness. BetterMe Rx includes a provider review and lists medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies and free 1 2 day shipping as included. Measured highlights same day prescription decisions, unlimited messaging, 24 7 Care Team access, and dietitian support, and cites a 4.8 star Trustpilot rating from over 51,000 users in the supplied content.

Shipping, billing terms and cancellation

BetterMe Rx advertises free 1 2 day delivery and notes longer 3, 6, or 12 month supplies are billed according to the selected plan with cancel anytime language alongside prepaid longer term plans. Measured advertises free two day shipping on every order and also states cancel anytime and no insurance required. Measured also notes promotional pricing may change and recommends confirming eligibility, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

Safety and regulatory note

Both sources include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. BetterMe Rx states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Measured states Rx required and that all prescriptions are issued at provider discretion.

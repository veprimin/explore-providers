Price and payment structure compared

TrimRx advertises compounded GLP-1 starting at $149/month and GLP-1+GIP from $249/month with Lifetime Price Protection that “locks in today’s price for life” and no membership fee. HealthRx advertises a lower headline price: $99/month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan but notes the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan and shows total prepaid amounts ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal). HealthRx also states plan price does not change when your dose changes.

Medication selection and clinical review

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies. TrimRx offers both compounded GLP-1 and compounded GLP-1+GIP and says providers may alternatively write a prescription for branded medications, although TrimRx does not sell or ship branded drugs. HealthRx emphasizes that the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is right, or whether neither is, and that the plan determines how the medication arrives.

Shipping and logistics

TrimRx markets free shipping that arrives in 1–2 days. HealthRx highlights overnight cold chain shipping that is tracked and free on every plan, indicating a focus on temperature control for compounded products. Both sources state compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies, and both include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Price protections, terms, and enrollment trade offs

TrimRx advertises Lifetime Price Protection and no monthly membership with the ability to cancel anytime. HealthRx’s lowest monthly price is tied to a longer prepaid commitment and notes pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change. HealthRx also notes there is no consultation fee, no shipping charge, and nothing else is added on its stated plans.

Regulatory and safety notes

Both providers include the same regulatory qualifier: compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. TrimRx specifically states its compounded drugs use the same active ingredients as branded products but are not FDA-approved finished products. HealthRx likewise states compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and is not FDA-approved.

Final thoughts on TrimRx vs HealthRx

TrimRx vs HealthRx boils down to whether you prefer an all-in program with a lifetime price lock and 1–2 day free shipping or a lower advertised monthly rate that requires a prepaid plan and offers overnight cold chain shipping with clinician-determined medication selection. Both use compounded GLP-1 or tirzepatide prepared by licensed pharmacies and both note compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Choose TrimRx if you want Lifetime Price Protection, an advertised all-in monthly price from $149 for compounded GLP-1, free 1–2 day shipping, and a no membership cancel-anytime model.

Choose HealthRx if you prefer the lowest advertised monthly price via a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan starting at $99/month, overnight cold chain shipping, and a clinician-driven decision about whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is appropriate.

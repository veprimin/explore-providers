Shed vs BetterMe Rx: the biggest differences

1) Medication types and regulatory notes

Shed offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded alternatives. BetterMe Rx offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and oral formats. Both providers note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a provider’s prescription. Treatment with either provider is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

2) Coaching and care model

Shed integrates continuous coaching into its treatment plans rather than issuing a prescription only. BetterMe Rx includes a provider review but does not advertise integrated continuous coaching; its model focuses on medication, provider review, compounding, supplies and shipping. For patients who want behavioral support alongside medication, Shed positions coaching as a core differentiator.

3) Pricing structure and starting prices

BetterMe Rx advertises compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan and lists monthly prices that can be higher unless you choose longer prepaid terms. Shed lists compounded semaglutide injections starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month and offers 10% off your first month. Shed does not publish a single standard price for all patients and provides a personalized quote after intake.

4) Formats, shipping and fees

BetterMe Rx offers injectable and oral compounded GLP-1 options and includes medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies and free 1–2 day shipping with no membership fee. Shed emphasizes name-brand plus compounded injections and bundles coaching into the treatment cost; Shed mentions a membership option for deeper services and a limited-time 10% off first month.

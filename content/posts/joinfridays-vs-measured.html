JoinFridays vs Measured: both offer compounded GLP-1 options, but they differ on starting price, shipping and support. Early verdict: Measured typically has a lower promotional entry price and free two-day shipping, while JoinFridays emphasizes clinician-guided care, flexible cancellation, and a competitive $117/month starting tier.

How they compare at a glance

This comparison focuses on the clearest differences supported by the source: starting price, available compounded medications, shipping and prescription speed, patient support and outcomes claims, and cancellation or membership terms. The primary keyword JoinFridays vs Measured appears throughout to reflect direct comparison intent.

Direct price and promo comparison

Measured advertises compounded semaglutide from $89/month and compounded tirzepatide from $189/month as promotional pricing. JoinFridays lists a starting price of $117/month and notes multi-month plans reduce effective monthly cost; a standalone one-month plan is $249 and twelve months averages $150/month. Promotional pricing on both platforms may change and specific discounts are subject to availability.

Which compounded GLP-1s each platform offers

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. JoinFridays highlights both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide on one platform with messaging that tirzepatide is “Most Effective” and semaglutide “Most Affordable.” Measured pairs the same two compounds with dietitian support and unlimited messaging. Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Shipping, prescription speed, and support

Measured promotes same-day prescription decisions and free two-day shipping on every order, plus unlimited messaging, 24/7 care team access, and dietitian support. JoinFridays emphasizes personalized, clinician-guided care and active promo codes but does not claim same-day Rx decisions or guaranteed two-day shipping in the captured evidence.

Outcomes claims and evidence

Measured includes an internal analysis claim that members reporting weight data averaged a 31 lb loss at six months, with the qualifier that individual results vary and are not guaranteed and that internal outcome claims are not independent evidence. JoinFridays notes that tirzepatide is associated with higher average weight loss (roughly 15-22%) than semaglutide (roughly 10-15%) as a referenced comparison point in its FAQ. These are provider statements and include qualifiers about variability and clinical appropriateness.

Cancellation, membership and plan structure

JoinFridays markets no long-term contracts, no membership fees, and the ability to cancel anytime, positioning flexible cancellation as a distinguishing feature. Measured also states cancel anytime and no insurance required. JoinFridays additionally highlights multi-month plans that reduce effective monthly cost, while Measured emphasizes promotional entry pricing and recurring free two-day shipping.

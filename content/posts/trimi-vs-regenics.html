How Trimi and Regenics differ at a glance

The primary differences between Trimi and Regenics are pricing structure, clinician oversight, shipping and fulfillment details, and additional program features. Both offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by U.S. compounding pharmacies and both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products.

Pricing structure: flat versus promotional

Trimi lists semaglutide from $99/month on the annual plan and tirzepatide at $125/month, with messaging that the price is the same at every dose. Regenics lists semaglutide at $139 first month (renewing at $199/month) and tirzepatide at $189 first month (renewing at $279/month). Trimi frames its model as flat pricing that never rises as dose increases. Regenics makes clear that its lower displayed prices apply to the first month and renew at higher rates unless otherwise stated at checkout.

Clinician oversight and medical model

Trimi conveys that prescriptions and medical oversight are provided by U.S.-licensed clinicians through Arora Health, a network led by a board-certified physician licensed in all 50 states. Regenics states that care is delivered by licensed nurse practitioners who review intake before prescriptions and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Shipping, pharmacies, and quality statements

Trimi promotes free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and names state-licensed 503A pharmacies including VialsRx and GreenwichRx, noting certificates of analysis are available on request. Regenics states free shipping with medication compounded in the USA and that its pharmacy network is LegitScript certified. Both include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Who should pick which provider

Trimi vs Regenics centers on steady cost and shipping versus promotional pricing and peptide options. Trimi emphasizes flat monthly costs that do not change with dose, free overnight temperature-controlled shipping, named compounding pharmacies, physician-led oversight via Arora Health, and a 6-month money-back promise. Regenics emphasizes lower first-month prices that renew higher, nurse-practitioner-led telehealth care, a Sermorelin peptide line, LegitScript-certified pharmacies, and FSA/HSA eligibility.

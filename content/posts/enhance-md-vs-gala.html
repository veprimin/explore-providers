Enhance MD vs Gala quickly: Enhance MD emphasizes very low introductory pricing and flat-rate dosing, while Gala promotes a flat all-inclusive plan with provider check-ins and a $220 promotion. Early verdict: for the lowest first-month entry cost choose Enhance MD; for included provider visit, shipping and a single bundled price consider Gala.

Head-to-head summary: Pricing and what is shown up front

Both platforms sell compounded GLP-1s and state treatment is subject to clinician review. Enhance MD lists semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month and emphasizes flat-rate pricing that stays the same at every dose. Gala advertises one flat all-inclusive price covering provider visit, medication, shipping and ongoing care and a $220 promotion, but the base dollar price was not exposed in readable page text.

Distinctive difference 1: First-month entry cost

Enhance MD supplies explicit first-month starting prices: semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99. Gala does not expose a clear base price in the captured text and instead highlights a $220 discount and a flat all-inclusive offering. If first-month sticker price is decisive, Enhance MD provides the clearest low-cost figures.

Distinctive difference 2: What the flat price covers

Enhance MD describes flat-rate pricing that stays the same at every dose and warns ongoing flat-rate pricing may be higher after introductory month. Gala claims one flat price at every dose and advertises that the flat all-inclusive price covers the provider visit, prescription, medication, shipping and ongoing care. Gala also emphasizes provider check-ins and home delivery in the captured evidence.

Distinctive difference 3: Service features and logistics

Gala explicitly lists provider visit and prescription included, provider check-ins, and free two-day shipping. Enhance MD highlights clinician-directed care, a platform offering both semaglutide and tirzepatide, and a stated clinical volume of more than 10,000 patients treated. Both operate on a direct-pay model in the captured evidence and both offer compounded medications.

Final comparison and recommendation

Enhance MD vs Gala centers on two main tradeoffs in the captured evidence: explicit low first-month prices and a published flat-rate approach at Enhance MD versus Gala’s bundled all-inclusive plan with provider visit, check-ins and free two-day shipping but no clear base price shown in the readable capture.

Pricing and membership differences

Both providers emphasize predictable pricing but present it differently. Trimi markets compounded semaglutide from $99/mo on an annual plan with flat pricing that does not rise as your dose increases and a stated price of tirzepatide at $125/month. CoreAgeRx lists semaglutide at $79/month and tirzepatide at $129/month and emphasizes no membership fee and the same price every month. Trimi also presents a 6 month money back promise under certain terms. CoreAgeRx highlights no billing surprises and that prices do not increase by dose according to captured claims.

Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Trimi explicitly states its semaglutide and tirzepatide are compounded medications prepared by state-licensed 503A pharmacies and includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. CoreAgeRx notes that medication specific approval status must be confirmed and also states that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved. If compounded-medication status or FDA approval is important to you, these qualifications are present in both providers’ captured information.

Shipping, convenience, and visit process

Shipping and visit details differ in what was captured. Trimi states free overnight, temperature controlled shipping and that medications are prescribed by US licensed clinicians and shipped to your door. CoreAgeRx captured details include a 3 minute online visit and a claim of no membership fee with same monthly price, but shipping timing was not fully verified on the captured landing page. CoreAgeRx notes treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Clinical oversight and guarantees

Trimi names a clinical oversight network, Arora Health, led by a board certified physician, and states that plans are reviewed and prescribed by licensed providers across all 50 states. Trimi also references a 6 month refund option if you do not lose weight and you meet their terms. CoreAgeRx captures emphasize clinician review and a short online visit but captured content did not show a comparable refund promise. Both providers include the qualifier that eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability should be confirmed before enrollment.

How to decide: the most distinctive differences

Focus on three practical distinctions when choosing between Trimi vs CoreAgeRx. First, starting price and plan structure: Trimi lists compounded semaglutide starting at $99/mo on annual plans with a same price at every dose promise while CoreAgeRx lists semaglutide at $79/mo and markets no membership fee and same monthly pricing. Second, compounded pharmacy and regulatory statements: Trimi documents specific 503A compounding pharmacies and the standard regulatory note; CoreAgeRx also flags approval status must be confirmed. Third, shipping and refund signals: Trimi claims free overnight, temperature controlled shipping and a 6 month money back promise subject to terms; CoreAgeRx highlights the quick online visit and stable monthly billing but captured landing page did not verify shipping timing or a money back promise.

Strut Health vs HealthRx: both offer clinician-prescribed compounded GLP-1 options, but they differ in oral focus, price structure, and shipping and plan models. Quick verdict: choose Strut Health if you want an oral-specialist program and guided consults; choose HealthRx if you prioritize a lower advertised entry price and overnight cold shipping.

How Strut Health vs HealthRx differ at a glance

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications prepared per prescription and note that compounded medications are not FDA approved. The most distinctive differences supported by the source are: specialization in oral therapy, pricing and plan structure, medication options and clinician review, shipping and storage, and program features like ongoing communication or dose policies.

1. Oral specialization and treatment focus

Strut Health is presented as an oral specialization platform with an emphasis on oral-based program options and guided consultations. HealthRx offers semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription and states that the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is appropriate, or whether neither is.

2. Pricing and plan structure

Strut Health lists oral GLP-1 from $199 per month as a starting price. HealthRx advertises plans from $99 per month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan with a total of $1,163 today and $1,188 at renewal, and tirzepatide plans starting at $179 per month. HealthRx notes the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan.

3. Medication options and clinician review

Strut Health highlights oral Semaglutide and says compounded medication is prescribed based on medical necessity and only available for patients that fail or cannot use commercially available GLP-1 medications, with medical necessity required by a licensed physician. HealthRx emphasizes that you are not picking a medication off a menu and that a clinician decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is the right fit, or whether neither is, so what you choose is the plan the medication arrives on.

4. Shipping and storage

Strut Health notes free shipping in its landing-page evidence. HealthRx specifies overnight cold shipping, cold chain tracked and free on every plan, and highlights that compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom.

5. Program features and ongoing care

Strut Health promotes guided consultations and ongoing progress communication with a clinical team focused on oral medication management. HealthRx highlights dose adjustments where plan price does not change when your dose does, and that there is no consultation fee or shipping charge on their plans as presented.

Conclusion: Strut Health vs HealthRx

Strut Health vs HealthRx come down to oral specialization and plan mechanics. Strut Health focuses on oral Semaglutide with guided consults and lists pricing from $199 per month. HealthRx advertises lower entry pricing from $99 per month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan, offers clinician-decided semaglutide or tirzepatide, and highlights overnight cold shipping and dose stability within plans. Both note compounded medications are not FDA approved.

Choose Strut Health if you want an oral-specialist program with guided consults and an emphasis on oral Semaglutide and ongoing progress communication.

SkinnyRx vs BreezeMeds centers on format, pricing transparency, and shipping. Quick verdict: SkinnyRx emphasizes oral semaglutide tablets with FSA/HSA eligibility and free overnight shipping, while BreezeMeds emphasizes a wider mix of injectable and oral GLP-1 options with lower starting injectable prices but less clarity on some offers.

How they differ: medication format and clinical approach

Both providers offer clinician-reviewed treatment, but the two highlight different formats. SkinnyRx markets daily oral semaglutide tablets taken under the tongue as an alternative to injections and positions those tablets as compounded where prescribed after an online consultation with a healthcare provider. BreezeMeds displays both injectable and oral GLP-1 options, specifically listing semaglutide and tirzepatide in injection form and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide among displayed options. For both, treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Pricing and payment features compared

Price evidence in the sources shows distinct starting points. SkinnyRx is presented as “As low as $249/mo” for compounded semaglutide tablets and mentions payment flexibility with Affirm Buy Now Pay Later plus FSA/HSA eligibility. BreezeMeds lists multiple starting figures: semaglutide injection from $199/mo, tirzepatide injection from $299, and oral options displayed from $399, with a separate $169 figure shown without a clear product association. The sources do not supply full billing terms, membership fees, or insurance acceptance details for either provider.

Delivery, testing, and regulatory notes

SkinnyRx emphasizes free overnight shipping on all orders and states partner pharmacies perform third-party testing through FDA- and DEA-compliant labs every 3 to 6 months, confirming concentration within plus or minus 10 percent for compounded lots. SkinnyRx also explicitly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. BreezeMeds highlights discreet home delivery and states that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Both require clinician review and prescription when appropriate.

Support, enrollment, and transparency

SkinnyRx presents ongoing patient access to a care team, monthly refill forms for subscription continuity, and a cancellation policy that allows a full refund if canceled before a licensed provider completes the prescription review. Once fulfillment begins, SkinnyRx states orders become non-refundable. BreezeMeds promotes online intake and a personalized treatment plan but the captured evidence notes that fees, billing commitments, and state availability were not fully visible and recommends confirming eligibility, total cost, renewal terms, and state availability before enrollment.

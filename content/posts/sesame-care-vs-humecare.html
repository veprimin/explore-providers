Sesame Care vs HumeCare+: core differences at a glance

Both services offer GLP-1 weight-loss options, but their approaches differ in three main areas: care model and clinician choice, what the monthly price includes, and protocol features like supplements, scans, and guarantees. Below I compare each area using only the supplied facts.

1. Care model and clinician access

Sesame Care operates as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing. It emphasizes a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states. HumeCare+ offers continuous physician optimization, unlimited clinician access with a U.S. care team available 24/7, and a dedicated app for tracking and logging. Sesame offers choice among providers; HumeCare+ offers bundled, continuous clinician support and app integration.

2. Price structure and what is included

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs, and messaging for as low as $59/mo* and cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo, with medication costs not included in subscription and varying by insurance and pharmacy. It also notes insurance accepted for weight loss medications and provides price examples for semaglutide and tirzepatide with and without insurance, including prices listed as low as $25 with insurance for some drugs and cash-pay pricing like $149 or $299/mo depending on formulation.

HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/mo and a tirzepatide protocol from $299/mo that includes glycine, B12, and B6 built into the formulation, plus free next-day delivery and 24/7 clinical support. HumeCare+ pricing as presented is cash-pay and described as reflecting a promotional discount that may change.

3. Protocol features, supplements, scans, and guarantees

HumeCare+ positions its program as a bundled protocol: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide combined with glycine, vitamin B12, and B6 for muscle support and side-effect relief, monthly body-composition scans that track lean mass versus fat, an app, and a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months with terms to verify. HumeCare+ also states compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated for safety, quality, or efficacy.

Sesame Care focuses on clinician choice and flexible appointments and does not supply evidence in the provided material that it bundles supplemental ingredients or body-composition scans. Sesame references milestone-based results from a study of injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg when paired with lifestyle changes, and it notes medication-specific approval status must be confirmed.

4. Outcomes messaging and evidence cited

Sesame Care references milestone results based on a study of injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg in non-diabetic adults with obesity or overweight when paired with healthy lifestyle changes, and it states results may vary. HumeCare+ cites internal, company-reported figures and an internal analysis of 6,846 members for 10 month results, while also noting testimonials are not a guarantee of specific results. HumeCare+ also references external clinical literature in support of GLP-1 research but presents headline figures as internal results.

5. Safety, compounded products, and regulation

Both sources include regulatory cautions. Sesame Care notes medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. HumeCare+ clearly states compounded medications are not evaluated by the FDA for safety, quality, or efficacy and emphasizes that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Which to choose: final guidance on Sesame Care vs HumeCare+

Choose Sesame Care if you want broad telehealth choice, flexible scheduling, and the ability to browse and select clinicians with subscription services starting as low as $59/mo* and cash-pay GLP-1 meds from $149/mo, with insurance accepted for weight loss medications.

Choose HumeCare+ if you want a bundled protocol that includes physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with glycine and vitamins B6 and B12, monthly body-composition scans, an app for tracking, 24/7 clinician access, free next-day delivery, and a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months (terms apply). Note that HumeCare+ offers compounded medications that are not FDA-evaluated for safety, quality, or efficacy and its pricing is presented as cash-pay promotional figures.

Both providers present different value propositions. Review the regulatory notes and pricing qualifiers in the source material and confirm medication-specific approval status where relevant before enrolling. Sesame Care vs HumeCare+ should be decided based on whether clinician choice and flexible entry pricing or a bundled protocol with supplements, scans, and a guarantee matter more to you.

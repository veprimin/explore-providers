Quick verdict: HumeCare+ focuses on a bundled GLP-1 protocol with muscle-preserving additives, body-composition scans, and a 10%-or-money-back promise, while Sprout Health competes on lightning-quick approval and a $50 first-month discount. If you want ongoing clinical scaffolding and tracking choose HumeCare+. If you want the fastest, low-friction start choose Sprout Health.

How pricing and discounts compare

Both providers use direct-pay pricing. HumeCare+ lists compounded semaglutide starting at $199 per month and a tirzepatide protocol at $299 per month. Sprout Health advertises $50 off the first month, bringing the initial cost to $199, with ongoing pricing at $249 per month after the discount.

Three distinctive differences that matter

1. Protocol support and bundled ingredients

HumeCare+ sells a bundled protocol: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide combined with glycine, vitamin B12, and B6 to address muscle support and common side effects. Sprout Health is positioned as a fast, no-frills GLP-1 provider; its materials emphasize quick approval and outcome framing but do not describe added muscle-preserving ingredients.

2. Body-composition tracking and clinician access

HumeCare+ includes monthly body composition scans to track fat versus lean mass, a dedicated app for tracking, and unlimited clinician access with a U.S. care team available 24/7. Sprout Health emphasizes a lightning-quick approval process and fast delivery but captured evidence does not show body-composition scans or 24/7 clinician access.

3. Outcome framing and guarantees

HumeCare+ advertises a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months, with eligibility and terms referenced on its landing page. Sprout Health frames outcomes as 10 to 26 percent body weight loss over 12 months but does not offer a formal outcome guarantee in the captured materials.

Side notes on medication type and regulatory status

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medication under clinician review. HumeCare+ explicitly states its compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated for safety, quality, or efficacy. Sprout Health notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Conclusion and recommendation

HumeCare+ vs Sprout Health breaks down to support and tracking versus speed and a first-month discount. HumeCare+ pairs GLP-1 medication with glycine, B12, and B6, includes monthly body-composition scans, and advertises a 10%-or-money-back promise over six months. Sprout Health offers a quick approval path and a $50 first-month discount, with ongoing pricing of $249 per month and outcome framing of 10 to 26 percent weight loss over 12 months.

Choose HumeCare+ if you want bundled muscle-support ingredients, monthly body-composition scans, continuous clinician access, and an advertised 10%-or-money-back promise. Choose Sprout Health if you prioritize lightning-quick approval and a lower first-month cost with a simple, fast-entry experience.

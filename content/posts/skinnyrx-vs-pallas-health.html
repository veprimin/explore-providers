Considering SkinnyRx vs Pallas Health? Quick verdict: SkinnyRx centers on daily oral compounded semaglutide tablets with FSA/HSA eligibility and free overnight shipping, while Pallas Health offers a clinician choice between FDA-approved brand pens and compounded GLP-1s with a lower first-month compounded price and plan-based care included.

How these providers differ overall

This SkinnyRx vs Pallas Health comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: medication format and options, pricing and billing cadence, clinician scope and included care, shipping and fulfillment, and regulatory and testing notes. Each section compares the two directly using only the supplied facts.

1. Medication format and options

SkinnyRx offers daily oral semaglutide tablets, described as an oral form of the same medication used in GLP-1 injections and taken daily under the tongue. Pallas Health offers both FDA-approved brand-name GLP-1s such as Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound and Mounjaro and clinician-personalized compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide. In short, SkinnyRx specializes in oral compounded semaglutide tablets, while Pallas provides a choice between brand pens and compounded preparations chosen by a clinician.

2. Pricing and payment structure

SkinnyRx lists a starting price of as low as $249 per month for compounded semaglutide tablets and mentions splitting payments with Affirm as an option. Pallas Health lists compounded semaglutide at $139 for the first month then $597 every 12 weeks, which averages to $199 per month. Pallas also states plan prices include provider care with no separate membership fee. Both providers note that pricing and plan details are subject to clinician review and intake.

3. Clinician involvement and included care

SkinnyRx requires a prescription after an online consultation with a healthcare provider and emphasizes personalized care, ongoing support, and dose adjustments by licensed providers. Pallas Health operates as a 100 percent online medical weight-loss program where a US-licensed clinician reviews health history and personalizes whether a brand or compounded medication is appropriate. Pallas explicitly includes clinician review, ongoing check-ins, and messaging inside the plan price with no membership fee.

4. Shipping, fulfillment, and testing

Both providers offer expedited or free shipping. SkinnyRx advertises free overnight shipping on all orders and states medications are processed with shipping and tracking information within two business days after prescription completion. SkinnyRx also says partner pharmacies perform third-party testing every three to six months confirming concentration within plus or minus 10 percent for compounded lots. Pallas Health offers free expedited and discreet shipping on every plan and states compounded medications are prepared by US-licensed compounding pharmacies; Pallas does not manufacture or compound medication itself.

5. Regulatory disclosures and limits

Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. SkinnyRx specifically states compounded semaglutide has not been approved by the FDA for weight loss and that physicians may prescribe compounded medications as needed. Pallas notes compounded medications are prepared by US-licensed compounding pharmacies and that eligibility is determined by a US-licensed clinician.

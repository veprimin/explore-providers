MedVi vs Enhance MD is a comparison of two telehealth platforms that prescribe compounded GLP-1s. Quick verdict: if you prioritize the lowest entry price and choice of both semaglutide and tirzepatide, Enhance MD is the cheaper, multi-compound option; if you want a simple, no-clinic process and 24/7 certified medical support with a promotional semaglutide price, MedVi is the clearer fit.

Head-to-head: pricing, compounds, and care model

Both platforms use clinician-directed telehealth and offer compounded medications that are not FDA-approved finished products. They differ most notably on starting price, compound availability, pricing model during titration, and support style. Below we compare these distinctive differences directly using only the supplied facts.

1. Starting price and promotional offers

MedVi advertises a winter promotional first-month price of $179 for semaglutide that includes fast, free shipping. Enhance MD advertises a lower first-month rate for semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99. Both providers present first-month introductory prices; Enhance MD’s semaglutide first month is the lowest first-month semaglutide price cited.

2. Compound options: semaglutide only vs semaglutide plus tirzepatide

MedVi offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only. Enhance MD offers both semaglutide and tirzepatide, with stated first-month starting prices of $49 and $99 respectively. If access to tirzepatide on the same platform is important, Enhance MD supplies that option.

3. Pricing behavior and ongoing costs

MedVi notes a promotional-first-month price of $179 but states ongoing standalone months can be $299 and a three-month plan is available at $649. Enhance MD emphasizes flat-rate pricing that stays the same at every dose with no fee escalation during titration. The evidence shows MedVi’s ongoing monthly cost rises after the promo while Enhance MD promotes predictable same-price-at-every-dose billing once ongoing rates apply.

4. Care model and support

MedVi highlights a simple online process requiring no clinic visits plus 24/7 support from certified medical professionals. Enhance MD presents clinician-directed care and notes it has treated 10,000+ patients, with metabolic lab testing every 6 months mentioned in landing page details. Both operate via telehealth clinician review and direct-pay models.

5. Regulatory and product notes

Both providers state their compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. MedVi includes additional regulatory qualifiers about clinician discretion and pharmacy partnerships; Enhance MD reiterates the compounded status and clinician medical appropriateness.

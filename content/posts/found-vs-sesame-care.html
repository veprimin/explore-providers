Care model and personalization compared

Found vs Sesame Care shows divergent approaches. Found centers care on a biology-first model that builds a personalized program around hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavior. That is presented as a medically supervised program tailored to an individual metabolic profile. Sesame Care uses a marketplace model where patients choose clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing, giving more control over who delivers care.

Pricing, insurance and payment structure

On price, Found advertises you can get started for as low as $39 and highlights affordable monthly plans and assistance with insurance paperwork. Found states most insurance is now accepted and lists programs like the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program for eligible Part D members. Sesame Care lists subscription services as low as $59/mo* based on a 12-month commitment and separates subscription fees from medication costs, which vary by insurance and pharmacy. Both providers note insurance affects final medication costs.

Medication options and regulatory notes

Found offers branded GLP-1s and compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, and explicitly states compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy and are not available in all states. Found also states certain medications are FDA-approved for conditions other than weight and that Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility. Sesame Care lists oral and injectable branded GLP-1 options including tirzepatide and the Wegovy pill, and notes medication costs are not included in subscription prices and will vary based on insurance. Sesame Care also emphasizes medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Clinician access, scheduling and experience variability

Sesame Care highlights a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states, and frames its model as letting patients choose their clinician. Found focuses on a clinician-led personalized program with provider review, clinical eligibility, and a holistic whole-body approach. The evidence notes Sesame Care experience can vary by clinician and Found notes results vary by individual.

How quickly results and commitments are described

Found states you can begin seeing and feeling results as soon as month 1 but cautions results vary by individual. Sesame Care lists milestones “in 3 months, in 6 months, in 12 months” based on a study of injectable semaglutide paired with lifestyle changes and notes results may vary.

Final thoughts on Found vs Sesame Care

Both platforms accept insurance in many cases and offer access to GLP-1 medications, but they target different needs. Found is positioned around a medically supervised, biology-first program with affordable monthly plans starting at $39 and explicit handling of insurance paperwork. Sesame Care is positioned as a marketplace with clinician choice, flexible appointments, and subscription care from $59/mo with medication costs separate.

2. Clinical model and services

Found positions a “biology-first” personalized medically supervised weight loss program that builds plans around hormones, metabolism, sleep, and behavior. SnagRx focuses on clinician-reviewed prescriptions delivered without coaching or nutrition programming; SnagRx includes prescription and telemed visits but states “No coaching or nutrition programming of any kind.” Found emphasizes a holistic whole-body approach and personalized plans; SnagRx emphasizes clinician review and telehealth delivery with OpenLoop Health.

3. Medication types and compounded status

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1s per their materials. Found lists Tirzepatide and Semaglutide and explicitly notes “Compounded” for some medications and the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. SnagRx offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and states its compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products while naming US 503A partner pharmacies.

4. Insurance, payment, and guarantees

Found states “Most insurance now accepted,” that they handle insurance paperwork, and references Medicare programs and varying coverage and copays. Found also provides specific promotional and membership pricing language captured. SnagRx is direct-pay with “No insurance” and “No monthly membership” language; it provides full refunds if a prescription is declined and advertises a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms that apply. The sources show Found emphasizes insurance acceptance and assistance while SnagRx emphasizes all-in direct-pay pricing and refund terms.

5. Shipping, pharmacies, and timing

SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies, states next-day UPS/FedEx delivery and temperature-controlled packaging, and says orders ship next day after approval. Found references medication offerings and plan logistics but the captured evidence focuses on insurance handling and membership pricing rather than shipping timelines. SnagRx explicitly claims next-day shipping and named pharmacies; Found highlights insurance handling and pricing options.

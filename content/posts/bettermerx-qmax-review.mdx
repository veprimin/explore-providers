QMAX is a compounded prescription erectile dysfunction treatment sold by
BetterMe Rx through telehealth. It is a liquid you hold under the tongue, and it
puts four actives in one dose: three PDE5 inhibitors and apomorphine.

What sets it apart from most compounded ED products is disclosure. BetterMe Rx
publishes the strength of every ingredient and a full price list, including
one-time packs. This review covers what is in the formula, what each plan
actually costs per dose, the billing terms worth reading first, and where QMAX
fits against the alternatives.

## Quick take

You will also see it written *Q MAX*, *Q-MAX*, *BetterMeRx QMAX* or *Better Me
Rx QMAX*. All refer to the same product.

**Best for:** men who use ED treatment often enough to benefit from the larger
plans, and who want to know exactly what they are taking before they pay.

**Main trade-offs:** the lowest price per dose needs $449 up front on a plan
that renews every three months, the smallest plan is the most expensive way to
buy it, and orders cannot be refunded once they are sent for fulfilment. It is
also compounded, so the four-ingredient combination is not FDA-approved.

The three PDE5 inhibitors improve blood flow in response to arousal. Each is
FDA-approved for ED on its own; the
[FDA label for sildenafil](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/020895s039lbl.pdf)
sets out its approved use and warnings. Apomorphine works centrally rather than
on circulation, which is the reason compounders add it. No clinical trial has
tested this particular four-ingredient combination, and more ingredients do not
automatically mean a better result for any one person.

Apomorphine is a common choice for the brain-side ingredient in this category:
[BraveRx](/braverx-reviews-4-in-1-ed-medication-formula-with-apomorphine/) and
[Embody RODEO](/embody-rodeo-reviews-top-rated-4-in-1-male-health-quadruple-ed-meds/)
use it too, while
[MEDVi QUAD](/medvi-quad-ed-review-pricing-how-it-works-pros-cons/) takes a
different approach.

### Onset and duration

BetterMe Rx says QMAX may begin working in about 10 minutes for some patients,
and advertises a window of up to 36 hours. Both are the company's claims, not
trial results for this formula. The long window comes from the tadalafil: it is
a period in which you can respond to arousal, not a continuous erection.

Holding the liquid under the tongue is meant to speed things up against a
swallowed sildenafil tablet, which is usually quoted at 30 to 60 minutes and can
be slowed by a heavy meal. BetterMe Rx has not published how much of each
ingredient is absorbed this way.

## Getting a prescription

1. **Online intake.** A short health questionnaire on BetterMe Rx's site,
   covering your history, current medications and cardiovascular risk, then a
   plan choice.
2. **Provider review.** A licensed provider decides whether QMAX is appropriate
   and sets the prescription. BetterMe Rx says you are not charged unless you
   are approved.
3. **Delivery.** Free discreet shipping, dispatched within 24 hours and
   typically arriving in about three days.

There is no in-person visit and no insurance billing.

### Billing terms to read first

BetterMe Rx's terms say recurring plans renew automatically until cancelled, and
that an order cannot be cancelled or refunded once it has been submitted for
fulfilment, except where the law requires. That makes the plan choice matter
more than usual. If you have never taken a combination ED product, a one-time
pack avoids committing to three months of something you have not tried.

## Is QMAX legitimate?

Yes, in the sense that matters: it is a prescription product, a licensed
provider must approve you before anything is dispensed, and BetterMe Rx states
plainly that it is compounded and not FDA-approved.

The caution is about compounding, not legitimacy. The
[FDA does not approve compounded drugs](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/guidance-compliance-regulatory-information/human-drug-compounding)
or verify their safety, effectiveness or quality, so there is far less published
data on this combination than on a branded single-ingredient tablet. The star
rating shown on BetterMe Rx's own page is published by the company; treat it as
marketing rather than evidence.

If that trade-off concerns you, an FDA-approved generic from
[Ro](/ro-ed-treatment-review-trusted-reliable-15-off-first-order/) or
[Hims](/hims-ed-review-popular-affordable-ed-treatment-under-2/) is the more
conservative choice.

## Safety

- PDE5 inhibitors must never be combined with **nitrates or riociguat**. The
  combination can cause a dangerous drop in blood pressure. The intake screens
  for these.
- Heart, blood pressure, liver and kidney conditions all need a clinician's
  review before starting.
- Common PDE5 side effects include headache, flushing, nasal congestion and
  indigestion. Apomorphine can cause nausea and dizziness.
- Alcohol lowers blood pressure and can add to both effects. Ask the prescriber
  before drinking with it.
- Seek urgent care for chest pain, sudden vision or hearing loss, or an erection
  lasting more than four hours.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does QMAX cost?**
From $129 a month for five doses. One-time packs are $165 for 10 doses and $224
for 20; three-month plans are $249 for 15, $299 for 30 and $449 for 60. Confirm
at checkout, as prices change.

**What is the cheapest cost per dose?**
$7.48, on the $449 three-month plan for 60 doses. Among the one-time packs, the
20-pack works out at $11.20 a dose.

**How do you take QMAX?**
It is a liquid held under the tongue rather than swallowed straight away.
Follow the directions that come with your prescription.

**How fast does QMAX work, and how long does it last?**
BetterMe Rx says it may start working in about 10 minutes for some patients,
with a window of up to 36 hours. Individual response varies.

**Is QMAX FDA-approved?**
No. The four-ingredient formula is compounded. Sildenafil, tadalafil and
vardenafil are FDA-approved for ED individually; apomorphine is approved for
Parkinson's disease and used off-label here.

**Can I cancel or get a refund?**
Recurring plans can be cancelled before they renew, but BetterMe Rx's terms say
an order cannot be refunded once it has been submitted for fulfilment, except
where the law requires. Read the current terms before choosing a three-month
plan.

**Are QMAX, Q MAX and BetterMe Rx QMAX the same thing?**
Yes. They are all names for the same sublingual liquid from BetterMe Rx.

Clinical review, provider oversight and state availability

Guarantees, commitments and published claims

HermanRx does not publish a money back promise; it publishes timeline and pricing commitments and states compounded medications are not FDA approved. HermanRx also notes advertised outcome figures that exceed some trial averages and lists that testimonials are first-name-only with no independent aggregate rating published.

Found vs HumeCare+ compares two different approaches to GLP-1 era weight care. Quick verdict: Found emphasizes a biology-first, personalized medically supervised program with plans starting as low as $39 and insurance support, while HumeCare+ offers a bundled compounded GLP-1 protocol with glycine, B vitamins, body composition scans, and prices starting at $199/month.

How the approaches differ: personalization vs protocol

Both brands provide clinician-reviewed prescriptions and use compounded GLP-1s in some offerings, but Found and HumeCare+ organize care differently. Found positions itself as a biology-first program that builds personalized plans around hormones, blood sugar, sleep, and behavior. HumeCare+ presents a set protocol: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide combined with glycine, vitamins B6 and B12, monthly body-composition scans, an app, and continuous clinician access.

Distinctive differences to decide on

1. Price and payment framing

Found advertises affordable monthly plans with a specific callout: get started for as low as $39 and most insurance now accepted. Found also notes advertised pricing for branded GLP-1s via insurance may involve 12 month commitments and that medication coverage and copays vary. HumeCare+ lists compounded semaglutide from $199/month and a tirzepatide protocol from $299/month and describes those prices as promotional and cash-pay oriented.

2. Clinical model and program focus

Found emphasizes a biology-first, personalized medically supervised program that targets hormones, metabolism, sleep, and behavior. HumeCare+ focuses on a bundled GLP-1 protocol that adds glycine and B vitamins for muscle and side-effect support, plus regular clinician optimization and an app.

3. Additional supports and tracking

HumeCare+ includes monthly body composition scans to track fat versus lean mass over time and provides a connected app for tracking medication and food logging. Found highlights a holistic whole-body approach and hands-on insurance paperwork support but does not supply captured evidence of monthly body-composition scans or a dedicated app in the source material.

4. Guarantees and outcome framing

HumeCare+ advertises a 10 percent or your money back promise over six months, with terms to verify directly with the provider. Found does not advertise a money-back guarantee in the supplied evidence; it instead emphasizes beginning to see results as soon as month one and varied advertised offers for discounts subject to terms.

Safety, regulations, and what is not supplied

Both providers’ sources note that compounded medications are not evaluated by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Found explicitly states that certain medications are FDA-approved for conditions other than weight and that compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved nor available in all states. HumeCare+ similarly states its compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated and distinguishes them from branded products. The supplied evidence does not include head-to-head clinical trial data comparing outcomes between the two programs.

Eden markets a guided GLP-1 program that combines medication, care, and support under one monthly price and emphasizes a lifetime price guarantee and no membership fees.

4. Shipping and delivery

Embody advertises free shipping with delivery in 1–2 days after clinician approval. Eden offers free expedited shipping according to the captured details. Both providers indicate free shipping, but Embody specifies a 1–2 day delivery window after approval while Eden is described as expedited shipping without a captured transit window.

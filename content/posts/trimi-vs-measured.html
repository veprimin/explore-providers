3. Compounding, clinical oversight and regulatory notes

Both Trimi and Measured offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prescribed by licensed clinicians. Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Trimi advertises a 6-month money-back promise: follow your plan for 6 months and, if you do not lose weight, you can request a refund with terms applying. Trimi also states that if not approved by their medical team there is a 100% automatic refund. Measured highlights internal analysis that members reporting weight data averaged a 31 lb loss at 6 months and includes language that individual results vary and are not guaranteed. Measured also notes cancel anytime and that internal outcome claims are not independent evidence.

Measured pairs compounded medication with same-day prescription decisions, unlimited provider messaging, a 24/7 Care Team, and dietitian support. Trimi emphasizes licensed U.S. providers and a named clinical network led by a medical director with telehealth oversight, and notes that one flat membership covers medication, clinician visits, ongoing check-ins, and free shipping.

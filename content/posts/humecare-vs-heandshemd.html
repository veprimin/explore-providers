Quick verdict: HumeCare+ vs He & She MD

HumeCare+ centers on a bundled GLP-1 protocol with built-in muscle support, body-composition scans, and a 10%-or-money-back promise, while He & She MD emphasizes lower introductory pricing and coaching with 1:1 clinician messaging. Early verdict: choose HumeCare+ if you want GLP-1 plus muscle-preserving supplements and monthly composition tracking; choose He & She MD if you prioritize a lower intro price and integrated coaching.

How the two programs differ at a glance

This comparison uses the supplied facts to highlight the most distinctive differences between HumeCare+ and He & She MD: medication bundle and supportive ingredients, body-composition tracking, pricing structure and promos, and the nature of coaching and clinician access.

1. Medication protocol and added ingredients

HumeCare+ sells a protocol, not just a prescription. It pairs physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with glycine, vitamin B12, and B6 built into the formulation for muscle support and side-effect relief. He & She MD also offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, including oral semaglutide, but emphasizes the medication plus coaching rather than adding glycine or B vitamins into a bundled formulation.

2. Body-composition tracking and measurement

HumeCare+ provides monthly body composition scans that track lean mass versus fat mass, which the company positions as addressing a limitation of bare GLP-1 programs. He & She MD focuses on progress tracking inside its app but the supplied evidence does not state monthly body-composition scans as part of its standard offering.

3. Price structure and promotional terms

Starting prices differ and carry qualifiers. HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/month and tirzepatide plus B12 and glycine from $299/month. He & She MD lists compounded semaglutide injections starting at $139/month and tirzepatide from $199/month as six-month introductory rates that renew higher after the first six months. He & She MD notes that intro rates are promotional and renew at a higher six-month price; HumeCare+ pricing is presented as monthly rates with a note that pricing shown reflects a promotional discount that may change.

4. Coaching, clinician access, and app features

Both programs include clinician interaction and an app, but the emphasis differs. He & She MD bundles 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1 coaching, and progress tracking at no extra cost inside its app. HumeCare+ includes unlimited clinician access with a U.S. care team available 24/7, plus an app for medication tracking, food logging, and a daily plan. Both advertise free or expedited shipping and clinician access; He & She MD highlights no membership fee while HumeCare+ highlights free next-day delivery and continuous physician optimization.

Safety and regulatory notes

Both HumeCare+ and He & She MD offer compounded medications. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. The supplied evidence notes that GLP-1 drugs carry serious risks.

Bottom line and who each program suits

HumeCare+ vs He & She MD comes down to bundled protocol and measurement versus lower intro pricing and coaching. HumeCare+ wraps GLP-1 medication with glycine, B12, B6, monthly body-composition scans, unlimited clinician access, and a 10%-or-money-back promise. He & She MD emphasizes 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1 coaching and tracking in its app, multiple medication formats including oral semaglutide, and lower six-month introductory pricing that renews higher.

Choose HumeCare+ if you want a bundled GLP-1 protocol that adds glycine and B vitamins for muscle support, monthly body-composition scans, and a money-back promise.

Choose He & She MD if you want lower introductory monthly pricing with integrated 1:1 clinician coaching, progress tracking, and options for injectable or oral semaglutide at promo rates for the first six months.

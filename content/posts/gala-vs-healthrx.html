Gala vs HealthRx: five key differences at a glance

This comparison focuses on five supported differences: pricing structure, medication options and compounded status, shipping speed and handling, clinician review and dose decisions, and membership/cancellation or plan terms.

1) Pricing structure: flat all-inclusive price vs prepaid monthly plans

Gala advertises a flat all-inclusive direct-pay price covering the provider visit, medication, shipping and ongoing care and shows a promotional $220 off; the landing content notes base price was not visible in readable page text. HealthRx advertises plans from $99 per month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month, with the lowest advertised monthly price requiring a longer prepaid plan.

2) Medication type and regulatory note

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. The sources state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HealthRx adds that compounds are prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and are prepared per prescription; Gala similarly describes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide but repeats the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

3) Shipping speed and handling

Gala advertises free 2-day shipping straight to your door. HealthRx advertises overnight shipping with cold chain tracked delivery and states free shipping is included on every plan.

4) Clinician review and dose decisions

HealthRx explicitly states that a clinician reviews your file and decides whether semaglutide, tirzepatide, or neither is right; the user chooses the plan the medication arrives on. Gala notes provider check-ins and dose monitoring are included and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

5) Membership, cancellation and pricing changes

Gala repeatedly states no monthly membership and cancel anytime in captured landing-page evidence and claims one flat price at every dose with no hidden fees. HealthRx emphasizes no insurance and no added consultation fee or shipping charge, and that plan price does not change when your dose does, while warning that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change and that the lowest price requires a longer prepaid plan.

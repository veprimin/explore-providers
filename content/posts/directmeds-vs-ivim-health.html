Comparing Directmeds vs IVIM Health? Quick verdict: Directmeds emphasizes promotional starting pricing, no separate membership fee, and next-day shipping; IVIM Health emphasizes strong third-party ratings, a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol and a free-month membership code. This article compares them side by side to highlight the clearest differences and help you decide.

Key differences at a glance

This Directmeds vs IVIM Health comparison focuses on five supported differences: pricing and promotions, membership and fees, compounded medication and regulatory notes, shipping and geographic availability, and clinical validation and reviews.

1) Pricing and promotions

Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and lists standard FAQ pricing of $297/mo for semaglutide and $399/mo for tirzepatide. IVIM Health shows a captured landing-page price of $75/mo for semaglutide and $133/mo for tirzepatide with a $74.99/mo program fee and a limited-offer free month membership code GIFT75; its FAQ also reports a $99 first month and $174/month ongoing rate. Preserve these provider qualifiers when comparing costs.

Check IVIM Health membership offer

2) Membership, fees, and cancellation

Directmeds emphasizes no monthly membership fee, no hidden fees, and month-to-month cancellation for standard customers, while some subscribe-and-save plans require a stated term. IVIM promotes a free month with code GIFT75 and a no-obligation free consultation valued at $75 for new patients, but it also documents program fees and a pricing structure that rises after the first month per its FAQ.

3) Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and include the same regulatory qualifier: compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Directmeds states its medications come from U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. IVIM states medications are prepared by independent, licensed compounding pharmacies and emphasizes informed consent and safety information.

Review Directmeds compounding details

4) Shipping, availability, and logistics

Directmeds advertises free temperature-controlled UPS or FedEx delivery generally in 1–2 days and free next-day UPS/FedEx shipping in other captured text. Directmeds also notes service is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. IVIM notes availability is not in all states and that actual product appearance may differ; its landing capture does not provide the same next-day shipping claim.

5) Clinical protocol, outcomes, and third-party validation

IVIM highlights unusually strong third-party validation with 4.9-star ratings on both Google and Trustpilot and promotes a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol that it says can accelerate weight loss outcomes by up to 40% or that Ivim patients lost an average of 46% more weight over 68 weeks in its published analyses. IVIM describes those as its own published or internal study findings and notes these are not head-to-head trials. Directmeds emphasizes clinician and nursing support but captured evidence does not include third-party ratings or a proprietary protocol claim.

Conclusion: Directmeds vs IVIM Health

Directmeds vs IVIM Health comes down to tradeoffs supported by each provider’s captured claims: Directmeds stresses promotional pricing starting at $147, no separate monthly membership fee, included clinician review and free temperature-controlled next-day UPS/FedEx shipping, and U.S.-based 503A compounding. IVIM Health emphasizes a free-month membership code GIFT75, a no-obligation $75-value consultation for new patients, strong 4.9-star third-party ratings, and a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol with published observational analyses. Both offer compounded medications that are not FDA-approved.

Choose Directmeds if you want clear promotional starting pricing, no monthly membership fee, included supplies and next-day temperature-controlled shipping. Choose IVIM Health if you value dual 4.9-star third-party ratings, a proprietary clinician-guided optimization protocol, and a free-month membership code to lower initial cost.

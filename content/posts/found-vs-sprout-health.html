How Found and Sprout Health differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on five supported differences: personalization and care model, pricing and insurance, medication types and regulatory notes, onboarding speed and approval, and outcome framing and claims. The comparison uses only supplied facts and preserves qualifiers such as “starting at,” “as low as,” “may,” “eligible,” and “subject to clinician review.”

Personalization and care model

Found centers on a biology-first approach that conducts a medical evaluation and builds a personalized program around each patient’s metabolic biology including hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavior. Sprout Health is described as a fast-entry GLP-1 platform with a streamlined onboarding; its emphasis is on fast, low-friction access rather than deep personalization. Both involve clinician review for prescriptions, but only Found’s evidence emphasizes holistic, medically supervised personalization.

Pricing and insurance handling

Found advertises plans getting started for as low as $39 and notes that most insurance is now accepted, that it handles insurance paperwork, and that certain Medicare programs may lower brand-name GLP-1 costs to as low as $50/month for eligible Part D members. Sprout Health offers a $50 off the first month, bringing the initial month to $199, with a stated ongoing price of $249/month; Sprout operates on a direct-pay model and does not bill insurance according to captured FAQ evidence.

Medication types and regulatory notes

Both providers reference compounded GLP-1 options in the captured evidence. Found specifically lists compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sprout Health’s evidence states compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Found also references access to branded GLP-1s via insurance in some contexts, while Sprout’s captured evidence notes compounded medication only, no branded Ozempic or Wegovy.

Onboarding speed and approval

Sprout Health emphasizes “lightning-quick approval” and a fast, hassle-free process, positioning itself for patients who want minimal onboarding friction and rapid shipment after approval. Found’s materials focus on a thorough evaluation and personalized planning; the captured evidence does not claim lightning-quick approval and instead signals a clinical review and eligibility process for prescriptions.

Outcome framing and expectations

Sprout Health frames outcomes around a 10 to 26 percent body weight reduction over 12 months based on GLP-1 clinical outcome literature and states individual results vary; Sprout does not offer a formal outcome guarantee. Found’s materials state “begin seeing and feeling results as soon as month 1” and warn that certain medications are FDA-approved for conditions other than weight, and that results vary by individual. Neither provider guarantees specific medical outcomes in the captured evidence.

1. Price and how starting costs differ

Price is the most striking difference in this comparison. Bodybuilding Health+ lists compounded semaglutide starting at $179 per month and tirzepatide starting at $209 per month. Telos Rx lists semaglutide as low as $99 per month and tirzepatide as low as $139 per month. Both providers note that treatment is prescription-only and subject to clinician approval.

2. Membership, billing and cancellation

Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes a commercial model with no monthly membership, no hidden fees, and the ability to cancel anytime; pricing examples include single-month to 12-month bundles with stated multi-month savings. Telos Rx also states cancel anytime with no penalties, but captured notes emphasize that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, and renewal terms may change and that details are subject to the live offer.

3. Program scope and offerings

Bodybuilding Health+ positions itself as a multi-program telehealth platform offering GLP-1s plus NAD+, sermorelin, and sexual performance options. Telos Rx focuses on GLP-1 medications and messaging about semaglutide and tirzepatide acting on different hormonal pathways, with clinician guidance to choose the right molecule. If you want broader services beyond GLP-1, the supplied evidence lists more program types from Bodybuilding Health+.

4. Medical review and transparency

Both providers require review by a US-licensed clinician before prescribing compounded GLP-1 medications. Bodybuilding Health+ names Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D., as medical oversight in the supplied information. Telos Rx states a US-licensed provider decides which medication is right for you. Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

5. Multi-month pricing and bundles

Bodybuilding Health+ provides explicit multi-month bundles and examples of savings for 3-, 6-, and 12-month options for compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide in the captured evidence. Telos Rx lists per-month ranges and emphasizes that your price depends on your plan and the live offer, but the captured evidence does not supply the same multi-month bundle detail.

Preserved qualifiers and coverage limits

Both providers’ materials captured here note that compounded medications are prescription-only, subject to provider review, and are not FDA-approved. Bodybuilding Health+ also notes limited state availability and that physician approval is required. Telos Rx captured statements warn that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage, and renewal terms may change.

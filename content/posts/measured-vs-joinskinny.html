Measured vs JoinSkinny is a direct comparison of two online weight-management services. Quick verdict: Measured offers lower promotional pricing from $89/mo and same-day Rx decisions, while JoinSkinny starts at $149 with temperature-controlled, FDA-regulated pharmacy fulfillment and flat-rate dosing options.

See Measured pricing and same-day Rx decisions

How these services differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on five distinctive areas where Measured and JoinSkinny differ: starting price and promotions, medication type and pharmacy handling, prescription process and state eligibility, shipping speed and handling, and pricing structure and plan options. The article compares each area directly using only the supplied facts.

Starting price and promotional offers

Measured advertises promotional compounded semaglutide from $89/mo and compounded tirzepatide from $189/mo. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149 and promotes a $140 promotional discount and bundle options. Both providers present promotional pricing that may require confirmation during signup.

Check Measured promotional pricing from $89/mo

Medication type and pharmacy handling

Measured pairs compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and explicitly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. JoinSkinny offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and states that prescriptions are filled by licensed, U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners, and that these pharmacies prepare authentic GLP-1 Injections and GLP-1 + GIP when prescribed. JoinSkinny also says compounds contain the same active molecules used in brand-name medications. Measured highlights compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide as its product offerings but includes the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Prescription process and eligibility

Measured emphasizes same-day prescription decisions and says Rx required; all prescriptions issued at provider’s discretion. JoinSkinny describes a clinician-reviewed telemedicine workflow: a licensed provider reviews your information, prescribes if appropriate, and dose adjustments and monthly check-ins are provided. JoinSkinny also notes availability depends on eligible U.S. states and that entering your state during signup will confirm availability.

Shipping speed and handling

Measured advertises free 2-day shipping and fast & free shipping on every order. JoinSkinny advertises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1-2 days, with same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time and next-day UPS delivery in a temperature controlled package. JoinSkinny notes weekend orders ship Monday to ensure temperature management.

View JoinSkinny plans with temperature-controlled shipping

Pricing structure, plans and ongoing support

Measured highlights promotional monthly prices and also promotes dietitian support, unlimited provider messaging, and 24/7 Care Team access, plus claims based on internal analysis about member outcomes that are not independent evidence. JoinSkinny offers flat-rate dosing, monthly and multi-month bundles, no membership fee, and installment options such as Afterpay, Klarna and Affirm. JoinSkinny also emphasizes ongoing medical support, dose adjustments, and monthly check-ins through telemedicine and 24/7 chat.

What to consider when choosing

Measured may appeal if the key factors for you are lower promotional starting price and same-day prescription decisions plus dietitian support and unlimited messaging. JoinSkinny may appeal if you prioritize licensed U.S. pharmacies, temperature-controlled shipping, flat-rate dosing, and clear multi-month bundle options with clinician-reviewed oversight and state-based eligibility confirmation.

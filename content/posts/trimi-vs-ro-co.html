How Trimi vs Ro.co differ at a glance

In short, Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/month on an annual plan and compounded tirzepatide from $125/month, with flat pricing that does not rise as dose increases and free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping. Ro.co advertises a $39 online get-started price and is the only mainstream platform here offering the FDA-approved Wegovy pill as an oral GLP-1 option. These are direct, material differences that shape who each provider suits.

Early verdict

If you want a lower per-month compounded injectable with flat dosing price and overnight shipping, Trimi is the closer match. If you need an FDA-approved oral GLP-1 option and a $39 entry point, Ro.co is the match.

1) Product type and regulatory status

Trimi primarily offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide prescribed by US-licensed clinicians and prepared by state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies. The source repeatedly states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Ro.co, by contrast, provides access to the Wegovy pill, described as the first and only FDA-approved oral GLP-1 weight loss medication available through the platform. Ro.co emphasizes that the Wegovy pill is a finished FDA-approved product while noting that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

2) Pricing and plan entry

Price positioning differs markedly. Trimi lists compounded semaglutide from $99/month on the annual plan and tirzepatide from $125/month, with advertising that all doses are the same price and a flat membership covers medication, clinician visits, ongoing check-ins, and free shipping. Ro.co advertises you can get started online for just $39 and states ongoing branded medication pricing depends on the prescribed plan; Ro.co also notes branded, FDA-approved medications typically carry higher ongoing costs than compounded alternatives.

3) Shipping, refunds and membership terms

Trimi promotes free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and a 6-month money-back promise where, if not approved by the medical team you can receive a 100% automatic refund and you may request a refund if you follow the plan for 6 months and do not lose weight – terms apply. Trimi states one flat membership covers medication, clinician visits, ongoing check-ins, and free shipping, and that pricing never changes as dose is adjusted. Ro.co’s captured page discloses the $39 entry price and that there are no hidden fees and that pricing is disclosed before completing the consultation; captured evidence did not verify shipping or refund specifics for Ro.co.

4) Clinical oversight and pharmacy sourcing

Trimi states prescriptions are provided by licensed U.S. providers through Arora Health, a network led by Medical Director Dr. Sean Arora, and that compounding pharmacies include named 503A pharmacies such as VialsRx and GreenwichRx with certificates of analysis available on request. Ro.co emphasizes clinician review for medical appropriateness before offering the Wegovy pill and highlights regulatory assurance because it prescribes FDA-approved finished products rather than compounded medications.

Pricing transparency and promotional structure

MedVi displays a winter promotional first-month price of $179 that explicitly includes fast, free shipping and notes the ongoing monthly price rises afterward. The platform documents a standalone month at $299 and a three-month plan at $649 as post-promo options. In contrast, RxActivate does not show any price or billing terms before the contact-information step; pricing is gated behind the eligibility questionnaire and submission of name, email and phone number. This makes MedVi more transparent up front, while RxActivate requires completing intake to learn costs.

Enrollment flow and clinician review

MedVi’s process is a simple online intake with no clinic visits and an online clinician review; OpenLoop Health clinicians review eligibility after checkout and retain prescribing discretion. RxActivate uses a two-minute eligibility questionnaire that requests goals, medication history, demographics, and a safety screen. In the synthetic test, RxActivate confirmed Alabama serviceability but required contact information before revealing likely plan details or pricing. If you prefer to see price and shipping terms immediately, MedVi is explicit. If you prefer being matched by a clinician before seeing cost, RxActivate enforces that sequence.

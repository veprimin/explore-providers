Pricing and billing models

Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/month on an annual plan and tirzepatide at $125/month, emphasizing flat pricing that never rises as dose increases. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149 with monthly and 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles and a promotional discount example of $140 off. JoinSkinny notes no membership fee and the ability to cancel before the next billing cycle; Trimi describes an annual plan and a 6-month money-back promise with terms that apply.

Compounding, pharmacies, and regulatory notes

Trimi supplies compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by state-licensed 503A pharmacies, including named partners with certificates of analysis available on request, and explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved. JoinSkinny states prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners and also notes that compounded products are not FDA-approved where relevant. JoinSkinny highlights branded and authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1 + GIP prepared by licensed pharmacies when prescribed.

Shipping speed, temperature control, and logistics

Trimi advertises free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and free overnight shipping for its compounded products. JoinSkinny advertises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1-2 days and same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, with next-day UPS for most orders and specific handling rules for weekend orders.

Clinical oversight, support, and monitoring

Trimi describes licensed U.S. clinicians prescribing compounded medications through a clinical network led by a named medical director, and mentions that plans are reviewed by licensed providers through Arora Health. It also states a 6-month money-back promise if not approved by the medical team with terms. JoinSkinny describes a quick health questionnaire, licensed provider review, personalized treatment plans, ongoing monthly check-ins, dose adjustments, and 24/7 chat support. JoinSkinny also highlights HIPAA protection and continuous monitoring by licensed providers.

Conclusion and recommendation

Trimi vs JoinSkinny comes down to priorities: Trimi emphasizes lower advertised monthly pricing for compounded semaglutide on an annual plan, flat pricing as dose increases, free overnight shipping, named compounding partners, and a 6-month refund promise. JoinSkinny emphasizes bundle flexibility, no membership fee, same-day shipping cutoff and 1-2 day delivery, licensed pharmacy certification, and 24/7 support with monthly check-ins.

Choose Trimi if you prioritize lower advertised monthly compounded pricing from $99/mo on an annual plan, flat dose pricing, named 503A compounding pharmacies, and free overnight shipping.

Choose JoinSkinny if you prefer plans starting at $149 with monthly or multi-month bundles, branded and licensed pharmacy partners with LegitScript certification, same-day shipping when ordered before 2 pm central time, and ongoing 24/7 support.

Conclusion and recommendation

Embody vs ReadyRx: based on the captured evidence, Embody presents clear documented pricing, fast shipping, and an online clinician review for compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, while ReadyRx had no verified pricing or landing-page evidence in the capture and requires live verification of offers.

Choose Embody if you prioritize documented low entry pricing, free 1–2 day shipping after approval, and a straightforward telehealth prescription flow for compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide, noting that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and final pricing may vary based on clinician recommendation.

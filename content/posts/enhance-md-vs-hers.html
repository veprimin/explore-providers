Pricing: starting cost and ongoing structure

Enhance MD advertises semaglutide starting at $49 for the first month and tirzepatide starting at $99 for the first month. Those are introductory first-month prices; ongoing flat-rate prices are higher and are described as a single price that stays the same at every dose. Hers lists Wegovy from $149/month for the medication portion, but medication is only available with an active Hers Weight Loss Membership which has a first month fee of $39 and auto-renews at $149/month thereafter. The Hers pricing notes that total cost includes membership plus medication and that membership is required.

Medication types and regulatory status

Enhance MD offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide on the same platform. The source explicitly notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Hers offers FDA-approved Wegovy, a brand-name semaglutide product, and emphasizes that it does not offer compounded medications. The Hers source also highlights other brand-name GLP-1s and states Ozempic and Wegovy are registered trademarks of Novo Nordisk, and Zepbound is a registered trademark of Eli Lilly.

Access, membership, and clinician oversight

Enhance MD operates as a licensed telehealth platform with clinician-directed review and has treated more than 10,000 patients, according to the source. It does not bill insurance and uses a direct-pay model. Hers requires an active membership for medication access and states that a prescription is not guaranteed; membership and medication are billed separately. Both providers note that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Pricing model, dose escalation, and predictability

Enhance MD highlights flat-rate pricing that stays the same at every dose, which it presents as avoiding fee escalation during titration. The source frames this as a budgeting advantage versus competitors that increase fees at higher doses. Hers emphasizes name-brand medications with no price markups but includes a membership model that affects total monthly cost and availability.

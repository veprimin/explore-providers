Product types and formulation options

Both providers present GLP-1 options. Luvo Health lists Tirzepatide Injection, Semaglutide Tablets, and Semaglutide Injection, plus microdosing options for injections and tablets. BreezeMeds lists Semaglutide injection, Tirzepatide injection, and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide. BreezeMeds explicitly calls out both injectable and oral forms on the landing evidence.

Support model and dose changes

Luvo Health repeatedly references ongoing support and dose changes across a MONTH 1 | MONTH 2–3 | 3+ MONTHS structure and notes dose adjustments based on clinical response and cancel anytime language on the captured page. BreezeMeds emphasizes ongoing support with discreet shipping but the captured evidence focuses more on pricing and delivery than a month-by-month dose schedule.

Regulatory and compounding notes

Both sources caution that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Luvo Health specifically states compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and are not reviewed or approved by the FDA. BreezeMeds notes medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Delivery, enrollment and enrollment limitations

BreezeMeds advertises discreet home delivery and asks users to confirm states serviced and billing details on the landing evidence. Luvo Health highlights that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and advises people to confirm eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

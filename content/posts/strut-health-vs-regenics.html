How the two providers differ at a glance

Both platforms provide compounded GLP-1 medications and telehealth care, but their emphases, pricing structures, and clinical staffing differ. Below are the most distinctive supported differences: pricing and promotions, oral specialization, clinician model and scope of therapies, pharmacy and regulatory notes, and additional features like shipping and peptides.

1. Pricing and promotional structure

Regenics advertises promotional pricing with exact figures: Semaglutide $139 first month (renews $199/mo) and tirzepatide $189 first month (renews $279/mo). Strut Health lists pricing From $199 per Month and advertises Oral GLP-1 from $199/month. These are presented as starting or promotional figures exactly as supplied.

2. Oral specialization versus multiple GLP-1 options

Strut Health is described as specializing in oral-based program options and notes Oral Semaglutide specifically. Regenics offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and highlights both as options, plus a Sermorelin peptide add-on. If oral Semaglutide lozenges are a priority, Strut positions itself as best for users specifically seeking oral plans. Regenics positions itself for those wanting semaglutide or tirzepatide options and an additional peptide line.

3. Clinical oversight and care model

Strut Health emphasizes guided consultations and ongoing progress communication with a clinical team focused on oral medication management. Regenics describes nurse-practitioner-led GLP-1 care with licensed nurse practitioners who review intake before prescribing and notes prescriptions are available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

4. Pharmacy, regulatory, and shipping notes

Both providers offer compounded medications prepared by U.S. compounding pharmacies and include the required regulatory language that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Regenics states its medications are compounded in the USA and shipped free to your door and cites a LegitScript certified pharmacy network. Strut Health advertises free shipping and no hidden fees, with compounded Semaglutide prepared pursuant to an individualized prescription and medical necessity required for some compounded options.

5. Additional features and limitations

Regenics lists FSA/HSA eligibility and a Sermorelin peptide therapy offering. Strut Health highlights its oral specialization, guided consultations, and a simpler, oral-focused telehealth process. Regenics notes promotional pricing applies to the first month and that monthly cost increases on renewal unless otherwise stated.

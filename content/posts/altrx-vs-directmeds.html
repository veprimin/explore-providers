Looking for a low-cost telehealth GLP-1 option? In this altRx vs Directmeds comparison the early verdict is simple: altRx positions itself as the lower-cost, limited-offer option while Directmeds emphasizes format choice and a promotional entry price. Both offer clinician-reviewed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and use U.S. 503A compounding pharmacies, but they differ on price, formats, availability and shipping speed.

Key differences at a glance: price, format, availability

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: starting price and pricing model, medication formats offered, shipping and delivery timing, state availability and refund/cancellation policies. Each point below compares altRx vs Directmeds directly using only the supplied evidence.

1. Starting price and pricing details

altRx advertises a limited offer with compounded semaglutide starting at $89/month and compounded tirzepatide at $149/month. altRx states multi month plans reduce the monthly rate and emphasizes same price every month and no membership or hidden fees. Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and lists standard FAQ pricing at $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. Directmeds also notes a summer promotion that saves $150 and that subscribe and save plans require a stated term.

2. Medication formats and product type

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications and include the regulatory caution that compounded medications are not FDA approved. Directmeds explicitly offers injectable and sublingual formats for semaglutide and tirzepatide. altRx describes compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide as part of its GLP 1 and GLP 1 plus GIP programs and highlights a complete treatment kit with vials, syringes and supplies included with its price.

3. Shipping speed and delivery features

altRx advertises free and discreet 2 day delivery and typical processing and delivery time of approximately 4 to 5 business days after a prescription is sent to a pharmacy. Directmeds advertises free temperature controlled UPS or FedEx delivery generally in 1 to 2 days and calls it free next day shipping. Both note medication ships from state licensed 503A compounding pharmacies.

4. Availability, cancellation and refunds

altRx states the membership is available to residents of all 50 U.S. states with provider availability and prescribing authority varying by state and that a licensed provider will review health evaluations as required by telehealth laws. altRx also emphasizes cancel anytime and no commitment. Directmeds notes it is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana, offers month to month cancellation for eligible customers and advertises a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Both reiterate that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

What this means for most patients

If your priority is lowest advertised monthly cost under a limited offer, altRx advertises the lowest starting prices and emphasizes no membership, no hidden fees and a complete kit included. If you value format choice and very fast temperature controlled shipping or a stated refund if a prescription is not approved, Directmeds highlights injectable and sublingual options and next day or 1 to 2 day shipping, while its FAQ lists higher standard prices beyond the promotional rate.

Safety and regulatory note

Both providers clearly state that offered compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Both require that a licensed clinician determine whether treatment is medically appropriate and will prescribe accordingly.

Conclusion: altRx vs Directmeds

In summary, altRx vs Directmeds splits primarily on price versus format and shipping. altRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $89/month and tirzepatide from $149/month with a focus on low cost, a complete kit and 2 day delivery. Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147, injectable and sublingual formats, free next day or 1 to 2 day temperature controlled shipping and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Both use U.S. 503A compounding pharmacies and require clinician review.

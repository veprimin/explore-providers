Looking for a telehealth weight loss program? Sesame Care vs IVIM Health comes down to choice vs a structured proprietary program. Quick verdict: choose Sesame Care if you want broad clinician choice, flexible pricing and insurance options; choose IVIM Health if you want a clinician-guided proprietary protocol with strong third-party ratings and a compounded medication model.

Explore Sesame Care clinician options

How these two providers differ at a glance

Sesame Care operates as a telehealth marketplace where patients browse and pick clinicians, with plans starting at $59/mo and cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo. IVIM Health offers a clinician-guided GLP-1 ID protocol, markets a free month membership with code GIFT75, and uses compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with listed pricing from $75/mo and $133/mo plus a program fee. Both emphasize clinician supervision, but Sesame accepts insurance for weight loss medications while IVIM operates direct-pay with compounded drugs that are not FDA-approved.

Care model and clinician selection

Sesame Care: marketplace choice. Sesame lets patients browse and select providers based on specialty, availability, and pricing, so experience can vary by clinician. IVIM Health: structured protocol. IVIM promotes its GLP-1 ID clinician-guided titration and optimization protocol that is a personalized, structured approach rather than a fixed-dose plan.

Medication type and regulatory notes

Sesame Care lists branded and insured options. It references semaglutide and tirzepatide pricing with examples showing price with insurance as low as $25 and cash-pay prices such as semaglutide $149 and tirzepatide $299, and notes medication-specific approval status must be confirmed. IVIM Health prescribes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide; the source repeatedly states these are compounded and are not FDA-approved, and that compounded drugs have not undergone FDA review for safety, efficacy, or quality.

Pricing structure, insurance acceptance, and offers

Sesame Care: starting services as low as $59/mo* for ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on insurance and pharmacy. Sesame also indicates insurance accepted for weight loss medications.

IVIM Health: lists compounded semaglutide at $75/mo, compounded tirzepatide at $133/mo plus a $74.99/mo program fee in some materials, and markets a free month membership with code GIFT75 and a no-obligation free consult valued at $75. The platform operates direct-pay and does not bill insurance according to the supplied FAQ content.

Third-party ratings and program claims

IVIM Health emphasizes unusually strong third-party validation: a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot and proprietary claims such as patients following the IVIM Protocol lost an average of 46% more weight over 68 weeks in their published study and a claim of up to 40% faster weight loss with GLP-1 ID. The source qualifies these as not head-to-head trials and notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Sesame Care highlights a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states, and frames results as varying by clinician and based on a 12-month commitment with milestones tied to a study of injectable semaglutide paired with lifestyle changes.

Sesame’s experience depends on the clinician you choose; its source lists pros such as easy booking and broad treatment coverage, and a con that experience can vary by clinician. IVIM’s source highlights high third-party ratings and a structured program with proprietary outcome claims, but repeatedly notes compounded medications carry regulatory differences and risks and are not FDA-reviewed.

Conclusion: Choose based on what matters most

Sesame Care vs IVIM Health splits on choice versus structure. Choose Sesame Care if you prioritize selecting your own clinician, insurance acceptance for weight loss meds, and flexible marketplace pricing. Choose IVIM Health if you prioritize a clinician-guided proprietary protocol, unusually strong third-party ratings, and introductory offers such as a free month with code GIFT75. All claims and prices above are taken from each provider’s published materials; medication approval status and eligibility must be confirmed with the provider.

Choose Sesame Care if you want broad telehealth choice, flexible scheduling, and the option to use insurance for medications. Choose IVIM Health if you want a structured GLP-1 ID program with high third-party ratings and are comfortable with compounded medications and a direct-pay model.

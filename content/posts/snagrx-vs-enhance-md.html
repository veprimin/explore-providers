Direct comparison: Pricing structure and commitments

Price framing is the clearest difference when you compare SnagRx vs Enhance MD. SnagRx lists compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month as 12-month plan rates. SnagRx notes month-to-month equivalents are higher: $99 and $149. SnagRx emphasizes the 12-month plan rate is locked flat at every dose for the life of treatment.

Enhance MD markets semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month, then applies a flat-rate ongoing price. Enhance MD states the same price applies at every dose after the introductory period and highlights very low first-month entry pricing.

What that means for patients

  • SnagRx: lowest annual-plan monthly price on the supplied evidence, but requires a 12-month commitment to get the $69/$119 rates.
  • Enhance MD: lowest first-month cost on the supplied evidence, useful if early affordability matters, with a flat-rate approach thereafter.

Availability of compounds, clinical volume, and provider network

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide rather than branded finished products. SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and operates with a clinician network called OpenLoop Health that covers 48 states. Enhance MD highlights that it has treated more than 10,000 patients and offers both compounds on one platform.

Price locking and fee escalation

Both platforms claim flat pricing across dosing, but they present it differently. SnagRx explicitly markets a price lock at every dose and at renewal for customers who enroll on the 12-month plan. Enhance MD stresses flat-rate pricing so the price does not rise as dose escalates once the ongoing rate is set, and it promotes a very low first-month price.

Operations, shipping, and clinician review

SnagRx states intake is about five minutes, OpenLoop clinicians typically review within five hours and always within 24, and approved orders ship next-day via UPS or FedEx in temperature-controlled packaging. SnagRx also states a full refund if a prescription is declined and offers a 6-month weight-loss guarantee subject to terms. Enhance MD documents clinician-directed review and highlights metabolic lab testing every six months in some published pricing tiers.

Regulatory and safety notes both providers disclose

When comparing SnagRx vs Enhance MD it is important to note both supply compounded medications. The evidence for both explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Each platform describes clinician review before prescribing and counsels prospective users to discuss risks with a provider. SnagRx additionally states its medications are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but are not FDA-approved finished products.

How care delivery and clinician access differ

altRx uses 100% online medical visits and assigns a licensed provider who reviews your evaluation; visits may be live video or asynchronous, depending on state telehealth laws. The provider determines if treatment is appropriate and may prescribe compounded semaglutide or compounded tirzepatide. Sesame Care operates as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and choose clinicians for video visits, offering flexible scheduling and a large clinician network. Sesame Care highlights ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs, and messaging.

Medication type and regulatory notes

altRx states its offerings are compounded medications and includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sesame Care describes availability of branded FDA-approved options such as Wegovy oral semaglutide and Zepbound tirzepatide pens, and cautions that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Quick verdict: SnagRx is the lower-cost, price-locked compounded option for patients who prioritize predictable per-dose pricing, while He & She MD is for patients who want coaching and 1:1 clinician access bundled with their GLP-1 plan. This article compares SnagRx vs He & She MD directly on price structure, program support, medication formats, pharmacy and shipping, and regulatory notes.

See SnagRx pricing and details

Price structure and commitment: locked annual pricing vs timed intro rates

SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month as 12-month plan rates; month-to-month equivalents are $99 and $149. SnagRx emphasizes a rate that is “locked flat at every dose” for the life of treatment, meaning the enrolment rate does not increase during titration. He & She MD lists semaglutide injections from $139/month and tirzepatide from $199/month as six-month introductory rates that renew higher after the initial six months. The He & She MD headline prices are promotional and the platform states renewal prices are materially higher after the intro term.

Program support: bare-bones medication delivery vs bundled coaching

SnagRx offers prescription and telemedicine visits included but does not include coaching or nutrition programming. He & She MD bundles its GLP-1 program with a coaching app that includes 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific coaching, and progress tracking at no extra cost. If ongoing coaching and clinician messaging are priorities, He & She MD includes those features in its plan; if core objective is predictable medication pricing without bundled coaching, SnagRx emphasizes cost and price stability.

Medication formats, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications and include clinician review and prescription as part of the workflow. SnagRx sells compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and states its medications are compounded in US 503A partner pharmacies; SnagRx notes its compounded medications are not FDA-approved or evaluated by the FDA. He & She MD offers semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection options, produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies, and likewise notes compounded GLP-1s are not reviewed by FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality. Both providers require clinician consultation and state not all customers will qualify for a prescription.

Pharmacy, shipping, and clinician networks

SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and partners with OpenLoop Health, a clinician network that covers 48 states. SnagRx states clinician review typically occurs within five hours and approved orders ship next-day via UPS or FedEx in temperature-controlled packaging. He & She MD highlights FDA-regulated pharmacies and free expedited shipping; its marketing states medication ships straight to your door with expedited shipping and no shipping upcharges. Availability and clinician eligibility vary by state for both platforms and specific state coverage is not exhaustively supplied here.

How eligibility, refunds, and guarantees compare

SnagRx states OpenLoop Health clinicians screen eligibility and will meet with an individual after checkout to determine qualification; SnagRx says a declined prescription is fully refunded and advertises a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms. He & She MD also requires clinician consultation and warns not all customers will qualify; He & She MD shows promotional savings for the first six months and specifies renewal pricing but does not supply the same refund or guarantee language in the supplied evidence.

Bottom line on SnagRx vs He & She MD

SnagRx vs He & She MD comes down to price predictability versus bundled support. SnagRx is the lower-cost, price-locked compounded offering on a 12-month plan; He & She MD provides coaching and clinician access included in the app with promotional intro rates that renew higher. Review state eligibility and the clinician screening process for either platform before enrolling.

Choose SnagRx if you want compounded semaglutide from $69/month on a 12-month plan with a rate that is locked flat at every dose and named US 503A pharmacies with next-day shipping.

Choose He & She MD if you want a GLP-1 program that bundles coaching, 1:1 clinician messaging, and progress tracking with medication formats including oral semaglutide and injectable options and you accept six-month intro pricing that renews higher.

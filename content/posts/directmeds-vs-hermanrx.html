Pricing clarity and promotional differences

Directmeds markets a promotional price starting at $147 and separately lists standard FAQ prices of $297/mo for semaglutide and $399/mo for tirzepatide. HermanRx instead publishes a single all-inclusive monthly price for each GLP-1 formula: semaglutide + B12 at $249/mo and tirzepatide + B12 at $349/mo. Both providers state what those monthly amounts cover, but Directmeds highlights a promotion that differs from its FAQ pricing.

Formulations, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both platforms offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options. Directmeds specifies injectable and sublingual compounded formats and notes its U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. HermanRx lists compounded GLP-1s combined with vitamin B12 and states it offers both FDA-approved and compounded medications depending on clinician recommendation. Both providers include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Shipping, timelines and availability

Directmeds advertises free temperature-controlled next-day UPS or FedEx delivery generally in 1 to 2 days. HermanRx publishes a faster-sounding operational timeline: roughly a 5-minute assessment, provider review in about 24 hours and delivery in about 72 hours when prescribed, while its FAQ gives a wider 3 to 7 business day window depending on pharmacy processing and location. HermanRx states delivery to all 50 states, while Directmeds notes it is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana.

Platform scope, support and cancellation terms

Directmeds highlights ongoing clinician and nursing support, no hidden fees, month-to-month cancellation available, and that subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term. HermanRx advertises 24/7 patient support, a secure patient portal, and multiple treatment categories including weight loss, hormones and longevity for consolidating care on one platform. Directmeds includes a full refund if a prescription is not approved; HermanRx does not publish a money-back promise in the supplied evidence.

## How It Works

Hone Health follows a structured telehealth workflow to deliver safe and compliant online ED treatment. The process is designed for clarity and speed while maintaining clinical standards.

### Online consultation flow

Patients begin with an online intake and confidential account set-up. The platform guides users through the steps required for assessment and prescription consideration.

### Medical questionnaire

A detailed medical questionnaire collects health history, current medications, allergies, and ED-specific information to identify suitable treatments and flag contraindications.

### Doctor evaluation

Licensed clinicians review the questionnaire and any uploaded information. They may follow up with requests for clarification to ensure safe prescribing.

### Prescription approval

When appropriate, clinicians issue a prescription. Hone Health documents clinical rationale and follows telehealth protocols before approval.

### Pharmacy fulfillment

Approved prescriptions are sent to partner pharmacies for fulfillment. Hone Health coordinates with U.S.-based or accredited pharmacy partners for authenticity and legal compliance.

### Shipping & ongoing care

Medications are shipped discreetly. Hone Health also provides follow-up messaging, refill management, and access to behavioral/performance support programs as part of ongoing care.

## Medications Offered

Hone Health offers a comprehensive selection of ED treatments, combining on-demand and daily options plus behavioral support programs to meet varied needs.

- **Sildenafil (generic Viagra)** — Common on-demand option. Typical dosages: 25 mg, 50 mg, 100 mg. Suited for men seeking occasional use and fast-acting relief. Priced competitively with generics; cost-per-dose can be under $1 in some plans.
- **Tadalafil (generic Cialis)** — Longer-acting treatment. Typical dosages: 5 mg (daily), 10 mg, 20 mg (as-needed). Ideal for men who prefer spontaneous intimacy or prolonged effect. Tadalafil online availability includes both daily and on-demand regimens.
- **Daily tadalafil** — Low-dose daily regimen (commonly 2.5–5 mg) for men who want continuous readiness and fewer planning constraints. Suited for those in stable relationships or with frequent sexual activity.
- **Rapid-dissolve tablets** — Fast-acting formats for discreet and quicker onset. Useful for men who need a fast response without swallowing pills.
- **Behavioral / performance support programs** — Coaching and cognitive-behavioral content to address performance anxiety, relationship factors, and lifestyle elements that influence ED.

Price context: Hone Health positions itself as the most affordable ED provider. With options as low as $0.85 per dosage and tiered pricing for monthly plans, it is competitive for both one-off medications and subscription models.

Sesame Care vs Vaylen: quick comparison and verdict

Quick verdict: Sesame Care suits people who want broad telehealth choice, browsing and selecting a clinician with flexible scheduling; Vaylen suits decisive buyers who accept compounded GLP-1 and want the lowest effective cash-pay monthly rate on a prepaid 12-month plan.

Pricing model and starting costs

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs, and messaging for as low as $59/mo with a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Cash-pay GLP-1 medications are listed as low as $149/mo for semaglutide and $299/mo for tirzepatide, with price with insurance as low as $25 for some options. Vaylen sells cash-pay compounded GLP-1 with headline annualized rates: semaglutide from $83.25/mo on the 12-month plan or $224/mo month to month; oral semaglutide from $99/mo on the 12-month plan or $249/mo month to month; tirzepatide from $149/mo on the 12-month plan or $299/mo month to month.

Medication formats and regulatory notes

Sesame Care offers oral GLP-1 and injectable options through a clinician marketplace and lists branded products such as Wegovy pill and Zepbound KwikPen as described on its pages, while noting medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Vaylen emphasizes compounded semaglutide injectable, oral dissolvable semaglutide tablets, and compounded tirzepatide across three formats, and states compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality.

Clinician selection, review timelines, and network model

Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select providers based on specialty, availability, and pricing, giving patients more control and a large clinician network with flexible scheduling and nationwide access in many states. Vaylen operates a pay-first model where you prepay a 12-month plan, then complete a health questionnaire; a board-certified U.S. clinician reviews the file within 24 hours and Vaylen says it issues a full refund if the clinician declines to prescribe.

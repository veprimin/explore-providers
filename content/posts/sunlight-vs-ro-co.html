Price and entry cost: upfront comparison

Sunlight advertises personalized GLP-1 plans starting at $88/mo. Ro.co advertises you can get started online for just $39. That means Ro.co has the lower entry fee, while Sunlight emphasizes a monthly starting price for ongoing treatment.

Start with Ro.co for $39

Medication options and regulatory differences

The most striking difference is that Ro.co is presented as the only mainstream platform here offering the Wegovy pill, the FDA-approved oral GLP-1, which is noted as available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Sunlight offers injections and oral options and emphasizes personalized GLP-1 plans, but its materials include the regulatory caution that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Pricing model and transparency

Sunlight highlights “Same price forever” and promotes no monthly membership and cancel anytime terms on its landing content. Ro.co emphasizes no hidden fees and that pricing is disclosed before you complete your consultation, while also noting ongoing branded medication pricing can vary and may be higher than compounded alternatives.

Shipping, membership, and convenience

Sunlight promotes free and discreet 1-2 day shipping and repeats “no monthly membership” and “cancel anytime” in captured landing-page evidence. For Ro.co, shipping, membership, and cancellation details were not verified on the captured page.

Who each provider is best for

  • Sunlight: promoted as best for people who should confirm eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment; emphasizes steady pricing, injections and oral options, free 1-2 day shipping, and no monthly membership.
  • Ro.co: presented as best for patients who prioritize access to the FDA-approved Wegovy pill or an injection-free oral option, and for those who value pricing disclosure before completing consultation; treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Final verdict and choosing between Sunlight vs Ro.co

Sunlight vs Ro.co come down to tradeoffs documented in their materials: Sunlight promotes a $88/mo starting plan with injections and oral options, same-price-forever messaging, and free 1-2 day shipping; Ro.co offers a $39 entry point and unique access to the FDA-approved Wegovy pill when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate, with disclosed pricing before consultation. Regulatory notes for both providers stress differences between FDA-approved branded medications and compounded products.

Choose Sunlight if…

  • You want a promoted monthly GLP-1 plan starting at $88/mo and value Sunlight’s landing-page claims of “Same price forever,” free 1-2 day shipping, no monthly membership, and injections plus oral options.

Choose Ro.co if…

  • You need access to the Wegovy pill or prefer an FDA-approved oral GLP-1 option and want the lower $39 get-started entry; remember treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and ongoing branded pricing can vary.

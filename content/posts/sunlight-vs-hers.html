How Sunlight vs Hers differ: 4 key distinctions

1. Starting price and pricing structure

Sunlight lists Personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo and highlights a “Same price forever” guarantee and promotions like “As low as $88.” Hers lists Wegovy® from $149/mo and additional price points for injectable or other brand options. Hers also documents that an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required and that total cost includes both membership and medication charges; Sunlight materials state no monthly membership and cancel anytime.

2. Medication type: compounded versus FDA-approved brand-name

Sunlight promotes “Personalized GLP-1” with injections and oral options but does not state brand-name only. Hers explicitly offers FDA-approved Wegovy® and states no compounded medications are offered; the source notes Wegovy® and Zepbound® are FDA-approved for weight loss and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

3. Care model and follow-up

Hers highlights a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins. Sunlight describes a 100% online medical visit and clinician adjustments in landing-page testimonials, but does not present the same explicit membership-based ongoing check-in model as Hers in the captured evidence.

4. Shipping, membership and cancellation

Sunlight states free shipping in 1-2 days, no monthly membership, cancel anytime, and promotional messaging including free discreet 2-day shipping and price promotions. Hers notes medication is not available without an active membership and that membership auto-renews, with total cost including membership and medication when prescribed.

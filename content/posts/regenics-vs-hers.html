Medication type and regulatory status

Regenics provides compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by U.S. licensed compounding pharmacies. The source explicitly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Hers offers FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy® and other named GLP-1s and states it does not offer compounded products. This is a primary distinction for people weighing regulatory status.

Pricing structure and membership

Price presentation differs sharply. Regenics lists semaglutide starting at $139 first month and tirzepatide at $189 first month, and the source warns promotional pricing applies to the first month only with higher renewal rates. Hers lists Wegovy® from $149/mo and shows membership requirements: an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required and billed separately, with a first-month membership fee and auto-renewal at a higher monthly rate. The Hers source states medication is not available without an active membership.

Clinical oversight and care model

Both providers require a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness. Regenics advertises nurse-practitioner-led GLP-1 care with nurse practitioners reviewing intake before prescribing. Hers emphasizes a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins and clinician review. The sources show Regenics centers on nurse-practitioner oversight and a compounding pharmacy network, while Hers emphasizes ongoing dose management and a multidisciplinary team.

Additional therapies and services

Regenics specifically offers Sermorelin peptide therapy as an add-on, described as a 15mg injectable peptide therapy priced at $399 in the captured FAQ. Hers does not offer compounded medications and focuses on brand-name GLP-1 access with dose check-ins. If a secondary peptide option matters, only Regenics lists that offering in the captured evidence.

Regenics vs Hers: final assessment

Regenics is best known from the source as a mid-pack, cash-pay telehealth option with compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide starting at $139 and $189 for the first month, nurse-practitioner oversight, and a Sermorelin add-on. Hers is presented as a brand-name, FDA-approved option offering Wegovy® from $149 but requires an active membership for medication access and emphasizes a personalized care team and ongoing dose check-ins. Choose based on whether you prioritize compounded pricing and a peptide add-on or FDA-approved brand medications and membership-based care.

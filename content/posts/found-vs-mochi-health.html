Found vs Mochi Health: both telehealth options support GLP-1 access, but they differ sharply in clinical approach, pricing structure, and how prescriptions are delivered. Early verdict: choose Found if you want a biology-first, medically supervised program that works with insurance and starts as low as $39; choose Mochi Health if you want live video doctor visits and a membership-plus-medication model starting at $99.

Overview comparison: clinical model, visit style, and pricing

Both platforms can prescribe GLP-1 medications, including compounded options, but the experience and cost framing differ. Found emphasizes a biology-first, personalized medically supervised weight loss program tailored to hormones, metabolism, sleep, and behavior. Mochi Health emphasizes synchronous live video visits with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes and a membership-plus-medication structure with pricing from $99/month.

Key difference 1: Clinical approach and visit model

Found builds programs around an individual’s metabolic biology and offers personalized medically supervised plans rather than a single medication protocol for all patients. Mochi Health uses live video visits with a licensed physician in real time, which Mochi positions as fundamentally different from the asynchronous text review most competitors use. Both require provider review and clinical eligibility for prescriptions.

Key difference 2: Pricing structure and starting costs

Found advertises affordable monthly plans with a get-started price as low as $39 and membership options that may start at other tiers; Found also notes advertised pricing may involve 12 month commitments for some branded GLP-1s and that copays, coinsurance, and deductibles may apply. Mochi Health highlights a membership-plus-medication pricing model starting from $99/month for standard membership and compound medication starting points (for example compounded semaglutide starting $99, with promotional first-month discounts available).

Key difference 3: Insurance, coverage, and availability

Found states that most insurance is now accepted, handles insurance paperwork, and promotes programs like a Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program that may help eligible Part D members access brand-name GLP-1s for as little as $50/month; Found also notes insurance coverage, pricing, and terms vary and eligibility is determined by program criteria. Mochi operates primarily on a direct-pay membership-plus-medication model and indicates insurance coverage is inconsistent, so most patients pay out of pocket; Mochi also states it provides reliable GLP-1 prescription access in all 50 states.

Key difference 4: Medication types and regulatory notes

Both vendors offer compounded GLP-1s. Found lists tirzepatide and semaglutide, including compounded options, and explicitly states compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved, are not evaluated for safety or efficacy, and are not available in all states. Mochi lists compounded tirzepatide, compounded semaglutide (injectable and oral), and other compounded options, and includes the same regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Other practical differences

Found emphasizes a holistic whole-body approach targeting hormones, metabolism, sleep, and behavior and highlights cancel-anytime affordable monthly plans and insurance handling. Mochi emphasizes rapid access to a live physician visit, 24/7 support, ongoing appointments, and promotional pricing such as 50% off the first month with a code and lower compound medication entry points. Both note that individual results vary and that prescriptions are subject to clinician review and clinical eligibility.

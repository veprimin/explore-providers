BetterMe Rx vs JoinSkinny: both sell clinician-reviewed online GLP-1 programs, but they differ on compounding, pricing structure, and ongoing support. Early verdict: Choose BetterMe Rx if you want multiple compounded injectable and needle-free oral GLP-1 formats with lower per-month equivalents on longer prepaid plans. Choose JoinSkinny if you prefer flat-rate dosing, branded or FDA-regulated pharmacy fills, and ongoing monthly support subject to state availability.

How they differ at a glance

The primary differences between BetterMe Rx and JoinSkinny are medication source and format, pricing model and billing commitment, pharmacy handling and regulatory notes, shipping and support, and state availability and monitoring. The sections below compare those points directly using only the supplied evidence.

Medication type and preparation

BetterMe Rx offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and oral formats through licensed U.S. providers. The source repeatedly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny provides GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and works with licensed U.S. pharmacies that prepare authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1+GIP when prescribed. JoinSkinny’s copy states prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners. Choose based on whether you prefer compounded products or pharmacy-prepared, FDA-regulated fills.

Pricing structure and commitments

BetterMe Rx lists monthly prices and lower 3, 6, or 12-month equivalent prices with the lowest rates requiring a 12-month plan. Examples include semaglutide injection $205 monthly or as low as $145/month on a 12-month plan, tirzepatide injection $275 monthly or as low as $215/month, oral semaglutide $250 monthly or as low as $185/month, and oral tirzepatide $265 monthly or as low as $215/month. BetterMe Rx emphasizes no membership fee, no insurance required and cancel anytime language appears alongside prepaid longer-term plans.

JoinSkinny uses flat-rate pricing so the monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases and offers monthly and 3, 6, and 12-month bundles. JoinSkinny advertises plans starting at $149 and notes a promotional $140 discount on its landing page. JoinSkinny highlights cancel anytime before the next billing cycle and installment options like Afterpay, Klarna, and Affirm. The two differ mainly in that BetterMe Rx shows lower per-month equivalents for prepaid longer plans while JoinSkinny stresses flat-rate monthly pricing and flexible payment options.

Shipping, timing, and support

Both vendors advertise free 1 to 2 day shipping. BetterMe Rx states medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies and free 1 to 2 day shipping are included. JoinSkinny advertises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days and same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time with next-day UPS delivery in a temperature controlled package. JoinSkinny also lists ongoing support with dose adjustments, monthly check-ins, secure telemedicine and 24/7 chat and claims continuous medical support and monitoring from licensed providers.

State availability, eligibility, and clinical review

BetterMe Rx requires that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. JoinSkinny provides care in all eligible U.S. states where its licensed providers operate and where telehealth-based prescribing is allowed, and says entering your state during signup will confirm availability. Both require clinician review and eligibility confirmation prior to prescribing.

Regulatory and safety notes

Both sources include a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny additionally states its pharmacy partners are FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified and that prescriptions filled by those partners are prepared fresh and shipped securely. BetterMe Rx repeatedly emphasizes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

What this means for you

If lower prepaid per-month pricing on multiple compounded formats matters and you are comfortable with compounded products that are not FDA-approved, BetterMe Rx documents lower 12-month equivalents and both injectable and needle-free oral options. If you prefer consistent flat-rate monthly pricing, branded or FDA-regulated pharmacy fills, same-day processing before 2 pm central and ongoing clinician monitoring with 24/7 chat, JoinSkinny documents those features and state-based eligibility checks during signup.

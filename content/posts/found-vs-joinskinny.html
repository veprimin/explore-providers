How Found and JoinSkinny differ at a glance

Both offer telehealth weight-management services and access to GLP-1 options, but their approaches, pricing models, and operational details diverge. This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences: treatment model and personalization, pricing and insurance, medication sourcing and regulatory notes, shipping and delivery, and cancellation and commitments.

Treatment model and personalization

Found takes a biology-first approach, conducting a thorough medical evaluation and building a personalized program around each patient s metabolic biology, including hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavior. JoinSkinny describes clinician-reviewed telemedicine with a quick health questionnaire and licensed providers prescribing personalized treatment plans, emphasizing GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options with dose adjustments guided by providers.

Pricing, insurance, and starting costs

Found advertises affordable monthly plans and says you can get started for as low as $39. Found states most insurance is now accepted and that it handles insurance paperwork for eligible patients. JoinSkinny uses transparent flat-rate pricing with plans starting at $149. JoinSkinny notes no insurance is required and offers monthly and multi-month bundles plus installment options. JoinSkinny also advertises a promotional Save $140 offer in captured landing copy.

Medication sourcing and regulatory notes

Found lists tirzepatide and semaglutide and notes compounded GLP-1s in its materials, with an explicit regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Found also states certain medications are FDA-approved for conditions other than weight and that Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility. JoinSkinny states prescriptions are filled by licensed, U.S. based, FDA regulated and LegitScript certified pharmacy partners and that some offerings include GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP. JoinSkinny s materials also include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Shipping, delivery, and customer support

JoinSkinny promotes free temperature controlled shipping with next day or 1 2 day arrival for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, shipped via UPS in temperature controlled packaging and tracked. JoinSkinny also highlights 24 7 chat, monthly check ins, and dose adjustments guided by providers. Found references plans and insurance assistance but captured landing evidence emphasizes handling insurance paperwork and Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program details for eligible Part D members.

Cancellation, commitments, and payment terms

Found advertises affordable monthly plans and cancel anytime language in short bullets, while detailed landing evidence mentions advertised pricing for branded GLP-1s via insurance with 12 month commitment in some offers and that terms vary. JoinSkinny explicitly states No Monthly Membership and Cancel Anytime, and offers monthly and 3, 6, and 12 month bundles that require confirmation of bundle commitments. Both providers present refund or offer details that vary by plan.

TrimRx vs Mochi Health: Overview and core models

Both platforms provide telehealth access to GLP-1 and related medications, but their delivery models differ. TrimRx centers on a compounded medication program with Lifetime Price Protection and no membership. Mochi Health combines a membership-plus-medication model with synchronous live video visits and nationwide prescription access.

Top differences to evaluate

1) Pricing structure and promotions

TrimRx advertises weight-loss plans starting at $149/month and promotional messaging including a $140 discount; it emphasizes Lifetime Price Protection so you pay the same price on every dose. Mochi Health presents membership-plus-medication pricing that starts from $99/month and a promotion of 50% off your first month with code BESTWLHALFOFF. Mochi also lists compounded semaglutide starting $99 $60 and tirzepatide starting $199 $90 in promotional terms. TrimRx states no monthly membership and cancel anytime, while Mochi requires a membership that affects ongoing cost.

2) Clinical visit style and speed

Mochi Health’s defining feature is live video visits with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes, offering a real-time consultation experience. TrimRx uses telehealth visits included with its program; the source notes TrimRx conducts 100% online medical review with U.S. licensed providers but contrasts with Mochi’s synchronous video model.

3) Medication type, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 options. TrimRx’s primary program provides compounded medications prepared and shipped by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy and explicitly states these compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. Mochi also sells compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide products and includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. TrimRx notes it may alternatively write prescriptions for branded medications but does not sell or ship branded products directly; patients fill those prescriptions separately.

4) Shipping and logistics

TrimRx advertises free shipping that arrives in 1–2 days. Mochi Health describes doctor-prescribed treatments shipped to your door but does not claim the same 1–2 day free shipping promise in the supplied evidence. Shipping and state eligibility details are noted by each provider as subject to their policies.

5) Support, scale, and additional services

TrimRx highlights 100k+ customers and a 4.8 Excellent Rating along with 24/7 support and HSA/FSA eligibility claims. Mochi highlights 24/7 support too and broader telehealth services beyond weight loss, including dermatology and hair care, and emphasizes ongoing appointment availability throughout the program.

How to choose based on priorities

If predictable, flat pricing and fast free shipping are your top priorities, TrimRx centers its program on Lifetime Price Protection and free 1–2 day shipping for compounded GLP-1 starting at $149/month. For patients who prioritize a live video conversation with a physician and a membership model that bundles ongoing access, Mochi Health offers bookable live video visits in about 5 minutes and membership-plus-medication pricing starting from $99/month with a 50% first month promotion.

Safety and regulatory notes

Both providers rely on compounded medication programs in some offerings and include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. TrimRx explicitly calls out that compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and that branded prescriptions, if written, must be filled separately by patients.

At a glance: core differences between Sesame Care and Oak Longevity

Both platforms sell GLP-1 weight loss care online, but they differ in how care is delivered, how medications are priced and sourced, and what additional services are included. The most distinctive supported differences are clinician choice and scheduling, subscription versus non subscription pricing, branded versus compounded or oral medication offerings, insurance acceptance, and shipping or coaching inclusions.

Clinician model and visit format

Sesame Care: a telehealth marketplace model lets patients browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing, offering ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging. Experience can vary by clinician.

Oak Longevity: clinical review is asynchronous with no video call required. Board certified physicians in your state review every case. Oak notes the whole process happens online with no video call needed.

Pricing model and starting costs

Sesame Care: subscription care available with ongoing video visits, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12 month Success by Sesame commitment. Cash pay GLP-1 medications are listed as low as $149/mo for semaglutide and $299/mo for tirzepatide, with price with insurance noted as low as $25 for some medications. Medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on your insurance status and pharmacy.

Oak Longevity: semaglutide from $133/mo and tirzepatide from $199/mo with one flat price that covers all dosages. Oak advertises no subscriptions, no membership fees, and billing on a non recurring basis so you choose to renew payment each month without penalty. Oak states three month prescriptions are billed upfront and no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped.

Medication types, regulatory notes, and dosing

Sesame Care lists branded options and prices, including semaglutide and tirzepatide dosing examples and mentions Wegovy pill and Zepbound KwikPen as FDA approved products. Sesame Care also includes regulatory language that medication specific approval status must be confirmed and branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Oak Longevity offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and explicitly notes that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Oak states these are compounded versions of active ingredients in branded products.

Shipping, coaching, and extra services

Sesame Care highlights flexible scheduling, a large clinician network, and nationwide access in many states. It emphasizes easy booking and broad treatment coverage. Sesame Care indicates medication costs will vary with insurance and pharmacy.

Oak Longevity includes free health coaching and free shipping with tracking with every plan and advertises same day approval available for qualifying patients. Oak also emphasizes no hidden membership fees.

Who each provider is best for

Sesame Care is best for people who want broad telehealth choice, flexible appointments, and insurance acceptance for weight loss medications. Oak Longevity is best for cost conscious patients with an uncomplicated health history who want the most affordable compounded GLP-1 online with no video call or insurance requirement.

Sesame Care vs Oak Longevity decision note: Sesame emphasizes clinician choice and insurance acceptance while Oak emphasizes low flat cash prices, no subscription, and compounded medications.

Final guidance: Choose based only on the supplied facts

Choose Sesame Care if you want to pick your clinician, access ongoing video visits, labs and messaging, and may use insurance for weight loss medications. Choose Oak Longevity if you prefer one flat cash price for all dosages, free shipping and coaching, no subscription, and are willing to use compounded medications that are not FDA approved.

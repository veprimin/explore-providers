1. Price and pricing protections

TrimRx lists compounded GLP-1 from $149/month and GLP-1+GIP from $249/month. TrimRx also promotes Lifetime Price Protection so you lock in today’s price for life and pay the same price on every dose. Omzo lists semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279 and frames plans as clinician-guided. The sources state that Omzo pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change. TrimRx advertises a promotional $140 discount in captured landing content. Both sources preserve qualifiers about changes and limitations.

2. Clinical oversight and how prescriptions are handled

Both TrimRx and Omzo connect patients with licensed U.S. providers who determine eligibility and prescribe medications. Omzo emphasizes clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed U.S. providers and notes that providers make the final determination as to whether to prescribe any medication. TrimRx states prescription and telemed visits are included and that the platform prescribes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide through an online medical review with U.S. licensed providers. Both sources note that branded medications may be prescribed as a traditional prescription which patients must fill separately.

3. Shipping and delivery claims

TrimRx specifically states free 1–2 day shipping in the captured material. Omzo states fast, discreet delivery but does not specify a 1–2 day timeline in the captured content. Both note shipping policies and that medication production and shipping are handled through pharmacy partners or compounding facilities described in their material.

4. Medication types and regulatory notes

Both platforms offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide as primary programs and both explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Each provider also says clinicians may write prescriptions for branded medications, but neither sells, dispenses, or ships branded products directly according to the captured material. TrimRx emphasizes a compounded medication program along with an alternative for branded prescriptions filled separately by the patient. Omzo explains clinical services are provided by third party networks of U.S. licensed clinicians and that compounded products are produced in FDA-registered facilities but are not FDA-approved finished products.

Bottom line and how to choose

TrimRx vs Omzo comes down to four clear differences supported by the sources: starting price, whether a lifetime price lock is offered, stated shipping speed, and how each frames clinician guidance. TrimRx: lower starting price, Lifetime Price Protection, 1–2 day free shipping and telehealth visits included. Omzo: clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed providers, slightly higher starting prices, and fast discreet delivery.

Choose TrimRx if you want the lower starting price, a Lifetime Price Protection promise, and stated free 1–2 day shipping. Choose Omzo if you prioritize clinician-guided plans explicitly framed as reviewed by licensed U.S. providers and want to confirm eligibility, pharmacy, and renewal terms before enrolling.

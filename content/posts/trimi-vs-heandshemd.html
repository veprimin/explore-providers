Trimi vs He & She MD: Pricing and renewal structure

Direct comparison: Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/mo and tirzepatide from $125/mo with flat pricing that stays the same as dose increases. He & She MD lists semaglutide injections from $139/mo and tirzepatide from $199/mo as six-month introductory rates that renew at higher six-month prices. The key difference is that Trimi emphasizes a single flat monthly price at every dose, while He & She MD offers lower intro pricing for six months that then renews at a higher rate.

Support and coaching: built-in app vs clinician network

He & She MD bundles a coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific guidance and progress tracking at no extra cost. Trimi emphasizes clinician oversight through Arora Health and licensed U.S. providers but does not present an included coaching app in the captured evidence. If ongoing app-based coaching and messaging are important, He & She MD explicitly includes those features; Trimi focuses on clinician-prescribed compounded medication and medical oversight.

Shipping and fulfillment

Both providers ship medication to patients, but the advertised shipping details differ. Trimi highlights free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping on its plans. He & She MD provides free expedited shipping and no membership fee, with medication shipped straight to your door. The explicit distinction is Trimi calling out overnight temperature-controlled shipping and He & She MD describing expedited shipping with no membership fee.

Medication sourcing and regulatory notes

Both companies rely on compounded GLP-1 medications. Trimi states its medications are compounded by state-licensed 503A pharmacies with certificates of analysis available on request and notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. He & She MD says its medications are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies and likewise notes compounded GLP-1s are not reviewed by FDA for safety, efficacy or quality. Both vendors include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Price transparency and guarantees

Trimi advertises a 6-month money-back promise: follow your plan for 6 months and request a refund if you don’t lose weight, with terms applied as stated on its site. He & She MD emphasizes six-month introductory pricing that renews higher and includes explicit renewal examples showing increased costs after the promo term. The comparison shows Trimi offering a specific refund promise tied to a 6-month period and He & She MD offering promotional pricing that requires budgeting for a higher renewal rate.

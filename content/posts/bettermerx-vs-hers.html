How the models differ: compounded products versus FDA-approved brands

BetterMe Rx sells compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in both injectable and oral formats through licensed U.S. providers. Hers offers FDA-approved Wegovy, a brand-name semaglutide option, and explicitly does not offer compounded medications.

Pricing structure and billing

BetterMe Rx lists lower monthly-equivalent pricing on longer prepaid plans. Hers shows Wegovy from $149/mo for medication only, but an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required; the membership is billed separately and medication is not available without an active membership.

Care model and ongoing support

BetterMe Rx provides a licensed U.S. provider review and ships compounded medication with supplies and free 1 to 2 day shipping. Hers highlights a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins around FDA-approved brand options such as Wegovy, and notes that prescriptions are subject to clinician review and state availability.

Comparing Sesame Care vs JoinFridays: Sesame Care emphasizes a marketplace model with clinician choice, flexible appointments, and insurance for weight loss medications, while JoinFridays emphasizes compounded GLP-1 options, no long-term contracts, and lower entry pricing. Early verdict: choose Sesame Care if you want broad clinician choice and insurance support; choose JoinFridays if you want compounded options and the lowest starting monthly price.

Quick contrast: how these services differ

This comparison highlights the most distinctive supported differences between Sesame Care and JoinFridays: clinician choice and marketplace model, pricing and insurance handling, compounded versus brand availability, membership terms and cancellation, and program features like ongoing visits and coaching. Every claim below is drawn from the supplied source material.

Clinician choice and care model

Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing, giving users control over their care relationship. JoinFridays offers personalized, clinician-guided care as well, but its emphasis is on delivering compounded or brand medications through a clinician review rather than a marketplace browsing model.

Pricing, insurance, and starting cost

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59 per month based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment, with cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149 per month and insurance accepted for weight loss medications. JoinFridays starts at $117 per month, highlights compounded GLP-1 options, and advertises no long-term contracts; JoinFridays also publishes multi-month plans that reduce the effective monthly rate.

Compounded medications and branded options

JoinFridays provides compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide options and notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sesame Care highlights brand-name options and price lines for semaglutide and tirzepatide with both cash-pay and insurance prices listed, and a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Membership terms, promos, and cancellation

JoinFridays markets no membership fees, cancel anytime, and active promo codes that may reduce costs. Sesame Care requires a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment for its cited “as low as $59/mo” offering; results and milestones are tied to that commitment and a cited study. JoinFridays explicitly notes flexible cancellation and that promo code availability can change.

Both sources include regulatory cautions: JoinFridays states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sesame Care includes a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Conclusion: Sesame Care vs JoinFridays

Sesame Care vs JoinFridays comes down to choice and insurance versus compounded access and flexibility. Sesame Care is built as a marketplace for selecting clinicians and lists both cash-pay and insurance prices, including brand-name semaglutide and tirzepatide price points and a 12-month commitment for its lowest subscription rate. JoinFridays emphasizes no long-term contracts, the lowest starting monthly price in its tier at $117, compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide availability, and active promo codes that can lower short-term cost.

Choose Sesame Care if you want broad telehealth choice, the ability to browse and select clinicians, and platforms that accept insurance for weight loss medications. Choose JoinFridays if you want compounded GLP-1 options, the lowest advertised starting monthly price, no long-term contract, and aggressive promo codes.

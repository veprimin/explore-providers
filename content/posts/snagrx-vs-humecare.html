Pricing and billing: lowest starting price versus bundled protocol

SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and compounded tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan, with month-to-month prices of $99 and $149 respectively. Those $69 and $119 figures require the 12-month commitment and are described as the 12-month plan rates. By contrast, HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/month (marked down from $299) and a tirzepatide protocol from $299/month (marked down from $399).

Early verdict on cost

For predictable, bottom-of-market compounded pricing on an annual plan, SnagRx is materially cheaper. For a higher-priced, bundled program that includes additional services, HumeCare+ charges more but includes extra elements not in SnagRx.

Formulation and supportive ingredients

SnagRx offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide only; its marketing emphasizes compound GLP-1 products and notes these are not FDA-approved finished products. HumeCare+ also offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide but positions its protocol as bundled with glycine and vitamins B6 and B12 added into the formulation to address muscle support and side effects.

Clinician review, monitoring and app features

SnagRx routes medical care through OpenLoop Health, a network of US-licensed clinicians that it names, and states clinician review typically within five hours and always within 24. SnagRx notes clinician coverage in 48 states. HumeCare+ advertises physician-prescribed medication with continuous physician optimization, unlimited clinician access and a dedicated app for medication tracking and food logging, plus monthly body-composition scans that track lean mass versus fat.

Shipping, refunds and guarantees

Both providers state next-day shipping in temperature-controlled, discreet packaging. SnagRx says approved orders ship next-day via UPS or FedEx, and a declined prescription is refunded in full. SnagRx also states a full refund if the prescription is declined and advertises a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms applied. HumeCare+ offers a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months and says to verify full terms and conditions directly with HumeCare+ because eligibility and documentation requirements apply.

Safety and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded medications dispensed by U.S. pharmacies. Each source states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HumeCare+ additionally notes that GLP-1 drugs carry serious risks, including a boxed warning for thyroid C-cell tumors, and both providers reference clinician assessment and exclusionary criteria applied by their prescribers.

Conclusion: Choose based on priorities

SnagRx vs HumeCare+: choose SnagRx if your priority is the lowest compounded GLP-1 price on a 12-month plan and a rate that is locked flat at every dose, with named US 503A pharmacies and quick clinician review. Choose HumeCare+ if you prefer a bundled protocol that includes glycine, B6 and B12 for muscle and symptom support, monthly body-composition scans, a dedicated app and continuous clinician access. Both use compounded medications that are not FDA-approved; compare the guarantees and terms before enrolling.

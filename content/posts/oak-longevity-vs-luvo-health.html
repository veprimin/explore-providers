How Oak Longevity and Luvo Health differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: published starting prices and pricing model, clinical review and access, medication format and compounding, support and shipping, and stated eligibility and regulatory notes. Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications and both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Pricing and pricing transparency

Oak Longevity publishes starting prices: semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month, with one flat price that covers all dosages and no subscriptions or membership fees. Oak states three-month prescriptions are billed upfront and no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped.

The page captured did not publish current details or verified prices.

Clinical review, access and enrollment

Oak Longevity: the process is online with no video call required; Oak says board-certified physicians in your state review every case and the clinical review is asynchronous. Oak also notes same-day approval is available for qualifying patients. Luvo Health: the site describes clinician-reviewed online weight-management treatment with ongoing support and dose changes, but captured details advise confirming eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment because live offers determine final terms.

Medication types and compounding status

Both providers reference compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options. Oak specifically markets compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide as one flat price for all dosages and labels them as compounded versions of the active ingredients in Ozempic/Wegovy and Mounjaro/Zepbound, prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies. Luvo’s captured content also notes compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and are not reviewed or approved by the FDA, and it lists tirzepatide injection, semaglutide injection and semaglutide tablets among its formats, with informational guides and microdosing resources.

Support, shipping and program features

Oak includes free health coaching and free shipping with tracking as part of its offering and states there are no hidden membership fees. Oak advertises free shipping, free health coaching and that billing is monthly on a non-recurring basis so you are not auto charged and choose to renew payment each month. Luvo highlights ongoing support and dose changes across month 1, month 2 to 3 and 3 plus months, and notes cancel anytime and dose adjustments based on clinical response, but specific shipping, coaching and price details were not published on the captured page.

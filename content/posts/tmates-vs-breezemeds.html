Medication types and formulation options

TMates is presented as offering clinically dosed compounded semaglutide formulated in U.S. facilities. BreezeMeds lists both injectable and oral GLP-1 options: semaglutide injection, tirzepatide injection, and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide. BreezeMeds therefore shows a broader set of delivery forms and both semaglutide and tirzepatide in listings, while TMates is focused on compounded semaglutide in the captured evidence.

Regulatory and compounding notes

Both sources include caveats about compounded medications. TMates’ evidence includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. BreezeMeds similarly indicates that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Both platforms require confirmation of eligibility and medical appropriateness by clinicians.

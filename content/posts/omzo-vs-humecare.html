Omzo vs HumeCare+: what differs most

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive, supported differences between Omzo and HumeCare+. Each point below compares both providers directly using only the supplied facts.

1. Pricing and plan starting points

Omzo lists personalized compounded semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279. HumeCare+ lists compounded semaglutide from $199 per month and a tirzepatide protocol from $299 per month. HumeCare+ presents monthly pricing and promotional markdowns in captured FAQ language, while Omzo states starting prices without the same monthly framing in the provided evidence.

2. Protocol support and bundled services

Omzo emphasizes clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed U.S. providers and offers both a compounded medication program and the option for a prescription for branded products that the user would fill elsewhere. HumeCare+ sells a protocol that wraps physician-prescribed compounded GLP-1 medication together with glycine, vitamins B6 and B12, monthly body composition scans, an app for tracking, and clinician access intended to address muscle loss and side effects.

In short, Omzo centers on clinician-guided access to medications and delivery, while HumeCare+ positions a broader supportive scaffold around the medication itself.

3. Compounded medication status and clinician role

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medication options dispensed through U.S. pharmacies, and both captured sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Omzo specifically notes that compounds are prepared by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy and that the platform connects patients with licensed medical providers who make final prescription decisions. HumeCare+ also describes physician-prescribed compounded medications dispensed from U.S. pharmacies licensed and inspected by state Boards of Pharmacy.

4. Outcomes, guarantees, and timelines

Omzo materials advise that individual results may vary and do not guarantee weight loss, and Omzo states providers make the final determination on prescriptions. HumeCare+ advertises a 10%-or-money-back promise over six months with terms to verify, and it reports internal, company-reported headline results and timelines such as a claim that some users see body weight changes in 10 months based on internal analysis. The HumeCare+ source explicitly cautions that testimonials and internal figures do not guarantee specific results and that individual results may vary.

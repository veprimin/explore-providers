Scope of services and care model

Bodybuilding Health+ is presented as a multi-program telehealth platform covering GLP-1 weight management, longevity/optimization (NAD+ and sermorelin), and sexual performance treatments, all delivered online with medical oversight by a named clinician. Eden Health focuses on a guided GLP-1 program that pairs medication, care, and support under a single monthly price and notes that most programs stop at the prescription.

Medication type, regulatory notes, and clinician review

Both providers require a licensed clinician to determine if treatment is medically appropriate. Bodybuilding Health+ offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and includes an explicit regulatory note: compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Eden Health states medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable; the captured content also reiterates clinician eligibility requirements.

Commercial terms: memberships, shipping, cancellation

Bodybuilding Health+ repeatedly states no monthly membership, no hidden fees, and cancel anytime; it highlights provider-reviewed medications, no insurance required, and shipment to your door. Eden Health emphasizes no insurance needed, no membership fees, free expedited shipping, and a lifetime price guarantee for its listed monthly prices.

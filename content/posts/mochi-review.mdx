## Mochi Health Review — The Live-Video Difference

Most GLP-1 telehealth runs asynchronously: you fill in a questionnaire, a clinician reviews it on their own time, and a prescription follows. Mochi Health inverts that. Its defining feature is a **live video visit with a doctor**, bookable in about five minutes — the closest thing in this category to an in-person physician appointment.

## About Mochi Health

Mochi is a licensed telehealth platform offering **GLP-1 prescription access in all 50 states**, with synchronous video consultations, 24/7 support, and ongoing appointment availability throughout your program. A licensed physician evaluates your case during the video call before any prescription is issued, which is a meaningfully different clinical experience from a text-only intake. The medication focus is compounded GLP-1 (semaglutide and tirzepatide), so the same compounded-versus-branded distinction applies here as elsewhere.

## How It Works

You book a live video appointment — often available within minutes — and discuss your health history, goals, and medication options with a physician in real time. If GLP-1 therapy is appropriate, the clinician prescribes it, and follow-up appointments and 24/7 support remain available as you progress. The real-time conversation is the point: you can ask questions and have your treatment plan explained directly, rather than waiting on asynchronous messages.

Semaglutide is FDA-approved for chronic weight management, and clinical research summarised by the [NIH](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8859692/) shows meaningful average weight loss under supervised GLP-1 therapy — the kind of supervision Mochi's model is built to provide.

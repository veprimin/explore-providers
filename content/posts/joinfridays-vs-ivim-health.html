Key differences at a glance: price, protocol, reviews

JoinFridays vs IVIM Health differ most on starting cost and promotional structure, their clinical approach to titration and optimization, and third-party satisfaction signals. Both prescribe compounded GLP-1 medications and include clinician review prior to prescription. Regulatory safeguards are similar: compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Clinical approach and outcomes claims

JoinFridays emphasizes personalized, clinician-guided care with dosing guidance and clinician review. It offers both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide and notes tirzepatide is associated with higher average weight loss ranges versus semaglutide, citing approximate ranges but framing offerings as compounded products not manufactured by Fridays.

IVIM Health promotes a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol and cites published internal analyses stating Ivim patients following that protocol lost an average of 46% more weight over 68 weeks compared to another published clinical trial. IVIM also claims its GLP-1 ID may accelerate weight loss by up to 40% compared to standard protocols, and notes these are not head-to-head randomized trials and that results may vary.

Third-party ratings and evidence

JoinFridays does not include third-party rating claims in the supplied evidence. IVIM Health highlights a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot, described as a rare dual verification signal in this market, and supports clinical outcomes with retrospective observational studies cited on its site. Both providers caution that compounded medications are not FDA-reviewed.

Membership, cancellation, and program fees

JoinFridays states no membership fees and the ability to cancel at any time, positioning itself for patients who want flexible commitments. IVIM offers a free consultation for new patients and a promotional free month with code GIFT75 but lists program fees in some pricing lines and an ongoing rate after initial offers.

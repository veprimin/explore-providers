How Omzo vs YourEra differ at a glance

The most distinctive differences between Omzo and YourEra are price and pricing model, whether branded prescriptions are available, what is bundled with treatment, and shipping speed and handling. Below we compare these points side by side using only the supplied facts.

1. Price and pricing model

Omzo lists semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279. YourEra advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/month and tirzepatide from $199/month on the 6-month plan, with the lowest monthly rates described as as low as $79.20/month reflecting the 6-month plan in some display text. YourEra emphasizes flat pricing at every dose and notes the lowest advertised rates require a 6-month commitment; shorter plans cost more per month.

2. Medication options and prescriptions

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide through clinician review. Omzo describes a Compounded Medication Program as its primary program and states providers may alternatively write a prescription for branded medications such as Ozempic or Wegovy, noting Omzo does not sell or ship branded medications and that prescriptions for branded drugs require the patient to fill and pay the pharmacy. YourEra dispenses compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide only and explicitly lists that it does not offer branded Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, or Zepbound.

Check Omzo medication details

3. What is bundled and clinical oversight

YourEra markets an all-inclusive bundle on its 6-month plan: provider consult, prescription, compounded medication, cold shipping, dose adjustments, and 24/7 support included with no membership fee. YourEra also notes prescriptions are issued only if a licensed clinician determines treatment is appropriate after evaluation. Omzo emphasizes clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed U.S. providers; clinical services are provided by third party networks of U.S.-licensed clinicians who determine eligibility and make final prescription decisions. Omzo stresses that the assessment on its website does not create a doctor patient relationship and providers determine eligibility based on medical history and provider assessment.

Both platforms require clinician review and do not guarantee prescriptions. YourEra bundles consult and shipping; Omzo may prescribe branded drugs but does not dispense branded drugs directly.

4. Shipping and speed

YourEra advertises free, discreet delivery from licensed US pharmacies with free 1 to 2 day shipping. Omzo states fast, discreet delivery and that compounded medications are prepared and shipped by a state licensed sterile compounding pharmacy; specific transit times are not supplied in the evidence. Both note medications are shipped from pharmacies but Omzo clarifies compounded drugs offered are not FDA approved.

Conclusion: Omzo vs YourEra

Omzo vs YourEra comes down to priorities.

2. Program scope and clinician oversight

Bodybuilding Health+ presents a broader multi-program platform: GLP-1 weight management, Longevity & Optimization (NAD+ and sermorelin), and Sexual Performance options. It highlights medical oversight led by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D. FeelGood Meds focuses on personalized GLP-1 treatments with clinician review and a weight loss money back guarantee mentioned on its page. Both require a licensed clinician to determine if treatment is medically appropriate.

3. Shipping, membership and refund claims

Bodybuilding Health+ explicitly states no monthly membership, no hidden fees, and cancel anytime; you pay only for your treatment program. FeelGood Meds also states no membership fee, free shipping, and a weight loss money back guarantee. Those statements are presented as the providers do on their pages.

4. Formulations and availability notes

Bodybuilding Health+ offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide plus NAD+, sermorelin, and ED options like tadalafil and sildenafil starting at listed prices. FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections and tablets and an optional branded medication. Both platforms say no insurance is required in the materials captured.

5. Regulatory and safety disclosure

Both providers note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Bodybuilding Health+ explicitly states compounded GLP-1s are prepared by licensed U.S. pharmacies but have not been evaluated or approved by the FDA. FeelGood Meds includes the same regulatory note about compounded medications.

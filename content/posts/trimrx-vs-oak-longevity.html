TrimRx vs Oak Longevity: price models and what you pay

The clearest difference in TrimRx vs Oak Longevity is pricing structure. TrimRx advertises weight-loss plans starting at $149/month for compounded GLP-1 and $249/month for GLP-1+GIP, with a Lifetime Price Protection policy that locks in today’s price for life. Oak Longevity lists semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month with a one price for all dosages and no subscription required. Both providers note that compounded medications are not FDA approved.

What those price claims mean

TrimRx: starting at $149/month and Lifetime Price Protection means TrimRx states you pay the same price on every dose and avoid dose-based increases. Oak Longevity: semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month and one flat price covers all dosages, billed monthly on a non-recurring basis unless you choose to renew.

Clinical access, review process, and support

TrimRx includes telehealth visits and prescription as part of its program and advertises 24/7 support. Oak Longevity performs an asynchronous online health questionnaire reviewed by a licensed physician in your state and notes no video call is required. Oak emphasizes same-day approval is available for qualifying patients. Both require a clinician to determine medical appropriateness.

Shipping, cancellations, and billing differences

TrimRx advertises free 1–2 day shipping and highlights no monthly membership and the ability to cancel anytime. Oak Longevity offers free shipping with tracking, bills monthly on a non recurring basis, and notes three month prescriptions are billed upfront and refunds are not available after physician approval and shipment. Each provider includes eligibility and state availability details on their sites.

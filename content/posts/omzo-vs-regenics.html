Pricing and promotional structure

Price is one of the clearest differences in Omzo vs Regenics. Omzo lists semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279. Regenics advertises semaglutide at $139 for the first month and tirzepatide at $189 first month, with stated renewals of $199 and $279 respectively. Regenics notes that the lower amounts apply to the first month and that promotional pricing applies to the first month only.

Clinical oversight and prescribing model

Omzo vs Regenics differ in the clinician who reviews and prescribes care. Omzo describes clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed U.S. providers and states clinical services are provided by third party networks of U.S.-licensed clinicians who determine eligibility and make final prescribing decisions. Regenics is described as nurse-practitioner-led GLP-1 care, with licensed nurse practitioners reviewing intake before anything is prescribed and overseeing every prescription.

Medication sourcing, regulatory notes, and fulfillment

Both providers offer compounded medications that are prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies and both include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Omzo explicitly states its compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and that it also may provide prescriptions for branded medications for patients to fill at their pharmacy. Regenics states its compounded medications are prepared by U.S. licensed compounding pharmacies and shipped free to your door and notes its pharmacy network is LegitScript certified.

Shipping, extras, and notable features

Omzo highlights fast, discreet delivery and says plans are FSA and HSA eligible. It also emphasizes that Omzo does not sell, dispense, or ship branded medications when prescriptions for branded products are written. Regenics advertises free shipping for medications compounded in the USA, no insurance required to start treatment, FSA and HSA eligibility, and an additional Sermorelin peptide therapy offering for recovery, energy, and muscle support.

The Gala vs JoinSkinny comparison shows two direct-pay telehealth options for GLP-1 care. Early verdict: JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149 and offers temperature-controlled, next-day shipping and clinician-reviewed plans; Gala emphasizes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with a flat all-inclusive price and a $220 promotion plus free two-day shipping.

How they differ at a glance: pricing, medication type, shipping and care model

This side-by-side comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported in the source: starting price and promotions, whether compounds or branded options are emphasized, shipping type and timing, and details of clinical oversight and pharmacy handling. Both require a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness and both state compounded medications are not FDA approved when offered.

1) Starting price and promotions

JoinSkinny advertises plans starting at $149 and a $140 promotional discount, with monthly and 3, 6, and 12 month bundles. Gala page adverts a $220 off promotion and promotes one flat all-inclusive price covering provider visit, medication, shipping and ongoing care, but the base price was not exposed in readable page text. Neither source guarantees long term pricing beyond the captured offer language.

View JoinSkinny plans from $149

2) Medication type and pharmacy details

Gala promotes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. The source repeatedly notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny offers GLP-1 and GLP-1 plus GIP options and states prescriptions are filled by U.S. based, FDA regulated and LegitScript certified pharmacy partners and that branded prescriptions are filled and paid for separately. JoinSkinny also notes compounds contain the same active molecules used in brand-name medications when prepared by licensed pharmacies.

3) Shipping speed and handling

Gala advertises free two-day shipping straight to your door. JoinSkinny advertises free temperature controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days and details same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time and next-day UPS in a temperature controlled package with tracking and insured shipments. JoinSkinny explicitly mentions weekend shipping handled Monday for temperature management and insured replacement rules when incorrect shipping information is provided.

Check Gala shipping and care details

4) Clinical oversight, support and membership

Both platforms include a provider visit and prescription as part of the service and state no monthly membership or membership fees. Gala highlights provider check-ins and dose monitoring included and ongoing care as part of the flat price. JoinSkinny describes licensed U.S. providers reviewing questionnaires, personalized treatment plans, ongoing support, dose adjustments, monthly check ins, and 24/7 chat, and states care is delivered by licensed U.S. providers and that information is protected under HIPAA.

Final take on Gala vs JoinSkinny

Both services are direct-pay telehealth options that include a provider visit and prescription. Choose based on what matters most: JoinSkinny provides explicit starting pricing from $149, temperature controlled next-day shipping with pharmacy certifications and more detailed shipping logistics and support. Gala emphasizes a flat all-inclusive price with a $220 promotion, compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide offerings, and free two-day shipping though the base price was not visible in the captured page text. Both remind users that compounded medications are not FDA approved and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Coaching, clinician review, and support

Shed integrates continuous coaching into treatment rather than simply issuing a prescription. The platform positions coaching as part of the bundled monthly cost and highlights help through titration and behavioral support. Direct Meds advertises clinician review and ongoing clinician and nursing support but does not bundle a separate membership; it emphasizes clinical review, supplies, and shipping included with treatment.

Pricing, promotions, and transparency

Shed lists compounded semaglutide starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month, and advertises 10% off your first month. Shed does not publish standard pricing for every plan and provides personalized quotes after intake. Direct Meds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and lists standard FAQ pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide; it also notes a summer promotion and clarifies subscribe-and-save terms for discounted plans.

Shipping, cancellation, and refund policies

Direct Meds explicitly includes medication, visits, supplies, and free next-day UPS or FedEx temperature-controlled shipping, and states month-to-month cancellation is available with subscribe-and-save plans requiring the stated term. Shed indicates medication is shipped after clinician review and that coaching is integrated; specific shipping and refund mechanics are provided after intake or in terms and conditions.

Regulatory and safety notes

Both providers offer compounded medications and include the same regulatory qualifier: compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Shed also highlights that name-brand options are FDA-approved while compounded options are not. Both platforms require a licensed clinician to determine whether treatment is appropriate.

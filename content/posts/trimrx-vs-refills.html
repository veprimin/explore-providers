TrimRx vs Refills: which telehealth weight-loss program fits you better? Short verdict: Refills is the lower-cost flat-price option starting at $99/month, while TrimRx starts at $149/month and emphasizes Lifetime Price Protection, faster 1–2 day shipping, and a 100k+ customer track record.

How pricing and price protection compare

Price is the clearest difference in this TrimRx vs Refills comparison. Refills advertises flat pricing with GLP-1 from $99/month and states the same price at every dosage. TrimRx lists weight-loss plans starting at $149/month and promotes Lifetime Price Protection so you “lock in today’s price for life” and pay the same price on every dose. Both providers describe no membership or app fees.

Medication type, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both TrimRx and Refills offer compounded GLP-1 products and note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not evaluated by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. TrimRx explicitly says its primary program provides compounded medications prepared and shipped by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy and also offers prescriptions for branded medications that patients must fill separately. Refills states its GLP-1s are offered exclusively from U.S. pharmacies and that compounded medications may be prescribed when medically appropriate.

Speed, shipping, and fulfillment

Shipping promises differ in detail. TrimRx advertises free 1–2 day shipping. Refills promises free discreet express shipping and indicates you will receive shipping and tracking information in 2 business days after the prescription is processed. Both advertise free shipping and month-to-month plans that can be canceled anytime.

Support, credentials, and scale

TrimRx highlights scale and user ratings, stating 100k+ happy customers and a 4.8 Excellent Rating along with unlimited access to a care team and 24/7 support. Refills emphasizes 24/7 health concierge support, unlimited clinician access for questions, and notes partner clinicians and licensed U.S. compounding pharmacies with third-party USP testing and Legitscript certification for its pharmacy partners.

Availability and clinician review

Refills states availability in all 50 US states and describes a health intake quiz followed by clinician review; depending on needs, you may chat with a doctor or receive a prescription within 24 hours. TrimRx lists eligibility and state/jurisdiction questions on its site but captured evidence does not list a complete state-by-state availability table in the supplied source.

What to expect from care and prescriptions

Both platforms bundle clinician review and prescriptions into a telehealth workflow. Refills describes a published flow: intake quiz, clinician review, potential chat with a doctor or prescription within 24 hours, monthly refill form, and clinician consultations at no extra cost. TrimRx states prescriptions and telemed visits are included, with compounded injections prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies and an option to receive prescriptions for branded medications that patients must fill separately.

Conclusion: TrimRx vs Refills — which to choose

In this TrimRx vs Refills comparison the decision rests on priorities:

  • Choose Refills if you prioritize lower advertised starting cost and flat pricing at every dosage, broad 50-state availability, and fast express shipping with concierge support.
  • Choose TrimRx if you value a larger stated customer base and ratings, Lifetime Price Protection that locks in today’s price for life, and explicit 1–2 day shipping claims with included telemed visits.

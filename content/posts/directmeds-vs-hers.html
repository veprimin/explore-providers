How Directmeds vs Hers differ at a glance

Directmeds offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats and advertises treatment starting at $147 with clinician review, supplies and free next-day UPS/FedEx shipping included. Hers offers FDA-approved Wegovy® and other brand-name GLP-1s from $149/mo and states medication is not available without an active Hers Weight Loss Membership, which is $39 for the first month and auto-renews at $149/month thereafter. Compounded medications at Directmeds are not FDA-approved. Directmeds is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. Hers notes its brand products are FDA-approved for weight loss when indicated and that not all products are available in all 50 states.

Key differences: regulatory status, medication formats and membership

Regulatory status and medications

Directmeds supplies clinician-reviewed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Hers promotes FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy® and Zepbound® for weight loss and notes Ozempic® is FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes and may be prescribed off-label for weight loss at a provider’s discretion. Hers emphasizes it does not offer compounded medications.

Formats and product types

Directmeds lists injectable and sublingual compounded options for semaglutide and tirzepatide. Hers focuses on brand-name options such as Wegovy® available as a pill offering from $149/mo and injectable brand options from $199/mo and up, with brand availability and dose options varying by product.

Pricing structure and membership

Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and includes medication, clinician review, telehealth visits, supplies and free shipping. Its FAQ lists standard pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide, and it states there is no separate monthly membership fee. Directmeds also notes month-to-month cancellation and that subscribe-and-save plans require a stated term. Hers lists products from $149/mo and states that an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required, with a $39 first month charge that auto-renews at $149/month; membership and medication are billed separately and medication is not available without an active membership. Hers also notes price statements such as from $149/mo typically refer to medication only if prescribed.

Shipping, cancellation and geographic availability

Directmeds advertises free temperature-controlled UPS or FedEx delivery generally in 1 to 2 days and free next-day shipping on its landing-page evidence. Directmeds says it is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. Hers notes that brand products are not available in all 50 states and that state availability and clinical eligibility are determined by a licensed healthcare provider. Both providers state that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

2. What each monthly fee covers

Both providers present a transparent monthly price, but the composition differs. altRx states the monthly fee includes medical oversight, medication, syringes, prep pads, and step-by-step guidance and notes buy now pay later options through Affirm, Klarna, and Afterpay. HermanRx emphasizes genuinely all-inclusive pricing with no membership fee, no consultation fee, ongoing support, and free discreet shipping; HermanRx specifically advertises the provider review, prescription, medication, and follow-up support as included.

3. Timeline from intake to delivery

HermanRx publishes a faster, more specific timeline: roughly a 5-minute assessment, provider review within about 24 hours, and delivery in about 72 hours if prescribed, while its broader FAQ lists 3 to 7 business days depending on pharmacy processing. altRx cites typical processing and delivery of approximately 4 to 5 business days after approval. If speed from approval to door matters, HermanRx publishes a faster turnaround in its marketing; altRx gives a multi-business-day timeline.

4. Medication type, regulatory note, and dosing details

Both platforms offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide in certain plans and explicitly note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HermanRx also advertises compounding with vitamin B12 for its GLP-1 plans. altRx describes prescriptions filled by state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies in its network and emphasizes clinician review and personalized care plans when treatment is appropriate.

5. Availability and platform differences

Each provider states availability in all 50 U.S. states. altRx notes provider availability and prescribing authority vary by state and that a licensed provider will review evaluations and may conduct telehealth visits live or asynchronously. HermanRx highlights a network of U.S. licensed, board-certified physicians across multiple categories and the ability to consolidate other treatments on one platform.

Final comparison and how to choose

In short: altRx vs HermanRx comes down to cost versus bundled features and speed. altRx positions itself as the lower-cost, limited-offer option with buy now pay later and a 100% online process starting at $89/mo. HermanRx positions itself as an all-inclusive, slightly higher-priced service with B12 included, no separate consultation fee, and a published faster review and delivery timeline.

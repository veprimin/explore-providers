Quick comparison: what differs most between MedVi vs bmiMD

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: medication type offered, starting price and multi-month pricing, online process and clinic visits, shipping and support, and regulatory notes about compounded medications.

Medication type and dosing options

MedVi offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only. bmiMD offers compounded tirzepatide, a GLP-1/GIP option. Both use compounded medication models; both sources state compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Price structure and multi-month plans

MedVi advertises a winter promotional starting price of $179 for the first month, including fast, free shipping, with an ongoing standalone month at $299 and a three-month plan at $649 (about $216/month). bmiMD lists tiered subscription pricing with four billing options: $179 month-to-month; $159 for three months; $149 for six months; and $139 for a 12-month plan, with free shipping on every subscription order. bmiMD emphasizes lower long-term monthly equivalents but notes the lowest price requires a longer commitment and a total prepaid charge should be confirmed.

Process, clinician review, and visits

MedVi operates a simple online intake with no clinic visits needed; an OpenLoop Health clinician reviews cases after checkout and retains prescribing discretion. bmiMD provides clinician-reviewed online treatment and 24/7 clinician access; treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Both state medication is prepared at licensed pharmacies and shipped to your door.

Support, shipping, and guarantees

MedVi highlights 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and fast, free shipping for the promotional month. bmiMD also offers 24/7 clinician access and free subscription shipping. bmiMD states a “100% Risk Free” refund if not eligible for the program, described as 100% refundable if not eligible for program. MedVi notes a guarantee phrase on their site but specifies eligibility and insurance/copay determinations occur at time of visit.

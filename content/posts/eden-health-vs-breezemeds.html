Pricing and transparency

Eden Health lists semaglutide at $99 per month and tirzepatide at $129 per month and emphasizes a lifetime price guarantee, no insurance required, and no membership fees. Choosing between them

Choose Eden Health if you prioritize lower published monthly starting prices for injectables, a stated lifetime price guarantee, and explicit free expedited shipping with no membership fees.

Choose BreezeMeds if you need or prefer providers that display both injectable and oral GLP-1 options and value discreet home delivery as presented in the captured evidence.

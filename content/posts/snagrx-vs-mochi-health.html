1. Pricing structure and commitment

SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month as 12-month plan rates and states the rate is locked flat at every dose and at renewal. The sources note month-to-month equivalents are $99 and $149. Mochi Health uses a membership-plus-medication model that the source describes as starting from $99/month, with a 50% off first month promotion using code BESTWLHALFOFF and a promotional first month rate example at $39 for some offers. SnagRx is presented as bottom-of-market on the annual compounded plan price; Mochi prioritizes membership features and an initial promotional price rather than a single locked-in medication rate.

2. Clinical model and visit experience

SnagRx operates with an OpenLoop Health clinician network and an intake that the source says is reviewed typically within five hours and always within 24. This is an asynchronous telehealth workflow where a clinician meets the patient after checkout to decide on eligibility. Mochi Health distinguishes itself by offering live video visits bookable in about 5 minutes, described as a synchronous conversation where a licensed physician evaluates the case in real time. If a live video with an immediate physician is important, Mochi provides that; if faster asynchronous review and a named clinician network are priorities, SnagRx emphasizes quick clinician review and screening.

3. Geographic coverage and pharmacies

Mochi Health states reliable GLP-1 prescription access in all 50 states. SnagRx states its OpenLoop Health clinician network covers 48 states and names its four US 503A partner pharmacies. SnagRx highlights named US 503A partner pharmacies as a differentiator and emphasizes next-day UPS/FedEx temperature-controlled shipping. Mochi emphasizes nationwide access and ongoing appointment availability plus 24/7 support.

4. Compounded medication status and regulatory notes

Both sources make clear they focus on compounded medications and include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. SnagRx explicitly states its compounded medications are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but are not FDA-approved finished products and encourages prospective users to speak with their provider about risks. Mochi also displays the same regulatory note for compounded medications.

5. Shipping, fulfilment, and guarantees

SnagRx states approved orders ship next-day via UPS or FedEx in discreet, temperature-controlled packaging and that a declined prescription is refunded in full. SnagRx also mentions a full refund if the prescription is declined and a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms. Mochi states doctor-prescribed treatments are shipped to your door and highlights ongoing support and appointment availability. Specific shipping timing and guarantee details are described more explicitly in the SnagRx source.

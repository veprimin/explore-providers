Quick comparison: Sesame Care vs Yucca Health

This comparison follows the sources for each provider. Key differences are clinician choice and marketplace model, pricing structure and insurance acceptance, compounded versus branded medication access, approval and shipping speed, and program features such as payment options. Five standout differences

  • Clinician selection: Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and choose clinicians; Yucca Health assigns licensed providers but highlights licensed providers in all 50 states and a physician-led review process.
  • Pricing model and lowest advertised starting rate: Sesame Care lists ongoing care starting at $59/mo* and cash-pay semaglutide as low as $149/mo; Yucca Health lists compounded semaglutide from $146/month and tirzepatide from $258/month.
  • Insurance and payment options: Sesame Care states insurance is accepted for weight loss medications and medication costs vary with insurance; Yucca Health does not accept health insurance but offers Klarna, Afterpay, and Affirm and multi-month plans.
  • Medication type and regulatory note: Yucca Health offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and explicitly notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products; Sesame Care offers branded and cash-pay options and includes regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed.
  • Speed and logistics: Yucca Health advertises fast 24-hour approvals and UPS 2-Day Air shipping; Sesame Care emphasizes flexible scheduling and a large clinician network with nationwide access in many states.

Pricing and payment: what each source actually says

Sesame Care presents a subscription for ongoing care as low as $59/mo* and lists cash-pay semaglutide starting at $149 and tirzepatide from $299 with insurance prices noted as low as $25 depending on coverage. The source emphasizes medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on insurance and pharmacy. Yucca Health advertises compounded semaglutide from $146/month and compounded tirzepatide from $258/month, offers 6-month plans, and provides Buy Now Pay Later options including Klarna, Afterpay, and Affirm. Yucca Health does not accept health insurance per the source.

Medication sources and regulatory notes

Yucca Health explicitly states its compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved finished products and are prepared by compounding pharmacies; the source says clinical outcomes are expected to be comparable when dosed equivalently but notes the FDA does not review compounded medications. Sesame Care materials include branded medication options and a general regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Program features and logistics

Sesame Care highlights a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states, framing the experience as a marketplace where patients can choose clinicians. Yucca Health highlights fast 24-hour approval, online intake, UPS 2-Day Air shipping, licensed providers in all 50 states, and financing options to split payments. The user experience will differ depending on whether you value picking a clinician or prioritizing quick online approval and lower compounded prices.

How to decide: the concise guidance

Choose Sesame Care if you want the ability to browse and select from a large clinician network, flexible appointments, and potential insurance coverage for weight loss medications. Choose Yucca Health if your priority is one of the lowest advertised monthly rates for compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide, fast 24-hour approval, and Buy Now Pay Later financing with UPS 2-Day Air shipping. Both platforms require clinician review and have distinct medication sourcing models; compounded medications from Yucca Health are not FDA-approved finished products and Sesame Care notes medication approval status must be confirmed.

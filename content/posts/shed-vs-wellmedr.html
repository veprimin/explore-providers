1. Medication offerings and regulatory notes

Shed: Offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded alternatives. The source explicitly states Shed provides name-brand and compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options. Shed’s materials include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a provider’s prescription.

Wellmedr: Offers compounded GLP-1 medications exclusively from licensed U.S. pharmacies, with the same regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy. Wellmedr lists semaglutide and tirzepatide among available medications and notes specific product starting points in captured landing-page evidence.

2. Cost and pricing structure

Shed: Advertises compounded semaglutide injections starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month, and a promotional 10% off your first month. Shed does not publish standard pricing across all plans and provides personalized quotes after intake; coaching is bundled into the monthly cost rather than charged separately.

Wellmedr: Lists GLP-1 plans starting at $89/month, with landing-page evidence that semaglutide plans can start lower on multi-month plans. Wellmedr promotes lifetime locked-in pricing and explicitly highlights no dose-based price increases in its captured materials.

3. Coaching and care model

Shed: Emphasizes continuous coaching integrated into treatment, not just a prescription. The source repeats that coaching is bundled and meant to support titration, lifestyle changes and plateaus. Shed positions this as a differentiator versus prescription-only competitors.

Wellmedr: The captured evidence centers on pricing guarantees, same-day review and shipping assurances rather than integrated coaching. The source does not provide captured evidence that Wellmedr bundles continuous coaching into every treatment plan.

4. Approval speed, shipping and guarantees

Shed: Uses a 2-minute approval assessment to check eligibility; a clinician review determines whether treatment is appropriate before a prescription is issued. Shed mentions a weight-loss guarantee with a Terms and Conditions reference for details.

Wellmedr: Highlights same-day review and free overnight shipping in the captured bullets and promotes on-time refills and a money-back guarantee statement on the landing page. Wellmedr also references a new-customer offer and lifetime discounts in captured evidence.

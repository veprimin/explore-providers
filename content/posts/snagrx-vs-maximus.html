SnagRx vs Maximus: pricing and billing structure

The two providers differ most obviously on price and plan commitment. SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan; month-to-month rates are $99 and $149 respectively. Maximus lists semaglutide at $132.99/month as a flat rate. Both operate on direct-pay telehealth models and do not bill insurance.

How commitments affect cost

SnagRx requires a 12-month plan to access the $69 and $119 rates; its monthly option is more expensive. Maximus presents a single low flat rate of $132.99/month with no published annual discount. The requirement to commit to 12 months for SnagRx is an explicit qualifier in its pricing.

Price lock and dose escalations

One distinctive difference is SnagRx’s advertised price lock. SnagRx states the rate is locked flat at every dose and at renewal, so the enrolled rate does not rise as the dose escalates during titration. Maximus advertises a low flat rate but does not publish a dose-specific price-lock claim in the supplied evidence.

Pharmacies, clinicians, and state coverage

Both providers use compounded medication filled through U.S. pharmacies and telemedicine prescribers, but they name different operational details. SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and its clinician network, OpenLoop Health, and states clinician coverage across 48 states. Maximus states prescriptions are approved by licensed doctors and filled through trusted U.S. pharmacies, and its Weight Loss Protocol is available in all 50 states except Mississippi.

Clinician networks and turnaround

SnagRx states OpenLoop clinicians typically review intake within five hours and always within 24, and approved orders ship next-day via UPS or FedEx in temperature-controlled packaging. Maximus highlights licensed doctors approving prescriptions and free expedited shipping but does not publish the same review-time claim in the supplied evidence.

Shipping, delivery, and included services

Both platforms include shipping with the stated monthly price. SnagRx advertises next-day UPS/FedEx shipping and free shipping that arrives in 1-2 days for approved orders. Maximus lists free priority or expedited shipping as part of its program. Neither provider bills insurance and both include telemedicine prescription processes rather than in-person visits.

Guarantees, outcomes, and regulatory notes

SnagRx promotes a full refund if a prescription is declined and a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms. Maximus states medication costs will be fully refunded if you are not approved. Both providers supply compounded medications; the supplied regulatory language notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

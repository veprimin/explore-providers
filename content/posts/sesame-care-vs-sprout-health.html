Top differences at a glance: Sesame Care vs Sprout Health

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: pricing and discounts, insurance and medication type, clinician selection and onboarding speed, outcome framing and evidence references, and ongoing subscription structure. The analysis compares each point directly, preserving qualifiers and limitations from the source material.

1) Pricing, discounts, and ongoing costs

Sesame Care: Subscription services including ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging start at $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Cash-pay GLP-1 medication prices are listed as low as $149/mo for semaglutide and $299/mo for tirzepatide, with price-with-insurance figures as low as $25 depending on insurance and pharmacy. Medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary.

Sprout Health: Markets a $50 off first month promotion, bringing the initial month to $199, with a stated ongoing rate of $249/month after the discount. The source notes the post-discount rate is higher than several competitors.

2) Insurance acceptance and medication type

Sesame Care: The source explicitly states insurance is accepted for weight loss medications and provides both cash-pay and in-network price points, noting medication costs vary by insurance status and pharmacy. The listing includes branded and named formulations such as semaglutide and tirzepatide with price-with-insurance examples.

Sprout Health: Operates on a direct-pay model per the captured content; the detailed description and FAQ say Sprout does not bill insurance and prescribes compounded semaglutide. The source notes compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Medication-specific approval status must be confirmed for both providers.

3) Clinician selection versus speed of onboarding

Sesame Care: Described as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing, offering more control over the care relationship and flexible scheduling across a large clinician network.

Sprout Health: Emphasizes lightning-quick approval and a low-friction online intake with rapid prescription review and home delivery, positioning itself for patients who want to start quickly rather than shop among clinicians.

4) Outcome framing and evidence references

Sesame Care: Presents milestone phrasing tied to a study of injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg in non-diabetic adults with obesity or overweight when paired with healthy lifestyle changes, noting results may vary and medication costs are separate from subscription fees.

Sprout Health: Frames outcomes around a 10–26% body weight reduction over 12 months based on GLP-1 clinical outcome literature, while explicitly stating individual results vary and that Sprout does not offer a formal outcome guarantee in the captured content.

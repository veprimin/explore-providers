Head-to-head: five key differences

This SkinnyRx vs Oak Longevity comparison uses only captured provider facts to compare distinctive differences: price and pricing model, medication format and options, payment and eligibility features, clinician review and access, and shipping and fulfillment details.

1. Price and pricing model

Oak Longevity lists semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month with one flat price for all dosages and no subscriptions or membership fees. SkinnyRx lists daily compounded semaglutide tablets as low as $249/mo and highlights payment flexibility such as split payments with Affirm. Oak advertises one price covers all dosages and bills monthly on a non-recurring basis; SkinnyRx presents an as low as monthly price and mentions subscription service options that can be canceled at any time.

2. Medication format and options

SkinnyRx emphasizes daily oral semaglutide tablets taken under the tongue as an alternative to injections. Oak Longevity offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and advertises both options, with semaglutide from $133/mo and tirzepatide from $199/mo. Both providers note compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

3. Payment features and eligibility

SkinnyRx specifically mentions FSA/HSA eligibility and the ability to use pre-tax dollars toward treatment. Oak emphasizes no subscriptions, no membership fees, and billing that is non-recurring so you choose to renew payment each month. Oak also notes three-month prescriptions are billed upfront and that no refunds are available after physician approval and shipment. SkinnyRx states plans are offered as a subscription service that can be canceled at any time and details a refund policy tied to prescription review completion.

4. Clinical review and access

Both operate via online consultations and require a licensed provider to prescribe. Oak states no video call is required and that clinical review is asynchronous with board-certified physicians in your state reviewing every case. SkinnyRx states prescriptions are available only if prescribed after an online consultation with a healthcare provider and that clinicians tailor treatment and monitor progress; clinician review is required and prescriptions are subject to provider review.

5. Shipping and fulfillment

SkinnyRx advertises free overnight shipping on all orders and states after prescription is written, medication is processed and shipping and tracking information is provided within 2 business days. Oak Longevity advertises free shipping with tracking and that most patients receive medication within a few days of approval, with same-day approval available for qualifying patients.

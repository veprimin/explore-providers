Five key differences that matter

1. Protocol scope and supportive ingredients

HumeCare+ explicitly bundles glycine and vitamins B12 and B6 into its GLP-1 formulation and positions those additives as muscle support and side-effect relief. bmiMD does not advertise those supporting ingredients and focuses on compounded tirzepatide alone.

2. Body-composition tracking

HumeCare+ includes monthly body-composition scans that the company says track lean mass versus fat mass. bmiMD does not list body-composition scans as part of its offering in the supplied materials.

3. Pricing structure and commitments

HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199 per month and tirzepatide with added nutrients from $299 per month. bmiMD lists tirzepatide from $139 per month but that lowest price requires a 12-month plan; shorter plans raise the monthly equivalent to $149, $159, or $179.

4. Refunds and guarantees

HumeCare+ advertises a 10% or your money-back promise over six months, with eligibility and terms to verify directly. bmiMD states a 100 percent refund if a customer is not eligible for the program, and emphasizes a risk-free claim for eligibility refunds in the captured content.

5. Delivery and clinician access

Both providers advertise free shipping on subscriptions and 24/7 clinician access. HumeCare+ calls out free next-day delivery and a U.S. care team available 24/7. bmiMD highlights free subscription shipping and online refills delivered to your door.

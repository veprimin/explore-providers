How they differ: price model, medication type, and shipping

The core differences between JoinFridays and HealthRx center on pricing structure, whether the lowest price requires a prepaid commitment, compounded medication notes, and shipping. JoinFridays advertises starting at $117/month with no long-term contracts and active promo codes. HealthRx advertises from $99/month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) and notes the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan.

Pricing and commitment

JoinFridays: Starting at $117/month, with standalone one-month and multi-month pricing disclosed. It states no membership fees and cancel anytime with no long-term contract. The captured FAQ lists one-month, three-month, six-month, and twelve-month examples and notes longer commitments lower effective monthly rate.

HealthRx: From $99/month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal). It explicitly says the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan and lists tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month.

Medication options and regulatory notes

Both platforms offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide options and include regulatory disclaimers: compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinFridays advertises both compounded and name-brand options on its pages, but makes clear compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved. HealthRx likewise states compounded medication is prepared per prescription and is not FDA-approved.

Clinician review and dosing

JoinFridays: Treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate; the platform emphasizes personalized, clinician-guided care and dosing guidance. HealthRx: The clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is the right fit or whether neither is. HealthRx also highlights dose adjustments and that plan price does not change when your dose does.

Shipping and fulfillment

JoinFridays landing-page evidence includes pricing and membership details but captured evidence does not specify a shipping method. HealthRx lists overnight cold shipping with cold chain, tracked, free on every plan and notes compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom. If shipping speed and cold-chain tracking are priorities, HealthRx documents overnight cold shipping.

Conclusion: Choose based on commitment, shipping, and counseling

JoinFridays vs HealthRx comes down to whether you prioritize flexible, cancel-anytime membership and a $117 starting price or a lower prepaid monthly rate with explicit overnight cold shipping and prepaid terms. Both require clinician review and both offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with regulatory disclaimers.

Choose JoinFridays if you want flexible cancellation, personalized clinician-guided care, and a starting price advertised at $117/month.

Choose HealthRx if you want the lowest advertised prepaid semaglutide rate from $99/month with overnight cold shipping and a 12-month prepaid structure.

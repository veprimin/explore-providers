How Sesame Care vs Trimi differ at a glance

This comparison uses only captured facts. Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients choose clinicians, offers ongoing video visits, labs, messaging and accepts insurance for weight loss medications. Trimi prescribes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide through licensed U.S. clinicians, emphasizes flat pricing that does not rise with dose, and provides free overnight temperature-controlled shipping.

Care model and clinician choice

Sesame Care operates as a marketplace that lets patients browse and select providers based on specialty, availability, and pricing. That means experience can vary by clinician but gives you more control over your care relationship. Trimi uses a clinical network overseen by Arora Health and a board-certified medical director; licensed providers prescribe compounded medications and the medical oversight network handles reviews.

Pricing structure and what is included

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo* on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Medication costs are separate and vary by insurance status and pharmacy; cash-pay GLP-1s are listed as low as $149/mo for semaglutide and $299/mo for tirzepatide with specified dose tiers and some in-network prices listed as low as $25 with insurance. Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/mo and compounded tirzepatide from $125/mo on annual plans, with the same price at every dose and one flat membership covering medication, clinician visits, ongoing check-ins and free shipping.

Medication type and regulatory notes

Sesame Care offers branded oral and injectable GLP-1 options including semaglutide and tirzepatide with dose and brand specifics captured, noting medication costs depend on insurance and pharmacy. The source includes a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Trimi prescribes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by state-licensed 503A pharmacies; the source explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Shipping, guarantees, and membership terms

Trimi highlights free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and a 6-month money-back promise: follow your plan for 6 months and, if you don’t lose weight, you can request a refund subject to terms. Trimi also states if a patient is not approved by their medical team they offer a 100 percent automatic refund. Sesame Care notes subscription features and pricing but captured evidence specifies medication costs are not included in subscription prices and will vary by insurance and pharmacy; no shipping or money-back guarantees were supplied for Sesame Care in the captured evidence.

What the evidence supports about results and claims

Sesame Care cites milestones based on a study of injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg paired with lifestyle changes, noting results may vary. Trimi references peer-reviewed clinical trials behind the active ingredients but cautions that figures are not guarantees of individual results. Both sources preserve variability and do not promise outcomes.

Final thoughts: Sesame Care vs Trimi

Sesame Care is best if you want broad telehealth choice, the ability to browse and select clinicians, flexible scheduling and insurance acceptance for weight-loss medications. Trimi is best if you want flat compounded pricing that does not rise with dose, free overnight shipping, and explicit refund terms tied to eligibility and results. Both platforms require medical review and have distinct regulatory notes about compounded versus branded products.

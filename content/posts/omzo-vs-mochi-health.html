Omzo vs Mochi Health: pricing model and starting costs

Price structure is a major practical difference. Omzo lists semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279, with medication costs included in fees collected by Omzo. Mochi Health presents a membership-plus-medication model starting from $99/month, with promotional offers such as 50% off the first month and examples like compounded semaglutide starting $99 and compounded tirzepatide starting $199. Mochi also notes a promotional first month rate of $39 for some treatments for new customers while standard monthly membership may be $79 after the promotion depending on state terms.

Visit style and clinical interaction

Omzo connects users with licensed U.S. clinicians through its platform; clinical services are provided by third party networks of U.S.-licensed clinicians who determine eligibility and prescribe at their discretion. Mochi Health distinguishes itself with synchronous live video visits bookable in about 5 minutes, allowing a real-time conversation with a physician who evaluates the case during the visit. Omzo emphasizes clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed providers; Mochi emphasizes live video appointments and ongoing appointment availability.

Medication sourcing and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded medication options and both include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Omzo specifically describes a Compounded Medication Program prepared and shipped by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy and notes that compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. Mochi lists multiple compounded options including weekly injections and an oral semaglutide pill, and likewise states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

State access, shipping, and support

Mochi Health claims reliable GLP-1 prescription access in all 50 states and 24/7 support plus ongoing appointments. Omzo notes clinician networks of U.S.-licensed providers and mentions state coverage, pharmacy capacity, and order details can affect availability and rates may vary; Omzo advises confirming eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

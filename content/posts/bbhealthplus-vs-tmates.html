How Bodybuilding Health+ vs TMates compare at a glance

This comparison focuses on five distinctive differences supported by the source: price structure, drug selection, clinical access and coverage, membership and cancellation terms, and regulatory notes about compounded medications.

Price structure and billing: starting vs flat pricing

Bodybuilding Health+ lists compounded semaglutide starting at $179/mo and tirzepatide from $209/mo with bundle pricing for multi-month plans. TMates advertises a flat $199/month price that stays the same at every dose, with free shipping noted. Both providers allow cancel anytime, but BBHealthPlus emphasizes no monthly membership or hidden fees while TMates emphasizes no step-up fees as dosage escalates.

Drug selection and program breadth

Bodybuilding Health+ offers a broader set of programs: compounded semaglutide (from $179/mo), compounded tirzepatide (from $209/mo), NAD+ and sermorelin starting at $129/mo, and sexual performance options starting at $96/mo. TMates focuses on compounded semaglutide clinically dosed and highlights semaglutide as its core GLP-1 offering; TMates has less public detail on tirzepatide availability.

Clinical access, oversight, and coverage

TMates advertises 24/7 access to U.S.-based doctors and licensed coverage in all 50 states with virtual check-ins. Bodybuilding Health+ states medical oversight led by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D., and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate; it also notes not available in all U.S. states. TMates emphasizes ongoing physician availability; Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes provider-reviewed personalized plans under licensed clinicians.

Nationwide access vs select states

TMates claims licensed coverage in all 50 states. Bodybuilding Health+ notes it is not available in all U.S. states, and physician approval is required.

Membership, shipping, and cancellation policies

Bodybuilding Health+ repeatedly states No Monthly Membership and Cancel Anytime and highlights transparent pricing with no hidden fees. TMates also offers cancel anytime and includes free shipping; TMates frames its model as a flat monthly rate with no step-up fees and direct-pay options.

Regulatory and safety notes on compounded medications

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide. The sources explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Bodybuilding Health+ additionally notes compounded medications are prepared by licensed U.S. pharmacies and dispensed when clinically appropriate.

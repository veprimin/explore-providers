3. Clinical support and monitoring

MedVi highlights a simple online process with no clinic visits and 24/7 support from certified medical professionals. Gala provides provider check-ins and dose monitoring included, with the provider visit and prescription included in its flat-price package. Both platforms operate via direct-pay telehealth and include clinician review and prescription where medically appropriate, but MedVi emphasizes around-the-clock certified support and Gala emphasizes scheduled provider check-ins with included visits.

4. Shipping, membership, and cancellation terms

MedVi states fast, free shipping for the first-month promotional price and says no membership or hidden fees; it also shows a three-month plan option. Gala advertises free two-day shipping, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime language. Both stress no insurance is needed for direct-pay compounded medication, and Gala specifically lists no monthly membership and cancel anytime in the captured content.

5. Regulatory and safety notes

Both sources include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. MedVi includes additional notes that compounded GLP-1s are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but the medications themselves are not FDA-approved, that prescriptions are at the discretion of clinicians, and that results vary and may be influenced by diet, exercise, clinician recommendations, and adherence. Gala repeats the regulatory note and includes patient testimonial-style weight-loss examples as captured marketing content.

How they differ at a glance

The most distinctive differences between Directmeds and Gala are pricing transparency and promotions, shipping speed and packaging claims, membership and cancellation terms, and how each frames regulatory and dose pricing. Below we compare those differences directly using only the supplied facts.

1. Pricing structure and visible rates

Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and lists standard FAQ pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. Gala advertises a promotional discount of $220 off and promotes one flat all-inclusive price covering provider visit, medication, shipping and ongoing care; the page did not expose a clear base monthly price in readable text. Both present promotions, but only Directmeds supplies specific standard FAQ dollar amounts.

2. Shipping and delivery promises

Directmeds advertises free temperature-controlled UPS or FedEx delivery generally in 1–2 days and specifically mentions free next-day shipping. Gala advertises free two-day shipping straight to your door. Directmeds highlights temperature-controlled packaging; Gala emphasizes two-day delivery without that specific temperature claim in the captured text.

3. Membership, cancellation and subscription terms

Both platforms state there is no monthly membership. Directmeds states month-to-month cancellation is available and that subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term. Gala states cancel anytime and claims no monthly membership and same price at every dose. Directmeds also notes a full refund if a prescription is not approved; Gala’s captured page highlights a flat price and promotional discount but the captured FAQ was not expanded.

4. Medication formats and clinical oversight

Both Directmeds and Gala offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and require that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Directmeds specifically lists injectable and sublingual formats and ongoing clinician and nursing support. Gala lists provider check-ins and dose monitoring included and a provider visit and prescription as part of the flat price claim.

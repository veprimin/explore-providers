1. Program philosophy and personalization

Found emphasizes a biology-first approach: it says it conducts a thorough medical evaluation and builds a personalized program around metabolic biology, hormones, sleep, and behavior. Direct Meds focuses on clinician-reviewed compounded GLP-1 delivery with ongoing clinician and nursing support, but its materials emphasize medication delivery and support rather than a broader biology-first program. If personalization beyond medication is a priority, Found positions itself around that goal.

2. Pricing structure and membership

Pricing is a major difference. Found advertises getting started for as low as $39 and notes Found membership starts at $49/mo when billed annually in its captured materials, plus references to insurance-handling and Medicare programs; it also lists promotional discount details. Direct Meds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and lists standard FAQ pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. Direct Meds explicitly states there is no monthly membership fee and that medication, visits, supplies and free shipping are included. The promotional headline differs from Direct Meds’ FAQ prices, which the provider itself captures as standard rates.

3. Medication formats, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 options and both include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Direct Meds specifies injectable and sublingual compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and notes U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. Found lists compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide alongside branded options and explicitly states that compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved and are not available in all states. Direct Meds adds that treatment is only available when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

4. Shipping, refunds, and state availability

Direct Meds advertises free temperature-controlled 1–2 day UPS or FedEx shipping and a full refund if a prescription is not approved; it also notes service is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. Found’s captured materials emphasize insurance handling, potential Medicare programs, and insurance-accepted claims but do not provide the same shipping or refund specifics in the captured evidence. Both providers note that Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility.

Direct differences summarized

  • Found: biology-first personalization, insurance handling, membership pricing details and discounts; compounded and branded options; start as low as $39.
  • Direct Meds: no membership fee, promotional starting price $147 with FAQ standard pricing higher, injectable and sublingual compounded options, included supplies and free next-day shipping, unavailable in two states.

Conclusion: Found vs Directmeds final thoughts

Found vs Directmeds comes down to whether you prioritize a personalized, insurance-integrated program (Found) or straightforward compounded GLP-1 delivery with included shipping and no membership fee (Direct Meds). Both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that prescriptions depend on clinician review.

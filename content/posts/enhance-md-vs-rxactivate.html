The Enhance MD vs RxActivate comparison centers on how each provider handles pricing transparency, compound options, intake gating, clinician review, and scale of membership. Quick verdict: Enhance MD is most notable for explicit low introductory prices and flat-rate dose pricing; RxActivate focuses on clinician-matched plans with pricing gated behind an eligibility and contact-information step.

Start with Enhance MD pricing and options

Price transparency and introductory offers

Enhance MD publishes introductory prices: semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month, and emphasizes flat-rate pricing that stays the same at every dose with no escalation. RxActivate does not display price or billing terms before users complete an eligibility questionnaire and submit name, email, and phone number; pricing is gated behind intake.

Compound options and regulatory notes

Both providers operate clinician-reviewed telehealth models and may offer compounded medications. Enhance MD explicitly offers both compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide on one platform. RxActivate requires eligibility confirmation before revealing plans and does not publish medication specifics prior to intake. Both sources include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Intake flow, eligibility, and state screening

RxActivate routes users through a two-minute eligibility questionnaire that asks goal, height, weight, medication history, sex assigned at birth, date of birth, state, and safety screening; a synthetic test confirmed Alabama as serviceable. The flow displays likely eligibility and then requires contact information before any price or plan details are shown. Enhance MD provides pricing details up front, with clinician-directed review noted for treatment appropriateness.

Scale and experience

Enhance MD reports treating 10,000+ patients, which the source cites as meaningful clinical volume. RxActivate’s page states 250,000+ members in its flow. These are provider-published figures captured in the source materials and are not presented here as independent outcome evidence.

Clinician review and safety screening

Both providers state that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. RxActivate emphasizes clinician-matched plans and a state and medical-safety screening in its eligibility flow. Enhance MD notes clinician-directed prescribing alongside its pricing statements.

Conclusion: Enhance MD vs RxActivate

Enhance MD stands out for explicit low introductory pricing and flat-rate, same-price-at-every-dose messaging, plus on-platform access to both semaglutide and tirzepatide. RxActivate emphasizes a gated eligibility flow with clinician matching and state-level screening before disclosing plan or price. Both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Choose Enhance MD if you want visible starting prices (semaglutide from $49, tirzepatide from $99 first month), transparent flat-rate dose pricing, and access to both compounds on one platform.

Choose RxActivate if you prefer to confirm eligibility, state availability, and clinician-matched plan details before learning pricing and commitments.

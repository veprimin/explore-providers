Program approach and clinical oversight

Found describes a biology-first, personalized medically supervised program that targets hormones, metabolism, sleep, and behavioral factors rather than a single medication protocol for everyone. FeelGood Meds positions Personalized GLP-1 Treatments with clinician review and notes that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it medically appropriate. Both emphasize clinician review, but Found emphasizes broader metabolic customization as its distinguishing approach.

Medications offered and regulatory notes

Price and promotional structure compared

Regenics advertises compounded semaglutide at $139 first month and tirzepatide at $189 first month, with renewals noted at $199 and $279 respectively and a specific note that promotional pricing applies to the first month only. BreezeMeds lists semaglutide injection starting at $199/mo and tirzepatide injection starting at $299, with oral semaglutide and oral tirzepatide displayed from $399. BreezeMeds also shows a separate $169 figure that is not clearly tied to a specific product.

Medications and formats: injectable, oral, compounded

Both providers offer GLP-1 medications, but their presented formats differ. Regenics focuses on compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide plus an optional Sermorelin peptide therapy. BreezeMeds lists injectable semaglutide and tirzepatide and also displays oral semaglutide and oral tirzepatide options. The comparison point to note: Regenics emphasizes compounded products prepared by U.S. licensed compounding pharmacies; BreezeMeds shows both injectable and oral formulations but some pricing lines lack clear product association.

Clinician oversight and regulatory notes

Both sites state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Regenics specifies nurse-practitioner-led GLP-1 care and that licensed nurse practitioners review intake before anything is prescribed. Both providers include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. BreezeMeds notes medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Shipping, pharmacy verification, and program features

Regenics advertises free shipping with medication compounded in the USA and a LegitScript certified pharmacy network. BreezeMeds emphasizes discreet home delivery and ongoing support, describing its offering as discreet shipping to your door. BreezeMeds mentions affordability and multiple starting prices but captured evidence shows fees and billing commitments were not fully visible.

Pricing and plan structure: BetterMe Rx vs Sprout Health

BetterMe Rx lists compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan with specific monthly-equivalent examples: semaglutide injection $205/mo or as low as $145/mo on a 12-month plan; tirzepatide injection $275/mo or as low as $215/mo; oral semaglutide $250/mo or as low as $185/mo; oral tirzepatide $265/mo or as low as $215/mo. Sprout Health advertises $50 off the first month, bringing the initial cost to $199, with an ongoing rate of $249/month.

Direct comparison

BetterMe Rx emphasizes lower monthly-equivalent pricing when patients choose longer 3-, 6-, or 12-month supplies and notes longer plans are billed according to the selected term. Sprout Health provides a one-time introductory discount followed by a higher ongoing price. Both operate on a direct-pay model and do not bill insurance per the supplied evidence.

Formulation and medication options

BetterMe Rx supports compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in both injectable and oral formats and highlights four medication and administration options, including a needle-free daily tirzepatide tablet. Sprout Health focuses on prescribing compounded semaglutide and does not list multiple branded or formulation choices in the captured evidence.

Onboarding speed, provider review, and delivery

Sprout Health markets a lightning-quick approval process and frames outcomes around 10–26% body weight reduction over 12 months; the captured evidence emphasizes fast entry and a quick review-to-shipment flow. BetterMe Rx describes a free evaluation, U.S.-licensed provider review, and discreet U.S. shipping with free 1–2 day delivery indicated in landing-page evidence.

Regulatory notes and outcome framing

Both providers supply compounded medications under clinician review. BetterMe Rx explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Sprout Health’s materials note that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed. Sprout Health frames potential outcomes with a 10–26% weight reduction over 12 months but does not offer a formal outcome guarantee in the captured evidence.

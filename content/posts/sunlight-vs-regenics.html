How they differ at a glance: Sunlight vs Regenics

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences between Sunlight and Regenics: pricing model and renewal terms, medication type and compounding status, clinician oversight and care model, shipping and membership terms, and additional therapies or features.

1. Pricing model and renewals

Sunlight: Advertises personalized GLP-1 plans starting at $88/mo and claims “same price forever” with no price increases ever and no hidden fees. Materials mention promotions and statements like “As low as $88” and “Get $140 Off Today!” Consumers are advised to confirm total cost and renewal terms before enrollment.

Regenics: Lists promotional first-month pricing: semaglutide $139 first month and tirzepatide $189 first month, with renewals noted at $199 and $279 respectively unless otherwise stated at checkout. Promotional pricing applies to the first month only.

2. Medication type and regulatory notes

Sunlight: Offers personalized GLP-1 options including injections and oral options. The source includes a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Regenics: Offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide prepared by U.S. licensed compounding pharmacies. The source explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

3. Clinician oversight and care model

Sunlight: Promotes a 100% online medical visit with prescription and telehealth included and no insurance required. The landing evidence includes patient testimonials describing clinician dose adjustments and responsive support.

Regenics: Care is delivered by licensed nurse practitioners who review intake before anything is prescribed; treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Regenics positions itself as nurse-practitioner-led and mentions pharmacy network LegitScript certification.

4. Shipping, membership, and extras

Sunlight: Copies mention fast, free shipping, free and discreet 2-day shipping in some captured content, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime language. These shipping and membership claims appear repeatedly in the captured landing evidence.

Regenics: Notes free shipping with medication compounded in the USA. Information on membership or cancellation was not verified on the captured page.

5. Additional services

Sunlight: Messaging centers on GLP-1 weight loss plans and patient support; testimonials in the capture reference clinician adjustments and support responsiveness.

Regenics: Also offers Sermorelin peptide therapy as an add-on and lists a 15mg injectable peptide option in longer FAQs. Regenics highlights FSA/HSA eligibility and its LegitScript certified pharmacy network.

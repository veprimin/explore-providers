Medication type and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. Telos Rx states semaglutide and tirzepatide are compounded medications and prescription only.

How treatment is prescribed and clinician review

altRx: a licensed provider reviews your health evaluation and conducts a telehealth consultation that may be live video or asynchronous, as determined by provider and state telehealth law, and if the provider determines treatment is appropriate a prescription will be sent.

Telos Rx: treatment is prescribed online and available only if a US licensed provider approves you, with the source repeatedly noting availability is subject to clinician review.

Availability, shipping and commitments

altRx states its membership is available to residents of all 50 U.S. states, though provider availability and prescribing authority vary by state. altRx advertises free and discreet 2-day delivery and no membership, insurance or hidden fees, with cancel anytime language.

Telos Rx notes pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change. Telos Rx also states cancel anytime, no penalties, and that your price depends on your plan.

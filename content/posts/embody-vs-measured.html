Quick take: Embody vs Measured

Embody vs Measured comes down to price versus support: Embody advertises compounded semaglutide at $69/month with free 1–2 day shipping and minimal extras, while Measured starts at $89/month and emphasizes same-day Rx decisions, dietitian support, and unlimited messaging. Early verdict: choose Embody for the lowest advertised entry price and fastest advertised post-approval shipping; choose Measured if you want same-day Rx decisions and integrated dietitian support.

Direct comparison: Embody lists compounded semaglutide at $69/month (was $299) and compounded tirzepatide at $119/month. Measured lists compounded semaglutide from $89/month and compounded tirzepatide at $189/month as promotional pricing. Both require a prescription and are direct-pay programs with cancel anytime policies.

3) Clinical workflow and support features

Measured highlights same-day prescription decisions, unlimited messaging, 24/7 care team access, and dietitian support. Embody emphasizes licensed provider review handled online and 24/7 support access for dosing and side-effect questions but notes fewer premium features like integrated coaching or advanced monitoring.

4) Medication type and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. Both sources explicitly note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Embody states it prescribes compounded medications after licensed provider review and fills at licensed pharmacies; Measured states prescriptions are issued at provider discretion and treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

5) Outcomes, claims, and limitations

Measured cites an internal analysis of members reporting weight data at 6 months with an average loss of 31 lbs and headlines like “Lose 30+ lbs in 6 months,” while also noting individual results vary and are not guaranteed and that internal outcome claims are not independent evidence. Embody includes user testimonial language on steady progress but does not provide the same internal average outcome figure. Both note promotional pricing may change and final pricing may vary based on dose and clinician recommendation.

Conclusion: Which to pick in Embody vs Measured

Embody vs Measured is primarily a trade-off between price and integrated support. Embody is the lower advertised entry price and promises very fast post-approval shipping with a no-frills model. Measured advertises higher promotional pricing but the convenience of same-day Rx decisions, unlimited messaging, and dietitian support.

Choose Embody if you prioritize the lowest advertised monthly entry price ($69/month for compounded semaglutide), free 1-2 day shipping after approval, and a straightforward telehealth prescription model without premium coaching.

Choose Measured if you prioritize same-day prescription decisions, dietitian support, unlimited messaging and a more feature-rich care experience, while accepting a higher promotional starting price from $89/month. Both providers note compounded medications are not FDA-approved and final prescriptions are subject to clinician review.

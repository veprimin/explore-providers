Access, clinical review, and practical limits

Oak uses an asynchronous licensed clinician review process with no video call required; patients complete an online questionnaire and a board-certified physician in the state reviews cases. Oak states patients with BMI below 20 don’t qualify for GLP-1 treatment and elsewhere notes not appropriate for BMI under 22 without comorbidities. Ro.co requires a licensed clinician to determine therapy is medically appropriate; pricing is disclosed before consultation. The captured Ro.co evidence does not detail video call requirements or shipping timelines.

Included services and operational differences

Ro.co’s captured content emphasizes regulatory assurance and that it does not offer compounded injectables as its primary differentiator; no captured evidence on shipping or coaching was available for Ro.co.

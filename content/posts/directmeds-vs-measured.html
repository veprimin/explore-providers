Directmeds vs Measured: quick verdict

Directmeds and Measured both sell compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide online, but they differ most on advertised starting price, clinician and dietitian support promises, membership fees, and shipping speed. Early verdict: Measured advertises lower promotional prices starting from $89/mo and emphasizes same-day Rx decisions and dietitian support. Directmeds highlights clinician and nursing support, no separate membership fee, and promotional starting price $147 while noting standard FAQ pricing for semaglutide $297/mo and tirzepatide $399/mo.

How to read this comparison

This article compares Directmeds vs Measured using only supplied facts. It focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: pricing and promotions, support and services, shipping and fulfillment, membership and cancellation terms, and regulatory notes about compounded medications.

Pricing and promotions

Measured advertises compounded semaglutide from $89/mo and compounded tirzepatide at $189/mo as promotional pricing. Directmeds advertises treatment starting at $147 and a summer promotion that saves $150, while its FAQ lists standard prices of $297/mo for semaglutide and $399/mo for tirzepatide. Both providers present promotional pricing that may differ from their standard listed fees.

Support and care model

Measured emphasizes same-day prescription decisions, unlimited messaging, 24/7 Care Team access, and dietitian support. Directmeds emphasizes clinician-reviewed treatment, ongoing clinician and nursing support, and telehealth visits included in its offerings. Both require a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness and both state their compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Shipping, membership and cancellation

Measured advertises free two-day shipping on every order and the ability to cancel anytime. Directmeds advertises free temperature-controlled UPS/FedEx delivery generally in 1 to 2 days, free next-day shipping in some statements, no separate monthly membership fee, month-to-month cancellation for standard customers, and subscribe-and-save plans that require a stated term. Directmeds also notes no hidden fees and a full refund if a prescription is not approved.

Regulatory and product format notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and explicitly state that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Directmeds additionally specifies its compounding pharmacies are U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. Directmeds notes treatment is unavailable in Mississippi and Louisiana; Measured does not supply state-availability details in the captured evidence.

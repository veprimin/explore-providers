SnagRx vs MedVi: quick verdict. SnagRx is the lower-cost, flat-rate option when you commit to the 12-month plan; MedVi emphasizes a low promotional first month with 24/7 support but higher ongoing pricing. Choose SnagRx for locked pricing and named 503A pharmacies, or choose MedVi for a simple intake and round the clock certified support.

See SnagRx pricing and plan details

Price and billing: locked annual rate versus promotional first month

Price is the clearest difference in this SnagRx vs MedVi comparison. SnagRx lists compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan, with month-to-month equivalents at $99 and $149. The $69 and $119 figures are explicitly the 12-month plan rates and SnagRx states the rate is locked flat at every dose and at renewal. MedVi advertises a winter promotional price of $179 for the first month including fast, free shipping, but notes ongoing standalone months cost $299 and a three-month plan is $649 (about $216/month).

Clinician review, support, and process

Both providers operate via telehealth and do not require clinic visits. SnagRx uses a clinician network called OpenLoop Health and states clinician review typically occurs within five hours and always within 24 hours. MedVi also uses online clinician review and highlights 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and a simple online intake. The comparison here is speed and ongoing support: SnagRx emphasizes fast clinician review timelines while MedVi emphasizes continuous support availability.

Medication types, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both providers supply compounded semaglutide rather than branded finished products. SnagRx explicitly offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and notes its compounded medications are not FDA approved finished products. MedVi provides clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only and also states compounded medications are not FDA approved. MedVi does not offer tirzepatide according to the source.

Shipping, pharmacies, and logistics

SnagRx names multiple US 503A partner pharmacies and advertises next-day UPS or FedEx shipping in temperature-controlled packaging. MedVi states medication is filled at a licensed pharmacy and shipped with fast, free shipping for the promotional month. SnagRx calls out named partner pharmacies and next-day shipping while MedVi focuses on free and fast shipping within its promotion.

Guarantees, outcomes, and program extras

SnagRx lists a full refund if a prescription is declined and a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms applied, and states LegitScript certification. MedVi highlights program elements like physician review, a personalized plan, metabolic report, and 1:1 guidance included in the cost for its program, and it publishes no comparable outcome guarantee. MedVi cites typical patient-reported average weight loss metrics under conditions explained on its site, and both sources warn results vary and that medication prescriptions are at clinician discretion.

How to choose between SnagRx and MedVi

Use SnagRx if your priority is predictable all-in pricing across titration and you are willing to commit to a 12-month plan to access the $69 semaglutide or $119 tirzepatide rates. SnagRx names its partner pharmacies, highlights a fast clinician review window, and advertises a refund if a prescription is declined plus a 6-month guarantee subject to terms.

Lock in SnagRx 12-month pricing

Use MedVi if you prefer a simple online intake with 24/7 certified medical support and want a low promotional first month at $179 with free shipping, accepting that ongoing standalone months cost substantially more and tirzepatide is not offered.

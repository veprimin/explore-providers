Start Willow vs Telos Rx: quick verdict

Start Willow vs Telos Rx is primarily a tradeoff between Start Willow’s higher direct-pay pricing and faster delivery versus Telos Rx’s lower advertised starting prices and clinician-dependent terms. Early verdict: choose Start Willow if you want no insurance, no membership fees, and a 3-day delivery guarantee; choose Telos Rx if your priority is lower starting price subject to clinician approval.

Price and ongoing cost comparison

Start Willow lists semaglutide starting at $249 for the first month and $299 per month ongoing. Telos Rx advertises semaglutide as low as $99 per month and tirzepatide as low as $139 per month, with pricing described as dependent on clinician approval and plan. Both use compounded formulations, and Telos Rx emphasizes prices are “as low as” figures that apply when a licensed provider approves you.

Medication types and regulatory status

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and references to tirzepatide alternatives. Start Willow describes its offering as compounded semaglutide comparable to Ozempic and tirzepatide and explicitly states compounded semaglutide is not FDA approved and is not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Telos Rx likewise notes semaglutide and tirzepatide are compounded medications, prescription-only, and not FDA approved. Neither source supplies branded, FDA-approved finished products in the captured evidence.

Delivery, access, and insurance requirements

Start Willow operates on a direct-pay model with no insurance required and no membership fees, marketed as an advantage for patients frustrated by prior-authorization. Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less through its licensed pharmacy network and highlights same-day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping in captured landing-page evidence. Telos Rx requires clinician approval; its materials emphasize that treatment is prescribed online by a US-licensed provider and that pricing and eligibility are subject to the live offer. Telos Rx also notes you may pay $0 if not approved, per the captured text.

Clinical review, cancellation, and terms

Telos Rx repeatedly frames availability and price as subject to clinician review and live offer terms and states you can cancel anytime with no penalties. Start Willow describes clinician-prescribed compounded semaglutide after an online review and emphasizes collaboration with a Willow doctor for personalized dosing. Start Willow also warns that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that tapering guidance should come from your Willow physician to manage changes after stopping therapy.

Side effects, patient support, and patient fit

Start Willow includes patient-facing details about common side effects such as nausea and notes support resources like written and video injection instructions and customer service responsiveness in testimonials. Telos Rx notes that individual results vary and are not guaranteed and repeatedly stresses prescription-only status after a licensed provider review. Captured evidence does not supply independent efficacy comparisons or broader outcome data for either provider.

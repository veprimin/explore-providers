Overview: care model and what the captured evidence shows

Both providers operate clinician-reviewed online weight-management care according to the captured information. Lemonaid Health’s captured landing page confirmed that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny’s captured landing page describes a full online workflow with a health questionnaire, licensed provider review, prescription when appropriate, and ongoing telemedicine support and 24/7 chat.

Shipping and delivery

JoinSkinny: The captured evidence repeatedly claims free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days for prescriptions shipped the same day when received before 2 pm central time, with tracking and insured shipments. Lemonaid Health: no verified shipping or delivery details were captured on the landing page.

Pharmacy, medication and regulatory notes

JoinSkinny: the captured evidence states prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA regulated and LegitScript certified pharmacy partners and that compounded medications are not FDA approved. Both providers’ captured evidence mentions clinician review; JoinSkinny provides more detail about pharmacy partners in the captured content.

Ongoing support and care model

JoinSkinny: The captured evidence describes ongoing support including dose adjustments, monthly check ins, secure telemedicine, and 24/7 chat, plus provider guided dose increases at no extra cost according to the landing page. Lemonaid Health: the captured evidence confirms treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate, but current captured content did not include ongoing support or program detail.

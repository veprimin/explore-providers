How they differ at a glance

SnagRx and Sesame Care take different approaches to telehealth weight management. SnagRx focuses on compounded GLP-1s with an annual-plan price lock. Sesame Care operates as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients choose clinicians, accepts insurance for some weight-loss medications, and lists cash-pay medication prices.

Pricing and plan structure

SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan and states those figures are locked flat at every dose and at renewal. The same medications month-to-month are $99 and $149 respectively. Sesame Care lists subscription care from $59/month based on a 12-month Success commitment, and separates subscription fees from medication costs, which are shown as cash-pay starting at $149/month or lower with insurance.

Medication type and regulatory notes

SnagRx provides compounded GLP-1 medications produced by named US 503A pharmacies. The source explicitly states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sesame Care lists options that include branded, FDA-approved products such as oral Wegovy and Zepbound pens and indicates medication-specific approval status must be confirmed; Sesame also shows cash-pay and in-network pricing variations.

Clinician model, access and experience

SnagRx delivers care through OpenLoop Health, a named clinician network that the source says covers 48 states and typically reviews intake within five hours. Sesame Care is a marketplace where patients can browse and select clinicians based on specialty and availability, offering flexible scheduling and the option to choose your provider. The captured evidence notes that experience can vary by clinician on Sesame.

Shipping, guarantees, and program extras

SnagRx states next-day UPS or FedEx shipping in temperature-controlled packaging and names partner pharmacies. SnagRx also advertises a full refund if a prescription is declined and mentions a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms that apply. Sesame Care emphasizes ongoing video visits, labs, messaging, and dedicated provider care as part of subscription services, and notes that medication costs are separate and vary by insurance.

Final comparison and how to choose

SnagRx vs Sesame Care comes down to tradeoffs: SnagRx is focused on low, locked-in compounded pricing and named pharmacy partners with next-day shipping. Sesame Care provides clinician choice, insurance acceptance for medications, and access to branded options, with subscription services separate from medication costs.

Embody vs Directmeds: if your priority is lowest ongoing price, Embody offers compounded semaglutide at $69/month and tirzepatide at $119/month while Direct Meds advertises a promotional start at $147 and lists standard FAQ prices higher. Early verdict: Embody is the lower-price, no-frills option; Direct Meds emphasizes multiple formats and transparent standard pricing.

How Embody and Direct Meds differ at a glance

Both providers sell compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide via telehealth and both state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Key differences are entry price, formulation options, shipping promises, availability notes, and refund or approval policies. The sections below compare these points directly using only the supplied facts.

1. Price and promotional framing

Embody presents semaglutide at $69/month, marked down from $299, and tirzepatide at $119/month. The provider advertises flat locked in monthly pricing, with the $69 semaglutide price noted as a limited-time offer that may increase. Direct Meds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and lists standard FAQ pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. Direct Meds also notes a summer promotion saves $150. Final pricing with both providers may depend on clinician recommendations or specific plans where stated.

2. Formulation and pharmacy details

Embody focuses on compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide injections; its materials emphasize compounded products rather than branded options. Direct Meds offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats and states medications are provided from U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. Both providers clarify that the compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products.

3. Shipping and delivery commitments

Embody promises free shipping with delivery in 1 to 2 days after clinician approval and describes free fast 1 to 2 day delivery. Direct Meds advertises free temperature-controlled UPS or FedEx delivery generally in 1 to 2 days and calls it free next-day shipping in some places. Both describe fast shipping but Direct Meds notes temperature-controlled shipping and specifies UPS or FedEx.

4. Support, refunds, and availability

Embody includes prescription and telemed visits, licensed provider review handled online, and advertises 24/7 support access for dosing and side-effect questions. Embody also highlights no long-term commitment and cancel anytime. Direct Meds includes clinician review, telehealth visits, supplies and nursing support, and explicitly offers a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Direct Meds notes treatment is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. Embody does not list state exclusions in the supplied material.

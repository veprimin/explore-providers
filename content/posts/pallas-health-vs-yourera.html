Pallas Health vs YourEra: what to expect in the first check-in

Both services use licensed US clinicians to review your history and determine if GLP-1 treatment is appropriate, but Pallas Health offers a choice of FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound, or Mounjaro when suitable, whereas YourEra dispenses compounded semaglutide or compounded tirzepatide only.

Five key differences that matter

1) Medication types available

Pallas Health: clinician-personalized choice between FDA-approved brand-name GLP-1s and compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared in the USA when a brand medication is not the right fit. YourEra: compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide only; no branded Wegovy, Ozempic, Mounjaro, or Zepbound are offered.

2) Pricing structure and starting costs

Pallas Health: compounded semaglutide is $139 for the first month, then $597 every 12 weeks (a $199/month average); compounded tirzepatide is $179 first month, then $897 every 12 weeks ($299/month average). Brand-name plans list cash-pay monthly figures such as Wegovy $1,349 and Ozempic $1,299. YourEra: advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/month and tirzepatide from $199/month on the 6-month plan, with lowest rates reflecting the 6-month commitment and mentions an advertised figure as low as $79.20/month on some screens reflecting the 6-month plan.

3) Billing cadence and commitments

Pallas Health: compounded plans renew every 12 weeks; the first-month price is an intro rate and plans renew at the 12-week rate. YourEra: lowest advertised monthly rates are tied to a 6-month plan and shorter plans cost more per month; monthly price depends on plan length.

4) Support and access

Pallas Health: provider care, check-ins and messaging are included in one all-in plan price with care-team hours weekdays 9 AM to 5 PM ET and no emergency services. YourEra: bundles the provider consult, prescription, dose adjustments, and offers 24/7 support as part of the plan.

5) Shipping and fulfillment

Pallas Health: free expedited, discreet shipping and compounded medications prepared by US-licensed compounding pharmacies. YourEra: free, discreet delivery from licensed US pharmacies, shipping in roughly 1 to 2 days.

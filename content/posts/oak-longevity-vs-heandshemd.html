Quick verdict: Oak Longevity is the lower up-front cost option with one flat price for all dosages and no subscription. He & She MD bundles 1:1 clinician messaging and a coaching app, but its headline prices are six-month introductory rates that renew higher. This article compares Oak Longevity vs He & She MD across pricing, support, program terms, medication format, and regulatory notes.

See Oak Longevity pricing and options

Price structure and what you actually pay

Oak Longevity lists semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month with one flat price that covers all dosages and no subscription required. He & She MD advertises semaglutide from $139/month and tirzepatide from $199/month as six-month introductory rates that renew higher after the first six months. Oak bills monthly on a non recurring basis and says three month prescriptions are billed upfront; He & She MD frames its pricing as six month terms with promotional rates that renew at larger six month prices.

Coaching, clinician access, and support

Oak Longevity includes free health coaching with every plan and emphasizes a low cost offering with free shipping. He & She MD bundles a coaching app that includes 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1 specific guidance, and progress tracking at no extra cost during the plan term. That makes He & She MD a bundled support option while Oak prioritizes low price plus included coaching.

Program terms, renewals, and commitment

Oak promotes no subscriptions, no membership fees, and non recurring monthly billing where you choose to renew payment each month. Oak also notes three month prescriptions are billed upfront and no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped. He & She MD sells six month introductory rates that renew at higher six month prices, so buyers should budget for the renewal price rather than the intro price.

Medication formats, clinical review, and eligibility

Oak offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and states the clinical review is asynchronous with no video call required. He & She MD offers semaglutide injections, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injections and requires consultation and prescription by a clinician through its platform. Both note that not all customers will qualify for a prescription and that compounded medications are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Conclusion and recommendation

Oak Longevity vs He & She MD comes down to cost predictability versus bundled clinical support. Oak Longevity is best for cost conscious patients who want the lowest listed prices, one flat price for all dosages, no subscription, and an asynchronous clinical review. He & She MD is best for patients who want integrated coaching and 1:1 clinician messaging through an app and are comfortable with six month introductory rates that renew higher.

Choose Oak Longevity if you prioritize the lowest up front cost, a flat price for all dosages, no subscription, and asynchronous review.

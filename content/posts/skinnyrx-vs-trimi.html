2. Medication format and compounding

SkinnyRx markets daily oral semaglutide tablets taken sublingually as an alternative to injections and notes these are compounded semaglutide tablets. SkinnyRx states prescriptions require an online consultation with a licensed provider and that partner pharmacies run third-party testing every 3 to 6 months confirming concentration within plus or minus 10 percent of the active ingredient.

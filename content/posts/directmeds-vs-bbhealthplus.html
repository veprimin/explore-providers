Pricing and bundle structure

Direct Meds markets a promotional start at $147 and in its FAQ lists standard pricing of semaglutide $297/mo and tirzepatide $399/mo. Bodybuilding Health+ lists semaglutide from $179/mo and tirzepatide from $209/mo and advertises multi-month bundles that reduce the effective monthly cost for 3, 6, and 12 month terms. Use the providers’ stated prices and bundle terms to compare total cost for the term you prefer; both sources emphasize no separate membership fee.

Program scope and medication formats

Direct Meds focuses on compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide delivered as injectable and sublingual options, fulfilled by U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. Bodybuilding Health+ offers GLP-1s alongside NAD+, sermorelin, and sexual health options, positioning itself as a multi-program platform with GLP-1 bundles up to 12 months.

Shipping, cancellation, and refunds

Direct Meds advertises free temperature-controlled UPS or FedEx delivery generally in 1 to 2 days, month-to-month cancellation for eligible customers, and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Bodybuilding Health+ notes shipment to your door, cancel anytime flexibility with no membership penalties, and available multi-month bundles; specific refund language was not supplied.

Clinical oversight and regulatory notes

Both platforms require clinician review and state that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Bodybuilding Health+ names medical oversight by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D. Both providers include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Which should you choose?

Choose Direct Meds if you prioritize a promotional starting price, injectable and sublingual compounded formats, free next-day temperature-controlled shipping, no separate monthly membership fee, and a stated full refund if a prescription is not approved. Choose Bodybuilding Health+ if you value a broader multi-program platform with GLP-1 bundles that lower monthly cost over multiple months, transparent no-hidden-fees language, cancel anytime flexibility, and named medical oversight by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D. Remember that both note compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that treatment is subject to clinician review.

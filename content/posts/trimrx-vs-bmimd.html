Key differences at a glance: price model, medication options, and commitments

The most distinctive differences between TrimRx and bmiMD are their price models, the medications they emphasize, and the terms tied to lower rates. TrimRx advertises weight-loss plans starting at $149/month and a promotional $140 discount, with a Lifetime Price Protection claim that locks in today’s price for life and the same price on every dose. bmiMD lists tirzepatide with four billing options and a lower long-term monthly equivalent, ranging down to $139/month on a 12-month plan, but the lowest price requires a longer commitment.

Pricing structure and guarantees

TrimRx: Plans starting at $149/month. TrimRx markets Lifetime Price Protection that keeps the same price on every dose and cites a promotional discount of $140. The TrimRx program states 0% interest financing options are available and that there is no monthly membership and you can cancel anytime.

bmiMD: Tirzepatide pricing varies by billing term: $179 month-to-month, $159 for three months, $149 for six months, and $139 for a 12-month plan. bmiMD advertises a Best Price Guarantee and a 100% refundable policy if not eligible for the program. The provider emphasizes that the lowest monthly equivalent requires a multi-month commitment and that total prepaid charges should be confirmed.

Medication offerings and how they differ

TrimRx offers compounded GLP-1 injections (compounded semaglutide) starting at $149/month and compounded GLP-1+GIP dual agonist injections (compounded tirzepatide) starting at $249/month, and also notes that providers may write prescriptions for branded FDA-approved medications which must be filled separately. TrimRx highlights both GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options in its primary program.

bmiMD emphasizes compounded tirzepatide (GLP-1/GIP) with subscription options and four billing choices. bmiMD states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Shipping, refill access, and clinician support

TrimRx claims free 1–2 day shipping and included prescription and telemedicine visits. TrimRx also notes 24/7 support, HSA/FSA eligibility, and no insurance required for its direct-pay model.

bmiMD advertises free subscription shipping on every order, online refills and delivery, and 24/7 clinician access. bmiMD highlights reliable refills and a confirmed production process described as prepared in a state-licensed pharmacy and independently verified in FDA- and DEA-registered labs in captured content.

Medications, regulatory status, and dosing

Maximus provides compounded semaglutide for once-weekly injections and notes options for custom titration unless a customer purchases a pre-determined 90 day starter pack. Maximus explicitly states it does not provide branded finished products such as Ozempic or Wegovy. Luvo Health lists tirzepatide injection, semaglutide injection, and semaglutide tablets and mentions microdosing options, dose adjustments based on clinical response, and a dosing guide, but many specifics were not published or verified on the captured page.

Availability, shipping and refunds

Maximus reports availability in all 50 states except Mississippi and offers free expedited shipping. If a patient is not approved for treatment, Maximus states medication costs will be fully refunded. Luvo Health includes a cancellation and refund policy on its site, and shows cancel anytime and dose adjustment language in the capture, but captured landing-page evidence did not verify state coverage, exact shipping terms, or numeric refund details.

Who should pick which provider

Choose Maximus if you want a clearly published low flat-rate direct-pay option, a telemedicine-first pathway with prescriptions filled through U.S. pharmacies, explicit statements about state availability (all states except Mississippi) and a refund if you are not approved for treatment.

Choose Luvo Health if you prefer a clinician-reviewed program that lists tirzepatide and multiple semaglutide formats and are willing to confirm current pricing, eligibility, pharmacy sourcing, state coverage and renewal terms before enrolling because those details were not verified on the captured landing page.

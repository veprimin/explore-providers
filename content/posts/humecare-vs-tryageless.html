Quick verdict: HumeCare+ centers on a bundled GLP-1 protocol with muscle-preserving additions and monthly body composition scans, while Try Ageless focuses on format choice and lower entry prices including needle-free tirzepatide drops from $159/mo. This comparison looks at price and formats, supportive ingredients and tracking, money-back or discount programs, clinician flexibility, and regulatory notes for HumeCare+ vs Try Ageless.

Price and formats compared

HumeCare+ lists compounded semaglutide starting at $199/month and a tirzepatide plus B12 and glycine protocol from $299/month. Try Ageless advertises three formats: semaglutide injection from $199/mo, tirzepatide injection from $249/mo, and needle-free tirzepatide oral drops from $159/mo on a yearly plan. Try Ageless highlights the lowest entry price on the menu at $159/mo for drops; HumeCare+ emphasizes bundled supportive ingredients and scans at its price points.

Supportive protocol and body-composition tracking

HumeCare+ sells a protocol not just a prescription. It pairs physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with glycine and vitamins B6 and B12 built into the formulation and includes monthly body-composition scans to track fat versus lean mass over time. Try Ageless bundles B12 in every plan for energy support but does not advertise body-composition scans in the supplied evidence. That makes HumeCare+ distinct in offering muscle-preserving ingredients and monthly lean-mass tracking.

Money-back promise versus built-in discounts

HumeCare+ carries a 10% or your money back promise over six months, with eligibility and terms to verify directly with HumeCare+. Try Ageless instead emphasizes a discount already applied to every plan with an extra stackable code SLIM50 for $50 off; Try Ageless notes the lowest advertised prices require the prepaid yearly plan. These are different approaches: HumeCare+ markets a results-based refund promise while Try Ageless markets built-in and stackable pricing discounts.

Clinician access, switching formats, and logistics

HumeCare+ highlights continuous physician optimization, unlimited clinician access and a dedicated app for tracking and food logging plus free next-day delivery and cancel-anytime flexibility according to the supplied evidence. Try Ageless highlights that clinicians can switch patients between semaglutide injection, tirzepatide injection, and tirzepatide drops under the same plan with no new consult fee, plus plain discreet shipping and cancel-anytime terms. Try Ageless also states LegitScript certification and HIPAA compliance in its materials captured here.

Safety and regulatory notes

Both providers dispense compounded medications and the supplied evidence states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Try Ageless specifically notes that oral tirzepatide drops are an off-label compounded format because tirzepatide is not FDA-approved in oral form. HumeCare+ also warns that compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated for safety, quality, or efficacy and that GLP-1 drugs carry serious risks which are noted in its materials.

What to choose: clear guidance

Choose HumeCare+ if you want a bundled GLP-1 protocol with glycine, B6, and B12 included, monthly body-composition scans to monitor lean mass, continuous clinician access, and a stated 10% or your money back promise over six months as described in the supplier materials.

Choose Try Ageless if you prioritize format choice and lower entry pricing, especially the needle-free tirzepatide oral drops from $159/mo on the yearly plan, built-in discounts plus a stackable SLIM50 code, and the ability to switch molecules or formats under one plan with no new consult fee.

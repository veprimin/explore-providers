2. Ongoing cost and dose escalation

Embody emphasizes a flat locked-in monthly price at $69 or $119, noting no membership and cancel anytime. Enhance MD emphasizes flat-rate pricing that remains the same at every dose once ongoing pricing is set, and warns the $49 and $99 figures are first-month introductory prices; ongoing flat rates are higher and not always prominently published before intake.

3. Medication type and regulatory note

Both providers offer compounded medications. Important regulatory note from each source: compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Each platform describes clinician-directed prescribing and clinician review before dispensing.

4. Delivery speed and logistics

Embody advertises free shipping with delivery in 1 to 2 days after clinician approval. Enhance MD does not promise the same rapid ship window in the supplied evidence, but highlights pricing and program structure instead.

5. Clinical features and scale

Enhance MD reports treating more than 10,000 patients and offers metabolic lab testing every 6 months as part of its care model in the captured materials. Embody emphasizes a lean, prescription-forward model without premium monitoring or integrated coaching, and includes 24/7 support access for dosing and side effect questions in its listing.

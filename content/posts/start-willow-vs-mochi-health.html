Quick verdict: Start Willow vs Mochi Health

Start Willow vs Mochi Health is a choice between fast direct-pay semaglutide delivery and a membership model that emphasizes live video visits. Early verdict: choose Start Willow if you want a no-insurance, fast-shipping compounded semaglutide path; choose Mochi Health if you want synchronous video visits, membership features, and lower entry pricing.

How these services differ in three key areas

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences between Start Willow and Mochi Health: pricing structure and transparency, clinical interaction model, and shipping and product type. Each claim below is drawn from the providers’ stated evidence.

Pricing structure and transparency

Start Willow operates on a direct-pay model with pricing stated as From $249 per Month and ongoing pricing noted as $299/month after the first month. There are no membership fees and no insurance requirement. Mochi Health uses a membership-plus-medication model with pricing described as membership plus medication starting from $99/month and promotional 50% off on the first month via code BESTWLHALFOFF. Mochi also notes a $39 promotional first-month membership rate for some treatments, then a standard rate of $79/month thereafter depending on state terms. In short, Start Willow is a flat direct-pay option; Mochi is membership-based so total monthly cost varies by plan and medication.

Clinical interaction model

Start Willow provides fully online clinician review and prescribes compounded semaglutide after an online intake and clinician approval. Mochi Health emphasizes live video visits bookable in about 5 minutes, describing a synchronous video visit where a doctor evaluates the case in real time. Mochi positions its live video model as fundamentally different from the asynchronous text reviews used by many competitors. Both platforms use licensed clinicians to evaluate prescriptions, but Mochi highlights a real-time video consult as a defining feature.

Shipping, fulfillment, and product type

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less from its licensed pharmacy network and advertises same-day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping when in stock. Start Willow offers compounded semaglutide described as a once-weekly injected formulation prescribed as part of a personalized treatment plan. Mochi Health offers a range of compounded options and notes compounded semaglutide starting at $60 and compounded tirzepatide starting at $90 with additional forms like a compounded oral semaglutide pill starting at $62. Both providers indicate their compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and that compounded medications are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Side effects, tapering, and practical use

Start Willow materials state semaglutide is administered once weekly via subcutaneous injection and that common side effects may include nausea which usually subsides over time. Willow recommends working with a physician to slowly decrease dose when stopping to manage potential weight regain. Mochi’s materials include a standard regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-reviewed and that individual results are not guaranteed; Mochi does not supply a side-effect list in the provided evidence beyond general statements that individual results vary.

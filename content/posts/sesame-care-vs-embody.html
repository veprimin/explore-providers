Sesame Care vs Embody: which fits you better depends on whether you value clinician choice and insurance access or the lowest cash price and fast shipping. Early verdict: pick Sesame Care if you want a marketplace with clinician selection and insurance-eligible branded options; pick Embody if your priority is the lowest advertised cash price and quick free delivery.

How these programs differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: clinician model and choice, pricing model and insurance, medication types and approvals, shipping and turnaround, and program features and commitments.

Clinician model and patient choice

Sesame Care operates as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing. That marketplace model is presented as giving users more control over the care relationship and may produce variable experience because clinician offerings differ. Embody uses a direct telehealth model with licensed provider review handled online. Embody emphasizes a streamlined prescription path without clinic visits.

Pricing model and insurance

Sesame Care lists subscription care “as low as $59/mo” for ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging, and notes medication costs are not included in that subscription price. Sesame also states insurance is accepted for weight loss medications and gives example prices with insurance as low as $25 for some branded options. Embody advertises a direct-pay flat pricing model: $69/month for compounded semaglutide and $119/month for compounded tirzepatide, with no insurance required and no monthly membership required. Embody’s $69 semaglutide price is presented as a reduced offer from $299 and may be a limited-time offer that could increase. Final pricing on both platforms may vary by dose and clinician recommendation.

Medication types and regulatory notes

Sesame Care lists both oral and branded injectable options. Examples include semaglutide with cash-pay pricing listed at $149 and tirzepatide with cash-pay pricing listed at $299, and price with insurance as low as $25 for some strengths. Sesame references Wegovy pill and Zepbound KwikPen as FDA-approved branded medications for weight loss and notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Embody offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide at cash prices and explicitly notes compounded medications are not FDA approved finished products. Embody states its compounded products use the same active ingredients as certain branded drugs but preserves the regulatory distinction that compounded medications are not FDA approved.

Shipping, turnaround and convenience

Embody highlights free shipping with delivery in 1 to 2 days after clinician approval, and positions that as a fast turnaround for a direct-pay program with no clinic visits. Sesame Care emphasizes flexible scheduling and a large clinician network with nationwide access in many states, but the source does not supply a specific shipping speed or free shipping promise for medications; Sesame notes medication costs vary by insurance status and pharmacy.

Program features and commitments

Sesame Care advertises features such as ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging and notes subscription pricing “as low as $59/mo” is “based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment” with milestone language tied to a study when paired with healthy lifestyle changes and with results that may vary. Embody emphasizes no long term commitment, cancel anytime, no monthly membership, and flat locked in monthly pricing for compounded products. Embody also highlights 24 7 support access for dosing and side effect questions in its feature list.

Which to choose

Choose Sesame Care if you want a marketplace model that lets you browse and select clinicians, want options that accept insurance for weight loss medications, and prefer access to branded, FDA approved oral or injectable products where listed. Sesame Care may be better for patients who value flexible appointments, dedicated provider care, and insurance pathways even if subscription pricing is described as based on a 12-month commitment and medication costs vary by insurance status and pharmacy.

Choose Embody if your priority is the lowest advertised cash entry price and a no membership, no long term commitment approach with fast free shipping in 1 to 2 days after clinician approval, and if you accept that Embody offers compounded medications that are not FDA approved finished products and that final pricing may vary by dose or clinician recommendation.

Sesame Care vs Embody: use Sesame Care for choice and insurance access; use Embody for low advertised cash pricing and speed.

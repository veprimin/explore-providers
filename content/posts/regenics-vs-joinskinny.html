Regenics vs JoinSkinny: quick verdict. Regenics is a nurse-practitioner-led telehealth option with compounded semaglutide starting at $139 first month and a Sermorelin peptide add-on, while JoinSkinny offers clinician-reviewed GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP plans starting at $149 with flat-rate dosing and multi-month bundles. For lower promotional entry pricing and a peptide option choose Regenics; for flat pricing, bundle options, and next-day temperature-controlled shipping choose JoinSkinny.

How Regenics and JoinSkinny differ at a glance

The two services both deliver telehealth weight-management care and work with LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners, but they differ on pricing structure, medication type and preparation, clinical staffing, shipping cadence, and additional offerings. Below are the most distinctive supported differences.

1. Pricing structure and promotional details

Regenics advertises compounded semaglutide at starting at $139 first month and tirzepatide at $189 first month, with renewals noted at higher monthly rates unless otherwise stated at checkout. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149 and highlights flat-rate pricing so monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases, plus 3, 6, and 12-month bundles and a promotional discount referenced on the landing page.

2. Medication type and compounding versus branded options

Regenics offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide prepared by U.S. licensed compounding pharmacies and notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved as finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny works with licensed U.S. pharmacies that prepare authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1 + GIP when prescribed and states its pharmacies meet FDA regulations and hold LegitScript certification. Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved when compounded products are involved.

3. Clinical model and ongoing support

Regenics describes nurse-practitioner-led GLP-1 care where licensed nurse practitioners review intake before anything is prescribed. JoinSkinny describes clinician-reviewed care delivered by licensed U.S. providers, ongoing support with dose adjustments, monthly check-ins, and 24/7 chat. JoinSkinny emphasizes continuous medical support and dose guidance; Regenics highlights nurse-practitioner oversight and a clinician review determining medical appropriateness.

4. Shipping speed and logistics

Regenics states free shipping with medication compounded in the USA but captured landing-page shipping details were not readable for verification. JoinSkinny advertises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days with same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time and insured shipments. If next-day, temperature-controlled delivery and tracking are priorities, JoinSkinny supplies explicit details in the source.

5. Add-on options and plan flexibility

Regenics specifically lists Sermorelin peptide therapy as an add-on and prices a 15mg injectable Sermorelin at $399 in the FAQ content. JoinSkinny highlights plan flexibility with monthly or multi-month plans, installment payment options, no membership fee, and the ability to cancel before the next billing cycle. Choose Regenics if a secondary peptide line matters; choose JoinSkinny if flat-rate dosing and bundle flexibility matter.

Conclusion: choosing between Regenics vs JoinSkinny

Both Regenics and JoinSkinny provide telehealth access to GLP-1 therapies through licensed providers and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners, but they serve slightly different needs. Regenics is best for people seeking a lower promotional entry price for compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide and a Sermorelin peptide option under nurse-practitioner-led care. JoinSkinny is best for people who prioritize flat-rate pricing, multi-month bundles, explicit next-day temperature-controlled shipping, and continuous dose guidance from licensed clinicians.

Choose Regenics if you want a cash-pay, nurse-practitioner-led option with compounded semaglutide starting at $139 first month and a Sermorelin peptide add-on. Choose JoinSkinny if you want flat-rate dosing, bundle options, and explicit temperature-controlled, next-day shipping and ongoing clinical support.

3. Regulatory and product status

Both sources state the medications are compounded and prescription only. Both include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Telos Rx also emphasizes a US licensed provider decides which medication is right for you, and both say treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

4. Membership, cancellation and renewal notes

Gala repeatedly states no monthly membership and cancel anytime on the captured page. Telos Rx copy captured indicates cancel anytime, no penalties, and warns that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change. Each provider advises confirming eligibility and state availability before enrollment.

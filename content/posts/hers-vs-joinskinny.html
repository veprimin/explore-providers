Comparing Hers vs JoinSkinny? Quick verdict: Hers emphasizes FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy® with a required membership and ongoing dose check-ins, while JoinSkinny emphasizes flat-rate GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP plans from $149 with no membership and free, fast shipping.

How the two services differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences between Hers and JoinSkinny: FDA-approved brand-name availability versus compounded options, membership and billing structure, pricing and shipping, clinical support and dose adjustments, and plan flexibility and bundles. The information below uses only the supplied facts.

1. Medication type and regulatory notes

Hers: Offers FDA-approved Wegovy® (brand-name semaglutide). Landing-page material states Wegovy® is FDA-approved and Hers does not offer compounded medications. The source emphasizes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

JoinSkinny: Offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and states that compounds contain the same active molecules used in brand-name medications. The JoinSkinny source also acknowledges that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

2. Membership, pricing structure, and billing

Hers: Lists prices with examples: Wegovy® pill from $149/mo and injectable from $199/mo in landing material. The detailed source states an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required ($39 for the first month, auto-renews at $149/month thereafter) and that medication and membership are billed separately. It notes a prescription is not guaranteed and medication is not available without an active membership.

JoinSkinny: States plans starting at $149, with monthly and 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles offered. JoinSkinny highlights no membership fee and that monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases. The source also mentions a $140 promotional discount and bundle options; bundle commitments and total charge require confirmation per the landing material.

3. Shipping and delivery commitments

Hers: The captured material does not provide a specific shipping speed or free-shipping promise in the supplied evidence; it notes medication availability and state limitations and that not all treatments are available in all 50 states.

JoinSkinny: Advertises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1–2 days. The source states prescriptions received before 2 pm central time will be shipped the same day and next-day via UPS in a temperature controlled package, with weekend handling and tracking information described.

4. Clinical support, dose checks, and ongoing monitoring

Hers: Describes a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins and mentions treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. The landing material notes dose monitoring and clinician review.

JoinSkinny: Describes clinician-reviewed care with prescription and telemed visits included, ongoing support, dose adjustments, monthly check-ins, and 24/7 chat. JoinSkinny states dose adjustments are fully guided by your provider and the starting dose is determined based on medical history.

5. Plan flexibility and extras

Hers: Emphasizes brand-name medication availability and says no compounded/generic alternative is offered. The source also notes membership requirements and separate billing for medication and membership.

JoinSkinny: Highlights flat-rate dosing so price stays the same regardless of dosage increases, multiple bundle lengths to potentially save, installment options (Afterpay, Klarna, Affirm), and the ability to cancel anytime before the next billing cycle.

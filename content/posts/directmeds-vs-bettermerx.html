Pricing presentation and what it means

Directmeds promotes a summer price starting at $147 and separately lists standard FAQ pricing of semaglutide $297/month and tirzepatide $399/month. BetterMe Rx shows monthly prices and lower 12-month equivalents such as semaglutide injection as low as $145/month on a 12-month plan and tirzepatide as low as $215/month on a 12-month plan. Directmeds offers a first-month discount and explicit month-to-month language. BetterMe Rx requires longer prepaid terms to reach the lowest monthly-equivalent pricing.

Medication formats and clinical review

Directmeds offers compounded injectable and sublingual options for semaglutide and tirzepatide through clinician review and U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. BetterMe Rx provides injectable and oral tablet options, including a needle-free daily tirzepatide tablet, with provider review and partnered U.S. compounding pharmacies. Both state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Plans, cancellation and refunds

Directmeds highlights month-to-month cancellation for customers who choose that option and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. BetterMe Rx advertises cancel-anytime language alongside prepaid longer-term plans and notes longer plans are billed according to the selected term. If a strict month-to-month arrangement is preferred, Directmeds documents month-to-month cancellation explicitly. If a lower monthly-equivalent via a 12-month plan matters, BetterMe Rx documents those pricing tiers.

Shipping, supplies and availability

Both companies include free 1-2 day shipping and supplies with the medication and provider review. Directmeds specifies temperature-controlled next-day UPS or FedEx shipping and notes it is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. BetterMe Rx states free 1-2 day delivery and does not list state exclusions in the supplied facts.

How to decide: Directmeds vs BetterMe Rx

Choose Directmeds if you want promotional starting pricing, month-to-month cancellation options, injectable or sublingual compounded GLP-1s, no separate membership fee, explicit free refund if a prescription is not approved, and you are not in Mississippi or Louisiana. Choose BetterMe Rx if you want both injectable and oral tablet options including a needle-free tirzepatide tablet and are comfortable with prepaid 3, 6 or 12 month plans to reach the lowest monthly-equivalent pricing.

Both providers require clinician review and state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

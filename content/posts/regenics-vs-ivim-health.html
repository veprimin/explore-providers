Clinician model and program approach

Both require clinician evaluation and prescribe compounded drugs only when clinically appropriate.

Third-party validation and outcomes claims

IVIM Health reports a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot, which the source frames as a rare dual third-party verification and a trust signal. Regenics’ provided evidence highlights LegitScript-certified pharmacy networks and licensed U.S. compounding pharmacy sourcing but does not present the dual third-party rating or the cited protocol studies.

Program features, add-ons, and service details

Sesame Care vs Maximus: overview of how each service works

Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select clinicians for ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs, and messaging, with subscription care starting at $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Maximus provides telemedicine access to licensed doctors who prescribe compounded semaglutide filled through trusted U.S. pharmacies with a low flat rate of $132.99/month and no insurance billing.

Distinctive difference 1: clinician choice and network model

Sesame Care: a marketplace model where patients choose providers based on specialty, availability, and pricing. Maximus: a direct telemedicine platform where prescriptions are approved by licensed doctors and the process is fully online with no clinic visits. The evidence shows Sesame Care emphasizes choice and a large clinician network, while Maximus highlights convenient telemedicine access and prescriptions approved by licensed doctors.

Distinctive difference 2: pricing approach and insurance

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs, and messaging for as low as $59/mo and cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo, and it states insurance is accepted for weight loss medications with medication costs varying by insurance and pharmacy. Maximus operates on a direct-pay model at $132.99/month and does not bill insurance; its messaging emphasizes a predictable low flat rate.

Distinctive difference 3: branded versus compounded medications and regulatory notes

Sesame Care references branded oral and injectable options including semaglutide and tirzepatide with price examples and notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Maximus offers compounded semaglutide from U.S. pharmacies and the evidence explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Distinctive difference 4: availability, shipping, and program scale

Sesame Care advertises nationwide access in many states, flexible scheduling, and a large clinician network. Maximus states its Weight Loss Protocol is currently available in all 50 states except Mississippi, offers free expedited or priority shipping in its materials, and notes a smaller patient base and thinner public track record compared with larger competitors.

How to decide: Sesame Care vs Maximus

Maximus is best for patients seeking a simple, predictable direct-pay compounded semaglutide program at a low flat monthly rate with telemedicine prescribing and U.S. pharmacy fulfillment.

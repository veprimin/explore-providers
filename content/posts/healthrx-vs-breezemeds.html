2) Medication format and compounded status

HealthRx offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and notes that compounded medication is not FDA approved. BreezeMeds presents injectable and oral GLP 1 options, including oral semaglutide and oral tirzepatide starting at higher price points, and states that medication specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

3) Shipping and cold chain handling

HealthRx advertises overnight cold shipping with a tracked cold chain and free shipping on every plan. BreezeMeds advertises discreet home delivery and discreet shipping to your door but does not provide the same cold chain detail in the captured evidence.

4) Clinician review and medication selection

HealthRx emphasizes that you are not picking a medication off a menu. A licensed clinician reviews your file and decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is the right fit, or whether neither is, and what you are choosing is the plan it arrives on. BreezeMeds also notes treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and offers a personalized treatment plan, but the captured evidence does not repeat the clinician-decides wording used by HealthRx.

Program personalization and clinical framing

Found emphasizes a biology-first, whole-body approach and builds personalized programs around metabolic biology, hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavior. Try Ageless centers on delivering compounded GLP-1 formats prescribed by licensed U.S. clinicians, with less emphasis in the captured evidence on broader metabolic personalization.

Price structure, insurance, and entry points

Found advertises getting started for as low as $39 and states most insurance is accepted, with Found handling insurance paperwork in some programs. Try Ageless lists yearly per-month prices starting at $159 for tirzepatide drops, $199 for semaglutide injection, and $249 for tirzepatide injection, with the captured evidence noting the discount is already applied and SLIM50 stacks an extra $50 off. Try Ageless is explicitly self-pay and does not require insurance.

Medication formats, switching, and included extras

Try Ageless highlights three formats including needle-free tirzepatide oral drops, which are an uncommon compounded format and the lowest entry price on its menu. Try Ageless allows clinicians to move patients between semaglutide injection, tirzepatide injection, and drops under one plan with no new consult fee, and bundles B12 in every plan. Found lists both compounded and brand-name GLP-1s in its offerings and notes options like tirzepatide and semaglutide, with finer program details and eligibility subject to clinician review.

Safety, regulatory notes, and what is not supplied

Both providers include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Try Ageless specifically notes that oral tirzepatide drops are an off-label compounded formulation because tirzepatide is not FDA-approved in oral form. Found likewise states compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved and that Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility. The captured evidence does not supply comparative clinical outcome data or head-to-head efficacy measures.

Final comparison and how to choose

Found vs Try Ageless comes down to priorities: Found centers a personalized, biology-first program with low advertised entry pricing and insurance support, while Try Ageless offers choice of three compounded GLP-1 formats, needle-free oral drops from $159/mo, and built-in discounts on self-pay plans.

Choose Found if you want a biology-first, medically supervised weight loss program that accepts most insurance and emphasizes personalized metabolic planning.

Choose Try Ageless if you want the lowest self-pay entry price for tirzepatide drops, the option to switch formats under one plan with no extra consult fee, and bundled B12 on prepaid yearly plans.

Oak Longevity vs JoinSkinny: how their approach differs

Oak Longevity vs JoinSkinny differ primarily on price structure, pharmacy and product sourcing, clinical model and visit format, shipping timing and packaging, and plan flexibility. Below we compare those differences using only the supplied facts.

Price structure and featured starting prices

Oak Longevity advertises semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month with one flat price that covers all dosages. Oak states there are no subscriptions, no membership fees, and patients are billed monthly on a non recurring basis though three month prescriptions are billed upfront. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149 and offers monthly and 3, 6, and 12 month bundles plus promotional discounts such as a $140 offer captured on the landing page.

Product sourcing and regulatory notes

Oak Longevity sells compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and includes a regulatory note that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny states prescriptions are filled by U.S. based, FDA regulated and LegitScript certified pharmacy partners and also notes that compounded products are not FDA approved when applicable. JoinSkinny also offers GLP 1 and GLP 1 plus GIP options.

Clinical process and ongoing care

Oak describes an asynchronous clinical review where a licensed clinician reviews the online questionnaire and no video call is required. JoinSkinny describes telemedicine with prescription and telemed visits included, ongoing support, dose adjustments, monthly check ins, and 24 7 chat, with care delivered by licensed U.S. providers.

Shipping speed and handling

Oak advertises free shipping with tracking and same day approval available for qualifying patients. JoinSkinny advertises free temperature controlled shipping that arrives in 1 2 days and states prescriptions received before 2 pm central time will be shipped the same day, with next day UPS in a temperature controlled package and tracking for every shipment.

Plan flexibility, cancellations and refunds

Oak says there are no subscriptions and no auto charges; you choose to renew each month and three month prescriptions are billed upfront. Oak states no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped. JoinSkinny states there is no monthly membership and you can cancel anytime before your next billing cycle, with support available 24 7; bundle commitments and total charge require confirmation according to captured notes.

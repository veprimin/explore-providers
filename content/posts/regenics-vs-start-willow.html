Regenics is a lower-cost, nurse-practitioner-led telehealth option with compounded semaglutide starting at $139 first month and a Sermorelin add-on; Start Willow is pricier from $249 per month but emphasizes faster shipping and a physician-prescribed, personalized plan. How they differ: Price, prescribing clinician, shipping, medication type, and extras

1. Price and promotional structure

Regenics advertises compounded semaglutide at $139 first month and tirzepatide at $189 first month, with renewals noted at higher rates (semaglutide $199/month and tirzepatide $279/month unless otherwise stated). Start Willow lists semaglutide from $249 first month and ongoing $299/month. Promotional first-month pricing and renewal increases are explicitly part of Regenics messaging while Start Willow highlights a higher ongoing rate.

2. Clinician oversight and care model

Regenics describes nurse-practitioner-led GLP-1 care where licensed nurse practitioners review intake before prescriptions. Start Willow describes prescriptions issued by a doctor as part of a personalized treatment plan and emphasizes collaboration with your Willow physician for tailored dosing. Both require clinician review and determine treatment is appropriate before dispensing.

3. Medication sourcing and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide alternatives and both include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Regenics adds that its pharmacy network is LegitScript certified and compounds are prepared by U.S. licensed compounding pharmacies. Start Willow states medications are prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies operating under state board oversight and meeting USP standards.

4. Shipping speed and logistics

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less and highlights same day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping when in stock. Regenics notes free shipping with medication compounded in the USA but captured evidence did not verify a specific guaranteed delivery window. If fast guaranteed shipping is a priority, Start Willow positions itself around speed.

5. Additional offerings

Regenics also markets Sermorelin peptide therapy as an add-on option for recovery, energy, and muscle support and lists a 15mg injectable peptide at $399 in its FAQs. Start Willow emphasizes personalized care and mentions combination options and supportive materials, but a specific secondary peptide offering like Sermorelin is not supplied in the captured Start Willow evidence.

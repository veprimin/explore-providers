Quick verdict: SkinnyRx vs Regenics

SkinnyRx focuses on daily oral semaglutide tablets with FSA/HSA eligibility and free overnight shipping, while Regenics offers lower promotional entry pricing for compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide overseen by licensed nurse practitioners. Early verdict: choose SkinnyRx for oral tablet format and fast delivery; choose Regenics for lower first-month cash pricing and access to tirzepatide and Sermorelin.

How these services differ at a glance

This comparison looks at five distinctive, supported differences between SkinnyRx and Regenics: price structure, medication format and options, clinician oversight, shipping and pharmacy verification, and regulatory notes about compounded products.

1. Price and promotional structure

SkinnyRx lists semaglutide tablets as low as $249/mo. Regenics advertises semaglutide at $139 for the first month with renewals at $199/mo and tirzepatide at $189 first month, renewing at $279/mo. Regenics promotional pricing applies to the first month only; renewal rates are higher.

See Regenics first-month pricing

2. Medication format and offerings

SkinnyRx offers daily oral semaglutide tablets taken under the tongue as an alternative to injections. Regenics offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide plus a Sermorelin peptide add-on. SkinnyRx emphasizes an oral tablet format; Regenics emphasizes multiple compounded GLP-1 options and a secondary peptide line.

Learn about SkinnyRx tablets

3. Clinician oversight and care model

SkinnyRx states licensed providers tailor treatment after an online consultation and monitor progress, with prescriptions provided after clinician review. Regenics delivers care overseen by licensed nurse practitioners who review intake before anything is prescribed. Both require a clinician determination that treatment is medically appropriate.

4. Shipping and pharmacy verification

SkinnyRx offers free overnight shipping on all orders and reports partner pharmacies perform third-party testing through FDA- and DEA-compliant labs every 3 to 6 months with a plus or minus 10 percent concentration confirmation. Regenics offers free shipping with medications compounded in the USA and reports a LegitScript certified pharmacy network. Regenics captured page did not verify shipping speed beyond free shipping.

5. Regulatory and quality notes

Both providers offer compounded medications in some plans and include the regulatory caution that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. SkinnyRx specifically notes compounded semaglutide has not been approved by the FDA for weight loss and that partner pharmacies run periodic third-party testing. Regenics notes compounding by licensed U.S. pharmacies and the need for clinician appropriateness.

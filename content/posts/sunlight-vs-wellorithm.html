Medication types and regulatory notes

Sunlight promotes injections and oral options for GLP-1 treatment. Wellorithm specifically offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. The source for Wellorithm notes that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sunlight includes a regulatory reminder that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and states branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Use the platforms one at a time as you confirm medication type and regulatory details.

Clinician access and state availability

Wellorithm advertises licensed clinicians in all 50 states, which the source frames as a major advantage for patients in states with limited telehealth options. Sunlight provides telehealth clinicians and prescriptions but cautions that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change. If nationwide clinician coverage is a requirement, Wellorithm is positioned to address that need.

Delivery, membership, and support

Both providers ship medication to the home and claim fast, free shipping. Sunlight repeatedly states free and discreet 2-day shipping and no monthly membership with cancel anytime terms. Wellorithm also advertises fast, free shipping and that everything needed is included with no membership or hidden fees. Sunlight emphasizes ongoing customer support through captured testimonials; Wellorithm highlights virtual check ins to keep titration on track.

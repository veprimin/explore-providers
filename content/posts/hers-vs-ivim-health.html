1. Medication type and regulatory status

Hers: Offers FDA-approved Wegovy, explicitly stating brand-name semaglutide that is not a compounded product. The provider emphasizes name brand medication and notes Ozempic and Wegovy trademarks. Compounded medications, when offered by others, are noted as not FDA-approved.

IVIM Health: Prescribes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. IVIM also notes medications are prepared by independent, licensed compounding pharmacies and used under clinician supervision.

2. Pricing, membership, and upfront costs

The source also clarifies that price includes medication only if prescribed and membership and medication are billed separately. Medication is not available without an active membership.

IVIM Health: Lists compounded semaglutide at $75/mo and tirzepatide at $133/mo in captured landing evidence, with a noted $74.99/mo program fee. The source advertises a Limited Offer: Free Month Membership using code GIFT75 and a no-obligation free consultation valued at $75 for new patients. IVIM’s FAQ notes a first month at $99 and $174/month ongoing in another captured statement; the source states ongoing pricing and program fees in more than one place.

3. Clinical protocol and outcome claims

Hers: Highlights personalized care team support and ongoing dose check-ins. The source references FDA-approved options and emphasizes treatment is available when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. It does not capture a proprietary outcome claim.

IVIM Health: Promotes a proprietary GLP-1 ID clinician-guided protocol and cites internal outcomes. IVIM states patients following the Ivim Protocol lost an average of 46% more weight over 68 weeks compared to another published clinical GLP-1 trial, and elsewhere claims up to 40% faster weight loss with GLP-1 ID. The source qualifies these as proprietary or based on internal or non-head-to-head comparisons and notes results may vary.

4. Third-party ratings and trust signals

IVIM Health: The source repeatedly highlights unusually strong third-party satisfaction: a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot. The comparison notes this dual rating as a rare verified satisfaction signal in the market.

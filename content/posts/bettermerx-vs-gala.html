BetterMe Rx vs Gala: both are direct-pay telehealth programs offering compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, free fast shipping, and clinician review. Early verdict: BetterMe Rx emphasizes four administration options and lower monthly-equivalent pricing on longer prepaid plans, while Gala highlights a single flat price at every dose and a $220 promotion; both note compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Key differences at a glance: BetterMe Rx vs Gala

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: pricing structure and visibility, medication formats and options, billing and plan terms, shipping and included services, and regulatory notes.

1. Pricing structure and transparency

BetterMe Rx lists monthly prices and lower 3-, 6-, or 12-month equivalents with the lowest monthly-equivalent price requiring a 12-month plan. Examples supplied include semaglutide injection $205 monthly or as low as $145/month on a 12-month plan; tirzepatide injection $275 monthly or as low as $215/month; oral semaglutide $250 monthly or as low as $185/month; oral tirzepatide $265 monthly or as low as $215/month. Gala markets a flat-price approach and a $220 promotion but the base dollar price was not visible in readable page text in the captured evidence.

2. Medication formats and administration options

BetterMe Rx explicitly offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in both injectable and oral formats, including a needle-free daily tirzepatide tablet option. Gala is documented as offering compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide but the captured evidence does not specify injectable versus oral formats.

3. Billing terms, membership and plan commitments

BetterMe Rx advertises no membership fee, no insurance required, cancel-anytime language, and that longer plans are billed according to the selected term; the lowest monthly-equivalent price requires committing to a 12-month plan. Gala advertises no monthly membership and cancel-anytime language and emphasizes a single flat price at every dose with provider visit and prescription included. The Gala source also includes a $220 discount promotion; the underlying base price was not exposed in the captured text.

4. Provider review, shipping and included services

Both programs include a licensed provider review or visit and home delivery. BetterMe Rx specifies medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies and free 1-2 day shipping included. Gala promises provider visit and prescription included, free 2-day shipping, and provider check-ins and dose monitoring in its marketing content.

Regulatory and safety note

The comparison relies only on the captured statements; no other safety or efficacy claims are supplied.

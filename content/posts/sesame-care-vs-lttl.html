Pricing, insurance and membership differences

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo* based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. It states cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo and that insurance is accepted for weight loss medications, with example insurance prices as low as $25 for some dosages. Medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on your insurance status and pharmacy. Lttl advertises a promotional reduction up to $140 off your first month and highlights compounded GLP-1 pricing examples and a steady per-dose pricing approach. Lttl materials note pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Care model and clinician selection

Sesame Care emphasizes a marketplace model where patients can browse and select providers based on specialty, availability and pricing, so experience can vary by clinician. Lttl describes clinician-reviewed online treatment but pairs that with medications compounded by state-licensed pharmacies; it advises treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Medication types, regulatory notes and quality checks

Sesame Care lists branded medications (examples include semaglutide and tirzepatide dosing and prices) and notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Lttl offers compounded GLP-1 medications prepared in U.S.A. licensed pharmacies and states these compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Lttl also states medications are independently verified in FDA registered labs and confirmed every 3–6 months to stay within ± 10% of target strength.

Fulfillment, testing and transparency

Sesame Care highlights nationwide access in many states, flexible scheduling and a large clinician network, while noting medication costs vary by insurance and pharmacy. Lttl emphasizes pharmacy preparation, lab verification and home delivery and warns pricing and state coverage may change. Where Sesame Care emphasizes choice and insurance acceptance, Lttl emphasizes compounded product preparation and lab confirmation.

Conclusion and who should pick which

Sesame Care vs LTTL comes down to marketplace clinician choice and insurance flexibility versus compounded medication fulfillment and lab verification. Choose Sesame Care if you want broad telehealth choice, the ability to pick clinicians, flexible scheduling and insurance acceptance for weight loss medications. Choose Lttl if you prioritize compounded GLP-1 formulations prepared by U.S.A. licensed pharmacies with independent lab verification and promotional first-month offers.

Shed vs Luvo Health: three key differences at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: medication options and regulatory notes, integrated coaching and support model, and pricing transparency and enrollment details.

1. Medication options and regulatory status

Shed: Offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded alternatives on one platform. The source notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a provider’s prescription.

Luvo Health: Lists tirzepatide and semaglutide in injection and tablet forms and references microdosing options. The captured landing-page evidence emphasizes that compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and are not reviewed or approved by the FDA.

2. Coaching and ongoing support

Shed: Integrates continuous coaching into treatment, not just a prescription. The source positions coaching as part of every treatment plan to support titration, lifestyle changes, and plateaus throughout the program.

Luvo Health: Describes clinician-reviewed online weight-management treatment with ongoing support and dose changes across months, but the captured evidence did not publish detailed coaching structure. It stresses dose adjustments based on clinical response and cancel-anytime language.

3. Pricing transparency and enrollment process

Shed: Lists starting prices for compounded options: Compounded Semaglutide Injections starting at $199/month and Compounded Tirzepatide Injections starting at $299/month. Shed does not publish standard pricing for all plans and gives personalized quotes after intake. New patients get 10% off the first month per the source.

Luvo Health: The captured affiliate landing page did not verify a starting price. The site indicates MONTH 1, MONTH 2-3, and 3+ months pricing structure visuals were present but current details were not published or could not be verified on the capture. The source advises confirming eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

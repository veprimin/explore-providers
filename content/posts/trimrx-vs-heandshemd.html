Both providers advertise low entry prices but they work differently. Medication options and regulatory notes

TrimRx centers on compounded GLP-1 injections and a compounded GLP-1+GIP dual option. TrimRx also states branded prescriptions may be written but must be filled separately. He & She MD offers compounded semaglutide injections, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injections. Both sources include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA approved and not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Those regulatory notes are explicit for both providers and should factor into decisions.

Clinical support and coaching

TrimRx includes telehealth visits and advertises 24/7 support as part of its program. He & She MD bundles a coaching app that includes 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1 specific coaching, and progress tracking at no extra cost. The distinction is direct: TrimRx emphasizes telehealth visits and continuous support, while He & She MD packages ongoing coaching and messaging in an app experience.

How to choose between TrimRx and He & She MD

Choose TrimRx if you prioritize a predictable, per-dose price over time, access to compounded GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP injections, and 1 to 2 day shipping backed by stated scale and ratings. Choose He & She MD if you want a lower introductory price for semaglutide, in-app 1:1 clinician messaging and coaching bundled into the program, or options that include oral semaglutide and tirzepatide formats.

Regenics vs bmiMD compares two telehealth options for compounded GLP-1 or GLP-1/GIP therapy. Quick verdict: Regenics is best for straightforward entry with both semaglutide and tirzepatide at promotional first-month prices, while bmiMD is best for lower long-term monthly equivalents if you commit to multi-month plans.

Explore Regenics pricing and options

How they differ in drugs offered and clinical model

Regenics offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide plus an optional Sermorelin peptide therapy, with care described as nurse-practitioner-led and prescriptions reviewed by a licensed clinician before prescribing. bmiMD focuses on compounded tirzepatide only, with clinician review also required. Both note that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Key difference 1: Pricing structure and promotional terms

Regenics advertises promotional first-month pricing: semaglutide $139 first month (renews $199/mo) and tirzepatide $189 first month (renews $279/mo). The source states promotional pricing applies to the first month only and that monthly cost increases on renewal. bmiMD lists tiered monthly equivalents tied to term commitments: $179 month-to-month, $159/mo for three-month plan, $149/mo for six-month plan, and $139/mo for a 12-month plan, with the lowest price requiring a 12-month commitment. bmiMD highlights lower long-term monthly equivalent pricing but notes the lowest price requires a commitment and that total prepaid charge should be confirmed.

Key difference 2: Drug selection and add-ons

Regenics supports both compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and also offers a Sermorelin peptide therapy option priced in the source at $399 for a 15mg injectable peptide. bmiMD focuses exclusively on compounded tirzepatide in the captured evidence. If access to semaglutide or a secondary peptide like Sermorelin matters, Regenics is the only provider in these sources that lists both.

Key difference 3: Pricing commitments, shipping, and clinician access

Both providers offer free shipping with medication compounded in the USA or free subscription shipping. Regenics notes free shipping with medication compounded in the USA and a LegitScript certified pharmacy network. bmiMD advertises free shipping on every subscription order and 24/7 clinician access. bmiMD emphasizes online refills and delivery and a 100% Risk Free refund if not eligible for the program, as stated in the captured content. Regenics notes no insurance required and that plans are FSA/HSA eligible.

How pricing and membership differ

Refills advertises personalized compounded GLP-1 from $99/month (~$5/day) with flat pricing, the same price at every dosage, no membership or app fees, and month-to-month cancelation. Mochi Health uses a membership-plus-medication model with pricing that starts from $99/month and offers 50% Off Your 1st Month with code BESTWLHALFOFF; ongoing cost varies by plan and medication.

Consultation style and clinician interaction

Refills: after a health intake quiz a licensed clinician will review your information and you may either have a chat with a doctor or receive a prescription directly within 24 hours, with clinician consultations available at no extra cost for questions. Mochi Health: defines itself by live video visits with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes, offering a synchronous physician conversation rather than an asynchronous text review.

Product types, shipping, and availability

Both platforms offer compounded GLP-1 options. Refills emphasizes free discreet shipping, express shipping, and availability in all 50 US states, delivering a 4-week supply each month with tracking updates. Mochi Health lists compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options and highlights nationwide prescription access and ongoing appointment availability.

Regulatory and product notes

Both providers note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Refills explicitly states: “Compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality by the FDA. Prescription required.” Mochi Health includes a similar regulatory note about compounded medications.

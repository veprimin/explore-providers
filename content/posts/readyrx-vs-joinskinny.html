Five key differences

1. Pricing transparency

JoinSkinny supplies specific starting prices and promotional messaging on the captured landing page, stating plans starting at $149 and a $140 offer. ReadyRx’s captured page explicitly flagged pricing as not verified and the affiliate link was blocked before redirect.

2. Shipping and fulfillment

JoinSkinny documents free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days, same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central, next-day UPS delivery, tracking, and insured shipments. ReadyRx shipping and fulfillment details were not verified on the captured page.

3. Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both sources include regulatory caution language. ReadyRx’s captured evidence states that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny also states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality, while noting that prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners when applicable.

4. Care model and support

JoinSkinny is described as a fully online medical service with a health questionnaire, licensed provider review, telemedicine monthly check-ins, ongoing support, dose adjustments, and 24/7 chat. ReadyRx evidence captured did not include readable details about telemedicine workflow or ongoing support on the blocked landing page.

5. Eligibility and state availability

JoinSkinny states it provides care in eligible U.S. states where regulations allow telehealth-based prescribing and confirms availability during signup by entering a state. ReadyRx’s captured page did not provide verified availability or state eligibility details in the preserved evidence.

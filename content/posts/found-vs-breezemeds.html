Medication options and regulatory notes

Both providers offer GLP-1 options, but the specifics differ. Found lists tirzepatide and semaglutide and notes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are available in some cases, with the important qualifier that compounded GLP-1s are not FDA approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy and are not available in all states. BreezeMeds lists injectable and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide options, and cautions that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Both providers state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Personalization and care model

Found emphasizes a biology-first, personalized medically supervised weight loss program that evaluates hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavior to build individualized plans. BreezeMeds describes a clinician-reviewed online treatment with a personalized treatment plan, but the captured evidence emphasizes intake and clinician review rather than the broader biology-first framing. In practice, Found positions personalization around metabolic biology, while BreezeMeds emphasizes clinician review and a personalized plan without the same biology-first language.

1. Treatment approach and personalization

Found describes a biology-first approach that conducts a thorough medical evaluation and builds a personalized program around each patient’s metabolic biology, including hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavioral factors. YourEra is described as doctor-guided telehealth that prescribes compounded GLP-1 medication with a focus on telehealth delivery and flat pricing; prescriptions are issued only if a licensed clinician determines treatment is appropriate after evaluation. Both emphasize clinician review, but Found emphasizes a broader personalized program beyond medication delivery, while YourEra emphasizes streamlined doctor-prescribed compounded GLP-1 delivery.

3. Insurance, coverage, and pharmacy handling

Found states that most insurance is now accepted, that it handles insurance paperwork, and highlights ways insurance may reduce costs for branded GLP-1s for eligible members; Found also cautions that coverage, pricing, and terms vary, and Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility. YourEra markets no insurance requirement, free shipping from licensed US pharmacies, and no membership fee; it explicitly positions its plans to work without insurance and notes FSA/HSA eligibility with certain payment options. Found therefore emphasizes insurance acceptance and paperwork handling while YourEra emphasizes straightforward direct-to-consumer shipping without insurance.

4. Medication types and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options and include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished drug products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Found additionally mentions branded options such as Zepbound and Wegovy and clarifies that certain medications are FDA-approved for conditions other than weight and that Found is not affiliated with or endorsed by the brand owners. YourEra emphasizes its compounded formulations and that they are prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies pursuant to individual prescriptions and are not the same as brand-name products.

5. Included services, shipping, and support

YourEra bundles consult, prescription, cold shipping, dose adjustments, and 24/7 support with free 1–2 day discreet shipping from licensed US pharmacies. Found highlights a medically supervised program that targets hormones, metabolism, and lifestyle and says it handles insurance paperwork; Found also notes membership plans, flexible pricing, and that results vary by individual. If bundled shipping speed and flat pricing at every dose matter most, YourEra emphasizes that. If a holistic medically supervised plan and insurance handling matter, Found emphasizes that.

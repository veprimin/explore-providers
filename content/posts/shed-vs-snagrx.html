Quick verdict: Shed vs SnagRx boils down to coaching and medication choice versus predictable low pricing. Shed pairs name-brand and compounded GLP-1 options with continuous coaching and a 2-minute approval assessment, while SnagRx focuses on compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with locked flat pricing as low as $69/month on a 12-month plan. Read on for a direct comparison of the most distinctive differences.

Key differences at a glance: medication type, pricing model, and support

The most important distinctions for Shed vs SnagRx are: (1) medication variety and regulatory status, (2) pricing structure and commitments, and (3) clinical support and coaching. These three areas determine whether a patient prioritizes branded options and behavioral support or lowest locked-in compounded pricing.

1) Medication options and regulatory notes

Shed: Offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded alternatives. The source emphasizes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a provider’s prescription. Medication is prescribed only after a clinician review determines treatment is appropriate.

SnagRx: Offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide only; compounded medications are produced in regulated facilities but are not FDA-approved finished products. SnagRx names its US 503A partner pharmacies and states that OpenLoop Health clinicians decide prescription eligibility after intake.

2) Pricing structure and what you pay

Shed: Lists compounded semaglutide injections starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month, and advertises 10% off your first month. Shed does not publish standard pricing for all plans; patients receive personalized quotes after intake and coaching is bundled into monthly cost rather than quoted separately.

SnagRx: Advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on the 12-month plan, with month-to-month equivalents of $99 and $149. The $69 and $119 rates require a 12-month commitment; SnagRx highlights a price-locked flat rate at every dose for the duration of treatment.

3) Clinical support, coaching, and timelines

Shed: Integrates continuous coaching into treatment rather than providing prescription-only service. Shed promotes a 2-minute approval assessment followed by clinician review and coaching that starts with the plan. This model is positioned for patients seeking behavioral support alongside medication.

SnagRx: Does not provide coaching or nutrition programming. SnagRx provides clinician review through OpenLoop Health, with intake that takes about five minutes and clinicians typically reviewing within five hours and always within 24 hours, plus next-day shipping for approved orders.

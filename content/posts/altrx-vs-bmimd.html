Quick verdict: In an altRx vs bmiMD comparison, altRx is the lower-cost, no-commitment option with a limited offer for compounded semaglutide from $89/mo and compounded tirzepatide from $149/mo, while bmiMD focuses on compounded tirzepatide with multi-month pricing that drops to $139/mo but requires longer prepaid commitments for the lowest rate.

How altRx vs bmiMD differ at a glance

The most important differences between altRx and bmiMD are pricing structure, medication focus, shipping and turnaround, commitment and billing options, and clinician access. These points drive which program fits a given patient.

1. Pricing and billing options

altRx advertises a limited offer: compounded semaglutide starting at $89/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $149/month. altRx also lists multi-month discounts in its FAQ that drop semaglutide to $85/mo on 3 months and as low as $75/mo on 12 months, and similar multi-month reductions for tirzepatide. altRx emphasizes no membership, no hidden fees, and pay-later options with Affirm, Klarna, Afterpay.

bmiMD offers compounded tirzepatide with four billing options: $179 month-to-month, $159/mo on a 3-month plan, $149/mo on a 6-month plan, and $139/mo on a 12-month plan. bmiMD notes the lowest price requires a 12-month commitment and advises confirming the total prepaid charge.

3. Shipping, turnaround and kit contents

altRx advertises free, discreet 2-day delivery and a complete treatment kit that includes vials, syringes, prep pads, and step-by-step guidance. Typical processing and delivery time is approximately 4–5 business days after prescription fulfillment.

bmiMD advertises free subscription shipping on every order and states prescriptions are prepared in a state-licensed pharmacy and delivered to your door; captured copy highlights reliable refills and timely arrival but does not specify a 2-day delivery promise.

4. Commitment, cancellations, and refunds

altRx promotes no commitment and cancel anytime membership terms and says there are no membership, insurance or hidden fees. altRx also highlights buy-now-pay-later options. bmiMD offers a 100% refund if a patient is not eligible for the program and notes the lowest monthly equivalent requires a longer prepay commitment; bmiMD advises confirming the total prepaid charge.

5. Clinician review and ongoing support

altRx states medical oversight is included in the monthly fee, with a licensed provider reviewing the health evaluation and conducting telehealth consultations, and a care team available throughout membership. bmiMD highlights 24/7 clinician access and online refills and delivery, with clinician confirmation of treatment appropriateness.

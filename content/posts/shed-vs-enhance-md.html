4. Pricing transparency and escalation

Enhance MD highlights flat-rate pricing that stays the same at every dose, explicitly noting no fee escalation during titration. Shed uses a personalized pricing quote after intake and bundles coaching into monthly cost, and the source notes name-brand options carry significantly higher costs than compounded alternatives. Which model suits you depends on whether predictable dose-independent pricing or bundled coaching and brand access is more important.

Both sources state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by licensed pharmacies based on a provider prescription. Enhance MD additionally notes it has treated more than 10,000 patients. Shed highlights that treatment is available only after a clinician determines it is medically appropriate and repeats the regulatory note on compounded medications.

Three distinctive differences between Enhance MD and Telos Rx

1) Advertised starting prices

Enhance MD advertises semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month. Telos Rx advertises semaglutide as low as $99 per month and tirzepatide as low as $139 per month. Those starting-price statements are the clearest, supported pricing claims in each provider’s source material and are often decisive for cost-sensitive patients.

2) Pricing model and dose escalation

Enhance MD emphasizes flat-rate pricing: the same price at every dose with no fee escalation during titration and the lowest first-month price for both compounds. Telos Rx materials repeatedly note “as low as” pricing and state that your price depends on your plan; Telos Rx also highlights cancel anytime terms. The difference: Enhance MD highlights flat-rate stability once ongoing pricing is set; Telos Rx emphasizes variable pricing tied to approval and plan.

3) Transparency and enrollment caveats

Enhance MD cites concrete first-month numbers and calls out their 10,000+ patients treated figure in the source. Telos Rx warns readers to confirm eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage, and renewal terms before enrollment, and its source repeatedly notes pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change. That makes Telos Rx’s messaging more conditional in the captured evidence.

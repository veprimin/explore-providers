Patients begin by creating an account and completing an online intake. The flow is optimized for quick decisions while collecting clinically relevant information.

Licensed clinicians review the submitted information, ask follow-up questions if needed, and determine appropriate treatment. This step follows standard telehealth protocols.

Approved prescriptions are fulfilled through partner pharmacies, many of which are U.S.-based or licensed partners. Pharmacy fulfillment includes labeling, dispensing, and shipping coordination.

### Shipping & ongoing care

Medication delivery is handled with standard shipping options, and HIMS offers ongoing care via follow-up questionnaires and clinician access for adjustments. Monthly plans simplify refills and continuity of treatment.

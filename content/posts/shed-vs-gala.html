Shed offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 and compounded alternatives with continuous coaching and a 2-minute approval assessment. Key differences to consider in Shed vs Gala

1. Medication options: name-brand plus compounded vs compounded only

Shed explicitly offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. The source notes name-brand options provide regulatory assurance but typically cost more. Gala emphasizes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide as its offering. Both providers include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

2. Care model: continuous coaching vs provider check-ins

Shed integrates continuous coaching into treatment, not just a prescription, framing coaching as support through titration, lifestyle changes, and plateaus. Gala includes provider check-ins and dose monitoring as part of its direct-pay service and states provider visits and prescriptions are included. This is a direct contrast: Shed bundles ongoing coaching support while Gala emphasizes scheduled provider oversight and visit inclusion.

3. Pricing transparency and promotions

Shed lists starting prices for compounded injections: compounded semaglutide starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month and advertises 10% off your first month. Shed also states standard pricing is not published and you receive a personalized quote after intake. Gala advertises a flat price model and a promotion of $220 off, claims one flat price at every dose with no hidden fees, and free two-day shipping, but captured evidence notes Gala’s base dollar price was not visible in readable page text.

4. Membership and cancellation differences

Shed promotes a Shed Membership as an option and notes coaching is bundled into monthly cost. The source references Terms and Conditions for a weight-loss guarantee. Gala repeatedly states no monthly membership and cancel anytime in captured text, highlighting a cancel-anytime, no-membership approach.

5. Speed and logistics: 2-minute assessment vs free two-day shipping

Shed uses a 2-minute online approval assessment to check eligibility quickly, followed by clinician review. Gala highlights free two-day shipping and says medication arrives at home; it also emphasizes that no in-office visits or insurance are required. Both require clinician review to determine medical appropriateness before prescriptions are issued.

The comparison Shed vs Sesame Care comes down to treatment model: Shed pairs name-brand and compounded GLP-1 with continuous coaching and a fast 2-minute approval assessment, while Sesame Care is a clinician marketplace that emphasizes choice, flexible booking, and pricing as low as $59/mo. Early verdict: choose Shed if you want bundled coaching plus name-brand or compounded options; choose Sesame Care if you prefer to pick your own clinician and lower subscription entry pricing.

Distinctive difference 1: Medication offerings and regulatory notes

Shed supports both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded alternatives on one platform. The source explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a provider’s prescription. Sesame Care lists branded oral and injectable GLP-1s through its clinician network, and the captured evidence notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed. That means Shed emphasizes offering both routes directly, while Sesame Care focuses on clinician access to branded options with approval checks via providers.

Distinctive difference 2: Coaching bundled vs clinician marketplace

Shed integrates continuous coaching into treatment so coaching starts with the program rather than being optional. The source positions this as a differentiator for patients who want behavioral support alongside medication. By contrast, Sesame Care is a marketplace where patients browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing, giving you more control over who manages your care. The marketplace model means experience can vary by clinician while Shed standardizes coaching as part of its program.

Distinctive difference 3: Pricing structure and entry points

Shed lists compounded semaglutide starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month and advertises 10% off your first month. The source also notes personalized quotes after intake for name-brand options. Sesame Care advertises ongoing video visits, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month commitment and cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo, with medication costs varying by insurance and pharmacy. The captured evidence emphasizes that medication costs are not included in Sesame Care subscription pricing.

Distinctive difference 4: Speed and logistics

Shed highlights a 2-minute online assessment to check eligibility quickly, followed by clinician review before issuing a prescription. Shed states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that a prescription is issued after consultation. Sesame Care emphasizes easy booking, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states via a large clinician network. The practical difference is rapid eligibility screening at Shed versus clinician selection and appointment flexibility at Sesame Care.

Other practical points from the sources

  • Shed promotes a membership path and a weight-loss guarantee referenced in its Terms and Conditions; details require review of those terms.
  • Sesame Care notes results milestones based on clinical study contexts and that results may vary. Medication costs and in-network prices can differ widely depending on insurance.

Shed vs Sesame Care: which to choose

Both platforms connect you with GLP-1 weight-loss care, but the primary distinction is treatment style. Shed is a single platform that bundles continuous coaching with both name-brand and compounded options and promotes a fast eligibility check and a 10% off first month offer for new patients. Sesame Care is a marketplace that gives you clinician choice, flexible booking, and lower subscription entry pricing with cash-pay meds starting at $149/mo and subscription services as low as $59/mo.

Key differences at a glance: price, medication type, and access

Direct comparison shows three decisive distinctions: Hers markets FDA-approved Wegovy® (brand-name semaglutide) with pricing starting at $149/mo and membership requirements, while TMates offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide at a flat $199/month with no step-up fees. Medication type and regulatory notes

Hers provides FDA-approved Wegovy®, explicitly stating it is not a compounded product. TMates supplies compounded semaglutide formulated in U.S. facilities and notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Both providers reference the regulatory distinction between FDA-approved brand-name products and compounded alternatives.

Why this matters

The source for Hers emphasizes FDA-approved brand-name GLP-1s, no compounded medications, and that compounded medications are not FDA-reviewed. TMates stresses that its compounded semaglutide uses the same active ingredient as Ozempic and Wegovy but is compounded and therefore not an FDA-approved finished product.

TMates offers a flat $199/month at every dose with no step-up fees as dosage escalates and lists various multi-month tiers in captured landing-page evidence.

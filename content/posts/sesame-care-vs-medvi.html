Quick verdict: Sesame Care vs MedVi

Sesame Care and MedVi both provide telehealth paths to GLP-1 weight care, but they take different approaches: Sesame Care is a clinician marketplace with plans starting at $59/mo and options for branded medications with insurance acceptance, while MedVi focuses on clinically dosed compounded semaglutide delivered after a simple online intake starting at a promotional $179 for the first month with free shipping. Early verdict: choose Sesame Care for clinician choice and insurance-friendly branded options; choose MedVi for a simple, no-clinic process and a promotional first-month price.

How they differ: model, meds, pricing, and support

Marketplace choice versus streamlined single-path care

Sesame Care operates as a telehealth marketplace where patients browse and select clinicians by specialty, availability, and pricing, giving more control over the care relationship. MedVi uses a straightforward online intake and clinician review with no clinic visits required, emphasizing simplicity and 24/7 certified medical support.

Medication approach: branded and insurance options versus compounded semaglutide

Sesame Care lists branded options including semaglutide and tirzepatide with cash-pay and in-network pricing; medication costs vary with insurance status and pharmacy and Sesame notes branded and compounded products are not interchangeable and approval status must be confirmed. MedVi prescribes clinically dosed compounded semaglutide, notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved, and says providers will explore compounded GLP-1s if appropriate.

Pricing structure and promotional differences

Sesame Care advertises ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs, and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment, and cash-pay medication examples such as semaglutide cash-pay as low as $149/mo and tirzepatide cash-pay starting at $299/mo. MedVi promotes a winter first-month price of $179 including fast, free shipping, with ongoing refills locked at a higher full price and a standalone month quoted at $299 or a three-month plan at $649.

Support and convenience

Sesame Care highlights a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states. MedVi highlights a simple online process with no clinic visits and 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and medication shipping included for the promotional month.

What this means for results and expectations

Both platforms note results vary. Sesame Care references milestones from a study of injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg when paired with lifestyle changes and states results may vary; medication costs are separate from subscription and depend on insurance. MedVi reports patient-reported averages and notes results vary based on adherence, dosing, and clinician discretion, and it cautions compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved.

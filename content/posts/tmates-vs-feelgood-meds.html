If you want a direct comparison of TMates vs FeelGood Meds, the quick verdict is: TMates emphasizes a flat $199/month price with 24/7 U.S. doctor access and consistent dose pricing, while FeelGood Meds highlights lower starting rates for compounded injections at $149/month and offers compounded tablets and a branded option at much higher prices. This guide compares the two across price, formulations, clinician access, shipping and eligibility so you can decide quickly.

See TMates $199/month plan

Price and billing: flat-rate versus lower starting options

TMates lists a flat $199/month price described as the same price at every dose, with examples showing per-month rates across term options and a direct-pay model. FeelGood Meds shows compounded injections starting at $149/month, compounded tablets starting at $249/month, and a branded option shown from $1,999/month. TMates also states it does not bill insurance for its flat $199/month model while FeelGood Meds emphasizes no insurance required for its advertised starting prices.

Medication types and formulations

Both providers offer compounded options. TMates specifically promotes clinically dosed compounded semaglutide formulated in U.S.-based facilities and notes no branded Ozempic, Wegovy, or Zepbound on its offering. FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections and compounded tablets and also displays a branded medication option starting at a much higher price. Both sources include the regulatory reminder that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Regulatory and eligibility notes

Both providers include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Each source also indicates availability is subject to medical eligibility and clinician review; FeelGood Meds specifically shows that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Final take on TMates vs FeelGood Meds

For a straightforward verdict: TMates is best if you value a flat $199/month fee with 24/7 U.S. doctor access and U.S.-formulated compounded semaglutide at the same price across doses. FeelGood Meds is best if your priority is the lowest advertised starting price for compounded injections at $149/month or if you want tablet options or a visible branded option at higher price points. Both emphasize compounded options and note the regulatory limits on compounded medications.

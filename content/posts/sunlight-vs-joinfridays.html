Medication options and regulatory notes

Sunlight presents injections and oral GLP-1 options and highlights prescription and telehealth included. JoinFridays offers both compounded tirzepatide and compounded semaglutide, and also lists name-brand options separately. JoinFridays explicitly notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sunlight includes a regulatory reminder that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Clinician care and treatment model

Both platforms use clinician review. Sunlight markets a 100% online medical visit with clinician adjustment of doses as exemplified in captured testimonials. JoinFridays emphasizes personalized, clinician-guided care rather than a templated protocol and includes clinician review for medical appropriateness of treatment.

Membership, cancellation, and promotions

Sunlight advertises no monthly membership and the ability to cancel anytime, plus promotional messaging such as “Get $140 Off Today” in captured landing-page evidence. JoinFridays highlights no membership fees, cancel anytime, and active promo codes like NEWYOU12 and NYNY12 with stated ranges of discount; captured FAQ warns promo availability changes over time. JoinFridays notes multi-month plans lower effective rates and provides sample plan pricing.

Shipping and fulfillment

Sunlight claims free, discreet 1-2 day shipping and repeats “Fast & Free Shipping” across captured landing-page evidence. JoinFridays landing evidence does not supply specific shipping speed claims in the provided source; it does note that actual product appearance and labeling may differ from website images when compounded medications are offered.

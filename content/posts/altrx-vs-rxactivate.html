Three distinctive differences: Pricing visibility, eligibility gating, and delivery details

1. Pricing visibility and advertised offers

altRx advertises concrete limited offers and specific starting prices including multi-month discounts and buy now, pay later options through Affirm, Klarna, and Afterpay. RxActivate publishes no price or billing terms prior to collecting contact information, so consumers cannot confirm costs without engaging with the intake flow.

2. Eligibility and clinician matching

Both services require clinician review before treatment is provided. RxActivate emphasizes a two-minute eligibility questionnaire and states plans are clinician-matched with state and medical safety screening. altRx also uses licensed provider review and telehealth consultation, with the provider determining if treatment is appropriate and prescribing compounded agents when indicated.

3. Compounded medication and regulatory notes

altRx explicitly markets compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. RxActivate notes that when compounded medications are offered, they are not FDA-approved and approval status must be confirmed. Both preserve that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed.

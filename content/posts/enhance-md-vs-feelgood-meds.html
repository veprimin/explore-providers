Enhance MD offers both semaglutide and tirzepatide as clinician-directed compounded medications. The source emphasizes both compounds on one platform. Enhance MD promotes a flat-rate pricing model with “the same price at every dose” and no fee escalation during titration; the long description repeats flat-rate pricing language and notes ongoing flat-rate pricing may be higher after the introductory period. FeelGood Meds highlights “no membership or hidden fees” and free shipping, but the source also notes medication-specific renewal terms require confirmation and that eligibility, pricing and renewal terms are subject to the live offer.

4. Shipping, fees, membership, and guarantees

FeelGood Meds explicitly lists free shipping and no membership fee in captured landing-page evidence, plus a Weight Loss Money Back Guarantee mentioned in its marketing. Enhance MD operates on a direct-pay model and the source states it does not bill insurance. The Enhance MD source also repeats promotional language about saving up to $150 on first order in captured landing-page text. Where the evidence is silent, no additional claims are made.

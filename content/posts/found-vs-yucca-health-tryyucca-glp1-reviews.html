How they differ: care model, pricing, medication options

The most distinctive differences between Found and Yucca Health are (1) care model and personalization, (2) pricing and insurance handling, and (3) medication source and fulfillment. Below we compare these areas directly using only supplied evidence.

1. Care model and personalization

Found describes a biology-first approach that builds a personalized medically supervised program around each patient, targeting hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavior. Yucca Health provides personalized compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide plans but emphasizes fast online approval and fully remote intake with provider review within about 24 hours.

2. Pricing and insurance

Found advertises affordable monthly plans and explicitly notes most insurance is now accepted; it also lists get started for as low as $39 and details about Medicare Bridge programs and insurance paperwork handling. Yucca Health offers compounded semaglutide from $146/month and tirzepatide from $258/month, does not accept health insurance, and provides Buy Now Pay Later options like Klarna, Afterpay, and Affirm.

3. Medication source and shipping

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1s in some form and the supplied evidence emphasizes regulatory notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety or effectiveness. Yucca Health highlights licensed U.S. compounding pharmacies, UPS 2-Day Air shipping, and licensed providers in all 50 states. Found mentions both compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and states compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved or available in all states; Found also notes Rx subject to provider review and clinical eligibility.

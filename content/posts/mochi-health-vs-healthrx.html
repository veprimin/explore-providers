Consultation experience and clinician interaction

Mochi Health’s defining feature is live video visits with a licensed physician, bookable in about 5 minutes, which the source says is fundamentally different from the asynchronous questionnaire review many competitors use. HealthRx emphasizes that a clinician reviews your file and decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is appropriate, but captured evidence does not describe live video as Mochi does.

Pricing structure and promotional differences

Mochi Health operates on a membership-plus-medication model with pricing from about $99/month and a promotional 50% off first month via code BESTWLHALFOFF. Mochi notes a $39 promotional first month rate for certain treatments for new customers and that ongoing pricing varies by plan and medication. HealthRx highlights prepaid plans where the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid commitment, for example $99/month on a 12-month semaglutide plan and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month.

Compounded medication and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 options in the captured evidence. The sources explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Mochi’s listing includes compounded semaglutide starting at $60 and compounded tirzepatide starting at $90 in landing-page evidence. HealthRx notes compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription and that compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom.

Shipping, dose changes, and plan stability

HealthRx specifically advertises overnight cold shipping with cold chain tracking included on every plan and that plan price does not change when your dose does. Mochi states treatments are shipped to your door and includes ongoing appointment availability and 24/7 support, but shipping cadence and cold chain details were not supplied in the captured Mochi evidence.

Support, appointments, and ongoing care

Mochi promotes 24/7 support plus ongoing appointment availability and says members continue care beyond their first month. HealthRx materials emphasize that care is clinician reviewed and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Neither source in the captured evidence guarantees specific outcomes.

Final comparison and how to choose

In short, Mochi Health vs HealthRx centers on whether you value a synchronous video appointment and membership-plus-medication pricing or a prepaid plan with overnight cold shipping and price stability when doses change. Both offer compounded medication options and both include regulatory notes that compounded medications are not FDA approved.

Choose Mochi Health if you want a live video visit with a licensed physician bookable in about 5 minutes, membership-plus-medication pricing starting around $99/month, and 24/7 support.

Choose HealthRx if you prefer prepaid plan pricing with overnight cold chain shipping included, plans from $99/month on a 12-month semaglutide plan, and a plan price that does not change when your dose does.

Quick verdict: For lowest advertised starting price choose altRx; for a flat all inclusive price with provider check ins choose Gala. This article compares altRx vs Gala on price, care model, shipping, compounded status, and transparency so you can decide which fits your needs.

Price and pricing transparency

altRx advertises compounded semaglutide starting at $89/month and compounded tirzepatide at $149/month, including multi month plan price breaks noted in captured FAQ entries. Gala’s page advertises compounded semaglutide from $249/mo in readable text and promotes a $220 discount and a flat all inclusive price, but the base dollar price was not exposed in readable page text. Both operate as direct pay platforms with claims of no hidden fees.

Care model and clinical oversight

Both altRx and Gala provide telehealth based care. altRx emphasizes 100 percent online medical visits and a licensed provider review that may be live video or asynchronous. Gala notes provider check ins and that a provider visit and prescription are included. altRx highlights ongoing care team support; Gala highlights provider check ins and dose monitoring as part of the platform.

Shipping, membership and cancellation

altRx advertises free and discreet 2 day delivery, no membership, no insurance required, and cancel anytime. Gala advertises free 2 day shipping, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime. Both state no insurance is needed and both reference free two day shipping in captured landing page text.

Compounded medication status and regulatory note

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide in their captured materials. The sources for altRx and Gala each include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. altRx explicitly states prescriptions are filled by state licensed 503A compounding pharmacies in its network.

  • Supply chain wording: altRx cites state licensed 503A compounding pharmacies in its network. Gala indicates home delivery and free two day shipping but captured text did not list pharmacy specifics.

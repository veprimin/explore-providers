Pricing and billing structure

Pallas Health publishes a compounded semaglutide price of $139 for the first month, then $597 every 12 weeks which the source frames as a $199/month average. Compounded tirzepatide is $179 first month then $897 every 12 weeks ($299/month average). Pallas also lists cash-pay prices for brand-name medications and states it does not bill insurance. Strut Health is described as having oral GLP-1 from $199 per month in one part of the source, while captured landing evidence also states $99 and free shipping for oral semaglutide; the sources present both price points. Both providers say no insurance billing is needed for their models in the captured material.

Clinical model and intake

Strut: Positions guided consults with a clinical team focused on oral medication management, and it notes compounded semaglutide prescriptions require medical necessity reviewed by a licensed physician.

Regulatory and safety notes

Both sources include the regulatory point that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Strut likewise notes compounded semaglutide is prepared at a U.S.-licensed compounding pharmacy pursuant to an individualized prescription and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Head-to-head: price structure and advertised starting costs

The clearest contrast between Refills and CoreAgeRx is how they position pricing. Refills promotes flat-price GLP-1 from $99/month (~$5/day) and states the same price at every dosage. CoreAgeRx markets flat monthly pricing with no membership fee and specific monthly prices listed: semaglutide at $79/month and tirzepatide at $129/month. CoreAgeRx emphasizes “Same Price. Every Month.” to avoid midstream hikes.

How pricing affects ongoing costs and transparency

Refills stresses a single bundled monthly price that includes prescriptions, clinician consultations, and ongoing support with no membership or app fees. CoreAgeRx warns that many programs raise monthly bills over time and positions its plan as avoiding surprise price increases. Both providers say no insurance is required, and both suggest confirming eligibility and clinician review prior to treatment.

Medication type, compounding, and regulatory notes

Refills offers personalized compounded GLP-1 and explicitly states that compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality by the FDA. CoreAgeRx notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and indicates that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Both providers require that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Support, shipping, and patient experience

Refills advertises free discreet shipping, express shipping options, and 24/7 health concierge support with unlimited access to patient care team and clinicians at no extra cost. Refills also highlights availability in all 50 US States and month-to-month plans you can cancel anytime. CoreAgeRx highlights a 3-minute online visit and emphasizes no membership fees and locked monthly price, but detailed shipping or concierge features were not supplied in the captured CoreAgeRx evidence.

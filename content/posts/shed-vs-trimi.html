1. Medication options: name-brand plus compounded vs compounded-only

Shed supports both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded alternatives, letting patients choose based on budget and preference. Trimi focuses on compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prescribed by US-licensed clinicians. Regulatory note for both: compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

2. Pricing structure and transparency

Shed does not publish standard pricing for every plan; you receive a personalized quote after intake and new patients can get 10% off the first month. Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/month on the annual plan and flat pricing that does not rise as dose increases, with tirzepatide reported at $125/month on annual plans.

3. Coaching, clinical oversight, and guarantees

Shed markets continuous coaching integrated into treatment rather than a prescription-only model, aimed at supporting titration and lifestyle changes. Trimi emphasizes licensed U.S. providers, a named clinical oversight network led by Dr. Sean Arora, free overnight shipping, and a 6-month money-back promise if you meet terms and do not lose weight.

4. Approval, shipping, and program mechanics

Shed uses a 2-minute online assessment to check eligibility, then a licensed clinician reviews your case before a prescription is issued and medication shipped. Trimi prescribes through licensed clinicians and ships from FDA-regulated compounding pharmacies with free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and a certificate of analysis available on request.

Who each provider is best for

Shed is the strongest choice for patients who want more than a prescription: access to name-brand options and integrated coaching. Trimi is best for people who confirm eligibility and want predictable, flat compounded pricing with free overnight shipping and a 6-month refund option.

Conclusion: Which should you pick in the Shed vs Trimi comparison?

Both platforms prescribe GLP-1 therapies with clinician review, but they prioritize different tradeoffs. Shed pairs name-brand and compounded options with continuous coaching and a quick 2-minute assessment, and offers 10% off your first month. Trimi emphasizes low, flat compounded pricing per dose, free overnight shipping, and a 6-month money-back promise subject to terms.

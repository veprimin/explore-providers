How they differ at a glance: Mochi Health vs JoinSkinny

1. Clinical visit format

Mochi Health: live synchronous video visits with a licensed physician bookable in about 5 minutes, allowing a real-time conversation where the doctor evaluates your case before any prescription is issued. JoinSkinny: a clinician-reviewed model that begins with a quick health questionnaire and provider review; consultations are described as quick and clear but the captured evidence emphasizes an intake questionnaire reviewed by a licensed provider rather than Mochi’s live video claim.

2. Pricing structure and transparency

Mochi Health uses a membership-plus-medication model with promotional pricing noted: new patients can get 50% off their first month with code BESTWLHALFOFF and membership-plus-medication pricing starts from $99/month, though ongoing cost varies by plan and medication type. JoinSkinny advertises flat-rate plans starting at $149, same price every dose, no membership fee, and monthly or multi-month bundle options. JoinSkinny emphasizes transparent flat-rate pricing and cancel anytime before the next billing cycle.

3. Medication type, sourcing, and regulatory notes

Mochi Health promotes compounded options such as compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with starting prices listed and explicitly notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny states prescriptions are filled by licensed U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners and that some products are compounded; the captured evidence also includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

4. Shipping, fulfillment, and support

JoinSkinny details free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1-2 days for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time and provides tracking and insured shipments, with an exception process if incorrect shipping information is provided. Mochi Health notes doctor-prescribed treatments shipped to your door and 24/7 support plus ongoing appointment availability throughout the program; specific shipping timing details are not supplied in the captured Mochi evidence.

5. Cancellation, bundles, and commitments

JoinSkinny highlights no monthly membership, the ability to cancel before the next billing cycle, and monthly or 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles with installment options. Mochi Health uses prepaid multi-month plans in some states and notes that terms vary by state; because Mochi is membership-plus-medication, ongoing pricing varies and is less transparent than flat-rate competitors.

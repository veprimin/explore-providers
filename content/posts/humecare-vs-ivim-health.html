Head-to-head snapshot: what differs most between HumeCare+ vs IVIM Health

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: pricing and entry cost, bundled supportive ingredients and tracking, clinician-guided optimization and third-party ratings, evidence claims and money-back language, and the regulatory status of compounded medications.

Price and entry cost

HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/mo and a tirzepatide plus B12 and glycine protocol from $299/mo. IVIM Health advertises lower starting pricing (listed as $75/mo on landing content) and promotional offers including a free month membership with code GIFT75. IVIM also notes an ongoing published price path in its materials that can rise after promotional periods; HumeCare+ pricing is presented as monthly protocol pricing. Neither provider bills insurance in the supplied facts.

Bundled supports and tracking

HumeCare+ sells a bundled protocol: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide combined with glycine and vitamins B12 and B6, plus monthly body-composition scans and an app for tracking. HumeCare+ positions these elements as muscle-preserving and side-effect support, and it says it tracks lean mass, not just weight. IVIM Health emphasizes a clinician-guided GLP-1 ID protocol and structured titration but does not present the same bundled ingredient list or monthly body-composition scans in the supplied evidence.

Clinician optimization, protocols, and third-party validation

IVIM Health highlights a proprietary GLP-1 ID clinician-guided protocol and dual 4.9-star ratings on Google and Trustpilot as a satisfaction signal. IVIM also references internal and published observational studies supporting its outcomes, while noting these are not head-to-head randomized trials. HumeCare+ emphasizes continuous physician optimization and 24/7 U.S. clinician access and app-based support; HumeCare+ cites internal analyses of member results and presents a money-back promise under specified terms.

Evidence claims and guarantees

HumeCare+ advertises a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months and refers to an internal analysis of 6,846 members. HumeCare+ also cites specific clinical references alongside its program messaging. IVIM Health cites published retrospective observational studies in Obesity Pillars and a proprietary claim that its protocol can accelerate weight loss by up to 40% compared to a referenced GLP-1 trial; IVIM notes that this is a protocol claim and not an independent head-to-head trial.

Regulatory status and safety notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications. The supplied material for each includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. HumeCare+ explicitly states compounded medications offered are dispensed from U.S. pharmacies licensed by state Boards of Pharmacy; IVIM notes compounding by independent licensed compounding pharmacies and that products are not available in all states per its materials.

Conclusion: HumeCare+ vs IVIM Health

HumeCare+ vs IVIM Health breaks down to bundled supportive care and body-composition tracking with a money-back promise at HumeCare+ versus lower entry pricing, a free month code, and unusually strong third-party ratings at IVIM Health. Both supply compounded GLP-1 medications and note that compounded drugs are not FDA-approved.

Choose HumeCare+ if you want a GLP-1 protocol that combines glycine, vitamins B12 and B6, monthly body-composition scans, an app, and a 10%-or-money-back promise under specified terms. Choose IVIM Health if you want lower promotional entry pricing, a no-obligation free consultation and free month code, plus a program highlighted by dual 4.9-star reviews and a clinician-guided GLP-1 ID protocol.

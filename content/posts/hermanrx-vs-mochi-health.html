HermanRx vs Mochi Health answers a core choice: do you want flat, all-inclusive monthly GLP-1 pricing or a membership model built around live video doctor visits? Early verdict: HermanRx is for people who prefer a single all-inclusive monthly price and no membership or consultation fee; Mochi Health is for people who prioritize synchronous live video visits and lower promotional entry pricing.

Quick comparison: HermanRx vs Mochi Health

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: pricing structure and transparency, visit style and clinical model, availability and shipping, medication type and regulatory notes, and speed of service.

1. Pricing structure and transparency

HermanRx lists clear flat monthly rates for compounded GLP-1s: semaglutide + B12 at $249/month and tirzepatide + B12 at $349/month. Those prices are described as all-inclusive, covering the provider review, prescription, medication, ongoing support and free discreet shipping with no membership fee and no separate consultation charge.

Mochi Health uses a membership-plus-medication model. Mochi advertises membership plus medication starting from $99/month and promotional first-month discounts such as 50% off with a code. Mochi notes the membership structure means total monthly cost varies by plan and medication type.

2. Visit style and clinical experience

HermanRx matches patients with licensed, board-certified physicians and documents an intake review process with about a 5-minute assessment and provider review in ~24 hours, typically asynchronous. Mochi Health emphasizes synchronous live video visits bookable in about 5 minutes, allowing a real-time conversation with a doctor who evaluates the case during the video consultation.

3. Medication offerings and regulatory note

Both platforms provide compounded GLP-1 options. HermanRx specifically lists compounded semaglutide + B12 and compounded tirzepatide + B12 at its all-inclusive prices and states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Mochi Health likewise offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options with a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

4. Availability, shipping and timelines

HermanRx states availability in all 50 states with free discreet shipping in plain unmarked packaging, a roughly 5-minute assessment, ~24 hour provider review and ~72 hour delivery when prescribed, while noting a broader general FAQ window of 3 to 7 business days for many orders. Mochi Health also advertises prescription access in all 50 states and ongoing appointment availability with 24/7 support; specific shipping timing was not supplied in detail beyond treatments being shipped to your door.

5. Speed, support and extras

HermanRx highlights a published timeline and 24/7 patient support with a secure patient portal and the ability to consolidate multiple treatment categories on one platform. Mochi highlights 24/7 support and ongoing appointments with the distinctive option of live video visits as the primary differentiator.

Final thoughts on HermanRx vs Mochi Health

HermanRx vs Mochi Health comes down to a simple trade-off in the sources: flat, transparent all-inclusive pricing plus consolidated services at HermanRx versus Mochi Health’s live video visits and membership-plus-medication pricing with promotional first-month offers. Both offer compounded GLP-1s and both note compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Choose HermanRx if you want a single, all-inclusive monthly price for compounded semaglutide + B12 ($249/mo) or compounded tirzepatide + B12 ($349/mo), no membership fee, and stated fast provider review and delivery timelines.

Choose Mochi Health if you prioritize booking a live video visit with a doctor in about 5 minutes, ongoing appointment availability, and membership pricing with promotional first-month discounts.

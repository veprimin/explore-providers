1. Medication type and regulatory status

Ro.co is unique in this comparison as the only mainstream platform here offering the Wegovy pill, the first and only FDA-approved oral GLP-1 weight loss medication. That means Ro can prescribe an FDA-approved finished product rather than a compounded injectable for patients who are injection-averse. He & She MD sells compounded GLP-1s produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies, and the source explicitly notes those compounded medications are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

2. Pricing: entry cost versus bundled terms

Ro.co promotes a low get-started online price of $39. The source emphasizes this is an entry point and that ongoing branded medication pricing varies and can be high depending on the plan. He & She MD advertises introductory six-month rates such as semaglutide injections starting at $139/month and oral semaglutide or tirzepatide at $199/month; those are six-month intro prices that renew higher after the initial term. The captured evidence warns readers to budget for the renewal price rather than the temporary promo rate.

3. Support and coaching

He & She MD bundles a coaching app with every plan, including 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific coaching, and progress tracking at no extra cost. The source positions He & She MD for patients who stalled on minimal subscriptions and want built-in accountability. Ro.co is described as an established, full-service telehealth platform that prescribes FDA-approved medications after clinician review, but the captured evidence does not present a bundled coaching app equivalent to He & She MD.

4. Shipping, fees and program structure

He & She MD includes free expedited shipping and no membership fee per the source, while promotional pricing applies to initial six-month terms that renew at a higher rate. For Ro.co the captured page did not verify shipping, fees, membership, or cancellation policy; the source only states Ro discloses pricing before you complete your consultation and that there are no hidden fees.

5. Clinician review and eligibility

Ro.co specifies treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. He & She MD also requires consultation and prescription by a clinician through its platform and notes not all customers will qualify for a prescription. Both providers therefore rely on clinician review and eligibility determinations.

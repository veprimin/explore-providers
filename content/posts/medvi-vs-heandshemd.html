Medication formats and clinical approach

MedVi focuses on clinically dosed compounded semaglutide delivered via a fully online intake and clinician review with no clinic visits required. He & She MD offers more formats: injectable semaglutide, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection, with medication and app coaching delivered after clinician consultation.

Support, coaching, and clinician contact

MedVi highlights 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and an online physician review process. He & She MD bundles a coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1 specific coaching and progress tracking at no extra cost, positioning itself for users who want built in accountability.

Regulatory and medication notes

Both providers use compounded GLP-1 medications produced in regulated pharmacies but note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality. MedVi explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA approved and that OpenLoop Health clinicians retain prescribing discretion. He & She MD also notes that medications are produced in USP compliant, state regulated pharmacies and that not all customers will qualify for a prescription.

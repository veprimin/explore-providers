Medications and clinical matching

Both Measured and Telos Rx offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. Telos Rx emphasizes the pharmacology distinction that semaglutide acts on GLP-1 while tirzepatide acts on GLP-1 plus GIP and that a US-licensed provider decides which is appropriate. Measured states it pairs compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with same-day prescription decisions and includes dietitian support as part of the offering.

Support, messaging and care access

Measured advertises unlimited messaging, a 24/7 Care Team and dietitian support and highlights same-day Rx decisions. Telos Rx describes “one care team” and that a licensed provider determines which medication is right for you. The captured Telos Rx evidence does not include the same explicit 24/7 messaging claim found in Measured materials.

Shipping, cancellation and price variability

Measured specifically states free two-day shipping and “cancel anytime” in captured materials. Telos Rx materials indicate prices “as low as” figures and state “your price depends on your plan” and “cancel anytime, no penalties,” but shipping details were not verified in the captured landing-page evidence.

Regulatory and outcome notes you must keep in mind

Both sources include the regulatory qualification that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Measured also cites an internal analysis of members reporting weight data at 6 months with an average loss reported; it explicitly notes individual results vary and are not guaranteed. Telos Rx materials state individual results vary and are not guaranteed and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

How to decide

Measured vs Telos Rx differ mainly by advertised starting price, stated shipping and support commitments, and how pricing is framed. If you prioritize an advertised lower promotional starting price for semaglutide and explicit two-day shipping and dietitian support mentioned in captured materials, Measured presents those claims. If you want pricing framed as “as low as” with clinician review emphasized and the claim that tirzepatide works on GLP-1 plus GIP, Telos Rx presents those points.

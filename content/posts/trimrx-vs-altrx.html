1. Pricing and pricing guarantees

Price is the clearest dividing line. TrimRx advertises compounded GLP-1 from $149/month and GLP-1+GIP from $249/month with a Lifetime Price Protection feature that claims you lock in today’s price for life and pay the same price on every dose. altRx positions itself as the most affordable option with a limited offer of semaglutide at $89/month and tirzepatide at $149/month and emphasizes “same price every month” for its plans. If long term price predictability is a priority, TrimRx uses the term Lifetime Price Protection. If the lowest current monthly cost is the priority, altRx is presented as the lower starting price under a limited offer.

2. Compounded medication status and fulfillment

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies. Each source includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. TrimRx highlights that medications are prepared and shipped by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy; altRx lists state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies in its network. Both providers state prescriptions will be issued only if a licensed clinician determines treatment is appropriate.

3. Shipping speed and delivery experience

TrimRx advertises free 1-2 day shipping. altRx advertises free and discreet 2-day delivery and a typical processing and delivery time of approximately 4-5 business days in other communications. If fastest listed arrival is essential, TrimRx emphasizes 1-2 day arrival; altRx emphasizes discreet 2-day delivery but also notes typical processing may be 4-5 business days.

4. Program inclusions and onboarding

Both include telehealth or medical visits and say there is no membership or hidden fees. TrimRx states prescription and telemed visits are included and highlights 24/7 support, HSA/FSA eligibility, no monthly membership and cancel anytime. altRx emphasizes 100% online medical visits, a complete treatment kit (vials, syringes, prep pads, step-by-step guidance), buy now pay later options, and ongoing care team support. altRx also notes a 2-minute quiz to start and claims availability in all 50 U.S. states with provider licensing that varies by state.

5. Marketing emphasis and limitations

TrimRx stresses scale and trust signals: “100k+ happy customers” and a 4.8 Excellent Rating, plus marketing about lifetime price lock and same-price dosing. altRx leads with affordability and a limited-time offer framework that may change when the promotion ends. Both note that if a branded FDA-approved medication is prescribed, fulfillment and costs for those branded drugs are handled separately; TrimRx explicitly says it does not sell or ship branded medications and that patients would fill branded prescriptions themselves.

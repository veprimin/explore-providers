How TrimRx vs Pallas Health differ at a glance

Both providers are 100% online and cash-pay only, and both offer compounded GLP-1 options. The most distinctive supported differences are: pricing model and entry price; availability of brand-name pens chosen by a clinician; how plan pricing renews; shipping speed and compounding disclaimers; and the presence of a published medical team or scale signals.

Pricing and plan renewal: predictable per-dose vs 12-week cycles

The clearest pricing contrast is how each vendor frames renewal. TrimRx advertises weight-loss plans starting at $149/month and Lifetime Price Protection so you “pay the same price on every dose” and there is no membership fee. Pallas Health advertises a lower intro month for compounded semaglutide at $139 first month, then renewals are $597 every 12 weeks, which averages to $199/month for semaglutide. Both are cash-pay and confirm renewal pricing on checkout, but TrimRx emphasizes a lifetime locked price while Pallas emphasizes a clinician-included all-in plan with 12-week renewals.

Medication choice and clinician role

TrimRx centers on compounded GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options; the provider may also write prescriptions for FDA-approved branded medications but TrimRx does not sell, dispense, or ship brand-name pens, and those branded prescriptions must be filled separately. Pallas Health explicitly offers both routes from one intake: a US-licensed clinician personalizes the plan and can select FDA-approved Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound, or Mounjaro, or a compounded preparation when a brand medication is not the right fit. That clinician-led choice is a primary differentiator supported in the sources.

Shipping, fulfillment, and compounding notes

Both providers rely on compounding pharmacies when supplying compounded medications, and both include a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. TrimRx states free 1–2 day shipping is included. Pallas promises free expedited and discreet shipping and specifies compounded medications are prepared in the USA by licensed compounding pharmacies; Pallas also notes the medication received may differ in appearance from photos on the site.

Care model, support hours and trust signals

TrimRx highlights scale and consumer ratings, citing 100k+ happy customers and a 4.8 Excellent Rating, along with claims of 24/7 support. Pallas emphasizes clinician personalization, an all-in plan that includes provider care and ongoing check-ins and messaging, and notes LegitScript Healthcare Merchant Certification plus a published medical team. Pallas lists care-team hours and clarifies no emergency services are offered; TrimRx materials describe unlimited access to the care team throughout the program. Both platforms operate without insurance billing.

Which should you choose?

Choose TrimRx if you prioritize a compounded GLP-1 or GLP-1+GIP program with a Lifetime Price Protection promise, starting at $149/month, free 1–2 day shipping, and scale signals like “100k+ happy customers” and a 4.8 rating.

Choose Pallas Health if you want a clinician to select between FDA-approved brand-name pens and compounded preparations in one intake, prefer an all-in plan that includes provider care and check-ins, and value LegitScript certification with published clinical disclosures.

This comparison highlights five distinctive differences supported by the source evidence: headline pricing structure, bundled coaching and clinician access, medication formats and compounded status, subscription term and renewal, and shipping and support offerings.

Headline pricing and how it is structured

Refills advertises personalized compounded GLP-1 from $99/month for semaglutide and $159/month for tirzepatide, with a flat price at every dosage. He & She MD lists semaglutide starting at $139/month and tirzepatide at $199/month, but those are six month introductory rates that renew higher after the initial term. The difference is simple: Refills promotes a flat low monthly price and month to month plans, while He & She MD offers lower intro pricing that is time limited and then renews at a higher six month rate.

Medication formats and compounded status

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 products. Refills explicitly notes personalized compounded GLP-1 and that compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality by the FDA. He & She MD also states medications are produced in USP compliant, state regulated pharmacies but are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality. He & She MD offers multiple formats including semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection according to the source. Refills highlights semaglutide and tirzepatide availability and that compounded medications may be prescribed if deemed medically appropriate.

Subscription terms, renewal, and cancellation

Refills describes month to month plans with no contracts, no cancellation fees, and the ability to cancel any time. He & She MD structures pricing as six month terms for its GLP-1 Platinum Program with promotional rates covering the first six months and renewal at higher six month prices. That means Refills is presented as true month to month while He & She MD requires planning for a six month term and higher renewals after the intro period.

Shipping, delivery, and ongoing support

Both providers offer expedited or express shipping and no membership fee on top of medication pricing. Refills notes free discreet shipping and text and email updates with tracking, plus free 24/7 Health Concierge support. He & She MD advertises free expedited shipping and in-app clinician access and coaching included at no extra cost.

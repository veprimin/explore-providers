Clinician access and care model

Sesame Care operates as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing, giving more control over the care relationship. bmiMD provides clinician-reviewed online treatment with 24/7 clinician access but centers its offering around compounded tirzepatide with enrollment subject to clinician determination.

Medication types and regulatory notes

Sesame Care offers branded options including oral semaglutide and branded tirzepatide pens and notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed; it states branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. bmiMD offers compounded tirzepatide and explicitly notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Pricing structure and commitments

Price formats differ. Sesame Care lists subscription care starting at $59/mo* for ongoing video visits, with cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo and insurance accepted for weight loss medications. Sesame preserves qualifiers: subscriptions are “as low as $59/mo*” and medication costs vary based on insurance and pharmacy, and some milestones reference a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. bmiMD lists tirzepatide options with four billing tiers: $179 month-to-month; $159/mo for three-month plan; $149/mo for six-month plan; and $139/mo for 12-month plan, noting the lowest price requires a 12-month commitment and that total prepaid charge should be confirmed.

Shipping, refills, and delivery

bmiMD advertises free shipping on every subscription order and online refills with delivery to your door. Sesame Care does not list free shipping in the captured evidence; its captured details focus on clinician choice, flexible scheduling, and pricing with insurance variability.

Transparency, guarantees, and quality checks

Sesame Care emphasizes broad treatment coverage, a large clinician network, and that outcomes or timeline milestones are based on a study of injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg paired with lifestyle changes, with results that “may” vary. bmiMD presents enrollment terms such as “100% Risk Free. 100% Refundable if not eligible for program” in captured landing content and states medications are prepared in a state-licensed pharmacy and independently verified in FDA- and DEA-registered labs while also noting compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

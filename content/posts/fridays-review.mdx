## JoinFridays Review — Both Compounds, No Lock-In

JoinFridays is a compounded-GLP-1 telehealth platform built around one idea: **flexibility**. It offers both compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, charges no membership fee, and lets you cancel at any time — a deliberately low-commitment model aimed at first-time GLP-1 patients who are not yet sure the medication will work for them.

## About JoinFridays

The platform prescribes **compounded semaglutide** (as in Ozempic and Wegovy) and **compounded tirzepatide** (as in Mounjaro and Zepbound) through a clinician-guided review, rather than a one-size templated protocol. As with every compounded provider, the medication uses the same active ingredients as the branded versions but is not an FDA-approved finished product. What sets Fridays apart in practice is the contract structure, not the pharmacology: no fees beyond treatment, and no penalty for stopping.

## How It Works

You complete an online intake, a licensed clinician determines whether GLP-1 therapy is appropriate and which compound fits, and — if approved — medication is filled and shipped. Personalized dosing guidance continues through the program. Because tirzepatide's dual GIP/GLP-1 mechanism is associated with higher average weight loss than semaglutide, having both on one platform lets the clinician escalate or switch without moving you to a new provider. The [NIH](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9486455/) has published the trial data behind that difference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I cancel JoinFridays anytime?

Yes. Fridays charges no membership fees and does not lock you into a long-term contract, so you can cancel at any time — a patient-friendly structure for anyone unsure whether GLP-1 therapy is right for them.

### Does JoinFridays offer tirzepatide?

Yes. It offers both compounded tirzepatide and compounded semaglutide, letting the clinician match the medication to your profile and goals.

### Is JoinFridays legitimate?

Yes. It is a licensed telehealth platform prescribing compounded GLP-1 medication after a clinician review, with ongoing dosing guidance. The medication is compounded, not FDA-approved, but uses the same active ingredients as the branded versions.

## Verdict

JoinFridays competes on flexibility rather than on any single headline feature: both compounds, no fees, cancel anytime. That makes it a low-risk way to try GLP-1 therapy — with the usual compounded-only caveats and the need to confirm whichever promo is live when you sign up.

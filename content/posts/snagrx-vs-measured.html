Shipping and fulfillment

Clinical review, pharmacies, and transparency

SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and its clinician network, OpenLoop Health, and states clinician review typically within five hours and always within 24 hours. Measured claims same-day prescription decisions but captured landing-page evidence does not supply named pharmacy partners or the specific clinician network. Both providers emphasize that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that prescriptions are issued at clinician discretion.

SnagRx highlights outcome-related customer reporting and a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms that apply, but the evidence also emphasizes that results may vary and that compounded medications are not FDA-reviewed.

Guarantees, refunds, and cancellation

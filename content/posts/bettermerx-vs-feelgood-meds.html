BetterMe Rx vs FeelGood Meds: key differences

This comparison uses only captured facts. Regulatory parity: both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

1) Price structure and multi-month plans

BetterMe Rx shows monthly-equivalent pricing that falls with longer prepaid plans: compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan, with specific examples listed (semaglutide injection $205/mo or as low as $145/mo on a 12-month plan; tirzepatide injection $275/mo or as low as $215/mo; oral semaglutide $250/mo or as low as $185/mo; oral tirzepatide $265/mo or as low as $215/mo). 2) Medication formats and standout options

BetterMe Rx offers four medication and administration options: injectable and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide. The sourced content highlights a needle-free daily tirzepatide tablet priced at parity with the injection.

FeelGood Meds offers compounded injections and compounded tablets and also lists a branded medication option. The provider materials list the three categories but do not list the specific GLP-1 molecules in the captured content.

3) Branded option and guarantees

FeelGood Meds explicitly shows a branded option priced from $1,999/month and promotes a “Weight Loss Money Back Guarantee” in the captured content. BetterMe Rx does not show a branded option in the supplied evidence; its marketing highlights compounded options, free shipping, and no membership fee.

4) Free shipping, membership and cancellation

Both providers state free shipping and no membership fee in the captured material. BetterMe Rx specifically lists free 1–2 day delivery, includes medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies and free shipping, and states “Cancel anytime” alongside prepaid longer-term plans. FeelGood Meds lists free shipping and “no membership or hidden fees” and suggests confirming renewal terms; cancellation specifics were not supplied beyond common landing-page language.

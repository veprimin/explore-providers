Quick verdict: Sesame Care vs Bodybuilding Health+

Sesame Care focuses on a marketplace model that gives patients clinician choice, flexible scheduling, and subscriptions starting at $59/mo, while Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes transparent pricing with GLP-1 programs from $179/mo and no membership or hidden fees. Early verdict: choose Sesame Care for clinician selection and insurance options; choose Bodybuilding Health+ for no membership, bundled GLP-1 pricing, and compound options.

How these services differ at a glance

This comparison looks only at captured facts about price structure, medication sourcing and approval status, membership and cancellation policies, and platform model. We compare Sesame Care vs Bodybuilding Health+ directly on the most distinctive differences supported by the source material.

1. Platform model and clinician access

Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select providers based on specialty, availability, and pricing. The platform highlights a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states. Bodybuilding Health+ is a broad telehealth platform that provides fully online programs overseen by identified medical leadership, with provider review of medications and online intake, but its model does not emphasize a marketplace for choosing individual clinicians.

2. Pricing structure and membership

Sesame Care advertises ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Medication cash-pay prices are shown as low as $149/mo for semaglutide and $299/mo for tirzepatide with noted insurance pricing possibilities as low as $25. Bodybuilding Health+ lists compounded semaglutide from $179/mo and tirzepatide from $209/mo, and emphasizes no monthly membership, cancel anytime, and no hidden fees. Bodybuilding Health+ also offers multi-month bundles to lower effective monthly cost.

3. Medication sourcing, approval, and regulatory notes

Sesame Care lists branded FDA-approved medications and specifies product and strength details, for example oral Wegovy pill described as FDA-approved and Zepbound KwikPen as FDA-approved for certain indications. Sesame Care also states medication costs are not included in subscription pricing and will vary by insurance and pharmacy. Bodybuilding Health+ offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by licensed U.S. pharmacies and explicitly notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Bodybuilding Health+ says treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

4. Scope of services and extras

Both platforms are fully online. Sesame Care promotes broad treatment coverage and ongoing visits, labs, and messaging as part of subscription-level care. Bodybuilding Health+ spans weight management, longevity, and sexual performance with offerings like NAD+ and sermorelin and ED options, and highlights shipping to your door and no insurance requirement.

What the sources say about results and claims

Sesame Care cites milestones based on a study of injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg in non-diabetic adults with obesity or overweight when paired with healthy lifestyle changes, and qualifies that results may vary. Bodybuilding Health+ frames offerings as science-backed treatments with provider oversight but its compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-reviewed.

Final thoughts on Sesame Care vs Bodybuilding Health+

Both platforms deliver online GLP-1 weight management options with distinct models. Sesame Care is best for people seeking a marketplace with clinician choice, flexible scheduling, and the option to use insurance for medications. Bodybuilding Health+ is best for people who want transparent pricing without membership, bundled GLP-1 programs, and a broader multi-program platform that includes longevity and ED options.

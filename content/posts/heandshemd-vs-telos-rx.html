He & She MD vs Telos Rx: pricing approach and introductory rates

He & She MD lists compounded semaglutide injections starting at $139 per month and oral semaglutide or tirzepatide injections at $199 per month, each described as six-month introductory rates that renew higher. Telos Rx presents semaglutide “as low as” $99 per month and tirzepatide as low as $139 per month, with final pricing “if a licensed provider approves you” and notes that your price depends on your plan.

Distinctive differences organized by evidence

1) Pricing presentation and term structure

He & She MD advertises specific six-month introductory prices ($139/mo semaglutide injections; $199/mo oral semaglutide and tirzepatide injections) and warns those promo rates renew at higher six-month prices. Telos Rx emphasizes “as low as” monthly pricing and that treatment is available only after review by a US-licensed provider, with final price depending on the plan and approval.

View He & She MD introductory rates

2) Coaching, clinician access, and care model

He & She MD bundles a coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging and GLP-1-specific guidance and progress tracking at no extra cost, positioning that support as part of the program. Telos Rx describes care from a licensed provider who decides which molecule is right, but the captured evidence does not document a bundled coaching app or 1:1 messaging included.

3) Medication formats and provider selection

He & She MD explicitly sells semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, or tirzepatide injection within its programs. Telos Rx advertises compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options and notes tirzepatide acts on GLP-1 and GIP, with a US-licensed provider deciding which medication is appropriate.

4) Regulatory and pharmacy notes

Both providers’ captured evidence states these are compounded medications and notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. He & She MD additionally states medications are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies but not reviewed by the FDA.

5) Cancellation, renewal and commitments

He & She MD frames pricing as structured in six-month terms with promotional introductory rates that renew higher after the first six months. Telos Rx materials indicate “cancel anytime, no penalties” in the captured content, but also emphasize that price, eligibility, and state coverage may change and final terms depend on clinician approval.

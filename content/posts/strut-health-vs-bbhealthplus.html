Strut Health vs Bodybuilding Health+ compares an oral-specialist telehealth service with a broader multi-program telehealth platform. Quick verdict: choose Bodybuilding Health+ if you want lower starting pricing, tirzepatide options and multi-program bundles; choose Strut Health if you specifically want an oral-focused Semaglutide program with guided consultations.

Top differences at a glance

This comparison highlights the most distinctive supported differences: medication format and specialization, starting price and available GLP-1 options, breadth of program offerings and bundles, and clinical notes on compounded medication status and medical necessity.

1. Medication format and clinical focus

Strut Health emphasizes specialized oral-focused treatment options, with oral Semaglutide lozenges and guided consultations. The provider presents oral specialization as its defining feature and notes that compounded Semaglutide is prescribed based on medical necessity by a licensed physician. Bodybuilding Health+ offers a broader GLP-1 set including compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, plus other programs such as NAD+ and sermorelin. Both providers state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that prescriptions require clinician review.

2. Starting price and GLP-1 options

Price is a clear differentiator in the supplied facts. Strut Health lists oral GLP-1 from $199 per month. Bodybuilding Health+ lists compounded semaglutide from $179/mo and compounded tirzepatide from $209/mo. Bodybuilding Health+ also advertises multi-month bundles that lower effective monthly cost for GLP-1 programs. These price points are presented as starting prices and bundle structures in the source material.

3. Breadth of services and platform model

Strut Health is described as a no-frills, oral-specialist telehealth approach focusing on oral medication management and guided ongoing progress communication. Bodybuilding Health+ is a multi-program telehealth platform covering weight management, longevity and sexual performance, with features like NAD+, sermorelin and ED treatments alongside GLP-1 care. Bodybuilding Health+ highlights no monthly membership, no hidden fees and cancel anytime flexibility in its presentation.

4. Clinical oversight and regulatory notes

Both sources state that compounded GLP-1 medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by licensed U.S. compounding pharmacies when offered. Strut Health notes compounded Semaglutide is prescribed based on medical necessity by a licensed physician. Bodybuilding Health+ indicates treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

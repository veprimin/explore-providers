Choosing between Found and Strut Health comes down to approach: Found emphasizes a biology-first, personalized medically supervised weight loss program with plans starting at as low as $39, while Strut Health specializes in oral GLP-1 options starting from $199 per month. Early verdict: pick Found for holistic, insurance-friendly care; pick Strut Health if you want an oral-focused semaglutide pathway.

Found vs Strut Health: core clinical focus

Found centers weight loss around each patient’s metabolic biology, targeting hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavioral factors as part of a personalized program. Strut Health narrows its clinical focus to oral GLP-1 therapy, especially oral semaglutide, with guided consultations and ongoing progress communication. Both mention compounded GLP-1 options; both also note compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Three distinctive differences that matter

1. Treatment breadth and personalization

Found: personalized medically supervised programs that build plans around an individual’s biology, not a single medication protocol. Strut Health: specialized oral-based program options where the clinical team focuses on oral medication management. If you want a broader, biologically tailored plan choose Found. If you specifically want an oral semaglutide pathway choose Strut Health.

2. Pricing positioning and insurance

Found advertises getting started for as low as $39 and describes most insurance now accepted, handling insurance paperwork and offering pathways like a Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program. Strut Health lists oral GLP-1 from $199 per month and emphasizes no hidden fees and no insurance needed. Found explicitly notes advertised pricing for branded GLP-1s via insurance with commitments and that copays, coinsurance and deductibles may apply; Strut Health presents a simpler, direct cash-style price model for oral plans.

3. Medication formats and prescribing notes

Found lists branded and compounded options including tirzepatide and semaglutide, and explicitly states compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved, not available in all states, and prescriptions are subject to provider review and clinical eligibility. Strut Health focuses on oral semaglutide lozenges and notes compounded semaglutide is prepared by U.S.-licensed compounding pharmacies pursuant to individualized prescriptions and requires medical necessity determined by a licensed physician.

4. Practical program features

Found highlights holistic, whole-body support, cancel anytime flexible pricing, and claims to handle insurance paperwork for eligible members. Strut Health highlights guided consults, ongoing progress communication, free shipping, and cancel anytime terms for its oral program options.

Conclusion: Found vs Strut Health

Strut Health offers a straightforward, oral-focused semaglutide pathway from $199 per month with guided consults and free shipping.

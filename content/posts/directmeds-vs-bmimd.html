Pricing, promotions, and billing options

Directmeds vs bmiMD differ most clearly on how pricing is presented. Final comparison and recommendation

Directmeds vs bmiMD comes down to flexibility versus long term cost. Directmeds is presented with a promotional starting price of $147, no separate monthly membership fee, injectable and sublingual options, month to month cancellation, and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. bmiMD is presented with multiple billing options for compounded tirzepatide, free subscription shipping, 24/7 clinician access, and lower long term monthly equivalents that require multi month commitments. Both note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and that treatment requires clinician determination of medical appropriateness.

Choose Directmeds if you want clinician reviewed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with injectable or sublingual formats, free next day shipping, no separate monthly membership fee, and month to month cancellation and refund protection for unapproved prescriptions.

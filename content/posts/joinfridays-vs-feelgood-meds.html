Quick verdict: JoinFridays vs FeelGood Meds

JoinFridays is best if you want the lowest advertised starting price and both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide on one platform. FeelGood Meds is best if you prefer a simple no membership fee policy and free shipping. This comparison focuses on pricing, medication formats, membership and fees, discounts and promos, and regulatory notes for JoinFridays vs FeelGood Meds.

How the two providers differ at a glance

Both providers offer clinician-reviewed telehealth programs and compounded GLP-1 options, but they emphasize different priorities. JoinFridays highlights a lower starting price and active promo codes plus flexible cancellation. FeelGood Meds highlights no membership fee, free shipping, and separate compounded injection and tablet pricing. Treatment availability is subject to clinician review at both providers and compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

1. Pricing and starting costs

Pricing is one of the clearest differences in JoinFridays vs FeelGood Meds. JoinFridays advertises programs starting at $117/month and notes multi-month plans that reduce effective monthly cost. FeelGood Meds advertises compounded injections from $149/month and compounded tablets from $249/month, with a branded option shown from $1,999/month. JoinFridays also lists tirzepatide as low as $198/month in some plans.

2. Medication formats and options

JoinFridays offers compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide on one platform and advertises both compounded and name brand options where available, while noting that its compounded offerings are not FDA-approved finished products. FeelGood Meds offers compounded injections and compounded tablets and also lists a branded medication option. Both require clinician review to determine medical appropriateness.

3. Membership, fees, shipping and cancellation

JoinFridays emphasizes no membership fees and a cancel-anytime model with no long-term contract. FeelGood Meds also advertises no membership fee and adds free shipping. JoinFridays mentions membership included free as part of some plans. Specific shipping, renewal or state availability terms for each provider should be confirmed directly with the provider because treatment is subject to clinician review and live offers may vary.

JoinFridays lists active promo codes including NEWYOU12 for up to $500 off tirzepatide and NYNY12 for up to $400 off semaglutide and notes that promo availability can change. FeelGood Meds highlights a Weight Loss Money Back Guarantee in its marketing and emphasizes no hidden fees. Both providers state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinFridays explicitly notes its compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and that actual product appearance and labeling may differ from website images.

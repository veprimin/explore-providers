Key differences at a glance for altRx vs IVIM Health

This comparison focuses on five supported differences: starting price and pricing structure, program model and protocol, third-party ratings and published outcomes, compounded medication regulatory notes, and membership/fees and promotions.

1) Starting price and pricing structure

altRx lists compounded semaglutide starting at $89/month and tirzepatide at $149/month, with multi-month plans lowering per-month cost. IVIM Health advertises semaglutide at $75/month on some offers but lists ongoing pricing that includes program fees and a published ongoing rate of $174/month after introductory pricing. altRx markets a consistent “same price every month” message during its limited offer; IVIM highlights a free month membership code GIFT75 and a no-obligation free consultation valued at $75.

Claim IVIM’s free month with code GIFT75

2) Program model and clinical approach

altRx: 100% online medical visits, a 2-minute quiz to start, and a care team that provides ongoing clinician-guided support. If prescribed, medications ship from licensed pharmacies and typical processing is approximately 4–5 business days. IVIM Health: clinician-guided GLP-1 ID protocol with weekly dose adjustments and a structured optimization approach the company says can accelerate outcomes; the 40% faster statement is a proprietary protocol claim and not an independent head-to-head result.

Start altRx’s 2-minute quiz and online visit

3) Third-party ratings, published data, and trust signals

IVIM Health reports a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot and cites retrospective observational studies and internal published analyses tied to its GLP-1 ID protocol. altRx’s materials emphasize affordability and accessibility but do not present the same dual third-party rating evidence in the supplied source.

4) Compounding and regulatory notes

Both providers prescribe compounded GLP-1 medications. altRx’s materials explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. IVIM Health likewise notes that compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and have not undergone FDA review; IVIM also clarifies that its published outcomes include data from branded and compounded formulations and that results may vary.

5) Memberships, fees, shipping, and availability

altRx emphasizes no membership, no insurance required, no hidden fees, cancel anytime, free and discreet 2-day delivery, and availability across all 50 U.S. states with provider availability varying by state. IVIM offers a no-obligation free consultation, a free month code, and mentions a program fee (for example a +$74.99/mo program fee shown alongside some pricing) and that medications are prepared by independent, licensed compounding pharmacies; availability is not universal in all states.

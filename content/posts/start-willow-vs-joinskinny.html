2. Medication type and regulatory status

Both providers involve medications that may not be branded finished products. Start Willow specifically offers a compounded semaglutide injection described as comparable to Ozempic and tirzepatide and notes that compounded semaglutide is not FDA-approved and not reviewed by the FDA. JoinSkinny provides GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and states its prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners; captured evidence also notes compounded products are not FDA-approved. These regulatory qualifiers are preserved in both providers’ materials.

3. Delivery speed and logistics

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less and highlights same-day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping in landing-page evidence. JoinSkinny promises temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1-2 days, with same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time and UPS next-day delivery. Both emphasize fast shipping, but JoinSkinny highlights temperature control and specific cutoffs for same-day ship dates.

4. Clinical flow and support

Start Willow prescribes compounded semaglutide after an online clinician review and positions its model as fully online with no in-person visits or insurance forms. JoinSkinny uses a quick health questionnaire followed by a licensed provider review, includes prescription and telemed visits in its plans, and offers ongoing support, dose adjustments, monthly check-ins, and 24/7 chat. The evidence shows JoinSkinny emphasizes continuous monitoring and dose adjustments at no extra cost, while Willow emphasizes a personalized clinician-prescribed compounded option.

What matters most: three decisive differences

1) Price and bundles: JoinSkinny starts lower and offers multi-month bundles and installment options; Start Willow has a higher ongoing rate after the first month. 2) Medication framing: Start Willow centers on a compounded semaglutide product comparable to branded GLP-1s; JoinSkinny offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options with prescriptions filled by FDA-regulated pharmacies. 3) Shipping specifics: Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less; JoinSkinny emphasizes temperature-controlled 1–2 day shipping with same-day ship cutoffs.

Near-conclusion summary

Start Willow vs JoinSkinny comes down to tradeoffs between a compounded semaglutide direct-pay option with a 3-day delivery guarantee and a lower-cost, flat-rate plan structure with bundle options and temperature-controlled next-day shipping. Both avoid insurance and both include clinician review, but the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved appears in both providers’ captured evidence.

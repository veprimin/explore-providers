Quick verdict

Found is best if you want a biology-based, personalized program and insurance assistance. WellMedr is best if you prioritize locked pricing, guaranteed on-time refills, and a 90-day money-back promise. Both offer compounded GLP-1s with regulatory cautions; clinician approval is required.

Found takes a biology-first approach, building a personalized medically supervised weight-loss program around hormones, blood sugar, sleep, and behavior. WellMedr focuses on GLP-1 medication plans and highlights dose guidance and medication shipping. The comparison matters if you want broader metabolic evaluation versus a medication-centered plan.

4) Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both providers offer or reference compounded GLP-1 medications. Found notes “Compounded GLP-1s are not FDA- approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy, and not available in all states.” WellMedr states compounded GLP-1s are “not evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy” and notes risks including thyroid C-cell tumors and MTC, urging immediate care for certain symptoms. Both emphasize that treatment requires clinician review and approval.

5) Shipping, refills, and logistics

WellMedr highlights same-day review and free overnight shipping, on-time refills guaranteed, and dosing shipped every 4 weeks for certain plans. Found highlights affordable monthly plans and membership options and says it handles insurance paperwork; specific shipping or refill speed details are not supplied in the Found evidence captured here.

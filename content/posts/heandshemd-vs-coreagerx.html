Quick verdict: He & She MD vs CoreAgeRx

He & She MD sells compounded GLP-1s with a coaching app and 1:1 clinician messaging and promotes semaglutide injections starting at $139/month as a six month introductory rate that renews higher. CoreAgeRx advertises flat monthly pricing with tirzepatide at $129/month and semaglutide at $79/month and says prices stay the same every month.

How these two differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences: pricing model and renewal terms, bundled coaching and clinician access, medication formats and dosing, shipping and membership rules, and regulatory notes about compounded meds. The primary keyword He & She MD vs CoreAgeRx appears throughout to help you compare quickly.

1. Pricing model and renewal transparency

He & She MD lists introductory six month rates: semaglutide injections start at $139/month for the first six months and then renew at higher six month prices. Oral semaglutide and tirzepatide injections are shown at $199/month introductory rates that renew higher after six months. The provider notes promo pricing applies to the six month term and that plans renew at standard six month prices.

CoreAgeRx emphasizes “Same Price. Every Month.” and lists flat monthly rates with tirzepatide at $129/month and semaglutide at $79/month. CoreAgeRx states no price hike, no membership fee, and no billing surprises, and that pricing will not change month to month.

Check CoreAgeRx flat monthly pricing

2. Coaching and clinician access

He & She MD bundles a coaching app that includes GLP-1 specific guidance, progress tracking, and 1:1 clinician messaging at no extra cost with each plan. That is central to its value proposition for people who want built in accountability.

CoreAgeRx notes clinician review for eligibility and a 3 minute online visit but the captured evidence does not document bundled 1:1 coaching or app-based progress tracking included with medication pricing. The source advises confirming eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

Explore He & She MD coaching and clinician access

3. Medication formats, dosing, and prescribing

He & She MD offers three formats: semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection, with specific promo prices for each format noted as six month introductory rates. Medications require consultation and prescription by a clinician through the He & She MD platform and not all customers will qualify.

CoreAgeRx lists tirzepatide up to 7.5 mg and semaglutide up to 1.0 mg and emphasizes consistency of treatment. CoreAgeRx also notes treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Both providers indicate clinician involvement matters for prescribing.

4. Shipping, fees, and membership

He & She MD includes free expedited shipping, states no membership fee, and says no insurance is needed. It also highlights that headline prices are six month intro rates that renew 30 to 45 percent higher. CoreAgeRx states no membership fee and no hidden fees, and presents a flat monthly price model with messaging that prices do not increase month to month.

5. Regulatory and pharmacy notes

He & She MD explicitly states its medications are produced in USP compliant, state regulated pharmacies but that compounded GLP-1 medications are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy or quality. CoreAgeRx notes medication specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Both sources preserve regulatory qualifiers and recommend clinician review.

If you prioritize a predictable flat monthly payment with no price increases month to month, CoreAgeRx documents that pricing model. Both require clinician review for treatment and preserve regulatory qualifiers about compounded medications. Confirm eligibility, specific renewal terms and state availability before enrolling.

Choose one if…

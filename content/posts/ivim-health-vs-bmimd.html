How they differ: pricing, programs, and meds

Both providers offer clinician-reviewed telehealth for compounded GLP-1 medications. Key differences supported by the sources include: IVIM lists semaglutide and tirzepatide pricing with a program fee and a promotional free month, and highlights a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol and dual 4.9-star ratings. bmiMD lists tiered tirzepatide subscription pricing with lower long-term monthly equivalents and free subscription shipping plus 24/7 clinician access.

1. Pricing structure and commitments

IVIM Health: starts at $75 in listing and advertises $99 first month with $174/month ongoing and a $74.99/month program fee in some materials; new patients can use code GIFT75 for a free month membership and a no-obligation free consultation valued at $75. bmiMD: tirzepatide pricing is tiered with $179 month-to-month, $159 for a three-month plan, $149 for six months, and $139 for a 12-month plan; bmiMD notes the lowest price requires a 12-month commitment and advises confirming total prepaid charge and eligibility.

2. Medication offerings and formulation notes

IVIM lists compounded semaglutide (GLP-1) at $75/mo and compounded tirzepatide at $133/mo alongside clinician-guided titration under GLP-1 ID. IVIM repeatedly states its compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not undergone FDA review. bmiMD focuses on compounded tirzepatide with same price at every dose and the same regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

3. Program features and third-party validation

IVIM highlights a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol that it says may accelerate weight loss by up to 40% compared to a published GLP-1 trial, and it emphasizes a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot. These are presented as IVIM’s claims and published-study context, not head-to-head trial proof. bmiMD emphasizes 24/7 clinician access, online refills and delivery, and a “100% Risk Free” refund if not eligible for the program, per its captured copy.

Early verdict: MedVi wins on a low promotional entry price and a no-clinic process, while HermanRx wins on clear all-inclusive pricing and the option of tirzepatide.

Key differences: price, molecule choice, timeline, support

1) Promotional entry price versus published all-in monthly rates

MedVi offers a promotional first month at $179 including fast, free shipping, but notes the ongoing standalone month is $299 and a three-month plan is $649 (about $216/month). HermanRx publishes all-inclusive monthly prices: compounded semaglutide + B12 at $249/month and compounded tirzepatide + B12 at $349/month, with no separate membership or consultation fee. The essential tradeoff is a lower promotional entry at MedVi versus HermanRx’s consistent all-in monthly pricing.

2) Molecule availability: semaglutide only versus semaglutide and tirzepatide

MedVi offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only. HermanRx offers both compounded semaglutide + B12 at $249/month and compounded tirzepatide + B12 at $349/month. If access to tirzepatide is important, HermanRx explicitly lists it; MedVi does not offer tirzepatide according to the supplied evidence.

3) Process and timeline: no clinic visits versus fast published review and delivery

MedVi advertises a fully online intake with no clinic visits and a licensed clinician review after checkout. HermanRx provides a roughly 5-minute assessment, a ~24 hour provider review and delivery in about 72 hours if prescribed, and publishes a wider 3 to 7 business day window in its FAQ. MedVi emphasizes simplicity and no in-person visits; HermanRx emphasizes a rapid, published timeline.

4) Pricing structure, fees and shipping

MedVi uses a promotional first-month price and notes ongoing price increases; it states medication is included and that it operates on a cash-pay model. HermanRx states genuinely all-inclusive pricing with no membership fee, no consultation fee and free discreet shipping in plain unmarked packaging. Both state no insurance is required and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved as finished products.

5) Support and scope of services

MedVi highlights 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and a simple online process. HermanRx offers 24/7 patient support and a secure patient portal and lists five treatment categories where a patient can consolidate services. MedVi emphasizes certified medical support plus telehealth clinician review; HermanRx emphasizes board-certified physician review across multiple categories.

Refills vs RxActivate: Pricing transparency and how you learn cost

Comparing Refills vs RxActivate, the most immediate difference is how and when price appears. Refills publishes a starting price and flat pricing model. RxActivate does not display price or billing terms before you complete the eligibility questionnaire and provide name, email and phone number.

Distinctive difference 1: Published starting price vs gated pricing

Refills states personalized compounded GLP-1 from $99/month (~$5/day) and lists specific example prices such as Semaglutide at $99/mo and Tirzepatide at $159/mo. RxActivate requires completion of its eligibility questionnaire and contact-information gate before revealing plan or price. The RxActivate evidence notes no price, medication fee, membership fee, shipping charge, cancellation term or billing commitment was displayed before the contact-information gate.

Review Refills plan details

Distinctive difference 2: Intake and clinician review process

Both providers use clinician review, but the flow differs. Refills describes a health intake quiz followed by clinician review; depending on needs, you may chat with a clinician or receive a prescription within 24 hours, then shipment in about 2 business days and a 4-week supply each month. RxActivate uses a two-minute eligibility questionnaire requesting goal, height, weight, target weight, timeline, medication history, sex assigned at birth, date of birth, state and safety screening and then requires contact information before showing likely eligibility and plan options.

Distinctive difference 3: Compounded medication and regulatory notes

Refills repeatedly states that compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality by the FDA and that compounded medications may be prescribed when medically appropriate; it also notes compounding pharmacies are licensed and inspected by Boards of Pharmacy. RxActivate evidence similarly notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Both require a prescription and clinician determination of medical appropriateness.

Distinctive difference 4: Shipping, support, and commitments

Refills advertises free discreet shipping, express shipping, free 24/7 health concierge support, no membership or app fees, month-to-month plans with cancellation anytime and no cancellation fees. RxActivate evidence does not display shipping, membership or cancellation terms before the contact-information gate and notes no payment is requested before that step.

## Sesame Care Review — A Marketplace, Not a Protocol

Sesame Care works differently from every other provider in this comparison. It is not a single-protocol GLP-1 platform — it is a **telehealth marketplace**. Instead of being assigned a clinician from an algorithmic queue, you browse a network of licensed providers and **choose your own** based on specialty, availability, and price.

<Score />

## About Sesame Care

Sesame connects patients to a large network of independent licensed clinicians across many specialties, including obesity medicine and weight management. For GLP-1 care, that network includes providers who can evaluate candidacy, prescribe appropriate medication, and provide follow-up. Because the clinicians practise independently, the experience is less standardised than a direct-to-patient platform — but you get genuine choice over who provides your care and when.

## How It Works

You browse Sesame's clinician network, read provider profiles, and book directly with the one you choose. Consultations happen by video or text. A clinician who determines GLP-1 therapy is appropriate issues a prescription, which you fill at your preferred pharmacy or through Sesame's network. The open-marketplace model means appointment times and clinician availability are more flexible than platforms with closed provider networks — and you can often address multiple health concerns in a single visit.

Semaglutide is FDA-approved for chronic weight management; the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/) provides the approval and safety information underpinning any GLP-1 prescription a Sesame clinician writes.

## Pricing

The comparison table reads **"Not published"** — and for Sesame that is more than a verification caveat. Because pricing **varies by clinician and appointment type**, there is no single monthly figure to report. The marketplace does show each provider's pricing before you book, which is one of its strengths; just expect the cost to depend on the clinician you choose rather than a fixed program rate.

## Who It's For

Sesame fits patients who want **control over their care relationship** — reading a clinician's background before booking, choosing appointment times that fixed platforms don't offer, or discussing several health concerns at once. It is a weaker fit for patients who want a streamlined, standardised GLP-1 program with predictable pricing; for that, a direct-to-patient platform like <Go>Yucca Health</Go> or <Go>Ro</Go> is cleaner. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is Sesame Care different from other GLP-1 platforms?

Sesame is a marketplace: rather than being matched to a clinician automatically, you browse profiles and choose your own provider based on specialty, availability, and price — then book directly.

### Can I see the price before I book on Sesame Care?

Yes. The marketplace model shows each provider's pricing before you book, though the amount varies by clinician and appointment type rather than a single program rate.

### Is Sesame Care legitimate?

Yes. Sesame connects patients to a large network of licensed, independently practising clinicians. GLP-1 prescriptions are written by those clinicians through the platform after an appropriate evaluation.

## Verdict

Sesame Care's marketplace is most valuable to patients who prize flexibility — in clinician choice, scheduling, and breadth of care. The trade-off is consistency: working with independent providers means the experience varies more than a platform-standardised program, and pricing isn't a single number. For patients who want to own their care relationship, Sesame offers something the streamlined GLP-1 platforms don't.

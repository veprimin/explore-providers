Shed vs JoinFridays: what differs most

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: pricing and promos, medication types offered, coaching and membership model, approval and treatment workflow, and cancellation or commitment terms.

Pricing and promos

Shed lists compounded semaglutide starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month and advertises 10% off your first month. JoinFridays advertises a lower entry point, starting at $117/month, with active promo codes (for example NEWYOU12 and NYNY12 cited) and multi-month plans that reduce effective monthly cost. Both sources show variable pricing by program and plan length.

Medication options: name-brand vs compounded

Shed provides both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 options and compounded alternatives on one platform, noting that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy. JoinFridays offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options and notes it does not manufacture compounded medications; JoinFridays also lists branded name options on its pricing page but emphasizes its compounded programs as the lowest-cost route.

Coaching, membership, and care model

Shed emphasizes integrated continuous coaching bundled into treatment rather than a prescription-only model, positioning coaching as part of the standard program. JoinFridays offers personalized, clinician-guided care and includes a Fridays membership free with programs, and highlights no membership fees and the ability to cancel anytime.

Approval, assessment, and treatment workflow

Shed advertises a fast 2-minute approval assessment and states prescriptions are issued only after a clinician review determines treatment is appropriate; medication is shipped after that clinician confirmation. JoinFridays also relies on clinician review and personalized dosing guidance; its materials note multi-month plan options and month-to-month alternatives with different effective rates.

Top differences at a glance for Gala vs bmiMD

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences between Gala and bmiMD: pricing model and commitments, medication options, clinician access and monitoring, shipping and delivery, and regulatory notes about compounded medications. The introduction above gives an early verdict; the sections below compare each topic directly using only supplied facts.

Pricing model and commitments

Gala advertises one flat all-inclusive price covering provider visit, medication, shipping and ongoing care and highlights a $220 promotion. The base dollar price was not visible in readable page text. bmiMD lists four billing options with month-to-month and multi-month plans: $179 month-to-month, $159 per month for a three-month plan, $149 per month for a six-month plan, and $139 per month for a 12-month plan. bmiMD notes the lowest monthly equivalent requires a 12-month commitment and that total prepaid charge should be confirmed.

Medication options and product framing

Gala offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, described as a direct-pay telehealth platform offering compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with provider check-ins and home delivery. bmiMD focuses on compounded tirzepatide, labeled as Compounded GLP-1/GIP (Tirzepatide). Both providers present compounded medications; neither presents those compounds as FDA-approved products in the captured evidence.

Clinician access and monitoring

Gala includes a provider visit and prescription as part of the offering and mentions provider check-ins and dose monitoring. bmiMD highlights 24/7 clinician access and clinician-reviewed online treatment and mentions confirmations every 3 to 6 months to stay within ± 10% of the target strength. Both state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Shipping, refills and delivery

Gala advertises free two-day shipping straight to your door. bmiMD offers free shipping on every subscription order and online refills and delivery. Gala positions itself as fully online with no in-office visits required. bmiMD emphasizes online refills and reliable delivery from state-licensed pharmacies prepared in labs it describes as independently verified in FDA- and DEA-registered labs.

Regulatory and quality notes

Both sources include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Gala repeats that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. bmiMD states medications are prepared in a state-licensed pharmacy and mentions independent verification in FDA- and DEA-registered labs in the captured landing-page evidence.

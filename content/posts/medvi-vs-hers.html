Side by side: what differs most in MedVi vs Hers

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: medication type and regulatory status, pricing and membership structure, clinical process and access, available medication options, and ongoing support.

Medication type and regulatory status

MedVi offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide. The source explicitly notes that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Hers offers FDA approved Wegovy as a brand product and states it is not a compounded medication. Hers emphasizes name brand, FDA approved options such as Wegovy and Zepbound when applicable.

Pricing and membership differences

MedVi advertises a winter promotional price of $179 for the first month including fast, free shipping, then lists a standalone month at $299 and a three month plan at $649. The platform states it is cash pay and does not bill insurance. Hers lists Wegovy from $149/month and injectable from $199/month but requires an active Hers Weight Loss Membership. The source states the membership is $39 for the first month and then auto renews at $149/month thereafter, and that total cost includes the membership fee and medication billed separately. Hers also notes medication is not available without an active membership.

Clinical process, eligibility, and access

MedVi operates entirely online with a simple intake and no clinic visits. A licensed clinician reviews the assessment and an OpenLoop Health clinician meets with individuals after checkout to determine prescription eligibility. MedVi notes clinicians retain the decision to prescribe compounded GLP 1s and that the assessment does not by itself create a doctor patient relationship. Hers provides a personalized care team with ongoing dose check ins and emphasizes prescriptions are available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Hers also warns treatment is not available in all 50 states and a prescription is required.

Medication options and dosing choices

MedVi explicitly offers semaglutide only via compounded formulations and does not offer tirzepatide or branded Ozempic or Wegovy. Hers lists FDA approved Wegovy and mentions availability of higher dose semaglutide and tirzepatide options in some plans, and states it does not offer compounded medications. Where offered, Hers lists multiple brand options and notes Ozempic and Mounjaro are FDA approved for type 2 diabetes and may be available to eligible patients at clinician discretion.

Support and program features

MedVi highlights 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and a simple online process with no clinic visits. MedVi states medication is included in program cost and lists program benefits such as physician review, a personalized plan, 1:1 guidance, and metabolic reporting for the promotional month. Hers highlights a personalized care team with ongoing dose check ins and the positioning of name brand medication without compounded alternatives.

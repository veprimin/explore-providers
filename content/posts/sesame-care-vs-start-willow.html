Quick verdict: Sesame Care vs Start Willow

Sesame Care emphasizes clinician choice, flexible scheduling, and subscription care starting at $59/mo* with insurance accepted for weight loss medications. Start Willow is a direct-pay platform offering compounded semaglutide from $249 first month and $299/month ongoing, with no insurance required and a 3-day delivery guarantee. Early verdict: choose Sesame Care for broad clinician choice and insurance options; choose Start Willow for direct-pay, fast shipping and compounded formulations when you want to avoid insurance hurdles.

1. Pricing and insurance: subscription versus direct-pay

Sesame Care advertises ongoing video visits, labs, messaging and dedicated provider care starting at $59/mo* based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Medication cash-pay examples include semaglutide from $149 and tirzepatide from $299 with price-with-insurance notes as low as $25 in some cases. By contrast, Start Willow operates on a direct-pay model: $249 for the first month and $299/month ongoing, with no insurance required and no membership fees. If insurance coverage matters to you, Sesame Care documents that medication costs will vary by insurance status and pharmacy; Start Willow explicitly removes insurance from the equation.

2. Medication type and regulatory notes

Sesame Care lists branded options including semaglutide and tirzepatide with specific price points and notes that branded products like Wegovy and Zepbound are FDA-approved for certain indications. Start Willow offers compounded semaglutide that is described as comparable to Ozempic and tirzepatide; the source clearly states that compounded semaglutide is not FDA-approved and is not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Regulatory notes are present for both: Sesame Care includes a regulatory reminder that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed, while Start Willow emphasizes the non-FDA-approved status of compounded medications and their oversight by state-licensed compounding pharmacies under USP standards.

3. Clinician access and personalization

Sesame Care operates as a telehealth marketplace where patients can browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing, giving users more control over the care relationship. Start Willow uses an online clinician review to prescribe a compounded formula as part of a personalized treatment plan; personalization is guided by the Willow doctor but the model emphasizes a direct-pay prescription process rather than an open marketplace of multiple clinician profiles.

4. Delivery speed and logistics

Start Willow promises fast fulfillment with a 3-day delivery guarantee and notes same-day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping in stock scenarios. Sesame Care notes that medication costs and pharmacy fulfillment vary by insurance and pharmacy; specific shipping timelines were not supplied in the Sesame Care evidence.

Making the choice: Sesame Care vs Start Willow

Sesame Care vs Start Willow comes down to whether you prioritize clinician choice and insurance flexibility or a direct-pay, fast-shipping compounded option. Sesame Care highlights a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and the option to use insurance for weight loss medications. Start Willow highlights no insurance required, no membership fees, and a guaranteed fast delivery window while using compounded semaglutide that is not FDA-approved.

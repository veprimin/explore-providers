Key differences at a glance: He & She MD vs RxActivate

Comparing these providers side by side highlights a few decisive distinctions: pricing visibility and structure, bundled coaching and clinician access, medication formats and regulatory notes, eligibility and lead capture, and shipping or fee disclosures. Each section below uses only supplied facts.

1. Pricing visibility and structure

He & She MD: Semaglutide injections start at $139/month and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide injections start at $199/month as six-month introductory rates. The landing page repeatedly notes these are six-month promos that renew higher. RxActivate: No price, medication fee, membership fee, shipping charge, cancellation term or billing commitment was displayed before the contact-information gate. Pricing is therefore gated behind the eligibility flow.

2. Bundled coaching and clinician access

He & She MD: Every plan bundles the He & She MD app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific coaching and progress tracking at no extra cost. RxActivate: Describes clinician-matched GLP-1 plans and state and medical-safety screening but does not show bundled coaching or app features on the captured pages.

3. Medication formats and regulatory notes

He & She MD: Offers compounded semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection. The provider states medications are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies but are compounded and not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality. RxActivate: Notes that when compounded medications are offered, they are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Both providers include regulatory qualifiers and require clinician prescription where applicable.

4. Eligibility flow and lead capture

He & She MD: Intake is described as a fast 2-3 minute process with no payment required to check eligibility. RxActivate: Uses a two-minute eligibility questionnaire that requests goal, height, weight, target weight, timeline, medication history, sex assigned at birth, date of birth, state and safety screening. The RxActivate flow displayed likely eligibility then required first name, last name, email and phone number before revealing any plan or price. RxActivate: Did not display shipping, medication, membership or cancellation fees before the contact-information gate, so those details must be confirmed during the eligibility flow.

Bottom line: He & She MD vs RxActivate

He & She MD provides visible starting prices, clearly described six-month introductory rates, and a bundled coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging and progress tracking. RxActivate uses a clinician-matched eligibility flow that confirms likely eligibility then requires contact details before disclosing price, fees, or specific plan terms. Both note that compounded medications are not reviewed by the FDA when offered and that clinician review determines medical appropriateness.

Choose He & She MD if you want published starting prices, a bundled GLP-1 coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging, free expedited shipping, and clear six-month introductory rates that explicitly renew at higher terms.

Choose RxActivate if you prefer to confirm medical eligibility, state availability and pricing only after completing a clinician-matching questionnaire and providing contact information so a live offer can be revealed.

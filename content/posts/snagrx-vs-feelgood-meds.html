Formulation and product choices

SnagRx offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide only, with no branded Ozempic Wegovy Mounjaro or Zepbound available according to the supplied facts. FeelGood Meds offers compounded injections and compounded tablets and also displays a branded medication option starting at the higher price point shown.

Clinician network, pharmacies and shipping

SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and its clinician network, OpenLoop Health, and states clinician review typically within five hours and next day UPS or FedEx shipping in temperature controlled packaging. SnagRx also states its clinician network covers 48 states and that a declined prescription is refunded in full. FeelGood Meds notes treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and states free shipping and no membership fee on the landing page evidence.

Guarantees, support and extras

SnagRx advertises a full refund if the prescription is declined, plus a 6 month weight loss guarantee with terms that apply. It also states no coaching or nutrition programming is provided. FeelGood Meds lists a Weight Loss Money Back Guarantee on its landing page evidence and highlights no membership or hidden fees, but captured FAQs or detailed terms were not provided in the supplied evidence.

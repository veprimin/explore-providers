Directmeds vs HumeCare+ compares two compounded GLP-1 telehealth options. Quick verdict: choose Directmeds if you want lower starting prices, month-to-month plans, and free next-day shipping; choose HumeCare+ if you want a bundled protocol with glycine and B vitamins, body composition scans, app tracking, and a 10% or money back promise.

Explore Directmeds offers and pricing

How pricing and fees differ

Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and lists standard FAQ pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. Directmeds states no monthly membership fee and month-to-month cancellation, although subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term. HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/month and a tirzepatide protocol from $299/month, with pricing shown as a promotional discount that may change. HumeCare+ is presented as cash-pay only in the source.

Protocol and clinical support compared

Directmeds provides clinician-reviewed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats, with telehealth visits, nursing support, supplies, and free next-day UPS or FedEx shipping included. HumeCare+ positions its offering as a protocol rather than a prescription alone. HumeCare+ combines physician-prescribed compounded GLP-1 medication with glycine, vitamins B6 and B12 built into the formulation, a connected app, monthly body composition scans, and continuous physician optimization with 24/7 clinician access.

Evidence on outcomes and guarantees

Directmeds offers a full refund if a prescription is not approved. HumeCare+ advertises a 10% or money back promise over six months with terms and eligibility that should be verified directly with HumeCare+. HumeCare+ also shares internal headline results based on an analysis of members, which the source identifies as company-reported figures and testimonials that do not guarantee results.

Availability and limitations

Directmeds notes it is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. Both providers state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. HumeCare+ notes pricing reflects a promotional discount that may change.

Summary and near-term verdict

Directmeds vs HumeCare+: Directmeds leans toward lower promotional pricing, no membership fee, and simple month-to-month plans with free next-day shipping. HumeCare+ emphasizes a bundled clinical protocol with glycine and B vitamins, body composition tracking, an app, 24/7 clinician access, and a money-back promise for 10% weight loss in six months under its terms.

  • Choose Directmeds if you want lower advertised starting prices, no separate monthly membership fee, month-to-month cancellation, and included next-day shipping for compounded injectable or sublingual semaglutide or tirzepatide.

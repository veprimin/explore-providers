How pricing and fees compare

Price is a primary difference between Sunlight and IVIM Health. Sunlight advertises personalized GLP-1 plans starting at 88 per month and emphasizes “same price forever” and no monthly membership. IVIM Health lists compounded semaglutide at 75 per month in some materials and a tirzepatide option at 133 per month, but its program includes a program fee and published ongoing pricing that rises after an initial month. IVIM also promotes a free first-month membership code GIFT75 and a no-obligation consultation valued at 75. Sunlight’s messaging highlights fast, free shipping and a Summer Promo offering as low as 88, while IVIM’s materials emphasize a structured membership and program fees.

Medication types and regulatory notes

Sunlight presents injections and oral GLP-1 options and notes prescription and telehealth are included, without specifying branded versus compounded products in captured evidence. IVIM Health explicitly prescribes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and states these compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. The regulatory distinction is important: IVIM’s materials repeatedly note that compounded medications carry different regulatory status and are prepared by independent licensed compounding pharmacies, while Sunlight’s captured content emphasizes prescription, telehealth, and multiple formulation options.

Program structure, support, and outcomes claims

Sunlight markets a 100 percent online medical visit, prescription and telehealth included, service touted as fast with free 1 to 2 day shipping and the ability to cancel anytime. IVIM Health markets a clinician-guided GLP-1 ID protocol and cites internal and published retrospective observational studies as the basis for protocol outcomes; IVIM states its patients following the protocol lost on average 46 percent more weight over 68 weeks compared to another published trial, while noting this was not a head-to-head study. IVIM also claims its GLP-1 ID protocol can accelerate weight loss by up to 40 percent versus standard GLP-1 protocols and points to unusually strong third-party ratings as a trust signal. Sunlight includes multiple patient testimonials in captured evidence but does not present comparative third-party rating figures in the supplied content.

Third-party ratings and trust signals

IVIM Health highlights a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot as a rare dual third-party verification. Sunlight’s captured evidence emphasizes promotional pricing, shipping speed, and customer testimonials describing weight loss and clinician support. The evidence shows IVIM focuses on external ratings and published observational studies, whereas Sunlight emphasizes pricing, a fully online experience, and user testimonials.

Quick verdict: Enhance MD vs HealthRx

Enhance MD is the lower-cost entry option for patients seeking both semaglutide and tirzepatide with flat-rate dosing. HealthRx emphasizes prepaid plan pricing, clinician-directed medication selection, and overnight cold shipping. Treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate; compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Introduction: What this comparison covers

The Enhance MD vs HealthRx comparison focuses on six supported differences: starting price and introductory offers, pricing model and dose pricing, compound options available, clinician review and how medication is chosen, shipping and fulfillment, and regulatory notes about compounded medications. Early verdict: for lowest first-month cost and flat-rate dose pricing, Enhance MD is the standout; for prepaid plan structure and overnight cold shipping with clinician-directed selection, HealthRx is the clearer fit.

Direct comparison: Pricing and introductory rates

Enhance MD lists semaglutide starting at $49 for the first month and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month. HealthRx advertises semaglutide from $99 per month on a 12-month prepaid plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month. Both sources emphasize that these are starting or plan prices and contain additional terms about renewal or ongoing costs.

Pricing model and dose changes

Enhance MD promotes flat-rate pricing: the same price at every dose with no escalation during titration after the introductory period. HealthRx also states that your plan price does not change when your dose does, but its lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan. Both providers operate direct-pay models and note that compounded medication pricing differs from brand-name retail prices.

Compound options and clinician selection

Enhance MD offers both compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide on one platform, which it positions as useful for patients choosing between compounds. HealthRx offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription, and emphasizes that the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is the right fit, or whether neither is.

Shipping, fulfillment, and plan mechanics

HealthRx explicitly offers overnight cold shipping with tracked, free shipping on every plan. Enhance MD materials captured do not include a specific shipping promise on the supplied evidence, instead focusing on pricing, flat-rate dosing, and patient volume. HealthRx also ties its lowest monthly price to a longer prepaid commitment; Enhance MD highlights a low first-month price and ongoing flat-rate pricing but notes ongoing rates can be higher after the introductory period.

Regulatory and eligibility notes

Both providers state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Enhance MD notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HealthRx notes compounded medication is prepared by a licensed 503A pharmacy for an individual patient with a valid prescription and similarly that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Both emphasize treatment availability subject to clinician review.

Direct comparison: price, format, and pricing model

Price is the most obvious difference in SnagRx vs Try Ageless. SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan, with the $69 and $119 figures applying to the annual plan and higher month-to-month rates. SnagRx also highlights a price lock flat at every dose and at renewal. Try Ageless lists yearly per-month pricing from $159 for drops, $199 for semaglutide injection, and $249 for tirzepatide injection, with the discount already applied and code SLIM50 available to stack an extra $50 off.

How the pricing commitment differs

SnagRx: the lowest annual compounded price for semaglutide at $69/month requires a 12-month plan; month-to-month is $99. The price-lock means the rate does not rise during titration, according to SnagRx materials. Try Ageless: its lowest entry point is $159/month for tirzepatide drops on a yearly plan, with all plans showing a built-in discount; monthly rates are higher if not prepaid. Both providers present their lowest rates on prepaid yearly plans.

Formats and clinical options

Format variety is a core distinction. SnagRx supplies compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide injections. Try Ageless sells compounded semaglutide injection, tirzepatide injection, and needle-free tirzepatide oral drops. Try Ageless states the oral drops are a daily sublingual liquid and positions the drops as the lowest entry price on its menu.

Pharmacies, clinician networks, and regulatory notes

Both companies use compounded medications and include telehealth-prescription workflows, but they present different third-party details. SnagRx names four US 503A partner pharmacies and cites OpenLoop Health as its clinician network, with clinician review typically within five hours and next-day temperature-controlled shipping. Try Ageless cites state-licensed U.S. pharmacies, LegitScript certification, HIPAA compliance, and clinician flexibility to switch formats under one plan with no new consult fee. Both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products.

Extras, shipping, and guarantees

Try Ageless bundles B12 in every plan and emphasizes unlimited messaging, free discreet shipping, and the ability to switch molecules or formats under a plan. SnagRx emphasizes flat pricing with no membership fee, next-day UPS or FedEx delivery in temperature-controlled packaging, a full refund if a prescription is declined, and outcome guarantees mentioned on its site with terms applied. Both advertise cancel-anytime flexibility, but specifics differ by provider and plan.

Bottom line and how to choose

In this SnagRx vs Try Ageless comparison the choice depends on priorities. SnagRx is best for cost-driven patients who want predictable, locked pricing across a full titration and who are comfortable with compounded injections. Try Ageless is best for patients who value format choice, especially needle-free tirzepatide drops, B12 included, and format switching under one plan.

Choose SnagRx if you want the lowest annual compounded semaglutide price with a price-lock and named 503A pharmacies. Choose Try Ageless if you want needle-free tirzepatide drops, B12 bundled, and the ability to switch formats under a single plan.

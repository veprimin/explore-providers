Regenics vs Mochi Health both provide telehealth access to compounded GLP-1 medications, but they differ on clinician model, pricing structure, and membership features. Early verdict: choose Regenics for nurse-practitioner-led, straightforward cash-pay compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with a Sermorelin option; choose Mochi Health if you want live video doctor visits, membership features, and 50% off the first month.

How Regenics vs Mochi Health handle clinical visits and prescriptions

Regenics delivers care that is nurse-practitioner-led; licensed nurse practitioners review intake and oversee every prescription subject to clinician review. Mochi Health uses synchronous live video visits with a licensed physician bookable in about five minutes and emphasizes a real-time doctor conversation before any prescription is issued. Both platforms provide clinician evaluation before prescriptions, but Regenics highlights nurse-practitioner oversight while Mochi highlights live physician visits.

Pricing and membership: direct-pay promos versus membership-plus-medication

Pricing structure is a central difference. Regenics advertises compounded semaglutide at $139 first month (renews $199/mo) and tirzepatide at $189 first month (renews $279/mo), with no insurance required and FSA/HSA eligibility. Mochi Health operates a membership-plus-medication model, with promotional 50% off the first month via code BESTWLHALFOFF and pricing that starts from $99/month for membership-plus-medication. Mochi notes that the first-month promotional rates vary and ongoing cost depends on plan and medication type.

Medication sourcing, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded medications and include the same regulatory qualifier: compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Regenics states its compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are prepared by U.S. licensed compounding pharmacies with a LegitScript certified pharmacy network and free shipping. Mochi also emphasizes compounded medication options and lists a wider set of compounded formats and oral options in its materials.

Distinctive differences summarized

  • Clinician model: Regenics is nurse-practitioner-led; Mochi uses live physician video visits.
  • Pricing model: Regenics advertises promotional first-month flat prices; Mochi uses membership-plus-medication with a 50% first-month promo code.
  • Service features: Regenics offers a Sermorelin peptide add-on; Mochi highlights 24/7 support and ongoing appointment availability.
  • Pharmacy claims: Regenics states LegitScript certified pharmacy network; Mochi emphasizes nationwide prescription access in all 50 states.

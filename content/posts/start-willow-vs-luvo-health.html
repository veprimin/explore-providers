Quick comparison: what differs most between Start Willow and Luvo Health

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: pricing model and stated rates, delivery and shipping commitments, the compounded medication regulatory note and formulation options, and how each platform frames eligibility and program details.

Pricing model and transparency

Start Willow publishes a clear direct-pay price: From $249 per month for the first month and $299 per month ongoing, with no membership fees and no insurance required. Luvo Health does not have verified captured pricing on the reviewed landing page; its materials show a multi-month program layout but say readers should confirm pricing, eligibility and renewal terms before enrollment.

Delivery and shipping speed

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less from its licensed pharmacy network and advertises same-day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping for in-stock items. Luvo Health’s captured evidence does not include a comparable guaranteed delivery window; shipping and pharmacy details should be confirmed directly with the provider.

Medication types and clinical framing

Start Willow offers compounded semaglutide described as a once-weekly injection prescribed by a doctor as part of a personalized treatment plan and positions its medications as comparable to Ozempic and tirzepatide. Luvo Health lists tirzepatide injection, semaglutide injection and semaglutide tablets among program options, but the landing-page capture emphasized that details like medication, eligibility and state availability must be confirmed. Both providers’ captured pages note compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and are not reviewed or approved by the FDA.

Eligibility, clinician review and program terms

Start Willow operates via online clinician review and prescribes compounded medication after that review; it highlights that no insurance is needed and no membership fees apply. Luvo Health’s materials also indicate treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and warn that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy and state coverage may change; the landing page advises prospective patients to confirm those items before enrolling.

Quick verdict: JoinFridays and Eden Health both offer clinician-reviewed GLP-1 programs, but JoinFridays emphasizes compounded options, active promo codes and flexible, cancel-anytime terms while Eden Health emphasizes fixed monthly pricing at every dose and a lifetime price guarantee. This comparison focuses on pricing structure, medication type and regulatory notes, care model, and shipping and guarantees to help you decide.

JoinFridays vs Eden Health: pricing structure

JoinFridays lists programs starting at $117/month and advertises multi-month plans that lower effective monthly cost. Listed figures include a standalone one-month plan at $249 and a twelve-month plan totaling $1,800 (about $150/month). Eden Health presents “Monthly pricing at every dose” with semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129 and a lifetime price guarantee.

Medication options and regulatory notes

JoinFridays offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide options and also lists name brand medication prices separately. JoinFridays explicitly states that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Eden Health provides monthly pricing for semaglutide and tirzepatide but the captured evidence notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Treatment on both platforms is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Care model and clinician review

Both providers emphasize clinician-guided care. JoinFridays promotes “personalized, clinician-guided care” with dosing guidance and positions its model as subscription plus coaching and care. Eden Health markets a “guided GLP-1 program, built around you” and highlights that most programs stop at the prescription but that Eden “brings medication, care, and support together under one clear monthly price.” The source material for Eden advises confirming eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

Shipping, guarantees and extra policies

Eden Health states free expedited shipping and a lifetime price guarantee and notes no membership fees. JoinFridays calls out active promo codes and aggressive discounts that are subject to change, and it highlights no membership fees and the ability to cancel at any time. Promo codes and savings at JoinFridays are presented as time sensitive and subject to availability.

Which to choose

Choose JoinFridays if you want access to compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide options, active promo codes, and a cancel-anytime model with personalized clinician-guided care and multi-month pricing that can lower your effective monthly cost. Choose Eden Health if you prefer clear per-dose monthly pricing listed as $99 for semaglutide and $129 for tirzepatide, free expedited shipping and a lifetime price guarantee, but confirm eligibility, renewal terms and state availability before enrolling.

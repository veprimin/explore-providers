Direct comparison: price and product options

Price is a core difference between HumeCare+ vs FeelGood Meds. HumeCare+ lists semaglutide starting at $199 per month and a tirzepatide protocol with B12 and glycine from $299 per month. FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections starting at $149 per month, compounded tablets from $249 per month, and a branded option shown from $1,999 per month. Both providers offer compounded options; both note that compounded medications are not FDA approved.

Why price matters here

The two providers present different entry points: HumeCare+ bundles medication with added ingredients and services at its listed prices, while FeelGood Meds highlights a lower starting price for compounded injections and also lists higher-cost branded options. Exact renewal terms, eligibility, and availability are not supplied in detail for either provider in the source material and should be confirmed with each vendor.

Bundled protocol and clinical support

One of the most distinctive differences in the HumeCare+ vs FeelGood Meds comparison is HumeCare+ positioning as a protocol rather than a bare prescription. HumeCare+ pairs physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with glycine and vitamins B6 and B12, provides monthly body-composition scans, an app for tracking, and unlimited clinician access, including a U.S. care team available 24/7. FeelGood Meds emphasizes clinician-reviewed online treatment and offers compounded injections, compounded tablets, and a branded option, but the source does not list an integrated body-composition scan or the same bundled vitamin protocol.

Money-back promises and guarantees

Both providers advertise money-back or refund messaging in captured text, but their language and conditions differ. HumeCare+ advertises a 10% or your money-back promise over six months and says to verify full terms and conditions directly with HumeCare+. FeelGood Meds lists a Weight Loss Money Back Guarantee in its captured materials. The comparison source does not provide complete eligibility or documentation rules for either guarantee; confirm terms with each provider.

Shipping, fees, and membership

FeelGood Meds highlights no membership fee, free shipping, and no insurance required in its captured copy. HumeCare+ lists free next-day delivery and notes cancel subscription anytime. Both providers’ materials say treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. The source for HumeCare+ also notes pricing shown reflects a promotional discount that may change and that services are cash-pay only.

Safety and regulatory notes

Both HumeCare+ and FeelGood Meds offer compounded medications in some form and both include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. HumeCare+ specifically calls out that compounded medications are not evaluated by the FDA and distinguishes compounded offerings from branded products. The source also notes GLP-1 drugs carry serious risks and that clinician oversight is required.

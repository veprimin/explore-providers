1. Pricing and promotional structure

Strut Health lists pricing “From $199 per Month” and markets oral GLP-1 options in a simple, no-frills way. The provider materials also reference “$99 | Free shipping | Cancel anytime” as captured on the landing content. IVIM Health presents a lower nominal entry price in captured landing content with items like “$75/mo” for compounded Semaglutide and a promotional code: “Free Month Membership with Code GIFT75.” IVIM also documents a first-month/ongoing pricing pattern in captured FAQ material: it notes a $99 first month and $174/month ongoing in its FAQ text while advertising $75/mo and other figures on landing content paired with a $74.99/month program fee.

2. Clinical focus and program style

Strut Health emphasizes an oral specialization: “Oral-based program options | Guided consultations | Ongoing progress communication” and positions itself as providing prescription oral weight loss options with a team focusing on oral medication management. IVIM Health emphasizes a clinician-guided, titration and optimization program called GLP-1 ID and describes a structured protocol that the company says can accelerate outcomes by up to 40% compared to a referenced GLP-1 trial. That 40% figure is explicitly IVIM’s proprietary claim and is not presented as an independent head-to-head clinical trial.

3. Third-party ratings and evidence

IVIM Health highlights unusually strong third-party verification: a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot as captured in the source. Strut Health’s evidence focuses on delivery of oral GLP-1 treatment options and guided consults; the supplied Strut materials do not include independent dual third-party rating details.

4. Medication type and regulatory notes

Both providers supply compounded GLP-1 options in captured content. Strut Health materials explicitly state “GLP-1 compounded weight management therapy with Semaglutide” and include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. IVIM Health likewise notes that its compounded drugs are not FDA-approved, that they have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality, and that compounded medications are prescribed only after consultation with an IVIM provider. Both sources emphasize that compounded medications carry specific regulatory distinctions.

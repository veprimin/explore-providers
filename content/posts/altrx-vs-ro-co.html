Key differences in altRx vs Ro.co

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences: starting price, medication type and regulatory status, visit and fulfillment model, delivery and ongoing pricing, and who each provider is built for.

1. Starting price and pricing structure

altRx advertises compounded semaglutide starting at $89/month and compounded tirzepatide at $149/month as a limited offer. Ro.co advertises that you can get started online for just $39, with ongoing branded medication pricing varying by plan and medication.

Get started with Ro.co for $39

2. Medication type and regulatory status

altRx primarily offers compounded injectable GLP-1s, explicitly noting that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Ro.co offers the Wegovy pill, described as the first and only FDA-approved oral GLP-1, and highlights that it prescribes FDA-approved finished medication when clinically appropriate.

3. Visit model, fulfillment, and delivery

Both providers use online clinician review. altRx emphasizes 100% online medical visits, a 2-minute quiz to start, and free discreet 2-day delivery when prescribed. Ro.co also allows online starts and requires a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness before prescribing branded medications.

4. Ongoing costs, commitments, and membership

altRx markets no membership, no hidden fees, and no commitment with the ability to cancel anytime. It lists buy now pay later options such as Affirm, Klarna, and Afterpay and says multi month plans reduce per month pricing. Ro.co states no hidden fees and that ongoing branded medication pricing can be significantly higher than compounded alternatives; exact ongoing costs depend on the medication prescribed.

5. Who each provider is built for

altRx is described as best for patients held back by price who want the most affordable GLP-1 program and are willing to use compounded injectables. Ro.co is described as best for injection averse patients or those who prioritize an FDA-approved oral option and brand regulatory clarity.

Price and payment structure

Embody positions itself as the low-cost option with compounded semaglutide advertised at $69/month, reduced from $299, and compounded tirzepatide at $119/month. Embody explicitly states no monthly membership and free shipping with fast 1-2 day delivery after clinician approval. IVIM Health lists semaglutide at $75/month and tirzepatide at $133/month and highlights a free month membership with code GIFT75 plus a program fee. Both operate on a direct-pay model and both use compounded medications, a status noted by each provider.

Program features and clinical approach

Embody offers prescription and telemed visits included with a straightforward, prescription-forward model. It emphasizes flat locked-in monthly pricing and minimal premium add-ons. IVIM Health emphasizes a structured GLP-1 ID protocol with clinician-guided titration and claims its protocol can accelerate outcomes by up to 40% compared to another published trial, a proprietary claim the provider qualifies as not a head-to-head study.

What that means for patients

If you want a lean, no-frills model focused on low monthly cost and quick shipping, Embody advertises that model directly. If you prefer a program with a formalized protocol, ongoing clinician optimization, and a membership structure that includes a program fee and promotional codes, IVIM Health presents that option.

Third-party ratings, evidence, and claims

IVIM Health highlights unusually strong external validation with a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot, which the provider presents as a rare dual satisfaction signal. IVIM also cites retrospective observational studies and internal published data tied to its protocol, while noting these are not randomized head-to-head trials and that results may vary. Embody does not present the same third-party rating claim in the supplied evidence but emphasizes rapid shipping and low entry price.

Medication status, shipping, and regulatory notes

Both providers prescribe compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Embody specifically advertises free shipping with arrival in 1-2 days after approval. IVIM notes that compounded drugs are prepared by independent compounding pharmacies and that medications may not be available in all states.

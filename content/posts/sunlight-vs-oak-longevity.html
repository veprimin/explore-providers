Price and pricing model compared

Sunlight advertises personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo and states “Same price forever” with no price increases ever and no hidden fees. Oak Longevity advertises semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month and highlights one flat price that covers all dosages and no subscriptions or membership fees. Oak also notes three-month prescriptions are billed upfront in some cases and that billing is non-recurring so you choose to renew each month.

Medication types, dosing and formulation

Sunlight lists injections and oral options for GLP-1 medications and advertises prescription and telehealth included. Oak Longevity offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, with semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month and one price covering all dosages. Oak explicitly states compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved finished products.

Clinical review and telehealth process

Sunlight emphasizes a 100% online medical visit with a clinician involved and prescription and telehealth included, with no insurance required. Oak Longevity also operates online but specifies no video call is required: patients complete a short online health questionnaire and a licensed physician reviews the case; Oak notes board-certified physicians in your state review every case and same-day approval is available for qualifying patients.

Shipping, coaching and other services

Sunlight promotes free and fast shipping, stating free shipping in 1-2 days and mentions cancel anytime and no monthly membership. Oak Longevity includes free shipping with tracking and free health coaching with every plan. Oak also clarifies refunds are not available after physician approval and once medication has shipped in some billing scenarios.

Regulatory notes and eligibility limits

Oak Longevity includes a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Oak also states patients with BMI below 20 do not qualify for GLP-1 treatment and lists other standard exclusions like age and pregnancy status. Sunlight’s regulatory capture notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

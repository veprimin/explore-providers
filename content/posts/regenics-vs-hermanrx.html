Clinician model and scope of care

Regenics delivers nurse-practitioner-led GLP-1 care; licensed nurse practitioners review intake before any prescription and oversee each prescription. HermanRx matches patients with independent, licensed, board-certified physicians across multiple categories including weight loss, and states physicians review intakes. Both platforms emphasize that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

What is included and service differences

HermanRx emphasizes genuinely all-inclusive pricing with no membership fee, no consultation fee and no separate shipping charge; it lists provider review, prescription, ongoing support and free discreet shipping in the monthly price. Regenics highlights free shipping and a LegitScript-certified pharmacy network and notes that no insurance is required; Regenics also states its compounding occurs in the USA. Regenics calls out FSA/HSA eligibility. HermanRx publishes a fast timeline and a secure patient portal with 24/7 support.

Shipping speed, availability and packaging

HermanRx provides a specific timeline on its landing evidence: about a 5-minute assessment, roughly 24h provider review and about 72h delivery if prescribed, and free discreet shipping in plain unmarked packaging to all 50 states.

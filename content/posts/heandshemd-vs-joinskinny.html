Pricing and promotional terms compared

He & She MD advertises semaglutide injections starting at $139/month and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide injections at $199/month as six-month introductory rates. The source repeatedly notes these are six-month promos that renew at higher six-month prices, so budget for renewal rather than the intro rate. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149, mentions a $140 promotional discount in landing copy, and offers monthly and multi-month bundles with flat-rate pricing so your monthly price stays the same regardless of dose increases.

Coaching, clinician contact and ongoing support

He & She MD bundles a coaching app that includes 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific guidance and progress tracking at no extra cost. JoinSkinny provides prescription and telemed visits, ongoing support, dose adjustments, monthly check-ins and 24/7 chat. Both emphasize clinician involvement, but He & She MD highlights an integrated app experience built into every plan while JoinSkinny highlights continuous medical monitoring and dose management via telemedicine.

Pharmacy type and regulatory notes

He & She MD uses compounded GLP-1 medications produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies and explicitly states compounded medications are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy or quality. JoinSkinny works with licensed U.S. pharmacies that meet FDA regulations and hold LegitScript certification and positions those pharmacies as filling branded or authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1 plus GIP when prescribed. The comparison here is direct: He & She MD emphasizes compounded products and a regulatory note about FDA review; JoinSkinny emphasizes FDA-regulated, LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners.

Shipping, fulfillment and cancellation

He & She MD promises free expedited shipping and no membership fee but frames pricing as six-month terms rather than true month-to-month. JoinSkinny promises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days with same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, and states you can cancel before your next billing cycle. If fast, temperature-controlled next-day shipment and the ability to cancel before the next charge are priorities, JoinSkinny documents those features directly.

Outcomes, eligibility and best for

JoinSkinny notes that GLP-1 and GLP-1 plus GIP medications are clinically shown to help patients lose 15 to 22 percent of body weight over 12 to 15 months when paired with consistent use and lifestyle guidance. He & She MD positions itself as best for patients who stalled with bare-bones subscriptions and want coaching and clinician access bundled rather than sold separately. Both providers require clinician review and prescriptions; He & She MD also notes not all customers will qualify for a prescription.

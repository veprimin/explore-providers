HumeCare+ vs Telos Rx: snapshot of the main differences

Both offer physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, but the services differ in four main ways: pricing starting points, whether supportive ingredients and scans are included, the presence of a money-back commitment, and how pricing and approvals are presented.

1) Price and how it is presented

Telos Rx advertises semaglutide as low as $99 per month and tirzepatide as low as $139 per month, with explicit language that a licensed provider must approve you. HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/month and a tirzepatide plus B12 and glycine protocol from $299/month. Both note that compounded medications are prescription-only and subject to clinician review.

Check Telos Rx starting prices and approval

2) Protocol packaging and supportive ingredients

HumeCare+ sells a bundled protocol: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with glycine and vitamins B6 and B12 included in the formulation, claiming these support muscle preservation and side-effect relief. Telos Rx presents semaglutide and tirzepatide as options prescribed online but the captured evidence does not show built-in supportive ingredients or bundled vitamins.

3) Body composition tracking and monitoring

HumeCare+ explicitly includes monthly body composition scans to track fat versus lean mass over time and an app for tracking medication and food logging, plus unlimited clinician access and a U.S. care team available 24/7. Telos Rx materials captured do not show a comparable body composition scan or app feature in the evidence provided.

See HumeCare+ scans and app features

4) Guarantees, promotional claims, and qualifiers

HumeCare+ advertises a 10% or your money back promise over six months and presents internal, company-reported results such as headline figures and an internal analysis of members. The company also states that compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated. Telos Rx emphasizes clinician review and that prices are as low as a stated amount, while captured landing page text warns that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Bottom line and recommendation

HumeCare+ vs Telos Rx comes down to whether you value a bundled protocol with supportive ingredients, body-composition scans, and a six month 10% money-back promise versus lower advertised starting prices with clinician approval required and fewer captured details about bundled supports. Both offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and both note compounded medications are not FDA-reviewed.

Choose HumeCare+ if you want a bundled GLP-1 protocol that includes glycine and B vitamins, monthly body composition scans, an app for tracking, 24/7 clinician access, free next-day delivery and a stated 10% money-back promise over six months, with pricing from $199/month for semaglutide or $299/month for tirzepatide plus supportive ingredients.

Choose Telos Rx if your priority is lower advertised starting prices for compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide, acknowledging that a US-licensed provider must approve you and that pricing, eligibility and state availability should be verified before enrolling.

Direct comparison at a glance

This comparison focuses on five key differences: pricing and promotions, pharmacy and regulatory status, shipping and delivery, medication options and dosing support, and cancellation or membership terms.

Pricing and promotions

LTTL: Advertising includes up to $140 off your first month and references prices such as $249 and $129 for 4 doses per month, with an emphasis on “same price, every dose.” JoinSkinny: plans start at $149, offer a $140 promotional discount, and provide monthly and 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles. Both recommend confirming total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

Pharmacy, formulation and regulatory notes

LTTL states its compounded GLP-1 medications are compounded by U.S.A. licensed pharmacies, prepared in a state-licensed pharmacy, and independently verified in FDA-registered labs. LTTL materials include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny says it works only with licensed U.S. pharmacies that prepare authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1 + GIP when prescribed, and that prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners. JoinSkinny also includes the same regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved when compounds are relevant.

Shipping, delivery and fulfillment

JoinSkinny documents free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1-2 days and same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, with next-day UPS delivery and tracking. LTTL indicates medications are delivered to your door and mentions independent lab verification and preparation in state-licensed pharmacies; specific same-day or next-day shipping timing is not supplied in the captured LTTL evidence. Both providers advise confirming state availability during signup.

Medication options, dosing and clinical support

LTTL emphasizes compounded full-dose GLP-1 and notes medications are confirmed every 3-6 months to stay within +/- 10% of target strength. Treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate; compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options, flat-rate dosing where monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases, and ongoing medical support including monthly check-ins, dose adjustments, secure telemedicine and 24/7 chat. JoinSkinny states most patients begin with a low dose and can increase over time at no extra cost, with dose adjustments guided by providers.

Membership, cancellation and customer experience

JoinSkinny highlights no membership fee, the ability to cancel anytime before the next billing cycle, and customer support available 24/7. LTTL materials caution that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change and advise confirming those details before enrollment; no specific membership or cancel-anytime language was captured for LTTL in the provided evidence.

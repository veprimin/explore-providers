How pricing and membership differ

Bodybuilding Health+ lists compounded semaglutide from $179/mo and tirzepatide from $209/mo and emphasizes no monthly membership, cancel anytime, and no hidden fees. Mochi Health presents a membership-plus-medication structure with pricing from $99/month and promotional 50% off your first month via code BESTWLHALFOFF; Mochi also notes a $39 promotional first month rate for some services and that standard membership after promotion may be $79 per month. The contrast is direct: Bodybuilding Health+ advertises flat treatment pricing with multi-month bundles, while Mochi uses membership plus medication where total monthly cost varies by plan and medication.

Consultation and clinical experience

Bodybuilding Health+ conducts online medical intake reviewed by a U.S.-licensed provider and builds personalized treatment plans via provider review. Mochi Health distinguishes itself by offering live video visits bookable in about 5 minutes, described as a synchronous visit where a licensed physician evaluates the case in real time. That live video model is Mochi’s defining feature compared with the asynchronous intake review typical of many competitors; Bodybuilding Health+ uses provider review rather than promoting live video as its core workflow.

Geographic availability and continuity of care

Mochi Health states reliable GLP-1 prescription access in all 50 states and 24/7 support with ongoing appointments. Bodybuilding Health+ notes that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that services are not available in all U.S. states. If nationwide access and 24/7 support matter, Mochi explicitly claims all 50 states coverage. If you prefer a no-membership model, Bodybuilding Health+ positions itself as a more transparent per-treatment option where availability depends on clinician approval and state restrictions.

Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both providers emphasize compounded medication options in their materials. Bodybuilding Health+ offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide pricing and includes the regulatory note that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Mochi Health likewise lists compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options and repeats the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. The comparison shows both platforms rely on compounded offerings and include the same regulatory caution.

Program breadth and features

Bodybuilding Health+ markets a multi-program telehealth platform including GLP-1, NAD+, sermorelin, and sexual performance options with multi-month bundles up to 12 months and medical oversight by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D. Mochi Health offers GLP-1 plus dermatology, hair, mental health, and intimacy services, and emphasizes membership services with ongoing appointments. In short, Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes weight management, longevity, and sexual performance under a single non-membership platform, while Mochi packages a broader set of specialties within a membership-plus-medication framework and synchronous doctor visits.

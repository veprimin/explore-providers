How each provider describes medication sourcing and regulation

Hers emphasizes FDA-approved brand-name options, stating Wegovy® is FDA-approved and not a compounded product. The source also notes Ozempic® is FDA approved for type 2 diabetes and may be prescribed off-label for weight loss at a clinician’s discretion, and that compounded medications, when offered elsewhere, are not FDA-approved. Refills explicitly offers compounded GLP-1 and repeats that compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality by the FDA. Both providers include regulatory qualifiers about compounded medications.

Ongoing care, support, and membership structure

Hers lists a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins and states an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required, with $39 for the first month and auto-renews at $149/month thereafter; medication is not available without an active membership. Refills highlights 24/7 health concierge support, unlimited clinician consultations at no extra cost, month-to-month plans with no membership or app fees, and free discreet shipping. These statements are captured from each provider’s source materials.

Quick verdict: Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes lower starting prices for compounded GLP-1s and a no monthly membership model, while BreezeMeds highlights injectable and oral GLP-1 options with starting prices shown from $199/mo and other figures that require confirmation. Price and published starting costs

Bodybuilding Health+ lists compounded semaglutide starting at $179/mo and tirzepatide from $209/mo, with multi-month bundles that lower effective monthly cost. BreezeMeds shows semaglutide injection starting at $199/mo, tirzepatide injection starting at $299, and oral options displayed from $399; a separate $169 figure appears without clear product association. Prices for both are subject to clinician review and medical eligibility.

Product forms and program scope

Bodybuilding Health+ emphasizes a multi-program telehealth platform: compounded GLP-1s (semaglutide and tirzepatide), NAD+, sermorelin, and sexual performance options. BreezeMeds lists injectable and oral GLP-1 options, explicitly showing both injection and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide as available on its site. The difference is that Bodybuilding Health+ frames a broader set of treatment categories beyond weight management, while BreezeMeds highlights both injectable and oral GLP-1 formats.

Membership, fees, cancellation, and transparency

Bodybuilding Health+ states no monthly membership, no hidden fees, and cancel anytime; it repeatedly emphasizes “No Monthly Membership | Cancel Anytime.” BreezeMeds markets “Affordable Pricing. Discreet Shipping.” but captured evidence notes fees and billing commitments were not fully visible, and advises confirming eligibility, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment. That makes Bodybuilding Health+ the clearer choice on membership rhetoric in the supplied sources, while BreezeMeds signals some price complexity that requires confirmation.

Regulatory and compound status

Both sources include regulatory notes that require confirmation. Bodybuilding Health+ explicitly states its compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are prepared by licensed U.S. pharmacies but “have not been evaluated or approved by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality.” BreezeMeds’ captured material says medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Both make clear treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Shipping, eligibility, and clinical review

Bodybuilding Health+ promotes fully online intake, provider review, and door-to-door shipping with medical oversight by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D., while noting not available in all U.S. states. BreezeMeds highlights discreet home delivery and ongoing support but the captured evidence urges users to confirm state availability and eligibility. Neither source supplies full nationwide availability details in the captured information.

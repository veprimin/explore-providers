Quick verdict: choose TrimRx if you want lifetime price protection and fast free shipping; choose IVIM Health if you prioritize third-party ratings and a proprietary clinician-guided protocol.

Direct comparison: 5 key differences

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: starting price and fees, pricing model and guarantees, third-party ratings and evidence, program structure and protocol claims, and shipping and program inclusions. Both providers use compounded medications that are not FDA-approved; that regulatory qualifier applies to both offerings.

1) Starting price and promotional offers

TrimRx lists compounded GLP-1 injections starting at $149/month and GLP-1+GIP starting at $249/month. TrimRx marketing notes a $140 promotional discount in captured landing-page evidence. IVIM Health advertises compounded semaglutide at $75/month and tirzepatide at $133/month in some captured bullets, and presents a promotional code GIFT75 for a free month membership; IVIM also lists a first-month price of $99 and ongoing $174/month in FAQ text. Use the exact pricing details on each provider page when you sign up because advertised offers and promotional codes vary.

2) Pricing model and guarantees

TrimRx emphasizes Lifetime Price Protection: lock in today’s price for life and pay the same price on every dose, with no monthly membership and the ability to cancel anytime. TrimRx states no insurance required and HSA/FSA eligibility. IVIM Health uses a membership model that includes program fees; IVIM highlights free month membership with code GIFT75 and a no-obligation free consultation. IVIM’s ongoing rate is presented as $174/month after initial pricing, and a $74.99/month program fee appears in captured evidence.

3) Third-party ratings and evidence

IVIM Health reports a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot, described as dual third-party verification and a rare trust signal. TrimRx reports serving 100,000+ customers and a 4.8 Excellent Rating, which are presented as strong real-world trust signals. IVIM also cites published observational studies and proprietary program outcomes; TrimRx cites internal averages and program outcome claims in its FAQ. Both providers note that individual results may vary and do not guarantee outcomes.

4) Program structure and clinical claims

IVIM Health promotes a proprietary GLP-1 ID clinician-guided protocol which the company says can accelerate outcomes by up to 40% compared to another published GLP-1 trial; IVIM labels that as a protocol claim and not a head-to-head study. IVIM also highlights weekly dose adjustments and clinician supervision. TrimRx offers telehealth visits and an online medical review with U.S. licensed providers, plus unlimited access to a care team and nutritional guidance. TrimRx states patients follow a clinically proven approach and provides program averages in captured content. Both emphasize clinician evaluation and prescription after review.

5) Shipping, fulfillment, and regulatory notes

TrimRx advertises free 1–2 day shipping and states medications are prepared and shipped by state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacies. IVIM notes medications are prepared by independent licensed compounding pharmacies and that availability may vary by state. Both providers explicitly state compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Quick verdict: SkinnyRx positions itself for people seeking daily oral semaglutide with FSA/HSA flexibility and free overnight shipping, while Eden Health emphasizes lower monthly pricing for semaglutide and a lifetime price guarantee. This SkinnyRx vs Eden Health comparison highlights the most distinctive supported differences to help you choose.

Pricing and payment options compared

SkinnyRx lists oral semaglutide starting at as low as $249/mo and notes the option to split payments with Affirm Buy Now Pay Later. Eden Health lists monthly pricing at every dose with semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129, and states no insurance is needed with a lifetime price guarantee. Both providers reference free expedited or overnight shipping.

See Eden Health monthly pricing

Delivery format and medication approach

Both SkinnyRx and Eden Health offer GLP-1 medication options, but SkinnyRx specifically promotes daily oral semaglutide tablets taken under the tongue as an alternative to injections. Eden Health lists semaglutide and tirzepatide programs but does not specify oral versus injectable formats in the captured evidence. Treatment at both providers is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Regulatory and compounded medication notes

SkinnyRx describes its semaglutide as compounded tablets and includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Eden Health notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Both sources indicate clinician review is required for prescriptions.

Support, testing and program structure

SkinnyRx highlights clinician-guided treatment with ongoing support, monthly refill forms, unlimited access to a patient care team, and partner pharmacies performing third-party testing every 3 to 6 months with a plus or minus 10 percent concentration confirmation for compounded lots. Eden Health frames its service as a guided GLP-1 program that combines medication, care, and support under a single monthly price, and advises confirming eligibility, pharmacy details, state availability, and renewal terms before enrollment.

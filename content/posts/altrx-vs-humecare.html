Key difference 1: Price and starting plans

Price separates these providers immediately. altRx advertises semaglutide starting at $89/month and tirzepatide at $149/month as a limited offer. HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/month and a tirzepatide protocol from $299/month. altRx emphasizes being the most affordable GLP-1 program on the market with multi-month price steps available per the source. HumeCare+ shows higher headline pricing and notes its prices reflect a promotional discount that may change.

Key difference 2: Protocol and included ingredients

altRx offers compounded semaglutide or compounded tirzepatide as medication options and positions itself as an all-inclusive monthly program covering medication, medical oversight, and supplies. HumeCare+ sells a bundled protocol: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide combined with glycine and vitamins B6 and B12 built into the formulation, plus supportive therapies. HumeCare+ frames these additions as muscle-preserving and side-effect support; altRx focuses on low cost and clinician-guided support.

Key difference 3: Tracking, scans, and guarantees

HumeCare+ includes monthly body-composition scans to track fat versus lean mass and offers a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months with terms to verify. The vendor reports internal results and stresses tracking lean mass not just weight. altRx does not supply body-composition scans or a money-back promise in the source; its core commitments are same price every month and free discreet 2-day delivery.

Key difference 4: Delivery, access, and visit model

Both providers use telehealth and prescriptions dispensed from state-licensed pharmacies per their sources, and both note compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated. altRx highlights 100% online medical visits, free discreet 2-day delivery, and buy now pay later options. HumeCare+ lists free next-day delivery, continuous physician optimization, a connected app for tracking, and unlimited clinician access with a U.S. care team available 24/7 according to the source.

Quick comparison: Sesame Care vs HealthRx serve online GLP-1 users differently. Quick verdict: Choose Sesame Care if you want a marketplace where you can browse and select clinicians with flexible appointments and pricing from as low as $59/mo*; choose HealthRx if you prefer prepaid compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide plans from $99/month with overnight cold shipping and a clinician review determining medication suitability.

How they differ at a glance

Both platforms offer online weight-management treatment paths that may include semaglutide or tirzepatide, but the most distinctive differences are clinician selection and marketplace model, pricing structure and insurance acceptance, compounded versus brand/insurance-covered medications, shipping and fulfillment, and how dosing and plan pricing are handled.

1. Clinician choice and care model

Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select providers based on specialty, availability, and pricing. HealthRx emphasizes that the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is appropriate and that you choose the plan it arrives on. Sesame highlights a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states. HealthRx centers the clinician review in its process rather than a marketplace selection model.

2. Pricing structure and insurance

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo* based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Sesame notes cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo and that insurance is accepted for weight loss medications; medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on insurance status and pharmacy. HealthRx advertises plans from $99/month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month, and states the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan. HealthRx also highlights that plan price does not change when your dose does and that there is no insurance billing layer on those plans.

3. Compounded medication versus brand/insurance options

HealthRx offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription and notes that compounded medication is not FDA-approved, is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom, and is not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HealthRx frames compounding as a way to avoid brand manufacturer markup and insurance billing. Sesame Care lists brand-name options such as semaglutide and tirzepatide with cash-pay and in-network prices and includes a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed; branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Sesame also lists Wegovy pill and Zepbound KwikPen details where applicable from its captured evidence.

4. Shipping and fulfillment

HealthRx describes overnight cold shipping with cold chain, tracked, free on every plan. Sesame Care captured evidence does not list shipping logistics in the supplied content; the landing-page evidence focuses on clinician choice, pricing, and treatment coverage. If shipping detail is critical to you, HealthRx provides explicit overnight cold shipping claims in the source evidence.

5. Dose changes, renewals and commitments

HealthRx states that dose adjustments are supported and that your plan price does not change when your dose does. HealthRx also warns that the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan and that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change. Sesame Care notes subscription pricing as low as $59/mo* is based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment and that results may vary; medication costs remain separate and vary by insurance and pharmacy.

Bottom line and who each is best for

Sesame Care is best for people who want broad telehealth choice, the ability to browse clinicians, flexible scheduling, and clear pricing that can start as low as $59/mo* under a 12-month commitment. HealthRx is best for people who confirm eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment and who prefer prepaid compounded plans with overnight cold shipping and a clinician review that determines medication suitability.

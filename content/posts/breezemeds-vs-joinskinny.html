Quick verdict: BreezeMeds and JoinSkinny both offer clinician-reviewed online weight-management treatment, but BreezeMeds emphasizes multiple injectable and oral GLP-1 options with discreet home delivery and pricing starting at $199, while JoinSkinny highlights flat-rate plans from $149 with free temperature-controlled 1–2 day shipping and multi-month bundles.

How BreezeMeds vs JoinSkinny differ at a glance

Both services use licensed clinicians to review intake and prescribe when medically appropriate, but the two providers differ most on price starting points, product formats offered, shipping speed and handling, pricing structure and bundles, and notes on compounded or branded products. Below we compare those points directly using supplied evidence.

Price starting point and pricing model

BreezeMeds: Semaglutide injection is shown starting at $199 per month; tirzepatide injection starting at $299; oral semaglutide and oral tirzepatide displayed from $399; a separate $169 figure appears without a clear product association. Pricing and eligibility are subject to the live offer and clinician review.
JoinSkinny: Plans start at $149 with a noted $140 promotional discount available; JoinSkinny uses transparent flat-rate pricing so the monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases and offers monthly and 3, 6, and 12 month bundles as presented.

Product formats and pharmacy notes

BreezeMeds lists injectable and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide options. The source notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality; medication-specific approval status must be confirmed.
JoinSkinny offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and states prescriptions are filled by licensed U.S. pharmacies that meet FDA regulations and hold LegitScript certification. JoinSkinny also notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Shipping, timing, and delivery

BreezeMeds promotes discreet home delivery and ongoing support but the landing evidence does not specify transit times. JoinSkinny specifies free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1 to 2 days for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, with next-day UPS shipping and details on weekend handling and insured shipments.

Commitments, cancellations, and support

BreezeMeds emphasizes clinician-reviewed treatment availability only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and cautions users to confirm eligibility, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment. JoinSkinny advertises no monthly membership, cancel anytime before your next billing cycle, 24/7 chat and monthly check-ins, and installment options with Afterpay, Klarna, and Affirm for bundles.

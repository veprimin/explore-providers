Strut Health vs BetterMe Rx is a comparison of two cash-pay telehealth options for compounded GLP-1 therapy. Quick verdict: choose Strut Health if you want a clinic focused on oral Semaglutide and guided consults; choose BetterMe Rx if you want a wider set of injectable and oral GLP-1 and tirzepatide formats and lower long-term monthly equivalents.

Strut Health vs BetterMe Rx: price structure and plans

Pricing is a core difference. Strut Health lists oral GLP-1 starting at From $199 per Month and highlights oral Semaglutide options. BetterMe Rx presents lower monthly equivalents when you choose longer prepay terms with 12-month equivalents as low as $145/mo for semaglutide injection and $185/mo for oral semaglutide, with monthly examples shown higher. BetterMe Rx makes clear that longer 3, 6, or 12 month supplies are billed according to the selected plan.

Why the pricing matters

Strut Health emphasizes a simple oral-specialist model with a single starting price point From $199 per Month. BetterMe Rx emphasizes multisize plans where the lowest per month figures require committing to longer prepaid terms, with explicit 12-month equivalents for multiple products.

Formats offered: oral specialization versus multiple GLP-1 formats

Strut Health specializes in oral-based program options and guided consultations, focusing clinical attention on oral Semaglutide and related dosing guidance. BetterMe Rx sells four medication and administration options: injectable and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide, and highlights a needle-free daily tirzepatide tablet as an uncommon cash-pay option.

Included services and logistics

Both platforms use U.S. licensed clinicians and compounding pharmacies when compounded medications are offered, but their included services differ. BetterMe Rx explicitly lists medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies and free 1 2 day shipping as included. Strut Health highlights guided consultations, ongoing progress communication, free shipping and cancel anytime language alongside its oral program messaging.

Safety and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded medications and both include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Strut Health adds that compounded Semaglutide is prepared by a U S licensed compounding pharmacy pursuant to an individualized prescription and that compounded medication is prescribed based on medical necessity with medical necessity required by a licensed physician for patients who fail or cannot use commercially available GLP-1 medications. BetterMe Rx states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

How each provider presents trade offs

Strut Health presents a focused oral approach and positions itself for users specifically seeking oral plans and guided consults, noting a smaller network than larger telehealth marketplaces. BetterMe Rx emphasizes choice across injectable and oral products, lower long-term monthly equivalents if you prepay, and bundled shipping and supplies, with the trade off that lowest prices require a 12-month plan.

Enhance MD vs BetterMe Rx compares two cash-pay telehealth options for compounded GLP-1s. Quick verdict: Enhance MD is strongest for lowest first-month entry and flat-rate dosing, while BetterMe Rx is strongest for patients who want injectable and oral formats with included shipping.

Price and billing: starting costs and ongoing models

Enhance MD advertises semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month, with flat-rate pricing that the provider says stays the same at every dose and no escalation during titration. BetterMe Rx lists 12-month equivalents starting at $145 per month and shows monthly prices that are higher unless you choose longer prepaid plans. For patients prioritizing the cheapest first month, Enhance MD is presented as the lower-entry option. For patients who prefer pricing smoothed across a prepaid plan with lower monthly equivalents, BetterMe Rx offers 3, 6, and 12-month options that reduce monthly-equivalent cost.

Early verdict summary

If your priority is the lowest first-month price and flat-rate dosing, Enhance MD fits that goal. If you want injectable and oral formats with included supplies and free 1 to 2 day shipping and are comfortable with prepaid terms, BetterMe Rx is the match.

Availability of compounds and formats

Both providers offer semaglutide and tirzepatide as compounded options. Enhance MD emphasizes both compounds on one platform and promotes availability of semaglutide and tirzepatide. BetterMe Rx offers both compounds in multiple administration formats: injectable and oral tablets for semaglutide and tirzepatide, noting four medication and administration options.

Distinctive differences organized

1. First-month entry vs longer-term plan pricing

Enhance MD: semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month. BetterMe Rx: compounded treatment from $145 per month on a 12-month plan, with monthly prices shown higher unless you elect a longer prepaid plan.

Enhance MD: flat-rate pricing that stays the same at every dose after the introductory period, described as avoiding fee escalation during titration. BetterMe Rx: lower monthly-equivalent pricing is tied to longer 3, 6, or 12-month supplies billed according to the selected plan; lowest prices require a 12-month plan.

3. Formats and fulfillment

Enhance MD: highlights access to both semaglutide and tirzepatide on one platform and a clinician-directed process; specific shipping or supply inclusions are not supplied. BetterMe Rx: explicitly includes medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies and free 1 to 2 day shipping in its model.

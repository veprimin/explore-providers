Sesame Care vs FeelGood Meds answers a common question: which telehealth route fits your priorities for GLP-1 weight-loss care? Early verdict: choose Sesame Care if you want a marketplace with clinician choice and insurance options; choose FeelGood Meds if you want compounded medication options starting at $149/mo and free shipping, with no membership fee.

Explore Sesame Care clinician options

Direct comparison summary: models, pricing, medication types

Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select clinicians, offering ongoing video visits, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo* and cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo. FeelGood Meds offers personalized GLP-1 treatments with compounded injections from $149/mo, compounded tablets from $249/mo, and a branded option shown from $1,999/mo, with no membership fee and free shipping. Both require a clinician review for medical appropriateness.

Key difference 1: Care model and clinician choice

Sesame Care emphasizes a marketplace model where patients can choose clinicians based on specialty, availability and pricing, which the provider states gives more control over the care relationship. FeelGood Meds presents clinician-reviewed online treatment but does not describe a marketplace browsing model in the supplied evidence. This leads to a clear distinction: Sesame Care highlights broad telehealth choice and a large clinician network; FeelGood Meds highlights clinician-reviewed personalized treatment and a specific focus on compounded and branded medication options.

Key difference 2: Pricing structure and insurance

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo* and notes that medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on your insurance status and pharmacy. Sesame Care also states insurance is accepted for weight loss medications and lists price with insurance as low as $25 for certain medications. FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections starting at $149/mo, compounded tablets from $249/mo and a branded option shown from $1,999/mo, and states no insurance is required. Both sources include qualifiers that medication availability and pricing depend on clinician review and other terms.

Key difference 3: Medication types and regulatory notes

Sesame Care offers both oral and injectable GLP-1 medication options in its pricing examples, including semaglutide and tirzepatide with specific dosing and price ranges, and notes the Wegovy pill is FDA-approved for certain adults. Sesame Care includes a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. FeelGood Meds offers compounded injections and compounded tablets and explicitly notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. FeelGood Meds also lists a branded medication option shown at a much higher price point and emphasizes that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Key difference 4: Fees, shipping and membership

FeelGood Meds markets no membership fee, free shipping and no hidden fees in the captured evidence. Sesame Care presents subscription pricing for ongoing visits and care as low as $59/mo* based on a 12-month commitment in one program called Success by Sesame, and notes medication costs are separate. Each provider includes specific qualifiers about commitments and that results may vary when referencing outcomes studies.

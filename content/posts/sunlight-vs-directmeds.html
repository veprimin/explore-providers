Pricing and transparency compared

Sunlight advertises personalized GLP-1 plans starting at $88/mo with messaging that price is the same forever and no hidden fees. Direct Meds shows a promotional starting price of $147 and separately lists standard FAQ pricing of $297/mo for semaglutide and $399/mo for tirzepatide. Both providers present promotions; Direct Meds explicitly notes its FAQ standard prices, and Sunlight repeats a same price forever claim. Users should confirm current pricing and terms directly with each provider.

Medication format and regulatory distinctions

Sunlight offers injections and oral options and advises that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Direct Meds focuses on compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats and clearly states that its compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. That regulatory distinction is a primary difference when comparing Sunlight vs Directmeds.

Shipping, supplies and clinical support

Both providers include medication and telehealth elements but emphasize different logistics. Sunlight promotes free 1-2 day shipping and no membership with clinician review and prescription included. Direct Meds advertises free temperature-controlled next-day UPS or FedEx shipping, supplies included, and ongoing clinician and nursing support. Direct Meds also notes a full refund if a prescription is not approved.

Policies, limitations and buyer considerations

Sunlight repeatedly advises confirming eligibility, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment. Direct Meds lists no monthly membership fee and month-to-month cancellation for standard service but warns that subscribe-and-save plans require a stated term and that service is unavailable in Mississippi and Louisiana. Direct Meds also documents a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Both providers mention no hidden fees in their captured evidence.

Most distinctive differences

  • Price headline: Sunlight starts at $88/mo with a same price forever claim; Direct Meds advertises a $147 promo and higher FAQ standard prices.
  • Medication status: Direct Meds offers compounded medications and states they are not FDA-approved; Sunlight highlights branded/medication approval must be confirmed and warns about interchangeability.
  • Formats and support: Sunlight lists injections and oral options; Direct Meds lists injectable and sublingual compounded options plus nursing support and a refund if prescription is not approved.
  • Geographic and policy limits: Direct Meds is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana; Sunlight directs customers to confirm state availability.

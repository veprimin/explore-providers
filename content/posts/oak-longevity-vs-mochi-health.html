2. Clinical visit model: asynchronous review vs live video

Oak uses an online intake questionnaire with a licensed clinician reviewing cases asynchronously; Oak states no video call is required and board-certified physicians review every case. Mochi offers live video visits bookable in about 5 minutes, a synchronous consultation model where a doctor evaluates the case in real time and answers questions during the visit.

Direct effect: Oak aims for speed and simplicity with asynchronous review; Mochi prioritizes a real-time physician conversation.

3. Membership, shipping, refunds, and treatment limits

Oak explicitly advertises no subscriptions, no membership fees, free health coaching, and free shipping with tracking. Oak notes three-month prescriptions are billed upfront, no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped, and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Oak also states patients with BMI below 20 don’t qualify for GLP-1 treatment and elsewhere notes not appropriate for patients under 18, pregnant, or nursing.

Mochi operates primarily on a membership-plus-medication direct-pay model; it states membership-plus-medication pricing starts as low as $99/month, ongoing cost varies by plan and medication, and terms vary by state. Mochi also advertises reliable GLP-1 prescription access in all 50 states, 24/7 support, and ongoing appointment availability.

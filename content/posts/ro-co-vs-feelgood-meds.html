Ro.co vs FeelGood Meds: key distinction in medication type

The most decisive difference between Ro.co and FeelGood Meds is medication type and regulatory status. Ro.co offers the Wegovy pill, presented as the first and only FDA-approved oral GLP-1 weight loss medication available on mainstream platforms, and emphasizes that its medication is an FDA-approved finished product, not compounded. FeelGood Meds offers compounded injections and compounded tablets, with a branded medication option also shown from $1,999/month. Both sources note that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

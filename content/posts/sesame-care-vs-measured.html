Choose based on whether you prioritize clinician selection and insurance options or lower promotional compounded prices.

Care model and clinician choice

Sesame Care offers a marketplace model where patients can browse and select providers by specialty, availability, and pricing, giving more control over the care relationship. Measured emphasizes same-day prescription decisions and unlimited messaging with dietitian support, but does not present a marketplace browsing model in the supplied evidence.

Medication type and regulatory notes

Sesame Care lists FDA-approved branded options and cash-pay GLP-1 medication pricing, including semaglutide and tirzepatide formulations and notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed. Measured offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide; the source explicitly states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Both providers note Rx and clinician discretion where applicable.

Pricing, insurance, and promotional claims

Sesame Care advertises ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment and shows cash-pay medication prices as low as $149/mo for semaglutide and $299/mo for tirzepatide with insurance pricing listed as low as $25 for some dosages; medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on insurance status and pharmacy. Measured advertises promotional compounded semaglutide from $89/mo and compounded tirzepatide from $189/mo and emphasizes promotional pricing, free two-day shipping, and cancel anytime policies; the source warns promotional pricing may change.

Support, features, and experience variance

Measured highlights unlimited messaging, 24/7 Care Team access, and dietitian support with an internal outcome claim that members averaged 31 lb loss at six months based on internal analysis; the source cautions individual results vary and are not guaranteed and that internal outcome claims are not independent evidence. Sesame Care highlights a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states, with the note that experience can vary by clinician.

Shipping, access, and cancellation

Measured includes free two-day shipping on every order and promotes cancel anytime with no insurance required. Sesame Care notes medication costs vary by insurance and pharmacy and that subscription pricing claims are based on a 12-month commitment; shipping or free-shipping claims are not supplied for Sesame Care in the provided evidence.

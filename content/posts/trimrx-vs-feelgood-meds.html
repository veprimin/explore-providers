Quick verdict: TrimRx vs FeelGood Meds

The core comparison is straightforward: TrimRx emphasizes compounded GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP plans with a Lifetime Price Protection and free 1–2 day shipping, while FeelGood Meds offers compounded injections and tablets starting at $149 and a branded medication option priced from $1,999. Verdict: choose TrimRx if you value lifetime price locking, fast 1–2 day shipping, and scale; choose FeelGood Meds if you want a wider product menu that includes tablets and a branded route.

1. Pricing and price protections

TrimRx: Weight-loss plans starting at $149/month for compounded GLP-1 and $249/month for GLP-1+GIP, with a promotional $140 discount mentioned and a specific Lifetime Price Protection that locks in today’s price for life. FeelGood Meds: Compounded injections from $149/month, compounded tablets from $249/month, and a branded medication option shown from $1,999/month. FeelGood Meds also highlights a Weight Loss Money Back Guarantee. Both providers state no membership fee and no insurance required in their captured claims.

2. Medication formats and offerings

TrimRx focuses on compounded GLP-1 injections and compounded GLP-1+GIP injections, and notes an alternative pathway where a provider may write a prescription for branded medications that the patient fills separately. FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections, compounded tablets, and a branded medication option priced much higher. The available formats differ: FeelGood Meds explicitly offers tablets and branded pricing while TrimRx emphasizes dual-agonist compounded injections as an in-stock option.

3. Shipping, membership and cancellation

TrimRx claims free 1–2 day shipping, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime. FeelGood Meds claims free shipping and no membership fee in captured landing-page evidence, but does not specify a 1–2 day timeline. Both operate on direct-pay models in the captured information and state no insurance is required.

4. Trust signals and customer protections

TrimRx highlights 100,000+ customers and a 4.8 Excellent Rating and promotes Lifetime Price Protection. FeelGood Meds highlights a Weight Loss Money Back Guarantee and customer weight-loss examples but does not include the same scale metrics in the captured evidence. These represent different approaches to customer reassurance: TrimRx with scale and price locking, FeelGood Meds with a refund guarantee claim.

5. Regulatory status and clinician role

Both providers state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. FeelGood Meds notes that treatment and refunds are available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. TrimRx states prescriptions for branded medications are filled separately and that compounded drugs are prepared by state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacies.

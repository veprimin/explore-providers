Key differences organized by what matters most

1. Bundled supportive ingredients and measurement

HumeCare+ advertises a bundled protocol: GLP-1 medication paired with glycine and vitamins B6 and B12 built into the formulation plus monthly body composition scans to track lean mass versus fat. HermanRx compounds GLP-1 medicines with vitamin B12 but does not list glycine or B6 as included supports and does not publish routine body-composition scanning as part of its offering.

Review HumeCare+ supportive protocol

2. Guarantees and published outcome claims

HumeCare+ promotes a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months and cites internal analyses and headline results from members, while HermanRx does not publish a money-back guarantee. HumeCare+ also makes specific timing notes about when effects may appear, including that it can take up to 8 to 9 weeks to feel full medication effects and staged dose increases; HermanRx publishes fast provider-review and delivery timelines but no refund promise.

3. Pricing model and fees

HermanRx emphasizes genuinely all-inclusive monthly pricing with no membership fee, no separate consultation charge, and free discreet shipping in all 50 states. HumeCare+ includes free next-day delivery and clinician access but lists its pricing as a promotional cash-pay price and notes pricing shown reflects a promotional discount that may change.

4. Timelines, clinician access, and support

HermanRx publishes a rough timeline: about a 5-minute assessment, provider review in about 24 hours, and delivery in about 72 hours if prescribed, plus 24/7 patient support and a secure portal. HumeCare+ highlights unlimited clinician access, a U.S. care team available 24/7, a dedicated app for tracking, and continuous physician optimization as the treatment evolves.

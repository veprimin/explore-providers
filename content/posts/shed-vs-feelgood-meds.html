Shed vs FeelGood Meds: 3 to 5 key differences

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: medication options and regulatory notes, pricing and starting costs, coaching and service model, membership and fees, and shipping or guarantees where stated.

1. Medication options and regulatory notes

Shed: Offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 and compounded options. The source explicitly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a provider’s prescription. FeelGood Meds: Offers compounded injections, compounded tablets, and a branded medication option. Its materials also state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Both providers require a licensed clinician to determine whether treatment is appropriate.

FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections starting at $149/month, compounded tablets from $249/month, and a branded option shown from $1,999/month. FeelGood Meds positions $149 as the lowest starting monthly cost for compounded injections.

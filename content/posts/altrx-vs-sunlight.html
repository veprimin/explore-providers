How they differ: price, product type, and pricing commitment

Both companies offer 100% online medical visits and no insurance required, but the comparison pivots on five main differences: advertised starting price and multi month tiers, whether medication is compounded or presented as branded options, shipping timing, pricing promises, and availability or regulatory notes. Below we compare those points directly.

1. Advertised starting price and plan details

altRx advertises semaglutide starting at $89 per month and tirzepatide at $149 per month with multi month discounts that reduce per month cost on 3, 6, and 12 month plans. Sunlight advertises plans as low as $88 per month with a summer promo and states “as low as $88” plus promotional offsets. Neither source provides full enrollment terms beyond the captured starting prices and promotional language.

2. Medication type and formulation options

altRx explicitly markets compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, noting that prescriptions are filled by state licensed 503A compounding pharmacies and including the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA approved. Sunlight emphasizes injections and oral options and says personalized GLP 1 plans are available, but captured evidence states that medication specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

3. Shipping speed and delivery

altRx offers free and discreet 2 day delivery for treatment kits once prescribed, with typical processing and delivery time approximately 4 to 5 business days from the provider review to fulfillment. Sunlight advertises fast free shipping in 1 to 2 days and free discreet 2 day shipping in landing page copy. Shipping timing language differs in the captured evidence.

4. Pricing promises and fees

altRx emphasizes “same price every month” and no membership, insurance or hidden fees, plus buy now pay later options. Sunlight uses the phrase “same price forever” and “no price increases ever” in its captured materials. Both claim cancel anytime and no monthly membership in the captured evidence, but altRx highlights multi month plan discounts while Sunlight highlights a perpetual price guarantee in marketing copy.

5. Availability, clinician review, and regulatory notes

altRx notes availability to residents of all 50 U.S. states with prescribing authority and provider availability varying by state and that a licensed provider will review health evaluation and conduct telehealth consultations; if treatment is appropriate, a prescription will be sent and the medication ships from a licensed pharmacy. altRx includes the regulatory statement that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety effectiveness or quality. Sunlight captured evidence notes medication specific approval status must be confirmed and cautions that pricing eligibility pharmacy state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Head-to-head: what each plan actually includes

Comparing HumeCare+ vs HealthRx requires looking at what is bundled with the medication and how pricing is shown. HumeCare+ sells a protocol that combines physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide together with glycine, vitamin B12, and B6, monthly body-composition scans, a connected app, and clinician access. HealthRx offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription with overnight cold shipping and a clinician review that determines medication suitability; the lowest advertised semaglutide price requires a longer prepaid plan.

Five most distinctive differences

1. Price framing and starting monthly cost

HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/month and a tirzepatide protocol from $299/month. HealthRx advertises semaglutide from $99/month on a 12-month prepaid plan and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month, but notes the lowest monthly price requires a longer prepaid commitment.

2. Bundled supportive ingredients vs bare compound

HumeCare+ specifically adds glycine plus vitamins B6 and B12 into its protocol for muscle support and side-effect relief, and presents these as included rather than optional. HealthRx states nothing else is added and describes plans as the compound prepared to prescription, so additional supportive ingredients are not part of the advertised offering.

3. Tracking and clinician access

HumeCare+ emphasizes monthly body-composition scans to track fat versus lean mass, a dedicated app for tracking and food logging, and unlimited clinician access with a U.S. care team available 24/7. HealthRx highlights clinician review of each file to decide medication suitability and offers overnight cold shipping and dose adjustments without price changes, but captured evidence does not show body-composition scans or 24/7 clinician access.

4. Shipping and dosing logistics

Both vendors ship medication, but HealthRx highlights overnight cold chain, tracked, free shipping on every plan. HumeCare+ advertises free next-day delivery and discrete shipping as part of its plan. HealthRx also notes your plan price does not change when your dose does.

5. Promises, refunds, and regulatory notes

HumeCare+ advertises a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months with terms to verify, and it repeatedly states results are company-reported and that individual results may vary. Both HumeCare+ and HealthRx offer compounded medications; each source explicitly notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

HealthRx focuses on lower advertised monthly pricing via prepaid plans and the clinician review to determine whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is appropriate; it also warns that the lowest monthly price requires a longer prepaid commitment and that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Safety and regulatory reminders

Both HumeCare+ and HealthRx offer compounded GLP-1 medications and explicitly note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HumeCare+ also notes GLP-1 drugs carry serious risks, including a boxed warning for thyroid C-cell tumors. Treatment at both providers is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Bottom line: who should pick which?

When weighing HumeCare+ vs HealthRx, choose based on whether you want bundled supportive care or lower advertised monthly pricing. Choose HumeCare+ if you want a GLP-1 protocol with added glycine and B vitamins, monthly body-composition scans, a connected app, unlimited clinician access and a 10%-or-money-back promise over six months. Choose HealthRx if your priority is lower advertised starting monthlies via a prepaid semaglutide plan, overnight cold shipping, and a clinician review that decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is appropriate.

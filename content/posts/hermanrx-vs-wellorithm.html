How the services compare at a glance

This direct comparison uses only the supplied evidence to compare HermanRx and Wellorithm across transparency of pricing, geographic clinician coverage, available GLP-1 options, shipping and timelines, and regulatory notes about compounded medications.

Pricing transparency and structure

HermanRx publishes all-inclusive monthly prices: compounded semaglutide + B12 at $249/month and compounded tirzepatide + B12 at $349/month, with no separate membership or consultation fee. Use HermanRx for upfront all-in rates; use Wellorithm if you accept quote-based pricing.

Clinician coverage and access

Wellorithm emphasizes licensed clinicians in all 50 states and positions that coverage as a key strength. HermanRx also operates in all 50 states but highlights matching patients with licensed, board-certified physicians across five treatment categories. If guaranteed 50-state clinician availability is your priority, Wellorithm is presented as the main advantage.

Available GLP-1 options and service features

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. HermanRx bundles vitamin B12 with its GLP-1 offerings and lists additional treatment categories such as hormones and sexual health on one platform. Wellorithm lists both compounds and a fully virtual consultation model with medication shipped to the home after an online assessment.

Shipping timelines and support

HermanRx publishes a roughly 5-minute assessment, about 24-hour provider review, and about 72-hour delivery when prescribed, plus free discreet shipping and 24/7 patient support. Wellorithm advertises virtual consultations and medication shipped to your home with fast, free shipping, but does not publish the same specific timeline details in the supplied evidence.

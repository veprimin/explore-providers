Pallas Health vs BetterMe Rx: Main differences at a glance

Comparing Pallas Health vs BetterMe Rx requires focusing on five distinctive differences supported by the source: medication routes and brand availability, pricing structure and introductory offers, plan billing terms and length, shipping and fulfillment, and clinical eligibility and regulatory notes.

1. Medication options and clinician role

Pallas Health offers FDA-approved Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound and Mounjaro alongside clinician-personalized compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared in the USA when a brand medication is not the right fit. BetterMe Rx offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and oral formats, including a needle-free daily tirzepatide tablet, and lists four medication and administration options.

2. Pricing structure and introductory pricing

Pallas Health publishes compounded semaglutide at $139 for the first month, then $597 every 12 weeks (a $199/month average), and compounded tirzepatide at $179 first month then $897 every 12 weeks ($299/mo average). Brand-name cash prices are listed per month for Wegovy, Zepbound, Ozempic and Mounjaro. BetterMe Rx advertises compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan, with monthly prices shown and lower monthly-equivalents available for longer prepaid plans; examples include semaglutide injection $205 monthly or as low as $145/month on a 12-month plan.

3. Billing cadence and cancellation terms

Pallas prices compounded plans by medication rather than by milligram so titration does not raise plan price, and compounded plans renew every 12 weeks for the stated renewal figures. Pallas is cash-pay only and does not bill insurance. BetterMe Rx offers 3, 6, or 12-month supplies billed according to the selected plan; the lowest per month prices require longer prepaid commitments, and cancel-anytime language appears alongside prepaid longer-term plans.

4. Shipping and fulfillment

Pallas includes free expedited and discreet shipping from US-licensed compounding pharmacies and states compounds are prepared in the USA. BetterMe Rx includes free 1 to 2 day delivery and discreet U.S. shipping, with medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding and supplies included.

5. Clinical review and regulatory notes

Both providers require a licensed clinician review to determine eligibility and explicitly note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Pallas notes eligibility is determined by a US-licensed clinician and that it does not manufacture or compound medication; compounded medications are prepared by US-licensed compounding pharmacies. BetterMe Rx similarly states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

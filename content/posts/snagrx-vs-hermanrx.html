Formulations, quality notes, and guarantees

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options. SnagRx states its compounded medications are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products, and it highlights a full refund if a prescription is declined plus a 6-month weight-loss guarantee where terms apply. HermanRx also states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and notes it may offer FDA-approved medications depending on a clinician’s recommendation; HermanRx does not publish an outcome guarantee.

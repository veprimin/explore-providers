Comparing Sprout Health vs IVIM Health quickly: Sprout Health emphasizes rapid, low-friction entry with a $50 first-month discount and outcome framing of 10–26% body weight loss over 12 months. IVIM Health emphasizes clinician-guided programming, third-party validation, and promotional codes that reduce upfront cost. Early verdict: choose Sprout Health if you want the fastest, simplest sign up; choose IVIM Health if verified third-party ratings and a structured protocol matter more.

Start with Sprout Health

How they differ: price and promotional structure

Sprout Health advertises a $50 off first month offer, bringing the initial cost to $199, with ongoing pricing at $249/month. IVIM Health publishes promotional options including a GIFT75 code for a free month membership and a no-obligation free consultation valued at $75, with first-month pricing described as $99 and ongoing pricing at $174/month in published FAQs. Both operate on a direct-pay model and do not bill insurance. These facts are from each provider’s stated materials.

Clinician process and program style

Sprout Health is presented as a fully online, fast-entry GLP-1 platform with a “lightning-quick approval” process and rapid shipment after clinician review, suited for patients wanting minimal onboarding friction. IVIM Health offers a clinician-guided GLP-1 ID protocol described as a structured, personalized titration and optimization approach. IVIM notes a no-obligation free consultation for new patients to evaluate history and goals before RX.

Evidence, outcomes, and satisfaction signals

Sprout Health frames outcomes as “lose 10–26% of your body weight in 12 months” based on GLP-1 clinical outcome literature, but it does not offer a published outcome guarantee. IVIM Health cites its own protocol claims and published observational studies and highlights a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot as a strong satisfaction signal. IVIM also reports protocol-based claims such as “up to 40% faster” or “46% more weight over 68 weeks” in its materials, noting those are not head-to-head randomized trials.

Medication types, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both providers prescribe compounded GLP-1 medications and state that compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Sprout Health specifically notes its medication is compounded and not an FDA-approved finished product. IVIM also emphasizes that compounded medications are prepared by independent licensed compounding pharmacies and are used under ongoing clinician supervision.

Risks, limitations, and transparency

Both companies note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that individual results vary. Sprout Health notes higher ongoing cost after the discounted month and that compounded medication status should be confirmed. IVIM warns compounded products carry risks, may not be available in all states, and that its protocol claims are not independent randomized comparisons.

Final comparison and concise recommendation

Sprout Health vs IVIM Health comes down to tradeoffs between speed and structured validation. Sprout Health is focused on the fastest possible online entry and a clear first-month discount. IVIM Health emphasizes clinician-driven optimization, third-party ratings, and promotional pathways that reduce upfront cost.

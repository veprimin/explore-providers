MedVi vs HumeCare+ answers a straightforward question: choose MedVi if you want the lowest promotional price and a no-clinic telehealth route; choose HumeCare+ if you want a bundled protocol with body composition tracking and a money-back promise. Pricing and promotional structure

MedVi advertises compounded semaglutide starting at $179/month as a winter promotional first month, including fast, free shipping, with ongoing standalone months rising to $299 and a three-month plan at $649. HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/mo and a dual-action tirzepatide protocol from $299/mo, with the site noting those prices reflect a promotional discount that may change. Both operate on a cash-pay model for compounded medications; neither offers branded FDA-approved medication prices in the supplied evidence.

Medication choices and clinical approach

MedVi offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only, with provider review and telehealth-based prescribing. HumeCare+ offers a choice between personalized compounded semaglutide or dual-action tirzepatide, with the dose set by your provider. If having tirzepatide available is important, HumeCare+ supports that option; if you prefer a semaglutide-only pathway and the lowest first-month outlay, MedVi is positioned around that use case.

Support, protocol extras, and monitoring

MedVi emphasizes a simple online process, no clinic visits, and 24/7 support from certified medical professionals. HumeCare+ wraps clinician-prescribed GLP-1 medication with glycine, vitamin B12 and B6 included in the protocol, monthly body composition scans to track fat versus lean mass, an app for tracking, and unlimited clinician access. HumeCare+ describes these extras as addressing muscle loss and side effects that bare GLP-1 programs ignore. These represent a core philosophical difference: MedVi prioritizes simplicity and lower promo cost; HumeCare+ prioritizes a bundled, muscle-preserving protocol and monitoring.

Refunds, cancellation, and delivery

HumeCare+ advertises a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months, subject to full terms and eligibility. HumeCare+ also notes cancel subscription anytime and free next-day delivery. MedVi describes fast, free shipping for the first month promo and a program that starts with no contract; MedVi also notes that OpenLoop Health clinicians retain prescription decisions and that the assessment does not create a doctor patient relationship. Both providers state medication is included in their program cost when applicable.

Regulatory and safety notes

Both vendors offer compounded medications. The supplied evidence for MedVi and HumeCare+ states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. MedVi materials emphasize that compounded GLP-1s are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy. HumeCare+ highlights similar regulatory language and adds that GLP-1 drugs carry serious risks, including a boxed warning for thyroid C-cell tumors. Both emphasize clinician discretion: prescriptions are provided only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

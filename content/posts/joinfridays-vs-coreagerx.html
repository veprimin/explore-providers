How pricing differs between JoinFridays and CoreAgeRx

JoinFridays advertises programs starting at $117/month with multi-month plans that lower effective monthly cost and a standalone one-month plan priced at $249. Early on, the core difference is variable introductory tiers and aggressive promo codes at JoinFridays versus CoreAgeRx keeping a steady monthly rate.

Medication form and availability: compounded versus stated dosing

JoinFridays provides compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide options and also lists name brand purchases as separate line items on its pricing page. The JoinFridays evidence states compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. CoreAgeRx lists tirzepatide up to 7.5 mg and semaglutide up to 1.0 mg with flat monthly prices but captured landing-page detail notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Clinician review, visit length, and guidance

Both providers state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. JoinFridays emphasizes personalized, clinician-guided care and dosing guidance throughout the program. CoreAgeRx highlights a 3-minute online visit on its landing page but also cautions that eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage, and renewal terms may change and should be confirmed.

Memberships, cancellation, and billing surprises

JoinFridays markets no membership fees and the ability to cancel at any time, calling out a cancel-anytime, no-fee structure. CoreAgeRx emphasizes no membership fee and no price hikes, claiming no billing surprises and same price every month. The evidence for both preserves qualifiers about checking eligibility and terms before enrollment.

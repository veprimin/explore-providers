Clinical review process and telehealth delivery

Both providers operate online with clinician review, but the patient experience differs. altRx offers 100% online medical visits that may occur via live video or through an asynchronous provider review, as determined by the provider and state telehealth laws. Oak Longevity conducts an asynchronous clinical review without requiring a video call, with board certified physicians reviewing cases in state. Both require a licensed clinician to determine whether treatment is medically appropriate.

Shipping speed, extras, and support

altRx advertises free, discreet 2-day delivery, ongoing care team support, and a complete treatment kit including syringes and prep pads. Oak Longevity offers free shipping with tracking, free health coaching included, and notes same-day approval is available for qualifying patients. These differences may influence who gets medication fastest and what ancillary resources are available during treatment.

Medication type, regulatory and eligibility notes

Both altRx and Oak Longevity supply compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. The providers explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Oak notes some eligibility limits such as not qualifying patients with BMI below 20 for GLP-1 treatment; Oak also lists exclusions for patients under 18, pregnant, or nursing. altRx states availability to residents of all 50 U.S. states while noting prescribing authority varies by state and treatment is subject to clinician review.

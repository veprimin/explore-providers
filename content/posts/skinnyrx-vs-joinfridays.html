The SkinnyRx vs JoinFridays decision comes down to three clear differences: price and discounting, medication options, and payment or delivery perks. Early verdict: choose SkinnyRx if you prioritize oral semaglutide with FSA/HSA eligibility and fast shipping; choose JoinFridays if you want the lowest entry price, access to compounded tirzepatide, and no long-term contract.

Price and discount structure

Cost is a stark contrast in this comparison. SkinnyRx lists oral semaglutide plans as low as $249 per month. JoinFridays advertises a lower starting point at $117 per month for compounded semaglutide, with tirzepatide options starting at $198 per month. JoinFridays also emphasizes aggressive promo codes for up to specific dollar amounts off tirzepatide and semaglutide, while SkinnyRx highlights split payments with Affirm as a payment flexibility option.

Medication options and clinical framing

Both platforms offer clinician-guided programs and compounded medication options, and both include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. SkinnyRx focuses on daily oral semaglutide tablets designed with clinically supported ingredients. JoinFridays provides both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide on one platform, noting tirzepatide as the more effective option and semaglutide as more affordable.

Payment, membership, and cancellation differences

JoinFridays markets no membership fees and the ability to cancel anytime with no long-term contract. It also shows multi-month plans that lower the effective monthly rate, including a 12-month option that averages $150 per month. SkinnyRx presents subscription plans that can be canceled and a refund policy that allows a full refund if canceled before a licensed provider completes the prescription review; once fulfillment begins, an order becomes non-refundable. Both require a clinician review and prescription as appropriate.

Shipping and administrative perks

SkinnyRx advertises free overnight shipping on all orders and processing that provides shipping and tracking information within two business days after a prescription is written. JoinFridays does not list overnight shipping as part of the captured evidence but highlights membership-included services and no-contract flexibility. Choose SkinnyRx if fast delivery and FSA/HSA eligibility matter; choose JoinFridays if entry price and cancellation flexibility matter more.

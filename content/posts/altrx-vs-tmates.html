Key differences at a glance: price, clinician access, formulation, availability

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences between altRx and TMates: pricing structure, clinician access and oversight, which compounded medications are emphasized, state coverage and shipping, and membership or payment features. The evidence is taken directly from each provider’s materials.

Pricing structure and transparency

altRx advertises a limited offer with compounded semaglutide starting at $89/month and compounded tirzepatide at $149/month. The altRx materials also present multi-month plan reductions for semaglutide and tirzepatide, and emphasize “same price every month” and “no membership, insurance or hidden fees.” TMates positions itself as a flat $199/month program for semaglutide, “same price all doses,” with published multi-month tiers that show higher per-month amounts on shorter plans but a flat list price of $199/month stated on landing content.

Clinician access and medical oversight

altRx: 100% online medical visits with a licensed clinician, ongoing clinician-guided support from a care team, and a licensed provider who reviews the health evaluation and may conduct telehealth via live video or asynchronous review based on state telehealth laws. TMates: emphasizes 24/7 access to U.S.-based doctors and virtual check-in appointments, describing actual physician availability rather than messaging-only support.

Medication formulation and regulatory notes

Both providers supply compounded semaglutide; altRx explicitly markets compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and notes state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies. TMates highlights clinically dosed compounded semaglutide formulated in U.S.-based facilities and notes compounded medication status. Both sources include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Final comparison and recommendation

In a direct altRx vs TMates comparison, the biggest tradeoffs are price versus continuous physician access and a flat-rate dose policy. altRx is built around an affordability claim with semaglutide starting at $89/month and tirzepatide at $149/month plus 100% online visits and free 2-day delivery. TMates charges a higher flat rate of $199/month for semaglutide but highlights 24/7 U.S.-based doctor access and a consistent per-dose price model.

Choose altRx if you prioritize lower starting price, 100% online visits, compound GLP-1 options including tirzepatide at advertised rates, and a membership described as available to residents of all 50 states with state-dependent prescribing. Choose TMates if you prioritize 24/7 access to U.S.-based doctors, a flat $199/month semaglutide price that does not increase with dose, and medications formulated in U.S. facilities.

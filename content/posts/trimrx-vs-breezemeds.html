TrimRx vs BreezeMeds: headline comparison

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: starting price and pricing model, medication formats and availability, shipping and fulfillment details, regulatory and product status, and transparency of fees and commitments. Evidence cited below comes only from the supplied source row for each provider.

1. Starting price and pricing model

TrimRx: Weight-loss plans starting at $149/month for compounded GLP-1 and $249/month for GLP-1+GIP. TrimRx promotes Lifetime Price Protection so you lock in today’s price for life and pay the same price on every dose, and it mentions a $140 promotional discount in captured landing-page text. BreezeMeds: lists semaglutide injection starting at $199/month and tirzepatide injection starting at $299; oral semaglutide and oral tirzepatide are displayed from $399. A separate $169 figure appears in BreezeMeds content but is not clearly tied to a specific product.

2. Medication formats and availability

TrimRx emphasizes compounded GLP-1 injections and compounded GLP-1+GIP injections prepared at state licensed sterile compounding pharmacies. TrimRx also notes providers may write prescriptions for branded medications that the patient fills separately; TrimRx does not sell, dispense, or ship branded medications. BreezeMeds lists injectable and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide options, including both injectable and oral formulations, but the capture notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that compounded offerings, when offered, are not FDA-approved.

3. Shipping and fulfillment

TrimRx advertises free 1–2 day shipping. BreezeMeds advertises discreet home delivery and discreet shipping but captured text does not specify a guaranteed transit time. Both providers mention clinician review and state availability checks in various excerpts.

4. Regulatory notes and product status

TrimRx explicitly states its compounded medication program uses compounded products that are not FDA-approved and that branded prescriptions are filled separately; the regulatory note states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. BreezeMeds capture says medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and notes branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

5. Transparency and commitments

TrimRx claims no monthly membership, cancel anytime, 24/7 support, HSA/FSA eligible, and Lifetime Price Protection as key commitments. BreezeMeds highlights online intake, personalized treatment plans, and discreet shipping, but captured evidence notes that fees and billing commitments were not fully visible and that the $169 offer lacked clear product association. Both require a clinician to determine medical appropriateness.

2) Membership, cancellation, and billing transparency

Both platforms emphasize no long-term lock-in, but the framing differs. Bodybuilding Health+ repeatedly states No Monthly Membership and Cancel Anytime, and advertises no hidden fees. JoinFridays likewise notes no membership fees, cancel anytime, and no long-term contracts, adding that membership is included free in some bundles. 3) Program variety and clinical model

Bodybuilding Health+ is described as a broader telehealth platform: GLP-1 (semaglutide & tirzepatide), NAD+, sermorelin, and sexual performance options. It notes medical oversight by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D. JoinFridays focuses specifically on GLP-1 care with personalized clinician-guided dosing and coaching, offering both compounded and name-brand options in some plans per the source. Both require clinician review to determine medical appropriateness.

4) Promotions, savings, and compounded vs name-brand options

JoinFridays lists active promo codes (NEWYOU12 and NYNY12) and claims aggressive discounts up to specified amounts; it also states both compounded GLP-1s and name-brand options may be available. Bodybuilding Health+ promotes bundle savings on extended plans but the source emphasizes compounded GLP-1s and repeats that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

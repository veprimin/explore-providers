1. Pricing structure and guarantees

Refills advertises personalized compounded GLP-1 from $99/month (~$5/day) with flat pricing the same at every dosage and lists tirzepatide at $159/mo. Eden Health lists semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129 with a lifetime price guarantee and describes one monthly price for its guided GLP-1 program. Both providers state no insurance is required and no membership fees, but Refills emphasizes a flat price at every dose while Eden emphasizes a lifetime price guarantee for its monthly price.

2. Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Refills repeatedly notes that compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality by the FDA and that compounded medications may be prescribed if deemed medically appropriate. Eden Health also notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Both sources preserve regulatory qualifiers rather than claiming FDA approval.

3. Support, concierge and membership terms

Refills highlights free 24/7 health concierge support, no monthly memberships or app fees, and unlimited clinician consultations at no extra cost after enrollment. Eden Health positions its offering as a guided clinician-reviewed program that brings medication, care, and support together under one monthly price and states there are no membership fees. The difference in emphasis is that Refills explicitly promises continuous concierge access while Eden frames care as part of a guided program.

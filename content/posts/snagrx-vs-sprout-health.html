SnagRx vs Sprout Health boils down to a choice between locked, low annual pricing and a fast onramp with an introductory discount. Early verdict: SnagRx is geared toward cost conscious patients who accept a 12 month commitment to lock pricing, while Sprout Health is built for people who want lightning quick approval and a frontloaded $50 discount.

How they differ at a glance

Both providers prescribe compounded GLP 1 medications via telehealth and ship to home. Key differences supported by the source are pricing structure and commitment, approval speed and onboarding friction, outcome messaging or guarantees, and named pharmacy and clinician transparency.

1. Price structure and commitment

SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and compounded tirzepatide from $119/month on the 12 month plan, with the rate locked flat at every dose for the life of treatment. The same medications month to month are $99 and $149. The $69 and $119 figures require the 12 month plan.

Sprout Health offers $50 off the first month, bringing the initial cost to $199, and states ongoing pricing at $249/month after the discounted month. The source explicitly notes the ongoing rate is higher than several competitors.

2. Approval speed and onboarding

Sprout Health markets a lightning quick approval process and positions itself as a fast, low friction entry to GLP 1 therapy. The source says prescription review is rapid and medication ships following approval.

SnagRx states an intake takes about five minutes and an OpenLoop clinician typically reviews it within five hours and always within 24. SnagRx emphasizes next day temperature controlled shipping for approved orders.

3. Outcome framing and guarantees

Sprout Health frames outcomes as 10 to 26 percent body weight reduction over 12 months and highlights that messaging in marketing. The source notes Sprout Health does not publish a formal outcome guarantee.

SnagRx references customer reported outcomes and offers a 6 month weight loss guarantee, subject to terms, while also cautioning results vary and depend on adherence and clinician recommendations. All SnagRx outcome claims are tied to self reported customer data.

4. Pharmacy and clinician transparency

SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and its clinician network, OpenLoop Health, and states clinicians cover 48 states. SnagRx also states LegitScript certification which users can verify independently.

Sprout Health is described as a licensed telehealth GLP 1 platform that prescribes compounded semaglutide after online clinician review. The captured source evidence did not list named pharmacies or clinician network details comparable to SnagRx.

Conclusion: SnagRx vs Sprout Health

SnagRx vs Sprout Health is a trade off between lower, locked-in annual pricing and a fast, discounted entry. Use the primary keyword above to guide your choice but rely on the facts: SnagRx requires a 12 month plan to access $69/month semaglutide and locks price at every dose, names its pharmacies and clinician network, and offers a 6 month guarantee. Sprout Health gives $50 off the first month to $199 and emphasizes lightning quick approval but has a $249/month ongoing rate and no published outcome guarantee in the captured evidence.

Choose SnagRx if you want the lowest annual compounded GLP 1 price with a price lock and named pharmacy and clinician transparency. Choose Sprout Health if you prioritize rapid approval and a significant first month discount to enter therapy quickly.

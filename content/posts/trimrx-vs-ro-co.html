2. Medication type and regulatory status

TrimRx primarily offers compounded GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP injections. The source notes: “compounded medications are not FDA-approved” and are prepared by state licensed compounding pharmacies. TrimRx also may write prescriptions for branded medications, but it does not sell, dispense, or ship branded products. Ro offers the Wegovy pill, described as the first FDA-approved oral GLP-1. Ro highlights that the Wegovy pill is an FDA-approved finished medication while many competitors offer compounded injectables.

3. Formulation and route of administration

TrimRx: Compounded weekly injections (semaglutide and tirzepatide dual agonist options) with injectable dosing. Ro: Oral Wegovy pill option, which is highlighted as an injection-free alternative and important for injection-averse patients. The sources emphasize that Ro is the only mainstream platform here offering the Wegovy pill.

4. Shipping, fulfillment and service features

TrimRx promotes free 1–2 day shipping, telehealth visits and prescription and telemed visits included, 24/7 support, and HSA/FSA eligibility. Ro’s landing evidence for shipping and fulfillment was not captured on the supplied page. Both platforms perform a licensed clinician review before prescribing, but TrimRx explicitly notes compounding fulfillment via state licensed sterile compounding pharmacies.

5. Scale, trust signals and trade offs

TrimRx cites “100k+ happy customers” and a 4.8 Excellent Rating as trust signals and positions itself as competitively priced among compounded programs. Ro positions itself as an established full service telehealth platform with access to FDA-approved branded medication. The trade off presented: TrimRx is more price competitive for compounded injectables, while Ro offers regulatory clarity and an oral, FDA-approved option at higher ongoing cost.

Pallas Health vs JoinSkinny: core difference in one line

Pallas Health presents both FDA-approved brand-name GLP-1s and clinician-personalized compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide from US compounding pharmacies. JoinSkinny focuses on flat-rate GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options with monthly and multi-month bundles and next-day temperature-controlled shipping.

How they differ: 5 distinctive comparison points

1. Medication options and clinician choice

Pallas Health: A US-licensed clinician reviews your history and may select FDA-approved Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound or Mounjaro, or choose a compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared in the USA when a brand medication is not the right fit. The provider materials state Pallas does not manufacture medication; compounding is done by US-licensed compounding pharmacies. Pallas notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

JoinSkinny: Offers GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options. Prescriptions are filled by licensed, U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners. JoinSkinny states its compounds contain the same active molecules used in brand-name medications and that compounded products are not FDA-approved.

2. Pricing structure and starting costs

Pallas Health: Compounded semaglutide is $139 for the first month, then $597 every 12 weeks which is presented as a $199 per month average; compounded tirzepatide is $179 first month then $897 every 12 weeks ($299 per month average). Pallas also lists cash-pay brand prices for Wegovy, Zepbound, Ozempic and Mounjaro. Pallas explicitly says prices are cash-pay only and not billed to insurance.

JoinSkinny: Lists plans starting at $149 and mentions a $140 promotional discount on its landing content. JoinSkinny uses transparent, flat-rate pricing so monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases and offers monthly and 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles plus installment options.

3. Shipping and fulfillment

Pallas Health: Offers free expedited and discreet shipping and states compounded meds are prepared in the USA. Pallas captured landing evidence references free expedited shipping on every plan.

JoinSkinny: Emphasizes free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1-2 days, next-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, and insured shipments with tracking. JoinSkinny details weekend shipping policies and insured replacement exceptions.

4. Care model, access and state rules

Pallas Health: 100 percent online with clinician review and asynchronous care in most states. Pallas notes 43 of 50 states need no video visit but a video visit is required in seven states plus Washington, D.C. Provider care, check-ins and messaging are included in the plan price and there is no membership fee. Care-team hours are weekdays 9 AM to 5 PM ET and Pallas states it offers no emergency services.

JoinSkinny: Describes a quick questionnaire and licensed provider review with ongoing support, dose adjustments and monthly check-ins through telemedicine and 24/7 chat. JoinSkinny says availability depends on state eligibility during signup and that care is provided in eligible U.S. states where regulations allow telehealth prescribing.

5. Cancellation, bundles and renewals

Pallas Health: Compounded plans renew every 12 weeks so cancellation timing matters. Pallas notes plans include provider care with no separate membership fee and that refund in full is possible if a clinician decides treatment is not right for you.

JoinSkinny: States you can cancel anytime before your next billing cycle and that multi-month bundles exist. JoinSkinny points to bundle commitments and total charge requiring confirmation.

Quick verdict: Oak Longevity vs RxActivate

Oak Longevity is the clearer choice for cost-conscious shoppers who want upfront pricing: semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month with no subscriptions or membership fees. How these two providers differ at a glance

1. Pricing transparency and billing

Oak Longevity publishes concrete starting prices: semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month. Oak states one flat price covers all dosages, no subscriptions, and three-month prescriptions are billed upfront. Oak also notes no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped. By contrast, RxActivate does not display price or billing terms before the contact-information gate; pricing is gated behind the questionnaire and submission of name, email and phone number.

2. Eligibility flow and who can enroll

RxActivate requires a two-minute eligibility questionnaire that asks goal, height, weight, target weight, timeline, medication history, sex assigned at birth, date of birth, state and safety screening. The flow then displayed likely eligibility and required contact information before revealing plans or price. Oak uses a short online health questionnaire with asynchronous licensed clinician review and states patients with BMI below 20 don’t qualify for GLP-1 treatment. Oak also notes treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

3. Clinical review, visit format, and medication type

Oak specifies that board-certified physicians in your state review every case and that the clinical review is asynchronous with no video call required. Oak offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide and includes a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. RxActivate emphasizes clinician-matched plans and state and medical-safety screening but also states that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. RxActivate additionally notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Oak advertises free health coaching and free shipping with tracking included, plus same-day approval available for qualifying patients. Oak markets no subscriptions or membership fees and says you are not auto charged and choose to renew payment each month without penalty when billed monthly on a non-recurring basis. RxActivate does not disclose shipping, membership, or cancellation details before the contact-information gate; its captured evidence lists no price, medication fee, membership fee, shipping charge, cancellation term or billing commitment before lead capture.

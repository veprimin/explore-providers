Approach to care: personalization versus structured support

Found builds programs around each patient’s metabolic biology, including hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavior. Willow positions its oral GLP-1 program as a structured weight management journey that emphasizes progress tracking and follow-up support to convert medication effects into sustainable outcomes. Both are telehealth-first but the emphasis differs: Found on individualized biology and holistic whole-body factors; Willow on behavioral scaffolding and digital follow-up.

Medication options and regulatory notes

Found lists medications including tirzepatide and semaglutide and explicitly notes compounded GLP-1s; Found states compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy and are not available in all states. Willow emphasizes oral GLP-1 delivery and notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Both providers state that prescriptions are subject to clinician review or medical appropriateness where supplied.

Pricing and insurance handling

Found advertises affordable monthly plans and getting started for as low as $39, and it states most insurance is now accepted and that it handles insurance paperwork. Found also provides specific program and membership pricing notes and mentions advertised pricing with commitments and Medicare Bridge program details where captured. Willow’s captured evidence shows a 299 starting price but captured landing-page evidence was not readable for additional pricing or shipping details.

Conclusion: Found vs Willow

Found vs Willow comes down to priorities. If you prefer an oral GLP-1 pathway embedded in a structured program with progress tracking and follow-up support, Willow emphasizes that model.

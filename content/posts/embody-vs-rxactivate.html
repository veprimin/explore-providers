Price transparency and starting cost

Embody lists specific starting prices: compounded semaglutide at $69/month, noted as cut from $299, and compounded tirzepatide at $119/month. Embody describes flat locked in monthly pricing, no monthly membership, cancel anytime, and that final pricing may vary based on dose and clinician recommendation. By contrast, RxActivate shows no price or billing terms before the contact information gate. Its starting price is unverified because pricing is gated behind completion of the medical eligibility questionnaire and submission of name, email and phone number.

Eligibility flow and clinician review

Both providers use clinician review, but they present it differently. Embody offers prescription and telemed visits included and states licensed provider review handled entirely online. RxActivate emphasizes a two-minute eligibility questionnaire and clinician-matched GLP-1 plans, with treatment available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. RxActivate requests goal, height, weight, target weight, timeline, medication history, sex assigned at birth, date of birth, state and safety screening before revealing plans.

Medication format and regulatory notes

Embody offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide; the source explicitly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. RxActivate notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable; it also states that when compounded medications are offered, they are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Shipping, membership, and cancellation

Embody advertises free shipping with delivery in 1–2 days after clinician approval, no long-term commitment, and cancel anytime. RxActivate does not disclose shipping, membership, cancellation, billing commitment, or medication fees before the contact-information gate; the flow displayed likely eligibility but required contact info before revealing any plan or price.

Regulatory and safety reminders

Both sources include regulatory notes: Embody states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. RxActivate notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable, and that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Conclusion: Embody vs RxActivate

In the Embody vs RxActivate comparison, the decisive difference is transparency versus gated clinician matching. Embody provides clear starting prices and fast free shipping after approval, while RxActivate prioritizes clinician-matched plans and requires an eligibility flow and contact information before disclosing pricing or plan details.

Choose Embody if you want upfront pricing ($69/month semaglutide; $119/month tirzepatide), free 1–2 day shipping after approval, no membership, and a low-cost, no-frills telehealth option. Choose RxActivate if you prefer a clinician-matched intake process and want eligibility and state screening completed before seeing medication and total cost.

How pricing and billing differ

Pricing is the clearest contrast in BetterMe Rx vs CoreAgeRx. BetterMe Rx lists monthly-equivalent prices that fall when you choose longer 3, 6 or 12-month plans, with compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan. CoreAgeRx emphasizes flat pricing with no price hike: tirzepatide at $129/month and semaglutide at $79/month and the same price every month.

Medication formats and dose availability

BetterMe Rx offers four medication and administration options: injectable semaglutide and tirzepatide and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide, sold as compounded products by partnered U.S. pharmacies. CoreAgeRx lists semaglutide and tirzepatide with dose details on its landing page: any tirzepatide dose up to 7.5 mg and semaglutide up to 1.0 mg. Both providers require a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness.

Shipping, membership and cancellation

BetterMe Rx includes medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies and free 1–2 day shipping and states no membership fee and cancel-anytime language alongside prepaid longer-term plans. CoreAgeRx advertises no membership fees and no billing surprises with messaging that prices do not increase month to month. CoreAgeRx captured details note that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Customer commitments

BetterMe Rx: no membership fee and free 1–2 day delivery are part of the captured page evidence. CoreAgeRx: promises same price every month and no memberships on the captured landing-page evidence.

Regulatory and safety notes

Both providers include regulatory cautions on the captured pages. BetterMe Rx states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. CoreAgeRx notes medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

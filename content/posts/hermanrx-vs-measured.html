HermanRx vs Measured quickly: HermanRx emphasizes all-inclusive monthly pricing and a published ~24h provider review with ~72h delivery, while Measured emphasizes promotional lower starting prices and free two-day shipping. Early verdict: choose HermanRx for predictable all-in pricing and consolidated care; choose Measured for lower starting promotional prices and faster shipping.

How pricing and inclusions compare

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide but with different published price points and inclusions. HermanRx lists compounded semaglutide + B12 at $249/mo and compounded tirzepatide + B12 at $349/mo and describes those prices as all-inclusive covering provider review, prescription, medication, ongoing support and free discreet shipping with no membership or consultation fee. Measured advertises promotional compounded semaglutide from $89/mo and compounded tirzepatide from $189/mo and notes promotional pricing may change.

Speed of clinical review and shipping

HermanRx publishes a roughly 5-minute assessment, ~24h provider review and ~72h delivery when prescribed, and also gives a general FAQ window of 3–7 business days for most orders. Measured promotes same-day prescription decisions and free two-day shipping on every order. If fast shipping is a priority, Measured highlights free two-day shipping; HermanRx publishes a fast provider review then delivery in about 72 hours.

Support, care model and platform breadth

HermanRx is a telehealth platform matching patients with independent, licensed, board-certified physicians across five categories including weight loss, hormones, longevity, hair loss and sexual health, and advertises 24/7 patient support and a secure patient portal. Measured pairs compounded GLP-1s with unlimited provider messaging, dietitian support and personalized diet and exercise support and emphasizes dietitian involvement and 24/7 care team access. Both say treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Regulatory and outcome claims

Both providers state compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HermanRx explicitly states compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved as finished products. Measured includes internal outcome claims based on members reporting weight data at 6 months, noting average loss was 31 lbs and that individual results vary and are not guaranteed; Measured also notes internal outcome claims are not independent evidence.

How to decide: choose based on these facts

Choose HermanRx if you want a single, legible monthly bill that the provider describes as all-inclusive and you value a platform that consolidates weight loss with other services and publishes a specific provider review and delivery timeline. Choose Measured if you prioritize promotional lower starting prices and the guarantee of free two-day shipping and same-day prescription decisions, while noting promotional pricing may change.

HermanRx vs Measured, final note: both sell compounded GLP-1s with clinician discretion required and both state compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Confirm eligibility, current pricing and state availability before enrolling.

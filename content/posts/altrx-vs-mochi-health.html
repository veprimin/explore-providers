altRx vs Mochi Health: how they differ at a glance

This comparison of altRx vs Mochi Health focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by source material: starting price and pricing structure, consultation style, membership and pricing transparency, shipping and extras, and promotional offers. Both providers use compounded medications and both operate across the U.S., but they take different approaches to access, cost, and the clinical visit model.

1. Starting price and pricing model

altRx advertises semaglutide starting at 89 per month and tirzepatide from 149 per month as a limited offer. altRx emphasizes a flat, all inclusive monthly fee that covers medical oversight, medication, supplies, and 24/7 support with no membership, insurance, or hidden fees. Mochi Health lists membership plus medication pricing starting from 99 per month, and notes new lower prices where compounded semaglutide is presented as starting at 99 with promotional first-month pricing down to 60. Mochi explicitly describes a membership-plus-medication structure, so ongoing monthly cost varies by plan and medication.

2. Consultation style and clinical experience

altRx promotes 100 percent online medical visits that can include a telehealth consultation reviewed by a licensed provider. Mochi Health highlights live video visits with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes, contrasting that with the asynchronous questionnaire review used by many competitors. Mochi positions its synchronous video consult as a core differentiator that enables a real time conversation where the doctor answers questions and explains the treatment plan directly.

3. Membership, transparency, and payment options

altRx emphasizes no membership and a transparent flat monthly price with buy now pay later options through Affirm, Klarna, and Afterpay. Mochi requires membership plus medication, which makes total monthly cost dependent on the selected plan and medications. Mochi also markets a promotional 50 percent off the first month with code BESTWLHALFOFF and a first month promotional rate that can be as low as 39 for certain new-customer offers, with standard rate afterward noted as 79 per month in one cited term.

4. Shipping, delivery, and included supplies

altRx states free and discreet 2-day delivery and that the monthly plan includes medication, sterile syringes, alcohol prep pads, and step by step guidance. Mochi states prescriptions are shipped to your door and advertises ongoing appointment availability and 24/7 support but does not advertise a guaranteed 2-day delivery window in the supplied evidence.

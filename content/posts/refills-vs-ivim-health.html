Key differences at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: pricing structure and ongoing cost; program protocol and published claims; third-party ratings and trust signals; membership, fees, and extras; and shipping and clinician access. All medication options referenced are compounded and not FDA-approved, per each provider’s disclosures.

Pricing structure and ongoing cost

Refills advertises personalized compounded GLP-1 from $99/month (~$5/day) with flat pricing that the company states is the same at every dosage. IVIM Health lists semaglutide at $75/month on some landing content and shows tirzepatide at $133/month plus a $74.99/month program fee, while IVIM also highlights a free month membership code GIFT75 and a no-obligation free consultation valued at $75. IVIM’s FAQ notes an ongoing rate example of $174/month after initial offers.

Program design and clinical claims

Refills presents a simple, flat-price subscription model with clinician review of intake and ongoing access to clinicians at no extra cost; prescriptions are processed and shipped monthly, and clinicians come from partner telehealth networks. IVIM Health promotes a proprietary GLP-1 ID protocol that the company says can accelerate weight loss outcomes by up to 40% compared to a cited clinical GLP-1 trial; that 40% figure is IVIM’s protocol claim and not an independent head-to-head trial.

Third-party ratings and trust signals

IVIM Health emphasizes a 4.9-star rating on both Google and Trustpilot as an unusually strong satisfaction signal. Refills emphasizes Legitscript-certified U.S. compounding pharmacies with third-party USP testing and zero membership or app fees as trust and convenience signals. Both providers note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Membership, extras, and shipping

Refills states free discreet shipping, express shipping options, no membership or app fees, month-to-month plans, and unlimited access to the patient care team and clinicians at no extra cost. IVIM offers promotional options such as a free month with code GIFT75 and a stated free consultation ($75 value) for new patients, but also lists an ongoing program fee and membership structure in its pricing details.

Clinician access and prescription process

Refills describes a 3-minute intake quiz followed by licensed clinician review; depending on needs, patients may chat with a doctor or receive a prescription within 24 hours, then receive shipping and tracking within 2 business days. IVIM requires a consultation with an IVIM clinician before prescribing compounded medications and highlights weekly dose adjustments within its program; both state prescriptions are required and that compounded medications are prepared by independent compounding pharmacies.

How HealthRx and RxActivate handle pricing and gating

HealthRx lists explicit pricing: from $99 per month on a 12 month prepaid semaglutide plan with a total of $1,163 today and $1,188 at renewal, and tirzepatide plans starting at $179 per month. HealthRx notes the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan. By contrast, RxActivate hides pricing behind a two minute eligibility questionnaire and requires first name, last name, email and phone number before revealing plans, billing terms or fees.

What that means for shoppers

Both providers state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. If knowing the monthly cost and renewal terms before enrollment matters, HealthRx provides that information up front. If you are comfortable completing an eligibility flow that reveals price only after contact capture, RxActivate uses that gated approach.

Three key differences: pricing transparency, medication form, and shipping

This section compares the most distinctive differences between HealthRx and RxActivate using only supplied facts.

1. Pricing transparency

HealthRx publishes specific plan prices and renewal totals for its prepaid semaglutide plan and starting prices for tirzepatide. RxActivate does not display price or billing terms before the contact information gate. The difference is clear: HealthRx reveals pricing and renewal numbers; RxActivate requires the eligibility flow to proceed before price disclosure.

2. Medication format and regulatory notes

HealthRx offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription and explicitly states compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and is not FDA approved. RxActivate notes that medication specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Both emphasize clinician review and medical appropriateness.

3. Shipping and logistics

HealthRx specifies overnight cold shipping with tracked cold chain and free shipping on every plan. RxActivate captured evidence did not display shipping, membership, cancellation or billing terms before the contact gate, so shipping details are not supplied.

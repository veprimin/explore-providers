Quick verdict: JoinFridays and Mochi Health both offer compounded GLP-1 programs, but JoinFridays focuses on low starting pricing and cancel-anytime plans while Mochi Health emphasizes live video visits and a membership-plus-medication model. Choose based on whether you value flexible month-to-month pricing or synchronous video access to a doctor.

How they differ: visit style and clinical interaction

JoinFridays uses a licensed clinician review and provides personalized clinician-guided care, described as clinician-guided rather than templated protocols. Mochi Health offers live video visits with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes, a synchronous model that the source highlights as different from the asynchronous questionnaire reviews used by most competitors. Both platforms require clinician evaluation before prescriptions are issued.

Pricing structure and promotional offers

JoinFridays: starting at $117/month and lists specific one month and multi-month prices. The source states no membership fees, cancel anytime, and active promo codes such as NEWYOU12 and NYNY12 with specified maximums. Mochi Health: membership-plus-medication pricing starting from $99/month, and a promotion of 50% off your first month with code BESTWLHALFOFF; the source notes the $39 promotional first month rate for some offers and that standard rate is $79 after that promotional first month in certain terms.

Medication types and compounded status

Both providers emphasize compounded GLP-1 medications. JoinFridays offers compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide options and notes availability of name brand injections as separate pricing items; the source explicitly states that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Mochi Health lists compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide options with starting prices and also lists various oral and injected compounds; the source includes the same regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA approved.

Membership, cancellation, and ongoing support

JoinFridays highlights no long term contracts, no membership fees, and cancel anytime. Mochi Health operates as membership plus medication, with ongoing appointments, 24/7 support, and prepaid multi month plans available; the source cautions that total monthly cost varies by plan and medication and that terms vary by state.

Bottom line: JoinFridays vs Mochi Health

In the JoinFridays vs Mochi Health comparison, JoinFridays stands out for its low advertised starting price, no membership fee model, and cancel anytime policy. Mochi Health stands out for offering live video doctor visits in about 5 minutes and a membership structure with 24/7 support and ongoing appointments. Both offer compounded GLP-1 options and both include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA approved.

Choose one if…

Choose JoinFridays if you want a low starting price, no membership fees, and the ability to cancel anytime while accessing compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide.

Choose Mochi Health if you want a synchronous live video visit with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes, ongoing appointment availability, and a membership plus medication model with promotional first month pricing.

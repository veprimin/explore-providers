Comparing Trimi vs Start Willow? Quick verdict: Trimi emphasizes lower flat pricing and free overnight shipping with a 6-month money-back promise, while Start Willow focuses on a no-insurance, no-membership direct-pay model and faster 3-day delivery for its compounded semaglutide.

Key differences at a glance

This Trimi vs Start Willow comparison focuses on five distinctive differences supported by the source: price and pricing model, shipping speed and timeline, membership and insurance requirements, clinical and pharmacy oversight, and refund or pricing commitments.

1. Price and pricing model

Trimi: Compounded semaglutide from $99/mo on the annual plan and compounded tirzepatide from $125/month. Trimi advertises flat pricing that never rises as your dose increases and states “same price at every dose.”

Start Willow: From $249 per Month for semaglutide with a first-month price of $249 and an ongoing $299/month rate disclosed in the FAQ. Start Willow’s materials note the $299 ongoing rate is on the higher end of the list.

2. Shipping speed and delivery commitments

Trimi: Offers free overnight, temperature controlled shipping. Start Willow: Guarantees delivery in 3 days or less and notes same day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping in some in-stock cases. These are distinct shipping promises that may affect how quickly you receive medication.

3. Membership, insurance, and payment structure

Trimi: Describes flat membership coverage that includes medication, clinician visits, ongoing check ins, and free shipping as part of a plan. Start Willow: Explicitly markets no insurance needed and no membership fees, operating on a direct-pay model to bypass insurance prior authorization.

Visit Start Willow to view purchasing options

4. Clinical oversight and pharmacy sourcing

Trimi: States medications are prescribed by US-licensed clinicians through Arora Health, led by Medical Director Dr. Sean Arora, MD. Trimi also names state licensed 503A compounding pharmacies including VialsRx and GreenwichRx and says a certificate of analysis is available on request.

Start Willow: Describes an online clinician review that prescribes compounded semaglutide as part of a personalized treatment plan and notes compounding pharmacies operate under state board of pharmacy oversight and USP compounding standards.

5. Refunds, guarantees, and regulatory notes

Trimi: Offers a 6-month money-back promise where you may request a refund if you do not lose weight after following the plan for 6 months, with terms that apply. Both providers include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Bottom line: Trimi vs Start Willow

Trimi vs Start Willow boils down to price and delivery model. Trimi provides lower advertised monthly pricing, flat dosing pricing, free overnight shipping, named compounding pharmacies, clinician network oversight, and a 6-month money-back promise. Start Willow offers a no-insurance, no-membership direct-pay model with a 3-day delivery guarantee and a disclosed ongoing monthly rate that rises after the first month.

Choose Trimi if you prioritize lower flat pricing per dose, free overnight shipping, named compounding pharmacies, and a stated 6-month money-back promise. Choose Start Willow if you need a direct-pay, no-insurance option with a fast 3-day delivery guarantee and prefer the convenience of no membership fees.

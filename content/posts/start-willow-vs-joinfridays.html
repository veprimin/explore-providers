Pricing and long term cost

Start Willow advertises a direct-pay semaglutide program from $249 for the first month and $299/month ongoing, with the source noting the $299 ongoing rate is on the higher end of similar platforms. JoinFridays lists a lowest entry price starting at $117/month with promo codes and multi-month plans that reduce the effective monthly rate to about $150 over 12 months. For patients focused on initial low cost or short trials, JoinFridays’ $117 entry is the clearest advantage. For patients prioritizing a fast, insurance-free transaction and are willing to pay more monthly, Start Willow is presented as a higher-priced but faster option.

Medication options and clinical framing

Start Willow centers on a compounded semaglutide injection prescribed after clinician review and described as a customized option for patients needing tailored GLP-1 therapy. The provider emphasizes semaglutide as a GLP-1 receptor agonist and explains its role in satiety and slowed gastric emptying. JoinFridays explicitly offers both compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide on one platform and markets tirzepatide as the most effective option and semaglutide as the most affordable. JoinFridays also lists name brand options and insurance support for brand-name products when a clinician determines they are appropriate.

Compounded status and safety language

Both sources include the regulatory caution that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Start Willow specifically notes compounded semaglutide is prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies and must meet USP compounding standards. JoinFridays repeats that compounded medications are not FDA-approved when offered.

Membership, contracts, and flexibility

Start Willow operates on a direct-pay model with no membership fees and no insurance required, positioning itself as an alternative for patients who have faced insurance pre-authorization denials. JoinFridays offers no membership fees as well and highlights cancel-anytime terms and the option of multi-month plans that lower the average monthly cost. If flexibility and the ability to cancel without long-term commitment matter, JoinFridays is presented as the patient-friendly choice.

Delivery and support

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less from its licensed pharmacy network and promotes same-day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping in-stock. The supplied JoinFridays evidence focuses more on pricing tiers, promo codes, and plan flexibility; specific delivery speed promises are not supplied in the evidence provided.

How to choose: clear guidance

Start Willow vs JoinFridays focuses on different priorities: Start Willow prioritizes fast delivery and a simple direct-pay route to compounded semaglutide, while JoinFridays emphasizes lower starting prices, both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide on one platform, active promo codes, and cancel-anytime flexibility.

Choose Start Willow if you prioritize guaranteed 3-day delivery, same-day prescriptions, and a direct-pay, no-insurance model for compounded semaglutide.

Choose JoinFridays if you want the lowest starting price, access to both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide, aggressive promo codes, and the ability to cancel anytime.

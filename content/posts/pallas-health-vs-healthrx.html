How Pallas Health vs HealthRx differ on price and billing

Pallas and HealthRx both present lower-cost compounded options but their pricing models differ. Pallas lists compounded semaglutide at $139 for the first month then $597 every 12 weeks, a $199/month average, and compounded tirzepatide at $179 first month then $897 every 12 weeks, a $299/month average. Pallas also publishes cash-pay brand-name prices for Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound and Mounjaro.

HealthRx advertises a lower entry point: from $99/month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month. HealthRx notes the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan. Both providers state they do not bill insurance.

Medication choices and clinician role

Both providers emphasize clinician review as the gatekeeper. Pallas offers both FDA-approved brand-name GLP-1s (Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound, Mounjaro) and clinician-personalized compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide when a brand medication is not the right fit. HealthRx stresses that you do not pick a medication from a menu; a clinician decides whether semaglutide, tirzepatide or neither is appropriate. Both state compounded medication is prepared per prescription.

Shipping, fulfillment and pharmacy details

HealthRx advertises overnight cold chain shipping that is tracked and free on every plan. Pallas promises free expedited and discreet shipping and says compounded medications are prepared by US-licensed compounding pharmacies. HealthRx states compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom. Both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared to prescription rather than being FDA-reviewed products.

Service model, accessibility and limitations

Pallas is 100 percent online with asynchronous intake in most states, noting a video visit is required in seven states plus Washington, D.C., and that 43 of 50 states need no video visit. Pallas includes provider care, check-ins and messaging in the plan price with no membership fee and lists care-team hours as weekdays 9 AM to 5 PM ET. HealthRx emphasizes clinician review and recommends confirming eligibility, state availability and renewal terms before enrollment. Both state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Price and pricing model compared

Omzo lists semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279, described as personalized compounded options. Embody lists compounded semaglutide at $69/month, reduced from $299, and compounded tirzepatide at $119/month with flat monthly pricing. Embody highlights no long-term membership and a cancel anytime policy. Final pricing for both may vary based on clinician recommendation and dose.

Medication type and regulatory notes

Both platforms offer compounded GLP-1 medications. Omzo describes a Compounded Medication Program where compounded drugs are prepared by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy and explicitly states these compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. Embody also states its compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products but use the same active ingredients as branded options. Omzo notes providers may instead prescribe branded medications, in which case the patient fills them at a pharmacy and assumes costs.

Clinical oversight and prescribing differences

Omzo positions its service as clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed U.S. providers and notes clinical services are provided by third party networks of U.S.-licensed clinicians who determine eligibility and make final prescribing decisions. Embody describes doctor-prescribed GLP-1 treatment with prescription and telemed visits included and licensed provider review handled online. Both emphasize licensed provider review, but Omzo frames its plans as clinician-guided with provider networks while Embody emphasizes a straightforward, prescription-forward model with included telemed visits.

What each platform is best for

Omzo is presented as a clinician-guided platform that connects patients with licensed U.S. providers, offers both compounded medication programs and the possibility of prescriptions for branded medications, and discloses that compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. Embody is presented as a low-cost, prescription-forward telehealth option with $69/month semaglutide pricing, free 1-2 day shipping, no membership commitment, and simpler, flat monthly pricing.

Choose Omzo if…

You want clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed U.S. providers, access to both compounded programs and the option that a provider may prescribe a branded medication you fill at a pharmacy, and you accept that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Choose Embody if…

How Omzo vs JoinSkinny differ at a glance

The comparison between Omzo and JoinSkinny centers on 1) medication type and compounding status, 2) pricing structure and bundles, 3) shipping and fulfillment, and 4) clinical support and monitoring. Both deliver care through licensed U.S. providers and require provider assessment for prescriptions, but they present those features differently.

1. Medication options and regulatory status

Omzo: Omzo connects patients with licensed medical providers who may prescribe compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide as part of a Compounded Medication Program. The source states these compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Omzo also notes providers may write prescriptions for branded FDA-approved medications, but Omzo does not sell or ship branded products.

JoinSkinny: JoinSkinny provides GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options and works with licensed U.S. pharmacies that prepare authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1 + GIP when prescribed. The source also notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved. JoinSkinny states prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners.

2. Pricing structure and promotional offers

Omzo: Pricing information captured lists semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279. Omzo emphasizes clinician-guided personalized care; specific membership, bundle, or promotional details were not supplied.

JoinSkinny: Plans start at $149 and the source reference includes a $140 promotional discount and multi-month bundles. JoinSkinny highlights flat-rate dosing where the monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases, plus options for monthly or 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles and installment payments via Afterpay, Klarna, and Affirm.

3. Shipping, turnaround and logistics

Omzo: The site mentions fast, discreet delivery and that compounded medications are prepared and shipped by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy. Omzo notes medication costs are included in fees collected by Omzo and that shipping and rates may vary based on pharmacy capacity and state regulations.

JoinSkinny: JoinSkinny advertises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1-2 days and same-day shipment for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, with next-day UPS delivery and tracking. Weekend shipments may be handled the following Monday. JoinSkinny also states each shipment is insured for lost prescriptions except when incorrect shipping information is provided by the patient.

4. Clinical support and ongoing care

Omzo: Clinical services are provided by third party networks of U.S.-licensed clinicians who determine eligibility for GLP-1 treatment based on medical history and provider assessment. The site stresses providers make the final determination on prescriptions and that the Omzo assessment does not create a doctor-patient relationship.

JoinSkinny: JoinSkinny describes a fully online service with a quick health questionnaire followed by licensed provider review, personalized treatment plans, ongoing support, dose adjustments, and monthly check-ins through telemedicine plus 24/7 chat. JoinSkinny states care is delivered by licensed U.S. providers and protected under HIPAA.

Side effects, dosing, and usage differences

Strut Health provides specific patient guidance captured on the landing page: oral Semaglutide lozenges are likely used once daily as a sublingual lozenge, and injections are typically once weekly if used. Strut lists common gastrointestinal and other side effects such as burping, bloating, nausea, vomiting, and low blood sugar in people with diabetes, and recommends discussing existing conditions like thyroid cancer, pancreatitis, kidney disease, and diabetic eye problems with your doctor. Sprout Health frames expected weight outcomes but does not supply detailed dosing instructions or a side effect list in the captured evidence.

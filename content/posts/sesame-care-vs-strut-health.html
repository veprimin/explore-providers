Quick comparison and verdict: Sesame Care vs Strut Health

Sesame Care vs Strut Health pits a clinician-choice telehealth marketplace against an oral-focused telehealth provider. Early verdict: Choose Sesame Care if you want broad clinician selection and flexible scheduling with plans starting at $59/mo*; choose Strut Health if you specifically want an oral semaglutide pathway and guided consults starting from $199 per month.

How they differ at a glance

The most distinctive differences supported by the source are: clinician choice and marketplace model, oral specialization and compounded medication options, pricing structure and insurance acceptance, treatment formats and medication types, and network size and scheduling flexibility. The sections below compare these features directly.

1. Clinician choice and platform model

Sesame Care: A telehealth marketplace lets patients browse and select clinicians by specialty, availability, and pricing, giving more control over the care relationship. The source highlights a large clinician network and nationwide access in many states. Strut Health: A smaller, specialized clinical team focused on oral medication management rather than a broad marketplace. The source notes a smaller network than larger telehealth marketplaces.

2. Oral specialization and compounded medication policies

Strut Health emphasizes oral-based program options, including oral semaglutide lozenges and compounded semaglutide prescribed based on medical necessity. The source states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by U.S.-licensed compounding pharmacies pursuant to individualized prescriptions. Sesame Care offers oral GLP-1 options among other medications but positions itself as a marketplace rather than an oral specialist.

3. Pricing structure, insurance, and subscription details

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs, and messaging for as low as $59/mo* based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Sesame notes cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo and that medication costs vary by insurance and pharmacy; it also states insurance is accepted for weight loss medications. Strut Health markets oral GLP-1 from $199 per month and highlights no hidden fees, free shipping, and cancel anytime language for some options. Strut also advertises a $99 oral semaglutide price point on its captured landing evidence and states compounded semaglutide is prescribed based on medical necessity.

4. Treatment formats, medication types, and regulatory notes

Sesame Care lists branded options like semaglutide and tirzepatide with price points and mentions FDA-approved branded products such as the Wegovy pill and the Zepbound KwikPen with specific strengths and monthly pricing ranges; it includes a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Strut Health focuses on oral semaglutide lozenges and notes that compounded semaglutide is not FDA-approved, with storage and safety guidance captured on its page.

5. Network size, scheduling, and patient experience

Sesame Care highlights a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states, and lists pros like easy booking and broad treatment coverage while cautioning that experience can vary by clinician. Strut Health offers guided consultations, ongoing progress communication, and a streamlined, no-frills approach tailored to oral treatment seekers.

Final thoughts on Sesame Care vs Strut Health

Sesame Care is built as a broad telehealth marketplace that emphasizes clinician choice, flexible scheduling, and multiple medication options with subscription care starting at $59/mo* and cash-pay GLP-1 medications from $149/mo. Strut Health is a streamlined option for people seeking oral semaglutide pathways and guided consults, offering oral specialization with compounded semaglutide available when medically necessary and program pricing from $199 per month.

Choose Sesame Care if you want broad telehealth choice, a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and options to use insurance for weight loss medications. Choose Strut Health if you specifically want an oral-focused plan, guided consultations, and access to compounded semaglutide when prescribed based on medical necessity.

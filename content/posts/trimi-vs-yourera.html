What is included and how care is delivered

Both are doctor-guided telehealth services that prescribe compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide shipped to your door. Shipping, speed, and delivery details

Shipping claims differ in speed and framing. Trimi advertises free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping. YourEra advertises free 1 to 2 day cold shipping from licensed US pharmacies with discreet delivery. Both state shipments come from licensed compounding pharmacies; YourEra specifically calls out free 1 to 2 day shipping while Trimi emphasizes overnight, temperature-controlled delivery.

Safety, regulatory notes, and refunds

Both providers dispense compounded medications and include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Trimi offers a 6-month money-back promise: follow your plan for 6 months and you can request a refund if you do not lose weight, with terms applying. YourEra notes prescriptions are issued only if a licensed clinician determines treatment is appropriate after evaluation and that compounded products are not FDA-approved.

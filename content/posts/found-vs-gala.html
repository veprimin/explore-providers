Found vs Gala: primary differences at a glance

This comparison highlights the most distinctive differences: care approach, pricing and insurance handling, medication type and regulatory notes, membership and billing model, and shipping and logistics.

1. Care approach and program focus

Found positions itself as a biology-first, personalized medically supervised weight loss program that builds plans around metabolic biology, hormones, blood sugar, sleep and behavior. Gala positions itself as a direct-pay telehealth platform offering compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with provider check-ins and home delivery. Both involve provider review; Found emphasizes a broader personalized program while Gala emphasizes direct-pay medication delivery and included provider visit and prescription.

2. Pricing, insurance and membership model

Found advertises getting started for as low as $39 and notes most insurance is now accepted. Found states insurance coverage, pricing and terms vary and medication coverage and copays vary based on eligibility and insurance approval. Gala advertises a direct-pay model with a flat all-inclusive price covering provider visit, medication, shipping and ongoing care, saying no insurance is needed and claiming one flat price at every dose. Gala also promotes a $220 discount and free two-day shipping. Gala’s base dollar price was not visible in readable page text.

3. Medications offered and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide as part of their services. Found mentions branded GLP-1s and tirzepatide and includes regulatory qualifiers: certain medications are FDA-approved for conditions other than weight and compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy and are not available in all states. Gala’s pages repeat that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

4. Billing flexibility, cancellation and logistics

Found notes affordable monthly plans, cancel anytime language, and highlights handling insurance paperwork and Medicare Bridge program references with specific qualifiers. Gala emphasizes no monthly membership, cancel anytime, same price every month claimed, and free two-day shipping straight to your door. Gala explicitly says provider visit and prescription are included in the direct-pay price.

How Sesame Care vs Enhance MD differ at a glance

The primary keyword Sesame Care vs Enhance MD frames three distinctive differences supported by the source: pricing structure and introductory rates, clinician choice and marketplace model, and medication sourcing and insurance handling. Below we compare those areas directly using only supplied facts.

Distinctive difference 1: Pricing approach and introductory costs

Sesame Care advertises ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo* with cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo; medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary by insurance status and pharmacy. Enhance MD offers semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month, then applies flat-rate pricing that stays the same at every dose. Enhance MD highlights the $49/$99 first-month offers as introductory; ongoing flat-rate pricing is higher.

Distinctive difference 2: Clinician selection and care model

Sesame Care is described as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select clinicians by specialty, availability, and pricing rather than assigning clinicians via an algorithmic queue. That gives patients choice in who provides care. Enhance MD is presented as a licensed telehealth platform with clinician-directed review but the evidence emphasizes pricing features and clinical volume (10,000+ patients treated) rather than a marketplace model.

Distinctive difference 3: Medication options, sourcing, and regulatory notes

Both platforms offer semaglutide and tirzepatide options in some form. Sesame Care lists branded and formulary details including semaglutide and tirzepatide pricing examples and notes Wegovy pill and Zepbound KwikPen as branded FDA-approved medications when applicable; it also states medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Enhance MD emphasizes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide availability, noting compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Distinctive difference 4: Insurance and billing

Sesame Care accepts insurance for weight loss medications and lists prices with insurance as low as $25 for some medications, while noting medication costs will vary based on insurance status and pharmacy. Enhance MD operates on a direct-pay model and does not bill insurance per the supplied evidence.

Pricing and product type: oral compounded semaglutide versus broader GLP-1 offerings

SkinnyRx markets compounded semaglutide tablets designed for daily use and positions FSA/HSA eligibility as a key benefit. The site lists benefits such as clinically supported ingredients and notes that compounded semaglutide has not been approved by the FDA for weight loss, with all relevant regulatory language preserved.

JoinSkinny highlights GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options, including injectable and oral treatments, with flat-rate pricing and multi-month bundles. JoinSkinny states branded prescriptions are filled and paid for separately when applicable and emphasizes that prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners.

Shipping, fulfillment, and quality checks

Shipping is a frequent deciding factor. SkinnyRx advertises free overnight shipping on all orders and that after a prescription is written you receive shipping and tracking information within 2 business days. SkinnyRx also notes partner pharmacies perform third-party testing every 3 to 6 months confirming concentration within plus or minus 10 percent for compounded lots.

JoinSkinny promises free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1–2 days, same-day shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time, and insured shipments. JoinSkinny also notes pharmacy partners are FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified.

Subscription, pricing transparency, and cancellation terms

SkinnyRx is presented as a subscription service that can be canceled at any time, with a refund policy allowing a full refund any time before a licensed provider completes the prescription review and a non-refundable status after fulfillment begins. SkinnyRx mentions split payments via Affirm Buy Now Pay Later as an option.

JoinSkinny uses transparent flat-rate pricing that stays the same regardless of dosage increases, and offers monthly and multi-month bundles plus installment options. JoinSkinny states you can cancel anytime before your next billing cycle and that bundle commitments and total charge require confirmation.

Support, monitoring, and clinical oversight

Both providers require clinician review. SkinnyRx emphasizes personalized care, ongoing support, dose adjustments, and unlimited access to the patient care team after subscription. JoinSkinny describes clinician review, monthly check-ins, 24/7 chat, and dose adjustments guided by a provider.

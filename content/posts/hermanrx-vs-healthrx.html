How HermanRx and HealthRx structure pricing and plans

HermanRx lists specific all-inclusive monthly prices: compounded semaglutide + B12 at $249/month and compounded tirzepatide + B12 at $349/month. Those prices are presented as covering the provider review, prescription, medication, ongoing support and free discreet shipping with no separate membership or consultation fee. HealthRx shows a lower starting monthly number: from $99/month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal). HealthRx notes tirzepatide plans start at $179/month and that the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan.

Key difference 1: All-inclusive monthly billing versus prepaid plan pricing

HermanRx emphasizes genuinely all-inclusive monthly pricing with no membership fee and no separate consultation charge. HealthRx emphasizes a lower per-month number when paid as a 12-month prepaid plan and cautions that the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan and that eligibility, renewal terms and state availability should be confirmed before enrollment. Both vendors are cash-pay and state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Key difference 2: Medication selection and clinician decision process

Both offer semaglutide and tirzepatide as compounded options, but the descriptions differ in workflow. HermanRx lists both molecules with set monthly prices and states U.S. licensed, board-certified physicians review every intake with about a 5-minute assessment, roughly 24 hour provider review and about 72 hour delivery if prescribed. HealthRx emphasizes that the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is the right fit or whether neither is, and that you are choosing the plan it arrives on rather than picking a medication from a menu.

Key difference 3: Shipping and fulfillment timing

HermanRx advertises free discreet shipping to all 50 states, packaging in plain unmarked packaging, and a published timeline of about 24 hour provider review and roughly 72 hour delivery in some descriptions, with a wider 3 to 7 business day window also noted depending on treatment type and processing. HealthRx highlights overnight cold shipping on every plan with cold chain, tracked delivery and free shipping noted on landing content. Both companies state compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared per prescription.

Key difference 4: Regulatory and pharmacy notes

Both providers explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HermanRx states it offers both FDA-approved and compounded medications depending on the provider’s recommendation and explicitly notes that compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved as finished products. HealthRx states compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and reiterates that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Conclusion: Choose based on billing, shipping and plan terms

HermanRx vs HealthRx centers on clear tradeoffs. HermanRx provides specific all-inclusive monthly prices with no membership or consultation fee, published review and delivery timelines and multi-category services on one platform. HealthRx advertises a lower entry price when you commit to a prepaid plan, emphasizes overnight cold shipping and clinician-led medication selection, and warns that the lowest per-month price requires longer prepaid terms.

Choose HermanRx if you want a single legible monthly bill with no membership or per-visit fees and value a published quick provider review timeline and free discreet shipping. Choose HealthRx if you prioritize a lower advertised monthly entry price tied to a 12-month prepaid plan and overnight cold chain shipping, after confirming eligibility, renewal terms and state availability.

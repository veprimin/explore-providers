Pricing structure and what is included

Sesame Care separates subscription care from medication costs. The platform advertises ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo* and cash-pay GLP-1 medication examples such as semaglutide from $149/mo and tirzepatide from $299/mo, noting medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on insurance status and pharmacy. Direct Meds advertises treatment starting at $147 with medication, clinician review, telehealth visits, supplies and free next-day shipping included, while its FAQ lists standard prices of $297/mo for semaglutide and $399/mo for tirzepatide.

Medication format and regulatory considerations

Sesame Care lists branded oral and injectable GLP-1 options and provides price examples for semaglutide and tirzepatide, and it advises that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Direct Meds offers compounded injectable and sublingual semaglutide and tirzepatide through U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies and clearly states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Treatment on Direct Meds is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Clinician access, scheduling and support

Sesame Care positions itself as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select providers based on specialty, availability and pricing, offering a large clinician network and flexible scheduling. Direct Meds emphasizes clinician review, ongoing clinician and nursing support, and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Sesame Care notes that experience can vary by clinician. Direct Meds notes no separate monthly membership fee and month-to-month cancellation for certain plans, while subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term.

Shipping, availability and cancellation terms

Direct Meds includes free temperature-controlled next-day UPS or FedEx shipping as part of its offering and states it is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. It advertises month-to-month cancellation for customers and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Sesame Care notes medication costs will vary by insurance and pharmacy and that insurance is accepted for weight loss medications; specific shipping or membership details beyond subscription and medication cost separation are not supplied in the captured evidence.

Final comparison and who each is best for

Sesame Care vs Directmeds boils down to choice of clinician and branded medication pathways versus bundled compounded medication delivery. Use Sesame Care if you want broad telehealth choice, the ability to browse and choose your clinician, flexible appointments and options for insurance coverage on branded medications. Use Direct Meds if you prioritize a bundled price that includes medication, visits, supplies and free next-day shipping and are aware that the offered medications are compounded and not FDA-approved.

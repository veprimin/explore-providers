How these providers differ at a glance

BetterMe Rx and IVIM Health share a clinician‑led telehealth model for compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, but they differ in formats offered, pricing structure, program features, and trust signals. Below we compare the most distinctive supported differences: formats and supplies, pricing and billing terms, program structure and claims, third party validation, and shipping and included services.

1. Formats and medication options

BetterMe Rx offers four medication and administration options: injectable semaglutide, injectable tirzepatide, oral semaglutide, and oral tirzepatide. IVIM Health offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and emphasizes a clinician‑guided GLP 1 ID protocol. Both providers state that compounded medications are not FDA approved and prescriptions are subject to a licensed clinician review.

2. Pricing structure and billing commitments

BetterMe Rx lists monthly equivalents that fall with longer prepaid plans. The captured pricing notes: compounded GLP 1 treatment from $145 per month on a 12 month plan. Specific figures include semaglutide injection $205 monthly or as low as $145 per month on a 12 month plan; tirzepatide injection $275 monthly or as low as $215 per month; oral semaglutide $250 monthly or as low as $185 per month; oral tirzepatide $265 monthly or as low as $215 per month. BetterMe Rx also states no membership fee, no insurance required, and cancel anytime language alongside prepaid longer term plans.

IVIM Health presents an introductory pricing path: a $75 per month listing and a published first month at $99 with ongoing pricing cited at $174 per month in some sources, plus a separate program fee noted as $74.99 per month in the captured details. IVIM promotes a no obligation free consultation valued at $75 for new patients and a free month membership via code GIFT75 that reduces upfront cost.

3. Program claims and outcomes

IVIM Health highlights a proprietary GLP 1 ID protocol and cites internal study results that the protocol yielded an average of 46 percent more weight loss over 68 weeks compared to another published clinical GLP 1 trial. The source notes this is not a head to head study and clarifies the 46 percent claim is IVIM’s published finding, not an independent clinical trial. IVIM also claims a protocol that can accelerate weight loss up to 40 percent faster compared to standard GLP 1 protocols and notes those are proprietary protocol claims.

BetterMe Rx’s captured materials focus on offering injectable and oral compounded GLP 1s with provider review, compounding, supplies, and free shipping. No outcome or comparative protocol claims are supplied for BetterMe Rx in the source material.

4. Third party validation and trust signals

IVIM Health is presented with unusually strong third party verification: a 4.9 star rating on both Google and Trustpilot according to the source. BetterMe Rx materials emphasize supply options and fulfillment features such as free 1 to 2 day delivery and no membership fee, but do not supply a dual third party rating in the captured evidence.

5. Shipping, inclusions, and program fees

BetterMe Rx includes medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies, and free 1 to 2 day shipping as part of its offering and states no membership fee and no hidden fees. IVIM Health includes a clinician guided program with a separate program fee referenced as $74.99 per month in the captured data and a free month code for enrollment. Both reiterate that compounded medications are not FDA approved and that prescriptions require clinician evaluation.

Pricing and billing: headline differences

Embody advertises compounded semaglutide at $69/month and compounded tirzepatide at $119/month, with a note that the $69 semaglutide price is a limited-time offer. Oak Longevity lists semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month and states one flat price covers all dosages. Oak bills monthly on a non-recurring basis and three-month prescriptions are billed upfront; Embody promotes a flat locked monthly price and no long-term membership.

Medication format and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. Each source includes the regulatory caution that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Embody explicitly contrasts compounded options with branded products by noting it does not offer branded Ozempic, Wegovy, or Zepbound.

Shipping speed, access, and approvals

Embody promises free shipping with delivery in 1–2 days after clinician approval. Oak Longevity offers free shipping with tracking and notes most patients receive medication within a few days; Oak also advertises same-day approval for qualifying patients. Both operate via online clinician review and do not require insurance.

Clinical review, coaching, and eligibility

Oak Longevity highlights free health coaching included with every plan and board certified physicians in your state reviewing cases; Oak also specifies patients with BMI below 20 do not qualify for GLP-1 treatment and notes not appropriate for patients under 18, pregnant, or nursing. Embody describes licensed provider review handled entirely online and includes telemed visits with prescription; Embody emphasizes a lean program with fewer premium features and no integrated coaching as a trade off for lower price.

Side note on commitments and cancellations

Embody markets no monthly membership and cancel anytime flexibility. Oak notes no subscriptions or membership fees and that three-month prescriptions are billed upfront with no refunds available after physician approval and once medication has shipped.

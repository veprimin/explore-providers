Luvo Health notes “Cancel anytime” and “Ongoing support and dose changes,” but the evidence does not provide the same flat pricing claim or the specific visit length captured for CoreAgeRx.

Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both sources include regulatory qualifiers: CoreAgeRx notes “Compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.” Luvo Health carries a similar statement: “Compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and are not reviewed or approved by the FDA.” Both also state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. These are important limitations to consider when comparing program offerings.

Start Willow vs HealthRx: both offer clinician-reviewed, compounded GLP-1 options, but they differ most on price model, shipping speed and cold-chain handling, and whether you must prepay for the lowest rate. Early verdict: choose Start Willow if you prioritize a direct-pay, no-membership model and guaranteed 3-day delivery; choose HealthRx if the lowest advertised monthly cost and overnight cold shipping matter most and you accept prepaid terms.

How pricing and payment structure compare

Start Willow lists semaglutide starting at $249 per month with ongoing pricing rising to $299/month; it emphasizes no insurance and no membership fees. HealthRx advertises semaglutide from $99/month but the lowest monthly price requires a 12-month prepaid plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal), and tirzepatide plans start at $179/month. HealthRx also states your plan price does not change when your dose does.

Shipping, cold-chain and delivery timing

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less from its licensed pharmacy network and cites same-day prescriptions and free 2-day shipping in some landing-page text. HealthRx offers overnight cold shipping with tracked delivery and free shipping on every plan, highlighting cold-chain handling for temperature-sensitive compounds.

Clinician review, medication choice and formulation

Both platforms use clinician review to determine whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is appropriate. Start Willow offers compounded semaglutide described as a personalized, once-weekly compounded injection prescribed as part of a treatment plan and notes that its compounded semaglutide is not FDA-approved and not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. HealthRx likewise states compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide is prepared per prescription in a licensed pharmacy and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Conclusion: who should choose each provider

Start Willow vs HealthRx centers on whether you prefer a direct-pay, no-membership model with a 3-day delivery guarantee or a lower advertised monthly cost that requires a prepaid commitment and provides overnight cold shipping. Both use clinician review and both offer compounded GLP-1s that are not FDA-approved.

Choose Start Willow if you want a direct-pay option with no insurance or membership fees, faster guaranteed delivery in 3 days or less, and a clinician-prescribed, personalized compounded semaglutide plan. Choose HealthRx if you want the lowest advertised monthly price via a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan, require overnight cold-chain shipping, and accept prepaid and plan renewal terms as stated.

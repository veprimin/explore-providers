Quick comparison: Sesame Care vs BetterMe Rx

Early verdict: Choose Sesame Care if you want a marketplace with clinician choice and options to use insurance; choose BetterMe Rx if you prefer compounded injectable or oral GLP-1s with lower 12-month cash pricing and included shipping.

How these programs differ most

This comparison of Sesame Care vs BetterMe Rx focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: clinical model and clinician choice, medication type and regulatory status, payment and pricing structure, insurance acceptance, and included services like shipping or supplies.

1. Clinical model and clinician selection

Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing. The source emphasizes a large clinician network and flexible scheduling so experience can vary by clinician. BetterMe Rx uses a cash-pay telehealth program with a licensed U.S. provider review before compounding and shipping; treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

2. Medication formats and regulatory notes

Sesame Care lists branded FDA-approved options such as oral Wegovy pill as well as semaglutide and tirzepatide products with specified strengths and prices. BetterMe Rx offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and oral formats. The BetterMe Rx source explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sesame Care includes a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

3. Pricing structures and commitments

Sesame Care advertises ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment and lists cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo while noting medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary by insurance and pharmacy. Sesame lists semaglutide cash price $149 and tirzepatide cash price $299 with price-with-insurance amounts as low as $25 for each in some cases.

BetterMe Rx presents monthly prices and lower 3, 6, or 12-month equivalents. It lists semaglutide injection from $145/mo on a 12-month plan or $205 monthly, tirzepatide injection from $215/mo on a 12-month plan or $275 monthly, oral semaglutide from $185/mo on a 12-month plan or $250 monthly, and oral tirzepatide from $215/mo on a 12-month plan or $265 monthly. BetterMe Rx notes lowest prices require a 12-month plan and longer plans are billed according to the selected term.

4. Insurance, shipping and included services

Sesame Care explicitly accepts insurance for weight loss medications and shows in-network pricing as low as $25 in some cases, while also offering cash-pay pricing. BetterMe Rx is cash-pay with no membership fee, includes provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies and free 1 to 2 day shipping, and states no insurance or membership fee required.

What the sources say about outcomes and commitments

Sesame Care cites milestones based on a study of injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg paired with lifestyle changes and notes that results may vary. BetterMe Rx notes cancel-anytime language alongside prepaid longer-term plans and that longer plans are billed according to the selected term.

How to decide: direct guidance

If you value choosing your clinician, flexible scheduling, and the option to use insurance for weight loss medications, Sesame Care is the option described in the source. If you are specifically comparing injectable and needle-free compounded GLP-1 formats, want included pharmacy compounding and free expedited shipping, and are comfortable with 3, 6, or 12-month prepayment terms and compounded medications that are not FDA-approved, BetterMe Rx is the option described in the source.

Conclusion

Both programs offer telehealth access to GLP-1 treatment pathways but with different approaches. Sesame Care emphasizes clinician choice, flexible appointments and insurance acceptance with subscriptions starting at $59/mo and cash-pay meds as low as $149/mo. BetterMe Rx emphasizes compounded injectable and oral options with lower 12-month-equivalent cash prices from $145/mo and includes compounding, supplies and free 1 to 2 day shipping, while noting compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

SnagRx vs YourEra: what differs most

This SnagRx vs YourEra comparison focuses on five clear differences supported by each provider: starting price and required commitment, price lock behavior, included services and support, shipping and pharmacies, and regulatory/compound status. The early verdict above summarizes which model suits cost-driven patients and which suits people wanting bundled support.

1) Starting price and plan commitment

SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on the 12-month plan; month-to-month is $99 and $149. YourEra advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/month and tirzepatide from $199/month on the 6-month plan, with a promo code that may reduce the checkout price. The lowest SnagRx rates require a 12-month commitment; YourEra’s lowest rates require a 6-month commitment.

View YourEra 6-month plan pricing

2) Price lock and dose-based pricing

Both providers state flat pricing at every dose, but SnagRx emphasizes a rate locked flat at every dose and at renewal for the life of treatment. YourEra also says the plan holds the same price at every dose with no dose-based increases. The distinction from source copy is SnagRx highlights a lifetime lock and names its pricing behavior as a core differentiator.

3) Included services, clinician access, and support

YourEra bundles the provider consult, prescription, cold shipping, dose adjustments, and 24/7 support into the plan price. SnagRx includes the telemed visit, clinician review via the OpenLoop Health network, and injection supplies; SnagRx states clinician review typically within five hours and always within 24. SnagRx does not include coaching or nutrition programming according to its materials.

Check SnagRx clinician and shipping details

4) Shipping speed and pharmacy sourcing

Both firms advertise fast shipping: SnagRx says next-day UPS/FedEx in temperature-controlled packaging and YourEra says free 1 to 2 day shipping from licensed US pharmacies. SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and its clinician network (OpenLoop Health); YourEra states shipments come from state-licensed US compounding pharmacies.

5) Regulatory notes and medication status

Both sources make the same regulatory point: compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products. SnagRx explicitly states its compounded medications are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but are not FDA-approved, and it encourages speaking with a provider about risks. YourEra likewise notes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved finished products and are prepared by compounding pharmacies pursuant to an individual prescription.

Conclusion: choosing between SnagRx vs YourEra

Both SnagRx and YourEra provide compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with flat pricing at every dose and fast shipping from US compounding pharmacies. Choose SnagRx if you want the lowest advertised annual cost and a stated lifetime price lock at every dose and are willing to commit to the 12-month plan. Choose YourEra if you prefer a shorter 6-month commitment that bundles consults, dose adjustments, and 24/7 support into the plan price.

Onboarding speed and clinician review

Oak conducts an online health questionnaire with a licensed clinician reviewing information asynchronously; Oak notes no video call is required and same-day approval is available for qualifying patients. Sprout Health emphasizes a lightning-quick approval process and fast, hassle-free onboarding. Both require a licensed clinician to determine if treatment is medically appropriate.

Product type and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications. Oak explicitly states its compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sprout’s materials also note that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. These regulatory qualifiers are present for both.

Features, extras, and limitations

Oak includes free health coaching and free shipping with tracking as part of its offering and emphasizes no subscriptions and no auto-charge; three-month prescriptions are billed upfront and Oak states no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped. Oak also notes patients with BMI below 20 do not qualify for GLP-1 treatment and not for patients under 18, pregnant, or nursing. Sprout highlights outcome framing of 10–26% body weight reduction over 12 months based on GLP-1 literature and a no-frills, fast-entry experience; Sprout does not publish an outcome guarantee in the captured content.

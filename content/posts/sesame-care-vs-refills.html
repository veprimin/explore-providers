Pricing and insurance considerations

Sesame Care distinguishes between subscription care and medication costs. The platform advertises ongoing video visits, dedicated care, labs, and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Medication costs are not included in the subscription and will vary based on insurance status and pharmacy. Sesame lists cash-pay semaglutide at $149 and tirzepatide at $299 with price with insurance as low as $25 in some cases.

Product types and regulatory notes

Refills focuses on personalized compounded GLP-1 products from $99/mo and is explicit that compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality by the FDA. Sesame Care offers branded options such as oral semaglutide and Zepbound tirzepatide pens and notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Access, shipping, and convenience

Refills highlights free discreet shipping, express shipping, and availability in all 50 US states with month-to-month service and no cancellation fees. Refills also emphasizes 24/7 health concierge support and a process where clinicians review intake and prescriptions are processed and shipped in about 2 business days. Sesame Care emphasizes nationwide access in many states, flexible scheduling, and a large clinician network to choose from, while pharmacy and medication costs can vary.

Support, clinician choice, and ongoing care

Sesame Care is built around clinician choice so experience can vary by clinician. It highlights a large clinician network and flexible appointments with ongoing video visits, labs, and messaging. Refills bundles licensed provider consultations and ongoing support into a monthly price and allows unlimited clinician consultations at no extra cost as described on its site.

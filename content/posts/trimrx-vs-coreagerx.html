TrimRx vs CoreAgeRx: headline differences

This comparison focuses on five supported differences: pricing model, medication options and regulatory notes, shipping speed, scale and ratings, and enrollment terms. All claims below are taken directly from the providers’ captured information.

1. Pricing model and guarantees

TrimRx: Weight-loss plans starting at $149/month with a stated Lifetime Price Protection that locks in today’s price for life. TrimRx mentions a $140 promotional discount on the landing evidence and says there is no monthly membership and you can cancel anytime. CoreAgeRx: Markets flat monthly pricing with “Same Price. Every Month.” and lists semaglutide at $79/month and tirzepatide at $129/month, with no membership fee. CoreAgeRx emphasizes no price hikes but the capture notes that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

2. Medication options and regulatory status

TrimRx primarily offers a compounded medication program with compounded GLP-1 injections starting at $149/month and compounded GLP-1+GIP starting at $249/month. TrimRx explicitly states that compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. TrimRx also notes providers may write prescriptions for branded FDA-approved medications, but TrimRx does not sell, dispense, or ship branded medications; patients would fill those separately. CoreAgeRx lists semaglutide and tirzepatide price points and notes that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate, and that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed; the capture also warns that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

3. Shipping speed and logistics

TrimRx: Captured landing-page evidence states free shipping that arrives in 1–2 days. CoreAgeRx: The capture asks “How fast is shipping?” but does not supply verified shipping speed. If fast shipping is a priority, TrimRx explicitly states 1–2 day delivery; CoreAgeRx does not provide a verified shipping speed in the captured details.

4. Scale, ratings, and real-world signals

TrimRx reports serving 100,000+ customers and a 4.8 Excellent Rating in the captured evidence. CoreAgeRx capture includes a customer-style testimonial line about weight and body changes but does not list a verified customer count or platform rating in the captured evidence.

5. Enrollment and clinical review

Both platforms operate with online clinician review. TrimRx notes prescription and telemed visits are included and that no insurance is required. CoreAgeRx highlights a 3-minute online visit and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician deems it medically appropriate. CoreAgeRx capture cautions readers to confirm eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

Embody vs BetterMe Rx compares a lowest-entry priced telehealth option to a multi-format compound pharmacy model. Early verdict: Embody is the lowest-cost, no-frills choice for patients focused on price, while BetterMe Rx is a better match for patients who want injectable and oral formats and lower monthly-equivalent pricing only with longer plans.

How they differ at a glance

Compare the two directly on price, medication formats, billing commitments, shipping speed, and regulatory status. Both sell compounded GLP-1 treatments through licensed U.S. providers and include provider review and free 1-2 day delivery, but they position those features differently.

Price and billing: lowest entry vs multi-month equivalents

Embody advertises compounded semaglutide at $69/month and compounded tirzepatide at $119/month, with language noting the $69 semaglutide price was $299 previously and that final pricing may vary based on dose and clinician recommendation. By contrast, BetterMe Rx shows lower monthly-equivalent pricing only on longer-term plans: compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan with specific product equivalents listed as “as low as” $145/mo for semaglutide injection and $215/mo for tirzepatide injection when spread over 12 months. BetterMe Rx also lists higher month-to-month prices when not on the long plan.

Medication formats and clinical review

Embody offers compounded injectable semaglutide and tirzepatide. BetterMe Rx offers both injectable and oral compounded formats for semaglutide and tirzepatide, including oral tablet options described as a needle-free daily tablet for tirzepatide at parity with the injection on some plans. Both platforms require a licensed provider review and provide prescriptions fulfilled by partnered pharmacies when appropriate.

Shipping, supplies, and membership terms

Both providers include free 1-2 day shipping after approval and state there is no membership fee. Embody emphasizes “no long-term commitment” and cancel anytime, with flat locked-in monthly pricing. BetterMe Rx emphasizes no membership fee but notes the lowest prices require committing to longer 3, 6, or 12-month supplies that are billed according to the selected term and that cancel-anytime language appears alongside prepaid longer-term plans.

Regulatory and safety notes

Both listings plainly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Embody explicitly notes its compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products but assert they use the same active ingredients as branded products. BetterMe Rx includes the same regulatory qualifier and adds that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Conclusion: Which to choose

Both sell compounded GLP-1s through licensed telehealth workflows with fast shipping. Choose Embody if your primary priority is the lowest entry price and a no-frills, cancel-anytime approach. Choose BetterMe Rx if you need injectable and oral format options and are willing to commit to longer prepaid plans to reach the lower monthly-equivalent pricing.

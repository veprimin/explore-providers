2. Compound availability and platform differences

Both platforms offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. Enhance MD positions itself as one of the few providers to offer both compounds at competitive introductory pricing on a single platform. Measured also offers both compounds and pairs them with dietitian and care team support.

3. Shipping, support, and service features

Measured explicitly lists free two-day shipping on every order, same-day prescription decisions, unlimited messaging, 24/7 care team, and dietitian support. Enhance MD emphasizes price structure and clinician-directed care and notes 10,000+ patients treated, but landing-page evidence focuses on pricing and flat-rate billing rather than shipping speed or unlimited messaging.

4. Prescription review and regulatory notes

Both providers state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Measured specifically notes Rx decisions can be same day. Enhance MD notes clinician-directed review and that medications are compounded.

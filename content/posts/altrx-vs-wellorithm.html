Overview: altRx vs Wellorithm at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences between altRx and Wellorithm: published starting price, clinician coverage, availability of semaglutide and tirzepatide, virtual care model and compounded medication regulatory status. Both platforms use telehealth and ship compounded GLP-1 medications to patients when a licensed clinician determines treatment is appropriate.

Price transparency and starting costs

altRx lists a limited offer with explicit starting prices: compounded semaglutide from $89/month and compounded tirzepatide from $149/month, and multi-month plans that reduce the per-month rate. Wellorithm lists a starting figure of $147 in some places and also advertises promotions like “Enjoy $100 off your first month,” but it does not publish full pricing and says you receive a personalized quote after the intake quiz. If clear, upfront starting price matters, altRx provides that detail directly.

Clinician coverage and state availability

Wellorithm highlights licensed clinicians in all 50 states and positions this as a primary strength for patients in states with limited GLP-1 telehealth options. altRx states its membership is available to residents of all 50 U.S. states, but it notes provider availability and prescribing authority vary by state and that an assigned provider will be licensed in your state of residence. For guaranteed 50-state clinician coverage, the source evidence emphasizes Wellorithm’s nationwide clinician network.

Medication options and compounding

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide options. altRx consistently describes its program as offering compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, with prescriptions filled by state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies in its network. Wellorithm similarly offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and delivers medication after virtual consultation. Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Care model, shipping and support

altRx promotes 100 percent online medical visits, a 2-minute quiz to start, free discreet 2-day delivery, and ongoing care team support included in the monthly fee. Wellorithm also uses a 2-minute quiz, virtual consultations, and ships medication to the home with free shipping mentioned in its materials. altRx details included supplies such as vials, syringes and prep pads; Wellorithm states the process is fully virtual and includes ongoing virtual check-ins for titration.

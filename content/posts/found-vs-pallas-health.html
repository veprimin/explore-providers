Medication options and clinician role

Found frames its approach as biology-first, building a personalized program around hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavior. Found lists tirzepatide and semaglutide, including compounded versions, and notes that Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility. Pallas Health offers both FDA-approved brand-name GLP-1s and clinician-personalized compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared in the USA when a brand medication is not the right fit. Pallas emphasizes that a US-licensed clinician reviews health history and personalizes the plan and that the clinician chooses brand or compounded medication.

Plan structure, fees, and what is included

Found markets affordable monthly plans with flexible pricing and cancel anytime language; Found membership pricing appears in various starting figures and Found says it handles insurance paperwork and accepts many plans. Pallas Health markets one all-in plan price where provider care, check-ins and messaging are included with no membership fee. Pallas notes compounded plans are priced by medication rather than by milligram so titrating up does not raise the plan price, and that plans renew every 12 weeks for compounded options.

Notable qualifiers

Found cautions that compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy and are not available in all states, and that certain branded medications are FDA-approved for conditions other than weight. Pallas similarly notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that Pallas does not manufacture or compound medications; they are prepared by US-licensed compounding pharmacies. Pallas also states eligibility is determined by a US-licensed clinician and that it is cash-pay only.

Delivery, telehealth experience, and accessibility

Pallas Health is 100 percent online, asynchronous in most states with only seven states plus Washington D.C. requiring a video visit, and offers free expedited discreet shipping on every plan. Found emphasizes a medically supervised program tailored to individual biology and says it handles insurance paperwork and that most insurance is now accepted. The source does not supply a detailed shipping policy for Found beyond membership and pricing disclosures.

Final comparison and who each is best for

Found vs Pallas Health boils down to whether you need insurance billing and a biology-first, personalized program or prefer a cash-pay, clinician-led plan that bundles provider care into a single price. Found highlights affordable monthly plans and insurance acceptance starting as low as $39 and focuses on tailoring treatment to metabolic biology. Pallas Health emphasizes clinician selection between brand-name and compounded GLP-1s, an all-in plan price that includes care, and a first month for compounded semaglutide at $139 then renewing at a $199 per month average.

Choose Found if you want a program that centers treatment around your individual biology and the option to use insurance paperwork handled by the provider, with plans starting as low as $39.

Choose Pallas Health if you prefer cash-pay pricing that includes clinician care and messaging in the plan price, want a clinician to select between brand-name pens and a compounded preparation, and value free expedited discreet shipping.

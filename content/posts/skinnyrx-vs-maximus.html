SkinnyRx vs Maximus: Pricing and payment details

Price is a clear distinction in SkinnyRx vs Maximus. SkinnyRx lists pricing as low as $249 per month and offers payment flexibility with Affirm Buy Now Pay Later. Maximus advertises a lower flat rate at $132.99 per month and multiple starting points as shown on its pages. Neither provider bills insurance for the listed plans in the source materials.

Formulation and delivery: oral tablets versus injections and shipping speed

SkinnyRx promotes daily oral semaglutide tablets taken under the tongue as an alternative to injections and includes free overnight shipping on all orders. Maximus emphasizes once-weekly compounded semaglutide injections as its weight loss protocol and offers free priority or expedited shipping depending on the plan. If you prefer an oral tablet format, the supplied evidence supports SkinnyRx as the documented option.

Pharmacy sourcing, clinician review, and regulatory notes

Maximus fills prescriptions through trusted U.S. pharmacies and states prescriptions are approved by licensed doctors via telemedicine. SkinnyRx mentions partner pharmacies and third-party testing for compounded lots, with testing every 3 to 6 months confirming concentration within plus or minus 10 percent. Both providers supply compounded medication options in their source text, and both include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved or reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Patient support, track record, and availability

SkinnyRx highlights ongoing patient access to care teams and licensed providers, ongoing support, and a stated trust by 50,000+ patients in landing-page bullets. Maximus emphasizes convenient telemedicine access, prescriptions approved by licensed doctors, and availability in all 50 states except Mississippi. Maximus is described in the source as a smaller, newer platform with a thinner public track record compared with larger providers mentioned in its materials.

Refunds, cancellation, and prescription review policies

SkinnyRx allows a full refund any time before a licensed provider completes the prescription review; once the prescription is completed and fulfillment begins the order becomes non-refundable. For subscriptions, SkinnyRx notes you can cancel future months at any time and must cancel before the next shipment is prepared to avoid being charged. Maximus states that if you are not approved for treatment, medication costs will be fully refunded. Both providers require clinician review and prescription approval as part of their processes.

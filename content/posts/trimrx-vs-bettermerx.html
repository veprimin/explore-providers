TrimRx vs BetterMe Rx compares two cash-pay telehealth weight-loss programs that sell compounded GLP-1 options and include telehealth review and free 1–2 day shipping. Quick verdict: TrimRx is focused on a price-lock, single-dose model with GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options starting at $149, while BetterMe Rx emphasizes four administration formats and lower monthly-equivalents when you choose longer prepaid plans.

How TrimRx and BetterMe Rx differ at a glance

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide through a U.S. clinician review and compounding pharmacies, and both state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. They differ most distinctly in pricing model, medication formats available, pricing transparency features, and how longer-term billing works.

1. Pricing model and promotional messaging

TrimRx lists weight-loss plans starting at $149/month and advertises a promotional $140 discount on some landing-page messaging. TrimRx highlights Lifetime Price Protection so you lock in today’s price for life and pay the same price on every dose. BetterMe Rx shows lower monthly-equivalent pricing when you pick longer prepaid plans: treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan with standard monthly prices also listed (for example semaglutide injection $205/month or as low as $145/month on 12-month plan). BetterMe Rx’s lowest advertised prices require selecting a 12-month plan and longer plans are billed according to the selected term.

Lock price with TrimRx Lifetime Price Protection

2. Medication formats and options

TrimRx provides compounded GLP-1 injections and a GLP-1+GIP dual agonist option with dosing starting at $149 and $249 respectively. BetterMe Rx offers four formats: injectable and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide, with monthly-equivalent pricing that falls on longer plans. If you want a needle-free oral tirzepatide option, BetterMe Rx explicitly lists oral tirzepatide and oral semaglutide as available formats.

3. Pricing transparency, membership, and cancellation

Both providers state no membership fee and claim cancel-anytime language. TrimRx emphasizes no hidden fees and a no monthly membership model with no insurance required. BetterMe Rx states no membership fee and no hidden fees but notes that the lowest per-month prices require committing to longer prepaid plans which are billed according to the selected term.

4. Shipping, support, and regulatory notes

Both TrimRx and BetterMe Rx include free 1–2 day shipping and a U.S. licensed provider review. Both sources state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. TrimRx also notes that if a branded FDA-approved medication is prescribed, patients must fill branded prescriptions separately and pay associated costs themselves.

Conclusion: TrimRx vs BetterMe Rx

TrimRx vs BetterMe Rx comes down to pricing structure and format needs. TrimRx centers on a locked single-dose price and offers both GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP injection programs starting at $149 with Lifetime Price Protection, free 1-2 day shipping, and no membership. BetterMe Rx offers more format variety including oral semaglutide and oral tirzepatide and advertises lower monthly-equivalent pricing when you choose longer prepaid plans, with standard monthly prices higher unless you select a 12-month term. Both stress that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Choose TrimRx if you want Lifetime Price Protection, injection-based GLP-1 or GLP-1+GIP options and a single predictable monthly price.

Choose BetterMe Rx if you want needle-free oral options or you are willing to commit to a longer prepaid term to reach the lowest monthly-equivalent pricing.

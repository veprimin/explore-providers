Which is better for your telehealth GLP-1 plan: IVIM Health vs JoinSkinny? Early verdict: IVIM Health stands out for third-party ratings and a structured GLP-1 ID protocol, while JoinSkinny focuses on flat-rate plans, fast temperature-controlled shipping, and simple cancel-anytime terms.

How they differ at a glance: ratings, pricing, protocol, shipping

IVIM Health and JoinSkinny both deliver clinician-reviewed telemedicine care and prescribe GLP-1 options, but they emphasize different strengths. IVIM highlights dual 4.9-star ratings on Google and Trustpilot, a no-obligation free consultation valued at $75 for new patients, a GIFT75 free month membership code, and a clinician-guided GLP-1 ID protocol that it says can accelerate weight loss by up to 40%. JoinSkinny emphasizes flat-rate pricing with plans starting at $149, free temperature-controlled shipping in 1 to 2 days, no monthly membership fee, cancel-anytime before the next billing cycle, and U.S.-based, LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners.

Distinctive difference 1: third-party ratings and trust signals

IVIM Health reports 4.9-star ratings on both Google and Trustpilot, presented as an unusually strong third-party satisfaction signal in this market. JoinSkinny does not present a dual 4.9-star claim in the supplied evidence. If external verified reviews are a primary decision factor, IVIM’s dual ratings are the supported distinction.

Distinctive difference 2: pricing model and membership

Pricing structures differ. IVIM lists a promotional entry with GIFT75 for a free month membership and a no-obligation free consultation ($75 value). IVIM’s published pricing examples include $75/mo for compounded semaglutide, $133/mo for compounded tirzepatide, plus a $74.99/mo program fee; IVIM’s FAQ notes $99 for the first month and $174/month ongoing. JoinSkinny shows plans starting at $149, offers monthly and 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles, a $140 promotional discount in captured evidence, and states no membership fee with the ability to cancel before the next billing cycle. JoinSkinny also emphasizes that the monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage and there are no hidden fees.

Distinctive difference 3: protocol and dosing approach

IVIM markets a proprietary GLP-1 ID titration and optimization protocol described as clinician-guided and claiming up to 40% faster weight loss compared to a standard protocol. The evidence notes that this 40% figure is IVIM’s own protocol claim and not from an independent head-to-head study. JoinSkinny uses flat-rate dosing options with provider-guided starting doses and dose adjustments at no extra cost, emphasizing same price every dose and clear bundle options.

Distinctive difference 4: dispensing, shipping, and pharmacy model

JoinSkinny states that prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners, shipped in temperature-controlled packaging with next-day UPS shipping for prescriptions received before 2 pm central, and includes tracking and insurance for shipments. IVIM’s materials indicate medications are prepared by independent, licensed compounding pharmacies and note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not undergone FDA review for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Both providers include regulatory notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Clinical claims and evidence notes

IVIM cites internal and published retrospective observational studies and says patients following the IVIM Protocol lost an average of 46% more weight over 68 weeks when compared to another published clinical GLP-1 trial; the evidence explicitly notes this was not a head-to-head study. JoinSkinny’s supplied evidence references generalized clinical ranges that GLP-1 and GLP-1 + GIP medications are clinically shown to help patients lose 15 to 22 percent of body weight over 12 to 15 months when paired with consistent use and lifestyle guidance. Both sources caution that results vary and compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Quick verdict: Trimi vs Maximus

Trimi vs Maximus is primarily a comparison of price structure, shipping speed, and program guarantees. Early verdict: Trimi emphasizes lower starting pricing on annual plans, same-price dosing, and free overnight shipping while Maximus emphasizes a straightforward low monthly rate of $132.99 with expedited shipping and wide state availability except Mississippi.

How they compare: core differences at a glance

This comparison uses only supplied facts. It focuses on the most distinctive differences between Trimi and Maximus: advertised price and pricing model, shipping promise, program guarantees and refunds, availability and medical oversight, and tirzepatide availability.

1. Price and pricing model

Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99 per month on the annual plan and lists compounded tirzepatide at $125 per month. Trimi highlights flat pricing that never rises as dose increases and states the same price at every dose on annual plans. Maximus lists a flat rate of $132.99 per month and emphasizes no membership fees and transparent pricing on a direct-pay model. Both providers present a low flat monthly cost, but Trimi’s $99 figure is tied specifically to an annual plan while Maximus presents $132.99 as its straightforward monthly rate.

2. Shipping and fulfillment

Trimi advertises free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and free overnight shipping repeatedly in its materials. Maximus offers free expedited or priority shipping and states medication is compounded in U.S. pharmacies. Both rely on U.S. pharmacies, but Trimi specifically promotes overnight temperature-controlled shipping while Maximus highlights free priority or expedited shipping without the overnight claim.

3. Guarantees and refund policies

Trimi includes a 6-month money-back promise: follow your plan for 6 months and if you do not lose weight you can request a refund, with terms applied. Trimi also states if a patient is not approved by the medical team there is a 100% automatic refund. Maximus states if you are not approved for treatment your medication costs will be fully refunded. Maximus does not present a published outcome or weight loss guarantee in the captured evidence.

4. Medical oversight and program scope

Trimi specifies prescriptions are provided by U.S. licensed clinicians and reviews are conducted through Arora Health, a clinical network led by Dr. Sean Arora, MD, described as licensed in all 50 states. Trimi names state licensed 503A compounding pharmacies including VialsRx and GreenwichRx and notes a certificate of analysis is available on request. Maximus states prescriptions are approved by licensed doctors and filled through trusted U.S. pharmacies and notes broader state availability with the program available in all 50 states except Mississippi. Maximus highlights telemedicine access with no in-person clinic visits.

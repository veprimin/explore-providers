Pricing model and commitment: locked annual rate versus flexible tiers

SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and compounded tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan, with the rate locked flat at every dose for the life of treatment. Month-to-month pricing for the same medications is $99 and $149. JoinFridays starts at $117/month and notes that multi-month plans lower the effective rate, with twelve months averaging $150/month; standalone month-to-month pricing is higher (example: one-month standalone plan $249). The clear difference is that SnagRx emphasizes a price lock across titration when you commit to 12 months, while JoinFridays emphasizes no long-term contract and promo codes that can lower upfront cost.

Medication options and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. SnagRx makes explicit that its medications are compounded, produced in FDA-regulated facilities, and not FDA-approved finished products. JoinFridays likewise states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that product appearance may differ from website images. Neither source claims FDA approval for compounded products; both advise treatment only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Clinician review, care style, and add-ons

SnagRx routes care through OpenLoop Health, a network of US-licensed clinicians covering 48 states, and states a clinician typically reviews intake within five hours and always within 24. SnagRx includes the clinician review in its stated price and notes that prescriptions are subject to clinician review. JoinFridays emphasizes personalized, clinician-guided care and pairs medication with coaching on some plans; it positions its approach as guidance rather than a templated protocol. The distinction: SnagRx highlights speed of review and a named clinician network; JoinFridays highlights an integrated coaching or membership experience on certain plans and flexible cancellations.

Shipping, fulfillment, and named partners

SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and promises next-day UPS or FedEx shipping in temperature-controlled packaging for approved orders. JoinFridays states it does not manufacture compounded medications and warns that actual medication appearance may differ from images; specific shipping timing or named pharmacy partners are not supplied in the source. If named pharmacies and next-day shipping are a priority, SnagRx supplies those specifics.

Promotions, pricing tradeoffs, and who each is best for

JoinFridays displays active promo codes in its marketing and claims up to $500 off tirzepatide and up to $400 off semaglutide with example codes; the source warns promo availability can change. SnagRx markets bottom-of-market annual pricing with a flat rate across titration and a refund if the prescription is declined. SnagRx is described as best for cost-driven patients who want predictable all-in pricing across a full titration. JoinFridays is described as best for patients who want personalized GLP-1 care with flexible cancellation and aggressive promo codes.

Conclusion: SnagRx vs JoinFridays

SnagRx vs JoinFridays comes down to whether you prefer a locked annual rate with named 503A pharmacies and fast clinician review or a flexible cancel-anytime model with promo codes and coaching included on some plans. SnagRx offers semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan with price locked at every dose. JoinFridays starts at $117/month, emphasizes no long-term contract, and promotes aggressive promo codes that can lower out-of-pocket cost.

Choose SnagRx if you want a bottom-of-market annual price with a price lock across titration, named US 503A partner pharmacies, and next-day shipping subject to clinician approval. Choose JoinFridays if you want flexible cancellation, promotional codes, and clinician-guided care with coaching options on certain plans.

2. Medication types, FDA status, and compounding

Hers explicitly offers FDA‑approved Wegovy® (brand‑name semaglutide) and states it does not offer compounded medications. The source preserves regulatory notes that compounded medications are not FDA‑approved. CoreAgeRx lists semaglutide and tirzepatide dosing options (semaglutide up to 1.0 mg; tirzepatide up to 7.5 mg) and notes that medication‑specific approval status must be confirmed; captured material says “Medication-specific approval status must be confirmed; branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.” Use only the supplied facts when weighing FDA‑approved brand‑name availability against CoreAgeRx pricing claims.

3. Care model and ongoing support

Hers markets a “personalized care team with ongoing dose check‑ins,” which indicates ongoing clinician involvement as part of the program. CoreAgeRx promotes a fast “3‑MINUTE ONLINE VISIT” and emphasizes consistent monthly billing; the landing page highlights that GLP‑1 treatment takes consistency and that CoreAgeRx seeks to avoid surprise price increases over time. The sources do not supply identical details about clinician qualifications or protocol; they state that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

4. Dosing options and limits

Hers lists semaglutide and mentions “High dose available” and includes tirzepatide pricing lines in captured content, though exact dose limits are not specified beyond advertised product types. CoreAgeRx specifically lists semaglutide “any dose up to 1.0 mg” and tirzepatide “any dose up to 7.5 mg” on its landing page. The comparison sources preserve that dose availability and any clinical eligibility are subject to clinician review.

Final verdict: Hers vs CoreAgeRx

Hers centers on providing FDA‑approved Wegovy® and emphasizes a personalized care team and that it does not offer compounded medications, with starting pricing shown at “From $149/mo” and a membership requirement. Choose based on whether you prioritize FDA‑approved brand medications plus ongoing dose check‑ins or a no‑membership, price‑stable monthly plan.

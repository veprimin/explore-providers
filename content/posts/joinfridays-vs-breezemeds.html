How JoinFridays vs BreezeMeds differ at a glance

Both platforms require a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness. Below are the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: starting price and plans, medication formats and specific tirzepatide offers, membership and cancellation terms, shipping and delivery, and regulatory status of compounded products.

1) Starting price and plan transparency

JoinFridays: Starting at $117/month with specified one-, three-, six-, and twelve-month plan examples and promotional codes. The provider highlights a $249 month-to-month plan and multi-month averages that reduce effective cost over time.

BreezeMeds: Pricing displayed as starting at $199 for semaglutide injection, $299 for tirzepatide injection, and oral formulations shown from $399. A separate $169 figure appears on the landing evidence without a clear product association. Fees, billing commitments, and renewal terms were not fully visible in captured evidence.

2) Medication formats and tirzepatide availability

JoinFridays explicitly lists compounded tirzepatide and compounded semaglutide on one platform and also references name brand injectables as options with separate prices. It emphasizes tirzepatide as “Most Effective” and semaglutide as “Most Affordable” while offering both compounds.

BreezeMeds lists semaglutide injection, tirzepatide injection, and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide. The landing evidence shows oral and injectable options but does not link the $169 figure to a specific product.

3) Membership, cancelation, and clinician guidance

JoinFridays markets “no long-term contracts,” “no membership fees, cancel anytime,” and personalized clinician-guided care with dosing guidance. BreezeMeds emphasizes an online intake and a personalized treatment plan with discreet shipping, but captured evidence did not show explicit cancel-anytime language or full fee details.

4) Shipping, discreet delivery, and coverage notes

Both sites indicate home delivery. BreezeMeds highlights discreet shipping to your door and ongoing support. JoinFridays notes membership included free and presents name-brand options with insurance support mentioned for name-brand products. State availability and insurance acceptance should be confirmed with BreezeMeds because captured evidence asks “What states do you service?” and “do you accept insurance?”

5) Regulatory and compound-product disclosures

JoinFridays and BreezeMeds both include clinician review requirements for treatment. JoinFridays explicitly states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. BreezeMeds notes medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

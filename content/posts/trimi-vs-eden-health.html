Trimi vs Eden Health quickly comes down to whether you want compounded medications with flat dose pricing and a short-term refund promise, or a clinician-guided program with a lifetime price guarantee. Early verdict: Trimi emphasizes compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with flat per-dose pricing and free overnight shipping, while Eden Health emphasizes a clinician-guided monthly program with lifetime price guarantee and free expedited shipping.

How pricing and guarantees compare

Both providers publish monthly prices for semaglutide and tirzepatide, but the structure and promises differ. Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/mo on the annual plan and compounded tirzepatide for $125/month, with a 6-month money-back promise if you follow the plan and do not lose weight, terms apply. Eden Health lists semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129 monthly and states a lifetime price guarantee with no membership fees. All price claims here are presented as the providers state them.

Three distinctive differences

1. Compounding and regulatory notes

Trimi explicitly offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by state-licensed 503A pharmacies and notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Eden Health indicates that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

2. Pricing model and membership

Trimi highlights flat pricing that never rises as your dose increases and cites an annual plan price point for semaglutide from $99/mo and tirzepatide $125/month. Trimi also describes one flat membership covering medication, clinician visits, ongoing check-ins, and free shipping, with terms and state availability varying. Eden Health presents a monthly guided GLP-1 program with one monthly price guaranteed for life, states no membership fees, and emphasizes a single monthly price for medication and support.

3. Shipping speed and logistics

Trimi advertises free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping from FDA regulated pharmacies. Eden Health offers free expedited shipping. Both providers state shipping is included in their messaging, with Trimi specifying overnight and temperature-controlled shipment and Eden Health using the term expedited shipping.

Safety and regulatory language to note

Both providers include language about compounded medications not being FDA-approved and not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

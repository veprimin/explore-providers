Looking for a direct comparison of Sesame Care vs Wellorithm for GLP-1 telehealth? Quick verdict: Sesame Care is best if you want a marketplace to choose clinicians and transparent subscription options, while Wellorithm is best if you need nationwide clinician coverage and compounded medication access.

How they differ at a glance: pricing, medication type, and coverage

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences between Sesame Care and Wellorithm: pricing models and transparency, branded versus compounded medications, clinician network and state coverage, consultation and delivery model, and regulatory notes. The primary keyword Sesame Care vs Wellorithm appears throughout to keep the comparison clear.

Pricing and transparency

Sesame Care lists a starting subscription price and medication ranges: ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Cash-pay GLP-1 medications are as low as $149/mo, and some medication prices with insurance can be as low as $25. Sesame also states medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on insurance status and pharmacy.

Wellorithm promotes starting options like Only $147 plus fast, free shipping and multiple starting prices for compounded products. However, Wellorithm does not publish full pricing for personalized plans; you receive a quote after completing an intake quiz. Their landing claims include free shipping and promotional first month offers, and stated starting prices for various compounded formats.

Medication types: branded versus compounded

Sesame Care lists branded medication options including semaglutide pricing and tirzepatide pricing with specific branded references such as Wegovy pill and Zepbound KwikPen and strength and price ranges. Wellorithm offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. Important regulatory note from both sources: when compounded medications are offered, they are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sesame also notes medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Clinician network and state coverage

Sesame Care positions itself as a marketplace with a large clinician network, flexible scheduling, and nationwide access in many states, emphasizing patient choice to browse and select providers by specialty and availability. Wellorithm emphasizes licensed clinicians in all 50 states, framing national coverage as a key advantage for patients in states with limited GLP-1 telehealth options.

Consultation model and delivery

Both platforms operate virtually. Sesame Care highlights ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging. Wellorithm offers a fully virtual consultation with medication shipped to your home after an online assessment and ongoing virtual check-ins for titration. Wellorithm also promotes a 2-minute quiz to check eligibility and states medication is shipped to the home.

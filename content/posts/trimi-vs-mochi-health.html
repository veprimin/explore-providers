This Trimi vs Mochi Health comparison focuses on five supported differences: pricing structure, visit model, medication types and compounding, shipping and refunds, and membership versus flat-rate billing. Each section compares both providers directly using only the supplied facts.

1. Pricing structure: flat-rate versus membership-plus-medication

Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/mo and compounded tirzepatide at $125/month with the explicit claim that all doses carry the same price and that pricing never rises as your dose increases. Mochi Health advertises membership-plus-medication pricing that starts from $99/month and offers 50% Off Your 1st Month with code BESTWLHALFOFF. Mochi also notes promotional first-month rates as low as $39 for some first-month memberships, with standard membership pricing after that. Trimi emphasizes a flat membership that covers medication, clinician visits, and ongoing check-ins; Mochi emphasizes variable total monthly cost that depends on plan and medication.

2. Visit and care model: synchronous video vs other approaches

Mochi Health distinguishes itself by offering live video visits with a doctor bookable in about 5 minutes, describing this as fundamentally different from the asynchronous questionnaire review used by most competitors. Trimi describes clinician oversight through licensed U.S. providers and a clinical network led by a medical director, but the supplied evidence does not state whether visits are live video or asynchronous. The key supported contrast is Mochi’s explicit live video visit capability versus Trimi’s emphasis on licensed clinician review and clinician network oversight.

3. Medication types and compounding details

Both providers focus on compounded medications and the evidence includes regulatory notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Trimi markets compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide prescribed by U.S.-licensed clinicians and prepared by state-licensed 503A pharmacies, with named pharmacies and a certificate of analysis available on request. Mochi lists a broader menu of compounded options in the captured evidence, including compounded semaglutide injections and pills, compounded tirzepatide, and compounded orforglipron, with specific starting prices for several compounded options.

4. Shipping, refunds, and guarantees

Trimi advertises free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and a 6-month money-back promise where you can request a refund if you do not lose weight after following your plan for 6 months, with terms applying. Trimi also states: Not approved by our medical team? 100% automatic refund. Mochi’s captured evidence emphasizes ongoing support and membership continuity; promotional first-month pricing is explicitly limited to new customers and terms vary by state. The evidence supplied does not state overnight shipping for Mochi.

5. State access, clinician oversight, and transparency

Mochi states reliable GLP-1 prescription access in all 50 states and emphasizes that a licensed physician evaluates cases during live video consultations. Trimi states that its clinical oversight is provided by licensed U.S. providers through Arora Health, led by a medical director licensed in all 50 states, and identifies state-licensed pharmacies used for compounding. Both include the regulatory qualifier about compounded medications not being FDA-approved.

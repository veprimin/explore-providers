Pallas Health documents that a US-licensed clinician reviews your health history and personalizes the plan, offering FDA-approved Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound and Mounjaro, or compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared in the USA when a brand medication is not the right fit. Verified pricing and billing differences

Pallas Health supplies clear price points for both compounded and brand options. Compounded semaglutide is $139 for the first month, then $597 every 12 weeks, a $199 per month average; compounded tirzepatide is $179 first month, then $897 every 12 weeks, a $299 per month average. Pallas also lists cash-pay brand prices: Wegovy $1,349 per month cash-pay, Zepbound $1,069, Ozempic $1,299 and Mounjaro $1,349, and states it is cash-pay only and does not bill insurance. ReadyRx captured evidence likewise records that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality, but ReadyRx details about compounding practices were not verified on the blocked affiliate page.

Plan inclusions, care access and shipping

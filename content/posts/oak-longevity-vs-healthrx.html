Oak Longevity vs HealthRx is a straight cost and process comparison for people considering compounded GLP-1 treatment. Quick verdict: Oak prioritizes low flat monthly pricing, no subscriptions, and free coaching and shipping, while HealthRx emphasizes low prepaid-plan pricing, overnight cold shipping, and clinician-led medication choice.

Start Oak Longevity: semaglutide from $133/mo

How they set price and payment expectations

Oak Longevity advertises semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month with one flat price covering all dosages and no subscription required. Billing is monthly on a non-recurring basis; three-month prescriptions are billed upfront and Oak notes no refunds after physician approval and once medication has shipped. HealthRx shows semaglutide from $99/month but that lowest monthly rate requires a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal). HealthRx notes tirzepatide plans start at $179/month and that the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan.

Verdict on pricing structure

If you want one flat monthly charge with no subscription commitment, the source shows Oak explicitly bills non-recurring monthly and emphasizes no subscriptions. If you are willing to prepay for a year to access the lowest per-month figure, HealthRx documents a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan from $99/month.

Clinical review, medication choice, and regulatory notes

Both providers require that treatment is determined medically appropriate by a licensed clinician. Oak uses an asynchronous clinical review without a required video call; board-certified physicians in your state review each case. HealthRx states the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is the right fit, or whether neither is appropriate. Both sources include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Shipping, fulfillment, and pharmacy handling

Oak includes free shipping with tracking and claims same-day approval for qualifying patients; shipping is included with plans. HealthRx emphasizes overnight cold shipping, cold chain tracked and free on every plan. HealthRx also specifies compounding occurs in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and that compounded medication is prepared per prescription.

Eligibility, returns, and plan limitations

Oak states patients with BMI below 20 do not qualify for GLP-1 treatment and that three-month prescriptions are billed upfront with no refunds available after physician approval and once medication has shipped. HealthRx advises confirming eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment and notes pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change. Both sources say treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Start Willow vs BetterMe Rx is a comparison of two cash-pay telehealth options for compounded GLP-1 medications. Quick verdict: Start Willow prioritizes fast delivery and a simple direct-pay model for compounded semaglutide starting at $249 the first month, while BetterMe Rx offers wider medication and administration options with lower monthly-equivalent prices if you commit to longer plans.

How they differ: pricing model, formats, shipping, and compounding

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: pricing structure and starting rates, medication formats offered, shipping speed and delivery promises, and whether the product is compounded and FDA-reviewed. Both providers sell compounded GLP-1 medications and include a licensed provider review, but they present different tradeoffs for price and format.

Pricing and billing

Start Willow lists “From $249 per Month” and specifies $249 for the first month and $299/month ongoing. The source frames Willow as a direct-pay model with no membership fees and no insurance required. BetterMe Rx reports multiple monthly prices and lower monthly-equivalent rates for longer prepay terms: for example, semaglutide injection $205 monthly or as low as $145/month on a 12-month plan, tirzepatide injection $275 or as low as $215/month on 12-month plan, and oral options with their own ranges. BetterMe Rx’s lowest rates require a 12-month plan, while Start Willow’s price is a straightforward monthly direct-pay with an ongoing $299 rate noted as higher than some competitors.

Medication formats and choice

Start Willow emphasizes a compounded semaglutide injection as its featured offering and describes medications comparable to Ozempic and tirzepatide dispensed through its licensed pharmacy network. BetterMe Rx explicitly sells compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in both injectable and oral formats, noting four medication and administration options including a needle-free daily tablet for tirzepatide. If format variety matters, BetterMe Rx supports more administration options according to the source.

Shipping and delivery commitments

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less from its licensed pharmacy network and advertises same day prescriptions and free 2-Day shipping in captured landing-page evidence. BetterMe Rx advertises free 1–2 day delivery and includes supplies and shipping with medication and provider review. Both platforms promise fast, discreet shipping but Willow emphasizes a 3-day or less guarantee while BetterMe Rx specifies free 1-2 day delivery.

Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both sources state that offered medications are compounded and are not FDA-approved. Start Willow includes an explicit regulatory note: compounded semaglutide is not approved by the FDA and the FDA does not review compounded medications for safety, effectiveness, or quality. BetterMe Rx likewise notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety or effectiveness. Both rely on state-licensed compounding pharmacies and licensed clinician review before dispensing.

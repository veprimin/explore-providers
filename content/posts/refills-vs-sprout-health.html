The most distinctive differences between Refills and Sprout Health are price structure and ongoing cost, onboarding speed and approval framing, compounded medication disclosures and regulatory notes, and included support or membership features. Pricing structure and discounts

Refills offers personalized compounded GLP-1 from $99/month with flat pricing at every dosage. Sprout Health promotes a $50 discount on the first month, bringing the initial cost to $199, with ongoing pricing at $249/month after the discounted month. The two providers therefore target different tradeoffs: Refills highlights a consistent low monthly price, while Sprout Health highlights a time-limited introductory discount followed by a higher ongoing rate.

Onboarding speed and clinician review

Both platforms use clinician review for prescriptions, but the evidence emphasizes different experiences. Refills says a licensed clinician will review intake and you may either chat with a doctor or receive a prescription within 24 hours, with shipping and tracking info in two business days. Sprout Health emphasizes lightning-quick approval and fast, hassle-free entry to GLP-1 therapy. If speed of approval is the primary concern, Sprout Health is presented as the faster, low-friction option; Refills documents a rapid clinician review process plus 24/7 health concierge support.

Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 in some form and include regulatory statements. Refills explicitly states compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness, or quality by the FDA and that compounded medications may be prescribed if deemed medically appropriate. Sprout Health likewise notes compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Refills also highlights that its compounding pharmacies are licensed, inspected, and Legitscript-certified with third-party USP testing per the captured evidence.

Support, shipping, and membership

Refills bundles free discreet shipping, express shipping options, no membership or app fees, and free 24/7 health concierge support into its flat monthly price, and says plans are month-to-month with cancel anytime terms. Sprout Health is described as fully online with fast delivery, but specific shipping, membership, and cancellation details were not verified on the captured Sprout Health page.

Which should you choose

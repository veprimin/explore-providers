Quick verdict: HermanRx offers all-inclusive monthly pricing with no membership or consultation fee and both semaglutide and tirzepatide listed at fixed monthly rates, while He & She MD emphasizes bundled coaching and clinician messaging with lower six-month introductory prices that renew higher. This comparison of HermanRx vs He & She MD focuses on pricing structure, coaching and clinician access, medication formats and availability, timeline and shipping, and regulatory notes.

View HermanRx all-inclusive GLP-1 plans

Pricing: all-inclusive flat rates versus six-month intro pricing

HermanRx lists compounded semaglutide + B12 at $249/month and compounded tirzepatide + B12 at $349/month, described as all-inclusive covering provider review, prescription, medication, ongoing support and free discreet shipping with no membership fee or separate consultation charge. He & She MD advertises semaglutide injections starting at $139/month and tirzepatide from $199/month, but those are six-month introductory rates that renew higher after the initial term. The renewal structure for He & She MD means headline prices may not reflect ongoing costs. Both providers note compounded GLP-1s are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Coaching and clinician access: bundled app versus standard telehealth support

He & She MD bundles a coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific coaching and progress tracking at no extra cost as part of the program. HermanRx provides ongoing support and a secure patient portal and 24/7 patient support, but its published evidence emphasizes clinician review and consolidated treatment categories rather than a dedicated coaching app. For patients seeking built-in coaching and tracking the evidence supplied highlights He & She MD; for those who prioritize a single all-inclusive monthly bill across multiple care categories HermanRx is presented as the consolidated option.

Medication formats, molecules, and availability

Both companies offer semaglutide and tirzepatide in compounded forms. HermanRx lists both compounded semaglutide + B12 and compounded tirzepatide + B12 as available options and states availability across five treatment categories on one platform. He & She MD lists semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection formats and highlights three formats in its program descriptions. He & She MD notes some prices shown are promotional and apply to the first six months. HermanRx states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Timeline and shipping

HermanRx publishes a roughly 5-minute assessment, about 24-hour provider review and about 72-hour delivery if prescribed, and also gives a wider 3 to 7 business day window for most orders. HermanRx emphasizes free discreet shipping in plain unmarked packaging to all 50 states. He & She MD advertises free expedited shipping and ships medication to the patient, with plans described as including expedited shipping. Both providers state no insurance is required and both reference free shipping, though HermanRx presents a specific published timeline and nationwide plain packaging messaging.

Safety and regulatory disclosures

Both providers explicitly note compounded GLP-1 medications are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HermanRx states some medications offered are compounded and not FDA-approved as finished products. He & She MD states medications included in the GLP-1 Program are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies but are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy or quality. Both require clinician review and prescription where medically appropriate.

Choosing between HermanRx and He & She MD

Choose HermanRx if you want an all-inclusive monthly price that covers provider review, medication, ongoing support and free discreet shipping with no membership or separate consultation fee, and if you value a single platform that covers multiple treatment categories. Choose He & She MD if you want built-in 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1 coaching and progress tracking in an app and are willing to start with a six-month introductory rate that renews higher.

Overview: Strut Health vs Eden Health at a glance

Strut Health markets specialized oral-focused treatment options and guided consultations with oral Semaglutide and compounded GLP-1 options. Eden Health presents clinician-reviewed, guided GLP-1 programs with monthly pricing at every dose and a lifetime price guarantee for its listed prices.

Key differences: What sets them apart

1. Oral specialization vs multi-drug monthly pricing

Strut Health highlights oral-based program options and oral Semaglutide lozenges as a core focus. Strut states “Oral Semaglutide” and emphasizes an oral specialization and guided consultations. Eden lists monthly pricing for multiple medications, naming Semaglutide at $99 and Tirzepatide at $129, and frames its offering as a clinician-guided program built around medication, care, and support.

2. Compounded medication and regulatory notes

Strut Health explicitly describes GLP-1 compounded weight management therapy with Semaglutide and states “Compounded medications are not FDA-approved” and that compounded Semaglutide is prepared by a U.S.-licensed compounding pharmacy pursuant to an individualized prescription. Eden cautions that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that “Compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.” Both sources preserve the regulatory qualifier on compounded products.

3. Pricing structure and guarantees

Strut Health lists “From $199 per Month” and landing-page claims include “No Hidden Fees” and “Free shipping” in captured evidence. Eden provides explicit monthly pricing at every dose with Semaglutide at $99 and Tirzepatide at $129 and repeatedly advertises a “Lifetime price guarantee” and “Free expedited shipping” and “No membership fees.” Pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change for Eden according to the captured notes.

4. Support model and care focus

Strut emphasizes guided consultations and ongoing progress communication tied to an oral specialization. Eden emphasizes a clinician-reviewed program that integrates medication, care, and support under a single monthly price and notes that “Most programs stop at the prescription. Eden brings medication, care, and support together under one clear monthly price.”

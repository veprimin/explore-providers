How the two platforms differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the clearest distinctions between MedVi and YourEra: upfront and ongoing price structure, medication options, plan commitment and pricing model, and shipping and support features. All claims below reflect each provider’s stated offerings and qualifiers.

Pricing and promotional structure

MedVi advertises a winter promotional first month starting at $179 including fast, free shipping, but notes the ongoing standalone month rises to $299 and a three-month plan is $649. YourEra advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/month and tirzepatide from $199/month on the 6-month plan, with the lowest advertised rates reflecting that commitment.

Medication options and formulation

MedVi offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only, with messaging about compounded GLP-1 ingredients and clinician discretion. YourEra offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide on its 6-month plan, noting both are compounded formulations and not FDA-approved finished products.

Pricing model and dose policy

YourEra emphasizes flat pricing at every dose on its 6-month plan with no dose-based price increases and bundles consult, prescription, cold shipping, dose adjustments, and 24/7 support. MedVi presents a promotional entry price that increases for ongoing months and does not offer tirzepatide; it highlights a simple online intake and no clinic visits.

Commitment, shipping and support

YourEra’s lowest rates require a six-month plan and lists free, discreet delivery in roughly 1 to 2 days from licensed US pharmacies; it also cites FSA/HSA eligibility and Klarna monthly payments. MedVi requires no clinic visits, provides 24/7 certified medical support, and includes medication and program services in its program price, with refills locked at an ongoing rate that is higher than the promo.

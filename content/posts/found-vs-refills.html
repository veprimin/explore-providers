How Found and Refills differ at a glance

Both providers offer clinician-reviewed prescriptions and may prescribe compounded GLP-1s, but they take different approaches: Found centers on a personalized, biology-first program with insurance support and variable pricing, while Refills offers flat pricing for compounded GLP-1 from $99/mo with free shipping and month-to-month plans.

1) Pricing model and starting cost

Found: Advertising shows you can get started for as low as $39 and mentions affordable monthly plans and flexible pricing. Found references specific membership starting points and examples including “Found membership starts at $49/mo when billed annually” and other listed figures, and notes that advertised pricing for branded GLP-1s may require commitments.

2) Personalized care versus flat-price simplicity

Found frames its program as personalized and medically supervised, building plans around an individual’s metabolic biology, hormones, sleep, and behavior. That biology-first approach is the core distinction Found highlights.

Refills emphasizes simplicity: personalized compounded GLP-1 delivered with flat pricing, a 3-minute intake quiz, and clinician review that may include a chat with a doctor or a direct prescription within 24 hours. Refills bundles licensed provider consultations, ongoing support, and patient care access into a single monthly price.

3) Insurance, availability, and pharmacy details

Found states “Most insurance now accepted” and says it handles insurance paperwork, including references to Medicare programs that may make brand-name GLP-1s available for as little as $50/mo for eligible Part D members. Found also cautions that insurance coverage, pricing, and terms vary and that RX is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility.

Refills states no insurance is required and promotes availability in all 50 US States. Refills also notes clinicians come from partner networks and that compounded products are offered from U.S. pharmacies with third-party testing referenced.

Regulatory and compounded medication notes

Both sources explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not evaluated by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Found specifies that compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved or evaluated and may not be available in all states. Refills likewise notes compounded drug products are not approved or evaluated by the FDA and that availability varies by state.

4) Shipping, support, and memberships

Refills highlights free discreet shipping, express shipping, and 24/7 health concierge support, with month-to-month plans that can be canceled anytime and no cancellation fees. Refills also emphasizes no monthly membership or app fees and text and email shipment updates.

Found emphasizes affordable monthly plans and cancel anytime language for membership tiers, but its captured evidence places more emphasis on insurance handling and personalized program features rather than free shipping or nonstop concierge support.

## Ro GLP-1 Review — Oral, FDA-Approved, and Injection-Free

Most of the weight-loss GLP-1 market runs on **compounded injectables**. Ro's weight-loss program is notable for a different reason: it offers access to the **oral Wegovy pill**, the first FDA-approved oral GLP-1 for chronic weight management. That single fact changes who this provider is for.

## About Ro

Ro is one of the largest full-service telehealth companies in the U.S., spanning weight loss, men's health, and more. Its weight-loss line prescribes **FDA-approved finished medications** rather than compounded blends, and it was among the first mainstream platforms to offer an oral GLP-1 at scale. Because the medication is a regulator-evaluated finished product, it carries stronger regulatory assurance than a compounded alternative — and, correspondingly, a higher cost.

## How It Works

The onboarding is deliberately streamlined: you create an account, complete an intake, and a licensed clinician reviews your case. If oral GLP-1 therapy is appropriate and available in your state, Ro prescribes it and handles fulfilment. State availability can vary, so it is worth confirming coverage before you start.

The oral route matters most for **needle-averse patients**. According to the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/), semaglutide is approved for chronic weight management, and an oral formulation removes the injection barrier that keeps some patients from starting GLP-1 therapy at all.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Ro offer the Wegovy pill?

Yes. Ro is one of the few mainstream telehealth platforms offering the oral Wegovy pill, the first and only FDA-approved oral GLP-1 for weight management — significant for patients who want to avoid injections.

### Is the Wegovy pill as effective as injections?

It is an FDA-approved oral semaglutide formulation and a legitimate, regulator-evaluated finished product. For patients who avoid GLP-1 therapy because of needle aversion, the oral option removes that barrier while keeping the same active ingredient.

### Is Ro legitimate?

Yes. Ro is a well-established telehealth platform prescribing FDA-approved medications after a licensed clinician review. Because it uses finished products rather than compounded medication, it carries stronger regulatory assurance.

## Verdict

Ro is the FDA-approved, injection-free option in a market dominated by compounded injectables. You pay more for that regulatory assurance and for the oral route, and availability depends on your state. For patients who prioritise a finished, approved medication or specifically want to avoid needles, Ro is the strongest fit here — just confirm the ongoing price before you commit.

Medication quality and verification

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications and include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. LTTL’s captured evidence specifically states medications are compounded by U.S.A. licensed pharmacies and independently verified in FDA-registered labs, with confirmation every 3–6 months to stay within ± 10% of target strength. Sprout Health notes its compounded medication uses the same active ingredient as branded products but is compounded and not an FDA-approved finished product.

Onboarding speed and user experience

Sprout Health emphasizes a lightning-quick approval process and positions itself for patients who want a fast, low-friction entry to GLP-1 therapy. LTTL’s captured affiliate landing page did not verify claims about approval speed; the source encourages confirming eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

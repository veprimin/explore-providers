Quick verdict: Directmeds vs Enhance MD for compounded GLP-1 care

Directmeds emphasizes clinician-reviewed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with medication, supplies, telehealth visits and free next-day shipping and no monthly membership fee, starting at $147; Enhance MD emphasizes very low first-month pricing (semaglutide from $49, tirzepatide from $99) and flat-rate pricing that stays the same at every dose. Early verdict: choose Directmeds if you want bundled shipping and no membership fee with month-to-month options; choose Enhance MD if you prioritize the lowest first-month cost and predictable flat-rate pricing across titration.

How Directmeds and Enhance MD differ at a glance

Directmeds and Enhance MD both offer clinician-directed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, but the comparison narrows to four distinctive differences supported by the sources: pricing structure and promotions, membership and billing approach, shipping and supplies, and compound availability and regulatory notes. We compare each side by side below.

1) Pricing structure and promotional details

Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and lists standard FAQ pricing: semaglutide $297/month and tirzepatide $399/month. The site also references a summer promotion saving $150 and a 50% first-month discount in detailed copy. Enhance MD advertises semaglutide from $49 and tirzepatide from $99 for the first month, with flat-rate pricing thereafter that stays the same at every dose. Enhance MD notes ongoing flat-rate amounts in captured details (for example semaglutide monthly rates listed in landing evidence) and the low first-month entry point is emphasized.

2) Membership, cancellation and billing approach

Directmeds: No separate monthly membership fee is advertised, month-to-month cancellation is available for month-to-month customers, and subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term. Enhance MD: operates as a direct-pay platform with flat-rate pricing that does not escalate with dose, letting patients budget without surprise increases; the low first-month prices are introductory.

3) Shipping, supplies and program inclusions

Directmeds states medication, clinician review, telehealth visits, supplies and free temperature-controlled next-day UPS/FedEx shipping are included. Enhance MD materials emphasize program features and discounts but do not list the same explicit bundled shipping and supply details in the supplied evidence.

4) Compound options and regulatory note

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide on a clinician-directed basis. Directmeds specifies injectable and sublingual compounded formats and U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies, and notes medications are not FDA-approved. Enhance MD likewise offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

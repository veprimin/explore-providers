Found vs Eden Health quickly comes down to approach and pricing. Verdict: choose Found if you want a biology-first, personalized medically supervised program that accepts many insurances and offers plans starting as low as $39; choose Eden Health if you prefer a simple, fixed monthly GLP-1 price with no insurance required and a lifetime price guarantee.

How they differ: personalization, pricing, insurance, meds, and shipping

1. Personalization and care model

Found positions itself as a biology-first, personalized medically supervised weight loss program that builds plans around hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavior. Eden Health frames its offering as a guided GLP-1 program “built around you” but the captured evidence emphasizes a one-price medication-plus-support model. The comparison shows Found highlights broader metabolic evaluation while Eden emphasizes a clear monthly medication price.

Explore Found’s personalized options

2. Pricing structure and starting cost

Found advertises affordable monthly plans and explicitly states you can get started for as low as $39. Eden Health lists monthly pricing at every dose with semaglutide for $99 and tirzepatide for $129, and says no insurance is needed with a lifetime price guarantee. These are direct contrasts: Found focuses on lower starting pricing and flexible plans; Eden focuses on fixed per-medication monthly prices.

See Eden’s GLP-1 pricing

3. Insurance and cost handling

Found states “Most insurance now accepted” and says it handles insurance paperwork, noting insurance coverage, pricing, and terms vary and Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility. Found also highlights programs that may get brand-name GLP-1s for as little as $50/mo through Medicare for eligible Part D members, with specific program criteria and limitations. Eden explicitly says no insurance required and no membership fees, positioning its prices as independent of insurance.

4. Medications offered and regulatory notes

Both providers include GLP-1 medications in their captured evidence, but the sources emphasize different details. Found lists tirzepatide and semaglutide and states some offerings include compounded GLP-1s, with a clear regulatory note that compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Eden lists semaglutide and tirzepatide pricing and notes that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate, and that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed; it also notes branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

5. Shipping, guarantees, and extras

Eden highlights free expedited shipping, no membership fees, and a lifetime price guarantee for its quoted monthly medication prices. Found emphasizes flexible pricing, cancel anytime language in bullets, and various offer terms tied to membership and insurance; specific shipping or lifetime price guarantees are not in the captured Found evidence.

Conclusion and quick recommendation

Found vs Eden Health is a choice between personalized, insurance-friendly care and straightforward fixed-price GLP-1 plans. Found emphasizes a biology-first, whole-body approach, affordable monthly plans starting as low as $39, and handling insurance paperwork. Eden guarantees set monthly prices for semaglutide ($99) and tirzepatide ($129), free expedited shipping, and no insurance requirement.

Choose Found if you want a biology-first, medically supervised weight-loss program, plans starting as low as $39, and support using insurance where available. Choose Eden Health if you prefer clear monthly GLP-1 pricing with no insurance required and a lifetime price guarantee.

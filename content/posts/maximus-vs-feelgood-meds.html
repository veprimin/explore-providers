Maximus vs FeelGood Meds: quick verdict up front. Maximus advertises a lower flat rate for compounded semaglutide at $132.99 per month and a straightforward telemedicine model, while FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections starting at $149 per month plus higher-priced tablet and branded options. Both offer compounded medications and online clinician review, and both note that compounded medications are not FDA approved.

Price and cost structure

Price is the clearest difference. Maximus promotes a low flat monthly rate of $132.99 per month for compounded semaglutide, a direct-pay model with no insurance billing. FeelGood Meds starts lower for some offerings at $149 per month for compounded injections, but also lists compounded tablets from $249 per month and a branded medication option shown from $1,999 per month. If an exact lowest monthly number is your primary factor, Maximus shows the lower listed flat rate.

Medication options and program flexibility

Maximus focuses on compounded semaglutide filled through U.S. pharmacies and does not offer branded Ozempic, Wegovy, or Zepbound according to the supplied evidence. FeelGood Meds offers compounded injections and compounded tablets and also displays a branded option at a much higher price. If you want multiple formulation choices or a branded product option, FeelGood Meds lists those options; if you want a single low-cost compounded injection program, Maximus emphasizes that approach.

Clinical process, shipping, and guarantees

Both providers use clinician review for prescriptions and operate on a telemedicine or clinician-reviewed online model. Maximus specifically notes prescriptions are approved by licensed doctors and filled through trusted U.S. pharmacies, plus a refund if not approved for treatment. FeelGood Meds highlights a refund policy and a stated weight loss money back guarantee in its landing messaging but also notes treatment availability is subject to a licensed clinician determining it is medically appropriate. Both sources reiterate that compounded medications are not FDA approved.

Availability and eligibility

Maximus states the weight loss protocol is available in all 50 states except Mississippi and offers telemedicine with no in-person visit required. FeelGood Meds emphasizes that eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability should be confirmed before enrollment and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

  • Availability notes: Maximus names one state exception; FeelGood Meds requires confirmation of state availability and terms.
  • Refund and guarantee language: Both mention refund or guarantee language but qualification by clinician review is explicit for FeelGood Meds.

Final take on Maximus vs FeelGood Meds

For a lower listed flat rate and a simple telemedicine, compounded-only model, Maximus is the lower priced option at $132.99 per month and is available in all 50 states except Mississippi. For more formulation choices and a branded option at much higher price points, FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections from $149 per month, compounded tablets from $249 per month, and a branded option from $1,999 per month, with eligibility and renewal terms that should be confirmed.

Choose Maximus if you want a straightforward, lower flat rate for compounded semaglutide with telemedicine prescribing and U.S. pharmacy fulfillment. Choose FeelGood Meds if you want multiple formulation choices or access to a branded medication option and are willing to confirm eligibility, cost, and renewal details.

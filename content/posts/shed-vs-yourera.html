Quick verdict: Shed vs YourEra favors Shed if you want integrated coaching plus the option of name-brand or compounded GLP-1; YourEra is the lower-cost, flat-priced option for compounded meds on a 6-month plan.

How Shed vs YourEra differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the source: availability of name-brand vs compounded drugs, pricing models and starting rates, integrated coaching and support, shipping and plan commitments, and regulatory status of compounded medications. Each claim below preserves source qualifiers.

1. Name-brand availability and medication options

Shed offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded alternatives on one platform, letting patients choose based on budget and preference. YourEra dispenses compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide only and does not offer brand-name Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, or Zepbound.

See YourEra compounded options

2. Pricing structure and starting rates

Shed lists compounded semaglutide starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month and promotes a limited time 10% off your first month. Shed does not publish standard pricing for every plan and provides a personalized quote after intake. YourEra advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/month and tirzepatide from $199/month on the 6-month plan; YourEra notes shorter plans cost more per month and the lowest rates reflect the 6-month commitment.

3. Coaching, bundled care, and approvals

Shed emphasizes continuous coaching integrated into treatment rather than only issuing a prescription, and uses a 2-minute approval assessment before clinician review. YourEra bundles consult, prescription, cold shipping, dose adjustments, and 24/7 support into a flat plan price and connects patients with licensed US clinicians; prescriptions are issued only if a licensed clinician determines treatment is appropriate.

Get Shed coaching and options

4. Shipping, plan commitments, and delivery

YourEra ships from licensed US pharmacies with free, discreet 1 to 2 day shipping and states medication ships to your door if treatment is appropriate. YourEra also highlights no membership fee and Klarna monthly payments. Shed indicates medication is shipped to your home after clinician approval and discusses Shed Membership as an optional deeper offering; it emphasizes coaching begins from the start of the plan.

5. Regulatory notes and what compounded means

Both providers state compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products. Shed explicitly notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a provider’s prescription. YourEra similarly states compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved finished drug products and are prepared by state-licensed US compounding pharmacies pursuant to an individual prescription.

What this means for you

If you value access to FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 options and ongoing behavioral coaching paired with medication, Shed supports both name-brand and compounded choices and integrates continuous coaching into the program. If your priority is lower advertised monthly cost for compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide and a flat price that does not change with dose, YourEra promotes lower entry pricing on a 6-month plan and bundles consult, shipping, and 24/7 support.

If you specifically want oral semaglutide tablets and FSA or HSA payment options, SkinnyRx positions its product around that format. If having options between injections, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide plus bundled coaching matters, He & She MD is the broader offering.

Clinical support and coaching

He & She MD bundles a coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1 specific guidance, and progress tracking at no extra cost. SkinnyRx emphasizes personalized care and ongoing support from licensed providers who tailor treatment and adjust doses, but does not bundle an app with 1:1 coaching in the same way He & She MD does. This difference of bundled coaching versus provider-guided prescription and follow up is a key decision point.

Shipping, payment flexibility, and testing

Both providers promote expedited shipping. SkinnyRx specifically notes free overnight shipping and FSA/HSA eligibility allowing use of pre-tax dollars. He & She MD offers free expedited shipping and no membership fee, and highlights that its prices are introductory for six month terms. SkinnyRx notes partner pharmacies perform third party testing every 3 to 6 months with checks for concentration within plus or minus 10 percent of the active ingredient. He & She MD states medications are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies but also notes compounded GLP-1s are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality.

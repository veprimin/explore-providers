Quick verdict: altRx and Embody both sell compounded GLP-1 therapies via telehealth, but Embody advertises the lowest entry price while altRx emphasizes an all-in monthly package and nationwide availability. This article compares altRx vs Embody on price, delivery speed, features, availability, and regulatory status to help you decide.

How altRx vs Embody differ on price

Price is the clearest difference. Embody lists semaglutide at $69/month and tirzepatide at $119/month. altRx advertises a limited offer with compounded semaglutide starting at $89/month and compounded tirzepatide at $149/month. Both platforms state prices are flat monthly amounts and direct-pay with no insurance required, but Embody’s published entry price is lower.

Delivery speed and shipping

Embody promotes free shipping with delivery in 1 to 2 days after clinician approval. altRx advertises free, discreet 2-day delivery and typical processing and delivery of approximately 4 to 5 business days. If delivery time is a priority, Embody advertises a faster 1-2 day arrival after approval; altRx emphasizes discreet 2-day shipping but includes a typical 4-5 business day processing window.

Features and program design

Both platforms use online clinician review and ship compounded medications from licensed pharmacies, but their feature sets differ. altRx highlights an all-inclusive monthly plan that covers medical oversight, medication, a complete treatment kit, 24/7 support, buy now pay later options, and no membership or hidden fees. Embody emphasizes a lean, no-frills model: telemed visits and prescription included, flat locked-in monthly pricing, free fast shipping, and fewer premium add-ons like integrated coaching or advanced monitoring.

Availability and state licensing

altRx states its membership is available to residents of all 50 U.S. states, with provider availability and prescribing authority varying by state and a licensed provider assigned in your state. Embody describes licensed provider review and pharmacy fulfillment but does not supply a full list of eligible states in the provided evidence. Use altRx if nationwide membership availability is a deciding factor based on the supplied claims.

Regulatory and medication status

Both altRx and Embody offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. The sources clearly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety effectiveness or quality. altRx notes that if a provider determines treatment appropriate they may prescribe compounded semaglutide or compounded tirzepatide and that prescriptions are filled by state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies in its network. Embody likewise states its compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are prescribed after a licensed provider review.

Conclusion: altRx vs Embody

Both altRx and Embody deliver clinician-prescribed, compounded GLP-1 therapies via telehealth and both note compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products. Embody is positioned as the lowest-cost, fast-delivery option. altRx positions itself as an affordable but more feature-rich all-in program with nationwide membership availability.

Choose Embody if you prioritize the lowest advertised entry price and fastest advertised post-approval delivery. Choose altRx if you prefer an all-inclusive monthly plan, stated availability across all 50 states, a full treatment kit, and buy now pay later options.

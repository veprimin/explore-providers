How they differ: medication, price structure, and approval speed

The most distinctive differences between Sprout Health and bmiMD are medication type and billing options, approval speed and onboarding friction, shipping and subscription terms, and outcome framing. Both providers offer clinician-reviewed, compounded medication only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate, and both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Medication type and outcome framing

Sprout Health promotes compounded semaglutide with explicit outcome framing: it presents a 10 to 26 percent body weight reduction over 12 months as its referenced outcome range. bmiMD offers compounded tirzepatide and does not present the same 10 to 26 percent outcome framing in the captured evidence. Both providers state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Price, discounts, and billing commitments

Sprout Health advertises a $50 off first month offer, bringing an initial cost to $199 and ongoing standard pricing at $249 per month after the discounted first month. bmiMD publishes multiple billing options: $179 month to month, $159 per month for a three month plan, $149 per month for a six month plan, and $139 per month for a 12 month plan, with the lowest monthly equivalent requiring a 12 month commitment. bmiMD also notes free shipping on every subscription order.

Approval speed, clinician access, and refills

Sprout Health emphasizes a lightning-quick approval process and a fully online, fast-entry experience suited for patients who want minimal onboarding friction. bmiMD highlights online refills and delivery with 24/7 clinician access and confirmed strength checks every 3 to 6 months, and it describes a quick turnaround for prescriptions arriving without delays in the captured copy.

Shipping, commitments, and cancellation considerations

bmiMD explicitly states free subscription shipping and that the lowest monthly price requires longer commitments, and it warns to confirm total prepaid charges. Sprout Health did not have shipping, membership, or cancellation details verified on the captured page, but it does highlight home delivery after approval and a direct-pay model that does not bill insurance.

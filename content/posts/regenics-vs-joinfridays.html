Quick comparison and verdict

Regenics vs JoinFridays quickly breaks down to a choice between Regenics’ nurse-practitioner-led telehealth with a Sermorelin add-on and promotional first-month pricing, and JoinFridays’ lower entry price, active promo codes, and cancel-anytime membership model. Verdict: Choose JoinFridays if you prioritize the lowest starting price and flexible cancellation; choose Regenics if you want a Sermorelin option and nurse-practitioner oversight alongside compounded GLP-1s.

How the two providers differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: pricing and promotions, clinical staffing and review, product mix and regulatory notes, membership and cancellation terms, and extra services like peptides or coaching. All claims below use only the supplied facts for Regenics and JoinFridays.

1. Pricing and promotional structure

Regenics advertises compounded semaglutide at $139 first month (renews at $199/mo) and tirzepatide at $189 first month (renews at $279/mo). JoinFridays advertises options starting as low as $117/month with active promo codes; standalone month-to-month and multi-month plans produce different effective rates. JoinFridays notes that longer commitments lower the effective monthly rate, and promo codes like NEWYOU12 and NYNY12 are described as aggressive but subject to change.

2. Clinical model and who reviews intake

Regenics is described as nurse-practitioner-led telehealth: licensed nurse practitioners review intake before anything is prescribed. JoinFridays emphasizes personalized, clinician-guided care with clinician review and dosing guidance. Both platforms require a licensed clinician to determine whether treatment is medically appropriate.

3. Product types, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide programs. Regenics specifies medication compounded in the USA with free shipping and a LegitScript certified pharmacy network. JoinFridays offers compounded options and also references brand-name medication options and insurance support on some plans. Both sources state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

4. Membership, cancellation, and ancillary services

JoinFridays highlights no membership fees and the ability to cancel anytime, positioning flexibility as a core feature. It includes membership in some plans at no extra fee and offers coaching plus care in certain bundles. Regenics does not list a published in-person clinic option and is telehealth only; it highlights no insurance required and FSA/HSA eligibility and includes a Sermorelin peptide therapy add-on priced separately in the FAQ.

5. Notable extras: Sermorelin vs coaching and promo codes

Regenics explicitly lists Sermorelin peptide therapy as an add-on and positions it for recovery, energy, and muscle support. JoinFridays emphasizes coaching, active promo codes, and multi-month plan structures that can reduce effective monthly cost. Promo code availability at JoinFridays is presented as aggressive but changeable; Regenics notes promotional pricing applies to the first month only and renewals are higher.

Final thoughts on Regenics vs JoinFridays

Regenics vs JoinFridays is a clear tradeoff between price and flexibility versus specific service features.

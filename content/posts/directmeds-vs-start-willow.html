Price and promotions: Directmeds vs Start Willow

Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and lists standard FAQ pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. Start Willow lists pricing from $249 per month with an ongoing rate of $299/month noted in its FAQ. Both providers emphasize no separate monthly membership fees in their landing information. The exact ongoing cost you will pay depends on the provider’s stated terms and any promotions that may apply; these are the advertised figures captured in the sources.

Shipping and speed commitments

Directmeds advertises free temperature-controlled UPS or FedEx delivery generally in 1 to 2 days and mentions free next-day shipping. Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less from its licensed pharmacy network and advertises same day prescriptions and free 2-day shipping. If delivery speed is a primary concern, Directmeds highlights next-day and 1 to 2 day shipping, while Start Willow emphasizes a 3 day or less guarantee and same day prescription fulfillment.

Medication formats, dispensing, and clinician review

Both companies offer compounded GLP-1 medication after a clinician review. Directmeds offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats and dispenses from U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. Start Willow offers a once-weekly compounded semaglutide injection described as customizable and created in collaboration with the Willow doctor. Both sources state that these are compounded medications and are not FDA-approved, and Start Willow notes its compounding pharmacies operate under state board of pharmacy oversight and USP compounding standards.

Membership, insurance, and best-fit patients

Neither Directmeds nor Start Willow advertises a monthly membership fee. Start Willow specifically promotes a direct-pay model that requires no insurance and positions itself for patients who have struggled with insurance pre-authorization, especially for branded products like Ozempic. Directmeds highlights no membership fee, month-to-month cancellation for many customers, and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. The sources identify Start Willow as the fit for patients prioritizing bypassing insurance and fast, predictable direct-pay access, while Directmeds is presented as best for patients seeking compounded injectable or sublingual GLP-1 care with transparent standard pricing and no separate membership fee.

Geographic and regulatory notes

Directmeds is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana according to its captured landing information. Both providers include a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Start Willow adds that its compounding pharmacies meet state oversight and USP standards; Directmeds specifies U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies.

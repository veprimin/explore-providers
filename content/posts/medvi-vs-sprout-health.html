MedVi vs Sprout Health: how they differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on five key differences supported by source evidence: introductory pricing and ongoing cost, approval and onboarding speed, medication type and regulatory notes, support and care model, and outcome framing and reported results. Both providers operate online and may offer compounded semaglutide, but their positioning and specifics differ.

1. Introductory price and ongoing cost

MedVi advertises a winter promotional first month starting at $179 including fast, free shipping. MedVi’s ongoing standalone month is listed at $299, and a three-month plan is available at $649 (about $216/month). Sprout Health advertises $50 off the first month, bringing the initial cost to $199, with ongoing pricing at $249/month. All price statements are promotional or subject to the provider terms documented on their pages.

2. Approval, onboarding, and process speed

MedVi emphasizes a simple online intake with no clinic visits needed and a licensed clinician review after checkout; medication ships after review. Sprout Health emphasizes lightning-quick approval and fast-entry onboarding, framing itself for patients who want minimal friction to start therapy. Both operate as telehealth platforms and require clinician review to determine medical appropriateness.

3. Medication type and regulatory notes

Both sources note compounded semaglutide is offered. MedVi specifically states its semaglutide is compounded and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Sprout Health includes a similar regulatory reminder that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Neither provider claims compounded products are interchangeable with branded finished FDA-approved products.

4. Support, services, and care model

MedVi highlights 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and a no-clinic online process. MedVi states its program covers physician review, a personalized plan, 1:1 guidance, a metabolic report, and the medication shipped to your door. Sprout Health highlights a fast, safe, hassle-free delivery model and rapid prescription review, focusing on quick approval rather than continuous 24/7 support in the captured evidence.

5. Outcome framing and reported results

Sprout Health frames outcomes as losing 10 to 26 percent of body weight in 12 months, citing GLP-1 clinical outcome literature and noting individual results vary. MedVi presents self-reported patient data and states that MedVi patients typically result in 1 to 2 pounds per week weight loss after 4 weeks when following diet and exercise changes, while also cautioning results vary and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

## The Problem With How ED Has Been Treated for the Past 20 Years

To understand what RODEO is doing differently, it helps to understand the limitations of what came before it.

Sildenafil and tadalafil — the active ingredients in Viagra and Cialis respectively — belong to a class of drugs called PDE5 inhibitors. They work by blocking an enzyme that restricts blood flow, allowing the blood vessels that supply penile tissue to dilate and enabling erections in response to sexual stimulation.

They are effective medications. The clinical evidence behind them is substantial and well-established. But they have two significant limitations that have gone largely unaddressed by the telehealth industry.

The first limitation is physiological. PDE5 inhibitors work exclusively through a vascular pathway. They do not address the neurological or psychological dimensions of sexual arousal. For men whose erectile dysfunction has any degree of central nervous system involvement — stress, anxiety, depression, performance pressure, or low baseline arousal — a PDE5 inhibitor alone is an incomplete solution. Research has consistently shown that anywhere from 30 to 40 percent of men who try standard PDE5 inhibitor therapy do not achieve satisfactory results. That is a large and historically underserved population.

The second limitation is pharmacokinetic. Standard oral sildenafil and tadalafil tablets must be swallowed, digested, and metabolized through the liver before they reach systemic circulation. That process takes time — typically 30 minutes to an hour — and it reduces the amount of active compound that actually makes it into the bloodstream. For many men, this timing window creates practical challenges that erode the real-world usefulness of the medication.

RODEO was built to solve both problems simultaneously.

RODEO’s formulation combines sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil, and apomorphine in a single sublingual dose — four compounds, three of them PDE5 inhibitors working at different speeds, plus one that acts on the brain rather than on blood flow. Here is what each one contributes.

**Vardenafil** is the third PDE5 inhibitor in the stack, best known as the active ingredient in Levitra. It sits between sildenafil and tadalafil on potency and onset, and many men report feeling it begin to work within 10 to 15 minutes. Its role in the formula is to sharpen the front end of the response rather than to extend it.

**Apomorphine** is the ingredient that distinguishes RODEO from the mainstream generics. A dopamine receptor agonist with a history of use in neurological medicine, apomorphine has been studied for its ability to activate arousal-related pathways in the central nervous system. Unlike PDE5 inhibitors, which act peripherally on vascular smooth muscle, apomorphine acts centrally — engaging the brain’s own arousal mechanisms rather than simply facilitating blood flow once arousal has already occurred.

For men whose ED has psychological, stress-related, or neurochemical components — a group that represents a substantial and frequently overlooked segment of the ED population — apomorphine addresses a dimension of the problem that a vascular-only treatment leaves untouched. ## Safety Information and Clinical Considerations

RODEO is a prescription medication and carries a profile of potential side effects and contraindications that prospective users should review carefully.

Side effects reported in association with RODEO’s active compounds include headache, skin flushing, rash, mild blurred vision, cyanopsia (a temporary blue-tinted visual disturbance associated with sildenafil), indigestion, nasal congestion, nausea, back pain, muscle soreness, dizziness, drowsiness, and mild irritation of the mouth and tongue consistent with sublingual drug delivery.

RODEO is contraindicated for men taking nitrate medications for cardiovascular conditions, as the combination with PDE5 inhibitors can produce clinically significant reductions in blood pressure. Men with liver or kidney disease should discuss use with their physician, as prolonged drug retention is possible. Certain other medications may interact with RODEO’s compounds to extend effects or produce adverse outcomes.

As a compounded prescription product, RODEO has not received individual FDA approval. The FDA does not evaluate the safety or efficacy of compounded medications. RODEO is available by prescription only, following review and approval by a licensed physician through the platform’s telehealth intake process.

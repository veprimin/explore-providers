Both providers offer compounded medications and note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. MedVi offers compounded semaglutide only, with a promotional first-month price of $179 and ongoing options including a standalone month at $299 or a three-month plan at $649. Measured offers promotional compounded semaglutide from $89/month and compounded tirzepatide from $189/month, with free two-day shipping and same-day prescription decisions.

2. Pricing structure and promotional details

3. Speed of prescription and shipping

Measured advertises same-day prescription decisions and free two-day shipping on every order. MedVi advertises fast, free shipping but does not claim same-day Rx decisions; MedVi emphasizes a fully online intake with clinician review after checkout and clinician discretion on prescriptions.

4. Support and care model

MedVi promotes 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and a simple online process with no clinic visits. Measured highlights unlimited messaging, a 24/7 care team, and dietitian support. Both platforms state Rx is issued at the provider’s discretion and that eligibility is determined by clinician review.

5. Outcome claims and evidence

Measured cites internal analysis where members reporting weight data averaged 31 pounds lost at six months, noting individual results vary and are not guaranteed and that internal outcome claims are not independent evidence. MedVi reports patient-reported averages such as 1 to 2 pounds per week after four weeks for patients following diet and exercise recommendations and states results vary; both preserve the qualifier that outcomes are not guaranteed.

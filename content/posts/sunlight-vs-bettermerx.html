Sunlight vs BetterMe Rx: quick verdict. If your priority is the lowest advertised starting price and a fixed monthly rate, Sunlight advertises personalized GLP-1 plans starting at $88/mo with a “same price forever” claim. If you want multiple compounded formats with lower long-term equivalents and explicit notice that compounded meds are not FDA approved, BetterMe Rx offers injectable and oral compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with 12-month equivalents as low as $145/mo. This article compares the two directly across pricing structure, medication format and regulatory status, shipping and billing terms, and clinician and compounding notes.

Sunlight vs BetterMe Rx: pricing structure and what “starting” means

Sunlight lists Personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo and highlights “Same price forever” and no monthly membership. BetterMe Rx shows monthly prices and lower 12-month equivalents: semaglutide injection from $145/mo on a 12-month plan, tirzepatide injection from $215/mo on a 12-month plan, and monthly prices shown as $205, $275, $250 and $265 respectively. Key difference: Sunlight promotes a single low starting price and a claim of price stability, while BetterMe Rx advertises lower monthly-equivalent pricing only when customers choose longer prepaid plans.

Medication formats and options

Both providers list injectable and oral options. Sunlight advertises injections and oral options without captured detail on specific branded or compounded status. BetterMe Rx explicitly offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and oral formats, including a needle-free tirzepatide tablet option and notes that the products are compounded.

Regulatory and clinician review differences

BetterMe Rx is explicit that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. BetterMe Rx states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that a provider review and pharmacy compounding are included. Sunlight includes “prescription and telehealth included” and a 100% online medical visit, but the captured evidence adds a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Neither source provides a captured complete list of which specific branded or compounded products are used for each patient, so verify medication approval and interchangeability with the provider.

Shipping, supplies and billing terms

Sunlight’s captured landing-page evidence highlights free shipping in 1-2 days, fast and free shipping, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime language. BetterMe Rx includes free 1-2 day delivery, supplies included, no membership fee, and cancel anytime, with the caveat that lowest prices require longer prepaid plans and longer plans are billed according to the selected term.

## MedVi GLP-1 Review — Semaglutide, Simply

MedVi is a telehealth platform offering **compounded semaglutide** for weight loss through a simple online process with 24/7 clinical support. It is a semaglutide-only provider — no tirzepatide option — which keeps things simple but narrows who it suits.

> This review covers MedVi's **GLP-1 weight-loss** program. MedVi separately markets a men's-health "Quad" ED product and a meal service; those are different offerings and are not covered here.

## About MedVi

MedVi prescribes **clinically dosed compounded semaglutide** — the same active ingredient as Ozempic and Wegovy, prepared by a licensed pharmacy but not an FDA-approved finished product. The intake is entirely online, there are no clinic visits, and support from certified medical professionals is available around the clock. The pitch is a low-friction on-ramp to semaglutide with a strong support layer.

## How It Works

You complete a simple intake form, a licensed clinician reviews your case, and — if eligible — compounded semaglutide is shipped to your door. No in-person visits or lab appointments are required at any stage. Ongoing questions about dosing or side effects are handled through 24/7 support.

Semaglutide is FDA-approved for chronic weight management, and the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/) label and clinical evidence base support its use under medical supervision — which is the model MedVi operates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does MedVi require a clinic visit?

No. The entire process is online — you complete an intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and if eligible your compounded semaglutide is shipped. No in-person visits are needed.

### Does the MedVi price go up after the first month?

Yes. The low rate is a promotional first-month price, and the ongoing monthly cost is meaningfully higher. Confirm the renewal price before committing.

### Is MedVi legitimate?

Yes. It is a licensed telehealth platform prescribing clinically dosed compounded semaglutide after an online clinician review, with 24/7 support. The medication is compounded rather than FDA-approved but uses the same active ingredient as Ozempic and Wegovy.

## Verdict

MedVi is a straightforward, well-supported way into compounded semaglutide, with a genuinely low first-month entry point. Two limits define it: semaglutide only, and a notable jump to the ongoing rate after the promo. For a patient who wants semaglutide with strong support and will verify the renewal price first, it is a reasonable choice.

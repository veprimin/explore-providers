Quick verdict: MedVi vs Eden Health

MedVi vs Eden Health comes down to tradeoffs: MedVi highlights a winter promotional starting price of $179 for compounded semaglutide with a fully online process and 24/7 certified medical support, while Eden Health advertises lower monthly pricing for semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129 plus a lifetime price guarantee and free expedited shipping. Early verdict: pick MedVi if you prioritize a no-clinic, clinically dosed compounded semaglutide program with round the clock support; pick Eden Health if you prioritize lower advertised monthly pricing and a lifetime price guarantee where available.

How pricing and ongoing cost compare

Both providers present clear monthly numbers but with different structures. MedVi promotes a winter promotional first month at $179 including fast, free shipping, while noting the ongoing standalone month is $299 and a three-month plan is $649 (about $216/month). Eden Health shows monthly pricing at every dose with semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129 and states a lifetime price guarantee and free expedited shipping. The sources show Eden advertises lower ongoing monthly pricing and an explicit lifetime guarantee, while MedVi emphasizes a promotional first-month price and higher ongoing rates.

Medication type, formulation, and regulatory notes

MedVi offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide and states compounded medications are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety, effectiveness or quality. MedVi also notes it prescribes semaglutide only and does not offer tirzepatide. Eden Health publishes pricing for both semaglutide and tirzepatide and warns that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate, and that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed. Both sources emphasize that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable and when compounded medications are offered they are not FDA-approved.

Care model, support, and access

MedVi positions its model as fully online with no clinic visits required, a simple intake form, clinician review after checkout, and 24/7 support from certified medical professionals. MedVi states medication is shipped to the door from licensed pharmacies and that OpenLoop Health clinicians retain prescribing discretion. Eden Health describes a doctor-guided online program that brings medication, care, and support together under one monthly price, but the captured evidence does not publish detailed enrollment steps or the level of aftercare available on the landing-page capture.

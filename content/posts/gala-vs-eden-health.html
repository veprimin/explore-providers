Quick verdict: Gala vs Eden Health

The key difference in Gala vs Eden Health is pricing model and medication format: Gala advertises a flat all-inclusive direct-pay plan with compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and a $220 promotion, while Eden Health lists monthly pricing at every dose (Semaglutide $99, Tirzepatide $129) with a lifetime price guarantee. Treatment availability is subject to clinician review for both.

Gala presents a direct-pay telehealth model with provider check-ins and dose monitoring and includes the provider visit and prescription in its flat price. Eden frames a clinician-reviewed, guided GLP-1 program that brings medication, care, and support together under one monthly price. The evidence shows Gala highlights monitoring and included visit costs, while Eden emphasizes an integrated guided program with ongoing support.

Shipping, membership, and cancellation

Both providers advertise expedited shipping: Gala states free 2-day shipping to your door and no monthly membership with cancel anytime language; Eden states free expedited shipping, no insurance needed, and no membership fees.

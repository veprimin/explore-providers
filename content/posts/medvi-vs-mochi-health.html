Pricing and membership structure compared

Clinical interaction and care delivery

MedVi uses a simple online intake and a licensed clinician review without in-person visits. Its materials note OpenLoop Health clinicians meet with individuals after checkout and retain prescribing discretion. Mochi Health emphasizes synchronous live video visits with a doctor typically bookable in about 5 minutes, allowing real-time discussion of history and treatment. Both platforms state prescriptions are at provider discretion and both supply 24/7 support.

Medication range and regulatory considerations

Mochi Health explicitly lists multiple compounded options including semaglutide injections, a compounded semaglutide pill, and compounded tirzepatide, plus other compound innovations. MedVi competes on clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only and does not offer tirzepatide in the supplied evidence. Both providers clearly state that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality, and both note that prescriptions are subject to clinician review and discretion.

Outcomes, support, and limitations

MedVi presents self-reported data and notes variability in results, sharing that MEDVi patients typically report 1 to 2 pounds per week after 4 weeks under certain conditions, while emphasizing results vary and are not guaranteed. Mochi Health also cautions that individual results are not guaranteed and provides an illustrative estimated result based on self reported member averages. Neither provider offers a published guarantee of specific outcomes in the supplied evidence.

Pricing models and what they mean for you

Sesame Care uses a marketplace model with subscription care “as low as $59/mo” for ongoing services and cash-pay medication examples starting at $149/mo for semaglutide and $299 for tirzepatide, and notes that medication costs vary by insurance status and pharmacy. TMates advertises a flat $199/month price that stays the same as dosage escalates, with free shipping included. TMates states it does not bill insurance and operates a direct-pay model for compounded semaglutide.

Clinician access and program structure

Sesame Care emphasizes choice: you can browse and select clinicians based on specialty, availability, and pricing, which means experience can vary by clinician. TMates emphasizes continuous physician availability, advertising 24/7 U.S.-based doctor access and virtual check-ins throughout the program. If immediate physician availability between check-ins is a priority, TMates documents that as a distinct feature.

Medication options and regulatory notes

Sesame Care lists branded medications and price ranges including semaglutide and tirzepatide products and notes that medication costs are not included in the subscription and will vary with insurance. It also states that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. TMates highlights clinically dosed compounded semaglutide formulated in U.S. facilities, and the source explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Final take on Sesame Care vs TMates

Sesame Care vs TMates splits along choice versus consistency. Sesame Care suits people who want broad telehealth choice, flexible scheduling, and the option to use insurance for branded medications. TMates suits people who want a dependable, flat $199/month compounded semaglutide program with 24/7 U.S. doctor access and consistent pricing at every dose. Choose Sesame Care if you want to browse a large clinician network, flexible appointments, and the option to pursue branded GLP-1 medications with insurance. Choose TMates if you prefer a simple flat-rate compounded semaglutide plan with 24/7 U.S. doctor access and same pricing at every dose.

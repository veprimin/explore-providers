Medication options and regulatory notes

Pallas Health: The captured evidence states Pallas offers FDA-approved Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound and Mounjaro, plus compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared in the USA when a brand medication is not the right fit. The source includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Pricing transparency and renewal structure

Pallas Health supplies specific pricing: compounded semaglutide is $139 for the first month, then $597 every 12 weeks, a $199/month average; compounded tirzepatide is $179 for the first month, then $897 every 12 weeks, a $299/month average. Pallas also lists cash-pay brand prices on the captured page and notes prices can change and to confirm figures before ordering. The source clarifies that compounded plans are priced by medication rather than by milligram so titrating up does not raise the plan price and that plans renew every 12 weeks for compounded options.

Lemonaid Health: The captured landing-page evidence did not verify starting price, price range, renewal terms or whether pricing is cash-pay or billable to insurance. The source advises confirming eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

Clinician review, eligibility and care model

Pallas describes provider care as included in an all-in plan price with ongoing check-ins and care-team messaging, no membership fee, and asynchronous care in most states with video required in seven states plus Washington, D.C. and a clinician-determined eligibility statement. The source notes care-team hours are weekdays 9 AM to 5 PM ET and no emergency services are offered.

Lemonaid Health: The captured evidence states treatment availability is subject to a clinician determining it is medically appropriate. No further details about care model, messaging, check-ins or state-specific video requirements were captured.

Shipping, enrollment and cancellation notes

It also notes compounded plans renew every 12 weeks rather than monthly, which affects cancellation timing.

Lemonaid Health: Shipping, fees, membership and cancellation details were not verified on the captured landing page. The captured summary cautions that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

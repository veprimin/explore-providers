Regenics vs Measured: both provide telehealth access to compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, but they differ in promotional price, shipping speed, and clinical support. Early verdict: Measured advertises a lower promotional semaglutide price from $89 and free two day shipping, while Regenics emphasizes nurse-practitioner oversight, a Sermorelin add-on, and semaglutide starting at $139 for the first month.

Direct head to head on the most distinctive differences

This comparison focuses on the most supported distinctions between Regenics and Measured: promotional starting price, shipping and delivery speed, clinical model and add-ons, messaging and support features, and regulatory safety notes about compounding. Each point below compares both providers directly using only supplied facts.

1. Promotional starting price and renewal details

Measured advertises compounded semaglutide from $89 per month as a promotional offer and lists tirzepatide at $189 in promotions. Regenics lists semaglutide starting at $139 for the first month and tirzepatide at $189 first month, with renewals noted at $199 for semaglutide and $279 for tirzepatide. Regenics explicitly states promotional pricing applies to the first month only; Measured warns promotional pricing may change.

2. Shipping speed and delivery promises

Measured offers free two day shipping on every order and describes free 2 day shipping as a site feature. Regenics offers free shipping with medication compounded in the USA, but the captured page does not specify a guaranteed delivery timeframe. If fast delivery is a priority, Measured explicitly promotes two day shipping.

3. Clinical model, oversight, and support features

Regenics delivers nurse-practitioner-led GLP-1 care; licensed nurse practitioners review intake before prescriptions and oversee every prescription. Regenics also offers a Sermorelin peptide therapy add-on and describes medications as prepared by U.S. licensed compounding pharmacies in a LegitScript certified pharmacy network. Measured pairs compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with same-day prescription decisions, unlimited provider messaging, and dietitian support, and highlights 24/7 Care Team access and unlimited messaging. Measured also cites same-day Rx decisions as a differentiator.

4. Outcome and evidence claims

Measured includes an internal claim that members reporting weight data averaged 31 pounds lost at six months, and a marketing line stating Lose 30+ lbs in 6 months. The source notes individual results vary and are not guaranteed and that this internal outcome claim is not independent evidence. Regenics does not provide independent outcome figures in the supplied evidence; its materials focus on medication options and clinical oversight.

5. Regulatory and compounded medication notes

Both providers offer compounded medications and both include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Regenics states compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved and not reviewed by FDA. Measured includes the same regulatory note and additionally notes Rx required and that prescriptions are issued at provider discretion.

Conclusion: Regenics vs Measured

Regenics vs Measured trade off different strengths. Measured is the lower promotional price point for semaglutide from $89 and explicitly offers free two day shipping plus same-day Rx decisions and dietitian support. Regenics starts semaglutide at $139 first month, emphasizes nurse-practitioner oversight, a LegitScript certified compounding network, and a Sermorelin add-on. Both note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Choose Regenics if you want nurse-practitioner oversight, a Sermorelin peptide option, and compounded medications from a U.S. pharmacy network described as LegitScript certified. Choose Measured if a lower promotional semaglutide price and free two day shipping with same-day prescription decisions and dietitian support are more important to you.

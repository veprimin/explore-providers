Pricing structure: prepaid long-term plans vs six-month promos

BetterMe Rx advertises lower monthly-equivalent pricing when customers select longer supplies, specifically noting a compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan. Examples include semaglutide injection $205/month or as low as $145/month on a 12-month plan and tirzepatide injection $275/month or as low as $215/month. He & She MD promotes semaglutide injections starting at $139/month and other formats at $199/month as six-month introductory rates that renew higher after the initial term. That contrast matters if you prefer a guaranteed longer-term per-month price versus an intro promotional rate that increases at renewal.

Compare BetterMe Rx formats and shipping

Medication formats and logistics

BetterMe Rx offers both injectable and oral compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, including needle-free daily tirzepatide tablets noted as uncommon in cash-pay markets. He & She MD lists semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide and tirzepatide injection. Both providers include expedited or free quick shipping in their descriptions, with BetterMe Rx specifying free 1-2 day shipping and He & She MD offering free expedited shipping.

Regulatory and qualification notes

Both sources underline that compounded GLP-1 medications are not FDA-reviewed for safety, effectiveness or quality. BetterMe Rx states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA. He & She MD notes the medications are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies but also not reviewed by the FDA. Both require clinician consultation and not all customers will qualify for a prescription when a licensed clinician determines treatment is appropriate.

Bottom line and who should choose which

BetterMe Rx vs He & She MD comes down to whether you prioritize lower long-term monthly equivalents and multiple administration formats or built-in coaching and clinician messaging with promotional intro pricing. BetterMe Rx is best for patients comparing injectable and needle-free compounded GLP-1 options and who are comfortable with prepaid multi-month billing and compounded medications that are not FDA-approved. He & She MD suits patients who want coaching, 1:1 clinician messaging and tracking included and who value a competitive six-month introductory rate that renews higher.

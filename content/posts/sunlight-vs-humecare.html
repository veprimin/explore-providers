Price and value: Sunlight vs HumeCare+

Price is the clearest split. Sunlight markets personalized GLP-1 plans starting at $88/mo and advertises a same-price-forever policy. HumeCare+ lists semaglutide from $199/mo and a tirzepatide protocol from $299/mo, with its pricing presented as promotional and cash-pay only. Protocol and clinical support

Sunlight emphasizes a 100% online medical visit with prescription and telehealth included, offering injections and oral options. HumeCare+ sells a bundled protocol: physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide plus glycine and vitamins B6 and B12, an app, and continuous clinician optimization. HumeCare+ highlights monthly body-composition scans to track lean mass versus fat, which it frames as addressing a GLP-1 limitation.

Compounded medications and regulatory considerations

HumeCare+ explicitly offers compounded GLP-1 formulations and notes that compounded medications are not evaluated by the FDA for safety, quality, or efficacy. Sunlight materials state medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and present prescription options; they do not claim compounded status. If regulatory review or branded approvals are a concern, HumeCare+ includes a clear regulatory qualifier about compounding.

Delivery, membership, and refund policies

Both vendors advertise fast shipping and cancel-anytime policies. Sunlight promotes free shipping in 1-2 days, no monthly membership, and cancel anytime language. HumeCare+ offers free next-day delivery and a 10%-or-your-money-back promise over six months, with a note to verify full terms and conditions and eligibility before enrolling. HumeCare+ also states the money-back promise has terms that should be confirmed directly with the company.

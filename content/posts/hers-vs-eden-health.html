Hers vs Eden Health compares two online GLP-1 weight-loss options: Hers emphasizes FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy® starting at $149/mo with a required membership, while Eden Health advertises semaglutide at $99/mo with no membership fees and a lifetime price guarantee. Quick verdict: choose Hers for access to FDA-approved, brand-name Wegovy® with a dedicated care team; choose Eden Health for lower listed monthly prices and no membership commitment.

Explore Hers Wegovy® options

How pricing and membership differ

Hers lists Wegovy® pill from $149/mo and injectable options from $199/mo, but notes an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required, with the first month at $39 and auto-renewal at $149/month thereafter. Price statements include the qualifier that “Price includes medication only, if prescribed” and that medication is not available without an active membership. Eden Health lists monthly pricing at every dose with semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129, and states no membership fees, no insurance required, free expedited shipping, and a lifetime price guarantee. Both providers note prescriptions and eligibility are subject to clinician review.

Medication types and regulatory notes

Both providers offer GLP-1 medications but present different product approaches. Hers specifically promotes FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy® and states it does not offer compounded medications. The Hers landing-page evidence emphasizes brand-name, FDA-approved options and reiterates that Ozempic® and Mounjaro® are FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes and may be prescribed off-label for weight loss at a clinician’s discretion. Eden Health presents semaglutide and tirzepatide pricing and notes that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Treatment for both is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Care model and support

Hers highlights a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins as part of its model. Eden Health frames its offering as a “guided GLP-1 program” bringing medication, care, and support together under one monthly price, and says most programs stop at the prescription but Eden brings medication, care, and support together under one clear monthly price. Both indicate no insurance is required; Hers emphasizes a care team and dose check-ins, while Eden emphasizes a bundled monthly program with support included at the listed price.

Availability and limitations to note

Hers notes that some branded products may be available only to patients who meet clinical eligibility criteria and that services are not available in all 50 states. Hers also reiterates that compounded medications, when offered elsewhere, are not FDA-approved. Eden cautions that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change and that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed.

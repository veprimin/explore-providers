How SkinnyRx and Sesame Care differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on five distinctive differences supported by the source: product format and regulatory notes, pricing and payment options, clinician and marketplace model, shipping and fulfillment, and transparency about medication status and testing.

Product format and regulatory status

SkinnyRx offers daily compounded semaglutide tablets described as an oral form of the medication used in GLP-1 injections. The provider notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that compounded semaglutide has not been approved by the FDA for weight loss, with partner pharmacies performing third-party testing every 3 to 6 months to confirm concentration within plus or minus 10 percent. Sesame Care operates as a telehealth marketplace that offers access to branded oral and injectable GLP-1 medications, including a first-ever oral GLP-1 pill and injectable pens, with medication-specific approval status to be confirmed per product.

Pricing, payment, and financial flexibility

Direct comparison: SkinnyRx lists pricing as low as $249/mo and highlights FSA/HSA eligibility and the option to split payments with Affirm Buy Now Pay Later. Sesame Care lists subscription services for ongoing visits as low as $59/mo and cash-pay medication starting at $149/mo, and notes that insurance is accepted for weight loss medications and that medication costs vary based on insurance status and pharmacy. Sesame Care also presents cash pay and in-network price examples showing lower in-network pricing as low as $25 for some medications.

Clinician relationship and care model

SkinnyRx offers clinician-guided treatment where licensed providers prescribe compounded semaglutide after an online consultation and monitor progress, with a refill form each month to continue medication. Sesame Care is a marketplace that lets patients browse and select clinicians by specialty, availability, and pricing, giving patients choice across a large clinician network and flexible scheduling. The source for Sesame Care notes that experience can vary by clinician.

Shipping, fulfillment, and access

SkinnyRx advertises free overnight shipping on all orders and says after prescription completion customers receive shipping and tracking information within 2 business days. Sesame Care does not advertise a universal shipping speed in the captured evidence; it focuses on clinician choice, appointment flexibility, and that medication costs and fulfillment depend on insurance and pharmacy.

Transparency and testing

SkinnyRx documents third-party testing by partner pharmacies in FDA- and DEA-compliant labs for compounded lots every 3 to 6 months and states that compounded drugs are not FDA-reviewed for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Sesame Care emphasizes treatment coverage options and that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that costs vary by insurance.

JoinFridays vs Telos Rx: how they compare on core differences

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences: starting price and pricing structure, availability of compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide, membership and cancellation terms, promo or offer patterns, and the clinician review and regulatory notes that apply to both.

1. Starting price and pricing structure

JoinFridays advertises programs starting at $117/month and lists a standalone month at $249 with multi month plans reducing the effective monthly rate. JoinFridays states tirzepatide programs can be as low as $198/month and shows a 12 month plan averaging $150/month. Telos Rx presents semaglutide as low as $99 per month and tirzepatide from $139 per month, with messaging that your price depends on your plan and that you pay $0 if not approved. Both providers include cancel anytime language.

2. Medication options and compounded status

Both platforms offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. JoinFridays highlights both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide on one platform and notes that its compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Telos Rx also states semaglutide and tirzepatide are compounded medications, prescription only, and are not FDA approved. JoinFridays additionally presents branded name brand pricing for semaglutide and tirzepatide as separate options on its site, while Telos Rx focuses on compounded offerings in the captured evidence.

3. Membership, cancellation and clinician guidance

JoinFridays promotes no membership fees, cancel anytime and emphasizes personalized, clinician guided care and dosing throughout programs. Telos Rx uses similar cancel anytime language and states treatment is prescribed online after review by a US licensed provider. Both make clear that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications are prescription only.

4. Promo codes, verifiable offers, and pricing notes

JoinFridays documents active promo codes and lists examples: NEWYOU12 for up to $500 off tirzepatide and NYNY12 for up to $400 off semaglutide, and describes aggressive promo availability that can change. Telos Rx captured evidence in this comparison does not show specific promo codes but highlights pricing floors and a promise to pay $0 if not approved in some messaging. The evidence for JoinFridays shows multi month plans that reduce effective rates; Telos Rx messaging repeatedly notes final price depends on plan and provider approval.

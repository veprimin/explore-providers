How they differ at a glance

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive supported differences between Start Willow and Maximus: price and ongoing cost, shipping speed and fulfillment source, program size and transparency, compounded medication regulatory notes, and clinician approval process.

Price and ongoing cost

Start Willow lists a first-month price from $249 and an ongoing rate of $299 per month. The source notes the $299 ongoing rate is on the higher end compared with several other platforms. Maximus advertises a low flat rate at $132.99 per month, positioning it as one of the lower-cost direct-pay options on this list.

Shipping speed and pharmacy sourcing

Start Willow guarantees delivery in 3 days or less from its licensed pharmacy network and cites same-day prescriptions and free 2-day shipping for in-stock items. Maximus provides free priority or expedited shipping and emphasizes that prescriptions are filled through trusted U.S. pharmacies. If rapid fulfillment is a top priority, Start Willow’s explicit 3-day guarantee is a supported advantage. If domestic pharmacy sourcing and a low monthly rate matter most, Maximus highlights U.S. pharmacies and free priority shipping.

Clinician approval, program model, and coverage

Both platforms operate a direct-pay telemedicine model that does not require insurance or membership fees. Start Willow frames its model as a way to bypass complex insurance prior-authorization processes and states a licensed clinician approves prescriptions after online intake. Maximus also uses licensed doctors for prescription approval via telemedicine and notes if you are not approved for treatment your medication costs will be fully refunded. Neither provider bills insurance according to the sources.

Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both Start Willow and Maximus offer compounded semaglutide formulations rather than branded finished products. Start Willow explicitly states its compounded semaglutide is not FDA approved and that the FDA does not review compounded medications for safety, effectiveness, or quality; they are prepared by state licensed compounding pharmacies under state board oversight meeting USP compounding standards. Maximus likewise notes its medication is compounded and not an FDA approved finished product, while sourcing from trusted U.S. pharmacies. These regulatory qualifications are important for patients to consider.

Side effects, patient experience, and program details

Start Willow includes patient information about common side effects such as nausea and guidance about tapering off medication with a physician to manage weight changes. Start Willow markets additional support like written and video injection instructions and customer service responsiveness cited in testimonials. Maximus highlights telemedicine access, licensed doctor approvals, and says participants in semaglutide studies experienced average weight loss in cited research summaries; the provider materials also note state availability in all 50 states except Mississippi and custom titration options unless using a fixed 90 day starter pack.

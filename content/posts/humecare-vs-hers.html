Medication type and regulatory status: compounded protocol vs FDA-approved

HumeCare+ offers physician-prescribed compounded semaglutide from $199/month and a tirzepatide protocol from $299/month, noting that compounded medications are not FDA-evaluated for safety, quality, or efficacy. Hers emphasizes FDA-approved, brand-name Wegovy® available from $149/month and states it does not offer compounded products. This is one of the clearest differences in the HumeCare+ vs Hers comparison: HumeCare+ uses compounded formulations with added ingredients; Hers provides FDA-approved brand medications.

Pricing and membership: upfront costs and billing structure

Both providers publish starting prices but with different billing models. HumeCare+ lists semaglutide starting at $199/mo and tirzepatide protocol at $299/mo; pricing is cash-pay and reflects a promotional discount that may change. Hers lists Wegovy® from $149/mo but requires an active Hers Weight Loss Membership; the membership fee is billed separately and medication is not available without it. The membership model and separate billing at Hers is a central factor when comparing total monthly cost.

Provider access, delivery, and cancellations

HumeCare+ advertises unlimited clinician access, a U.S. care team available 24/7, free next-day delivery, and a cancel-anytime policy. Hers highlights a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins and also offers cancel-anytime terms, with availability and prescription subject to clinician review and state limits. Both require a licensed clinician to determine medical appropriateness.

Support features and outcome claims: scans, supplements, and guarantees

HumeCare+ bundles glycine and vitamins B6 and B12 into its GLP-1 formulation for what it describes as muscle-preserving support and side-effect relief, and it provides monthly body-composition scans to track fat versus lean mass over time. Hers focuses on FDA-approved Wegovy® with a personalized care team and ongoing dose check-ins but does not offer compounded supportive ingredients or the same body-composition scan feature in the captured evidence.

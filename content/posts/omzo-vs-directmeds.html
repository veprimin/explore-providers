Omzo vs Directmeds comes down to clinician-guided compounded care and how each platform handles pricing, formats, shipping, and policies. Quick verdict: Omzo emphasizes clinician-guided plans with compounded options starting at $179 and the possibility of prescriptions for branded medications, while Direct Meds highlights lower promotional pricing starting at $147, injectable and sublingual formats, and free next-day shipping.

Head-to-head: Pricing and pricing structure

Both providers publish starting prices but present them differently. Omzo lists personalized compounded semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279 as part of clinician-guided plans. Direct Meds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and notes standard FAQ pricing of $297 per month for semaglutide and $399 per month for tirzepatide. Direct Meds also states Summer promotion savings and a FAQ that gives higher standard rates; Omzo’s pricing is presented as program starting prices. The comparison shows Direct Meds may use a promotional headline while Omzo emphasizes clinician-guided starting fees.

Medication formats, prescribing, and pharmacy model

Omzo connects users with licensed U.S. providers who may prescribe compounded medications through state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacies or write prescriptions for branded FDA-approved products which Omzo does not sell or ship. Omzo states compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. Direct Meds offers clinician-reviewed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in both injectable and sublingual formats, supplied by U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies, and also notes those compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Both platforms rely on clinician assessment and third party compounding pharmacies, but Omzo explicitly calls out the option that providers may prescribe branded medications to be filled elsewhere.

Shipping, supplies, and support

Direct Meds advertises medication, clinician review, telehealth visits, supplies and free temperature-controlled next-day UPS or FedEx shipping included, and notes free next-day shipping generally in 1 to 2 days. Omzo advertises fast, discreet delivery and says medication costs are included in fees Omzo collects and pays to pharmacies, and that results may vary. Direct Meds emphasizes included supplies and ongoing clinician and nursing support. These differences affect total service convenience and how much is bundled into the published price.

Membership, cancellation, refunds and availability

Direct Meds states no monthly membership fee, no hidden fees, month-to-month cancellation for customers, subscribe-and-save plans that require a stated term, and a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Direct Meds is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. Omzo notes that clinical services are provided by third party networks of U.S.-licensed clinicians who determine eligibility, that Omzo is not a medical practice, and that eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change. Omzo also reiterates that compounded medications offered through its platform are not FDA-approved.

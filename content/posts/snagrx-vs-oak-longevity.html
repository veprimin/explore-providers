Quick verdict: SnagRx is the lower-cost compounded option if you are willing to commit to a 12-month plan and want a price locked at every dose; Oak Longevity charges higher flat monthly rates but includes free health coaching and no subscription requirement.

Compare SnagRx pricing and terms

SnagRx vs Oak Longevity: headline differences

This comparison looks only at the supplied facts. SnagRx lists compounded semaglutide from $69/month and compounded tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan, with the rate locked flat at every dose. Oak Longevity lists semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month with one price covering all dosages and free health coaching included. Both sell compounded GLP-1s that are not FDA approved finished products.

What this means for cost and commitment

SnagRx advertises the lowest annual-plan price in this pair: semaglutide from $69/month on a 12-month plan, and month-to-month is $99/month. The advertised low rates require the 12-month plan. Oak bills semaglutide from $133/month and tirzepatide from $199/month on a non-recurring monthly billing model with no subscription required; three month prescriptions are billed upfront. Neither provider offers branded finished drugs in the supplied evidence; both offer compounded medications.

Clinical review process and shipping

SnagRx states telemed prescription and clinician review are delivered by OpenLoop Health, a US-licensed clinician network covering 48 states, with clinician review typically within five hours and orders shipping next-day via UPS or FedEx in temperature controlled packaging. Oak Longevity says a licensed physician reviews each case, approval can be same-day for qualifying patients, and it offers free shipping with tracking. Both note compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products.

Conclusion: SnagRx vs Oak Longevity

Near-term verdict restated: For patients who prioritize the lowest advertised monthly rate and a locked price across dose escalation and renewals, SnagRx is the choice in this comparison when you accept the 12-month plan requirement. For those who prefer no subscription billing, want included health coaching, and accept higher monthly rates, Oak Longevity fits that profile. Choose SnagRx if you want the lowest annual-plan compounded GLP-1 price and a price locked at every dose. Choose Oak Longevity if you prefer no subscription commitment and want free health coaching with your plan.

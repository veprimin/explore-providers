4) Clinical access and support

MedVi highlights a simple online intake with no clinic visits needed and 24/7 support from certified medical professionals. 5) Compounded medication regulatory notes and testing

Both sources include regulatory notes that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. MedVi states their compounded GLP 1s are produced in FDA regulated facilities but also reiterates that compounded medications are not FDA approved.

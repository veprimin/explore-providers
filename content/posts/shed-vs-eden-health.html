Shed vs Eden Health: How they differ at a glance

This direct comparison focuses on five distinctive differences supported by the sources: medication options, pricing model, coaching and support, approval and eligibility steps, and shipping and membership terms.

1. Medication options and regulatory notes

Shed offers both FDA approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded alternatives on one platform. The source explicitly states compounded medications are not FDA approved and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy based on a provider’s prescription. Eden Health presents medication options by name, including Semaglutide and Tirzepatide, but the captured evidence notes that medication specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

2. Pricing: predictable monthly prices versus bundled quotes

Shed lists starting prices for compounded products: compounded Semaglutide injections starting at $199 per month and compounded Tirzepatide starting at $299 per month, and offers 10% off your first month. The source also notes that standard pricing is not published and that you receive a personalized quote after intake. Eden Health advertises monthly pricing at every dose with Semaglutide at $99 and Tirzepatide at $129, a lifetime price guarantee, no membership fees and no insurance needed.

3. Coaching and care model

Shed integrates continuous coaching into treatment and positions coaching as part of the bundled monthly cost rather than a separate fee. The source frames this as a differentiator for patients who want behavioral support alongside medication. Eden Health states its guided GLP-1 program brings medication, care, and support together under one monthly price, but the captured evidence does not include details on the depth or structure of coaching.

4. Approval, eligibility and timing

Shed uses a 2-minute online assessment to check eligibility quickly, followed by clinician review before a prescription is issued. Eden Health requires confirmation that treatment is medically appropriate by a licensed clinician as well, and the source advises people to confirm eligibility, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment. The evidence for Eden does not provide a specific assessment duration.

5. Shipping, membership and guarantees

Eden Health advertises free expedited shipping, no membership fees, and a lifetime price guarantee. Shed advertises medication shipping after clinician approval and a limited time offer of 10% off your first month; it also notes the Shed weight loss guarantee has full details in the Terms and Conditions. Sources caution that compounded medications are not FDA reviewed for safety, effectiveness or quality.

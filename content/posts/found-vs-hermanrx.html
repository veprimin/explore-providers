How Found and HermanRx differ on pricing and billing

Found promotes affordable monthly plans and explicitly notes you can get started for as low as $39, and that most insurance is now accepted. Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 options but with careful qualifiers. Found states compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy and are not available in all states. HermanRx likewise states its compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Insurance, eligibility, and clinician review

Found emphasizes insurance acceptance and handling paperwork, saying most insurance is accepted and that advertised pricing for branded GLP-1s may be via insurance with a 12 month commitment. It also notes Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility. HermanRx is cash-pay with no insurance required and notes that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Delivery timeline, shipping, and support

HermanRx publishes a fast timeline: a roughly 5-minute assessment, about 24 hour provider review, and delivery in about 72 hours if prescribed, plus free discreet shipping in plain unmarked packaging to all 50 states. Found’s captured evidence notes shipping and membership details tied to plans and insurance, but a specific standard delivery timeline was not supplied in the source evidence.

Program focus and features

Found positions itself as a biology-based personalized program that targets hormones, metabolism, sleep and behavior as part of a holistic whole-body approach. HermanRx positions itself as a telehealth platform matching patients with licensed, board-certified physicians across multiple treatment categories and highlights genuinely all-inclusive pricing for compounded GLP-1s and 24/7 patient support through a secure portal.

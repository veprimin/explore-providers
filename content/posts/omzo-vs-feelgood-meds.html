Clinical oversight and how medications are supplied

Omzo emphasizes clinician-guided plans reviewed by licensed U.S. providers and connects users with providers who determine medication eligibility. Omzo notes its primary program provides access to compounded medications prepared by a state licensed sterile compounding pharmacy and that providers may alternatively write prescriptions for branded medications which users must fill at their chosen pharmacy. FeelGood Meds states treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and offers compounded injections and tablets plus a branded option.

Directmeds vs Try Ageless: which telehealth GLP-1 option fits you? Early verdict: choose Directmeds if you want transparent month-to-month pricing with no membership fee and free next-day shipping; choose Try Ageless if you want the most format options, including needle-free tirzepatide drops and built-in discounts.

Explore Directmeds pricing and options

Key difference 1 — Price structure and entry cost

Directmeds advertises treatment starting at $147 and lists standard FAQ pricing of semaglutide $297/month and tirzepatide $399/month. There is a stated Summer promotion that saves $150 and a promotional starting price of $147, with medication, clinician review, telehealth visits, supplies and free next-day UPS/FedEx shipping included. Try Ageless lists formats from $159/month, with yearly per-month pricing from $159/mo for drops, $199/mo for semaglutide, and $249/mo for tirzepatide. Try Ageless states the discount is already applied to every plan and that code SLIM50 stacks an extra $50 off every order.

Key difference 2 — Medication formats and flexibility

Directmeds offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats prepared by U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. Try Ageless offers three formats: semaglutide injection, tirzepatide injection, and needle-free tirzepatide oral drops, and says clinicians can switch molecules or formats under one plan with no new consult fee.

See Try Ageless formats and the oral drops option

Key difference 3 — Memberships, discounts, and billing terms

Directmeds advertises no separate monthly membership fee, no hidden fees, and month-to-month cancellation for customers who are not on subscribe-and-save terms; subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term. Try Ageless emphasizes a discount built into every plan rather than a first-month teaser, offers a stackable SLIM50 code, and notes its lowest advertised prices require the prepaid yearly plan.

Key difference 4 — Shipping, refunds, availability and regulatory notes

Directmeds includes free temperature-controlled 1–2 day UPS or FedEx shipping and says it offers a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Directmeds is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana. Try Ageless advertises free discreet shipping and plain discreet packaging, cancel anytime with no fees and no contracts. Both providers state that their compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality; Try Ageless additionally notes that oral tirzepatide drops are an off-label compounded format because tirzepatide is not FDA-approved in oral form.

Conclusion: Directmeds vs Try Ageless

Directmeds vs Try Ageless comes down to priorities. Directmeds is oriented toward transparent month-to-month pricing, no membership fee, free next-day temperature-controlled shipping, and both injectable and sublingual compounded options. Try Ageless is best known for offering three formats including needle-free tirzepatide oral drops, built-in discounts and a stackable code, and format switching under one plan.

Choose Directmeds if…

you want month-to-month billing with no separate membership fee, free next-day temperature-controlled shipping, a promotional starting price option, and injectable or sublingual compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide. Note: Directmeds is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana and its compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Choose Try Ageless if…

you want the widest choice of formats including needle-free tirzepatide oral drops, built-in plan discounts with an extra SLIM50 code, yearly prepaid per-month pricing from $159/mo for drops, and the option to switch formats without a new consult fee. Try Ageless also states its compounded medications are not FDA-approved and that oral drops are an off-label compounded format.

Both providers require clinician review and prescription via telehealth and state that their compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

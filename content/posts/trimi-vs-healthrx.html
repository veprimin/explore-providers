Quick verdict: Trimi and HealthRx both offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with overnight shipping, but Trimi emphasizes flat per-dose pricing, a 6-month money-back promise, and named compounding pharmacies while HealthRx ties its lowest $99/month semaglutide price to a 12-month prepaid plan and highlights cold-chain overnight shipping. This guide compares Trimi vs HealthRx on pricing structure, shipping and cold-chain handling, prescribing and plan terms, and regulatory notes.

Targeted comparison: price structure and billing

Trimi lists compounded semaglutide from $99/month on the annual plan and tirzepatide at $125/month with a stated flat pricing model so the price “never rises as your dose increases.” HealthRx lists semaglutide from $99/month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/month, and notes the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan.

Shipping and cold chain handling

Both providers advertise overnight shipping. Trimi specifies free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping. HealthRx specifies overnight cold shipping with a cold chain that is tracked and free on every plan. If maintaining temperature control in transit is a top concern, HealthRx explicitly highlights tracked cold-chain handling while Trimi emphasizes temperature-controlled overnight shipping.

Prescribing process and clinician review

Trimi states prescriptions are written by licensed U.S. clinicians through Arora Health with medical oversight led by Dr. Sean Arora, MD, and that not approved by the medical team receive a 100% automatic refund. HealthRx emphasizes that the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is appropriate or whether neither is, and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Regulatory and compounding notes

Both providers make the same regulatory point: compounded medications are prepared per prescription in licensed 503A pharmacies and are not FDA-approved for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Trimi names state-licensed 503A pharmacies including VialsRx (Texas Board of Pharmacy #35264) and GreenwichRx and notes certificates of analysis are available on request. HealthRx states compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and repeats that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Plan features, guarantees and limitations

Trimi advertises additional commitments such as a 6-month money-back promise: “Follow your plan for 6 months, and if you don’t lose weight, you can request a refund. Terms apply.” Trimi also promotes one flat membership that covers medication, clinician visits, ongoing check-ins, and free shipping. HealthRx highlights that there is no consultation fee, no shipping charge, and that plan price does not change when your dose increases; it also warns the lowest monthly price is tied to a longer prepaid plan and that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy and renewal terms may change.

How the clinician decision differs

Both services require clinician review. HealthRx explicitly says you do not pick a medication from a menu and that the clinician decides whether semaglutide, tirzepatide, or neither is appropriate. Trimi likewise uses licensed clinicians through Arora Health to prescribe compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and highlights medical oversight by Dr. Sean Arora, MD. If you want a service that emphasizes clinician discretion of medication choice, HealthRx makes that point central; if you want named clinical oversight and a specific refund promise tied to medical approval and outcomes, Trimi includes those elements.

Conclusion: choosing between Trimi vs HealthRx

Trimi and HealthRx both offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide with overnight shipping and clinician review, but they differ on price structure, shipping emphasis, and guarantees. Trimi highlights flat pricing per dose, named compounding pharmacies, a 6-month money-back promise and automatic refunds if not approved by the medical team. HealthRx emphasizes a tracked cold chain, and that the $99/month semaglutide price is tied to a 12-month prepaid plan with tirzepatide starting at $179/month.

Choose Trimi if you prioritize flat per-dose pricing that “never rises as your dose increases,” named 503A pharmacies and an advertised 6-month money-back promise. Choose HealthRx if you prioritize a tracked cold-chain overnight shipping process and are willing to commit to a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan to access the $99/month advertised price.

At a glance: Measured vs IVIM Health

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide under clinician review and require an Rx. The most distinctive differences are pricing structure and promotions, third-party ratings and published outcomes, program model and clinical support, shipping and membership details, and how each frames outcome evidence. Below we compare those points using only the supplied source information.

Key difference 1: Pricing and promotional structure

Measured lists promotional compounded semaglutide at $89/mo and compounded tirzepatide at $189/mo, with promotional language such as “From $89/mo” and specific promotional price calls. Measured also highlights “Free 2-day shipping.” IVIM Health lists semaglutide at $75/mo and tirzepatide at $133/mo but also notes a program fee of $74.99/mo and a published first-month offer code GIFT75 for a free month membership. IVIM’s materials also reference a first month at $99 and $174/month ongoing in some FAQ language; the supplied materials state ongoing pricing may rise to $174/month.

Key difference 2: Third-party ratings and published outcome claims

IVIM Health emphasizes dual 4.9-star ratings on Google and Trustpilot and cites published observational studies and proprietary protocol claims, including that IVIM patients following the IVIM Protocol lost an average of 46% more weight over 68 weeks in a non head-to-head comparison and that GLP-1 ID may accelerate weight loss by up to 40% compared to another published trial. Measured highlights a 4.8-star Trustpilot rating from 51,000+ users and internal analysis claiming an average loss of 31 lbs at 6 months among members reporting weight data. Both providers qualify outcomes and note that results may vary and that their claims are not independent randomized head-to-head trials.

Key difference 3: Program model and clinical support

Measured promotes same-day prescription decisions, unlimited provider messaging, 24/7 care team access, and dietitian support as part of its offering. IVIM Health positions a clinician-guided GLP-1 ID protocol with weekly dose adjustments and a structured optimization approach described as proprietary; IVIM also offers a no-obligation free consultation for new patients (a $75 value in the source). Both state prescriptions are issued at a clinician’s discretion and that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Key difference 4: Shipping, membership, and cancellation

Measured repeatedly notes free two-day shipping and says “Cancel anytime” in landing-page evidence. IVIM highlights a free month membership code (GIFT75), a no-obligation free consultation, and an explicit program fee; IVIM materials note membership and program-fee structure with ongoing pricing. Both sources mention that compounded medications are not FDA-reviewed and that availability may vary by state.

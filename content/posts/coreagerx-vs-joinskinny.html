If you want clearly advertised flat monthly list prices and no membership fee for specific GLP-1 doses, CoreAgeRx highlights semaglutide at $79 per month and tirzepatide at $129 per month. How CoreAgeRx vs JoinSkinny differ on pricing and billing

CoreAgeRx promotes flat monthly pricing tied to medication: semaglutide any dose up to 1.0 mg for $79 per month and tirzepatide any dose up to 7.5 mg for $129 per month, with messaging of same price every month and no membership fee. CoreAgeRx also warns other programs may raise monthly bills after the first month. JoinSkinny lists plans starting at $149 with an advertised $140 promotional discount available on the landing page, plus monthly and multi-month bundles. JoinSkinny also says monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases and that bundle pricing is offered.

Prescribing model, pharmacies, and regulatory notes

Both services use licensed clinicians to review patients and prescribe when appropriate. JoinSkinny states prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners and that medications are prepared fresh and shipped securely. CoreAgeRx notes treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny includes a similar regulatory note: compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Shipping, delivery speed, and support

JoinSkinny advertises free temperature-controlled shipping with next-day UPS for prescriptions received before 2 pm central time and tracking for each shipment. JoinSkinny also mentions 24/7 chat and monthly check-ins for ongoing support. CoreAgeRx materials captured in the source mention questions like how fast is shipping but do not provide a specific shipping speed in the captured content. CoreAgeRx emphasizes a 3-minute online visit and no insurance required.

Medication types, dosing, and plan flexibility

CoreAgeRx lists specific dosing caps in its captured details: tirzepatide any dose up to 7.5 mg and semaglutide any dose up to 1.0 mg, with messaging that flat monthly pricing applies. JoinSkinny highlights GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP options, flat-rate dosing, and that dose increases do not change the monthly price. JoinSkinny also offers installment payment options like Afterpay, Klarna, and Affirm and allows cancellation before the next billing cycle. CoreAgeRx repeats no membership fee and no price hike claims in the captured text and cautions that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Head-to-head at a glance: pricing, support, medication formats

This comparison focuses on the clearest differences between JoinFridays vs He & She MD: starting price and promo structure, membership and cancellation policy, bundled coaching and clinician access, and available medication formats and regulatory notes. All statements below reflect each provider’s published terms.

1. Pricing structure and promotional terms

JoinFridays lists a starting price of $117/month with multi-month plans that lower the effective monthly rate; standalone month-to-month pricing examples include $249 for one month and $1,800 for twelve months (about $150/month). JoinFridays also advertises active promo codes such as NEWYOU12 and NYNY12 for tirzepatide and semaglutide respectively, with promotional amounts described on their landing page.

He & She MD advertises semaglutide injections from $139/month and tirzepatide from $199/month as six-month introductory rates. The introductory rates apply for the first six months and then renew at a higher six-month price, for example semaglutide promo $139/month renewing at $199/month equivalent.

2. Membership, cancellation, and included fees

JoinFridays emphasizes no membership fees and the ability to cancel at any time, describing itself as month-to-month without long-term contracts. He & She MD also advertises no separate membership fee, but its headline prices are structured as six-month terms with intro rates that renew at higher prices after six months.

3. Coaching, clinician access, and care model

He & She MD bundles a coaching app that includes 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1-specific coaching, and progress tracking at no extra cost. JoinFridays highlights personalized, clinician-guided care rather than templated protocols, but the source emphasizes flexibility and pricing rather than an app-based coaching bundle.

4. Medication formats and offerings

JoinFridays promotes both compounded tirzepatide and compounded semaglutide on one platform and notes options for brand-name products with higher month-to-month pricing. JoinFridays explicitly states that its compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are subject to clinician review to determine medical appropriateness.

He & She MD offers semaglutide injections, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injections as part of program options. He & She MD also states medications are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies, and that compounded GLP-1s are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality. Both providers require clinician consultation and prescription through their platforms and note that not all customers will qualify.

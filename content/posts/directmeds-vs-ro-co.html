Primary difference: FDA-approved oral pill versus compounded formulations

Direct Meds prescribes clinician-reviewed compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and sublingual formats. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Ro.co is the only platform here offering access to the Wegovy pill, described as the first and only FDA-approved oral GLP-1 weight loss medication on this list. That regulatory distinction is the most consequential factual difference between these providers.

Pricing and promotions compared

Both platforms present distinct price entry points and messaging. Direct Meds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and notes standard FAQ pricing of $297/month for semaglutide and $399/month for tirzepatide. Ro.co markets a “Get Started Online for Just $39” entry point; ongoing branded medication pricing varies by plan and tends to be higher than compounded alternatives.

Quick verdict: Measured positions itself around lower promotional monthly pricing and free two-day shipping, while HealthRx emphasizes prepaid plan pricing from $99/month and overnight cold shipping. This comparison looks at price structure, shipping and handling, medication options and clinical review, support and care features, and regulatory notes to decide which fits your needs in the Measured vs HealthRx comparison.

Explore Measured pricing and offers

Price structure: promotional monthlies versus prepaid plans

Measured advertises promotional compounded semaglutide from $89/mo and compounded tirzepatide $189/mo. HealthRx lists semaglutide from $99/month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan with a stated cost of $1,163 today and $1,188 at renewal, and tirzepatide plans start at $179/month. Measured highlights promotional pricing, while HealthRx ties its lowest monthly price to a longer prepaid commitment. If upfront term length matters, HealthRx explicitly requires a 12-month prepaid plan for the $99 monthly price.

Shipping and delivery differences

Measured offers free two-day shipping and describes fast and free shipping on every order. HealthRx provides overnight shipping with cold chain, tracked delivery and states free shipping on every plan at checkout. Both providers call out rapid shipping, but HealthRx emphasizes cold chain and tracking as part of overnight delivery, while Measured highlights free two-day shipping.

Medication options and clinician review

Both Measured and HealthRx offer compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide prepared per prescription. HealthRx specifically notes that you are not picking a medication off a menu and that a clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is the right fit, or whether neither is. Measured states same-day prescription decisions and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Both providers require an Rx and clinician discretion.

Support, account features, and flexibility

Measured promotes unlimited provider messaging, 24/7 Care Team access, dietitian support, and cancel anytime language. HealthRx highlights that plan price does not change when your dose does and notes no consultation fee, no shipping charge, and no price change when dose increases. Measured emphasizes dietitian and messaging support, while HealthRx focuses on price stability across dose adjustments and inclusion of shipping in the plan.

Regulatory and quality notes

Both sources include the same regulatory caution: compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. HealthRx adds that compounded medication is prepared in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and is prepared for an individual patient with a valid prescription. Measured also reiterates that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and that prescriptions are issued at provider discretion.

Conclusion: which to choose in the Measured vs HealthRx decision

Choose Measured if you prioritize lower promotional month to month pricing like compounded semaglutide from $89/mo, free two-day shipping, same-day Rx decisions, and dietitian support. Choose HealthRx if you prefer a prepaid plan structure with semaglutide from $99/mo on a 12-month prepaid plan, overnight cold chain shipping and tracked delivery, and explicit price stability when your dose changes. In all cases confirm eligibility, medication choice, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment, and remember compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

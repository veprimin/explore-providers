## Willow Review — Medication Plus Structure

Willow treats GLP-1 therapy as a **structured program**, not a medication subscription. Its pitch is the scaffolding around the drug: progress-tracking tools and built-in follow-up consultations designed to convert GLP-1's appetite effect into durable results. For patients who have learned that medication alone doesn't produce lasting change, that structure is the differentiator.

## About Willow

Willow is a telehealth-first weight-management platform offering **compounded GLP-1 medication** — comparable in active ingredient to Ozempic and tirzepatide, prepared by a licensed pharmacy but not FDA-approved as a finished product — alongside wellness-tracking tools and scheduled follow-up. Its clinical model is built on the premise that GLP-1's maximum benefit comes from pairing the medication with behavioural change, and the platform is designed to support that integration rather than simply dispense.

## How It Works

Intake captures your health history, weight-loss goals, and behavioural patterns. A licensed telehealth team evaluates your candidacy and designs a program with integrated tracking and follow-up visits built in. Between consultations, progress-monitoring tools let you log milestones and communicate with your care team. Follow-up visits review progress and adjust the protocol as your body responds.

Supervised GLP-1 therapy is well supported clinically; the [NIH](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9486455/) has published trial data on both semaglutide and tirzepatide that inform the kind of monitored, adjusted treatment Willow is structured around.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes Willow different from other GLP-1 platforms?

Willow builds progress tracking and scheduled follow-up consultations directly into the program, treating GLP-1 therapy as a structured weight-management journey rather than a simple medication subscription.

### What medications does Willow offer?

Willow offers compounded GLP-1 medications comparable in active ingredient to Ozempic and tirzepatide, dispensed through its licensed pharmacy network. A clinician determines the appropriate medication during the online review.

### Is Willow legitimate?

Yes. It is a licensed telehealth platform prescribing compounded GLP-1 medication after a clinician review, with integrated tracking and follow-up. The medication is compounded rather than FDA-approved but uses the same active ingredients as the branded versions.

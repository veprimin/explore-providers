Sunlight vs Telos Rx: quick comparison and early verdict

Sunlight and Telos Rx both offer online GLP-1 weight loss options, but they differ on pricing structure, whether medications are compounded, and how they describe pricing guarantees. Early verdict: choose Sunlight if you prioritize a stated flat monthly price starting at $88/mo and injections or oral options; choose Telos Rx if you are specifically seeking compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide options described as available “as low as” $99 per month for semaglutide and $139 per month for tirzepatide, subject to clinician approval.

How pricing and pricing guarantees compare

Sunlight advertises “Personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo” and repeatedly states “Same price forever” and “Same price every month” on its landing content. Telos Rx lists semaglutide “as low as $99 per month” and tirzepatide “as low as $139 per month” but emphasizes that prices depend on plan and that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Telos Rx also includes phrasing like “Pay $0 if not approved” in captured content. Both providers note that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Medication format and prescribing model

Sunlight offers injections and oral options with a 100% online medical visit and prescription and telehealth included, stating “no insurance required.” Telos Rx offers semaglutide and tirzepatide and describes them as prescribed online after review by a US-licensed provider. Telos Rx specifically states the medications it offers are compounded when offered and are prescription-only.

Regulatory and formulation differences

Telos Rx captured content repeatedly notes that “Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality” and that semaglutide and tirzepatide are compounded medications, prescription-only, and subject to medical approval by a US-licensed provider. Sunlight includes a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. These are distinct statements about formulation and regulatory review.

Shipping, membership, cancellation and support signals

Sunlight’s captured landing content claims “Free shipping in 1-2 days,” “No Monthly Membership,” “Cancel Anytime,” and marketing phrases such as “Fast, Free Shipping” and a Summer Promo “As low as $88.” Telos Rx notes “Cancel anytime, no penalties” in its captured content and emphasizes that actual availability and pricing are subject to clinician review and plan details. Neither source provided independent verified shipping or insurance coverage details beyond these captured statements.

1. Pricing structure and commitments

SnagRx advertises compounded semaglutide from $69/month and tirzepatide from $119/month on a 12-month plan, with the rate “locked flat at every dose” for the life of treatment. Month-to-month equivalents are $99 and $149, and the low figures require the 12-month plan. BreezeMeds displays “starting at” prices such as semaglutide injection from $199/month and tirzepatide injection from $299, plus oral options from $399, while a separate $169 figure appears without clear product association. The sources show SnagRx ties its lowest rates to a 12-month commitment and an explicit price-lock; BreezeMeds lists multiple starting prices and does not supply full billing visibility in the captured evidence.

2. Product types and regulatory notes

SnagRx is described as offering compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide only. The source explicitly notes compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. BreezeMeds advertises injectable and oral options: semaglutide injection, tirzepatide injection, oral semaglutide and oral tirzepatide. Its captured evidence states medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Both providers require clinician review to determine medical appropriateness.

3. Transparency: named partners and clinician networks

SnagRx names its four US 503A partner pharmacies and its clinician network, OpenLoop Health, and states LegitScript certification. The source notes OpenLoop Health clinicians cover 48 states and that named pharmacies are used to fill compounded prescriptions. BreezeMeds provides less captured detail about pharmacy partners or clinician network names; the guidance in the source is to confirm eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

4. Approval speed and shipping

SnagRx states an intake takes about five minutes, an OpenLoop clinician typically reviews it within five hours and always within 24, and approved orders ship next-day via UPS or FedEx in discreet, temperature-controlled packaging. BreezeMeds markets discreet home delivery but the captured evidence does not provide specific review or shipping timeframes.

5. Guarantees, refunds and billing clarity

SnagRx’s source mentions a full refund if the prescription is declined and a 6-month weight-loss guarantee with terms that apply. The source also highlights “no hidden fees” and “no monthly membership” claims for SnagRx. For BreezeMeds the source notes fees and billing commitments were not fully visible in captured content and recommends confirming costs and terms before enrolling.

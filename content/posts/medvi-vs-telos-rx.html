Quick comparison of core differences for MedVi vs Telos Rx

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences evident in the sources: pricing structure and promos, medication options, care model and support, compounded status and regulatory notes, and cancellation or renewal terms.

1. Pricing structure and promotional terms

MedVi advertises a winter promotional starting price of $179 for the first month including fast, free shipping. The provider notes the ongoing standalone month price is $299 and a three-month plan is $649. Telos Rx lists semaglutide as low as $99 per month and tirzepatide as low as $139 per month, while also noting your price depends on your plan and that a licensed provider must approve treatment.

2. Medication options: semaglutide only vs semaglutide and tirzepatide

MedVi offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only. Telos Rx offers both compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, describing tirzepatide as acting on GLP-1 and GIP together and semaglutide as acting on GLP-1. Telos Rx therefore provides a dual-molecule option that MedVi does not offer.

MedVi: clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only. Telos Rx: semaglutide and tirzepatide available, if approved.

3. Care model and support

MedVi emphasizes a simple online intake requiring no clinic visits and 24/7 certified medical support. Telos Rx describes online prescription after review by a US-licensed provider and notes treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Telos Rx mentions cancel anytime terms but does not reproduce the same 24/7 support claim in the captured evidence.

4. Compounded status and regulatory notes

Both sources state compounds are prescription-only and are not FDA-approved. MedVi explicitly explains compounded GLP-1s are produced in FDA-regulated facilities but are not FDA-approved or reviewed for safety, effectiveness or quality. Telos Rx states compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved and are prescribed only after review by a US-licensed provider.

5. Renewal, cancellation and eligibility details

MedVi notes refills are locked in at a full price and that insurance is not billed; it also highlights that ongoing price is substantially higher than the first-month promo. Telos Rx indicates your price depends on your plan and that cancel anytime no penalties may apply, while also warning that pricing, eligibility and state coverage may change.

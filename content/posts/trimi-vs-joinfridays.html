What Trimi and JoinFridays offer in common

Both Trimi and JoinFridays provide clinician-prescribed GLP-1 treatment options that include compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. Both sources note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Treatment is prescribed when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate; clinicians review eligibility and prescribe dosing.

Top differences: price model, shipping, membership, refunds, and promo codes

The most distinctive supported differences are: (1) price model and starting price, (2) shipping and fulfillment promises, (3) membership and cancellation flexibility, (4) refunds and guarantees, and (5) promo codes and multi-month pricing. Each section below compares Trimi vs JoinFridays using only the supplied facts.

1. Price model and starting price

Trimi advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/month on an annual plan with flat pricing that never rises as dose increases. Trimi lists tirzepatide at $125/month and states semaglutide is $99/month on annual plans. JoinFridays lists a starting price of $117/month, with standalone one-month options that can be higher; JoinFridays also presents tirzepatide as low as $198/month and notes multi-month plans reduce the effective rate.

2. Shipping and fulfillment

Trimi explicitly offers free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and notes medications are compounded by state-licensed 503A pharmacies including named pharmacies with certificates of analysis available on request. JoinFridays states it does not manufacture compounded medications and that actual product appearance and labeling may differ from website images; details on shipping speed or temperature control are not supplied in the source.

3. Membership terms and cancellations

JoinFridays highlights no membership fees and cancel-anytime with no long-term contract. Trimi emphasizes a flat membership that covers medication, clinician visits, ongoing check-ins, and free shipping on its plans, but the source also cautions that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change. JoinFridays positions itself for patients who want to trial GLP-1 without a long-term commitment.

4. Refunds and guarantees

Trimi offers a 6-month money-back promise: follow your plan for 6 months and if you do not lose weight you can request a refund, with terms applying. JoinFridays does not advertise a comparable money-back promise in the supplied material; instead it emphasizes flexible cancellation and promo codes as primary consumer protections.

5. Promo codes and multi-month pricing

JoinFridays documents active promo codes and aggressive discounts: NEWYOU12 offers up to $500 off tirzepatide and NYNY12 up to $400 off semaglutide, and multi-month plans lower effective rates (twelve months averages $150/month in the example). Trimi’s pricing claims focus on flat pricing and the annual plan low rates; the supplied Trimi evidence does not list comparable promo codes.

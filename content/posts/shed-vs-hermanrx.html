Shed vs HermanRx: how they differ at a glance

The primary differences between Shed and HermanRx center on medication options, pricing transparency and billing structure, ongoing coaching and support, and published timelines and shipping. Below we compare these features directly using only the supplied evidence.

1. Medication types and regulatory notes

Shed offers both FDA-approved name-brand GLP-1 medications and compounded GLP-1 alternatives on the same platform, letting patients choose based on budget and preference. The source explicitly states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are prepared by a licensed pharmacy. HermanRx lists compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide with B12 as its published GLP-1 offerings and also states that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

2. Pricing model and what is published

Shed highlights starting prices for compounded injections: compounded semaglutide starting at $199/month and compounded tirzepatide starting at $299/month, and it offers 10% off your first month as a limited time offer. Shed does not publish a single standard price for all patients; standard pricing is not published and patients receive a personalized quote after intake. HermanRx publishes all-inclusive monthly prices: compounded semaglutide + B12 at $249/month and compounded tirzepatide + B12 at $349/month, with no separate membership or consultation fee.

3. Coaching, support, and bundled services

Shed integrates continuous coaching into treatment and positions coaching as part of the service rather than a separate add-on; coaching is bundled into the monthly cost rather than charged separately. HermanRx describes ongoing clinical support and a secure patient portal, and its published pricing is described as all-inclusive covering provider review, prescription, medication, ongoing support and shipping, with no membership fee and no separate consultation charge.

4. Timeline and shipping details

HermanRx advertises specific timelines: roughly a 5-minute assessment, ~24 hour provider review and ~72 hour delivery if prescribed, and also gives a wider 3 to 7 business day window in its FAQ. HermanRx offers free discreet shipping in plain unmarked packaging to all 50 states. Shed cites a fast 2-minute approval assessment and indicates medication is shipped after a clinician review, but the source does not supply a specific standard delivery timeline for every order.

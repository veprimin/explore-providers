Hers vs HealthRx comes down to one main split: Hers sells FDA-approved brand-name Wegovy® and other name-brand GLP-1s starting at $149/mo with a required membership, while HealthRx offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide plans starting as low as $99/mo on a 12-month prepaid plan and ships overnight with cold chain handling. Early verdict: choose Hers if you want FDA-approved, brand-name medication and ongoing dose check-ins; choose HealthRx if you prioritize lower advertised monthly pricing and compounded medication prepared per prescription.

How they differ: branded FDA-approved vs compounded options

Hers explicitly markets FDA-approved Wegovy® (brand-name semaglutide) and states it does not offer compounded medications. HealthRx offers compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription and notes that compounded medication is not FDA-approved. That regulatory distinction is central: Hers provides a name-brand, FDA-approved product when prescribed; HealthRx provides a compounded product made in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and flags that compounded medications are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Pricing and payment structure comparison

Pricing formats differ. Hers lists Wegovy® pill from $149/mo and injectables from $199/mo, and also states an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required with separate membership billing. HealthRx advertises semaglutide from $99/mo on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) and tirzepatide plans starting at $179/mo, noting the lowest monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan. HealthRx also highlights that plan price does not change when your dose does.

Clinician review, prescribing and medication selection

Both providers indicate treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and a prescription is required. HealthRx emphasizes the clinician reviews the file and decides whether semaglutide or tirzepatide is the right fit, or whether neither is, and that you are choosing the plan the medication arrives on. Hers notes that Ozempic® may be prescribed off-label at a provider’s discretion and reiterates that Wegovy® and Zepbound® are FDA-approved for weight loss.

Shipping, dosing support and membership details

HealthRx advertises overnight cold shipping and a cold chain that is tracked and free on every plan. Hers highlights a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins and says medication is not available without an active membership; Hers also states membership and medication are billed separately and that a prescription is not guaranteed. Both recommend confirming eligibility, medication, total cost, renewal terms and state availability before enrollment.

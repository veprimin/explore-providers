1. Pricing transparency and promotions

Directmeds advertises a promotional starting price of $147 and, in its FAQ, lists standard pricing of $297 per month for semaglutide and $399 per month for tirzepatide. The promotion and FAQ prices are both presented in the source; the promotional headline differs from the FAQ standard prices. Luvo Health’s captured landing-page evidence did not publish verifiable standard prices or a clear starting price in the captured content.

2. Medication formats and compounding status

Both providers offer clinician-reviewed treatment options that include semaglutide and tirzepatide in various formats in the captured material. Directmeds explicitly offers injectable and sublingual compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by U.S.-based 503A compounding pharmacies. Luvo Health’s captured content lists tirzepatide injection, semaglutide tablets and injections and notes compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies, but many current details were not published or could not be verified on the captured page.

3. Shipping, supplies and clinician support

Directmeds states medication, clinician review, telehealth visits, supplies and free temperature-controlled next-day UPS or FedEx shipping are included. Luvo Health’s captured content references ongoing support, dose changes and cancellation terms, but it does not provide the same explicit shipping promise in the captured evidence.

Review Luvo Health program details

4. Membership, cancellation and refund policies

Directmeds advertises no separate monthly membership fee, no hidden fees, and month-to-month cancellation for customers; subscribe-and-save plans require the stated term. Directmeds also notes a full refund if a prescription is not approved. Luvo Health’s captured information includes phrases like cancel anytime and references a cancellation and refund policy, but many specific terms and pricing, eligibility and renewal details were not published or could not be verified on the captured page.

5. State availability and eligibility notes

Directmeds is not available in Mississippi or Louisiana, according to the captured evidence. Both sources state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. The captured Luvo Health content cautions that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change and that some current details were not published or could not be verified.

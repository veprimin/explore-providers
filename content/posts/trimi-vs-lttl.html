Quick verdict: Trimi emphasizes low flat pricing and free overnight shipping for compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, while LTTL highlights an up-to-$140 first-month credit and independent lab verification for compounded GLP-1s. This comparison, Trimi vs LTTL, focuses on pricing structure, shipping and delivery, laboratory verification, membership terms and refund or promotional details.

Pricing model and membership pricing

Trimi lists compounded semaglutide from $99 per month on the annual plan and compounded tirzepatide at $125 per month, with language that the price is the same at every dose and flat pricing that never rises as dose increases. LTTL shows a starting price reference of $140 and advertises up to $140 off your first month with a code auto-applied, and states “same price, every dose.” Both providers caution that pricing, eligibility, pharmacy, state coverage and renewal terms may change.

Direct comparison

Trimi: explicit per-month figures on an annual plan and a claim of same price at every dose. LTTL: a promotional credit up to $140 off first month and a note of consistent pricing per dose without the explicit per-month breakdown captured here.

Shipping, pharmacy sourcing, and verification

Trimi promotes free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and notes medications are compounded by state-licensed 503A pharmacies with named pharmacies listed and a certificate of analysis available on request. LTTL states medications are compounded by U.S. licensed pharmacies, prepared in state-licensed pharmacies, and independently verified in FDA registered labs, with strength confirmed every 3 to 6 months to stay within plus or minus 10 percent of the target strength.

Clinical oversight, regulatory notes, and claims

Both providers supply compounded GLP-1 medications and include the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Trimi specifies that prescriptions are provided by U.S.-licensed clinicians through Arora Health, led by Medical Director Dr. Sean Arora, and that plans are reviewed and prescribed by licensed providers. LTTL indicates treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and emphasizes independent lab verification.

Refunds, promotions, and guarantees

Trimi advertises a 6-month money-back promise: follow your plan for 6 months and if you do not lose weight you can request a refund, with terms that apply. LTTL highlights a promotional credit “up to $140 off your first month” applied automatically; no refund policy details were captured for LTTL in the provided evidence.

MEDVi QUAD is a prescription erectile dysfunction treatment delivered through
telehealth. It uses a compounded sublingual formula — dissolved under the
tongue rather than swallowed — which is designed to act faster than a standard
oral tablet.

This review covers what is actually in the formula, how quickly it works, what
it costs, and where it fits against the alternatives.

## Quick take

Searchers find this product under several names — *Quad by MEDVi*, *Quad MEDVi*,
and *quad.medvi* — all of which refer to the same treatment.

**Best for:** men who have tried single-ingredient ED medication and want faster
onset with a longer response window.

**Main trade-off:** it is a compounded product. Compounded combinations are not
FDA-approved as finished products, even when the individual ingredients are.
That distinction matters and is covered below.

## How MEDVi QUAD works

The treatment is a sublingual troche containing four active ingredients. Because
it dissolves under the tongue, absorption bypasses first-pass metabolism in the
liver, which is the mechanism behind the faster reported onset.

Reported onset is roughly 10–15 minutes, with a response window of up to 36
hours. Both figures vary considerably between individuals.

## Getting a prescription

1. Complete an online medical intake covering history, current medications, and
   cardiovascular risk factors.
2. A licensed clinician reviews the submission.
3. If approved, a compounding pharmacy prepares and ships the prescription.

There is no in-person visit. A clinician must approve before anything is
dispensed — this is a prescription product, not a supplement.

## Is MEDVi QUAD legitimate?

It is a genuine telehealth service that requires clinician approval and
dispenses through a licensed compounding pharmacy. The reasonable caution is not
about legitimacy but about compounding: combination products made by compounding
pharmacies do not go through FDA review as a finished combination, so there is
less published safety and efficacy data than for a branded single-ingredient
tablet.

If that trade-off concerns you, an FDA-approved generic from a provider like Ro
or Hims is the more conservative choice.

**Cons**

## Frequently asked questions

**Does MEDVi QUAD work?**
Reported onset is 10–15 minutes with effects lasting up to 36 hours, but
individual response varies. It is not effective for everyone, and it does not
treat the underlying causes of erectile dysfunction.

**What does MEDVi QUAD cost?**
See the pricing section above. Verify on MEDVi's site before ordering, as
promotional pricing changes frequently.

**Is this the same as MEDVi's weight-loss programme?**
No. MEDVi runs a separate GLP-1 weight-loss service. This review covers the ED
product only — see our separate MEDVi GLP-1 review for that.

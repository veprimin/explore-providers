Comparing TMates vs Mochi Health? Quick verdict: TMates is best if you want a single flat $199/month semaglutide program with 24/7 U.S. doctor access and the same price at every dose; Mochi Health is best if you want lower entry pricing, a membership model, and live video doctor visits bookable in about 5 minutes.

How these services differ at a glance

The choice between TMates vs Mochi Health comes down to three distinctive differences supported by the source material: pricing model and transparency, clinician access style, and medication options and structure. Both operate in all 50 states and both offer compounded GLP-1 options; beyond that, their approaches diverge.

1. Pricing model and transparency

TMates uses a flat-rate pricing model: priced at $199/month, same price at every dose, with no step-up fees as dosage escalates during titration. TMates markets free shipping and cancel anytime terms. Mochi Health uses a membership-plus-medication structure with promotional and starting rates. Mochi advertises membership-plus-medication costs that can start from $99/month, with a 50% off first month promotion using code BESTWLHALFOFF and other promotional first-month rates as low as $39 for some services. Mochi warns that ongoing cost varies by plan and medication and that terms vary by state.

2. Clinician access and visit format

TMates emphasizes continuous physician availability, stating 24/7 access to U.S.-based doctors and virtual check-in appointments throughout the program. Mochi Health highlights synchronous live video visits, bookable in about 5 minutes, offering a real-time doctor consultation rather than the asynchronous questionnaire review used by many competitors. Mochi also offers 24/7 support and ongoing appointment availability, but the defining clinical difference is TMates 24/7 physician access claim versus Mochi’s live video visit model.

3. Medication options and structure

Both platforms focus on compounded medications. TMates explicitly markets clinically dosed compounded semaglutide formulated in U.S.-based facilities and notes its compounded medication status with the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. TMates appears focused on compounded semaglutide as its main offering and notes limited public detail on tirzepatide availability.

Mochi lists a broader set of compounded options and price points in its materials: compounded semaglutide starting at $60, compounded tirzepatide starting at $90, a compounded oral semaglutide pill starting at $62, and other compounded oral agents at various starting prices. Mochi also emphasizes that prescriptions are issued at provider discretion during live video evaluation. Mochi similarly includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Patient experience and support

TMates positions itself as a clinician-forward, flat-fee program with same-dose pricing and continuous physician availability, which the source frames as valuable for patients new to GLP-1 therapy who may need injection technique help or dose adjustments between check-ins. Mochi positions its live video visit as the core differentiator, offering a real-time session where the doctor evaluates the case and explains the treatment plan directly, which the source contrasts with asynchronous intake reviews.

Regulatory and eligibility notes

Both sources include the same regulatory qualifier: compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Both mention availability is subject to medical eligibility and that prescriptions are issued at clinician discretion.

Conclusion: TMates vs Mochi Health

In short, TMates is the clearer choice if you prioritize a predictable, flat $199/month semaglutide program with 24/7 U.S. doctor access and consistent pricing at all doses. Mochi Health is the clearer choice if you want lower starting costs, a membership-plus-medication model with promotions such as 50% off your first month with code BESTWLHALFOFF, and live video doctor visits bookable in about 5 minutes.

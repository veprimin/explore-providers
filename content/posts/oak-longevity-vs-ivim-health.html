Distinctive differences: price model, program structure, and trust signals

1. Price model and billing

Oak Longevity: semaglutide from $133/mo and tirzepatide from $199/mo. Oak says one flat price covers all dosages, billed monthly on a non recurring basis so you are not auto charged and choose to renew each month. Three month prescriptions are billed upfront and refunds are not available after physician approval and once medication has shipped.

IVIM Health: lists semaglutide at $75/mo and tirzepatide at $133/mo with an additional $74.99/mo program fee. IVIM also advertises a free month membership code GIFT75 and a no obligation free consultation valued at $75 to lower upfront cost. IVIM’s FAQ notes an ongoing rate of $174/month in some listings; the source frames first month and ongoing pricing separately.

2. Program structure and clinical process

Oak Longevity: The process is online with no video call required. Oak states licensed clinicians review cases and that the clinical review is asynchronous. Oak includes free health coaching with plans and free shipping. The source notes patients with BMI below 20 do not qualify for GLP 1 treatment and that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA approved.

IVIM Health: Positions a clinician guided GLP 1 ID protocol with weekly dose adjustments and a structured optimization approach. IVIM publishes internal data and retrospective studies supporting its protocol claims, but those are not head to head trials. The source states compounded medications are prescribed only after consultation with an Ivím provider and are not FDA approved.

3. Third party ratings and outcomes claims

IVIM Health: The source highlights a 4.9 star rating on both Google and Trustpilot as a strong satisfaction signal. IVIM also cites internal and published retrospective observational studies in Obesity Pillars reporting larger average weight loss for patients following its protocol versus another published trial, with the source noting these are not randomized or head to head comparisons and results may vary.

Oak Longevity: The source emphasizes low prices, free coaching, and same day approval availability for qualifying patients. Oak states it has served 10,000+ patients according to the captured materials. Oak also warns compounded medications are not FDA approved.

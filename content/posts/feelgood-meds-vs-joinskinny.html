This article compares FeelGood Meds vs JoinSkinny across pricing, medication type, shipping and regulatory notes.

Top differences at a glance

The most distinctive supported differences between FeelGood Meds and JoinSkinny are: pricing tiers and branded option availability, compounded versus branded product handling and FDA notes, shipping speed and temperature control, and pricing structure and plan bundles. Below we compare those features directly using only the supplied evidence.

Pricing structure and medication options

FeelGood Meds advertises compounded injections starting at $149/mo, compounded tablets starting at $249/mo, and a branded medication option shown from $1,999/mo. JoinSkinny advertises plans starting at $149 with monthly and 3-, 6-, and 12-month bundles and a stated $140 promotional discount. JoinSkinny also emphasizes flat-rate dosing so your monthly price stays the same regardless of dosage increases.

Compounded vs branded and regulatory notes

FeelGood Meds lists compounded injections and tablets plus a branded option; its regulatory note states compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny says it works with licensed U.S. pharmacies filling authentic GLP-1 injections and GLP-1 + GIP when prescribed and also notes that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. JoinSkinny notes branded prescriptions are filled and paid for separately.

Shipping, fulfillment and timing

FeelGood Meds mentions free shipping. JoinSkinny specifies free temperature-controlled shipping that arrives in 1-2 days, and prescriptions received before 2 pm central time ship the same day with next-day UPS in a temperature controlled package. JoinSkinny also details tracking, insured shipments, and an exception if incorrect shipping information is provided.

Ongoing care, telemedicine and support

JoinSkinny describes a fully online medical service with a health questionnaire, licensed provider review, prescription when appropriate, ongoing support, dose adjustments, monthly check-ins, secure telemedicine and 24/7 chat. JoinSkinny says all care is delivered by licensed U.S. providers and prescriptions are filled by U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners. FeelGood Meds notes clinician-reviewed online weight-management treatment with pricing and eligibility subject to the live offer; no expanded FAQ answers were captured for FeelGood Meds in the supplied evidence.

Final take and who should choose which

FeelGood Meds vs JoinSkinny differs most on how medications are presented and fulfilled: FeelGood Meds highlights lower starting prices for compounded injections and tablets and shows a branded option at a much higher price point, with a regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. JoinSkinny emphasizes flat-rate pricing, multi-month bundles, fast temperature-controlled shipping, licensed U.S. pharmacy fulfillment and continuous telemedicine support.

Choose FeelGood Meds if you prioritize the lowest advertised starting price for compounded injections or want both compounded injection and tablet options and are willing to confirm eligibility, medication, renewal terms and state availability before enrolling.

Choose JoinSkinny if you want flat-rate dosing, bundled plan options, same-price-per-dose assurances, temperature-controlled next-day shipping and prescriptions filled through U.S.-based, FDA-regulated and LegitScript-certified pharmacy partners.

Omzo vs Hers is a choice between compounded medication access with clinician-guided plans and branded FDA-approved Wegovy with a personalized care team. Early verdict: choose Omzo if you want clinician-guided access to compounded semaglutide starting at $179; choose Hers if you prefer FDA-approved Wegovy from $149/mo and ongoing dose check-ins.

Key difference 1: Compounded medications versus FDA-approved brand

Omzo emphasizes a compounded medication program: compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by a state licensed sterile compounding pharmacy. The Omzo source explicitly states these compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. By contrast, Hers highlights FDA-approved Wegovy, a brand-name semaglutide, and states it does not offer compounded medications.

Key difference 2: Pricing structure and starting costs

Both providers publish starting prices but with different structures. Omzo lists semaglutide starting at $179 and tirzepatide starting at $279, with medication costs included in fees collected by Omzo. Hers lists Wegovy from $149/mo and injectable from $199/mo, but notes an active Hers Weight Loss Membership is required and membership and medication are billed separately.

Key difference 3: Clinical model and follow up

Omzo connects users with licensed U.S. medical providers who determine eligibility and prescribe based on clinical judgment; Omzo is not a medical practice and clinical services are provided by third party networks. Hers promotes a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins and emphasizes doctor-trusted, FDA-approved options. Both note that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Key difference 4: What you receive and who fills prescriptions

Omzo states its primary program provides access to compounded medications produced in FDA-registered facilities but not FDA-approved, and that if a branded medication is prescribed the patient is responsible for filling it at their chosen pharmacy. Hers provides brand-name Wegovy as an option and says name brand medication is not compounded.

How pricing and billing differ

Both providers promote a starting price of $99/mo for certain GLP-1 therapies. Trimi lists compounded semaglutide from $99/mo on the annual plan and tirzepatide at $125/mo, with language that the semaglutide price is the same at every dose. Refills advertises personalized compounded GLP-1 from $99/mo (~$5/day) with flat pricing at every dosage and explicitly notes month-to-month billing with no contracts or cancellation fees. If you need a month-to-month option, Refills states you can cancel anytime. If you want a flat per-dose price with an annual plan, Trimi highlights the annual pricing and consistency as well.

Shipping, fulfillment, and pharmacy notes

Trimi highlights free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and says medications are shipped from named compounding pharmacies such as VialsRx and GreenwichRx with certificates of analysis available on request. Refills notes free discreet and express shipping, and that it offers GLP-1s exclusively from U.S. pharmacies; both providers include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

Logistics and transparency

Trimi emphasizes overnight temperature-controlled shipping and named 503A pharmacies. Refills emphasizes free discreet shipping and U.S. pharmacy fulfillment; Refills also mentions third-party USP testing for partner compounding pharmacies in its bullets.

Clinical access, clinician review, and support

Both services require prescription review by licensed clinicians. Trimi states prescriptions are provided by U.S.-licensed clinicians through Arora Health and names a board-certified physician leading clinical oversight. Refills says a licensed clinician will review intake information, may offer a chat with a doctor, and offers unlimited clinician consults at no extra cost along with 24/7 health concierge support.

Support model

Trimi frames care under a named medical director and a clinical network spanning all 50 states. Refills highlights 24/7 health concierge and unlimited patient care team and doctor access included in the monthly price.

Refunds, trial terms, and cancellation

Trimi advertises a 6-month money-back promise: follow your plan for 6 months and you may request a refund if you do not lose weight, with terms applied. Refills emphasizes month-to-month plans with no contracts and the ability to cancel anytime with no cancellation fees. Both reiterate that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

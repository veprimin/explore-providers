Brello had no verified pricing on the captured landing page and current details were not published or could not be verified, so price and renewal terms must be confirmed directly with Brello.

3. Medication formats and regulatory notes

He & She MD offers semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection as documented options, with medication produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies but noted as not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality. The company states medications require consultation and prescription by a clinician through its platform and that not all customers will qualify for a prescription. For Brello, the captured evidence only records that treatment is available when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate and that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality.

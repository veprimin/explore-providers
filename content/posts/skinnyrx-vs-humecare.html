SkinnyRx vs HumeCare+: what each offers

This comparison focuses on the differences most likely to matter: price and options, formulation and support, outcome tracking and guarantees, shipping and access, and regulatory notes. The primary keyword SkinnyRx vs HumeCare+ frames the direct comparison below.

Price and plan starting points

SkinnyRx lists compounded semaglutide tablets starting at as low as $249 per month. HumeCare+ presents semaglutide from $199 per month and a tirzepatide protocol plus B12 and glycine from $299 per month. HumeCare+ states its semaglutide price may be a promotional markdown from $299. Both providers present monthly pricing as the way they sell treatment.

Formulation and supportive components

SkinnyRx focuses on daily oral semaglutide tablets designed with clinically supported ingredients to help support weight loss and metabolic health. The offering highlights an oral tablet format as an alternative to injections. HumeCare+ sells a protocol that pairs physician prescribed compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide with glycine and vitamins B6 and B12 built into the formulation, positioning these additions as muscle preserving and side-effect supportive.

Outcome support, monitoring and guarantees

HumeCare+ bundles monthly body composition scans that track lean mass versus fat, an app for medication tracking and food logging, and continuous physician optimization. HumeCare+ advertises a 10 percent or your money back promise over six months, with terms to verify directly with the provider. SkinnyRx emphasizes clinician-guided treatment, ongoing support, dose adjustments, and unlimited access to patient care after prescription, but does not advertise a money-back promise in the supplied material.

Shipping, delivery and access

SkinnyRx offers free overnight shipping on all orders and states you will receive shipping and tracking information within two business days after prescription processing. HumeCare+ advertises free next-day delivery, discreet shipping, and a U.S. care team available 24/7. Both note treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

Regulatory and quality notes

Both providers offer compounded medications and include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not evaluated by the FDA for safety, quality, or efficacy. SkinnyRx states partner pharmacies perform third party testing every 3 to 6 months to confirm concentration within plus or minus 10 percent of the active ingredient. HumeCare+ notes compounded medications are dispensed from U.S. pharmacies licensed and inspected by state Boards of Pharmacy.

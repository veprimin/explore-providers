Quick verdict: altRx vs MedVi — altRx positions itself as the lower-cost, limited-offer option (semaglutide starting at $89/month; tirzepatide $149/month), while MedVi emphasizes a clinically dosed semaglutide program with a promotional first month at $179 and 24/7 certified medical support. Price and pricing structure compared

Directly on price, altRx is marketed as the most affordable GLP-1 program with a limited-offer starting price: semaglutide from $89/month and tirzepatide from $149/month. altRx notes multi-month discounts in its FAQ (eg, semaglutide drops on 3-, 6-, and 12-month plans). MedVi advertises a winter promotional first month at $179 including free shipping, but its ongoing standalone month can be $299, with a three-month plan at $649 (about $216/month). These differences make cost predictability and long-term price materially different between the two.

Medication options and clinical dosing

altRx offers compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide (GLP-1 + GIP) as part of its program, with clinicians able to prescribe either based on individual health profiles. MedVi offers clinically dosed compounded semaglutide only; it does not offer tirzepatide per the supplied evidence.

Care model and support

Both platforms use a telehealth model with no clinic visits required. altRx provides 100% online medical visits, ongoing care-team support, and claims a responsive care team. MedVi also uses an online intake with clinician review and highlights 24/7 support from certified medical professionals and a personalized physician review that covers a metabolic report and 1:1 guidance.

Compounded medication status and regulatory notes

Neither provider offers FDA-approved branded products in the supplied evidence; both describe their medications as compounded. altRx explicitly notes its medications are compounded and not FDA-approved. MedVi likewise states compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Both platforms say prescriptions are at the discretion of licensed providers and subject to clinician review.

Availability, shipping, and commitments

altRx states membership is available to residents of all 50 U.S. states, though provider availability and prescribing authority vary by state; it also advertises free, discreet 2-day delivery and no membership, insurance, or hidden fees with cancel-anytime terms. MedVi advertises fast, free shipping for the promotional month, no clinic visits, no membership or hidden fees in the supplied materials, and notes that refills are locked in at the ongoing price; MedVi also points out post-promo price increases for ongoing months.

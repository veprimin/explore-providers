Pricing and membership: starting at vs flat monthly rates

Personalization and clinical approach

Found positions itself as biology-first: a thorough medical evaluation builds a personalized program around metabolic biology, hormones, blood sugar, sleep and behavior. Trimi focuses on prescribing compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide through licensed clinicians with a clinical network and a named medical director, and emphasizes evidence behind the active ingredients. The difference: Found stresses a holistic biology-based plan beyond medication, whereas Trimi emphasizes medication delivery with medical oversight.

Medication types, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications. Found lists tirzepatide and semaglutide as compounded options and notes that compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved or evaluated for safety or efficacy and are not available in all states. Trimi likewise offers compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide from FDA regulated pharmacies and repeats that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Each provider specifies prescriptions are subject to clinician review and eligibility.

Shipping, pharmacies, and logistics

Trimi advertises free overnight, temperature-controlled shipping and that medications are shipped to your door from named state-licensed 503A pharmacies with certificates of analysis available on request. Found mentions handling insurance paperwork for eligible users and a range of pricing and coverage disclosures but does not supply the same overnight shipping claim in the captured evidence.

Refunds, guarantees, and program commitments

Trimi offers a 6-month money-back promise with terms: follow your plan for 6 months and you can request a refund if you do not lose weight, subject to terms. Found discloses offer and discount terms, insurance caveats, and that Rx is subject to provider review and clinical eligibility; Found also shares specific promotional details captured on the landing page. Neither provider guarantees individual results and both note individual results vary.

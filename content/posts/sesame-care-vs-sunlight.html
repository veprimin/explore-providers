Sesame Care vs Sunlight: both offer online GLP-1 weight loss care but take different approaches. Quick verdict: choose Sesame Care if you want broad clinician choice and flexible scheduling; choose Sunlight if you prioritize a lower advertised starting price and fixed monthly pricing.

Explore Sesame Care clinicians and plans

How they differ at a glance: choice, pricing, and delivery

Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select providers based on specialty, availability, and pricing. Sesame advertises ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo, and cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo, with insurance accepted for weight loss medications. Sunlight advertises personalized GLP-1 from $88/mo, injections and oral options, a 100 percent online medical visit, prescription and telehealth included with no insurance required, and “same price forever” plans.

Clinician access and care model

Sesame Care emphasizes a marketplace model so patients can choose their clinician by specialty, availability, and pricing, which may give more control over the care relationship. Sunlight emphasizes a 100 percent online medical visit with clinician involvement but highlights simplicity and speed from sign up to medication delivery.

Pricing structure and membership differences

Sesame Care lists an ongoing plan price “as low as $59/mo” for visits, labs and messaging with a note that medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on insurance status and pharmacy. Sesame also lists cash-pay medication prices and notes price with insurance may be as low as $25 for some medications. Sunlight advertises plans “from $88/mo” and promotes “same price forever” with no monthly membership and cancel anytime language, plus promotional references to as low as $88 and summer promo details.

Medication types, delivery, and expectations

Both sources present injections and oral options. Sesame Care lists semaglutide and tirzepatide price ranges and notes Wegovy pill is FDA-approved; Sesame emphasizes that medication costs are separate and vary by insurance. Sunlight highlights injections and oral options, fast free shipping in 1-2 days, and promotional pricing language. Both include the regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Outcomes and evidence language

Sesame Care references milestones “In 3 months | In 6 months | In 12 months” and notes “Results may vary” with milestones based on a study of injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg when paired with healthy lifestyle changes. Sunlight includes customer testimonials and promotional claims of weight loss experiences but does not provide trial milestone details in the captured evidence. Neither source supplies guarantees of outcomes beyond their cited study or testimonials.

Conclusion: which to pick

Both platforms offer online GLP-1 options. Choose Sesame Care if you want broad telehealth choice and the ability to browse and select clinicians, and if you value flexible scheduling and insurance acceptance for weight loss medications. Choose Sunlight if you prioritize an advertised lower starting plan price, fixed monthly pricing with “same price forever,” and fast free shipping.

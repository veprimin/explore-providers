How they differ: formulation, approval, and pricing

Start Willow and Hers diverge in three key areas: medication type and regulatory status, cost and membership structure, and clinical support plus delivery commitments. These differences drive who each provider is best for.

Medication type and regulatory status

Start Willow delivers once-weekly compounded semaglutide that is a customized treatment option created for Willow patients. Compounded semaglutide is not FDA-approved and is not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. It is prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies operating under state board of pharmacy oversight and USP compounding standards. Hers offers FDA-approved Wegovy, a brand-name semaglutide product, and emphasizes that it is not a compounded medication. Hers also lists other FDA-approved options such as Zepbound and notes Ozempic is FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes and may be prescribed off-label at a clinician’s discretion.

Cost, membership, and access

Start Willow operates on a direct-pay model: first month priced at $249 and ongoing at $299 per month, with no membership fees and no insurance required. This model is positioned for patients frustrated by insurance pre-authorization. Hers lists medication “from $149/mo” for a Wegovy pill and from $199/mo for injectables, but medication requires an active Hers Weight Loss Membership. The membership is $39 for the first month and auto-renews at $149/month thereafter, and medication is not available without an active membership. Hers cautions total cost, eligibility, renewal terms and state availability should be confirmed before enrollment.

Clinical support, personalization, and shipping

Hers highlights a personalized care team with ongoing dose check-ins and emphasizes doctor-trusted GLP-1s and brand-name medication with no price markups. Start Willow describes prescribing compounded semaglutide as part of a personalized treatment plan in collaboration with a Willow doctor and offers written and video injection instructions and support from the Willow Care Team. For delivery, Start Willow guarantees shipping in 3 days or less and offers same-day prescriptions and free 2 day shipping when in stock. Hers notes availability is not uniform in all 50 states and that prescriptions are subject to clinician determination.

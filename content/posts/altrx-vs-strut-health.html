Compounded medications and regulatory note

Both providers use compounded semaglutide in some offerings. Strut Health adds that compounded semaglutide is prepared by a U.S. licensed compounding pharmacy per a prescription when medically necessary.

Side effects, usage notes, and medical necessity

Strut Health materials captured describe common gastrointestinal side effects of semaglutide and emphasize that oral lozenges are used once daily while injections are typically weekly. Strut Health states compounded semaglutide is available only for patients who fail or cannot use commercially available GLP-1 medications and that medical necessity may be required. altRx materials note that a licensed provider will review health information and may prescribe compounded GLP-1 or GLP-1/GIP if treatment is appropriate, subject to clinician review.

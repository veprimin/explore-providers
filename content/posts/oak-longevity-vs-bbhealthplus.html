Oak Longevity vs Bodybuilding Health+ compares two telehealth platforms offering compounded GLP-1s. Quick verdict: Oak Longevity is the lower-cost option with one flat price for all dosages and free coaching, while Bodybuilding Health+ bundles GLP-1s into multi-month plans and emphasizes a broader multi-program platform and physician-led oversight.

Key differences at a glance for Oak Longevity vs Bodybuilding Health+

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive, supported differences: pricing and pricing structure, program breadth and extras, clinician oversight and availability, compounded medication and regulatory notes, and shipping, billing, and cancellation policies.

1. Pricing and pricing structure

Oak Longevity advertises semaglutide starting at $133/month and tirzepatide starting at $199/month with one flat price that covers all dosages. Bodybuilding Health+ lists semaglutide from $179/month and tirzepatide from $209/month and offers multi-month bundles where longer commitments lower the effective monthly rate. The core difference: Oak presents a single low starting price and one price for all dosages, while Bodybuilding Health+ provides tiered monthly pricing through 1 to 12 month bundles.

2. Program breadth and extras

Oak Longevity is positioned as focused on GLP-1 weight loss with free health coaching and free shipping included, and no subscriptions or membership fees. Bodybuilding Health+ is a multi-program telehealth platform offering GLP-1s plus NAD+, sermorelin, and sexual performance options, emphasizing a broader set of therapies beyond weight management.

If you want a broader set of longevity and performance offerings, Bodybuilding Health+ documents NAD+ and sermorelin programs and ED options alongside GLP-1s. If you want a low-cost, GLP-1-focused plan with free coaching, Oak states free coaching and free shipping are included.

3. Clinician oversight, approvals, and process

Both platforms operate fully online and require a clinician to determine medical appropriateness. Oak Longevity notes clinical review is asynchronous with no video call required and board-certified physicians in your state review every case. Bodybuilding Health+ says a U.S.-licensed healthcare provider reviews intake and that medical oversight is led by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D. Both require physician approval before medication ships.

4. Compounded medications and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. The comparison evidence for each includes the regulatory note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Oak specifically states its offerings are compounded versions of the active ingredients in prescription GLP-1 products prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies. Bodybuilding Health+ similarly notes compounded GLP-1s are prepared by licensed U.S. pharmacies but not evaluated or approved by the FDA.

5. Shipping, billing cadence, cancellation, and eligibility limits

Oak states free shipping with tracking, same-day approval availability for qualifying patients, and billing monthly on a non-recurring basis with no subscription; three-month prescriptions are billed upfront and no refunds are available after physician approval and once medication has shipped. Oak also notes patients with BMI below 20 do not qualify for GLP-1 treatment and elsewhere states not appropriate for BMI under 22 without comorbidities. Bodybuilding Health+ states no monthly membership, cancel anytime, and no hidden fees, with bundles up to 12 months for lower effective monthly cost. Bodybuilding Health+ also notes not available in all U.S. states and that physician approval is required.

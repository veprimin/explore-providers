How SkinnyRx vs Mochi Health differ on pricing and membership

SkinnyRx presents a flat starting price for its compounded semaglutide tablets, listed as as low as $249/mo and notes options to split payments with Affirm. Mochi Health operates a membership-plus-medication structure, with pricing from $99/month and promotional offers such as 50% off your first month with code BESTWLHALFOFF. Mochi also advertises a $39 promotional first-month rate for some treatments for new customers; after that the standard membership rate is $79 per month where applicable. The providers therefore differ in transparency and structure: SkinnyRx advertises an itemized monthly cost for its oral semaglutide product while Mochi bundles membership and medication with variable final monthly cost depending on plan and medication.

Clinical access and visit model compared

SkinnyRx uses online consultations with licensed providers and requires a prescription after clinician review; it emphasizes ongoing provider access and dose adjustments. Mochi Health distinguishes itself with live video visits bookable in about 5 minutes, a synchronous model that the source calls fundamentally different from the asynchronous text-review model used by many competitors. Mochi states a licensed physician evaluates cases during the video consultation before any prescription is issued.

Medication formats, delivery, and testing

Both providers work with compounded medications and include regulatory disclaimers that compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. SkinnyRx focuses on daily oral semaglutide tablets taken under the tongue and highlights free overnight shipping and partner pharmacy third-party testing every 3 to 6 months confirming concentration within plus or minus 10 percent of the active ingredient. Mochi Health lists multiple compounded formats including once-weekly subcutaneous injections (semaglutide or tirzepatide) and a once-weekly oral semaglutide pill, plus a range of other compounded options; Mochi emphasizes national prescription access in all 50 states.

What to expect on outcomes, monitoring, and regulatory notes

Neither provider claims guaranteed results. SkinnyRx cites potential outcomes such as losing up to 8.6 lbs your first month and includes patient examples captured on the landing page, while also noting semaglutide-related side effects like nausea or digestive changes and that compounded semaglutide has not been approved by the FDA for weight loss. Mochi reports estimated member results based on self-reported averages and explicitly cautions that individual results are not guaranteed and will vary. Both platforms require clinician review before prescriptions and both include regulatory notes about compounded medications not being FDA-reviewed.

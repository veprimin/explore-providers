1. Pricing structure and commitments

Both providers list a $99 entry point for semaglutide plans, but the models differ. Eden Health advertises semaglutide at $99 and tirzepatide at $129 on a one monthly price model and states a lifetime price guarantee. By contrast HealthRx lists $99/month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan and notes the lowest advertised monthly price requires a longer prepaid plan; tirzepatide plans start at $179/month. HealthRx also specifies a sample prepaid total ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal) for a 12-month plan. Confirm eligibility, renewal terms and total cost before enrolling with either provider.

2. Medication sourcing and regulatory notes

Eden Health promotes semaglutide and tirzepatide within a guided GLP-1 program and includes a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. HealthRx is explicit that its semaglutide or tirzepatide is compounded per prescription in a licensed 503A pharmacy cleanroom and also states compounded medication is not FDA-approved and is prepared for an individual patient with a valid prescription. Both note that treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate.

3. Shipping and handling differences

Shipping is a clear contrast. Eden Health advertises free expedited shipping. HealthRx specifies overnight cold shipping with a cold chain that is tracked and free on every plan. If maintaining cold chain shipping is a priority, HealthRx documents overnight cold shipping and tracking; Eden highlights expedited shipping included at no cost.

4. Clinical decision process and dose policy

Both providers emphasize clinician review. Eden frames its program as clinician-reviewed with treatment available when a licensed clinician deems it medically appropriate. HealthRx emphasizes that you are not selecting a medication from a menu; rather the clinician reviewing your file decides whether semaglutide, tirzepatide or neither is appropriate. HealthRx also notes that your plan price does not change when your dose does, and there is no separate consultation fee or shipping charge on plans described.

Bottom line and how to choose

When comparing Eden Health vs HealthRx: Eden Health is built around a single monthly price with a lifetime price guarantee and free expedited shipping, and lists semaglutide $99 and tirzepatide $129. HealthRx centers on compounded medication prepared per prescription, lowest rates on longer prepaid plans ($99/month for a 12-month prepaid semaglutide example), overnight cold shipping, and a clinician-driven medication decision; compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Confirm eligibility, medication approval status, pharmacy and renewal terms before enrolling with either service.

Choose Eden Health if you want a one monthly price with a stated lifetime price guarantee, free expedited shipping, and listed semaglutide $99 or tirzepatide $129.

Choose HealthRx if you prefer compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide prepared per prescription with overnight cold chain shipping and are willing to use a longer prepaid plan for the lowest advertised monthly price.

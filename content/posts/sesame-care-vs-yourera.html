Overview: Sesame Care vs YourEra

Both platforms offer telehealth for GLP-1 weight loss care, but they do so with different models. Sesame Care is a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select providers based on specialty, availability, and pricing. YourEra is a doctor-guided telehealth platform that prescribes compounded GLP-1 and ships medication with an all-inclusive plan price on a 6-month plan.

1. Clinician access and care model

Sesame Care’s marketplace model is its defining feature. Patients can browse a large clinician network and select providers based on specialty, availability, and pricing, which may give more control over the care relationship. YourEra connects you with licensed US clinicians for a doctor-guided telehealth workflow, but the site emphasizes a bundled, flat-price plan rather than clinician selection as the primary differentiator.

2. Pricing structure and insurance

Sesame Care lists a subscription starting at $59/mo for ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs, and messaging based on a 12-month commitment option. Sesame also states cash-pay GLP-1 medications can be as low as $149/mo and that insurance is accepted for weight loss medications, with prices varying by insurance status and pharmacy. YourEra advertises compounded semaglutide from $99/mo and tirzepatide from $199/mo on a 6-month plan; the lowest advertised rates reflect that 6-month commitment and YourEra notes shorter plans cost more per month.

3. Medication types, regulatory notes, and implications

Sesame Care offers branded options including semaglutide and tirzepatide with cash-pay and insurance pricing details. The source warns that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. YourEra dispenses compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies; the platform clearly states compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not the same as brand-name drugs.

4. What is bundled and how medication is delivered

YourEra bundles the provider consult, prescription, compounded medication, cold/discreet shipping, dose adjustments, and 24/7 support into a flat plan price and offers free 1-2 day shipping from licensed US pharmacies. Sesame Care’s subscription covers ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs, and messaging for the subscription price; medication costs are separate and depend on insurance and pharmacy.

Choosing between Sesame Care and YourEra

Sesame Care vs YourEra comes down to tradeoffs: Sesame provides clinician choice, flexible scheduling, nationwide access in many states, and insurance acceptance for weight loss medications. YourEra focuses on lower entry pricing for compounded GLP-1 with a flat price at every dose, bundled shipping and support, and a 6-month plan structure. Both note that prescriptions are issued only if a licensed clinician determines treatment is appropriate and that individual results vary.

Choose Sesame Care if…

You want broad telehealth choice, the ability to browse and select clinicians, flexible scheduling, and options to use insurance for weight loss medications. Sesame Care also lists subscriptions starting at $59/mo that cover ongoing visits, labs, and messaging, with cash-pay branded GLP-1 meds from $149/mo.

Choose YourEra if…

You prioritize lower flat pricing for compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide on a 6-month plan, want an all-inclusive bundle that includes consult, prescription, cold shipping, dose adjustments, and 24/7 support, and are comfortable using compounded medications that are not FDA-approved finished products.

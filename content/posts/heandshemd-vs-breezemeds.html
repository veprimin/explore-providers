Quick verdict: He & She MD vs BreezeMeds shows He & She MD emphasizes bundled coaching, 1:1 clinician messaging, and lower introductory semaglutide injection pricing, while BreezeMeds highlights multiple injectable and oral GLP-1 options with discreet shipping and starting prices that begin at $199/month.

How they differ: Price structure and introductory rates

He & She MD lists compounded semaglutide injections starting at $139/month as a six-month introductory rate, with oral semaglutide and tirzepatide injections starting at $199/month for the first six months. The vendor notes those are promo prices that renew higher after the six-month term. BreezeMeds shows semaglutide injection starting at $199/month, tirzepatide injections starting at $299, and oral options displayed from $399; a separate $169 figure appears on BreezeMeds without a clear product association. Treatment availability and exact billing commitments for BreezeMeds were not fully visible in the captured evidence.

Practical takeaway

If introductory price for injectable semaglutide matters, He & She MD provides a clearly stated six-month promo starting at $139/month. BreezeMeds documents higher starting figures and an unclear $169 note that is not tied to a specific product in the captured evidence.

Coaching, clinician access, and program support

He & She MD bundles a coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging, GLP-1 coaching, and progress tracking at no extra cost as part of its plans. BreezeMeds lists online intake and a personalized treatment plan and notes discreet home delivery and ongoing support, but the captured evidence does not show bundled 1:1 clinician messaging or an app included with price the way He & She MD does.

Medication formats and regulatory notes

Both vendors offer injectable semaglutide and tirzepatide plus oral GLP-1 options. He & She MD specifies semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection. BreezeMeds lists injectable and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide. Both sources caution about compounded medication regulatory status: He & She MD explicitly states medications included in the GLP-1 Program are produced in USP-compliant, state-regulated pharmacies but are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, efficacy, or quality. BreezeMeds captured evidence says medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable.

Transparency on billing terms and shipping

He & She MD provides explicit detail that promotional rates apply for the first six months and that plans renew at higher six-month prices; it also notes free expedited shipping and no membership fee. BreezeMeds markets discreet home delivery and starting prices but the captured evidence did not fully display fees, renewal terms, or clear billing commitments.

Final comparison and recommendation

Across He & She MD vs BreezeMeds, He & She MD emphasizes bundled coaching, explicit six-month promotional pricing beginning at $139/month for semaglutide injection, and free expedited shipping. BreezeMeds documents a broader set of listed starting prices including $199/month for semaglutide injection and higher listed prices for oral products, and highlights discreet home delivery and an online intake. Both require clinician review to determine medical appropriateness and both signal that medication approval or compounding status should be confirmed.

Choose He & She MD if you want lower introductory semaglutide injection pricing plus a coaching app with 1:1 clinician messaging and built-in progress tracking.

Choose BreezeMeds if you prefer a provider that emphasizes discreet delivery and a personalized treatment plan but you will confirm eligibility, total cost, renewal terms, and state availability before enrolling.

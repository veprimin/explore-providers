How these two programs differ at a glance

This comparison uses only captured landing-page facts. The most distinctive differences are pricing transparency and starting price, medication and administration formats, billing term structure, shipping and membership terms, and the extent of published program details and eligibility notes.

Pricing transparency and starting price

BetterMe Rx publishes specific monthly-equivalent prices and a clear starting price: compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan, with listed monthly prices for semaglutide injection $205 or as low as $145 on a 12-month plan, tirzepatide injection $275 or as low as $215, oral semaglutide $250 or as low as $185, and oral tirzepatide $265 or as low as $215. Luvo Health’s captured landing page did not publish verifiable current pricing or a confirmed starting price on the captured affiliate landing page.

Medication types and formats

Both programs offer semaglutide and tirzepatide in some form and both note compounded medications are not FDA-approved. BetterMe Rx explicitly offers four formats: injectable and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide, including a needle-free daily tirzepatide tablet option. Luvo Health lists tirzepatide injection, semaglutide tablets and semaglutide injection on the captured landing page, but captured details about formats and microdosing notes were present in lists rather than clear pricing or plan structure.

Billing terms, plan length and cancellation

BetterMe Rx shows that longer 3, 6 or 12-month supplies are billed according to the selected plan, that lowest monthly equivalents require a 12-month plan, and that plans include cancel-anytime language alongside prepaid longer-term plans. Luvo Health’s captured landing page references month 1, month 2 to 3 and 3 plus months and notes cancel anytime and dose adjustments based on clinical response, but current pricing, renewal or billing specifics were not verified on the captured affiliate landing page.

Shipping, membership and included services

BetterMe Rx states medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies and free 1 to 2 day shipping are included, and that there is no membership fee and no hidden fees. Luvo Health’s captured landing page noted ongoing support and dose changes and cancel anytime, but captured evidence did not verify shipping terms, membership fees or exact inclusions.

Regulatory notes and eligibility

Both sources include the regulatory caution that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Both state treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate. Luvo Health captured content also included trademark notices for Ozempic and Wegovy and editorial resources, while BetterMe Rx explicitly states provider review and partnered U.S. pharmacy compounding.

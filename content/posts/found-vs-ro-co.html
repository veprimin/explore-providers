1. Medication type and regulatory status

Found commonly offers compounded GLP-1 options such as compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and also supports branded GLP-1 prescriptions when appropriate. The source explicitly states compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved and are not evaluated for safety or efficacy. Ro.co is unique among the two for offering access to the Wegovy pill, identified in the source as the first and only FDA-approved oral GLP-1 weight loss medication; the Wegovy pill is presented as a finished, FDA-approved product rather than a compounded option.

2. Pricing entry point and ongoing costs

Both platforms promote a $39 online entry point. Found advertises “get started for as low as $39” and emphasizes affordable monthly plans and flexible pricing, with multiple references to specific plan pricing structures and offers. Ro.co also states “get started online for just $39” and notes ongoing branded medication pricing can be higher; the source warns that branded FDA-approved medications generally cost more than compounded alternatives and that ongoing pricing depends on the plan and medication prescribed.

3. Insurance, Medicare, and paperwork

Found explicitly states most insurance is now accepted, that Found handles insurance paperwork, and references a Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program with examples of potential $50/month pricing for eligible Part D members; the source preserves qualifiers such as eligibility criteria and that this is not a guarantee of coverage. Ro.co’s source notes that insurance coverage for GLP-1 weight loss medication is inconsistent, that Ro’s $39 entry is direct-pay, and that ongoing branded medication pricing depends on plan and often requires out-of-pocket payment. Neither source guarantees insurance coverage; both preserve clinician review and eligibility qualifiers.

4. Clinical model and personalization

Found describes a biology-first, personalized medically supervised program that builds plans around an individual’s metabolic biology, hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, and behavior. Ro.co is presented as an established full-service telehealth platform that prescribes FDA-approved medications after a licensed clinician determines treatment is medically appropriate. The difference is framed in the sources as Found emphasizing holistic, biology-based personalization and Ro.co emphasizing access to FDA-approved, finished medications like the Wegovy pill through clinician review.

Final thoughts on Found vs Ro.co

Found vs Ro.co comes down to priorities: Found emphasizes a biology-first, personalized program with insurance handling and offers compounded options alongside branded GLP-1s; Ro.co emphasizes access to the FDA-approved Wegovy pill and transparent pricing before consultation. Both require clinician review and note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

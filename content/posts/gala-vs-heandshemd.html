Gala vs He & She MD compares two direct pay telehealth options for compounded GLP 1 care. Quick verdict: Gala emphasizes one flat all inclusive price with free two day shipping and a $220 promotion, while He & She MD emphasizes lower introductory monthly rates from $139 with an included coaching app but prices renew higher after six months.

Side by side: what sets Gala and He & She MD apart

This comparison focuses on the most distinctive differences supported by the sources: pricing structure and transparency, medication formats offered, support and coaching, shipping and membership terms, and compounded medication regulatory notes.

Pricing structure and transparency

Gala advertises a single flat all inclusive price that allegedly covers provider visit, medication, shipping and ongoing care and promotes a $220 off offer. The capture notes that Gala’s base dollar price was not visible in readable page text. He & She MD lists explicit starting rates: semaglutide injections start at $139/month and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide at $199/month as six month introductory rates that renew higher after the first six months. He & She MD’s page explicitly warns the headline prices are six month intro rates that renew 30 45 percent higher.

View He & She MD intro rates and app details

Medication formats and availability

Gala offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide according to the source. He & She MD offers three formats: semaglutide injection, oral semaglutide, and tirzepatide injection. Both providers require a clinician review before prescriptions are issued and both reference compounded GLP 1 products in their materials.

Support model: check ins versus coaching app

Gala highlights provider check ins and dose monitoring included with the service. He & She MD bundles a coaching app with 1 1 clinician messaging, GLP 1 specific coaching and progress tracking at no extra cost. He & She MD positions this as built in support meant for patients who want ongoing accountability rather than buying coaching separately.

Shipping, membership and cancellation

Gala advertises free two day shipping, no monthly membership and cancel anytime language on the landing page. He & She MD advertises free expedited shipping and no membership fee, but the pricing is structured as six month terms with promotional first six months followed by higher renewal pricing.

Regulatory note and disclaimers

Both sources include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety effectiveness or quality. Gala’s source repeats that compounded medications when offered are not FDA approved. He & She MD notes medications are produced in USP compliant state regulated pharmacies but not reviewed by the FDA for safety efficacy or quality.

Quick verdict: BetterMe Rx focuses on four compounded GLP-1 formats including injectable and oral semaglutide and tirzepatide with free 1–2 day shipping and lower monthly equivalents for longer plans, while JoinFridays emphasizes the lowest starting price in its tier at $117/month, personalized clinician-guided care, and cancel-anytime flexibility.

How BetterMe Rx vs JoinFridays differ at a glance

This comparison uses the supplied facts to focus on the most distinctive differences: pricing and starting rates, plan flexibility and cancellations, medication formats and availability, discounts and promo codes, and shipping and membership terms. Both prescribe compounded GLP-1 options after a licensed clinician review and both note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Pricing and starting rates

BetterMe Rx lists compounded GLP-1 treatment from $145/month on a 12-month plan with monthly examples: semaglutide injection $205/mo or as low as $145/mo on a 12-month plan; tirzepatide injection $275/mo or as low as $215/mo; oral semaglutide $250/mo or as low as $185/mo; oral tirzepatide $265/mo or as low as $215/mo. JoinFridays starts at $117/month, with standalone one-month plan at $249 and multi-month plans that lower the effective rate to about $150/mo over 12 months; tirzepatide can be as low as $198/mo.

Plan flexibility and cancellation

JoinFridays explicitly promotes no long-term contracts and cancel-anytime terms, and notes its lowest starting price reflects introductory or shortest-term pricing. BetterMe Rx offers cancel-anytime language alongside prepaid longer-term plans and bills longer 3-, 6-, or 12-month supplies according to the selected plan; its lowest monthly equivalents require a 12-month plan.

Medication formats and clinical model

BetterMe Rx sells compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide in injectable and oral formats and highlights a needle-free daily tirzepatide tablet option. It includes provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies, and free 1–2 day shipping when offered. JoinFridays provides both compounded tirzepatide and semaglutide and emphasizes personalized, clinician-guided care; it offers both compounded and name-brand options on some plans and notes that Fridays does not manufacture compounded medications.

Discounts, promo codes, and membership

JoinFridays advertises aggressive promo codes (for example NEWYOU12 and NYNY12) that the source says offer up to $500 off tirzepatide and up to $400 off semaglutide, and it states membership is included free and there are no membership fees. BetterMe Rx states no membership fee and no insurance or hidden fees, but its lowest pricing depends on longer prepaid plans rather than promo codes.

Shipping, supplies, and billing cadence

BetterMe Rx includes medication, provider review, pharmacy compounding, supplies, and free 1–2 day shipping in its model. JoinFridays does not manufacture compounded medications and notes actual product appearance will differ from website images; its pricing examples show month-to-month and multi-month billing that reduce effective monthly rates for longer commitments.

What matters most: direct tradeoffs

If lowest possible advertised entry price is your priority, JoinFridays has the lowest starting tier at $117/month and promotes active promo codes and no-contract cancel-anytime terms. If you want multiple administration formats including injectable and needle-free oral options plus included fast shipping and are comfortable with prepaid multi-month billing to reach lower monthly equivalents, BetterMe Rx emphasizes those features and lower 12-month equivalents.

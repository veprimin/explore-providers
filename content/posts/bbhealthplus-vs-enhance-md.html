Clinical oversight and scale

Bodybuilding Health+ names medical oversight by Dr. Mark Rosenberg, M.D. and highlights provider-reviewed medications and online clinician review. Regulatory and formulation notes to consider

Both sources clearly state that compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. Bodybuilding Health+ also notes limited availability in some U.S. states and that physician approval is required. Enhance MD clarifies that the low advertised rates are introductory first-month prices and that ongoing flat-rate pricing applies thereafter.

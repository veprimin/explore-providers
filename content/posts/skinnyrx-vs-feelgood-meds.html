How SkinnyRx vs FeelGood Meds differ at a glance

The most distinctive differences supported by the source are: medication format and delivery, starting price points, FSA/HSA eligibility and shipping speed, availability of compounded injections versus tablets, and presence of a branded, higher-cost option at FeelGood Meds. Below we compare each directly so you can decide which aligns with your priorities.

Medication format and prescribing model

SkinnyRx: Offers daily oral semaglutide tablets taken under the tongue, positioned as an alternative for people who prefer not to use injections. Treatment is available only if prescribed after an online consultation with a licensed provider and may include compounded medications if prescribed; compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

FeelGood Meds: Offers both compounded injections and compounded tablets, plus a branded medication option. Treatment availability is subject to clinician review and treatment is available only when a licensed clinician determines it is medically appropriate; compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

Price and plan starting points

SkinnyRx lists oral semaglutide tablets starting at “As low as $249/mo” with options to split payments via Affirm Buy Now Pay Later. FeelGood Meds shows multiple starting points: compounded injections from $149/mo, compounded tablets from $249/mo, and a branded option shown from $1,999/mo. FeelGood Meds emphasizes “no membership fee” and no insurance required for starting.

Payment, shipping, and financial flexibility

SkinnyRx: Advertises FSA/HSA eligibility to use pre-tax dollars toward treatment and free overnight shipping on all orders. This may provide financial flexibility not offered by all competitors. After prescription completion, medication is processed and customers receive shipping and tracking information within 2 business days.

FeelGood Meds: Highlights free shipping and no membership fees. The provider also notes “no insurance required” for starting and a weight loss money back guarantee is mentioned on their page. Exact renewal terms and eligibility are stated as items customers should confirm.

Regulatory and quality notes both providers share

Both SkinnyRx and FeelGood Meds offer compounded medications in some options and both sources explicitly state that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. SkinnyRx adds that partner pharmacies perform third-party testing every 3 to 6 months to confirm concentration within plus or minus 10 percent for each compounded lot.

Outcomes, claims, and patient reports

SkinnyRx presents claims such as “Lose up to 8.6 lbs your first month” and patient examples like losing 37 lbs in 5 months. FeelGood Meds presents multiple user-reported outcomes such as 119 lbs lost in 13 months and shorter-term testimonials. These are presented as landing-page claims or customer reports; the sources do not supply clinical trial results within the captured evidence here.

Which should you choose: final guidance

Choose SkinnyRx if you prioritize daily oral semaglutide tablets, FSA/HSA eligibility to pay with pre-tax dollars, and free overnight shipping with partner pharmacy testing disclosed by the provider. Choose FeelGood Meds if you want lower starting prices for compounded injections (from $149/mo), a no membership fee structure, and options that include a higher-cost branded medication around $1,999/mo that you can confirm with a clinician.

Both providers require clinician review for treatment and note that compounded medications are not FDA-approved. Confirm eligibility, renewal terms, and state availability with each provider before enrolling.

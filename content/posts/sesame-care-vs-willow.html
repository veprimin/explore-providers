Sesame Care vs Willow: which telehealth weight management option fits you? Quick verdict: Sesame Care emphasizes clinician choice, flexible scheduling, and lower-starting subscription options, while Willow emphasizes a structured program with progress tracking and follow-up support.

Explore Sesame Care clinician options

How Sesame Care and Willow differ at a glance

Both offer telehealth weight management with oral GLP-1 options available, but they take different approaches. Below we compare the most distinctive differences: pricing and starting fees, care model and clinician access, follow-up and tracking, medication pricing and insurance signals, and regulatory notes captured for each provider.

1. Pricing and starting fees

Sesame Care lists ongoing video visits, dedicated provider care, labs and messaging for as low as $59/mo based on a 12-month Success by Sesame commitment. Sesame also lists cash-pay GLP-1 medications as low as $149/mo and shows medication price examples with price with insurance as low as $25 for some branded options. Willow shows a starting monthly price of 299 on the captured page.

See Sesame Care pricing details

2. Care model and clinician access

Sesame Care is presented as a telehealth marketplace that lets patients browse and select clinicians by specialty, availability, and pricing. That marketplace model emphasizes patient choice and a large clinician network with nationwide access in many states. Willow positions its program as a telehealth-first weight management journey with a licensed telehealth team and a structured support model.

3. Follow-up, progress tracking, and program structure

Willow emphasizes progress tracking tools, follow-up consultations, and structured digital follow-up to support long-term outcomes. Sesame Care highlights flexible appointments, ongoing video visits, and messaging, noting dedicated provider care but also that experience can vary by clinician.

View Willow program features

4. Medication pricing, insurance, and examples

Sesame Care provides detailed medication examples and notes that medication costs are not included in the subscription price and will vary based on insurance status and pharmacy. Sesame lists semaglutide cash-pay prices and insurance-price examples, and lists tirzepatide price examples including multiple strengths and monthly cash-pay amounts. Willow captured evidence includes a listed price of 299 and does not provide the same medication-by-medication price breakdown on the captured page.

5. Regulatory and availability notes

Both sources include a regulatory note that medication-specific approval status must be confirmed and that branded and compounded products are not interchangeable. Willow explicitly notes that compounded medications, when offered, are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Sesame Care includes the same caution about approval status and interchangeability.

Practical differences that matter

If you prioritize selecting a clinician from a large network and flexible scheduling with lower-starting subscription pricing, Sesame Care provides a marketplace model and example medication price points including insurance-based pricing as low as $25 in captured evidence. If you prioritize structured programs with integrated progress tracking and scheduled follow-up consultations, Willow emphasizes those program features and a telehealth-first workflow.

Limitations of the captured evidence

Captured pages did not provide complete pricing for every plan or full details on shipping, fees, membership cancellation, or availability by state for Willow. Sesame Care captured evidence includes detailed medication pricing examples and the note that medication costs vary by insurance and pharmacy. Where evidence is absent, it is not supplied in the source material and is not included here.

Conclusion: Choose based on care model and priorities

Sesame Care vs Willow comes down to choice versus structure. Sesame Care favors marketplace choice, flexible appointments, and lower-starting subscription evidence. Willow favors a structured program with progress tracking and follow-up support and shows a 299 monthly entry on the captured page. Both include regulatory cautions about approval status and compounded products.

Choose Sesame Care if you want broad telehealth clinician choice, flexible scheduling, and subscription options that start as low as $59/mo based on captured evidence.

Choose Willow if you want a telehealth-first, structured weight management journey with progress tracking and planned follow-up consultations.

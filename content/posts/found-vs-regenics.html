Found vs Regenics: how their clinical approach and pricing differ

Regenics provides nurse-practitioner-led GLP-1 care focused on access to compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, positioned as a straightforward cash-pay entry with semaglutide at $139 first month and tirzepatide at $189 first month.

Medications, compounding, and regulatory notes

Both providers offer compounded GLP-1 medications and include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality. Found mentions Tirzepatide and Compounded Semaglutide and references brand-name GLP-1s in some plans. Regenics offers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide prepared by U.S. compounding pharmacies and also offers a Sermorelin peptide therapy option.

Pharmacy, shipping, and pharmacy network

Regenics states medications are compounded in the USA and shipped free to your door, and that its pharmacy network is LegitScript certified. Found emphasizes insurance handling and programs including Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program details for eligible Part D members and says it allows use of insurance benefits to save on compounded GLP-1s for some patients.

Price and promotions: cheapest entry versus lifetime lock

Embody advertises the lowest entry price with semaglutide at $69/month and tirzepatide at $119/month. That $69 semaglutide price is presented as a cut from $299 and described as a limited-time offer that may increase. TrimRx starts at $149/month for compounded GLP-1 and $249/month for GLP-1+GIP, and separately offers a $140 promotional discount. TrimRx highlights Lifetime Price Protection so you pay the same price on every dose once locked in.

Program features and scope: bundled services versus lean value

TrimRx positions itself around scale and bundled features: 100k+ customers, a 4.8 Excellent Rating, telehealth visits included, lifetime price protection, HSA/FSA eligibility, 24/7 support, and marketing copy that highlights no membership and cancel anytime. Embody emphasizes a lean, low cost model: prescription and telemed visits included, fast free shipping, no long term commitment, and fewer premium add-ons. Which matters depends on whether you value bundled service and scale or the lowest monthly cash price.

Medication options and regulatory disclosure

Both providers primarily offer compounded GLP-1 medications and both include the regulatory qualifier that compounded medications are not FDA-approved finished products and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. TrimRx also notes providers may write prescriptions for branded medications like Ozempic or Wegovy, but TrimRx does not sell or ship branded medications and patients would fill those prescriptions separately. Embody does not list branded fills and positions its offering as compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide only.

Who the platforms are best for and support details

Embody is described as best for cost-driven patients seeking a straightforward, no-frills GLP-1 program at very low introductory prices. TrimRx is described as an online GLP-1 weight loss care provider with broader scale, lifetime price protection, and bundled customer support and services, positioning it for patients who want predictable long term pricing and access to both GLP-1 and GLP-1+GIP compounded options. Both platforms state prescription and telemed visits are included and both offer fast shipping after approval.

Conclusion and choosing guidance

TrimRx vs Embody comes down to whether you prioritize lowest possible monthly cost now or predictable long term pricing and a broader bundled service set. Both use compounded medications that are not FDA-approved, provide telehealth clinician review, and ship in 1–2 days after approval.
